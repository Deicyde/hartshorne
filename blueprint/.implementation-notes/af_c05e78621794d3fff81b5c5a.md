# Projective bundles

## Status

Partial. The definition of `P(E)` and condition (†) for `Sym(E)` are formalized, and so is the
free case over an affine open (with the projection and every `𝒪(k)`). Over an arbitrary open `U`
(the article does not assume `U` affine) the isomorphism `π⁻¹(U) ≅ ℙⁿ_U` over `U` is formalized;
its compatibility with `𝒪(1)` over a non-affine `U` is still open, so `lean:` is not set.

## Remaining goal

For an arbitrary open `U` with a frame `e` of rank `n + 1`, and `ψ` from
`exists_projectiveBundleIso_of_frame`: `ψ^* 𝒪_{ℙⁿ_U}(1) ≅ 𝒪_{P(E)}(1)|_{π⁻¹(U)}` (and then the
final statement for any open `U` with `E|_U ≅ 𝒪_U^{n+1}`, projection and `𝒪(1)` together).

The affine isomorphisms `projectiveBundleFrameIsoTwist` cannot simply be glued: local isomorphisms
of invertible sheaves glue only if they agree on overlaps, and comparing those composites on
sections is hard. Route: `Hartshorne.Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` (invertible sheaves with
one-element frames on a cover and equal transition functions are isomorphic), as in
`exists_iso_of_chartData` (Theorem 7.1). Cover `π⁻¹(U)` by the opens
`W_{V,i} = π⁻¹(V) ∩ ψ⁻¹(D₊(z_i))`, `V ⊆ U` affine.
- `a_{V,i}`: restriction of `ψ^* z_i` (`pullbackTwist`, `pullbackGlobalSection`); frames by
  `isTrivializingSection_chartPreimage`, transitions `ψ^#(z_j/z_i)` by
  `restrict_pullbackGlobalSection_eq_smul`.
- `b_{V,i}`: the degree-one section `e_i` of `𝒪_{Proj Γ(V, S(E))}(1)`
  (`ProjTwistingSheaf.degreeOneSection`, a frame on `D₊(e_i)` by `isLocalFrame_degreeOneSection`,
  transitions `e_j/e_i` by `restrict_degreeOneSection_eq_smul`), carried to `𝒪_{P(E)}(1)` by
  `relTwistingSheafRestrictIso`.
- Needed: (1) `b_{V,i}|_{D(s)} = b_{D(s),i}` for a basic open `D(s) ⊆ V` (`ψ_compat` of the
  directed gluing, and `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap.twistRestrictHom_app_degreeOneSection`, proved:
  the transition map sends the restriction of `degreeOneSection s` to `degreeOneSection (φ s)`);
  (2) on the
  piece over `V`, `ψ⁻¹(D₊(z_i)) = D₊(e_i)` and `ψ^#(z_j/z_i) = e_j/e_i` (`Proj.map_preimage_basicOpen`,
  `Proj.awayToSection_comp_appLE` applied to the private `projectiveBundleFrameIso_hom_fst`);
  (3) the transition relation on `W_{V,i} ∩ W_{V',j}`, checked on opens basic in `V` and `V'`;
  (4) transport of trivializing sections along `M.restrict f` for an open immersion `f`
  (sections over `W` are sections of `M` over `f(W)`), for `relTwistingSheafRestrictIso` and for
  `B = 𝒪_{P(E)}(1)|_{π⁻¹(U)}`; `IsTrivializingSection.map_iso` handles the isomorphisms.
