# Projective bundles

## Status

Partial. The definition of `P(E)` and condition (†) for `Sym(E)` are formalized, and so is the
free case over an affine open (with the projection and every `𝒪(k)`). The free case over an
arbitrary open `U` (the article does not assume `U` affine) is still open, so `lean:` is not set.

`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleTrivialization`.

- `Hartshorne.SheafAlgebraOperations.PresheafAlgebra.IsGraded.isInternal_sections`: on a ringed

## Remaining goal

For an arbitrary open `U` and a frame of `E` of rank `n + 1` over `U` (or `E|_U ≅ 𝒪_U^{n+1}`):
`π⁻¹(U) ≅ ℙⁿ_U` over `U`, with `ψ^* 𝒪(k) ≅ 𝒪_{P(E)}(k)|_{π⁻¹U}`.

Route: glue `projectiveBundleFrameIso` over the affine opens `V ⊆ U` (frame restricted). Needed:
naturality of `projectiveBundleFrameIso` for a basic open `D(f) ⊆ V` (relative Proj side:
`isPullback_projι`/`projMap_toSpec` along site arrows; ring side: `chartRingHom_naturality`;
projective-space side: naturality of `projectiveSpaceOverSpecIso` in the ring for `A → A_f`,
which does not exist yet), covering `V ∩ V'` by opens basic in both; then glue the morphisms
(`glueOpens`), show the glued map is an isomorphism (iso locally on the target), and glue the
twisting-sheaf isomorphisms compatibly.
