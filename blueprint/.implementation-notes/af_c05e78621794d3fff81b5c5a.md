## Lean (candidate names)

Modules: `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.GradedSheafSections`, `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundle`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleTrivialization`, `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleGluing`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleTwist`.

- `Hartshorne.SheafAlgebraOperations.PresheafAlgebra.IsGraded.isInternal_sections`: on a ringed
  space, for a graded presheaf of algebras `P = ⊕ C r` and a quasi-compact open `U`,
  `Γ(U, aP) = ⊕_r Γ(U, a(C r))`. The projections are `IsGraded.sheafProj` (sheafified presheaf
  projections). Surjectivity: every section is locally the image of a presheaf section, which has
  finitely many components (`exists_unit_eq_map`); compactness bounds the degrees.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.symmetricGrading`, `symmetricGrading_isInternal`,
  `symmetricGradedRing`: the grading `Γ(U, Sym E) = ⊕_d Γ(U, Sym^d E)` over quasi-compact `U`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.symmetricGradedAlgebra`: `Sym(E)` as a
  `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (rings, structure map and coequifibered condition from
  the pieces of `ModuleSheafAlgebra.toQuasiCoherentAlgebra`, i.e. Lemma II.5.3).
- `symmetricGradedAlgebra_degreeZero` (`S₀ = 𝒪_X`, from `symmetricPowerZeroIso` and
  injectivity of `ι₀` via `sheafProj`), `symmetricGradedAlgebra_degreeOneCoherent` (from
  `symmetricPower_isCoherent` and `forall_affine_finite_of_isCoherent`),
  `symmetricGrading_generated` (generation in degree one over every affine `U`: on a basic open
  `D(f)` with a frame, `chartRingHom` is bijective; frame elements times a power of `f` extend to
  `U` (Lemma II.5.3); sections vanishing on `D(f)` are killed by a power of `f`; the basic opens
  with frames generate the unit ideal, `Submodule.mem_of_span_eq_top_of_smul_pow_mem`).
- `symmetricRelativeProjData` (condition (†)), `projectiveBundle`, `projectiveBundleProj`,
  `projectiveBundleTwistingSheaf`.
- Free case over an affine open `V` with a frame `e` of rank `n + 1`:
  `frameGradedHom`, `frameGradedHom_bijective` (graded iso `A[x₀..xₙ] ≅ Γ(V, Sym E)`),
  `projectiveBundleFrameIso` (`π⁻¹(V) ≅ Proj Γ(V, Sym E) ≅ Proj A[x] ≅ ℙⁿ_{Spec A} ≅ ℙⁿ_V`),
  `projectiveBundleFrameIso_hom_projection` (over `V`), `projectiveBundleFrameIsoTwist`
  (`ψ^* 𝒪_{ℙⁿ_V}(k) ≅ 𝒪_{P(E)}(k)|_{π⁻¹V}`, any `k : ℤ`), and the packaged statement
  `nonempty_projectiveBundle_iso_of_restrict_iso_free` (from `E|_V ≅ 𝒪_V^{n+1}`, V affine).
- Free case over an arbitrary open `U` with a frame `e` of rank `n + 1`
  (`ProjectiveBundleGluing`): `exists_projectiveBundleIso_of_frame` (`π⁻¹(U) ≅ ℙⁿ_U` over `U`,
  restricting over every affine `V ⊆ U` to `projectiveBundleFrameIso` of `e|_V` followed by
  `ℙⁿ_V ⊆ ℙⁿ_U`) and `nonempty_projectiveBundle_iso_of_restrict_iso_free_open` (from
  `E|_U ≅ 𝒪_U^{n+1}`). Proof: the pieces `π⁻¹(V) ≅ ℙⁿ_V ⊆ ℙⁿ_U` (private `framePiece`) agree on
  every basic open `D(s) ⊆ V` because, followed by `ℙⁿ_U → ℙⁿ_ℤ`, both are
  `π⁻¹(D(s)) ≅ Proj Γ(D(s), S(E)) ⟶ Proj ℤ[z]`, `z_k ↦ e_k` (private
  `projectiveBundleFrameIso_fst_naturality`, from `colimit.w` of the gluing diagram and
  `symDegreeOne_naturality`; no naturality of `projectiveSpaceOverSpecIso` in the ring is needed).
  Opens basic in both `V` and `V'` cover `V ∩ V'` (`exists_basicOpen_le_affine_inter`), and the
  general gluing lemma `Hartshorne.exists_iso_of_openImmersions_over` (open immersions over a base
  whose images are the preimages of the opens, agreeing on a basis of overlaps, glue to an
  isomorphism; `glueOpens` and `IsZariskiLocalAtTarget`) finishes. The relative-Proj gluing
  criterion `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.isIso_relProjDesc` does not apply directly: it needs the
  pieces over all affine opens of `X`, and relative Proj has no restriction to a non-affine open.
- General helpers (namespace `Hartshorne`): `gradedInvOfBijective`, `projIsoOfBijective`,
  `pullbackProjMapTwistIso` (`(Proj φ)^* 𝒪(k) ≅ 𝒪(k)` when `T₊ ⊆ φ(S₊)T`, from
  `ProjTwistPullback.pullbackTwistIso` and `projPartialMap_eq_map`).

Convention: `P(E) = Proj_X Sym(E)` (Hartshorne's, quotient convention); hypotheses
`E.IsLocallyFree` and `∀ x, E.rankAt x < ℵ₀` as in the graded-symmetric-algebra article.

- Twisting sheaf over an arbitrary open (`ProjectiveBundleTwist`):
  `exists_projectiveBundleIso_of_frame_twist` (frame version) and the packaged
  `nonempty_projectiveBundle_iso_twist_of_restrict_iso_free` (from `E|_U ≅ 𝒪_U^{n+1}`). Route, as in
  the proof of Theorem 7.1(b) (`exists_iso_of_chartData`): on the affine piece `Proj Γ(V, S(E))`
  (private `pieceι`, an open immersion into `π⁻¹(U)`) the map `π⁻¹(U) ≅ ℙⁿ_U ⟶ ℙⁿ_ℤ` is
  `Proj(z_k ↦ e_k)`, so `ψ⁻¹(D₊(z_i))` meets it in `D₊(e_i)` and `ψ^#(z_j/z_i) = e_j/e_i`
  (`Proj.map_preimage_basicOpen`, `Proj.awayToSection_comp_appLE`). The degree-one sections `e_i`
  of the affine pieces are compatible along basic opens (`DataTr.ψ_compat`, packaged as
  `Hartshorne.DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr.ψ_hom_app_top`, and
  `ProjTwistMap.twistRestrictHom_app_degreeOneSection`) and glue (`DirectedModuleGluing.mod_glue`)
  to `s_i` over `π⁻¹(U)`. On `ψ⁻¹(D₊(z_i))`, `ψ^* z_i` and `s_i` are frames with the same
  transition functions `ψ^#(z_j/z_i)`, and `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` finishes.
- General helpers (namespace `Hartshorne`): `IsTrivializingSection.image_of_restrict`,
  `restrict_iso_hom_app_map`, `restrictAppIso_inv_smul`, `restrict_inf_smul`,
  `DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr.ψ_hom_app_top`. Made public in `ProjectiveBundleGluing`:
  `frameIntHom`, `frameIntHom_X`, `projectiveBundleFrameIso_hom_fst`.

Friction: statements whose sections of `𝒪_{P(E)}(1)|_W` along an open immersion are used as
sections of `𝒪_{P(E)}(1)` over the image (a definitional equality) made the kernel time out once
`𝒪_{P(E)}(1)` was concrete, since it unfolds the glued module. The local part is therefore proved
for an arbitrary module `M` on `P(E)` with isomorphisms to the twisting sheaves of the affine pieces
compatible along basic opens, and instantiated at the end; rewriting goes through
`Scheme.Modules.restrictAppIso` to keep terms type-correct at instance transparency.
