# Uniqueness of a dualizing sheaf with trace

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.DualizingSheafUnique`.

- `Hartshorne.topCohomologyDual f n`: the contravariant functor `F ↦ Hⁿ(X, F)^∨` on the full
  subcategory of coherent modules (`ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory IsCoherent`), valued in types.
- `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf.representableBy`: a dualizing sheaf represents it
  (`Functor.RepresentableBy`), through `DualizingSheaf.dualityEquiv`; naturality is
  `DualizingSheaf.dualityEquiv_naturality`. `representableBy_homEquiv_id` says the identity of
  `ω` corresponds to the trace.
- `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf.uniqueIso D D'`: the image in `X.Modules` of Mathlib's
  `Functor.RepresentableBy.uniqueUpToIso`, the uniqueness of representing objects that the book
  cites. `trace_eq_comp_uniqueIso`: `t = t' ∘ Hⁿ(φ)`.
- `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf.existsUnique_iso`: the proposition, as
  `∃! φ : D.sheaf ≅ D'.sheaf, D.trace = D'.trace ∘ₗ (Hⁿ(φ.hom))`. Uniqueness is the injectivity
  of `Hom_X(ω, ω') ⟶ Hⁿ(X, ω)^∨` (the field `bijective_dualityPairingMap` of `D'`).

The book's step that `ψ ∘ φ` and `φ ∘ ψ` are identities is the Yoneda argument inside
`uniqueUpToIso`.
