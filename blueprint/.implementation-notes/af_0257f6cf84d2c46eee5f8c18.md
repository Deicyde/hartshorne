# Composition of very ample sheaves

## Status

Not stated or proved in Lean.

## Expected obstruction

The same gap as Ex. 5.12(a) (very-ample-tensor-product; see its implementation note). The
natural proof composes `X ⟶ ℙʳ_Y` with `ℙʳ_Y ⟶ ℙʳ_{ℙˢ_Z}` and the Segre embedding. Each step is
an immersion in the p.120 sense (open subscheme of a closed subscheme), but the composite comes out
as closed ∘ open ∘ closed. Rewriting it as open-in-closed needs the composite to be quasi-compact
(Stacks 01QV, counterexample shape 01QW).

The supervisor ruled (2026-11-10) that the article keeps the book's generality and stays
unrecorded until the general case is proved. A version with `X ⟶ Z` quasi-compact would be partial
progress, and it would use the same Segre pullback identity as
`Hartshorne.nonempty_relativeSegre_pullback_iso`.
