# The internal Hom of abelian étale sheaves

## Status

Added by a roadmap revision that splits the etale-tate-twist leaf. Per reviewer r75 it depends
only on small-etale-site; the dual `Hom(F, ℤ/ℓ^r)` that the leaf uses, which involves
constant-torsion-etale-sheaf, belongs to the leaf. The Lean below already exists in the
sorry-free module `Hartshorne.Weil.EtaleTateTwist`. Nothing is recorded in the frontmatter.

## Mathlib gap

Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.presheafHom` / `CategoryTheory.sheafHom`
(`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafHom.lean`) give the internal Hom only as a sheaf of types in
`Type (u+1)` for the étale site. The smallness argument and shrinking fill this gap. Mathlib's
`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Monoidal.lean` TODO mentions a closed structure on sheaves, which
is not in the pinned checkout.
