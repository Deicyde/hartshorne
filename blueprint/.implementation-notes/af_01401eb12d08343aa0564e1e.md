# Affine covers compute quasi-coherent cohomology

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.AffineCoverComparison`.

- `Hartshorne.isIso_cechToSheafCohomology_of_isQuasicoherent`: for `X` with `[IsNoetherian X]`
  `[X.IsSeparated]`, a linearly ordered index type `ι : Type u`, `U : ι → X.Opens` with
  `IsOpenCover U` and every `U i` affine, and `F : X.Modules` quasi-coherent, the component at the
  underlying abelian sheaf of `F` of `cechToSheafCohomology U hU p` (Lemma III.4.4) is an
  isomorphism, for every `p`.
- `Hartshorne.cechCohomologyIsoOfIsQuasicoherent`: the same map as an `Iso`.

The linear order on the index set is the library's convention for ordered Čech cochains
(Hartshorne well-orders the index set).

## Route (Hartshorne's printed proof)

Induction on `p`, generalising `F`. `p = 0` is `isIso_cechToSheafCohomology_zero`. For `p + 1`:
`exists_mono_isFlasque_isQuasicoherent` (Cor. III.3.6) gives `F ↪ G`, `R = coker` is
quasi-coherent (`isQuasicoherent_cokernel`). Finite intersections of the `U i` (and of `W ⊓ U i`
for affine `W`) are affine by `IsAffineOpen.iInf` (separatedness). Section-level exactness over
those opens comes from `modules_app_injective`, `modules_sections_exact` and, for surjectivity,
`affine_globalSections_shortExact` applied to the restriction to the affine open
(`AffineCoverComparison.sections_surjective`). This gives short exactness of the ordered Čech
complexes (`cochainsSC_shortExact`) and of the sheafified Čech complexes degreewise
(`sheafifiedCech_shortExact`, epi via local surjectivity on affine neighbourhoods). Flasque `G`:
`orderedCechCohomology_isZero_of_isFlasque` and `sheafCohomology_isZero_of_isFlasque`. The ladder
lemma `isIso_of_cokernel_ladder` concludes.

Compatibility of the comparison maps with connecting morphisms:
`AffineCoverComparison.cechδ_comp_cechToSheafCohomology`, an instance of the general
`ResolutionDelta.Data.δ_comp_comparison` (a degreewise short exact sequence of resolutions maps
to the horseshoe resolution used by `rightDerivedδ`; the middle map is built from an extension
`α` of `φ₁`, its defect `τ`, and a homotopy `H` between `φ₃ ≫ h` and `τ + N` obtained from
`ResolutionToInjectiveComplex.homotopy`, mirroring `RightDerivedLES.Horseshoe.δ_naturality`).

Surjectivity on affine intersections uses Proposition II.5.6 (`affine_globalSections_shortExact`);
Hartshorne cites III.3.5 at that step. The affine-scheme form of III.3.5 is
`Hartshorne.sheafCohomology_isZero_of_isQuasicoherent_of_isAffine` (module
`Hartshorne.Cohomology.AffineQuasicoherentAcyclic`).
