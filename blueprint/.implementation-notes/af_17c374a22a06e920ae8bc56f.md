# Tensor products of very ample sheaves

## Added hypothesis: `X` quasi-compact over `Y` (review question)

`IsVeryAmpleRelative` now uses Hartshorne's p.120 immersions: an open immersion followed by a
closed immersion. The Segre route naturally gives a different shape.

- `(i, j) : X ⟶ ℙʳ_Y ×_Y ℙˢ_Y` factors through the diagonal of `X`, which is a closed immersion
  into an open subscheme.
- Rewriting it as an open subscheme of a closed subscheme goes through the scheme-theoretic image
  (`Scheme.Hom.toImage`, `Scheme.Hom.imageι`).
- That rewriting needs the immersion to be quasi-compact. Here it is quasi-compact exactly because
  `p` is: the immersion followed by the separated projection `ℙ^N_Y ⟶ Y` is `p`.

The hypothesis holds whenever `Y` is Noetherian, since then `X` is an open subscheme of a
Noetherian scheme. Hartshorne gives no qualification. Without quasi-compactness, the ideal of the
diagonal need not extend to a quasi-coherent ideal on `ℙ^N_Y`, and I have not found an argument.
The reviewer should decide between two options:
- keep the hypothesis, and disclose it in the article;
- record a Roadmap question on whether the exercise holds in the non-quasi-compact case under
  Hartshorne's definition.
