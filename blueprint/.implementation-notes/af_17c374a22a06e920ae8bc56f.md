# Tensor products of very ample sheaves

## Status

Partial result in Lean, not recorded. Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.VeryAmpleTensorProduct`.

```lean
theorem Hartshorne.isVeryAmpleRelative_moduleSheafTensor {X Y : Scheme.{u}} {p : X ⟶ Y}
    [QuasiCompact p] {L M : X.Modules} (hL : IsVeryAmpleRelative p L)
    (hM : IsVeryAmpleRelative p M) : IsVeryAmpleRelative p (moduleSheafTensor L M)
```

The article's statement has no quasi-compactness hypothesis. The Lean proves it only when `X` is
quasi-compact over `Y`. That holds automatically when `Y` is locally Noetherian.

## Why the hypothesis appears

`IsVeryAmpleRelative` uses Hartshorne's p.120 immersions: an open immersion followed by a closed
immersion. The Segre route naturally gives a different shape.

- `(i, j) : X ⟶ ℙʳ_Y ×_Y ℙˢ_Y` factors through the diagonal of `X`. It is a closed immersion into
  an open subscheme.
- An immersion of that shape need not be an open immersion into a closed subscheme (Stacks).
- With `p` quasi-compact, the immersion `σ_Y ∘ (i, j)` is quasi-compact: followed by the separated
  projection `ℙ^N_Y ⟶ Y`, it is `p`. A quasi-compact immersion factors through its
  scheme-theoretic image (`Scheme.Hom.toImage`, `Scheme.Hom.imageι`).

Whether the hypothesis can be removed is with the supervisor.

A possible route to the statement for reduced `X`, with no quasi-compactness: replace the
scheme-theoretic image by the reduced closure of the image. A locally closed subset is open in its
closure, so a reduced `X` is open in the reduced closed subscheme on that closure.

## Ingredients

- `Hartshorne.SegreTwist.nonempty_segre_pullback_iso`: over the integers,
  `σ^*𝒪(1) ≅ p₁^*𝒪(1) ⊗ p₂^*𝒪(1)`.
  - On the chart `D₊(xᵢ) ×_ℤ D₊(yⱼ)`, both `σ^* z_{ij}` and `p₁^* xᵢ ⊗ p₂^* yⱼ` are frames.
  - Both families have transition functions `(xₖ/xᵢ)(yₗ/yⱼ)`, from
    `segreMorphismInt_appLE_chartRatio` (the coordinate statement of `relativeSegre_spec`).
  - The iso is then `Hartshorne.Ringed.nonempty_iso_of_frames`.
- `Hartshorne.nonempty_relativeSegre_pullback_iso`: the same over `Y`, obtained by base change
  using `relativeSegre_fst` and `pullbackTensorIso`.
- `Hartshorne.ProjTwistingSheaf.restrict_degreeOneSection_eq_smul` and
  `isLocalFrame_degreeOneSection`: the transition functions and frames of `𝒪(1)` on `Proj`.
