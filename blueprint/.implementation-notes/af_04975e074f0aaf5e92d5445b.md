  `k`-space `GlobalSectionsOver`.
- `Hartshorne.plurigenus_eq_of_birational`: `X`, `X'` integral, regular, proper over an
  algebraically closed `k`, with a partial isomorphism `X ⤏ X'` over `k`; then
  `plurigenus pX m = plurigenus pX' m` for all `m`.
- `Hartshorne.rank_globalSections_le_of_partialIso`: one direction, as a rank inequality.
