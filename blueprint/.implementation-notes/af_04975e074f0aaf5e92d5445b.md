# Birational invariance of plurigenera

## Status

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23d), not closable in budget. No Lean was
written, and nothing is recorded. The proof runs the pullback injection of
canonical-pullback-injective for `ω^{⊗m}`. That injection is not formalized, and its
infrastructure is missing (see that article's implementation note).

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.GlobalSectionsOver f M` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.GeometricGenus`):
  `Γ(X, M)` as a `k`-vector space. The plurigenus would be
  `Module.finrank k (GlobalSectionsOver f (moduleSheafTensorPower m (canonicalSheaf f)))`,
  with `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorPower` from `Hartshorne.Scheme.SheafTensorOperations`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.RationalMap.codim_compl_domain_birational`: the domain `V` of a
  birational map from a nonsingular variety to a proper variety has complement of codimension at
  least two.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.bijective_presheaf_map_top_of_two_le_codim`: on a Noetherian
  normal integral scheme, restriction `Γ(X, E) ⟶ Γ(U, E)` of a locally free `E` is bijective when
  `codim(X \ U) ≥ 2`.
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.isInvertible_moduleSheafTensor` (tensor of invertible sheaves is invertible).

## Lean gaps

1. The pullback map `Γ(X', ω_{X'}^{⊗m}) ⟶ Γ(V, ω_V^{⊗m})` for a morphism `V ⟶ X'` representing
   the birational map. This needs step 1 of canonical-pullback-injective (the map on `⋀^n Ω`),
   then functoriality of `moduleSheafTensorPower` in morphisms and under pushforward. Neither is
   in the project.
2. `moduleSheafTensorPower m L` is invertible (locally free of rank one) for invertible `L`. This
   needs an identification `T^{m+1} L ≅ T^m L ⊗ L`, or a local-frame argument like
   `Hartshorne.Ringed.IsLocallyFreeOfRank.moduleSheafExteriorPower`. Not found.
3. Injectivity on a dense open, as in steps 2 and 3 of canonical-pullback-injective.

With these, `P_m(X') ≤ P_m(X)`: compose with the inverse of the bijective restriction
`Γ(X, ω_X^{⊗m}) ≅ Γ(V, ω_V^{⊗m})`, which is `bijective_presheaf_map_top_of_two_le_codim` plus
gap 2. Symmetry gives equality. The article asks to run both injections on chosen dense opens
rather than deduce the result from `m = 1`.
