# A very ample sheaf descends from a curve normalization

## Statement revision (faithfulness item 7, III Ex. 5.8(b))

The article states the book's form: an effective divisor $D = \sum P_i$ on
$\widetilde X$ with $\mathcal O(D) \cong L$ and each $P_i$ over a nonsingular
point of $X$, and hence $L_0$ on $X$ with $\nu^* L_0 \cong L$ (no power). A
recording must cover both conclusions; nothing should target $L^m$.

## Status: first conclusion proved, descent to $X$ open

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.NormalizationDivisorDescent`. Everything below
compiles with axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, and the
`unusedArguments` lint is clean.

### The divisor (first conclusion): done

`AlgebraicGeometry.exists_effective_divisor_of_isVeryAmpleRelative`:

```lean
theorem exists_effective_divisor_of_isVeryAmpleRelative {k : Type u} [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k]
    {X : Scheme.{u}} [IsIntegral X] (p : X ⟶ Spec (.of k)) [IsProper p]
    (hdim : topologicalKrullDim X = 1) {L : (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalization X).Modules}
    (hL : Hartshorne.IsVeryAmpleRelative (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalizationMap X ≫ p) L) :
    ∃ D : (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalization X).CartierDivisor, D.IsEffective ∧
      Nonempty (D.sheaf ≅ L) ∧
      ∀ x, ¬ X.IsRegularAt (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalizationMap X x) →
        ∃ V : (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalization X).Opens, x ∈ V ∧ D.restrict V = 0
```

"Each $P_i$ maps to a nonsingular point" is expressed as: $D$ is trivial near
every point of $\widetilde X$ over a singular point of $X$.

Ingredients, all in the module:

- `exists_isIso_absoluteNormalizationMap_restrict`: a nonempty open `U` of
  regular points of `X` over which `ν` is an isomorphism. It is built from
  `exists_nonempty_dense_isRegularAt`, `isNormal_of_forall_isRegularAt` and
  `isIso_absoluteNormalizationMap_restrict_of_isNormal`.
- `finite_compl_of_topologicalKrullDim_eq_one` and
  `finite_preimage_absoluteNormalizationMap`: `Uᶜ` is finite, and so is
  `ν⁻¹(Uᶜ)`. The topological input is
  `finite_of_isClosed_of_ne_univ_of_topologicalKrullDim_eq_one`, the T₀
  version.
- `isGloballyGenerated_of_isVeryAmpleRelative`: `O(1)` is generated by its
  coordinate sections, and pullback preserves generation
  (`isGloballyGenerated_pullback`, through `SheafOfModules.pullbackObjFreeIso`
  and the instance `opensMap_final`).
- `exists_mem_nonvanishingLocus_of_isGloballyGenerated`,
  `isTrivializingSection_trivSection` and
  `isUnit_trivGerm_of_mem_nonvanishingLocus` connect nonvanishing loci with
  coordinates in a trivialization.
- `exists_isUnit_trivGerm`: since `k` is infinite and a finite union of proper
  subspaces is proper, one section generates at all points of the finite set.
- `exists_finite_set_divisor_of_isUnit_trivGerm`: the divisor of zeros

### Remaining goal: descent

```lean
∃ (L₀ : X.Modules), L₀.IsInvertible ∧
  Nonempty ((Scheme.Modules.pullback (Hartshorne.absoluteNormalizationMap X)).obj L₀ ≅ L)
```

The proof of `exists_effective_divisor_of_isVeryAmpleRelative` gives more than
its statement. There is an open `U ⊆ X` over which `ν` is an isomorphism, and
an effective `D` with `𝒪(D) ≅ L` that is trivial near every point of
`ν⁻¹(Uᶜ)`. The first step is to restate the theorem so that it exports these
facts.

Route, using `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisorPullback`:

1. `isIso_cartierPullbackMap` gives `ν^* 𝒪_X(D₀) ≅ 𝒪_{X̃}(D)` whenever
   `CartierPullbackData ν hν D₀ D` holds. Also needed:
   - `hν`: `ν` sends the generic point to the generic point;
   - `ν^♯ : K(X) ≅ K(X̃)`, i.e. `genericFunctionFieldMap` is bijective because
     `ν` is birational.
2. Construct `D₀` with `CartierLocalEquations` indexed by the points of `X`,
   writing `Z := Supp D` (closed, contained in `ν⁻¹U`):
   - For `y ∉ ν(Z)`: take `V_y := X ∖ ν(Z)` (open because `ν` is finite,
     hence closed) and local equation `1`.
   - For `y ∈ ν(Z)`: take the unique preimage `x`, a neighbourhood
     `W ⊆ ν⁻¹U` with `D|_W = div(h)`, `V_y := ν(W)`, and local equation
     `g = (ν^♯)⁻¹ h`.
3. Transition units. A ratio of local equations on `V_i ∩ V_j ⊆ U` is a
   regular unit, because its pullback is a regular unit on `ν⁻¹(V_i ∩ V_j)`
   (`exists_eq_mul_of_restrict_eq` in both directions), and over `U` the map
   `ν.app` is an isomorphism compatible with `genericFunctionFieldMap_germ`.
   Where `D` is trivial, `h` is locally a regular unit, and local units glue
   (`exists_toK_eq_of_locally`).
4. `CartierPullbackData ν hν D₀ D` then holds by cases on `ν x ∈ ν(Z)`.
   Set `L₀ := D₀.sheaf`; it is invertible by `isInvertible_sheaf`.

Recording also needs the `r9-44` reachability check and a `lean:` list naming
the final theorem and the divisor theorem.
