# The bad-hyperplane incidence correspondence

## Status

Partial. The pointwise half of the statement is formalized; the incidence half
(closedness, irreducibility and dimension of the incidence set) is not. No `lean:`
frontmatter is recorded.

## Setting used by the Lean

`X ⊆ ℙⁿ_k` is a closed immersion `φ : X ⟶ projectiveSpaceOverScheme n (Spec k)`, with
`X` integral, `X.IsRegular`, `topologicalKrullDim X = r`, `k` algebraically closed. A
hyperplane `H_a = {∑ aᵢ xᵢ = 0}` is indexed by its coefficient vector `a ∈ kⁿ⁺¹`
(nonzero vectors up to scalars are the points of `|H|`), matching the linear-system
framework (`linearSection`, `linearSystemSection`, `invertibleZeroIdeal`). The
hyperplane section `H_a ∩ X` is the scheme-theoretic preimage
`(invertibleZeroIdeal _ (linearSection k n a)).comap φ`.

## Formalized (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.BertiniBadHyperplaneIncidence`)

* `Hartshorne.BertiniIncidence.hyperplaneSectionIdeal`, `IsBadHyperplaneAt`: `H_a ∩ X`
  and "H_a contains X, or `x ∈ H_a ∩ X` and `H_a ∩ X` is not regular at `x`".
* `badHyperplaneSubspace φ x`: the `k`-subspace of `a` whose pulled-back linear form lies
  in `𝔪_x² 𝒪_X(1)_x`.
* `mem_badHyperplaneSubspace_iff`: `a ∈ badHyperplaneSubspace φ x ↔ IsBadHyperplaneAt φ a x`
  (for every `a`; `a = 0` gives the zero form, which "contains X").
* `finrank_badHyperplaneSubspace`: at a closed point the subspace has dimension `n - r`, so
  `B_x = ℙ(badHyperplaneSubspace φ x)` has dimension `n - r - 1`.

Supporting results in the same module: `finrank_quotient_maximalIdeal_sq`
(`dim_k R/𝔪² = dim 𝔪/𝔪² + 1` when `k → R/𝔪` is bijective), `finrank_badSubspace` (general
version for a closed immersion defined by sections `sᵢ` of `L`, using
`sectionsSeparateTangentVectors_of_isClosedImmersion` from Proposition II.7.3),
`ker_stalkMap_eq_map_ker_app` (kernel of the stalk map of a closed immersion is generated by the
germs of the kernel over an affine open), `germ_mem_maximalIdeal_sq_smul_top_iff` (the zero
scheme `Z(t)` of a section of an invertible sheaf on an integral scheme is singular at a regular
point `x` iff `t_x ∈ 𝔪_x² L_x`, via the regular-hypersurface criterion).

Routing note: the book derives "not regular at `x` iff the local equation is in `𝔪_x²`" from the
regular-hypersurface criterion; the Lean uses
`isRegularLocalRing_quotient_span_singleton_iff_notMem_sq` exactly there. The conormal criterion
(Theorem 8.17) is not used by the pointwise part. Surjectivity of `V → 𝒪_{X,x}/𝔪_x²` comes from
Proposition II.7.3 (⇒), recorded on the separates-points-and-tangent-vectors article, which the
article's Depends on does not yet reach (dag-reach gap to be fixed by a link-only edge when the
article is recorded).

## Remaining goal

Write `P := projectiveSpaceOverScheme n X` (`= X ×_k (ℙⁿ)^∨`, coordinates `X_j =
projectiveSpaceCoordinate n X j`, projection `π`). For a closed point `z` of `P` say `a ≠ 0`
represents `z` when every section `a_j • X_l - a_l • X_j` of `𝒪_P(1)` vanishes at `z`
(constants via `π ≫ q`). Remaining statement, for `r < n`:

  `∃ Z : Set P, IsClosed Z ∧ IsIrreducible Z ∧ topologicalKrullDim Z = (n - 1 : ℕ) ∧
    ∀ z, IsClosed {z} → ∀ a ≠ 0, a represents z → (z ∈ Z ↔ a ∈ badHyperplaneSubspace φ (π z))`

(with `Z := closure` of that closed-point set), plus the transport along
`projectiveSpaceOverSchemeBaseChangeIso` if the statement is to be phrased on
`pullback q (ℙⁿ_k ⟶ Spec k)`.

## Route for the remaining goal

1. Local frame of the jet evaluation (the "kernel bundle"). On `A = Γ(X, X_{sᵢ})`, with
   `f_j = s_j/s_i` (`sectionRatio`; `A` is generated by the `f_j`,
   `isClosedImmersion_iff_isAffineOpen_and_surjective_chartSectionPolynomialMap`), let
   `P¹ = (A ⊗_k A)/J²`, `J = ker(mult)`, a finite left `A`-module. At a closed point `x`
   with `κ(x) = k`, `P¹/𝔪_x P¹ ≅ A/𝔪_x²` via `a ⊗ b ↦ a(x) b` (injectivity: `1 ⊗ pq ≡
   (1⊗p - p⊗1)(1⊗q - q⊗1)` modulo `𝔪_x ⊗ A`). Pick `a_0, …, a_r` whose forms
   `g_ℓ = ∑ (a_ℓ)_j f_j` give a basis of `A/𝔪_{x₀}²` (possible by `finrank_badSubspace`'s
   surjectivity). Nakayama (`Submodule.exists_sub_one_mem_and_smul_eq_zero_of_fg_of_le_smul`)
   gives `h ∉ 𝔪_{x₀}` and `μ_{jℓ} ∈ A` with `h ⊗ f_j - ∑ μ_{jℓ} ⊗ g_ℓ ∈ J²`, hence
   `h(x) f_j ≡ ∑ μ_{jℓ}(x) g_ℓ (mod 𝔪_x²)` at every closed `x ∈ D(h)`. With the dimension
   count, `g_ℓ` is a basis of `A/𝔪_x²` there, so `a ∈ K_x ⟺ ∑_j a_j μ_{jℓ}(x) = 0` for all
   `ℓ`, and `μᵀ (a_0 | … | a_r) = h·I`, so some `(r+1)`-minor of `μᵀ` is a unit after shrinking.
2. Charts of `P` over an affine `V ⊆ D(h)`: `π⁻¹V ∩ D₊(X_β)` is affine
   (`isAffineOpen_preimage_inf_projectiveSpaceChart`) with ring `Γ(V)[w_j : j ≠ β]`; the
   incidence there is the zero set of `τ_ℓ = ∑_j π^#(μ_{jℓ}) w_{βj}`. For `β` outside the
   unit minor's columns, solving the `J`-variables gives `Γ(V)[m variables]`, `m = n - r - 1`:
   irreducible, of dimension `r + m = n - 1`
   (`MvPolynomial.ringKrullDim_of_isNoetherianRing`); these charts cover the incidence over `V`.
3. Glue: `Z ∩ W = V_W(τ)` on each chart `W` (both closed in `W`, equal closures of their closed
   points since `P` is Jacobson); charts over different `V` meet because `X` is irreducible
   and every fibre `ℙ(K_x)` is nonempty (`n - r ≥ 1`); a space covered by pairwise-meeting
   irreducible opens of dimension `n - 1` is irreducible of dimension `n - 1`.
