# Adjunction via the determinant of the normal bundle

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.AdjunctionDeterminantNormalBundle`.

- `Hartshorne.adjunction_determinant_normalSheaf`: for `p : X ⟶ Spec k` (`k` algebraically
  closed, `X` irreducible, regular, of finite type) and a closed immersion `i : Y ⟶ X` with `Y`
  irreducible and regular and `codim (Set.range i.base) = r`,
  `Nonempty (canonicalSheaf (i ≫ p) ≅ moduleSheafTensor (i^* canonicalSheaf p) (⋀^r normalSheaf i))`.
  The isomorphism is explicit (a composite of named isomorphisms); it is stated with `Nonempty`
  because the recorded determinant isomorphism of Exercise II.5.16(d)
  (`ExteriorPowerExactFiltration.exteriorPower_exact_det`) is itself only `Nonempty`.
- `Hartshorne.ExteriorDual.exteriorPowerDualMap`, `exteriorPowerDualMap_isIso`,
  `exteriorPowerDualIso`: on any ringed space, the determinant pairing
  `(ψ₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ ψ_r, x₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ x_r) ↦ det (ψ_j(x_i))` gives `⋀^r F^∨ ⟶ (⋀^r F)^∨`, an isomorphism
  for `F` locally free of rank `r` ("the top exterior power commutes with duals" in the proof).
  Built from Mathlib's `exteriorPower.pairingDual` on sections, extended to sheafifications with
  `SheafAlgebraOperations.extendBilinear`, curried with `Ringed.bilinearCurry`; bijectivity is
  checked on frames (`e₁^∨ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r^∨ ↦ (e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r)^∨`).

## Route

As in the book: the conormal sequence of Theorem 8.17
(`NonsingularConormal.nonsingular_closedImmersion_conormal`, transported along
`immersionConormalSheafIso`), the determinant isomorphism
`⋀^n (i^* Ω_X) ≅ ⋀^r N^* ⊗ ω_Y`, then `i^*(⋀^n Ω_X) ≅ ⋀^n (i^* Ω_X)`
(`pullbackExteriorPowerIso`, recorded on the canonical-sheaf-of-product article), the
comparison above, and `L ⊗ L^∨ ≅ 𝒪` for the invertible `L = ⋀^r N^*`
(`Ringed.moduleSheafDualEvalIso`, recorded on the Picard-group article), plus the
commutativity, associativity and unit isomorphisms of the tensor product.

The dimensions `n = dim X`, `m = dim Y` are obtained inside the proof; the relation
`n = m + r` is not needed.
