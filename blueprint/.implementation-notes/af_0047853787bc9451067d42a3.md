# Ampleness and tensor powers

## Lean layout

- Main statement: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isAmple_tfae` in
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.AmplePowerEquivalence`, a `List.TFAE` of the three
  conditions, with invertibility of `L` as a hypothesis (used in the
  implication from "some power is ample").
- Tensor-power arithmetic at ringed-space level in
  `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSheafTensorPowerIso`:
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerZeroIso` (`T^0 F ≅ 𝒪_X`),
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso` (`T^{n+1} F ≅ T^n F ⊗ F`),
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerAddIso`, `moduleSheafTensorPowerMulIso`,
  `Ringed.isInvertible_moduleSheafTensorPower`. The successor isomorphism uses
  the universal property of the tensor power in one direction and, in the
  other, curries the last variable of an `(n+1)`-multilinear map into
  `ℋom(F, H)` (`snocHomMultilinear`, `tensorPowerSnocDesc`), mirroring the
  associativity construction of the Picard-group module.
- Coherence of `F ⊗ L` (coherent `F`, invertible `L`, locally Noetherian `X`):
  `Hartshorne.isCoherent_moduleSheafTensor_of_isInvertible`, proved on the
  affine opens carrying a one-element frame of `L`, where `s ↦ s ⊗ e` is an
  isomorphism of the restrictions (`AmplePower.twistRestrictHom`), by
  `IsLocalFrame.tensorFrameMap_bijective`.
- `Hartshorne.isGloballyGenerated_of_iso`: global generation is invariant under
  isomorphism (via the epimorphism characterization).

## Notes

- The scheme-level `Hartshorne.isInvertible_moduleSheafTensorPower` (from the
  ample-sections article) duplicates `Ringed.isInvertible_moduleSheafTensorPower`
  in content; the two were developed in parallel and could be merged.
- The graded multiplication of the tensor-algebra sheaf was not used: its
  values on elementary tensors are not exposed.
- Restriction is only known to commute with tensor products when both factors
  are invertible, hence the direct frame-based comparison for coherence.
