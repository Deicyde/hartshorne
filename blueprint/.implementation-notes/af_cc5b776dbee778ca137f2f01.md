# Gluing sheaves of modules along open immersions

## Status

No Lean yet. The article was split off the relative twisting sheaf
(`af_de36af0b3bcf4daf571b0c25`) by a roadmap revision.

## Library search

- Pinned Mathlib: `CategoryTheory.Pseudofunctor.IsStack`
  (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/IsStack.lean`) exists, but no stack instance
  for `SheafOfModules` is proved. `SheafOfModules.QuasicoherentData.bind`
  (`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/Quasicoherent.lean`) glues quasi-coherent
  data of one given module, not modules. No gluing of module objects was found.
- Project: `Hartshorne.SheafGluing.GluingData` and
  `Hartshorne.SheafGluing.GluingData.exists_unique_gluing`
  (`Hartshorne.Scheme.GluingSheaves`) glue sheaves valued in one fixed category `C` on
  an open cover of a topological space, in exactly the open-cover form of the article
  (`U : ι → Opens X`, `φ i j` on `U i ⊓ U j`, `φ_self`, `cocycle`). Modules over a sheaf
  of rings are not sheaves valued in one fixed category, so this does not apply
  directly; the module version should reuse its glued sheaf of abelian groups (or its
  construction) and add the module structure.
- `autoform search` found no other article for this result.

## Formalization guidance

- State it for `SheafOfModules` over a ringed space (or `X.Modules` for a scheme
  `X`, if the ringed-space form is too costly; the Picard consumer
  `af_6344590cb96ba27cc910e6bc` is stated for ringed spaces), in the open-cover form
  of the article, mirroring `GluingData`.
- Alternative route (from the relative twisting sheaf's note):
  `M = lim (ι_i)_* M_i` in `Y.Modules` (limits exist and are sectionwise), then show
  `ι_i^* M ⟶ M_i` is an isomorphism on sections using the sheaf property of each `M_j`.
- Consumer shape: the relative twisting sheaf's descent data
  (`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.twistTransition`, `twistTransitionHom_id`,
  `twistTransitionHom_comp` in `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjTwist`) are indexed by
  arrows of `X.AffineZariskiSite` (basic-open inclusions), a thin, locally directed
  index category, not by pairwise intersections. To apply the open-cover form, that
  leaf must first build overlap isomorphisms on `π⁻¹(U ∩ V)` by gluing the transition
  isomorphisms over basic opens lying in both `U` and `V` (morphisms of modules glue),
  and check the cocycle condition locally. A variant for a thin, locally directed
  index category (`F : I ⥤ Sch`, open immersions `ι_i`, `θ_f : (F f)^* M_j ≅ M_i` with
  identity and cocycle conditions) may be proved as a helper in the same module; it is
  not the article's statement.

## Scope estimate and encoding (recording pass, 2026-11-09)

Not attempted in a recording pass: there is no candidate Lean, and the sheaf-level analogue
(`Hartshorne.Scheme.GluingSheaves`) is about 1100 lines. Points for the Formalize pass:

- Ringed-space encoding: the project restricts an `𝒪_X`-module to an open `U` as
  `F.over U` (`SheafOfModules.overFunctor`, see `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.OpenRestriction`), a
  module on the site `Over U`. Restricting a module on `Over (U i)` to `U i ⊓ U j` with
  `SheafOfModules.over` lands on `Over (Over.mk (U i ⊓ U j ≤ U i))`, not on `Over (U i ⊓ U j)`;
  comparing the restrictions of `F i` and `F j` needs a transport along
  `Over.iteratedSliceEquiv` (or a restriction functor `Over V ⥤ Over U` for `V ≤ U` built
  directly). That transport, with its identity and composition coherences, is the first
  missing piece.
- `GluingData` glues sheaves on the subspaces `(Opens.toTopCat X).obj (U i)` with values in a
  fixed category, so it does not give the module structure directly. Its construction (limit
  over `Pairwise ι` of pushforwards; `ψ i` bijective sectionwise by the cocycle condition alone;
  uniqueness from the pairwise-intersection sheaf condition of the second solution) is the
  template.
- Scheme corollary: `Scheme.Modules` already has `restrictFunctor`, `restrictFunctorComp`,
  `restrictFunctorCongr`, `restrictFunctorId`, `restrictAdjunction` and limits, and
  `(M.restrict f).obj W = M(f ''ᵁ W)` holds definitionally. The relative twisting sheaf
  (`af_de36af0b3bcf4daf571b0c25`, blocked only by this article) uses those.
