`R : Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) RingCat` (open-restriction-injective) need no
change. Lemma 6.6 (finite-locally-free-tensor-injective) is proved only for schemes
(`Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensor_injective`, hypotheses `Scheme.Modules.IsLocallyFree` and
