## Proof structure

Everything about `P(E)` is stated for a module `M` on `P(E)` with identifications
`α V : M|_{Proj Γ(V, S(E))} ≅ 𝒪(1)` compatible with transitions (`TwistCompatible`), and the
quotient `tautQuotientOf M α hα : π^* E ⟶ M`; the concrete results instantiate it.

Elaborator friction: the implicit `S` in `TwistCompatible` is elaborated as
`(symmetricRelativeProjData hE hfin).algebra`; passing `twistCompatible_relTwistingSheaf _ 1`
directly, or comparing `relTwistingSheaf ((1 : ℕ) : ℤ)` with `projectiveBundleTwistingSheaf 1` by
unfolding, hits deterministic timeouts. `twistCompatible_one` (stated with that `S`) and defining
`projectiveBundleTautologicalQuotient` through `tautQuotientOf` make the bridge
`projectiveBundleInvertibleQuotientOfHom_equiv_iff` an `Iff.rfl`.

1. Generic module lemmas (namespace `Hartshorne`): `pullback_hom_ext_pullbackSection`,
   `hom_ext_of_isLocalFrame_top`, `isGeneratedBySections_of_epi_of_isLocalFrame_top`,
   `isEmpty_of_isInvertible_of_isGeneratedBySections`, `eq_zero_of_pullback_map_eq_zero`,
   `exists_iso_comp_eq_of_pullback` (locality of equivalence of quotients, via
   `Abelian.epiDesc`), `pullbackObjIsoOfComp` with its composition lemma.
2. Degree one: `symmetricPowerOneIso_inv_app`, `symmetricPowerGradedSectionsMap_one_inv`,
   `tautQuotientOf_piece`.
3. Frame chart `exists_frameChart_iso`: for a frame `e` of `E` over `U` and `ψ : π⁻¹U ≅ ℙⁿ_U` from
   `exists_projectiveBundleIso_of_frame`, `ψ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ ι^* M` carrying `ψ^* x_i` to
   `ι^* τ(π^* e_i)`. Uses the public `pieceSection`, `global_trivializing`, `global_transition` of
   `ProjectiveBundleTwist.lean` (made public in this pass, statements unchanged) and
   `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames`.
4. `RepresentsQuotient φ hφ q` (some `φ^* M ≅ L` intertwines the quotients),
   `RepresentsQuotient.pullback`, `RepresentsQuotient.of_cover`.
5. Local (frame open, `⊤ ≤ g ⁻¹ᵁ U`): `exists_iso_pullback_chart`, `eq_of_represents_local`,
   `exists_represents_local`, from Theorem 7.1(b)
   (`existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`).
6. Global: `eq_of_represents` (`hom_ext_of_forall`), `exists_represents` (`glueMorphisms` over the
   cover `g⁻¹U`, compatibility from `eq_of_represents`); opens with the empty frame are handled by
   `isEmpty_of_isLocalFrame_zero`.

The isomorphism of targets is not glued through the internal hom, and Exercise 7.1 is not used:
locally equivalent epimorphisms are equivalent because an epimorphism of modules is the cokernel
of its kernel.
