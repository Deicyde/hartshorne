# The tautological quotient and functor of points

## Status: partial

Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleTautologicalQuotient`.

Proved (sorry-free):

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.projectiveBundleTautologicalQuotient`: the natural map
  `π^* E ⟶ 𝒪_{P(E)}(1)`, defined as `π^*(E ≅ S^1(E))` followed by the adjoint of the degree-one
  natural map `S^1(E) ⟶ π_* 𝒪(1)` (the map `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom 1` of
  Proposition 7.11(a); no rank hypothesis is needed for the map).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.epi_projectiveBundleTautologicalQuotient`: it is an
  epimorphism (surjective).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.InvertibleQuotient` with its setoid
  (`InvertibleQuotient.Equiv`: an isomorphism of the invertible sheaves intertwining the quotient
  maps).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.projectiveBundlePullbackQuotient`,
  `projectiveBundleInvertibleQuotientOfHom`: for `φ : Y ⟶ P(E)` with `φ ≫ π = g`, the invertible
  quotient `g^* E ≅ φ^* π^* E ⟶ φ^* 𝒪(1)` (the direction "morphism ↦ quotient" of
  Proposition 7.12; "the represented quotient is the pullback of `𝒪(1)`" holds by definition).

The generic part, in `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`, is stated for an arbitrary
quasi-coherent graded algebra `S`, a module `M` on `Proj_X S` with identifications
`α U : M|_{Proj S(U)} ≅ 𝒪_{Proj S(U)}(1)` (`TwistCompatible`) and a degree-one sections map
`τ : S.GradedSectionsMap 1 F`: `pullbackTwistQuotient`, `pullbackTwistQuotient_piece` (on the piece
`Proj S(U)`, `π^* s ↦ τ(s)/1`), `epi_pullbackTwistQuotient` (surjective when `S` is generated in
degree one and each `τ.toFun U` is surjective). Kernel friction: stating these lemmas directly for
`P(E)` made the kernel unfold the glued `𝒪(1)` (single declarations took 40–120 s of kernel time
and one hit a deterministic timeout); the generic statements avoid this, as in
`ProjectiveBundleTwist.lean`.

## Remaining goals

1. Inverse direction (Proposition 7.12, quotient ↦ morphism): for `g : Y ⟶ X` and an invertible
   quotient `q : g^* E ⟶ L`, a morphism `φ : Y ⟶ P(E)` with `φ ≫ π = g` and an isomorphism
   `e : φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L` with `projectiveBundlePullbackQuotient hE hfin φ hφ ≫ e.hom = q`.
2. Uniqueness: two such `φ` coincide.
3. The bijection, as an `Equiv` between `{φ : Y ⟶ P(E) // φ ≫ π = g}` and
   `Quotient (InvertibleQuotient.setoid ((pullback g).obj E))`, forward map
   `projectiveBundleInvertibleQuotientOfHom`; the case `Y = X`, `g = 𝟙` gives Exercise 7.8.

## Route for the remaining goals

Work locally on `X` over opens `U` on which `E` has a frame `e_0, …, e_n` (rank-zero pieces: there
`g^* E = 0` on `g⁻¹ U`, so an invertible quotient forces `g⁻¹ U = ∅`).

- Frame chart: `exists_projectiveBundleIso_of_frame` gives `ψ : π⁻¹ U ≅ ℙⁿ_U` over `U`. Needed: the
  sections `s_i = τ(π^* e_i)|` of `𝒪(1)` over `π⁻¹ U` are trivializing exactly on
  `chartPreimage ψ.hom i` with transition functions `ψ^#(z_j/z_i)`. On each affine piece
  `Proj Γ(V, S(E))`, `V ⊆ U`, `s_i` restricts to the degree-one section `e_i` (use
  `pullbackTwistQuotient_piece`, and `symmetricPowerOneIso_inv` ↔ `symDegreeOne` — the identity
  `ι_1(S^1 ≅ E)^{-1}(s) = symDegreeOne s` follows from the triangle identity of the sheafification
  adjunction and `symmetricPowerOneEquiv_tprod`), and the chart/transition facts are the private
  lemmas `pieceι_preimage_chart`, `pieceι_appLE_ratio`, `global_trivializing`,
  `global_transition` of `ProjectiveBundleTwist.lean` (currently private: either make them public
  there, which edits the projective-bundle article's Lean, or reprove them). With these,
  `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` gives `β : ψ^* 𝒪_{ℙⁿ}(1) ≅ 𝒪(1)|` carrying `ψ^* x_i` to `s_i`.
- Local existence/uniqueness: transport through `β` and apply
  `existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections` (Theorem 7.1(b)) on `g⁻¹ U` to the sections
  `q(g^* e_i)`, which generate `L` because `q` is surjective and the `g^* e_i` form a frame
  (`Ringed.IsLocalFrame.pullback`). Section bookkeeping through `pullbackComp`/`pullbackCongr`/
  `restrictFunctorIsoPullback` uses `pullbackComp_hom_unit`, `pullbackComp_inv_unit`,
  `pullbackCongr_inv_unit`, `restrictFunctorIsoPullback_inv_unit`.
- Global: glue the local morphisms over the cover `{g⁻¹ U}` (agreement on overlaps by local
  uniqueness), and glue the compatible isomorphisms `φ^* 𝒪(1)|_{g⁻¹ U} ≅ L|_{g⁻¹ U}` with the
  internal-hom sheaf (they agree on overlaps because the quotient maps are epimorphisms); the glued
  map is an isomorphism by Exercise 7.1 (`BlowupUniversalProperty.lean`) since it is a surjection
  of invertible sheaves.
- Keep every statement about `P(E)` generic in the module `M` with the piece identifications, or
  the kernel unfolds `𝒪(1)`.
