# The projective closed-immersion chart criterion

## Status

Statement phase. Lean complete and sorry-free in
`Hartshorne/Scheme/ProjectiveClosedImmersionChartCriterion.lean`, pending review.

## Lean

- `Hartshorne.isClosedImmersion_iff_isAffineOpen_and_surjective`: for affine `Y` and
  `φ : X ⟶ projectiveSpaceOverScheme n Y` with `e : φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L` carrying `φ^* xᵢ` to `sᵢ`
  (the data characterizing the morphism of Theorem 7.1), `φ` is a closed immersion iff each
  `nonvanishingLocusOpens hL (s i)` is affine and
  `φ.appLE (projectiveSpaceChart n Y i) (nonvanishingLocusOpens hL (s i)) _` is surjective.
  Here the coordinate ring of `D₊(xᵢ)` appears as `Γ(D₊(xᵢ), 𝒪)`, and the map sends `xⱼ/xᵢ` to
  `sⱼ/sᵢ` by `appLE_projectiveSpaceChartRatio_smul` (coordinate-nonvanishing-open).
- `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceChartPolynomialMap` and `..._surjective`: the map
  `Γ(Y, ⊤)[y₀, …, yₙ] → Γ(D₊(xᵢ), 𝒪)`, `yⱼ ↦ xⱼ/xᵢ`, is surjective over affine `Y`, i.e.
  `Γ(D₊(xᵢ), 𝒪) = A[x₀/xᵢ, …, xₙ/xᵢ]`. Proof: `D₊(xᵢ) ⊆ ℙⁿ_Y` is the fibre product of the
  chart of `ℙⁿ_ℤ` with `Y` (`Scheme.Hom.isPullback_resLE`), so its ring is a pushout
  (`isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`), and a pushout of rings is generated by the two images
  (`Hartshorne.subring_eq_top_of_isPushout`). The `ℤ` chart ring is generated by the ratios via
  `chartOfPoly_comp_chartToPoly` and `Proj.basicOpenIsoAway`.
- `Hartshorne.chartSectionPolynomialMap` (`a ↦ p^# a`, `yⱼ ↦ sⱼ/sᵢ` with `sectionRatio`),
  `Hartshorne.chartSectionPolynomialMap_X_smul` (its value on `yⱼ` is the `g` with
  `sⱼ = g · sᵢ`), and
  `Hartshorne.isClosedImmersion_iff_isAffineOpen_and_surjective_chartSectionPolynomialMap`:
  the criterion with the polynomial ring `A[y₀, …, yₙ]`, `A = Γ(Y, ⊤)`, for a `Y`-morphism
  (`φ ≫ projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection n Y = p`).
- Helpers: `iSup_projectiveSpaceChart`, `isAffineOpen_projectiveSpaceChart`,
  `isClosedImmersion_morphismRestrict_iff` (restriction over an affine open is a closed
  immersion iff the preimage is affine and the ring map surjective).

## Proof route

Closed immersions are local on the target (`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.iff_of_iSup_eq_top`) over
the cover by the `D₊(xᵢ)`. Over an affine `U`, forward direction: `IsClosedImmersion.Spec_iff`
(article affine-closed-subschemes, Ex. II.3.11(b)) applied to `f ∣_ U ≫ U.isoSpec.hom`.
Converse: `arrowIsoSpecΓOfIsAffine` and `IsClosedImmersion.spec_of_surjective` (article
surjection-closed-immersion, Ex. II.2.18(c)).

The Proof depends on edge to affine-cover-criterion (Ex. II.2.17(b),
`isAffine_of_isAffineOpen_basicOpen`) is not used by this Lean: affineness of `X_{sᵢ}` is a
hypothesis or comes from the closed-subscheme description.

The base is any affine `Y` rather than `Spec A`; `A` is `Γ(Y, ⊤)`.
