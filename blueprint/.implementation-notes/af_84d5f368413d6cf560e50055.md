# Infinitesimal lifting for a nonsingular affine scheme

## Lean

- `Hartshorne.AffineNonsingularLifting.exists_lift_of_isRegular` (module
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.AffineNonsingularInfinitesimalLifting`): the exercise.
  "Spec A nonsingular" is `(Spec (CommRingCat.of A)).IsRegular` (regular at every point);
  irreducibility, which Hartshorne's "nonsingular variety" includes, is not assumed. The
  extension is a surjective `g : B' →ₐ[k] B` with `RingHom.ker g ^ 2 = ⊥`.
- `Hartshorne.AffineNonsingularLifting.formallySmooth_of_isRegular`: such an `A` is
  `Algebra.FormallySmooth k A`.
- `Hartshorne.AffineNonsingularLifting.isSmoothAt_of_isMaximal`: `Algebra.IsSmoothAt k m` at a
  maximal ideal `m` with `A_m` regular.

## Route

The lift is `Algebra.FormallySmooth.liftOfSurjective` (Mathlib,
`Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean`). Formal smoothness: the smooth locus of a finitely
presented algebra is open (`Algebra.isOpen_smoothLocus`), so its complement, if nonempty,
contains a maximal ideal; at a maximal ideal the closed fibre of `Ω_{A/k}` is the cotangent
space (`Hartshorne.finrank_cotangentSpace_eq_finrank_residueField_tensor_kaehlerDifferential`,
Prop. 8.7, via `DifferentialsNonsingular.finrank_tensor_kaehler_localization` and
`DifferentialsNonsingular.bijective_algebraMap_residueField`), of dimension `dim A_m` by
regularity, and `Hartshorne.isSmoothAt_of_finrank_le` applies with `dim A/m = 0`.

Divergence from the printed hint: the hint uses Theorem 8.17 (left exactness of the conormal
sequence for `Spec A ⊂ 𝔸^n`) and the local freeness of `Ω_{A/k}` to split the conormal
sequence. The recorded 8.17 is a statement about sheaves on an irreducible `Y`; translating its
short exactness to the algebraic map `J/J² → A ⊗ Ω_{P/k}` (Mathlib's `kerCotangentToTensor`)
would need identifications of the pullback of the ideal sheaf and of `Ω` on affine schemes with
the algebraic modules and maps, and a reduction from regular to irreducible `Spec A`. The
differential criterion `isSmoothAt_of_finrank_le` is proved by the local form of the 8.17
argument (independent differentials cut out a regular quotient; Jacobian criterion), so the
route uses the same mathematics at the level of local rings. The splitting itself is Mathlib's
`Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_split_injection` (packaged in `FormallySmooth`).
