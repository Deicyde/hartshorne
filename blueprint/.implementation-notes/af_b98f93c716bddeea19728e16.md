# Product of an ideal sheaf with a submodule sheaf

## Status

No `lean:` recorded yet. A roadmap revision added this article as the follow-up of review r60
on the Artin–Rees kernel article (`af_f0015eba983da3bec5f18766`). The definition already exists
in the project. A later Formalize pass should record the declarations below after independent
review, once the quasi-coherence and affine-sections statements are proved.

## Candidate Lean (existing project code)

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ArtinReesKernelSheaf`
(`src/Hartshorne/Cohomology/ArtinReesKernelSheaf.lean`), namespace
`Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernelSheaf`:

- `powSmul (I : X.IdealSheafData) (k : ℕ) (N : E.val.Submodule) : E.val.Submodule`: the
  article's `I^k N`, defined as `(powSmulFamily I k N).closure`. These are the sections lying,
  near every point, in `I.ideal V ^ k • N(V)` on an affine open `V`. `N` is a submodule
  presheaf of an ambient `E : X.Modules`, so the subsheaf is taken inside `E`.
- `powSmulFamily`: the affine family `V ↦ I.ideal V ^ k • N(V)`.
- `AffineFamily`, `AffineFamily.closure`, `AffineFamily.isLocal_closure`,
  `AffineFamily.closure_le`, `AffineFamily.closure_mono`: the generic "sheaf generated by an
  affine family" construction.
- `isLocal_powSmul`, `powSmul_antitone`: locality, which with `toModules` makes `I^k N` an
  object of `X.Modules`, and antitonicity in `k`.
- `pow_smul_map_mem`: `J ^ k • P` maps into `J' ^ k • P'` under a semilinear map.
- `IsLocal`, `toModules`, `isLocal_of_isSheaf`, `inclusion`. Sheafiness comes from
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isSheaf_submodule_of_local` in `Hartshorne.Scheme.CartierDivisorSheaf`.

Not yet in Lean, and needed for this article's statement:

- quasi-coherence of `toModules (powSmul I k N) _` when `N` (as a module) is quasi-coherent;
- the affine-sections identity `sub (powSmul I k N) V = I.ideal V ^ k • sub N V` for
  `V : X.affineOpens` and quasi-coherent `N`;
- coherence when `X` is Noetherian and `N` is coherent.

The section identity is the key lemma. The inclusion `⊇` is immediate from the closure. For
`⊆`, use the tilde description on `V` (`Hartshorne.affineTildeBasicOpenIso`) and
`(a ^ k • M)_f = (a_f) ^ k • M_f`. Quasi-coherence then follows from
`Hartshorne.quasicoherent_affine_local_tfae`, and coherence from
`Hartshorne.coherent_affine_local_tfae` with `IsNoetherian` submodules of finite modules.

## Representation caveat

The candidate code takes powers on Mathlib's `IdealSheafData`. The II.9 source page asks that
powers be taken on the module ideal sheaf and transported through the bridge article
`af_8763f99884b849b82e0825eb`, which is unformalized. Review r60(a) asks to add that bridge as a
`Depends on` prerequisite of the proved Artin–Rees kernel article once the bridge is formalized; this unformalized article does not list it either, so the Lean statement must either take a module ideal sheaf or add the bridge edge.
