# Base-point-freeness and global generation

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.BasePointFree` (namespace `Hartshorne.LinearSystem`).

## Declarations

- `IsBasePointFree`: no point is a base point (`IsBasePoint` is the linear-system API's
  definition, already the Lemma 7.8 criterion "every `s ∈ V` vanishes at `x`").
- `isBasePoint_iff_forall_germ_mem`: Lemma 7.8, first part, in the stalk form
  `s_x ∈ 𝔪_x L_x`; `forall_mem_support_iff_forall_germ_mem` composes it with the API's
  `isBasePoint_iff` to state it with Hartshorne's divisor definition (integral `X`).
- `isBasePointFree_iff_isGeneratedBySections`: Lemma 7.8, second part, with generation meaning
  `IsGeneratedBySections` for the family of all sections in `V` (indexed by the subtype).
- `isBasePoint_iff_of_span_eq`, `isBasePointFree_iff_isGeneratedBySections_of_span_eq`: the same
  for a spanning family `s₀, …, sₙ` of `V` (hypothesis `Submodule.span k (Set.range s) = V`).
- Remark 7.8.1: `existsUnique_morphism_of_isBasePointFree` (wrapper over
  `existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`), the chosen morphism `morphism` with
  `morphism_spec`, and the converse `isBasePointFree_ofFamily_pullback` (the `φ^* xᵢ` span a
  base-point-free system of `φ^* 𝒪(1)`, via `isGeneratedBySections_pullback_projectiveSpaceCoordinate`).
- Remark 7.8.2: `isClosedImmersion_morphism_iff` for the chosen morphism, and
  `isClosedImmersion_iff_of_span_eq` for any `φ` with `e : φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L`, `e(φ^* xᵢ) = sᵢ`; both
  wrap Proposition 7.3, with `V` presented as `toSection '' V`.

## Generalizations

- Lemma 7.8 is proved on any scheme over a field (Hartshorne: nonsingular projective variety over
  an algebraically closed field); the divisor form needs only integrality.
- Remarks 7.8.1–7.8.2 accept any spanning family instead of a basis; 7.8.2 needs only
  `Projective q` and `IsAlgClosed k`.

## Proof route

A private lemma shows that a family of global sections of an invertible sheaf generates it iff
at every point some member is nonvanishing: a nonvanishing section trivializes `L` on its
nonvanishing locus (`isTrivializingSection_nonvanishingLocusOpens`), and if all germs lie in
`𝔪_x L_x` their span cannot be the stalk, because the germ of a local trivializing section is not
in `𝔪_x L_x` (the stalk is free on it). The spanning-family bridge uses
`mem_ofFamily_sections_iff` and `nonvanishingLocus_linearSystemSection_subset`.
