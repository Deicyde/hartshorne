# Tilde commutes with tensor products

## Partial progress

The module `Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineTildeTensor` (sorry-free, not recorded in
the article frontmatter) proves the following for a commutative ring $R$ and
$R$-modules $M$, $N$, using the project's module-sheaf tensor product:

- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso`: the isomorphism
  $\widetilde{M\otimes_R N}\cong\widetilde{M}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\widetilde{N}$.
  Its underlying morphism is `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorHom`, adjoint under the
  tilde–global-sections adjunction to $m\otimes n\mapsto m\otimes n$ on global
  sections. It is an isomorphism because, on stalks, it is a map
  $(M\otimes_R N)_{\mathfrak p}\to M_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_{R_{\mathfrak p}}N_{\mathfrak p}$
  compatible with the localization maps from $M\otimes_R N$.
- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso_hom_app_tmul` and
  `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorHom_app_tmul`: on every open $U$ the section
  $m\otimes n$ goes to the tensor of the sections of $m$ and $n$.
- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso_hom_naturality`: naturality in both
  arguments.
- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorMap` and `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorMap_bilinear`:
  new functoriality $a\otimes b$ of the module-sheaf tensor product, for any
  ringed space, with its formula on generators.

## Blocker

The single completion criterion asks for tilde to be strong monoidal. Neither
Mathlib at the current pin nor the project has a monoidal structure on sheaves
of modules: the project's module-sheaf tensor product has no associator and no
unitors, so the coherence conditions cannot be stated.

## Needed

A prerequisite article giving a monoidal structure on module sheaves, with its
associator and unitors (and the unit object, the structure sheaf).

## Next route

With that structure in place, prove the coherence squares for the isomorphism
above: compatibility with the associators, and with the unitors via
$\widetilde{R}\cong\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec}R}$. As for naturality, each
square can be checked on global sections of the form $m\otimes n$ (or
$m\otimes n\otimes p$), since morphisms out of a tilde sheaf are determined by
their global sections.
