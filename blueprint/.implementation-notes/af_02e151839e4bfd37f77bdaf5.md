# Tilde commutes with tensor products

## Status

The isomorphism and the completion criterion (tilde is strong monoidal) are both
proved in sorry-free modules imported by the library root. Nothing is recorded in
the article frontmatter yet, because the monoidal structure on module sheaves
that the criterion needs is a prerequisite missing from the roadmap (see Needed).

## Checked declarations

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineTildeTensor` (the isomorphism):

- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso`: the isomorphism
  $\widetilde{M\otimes_R N}\cong\widetilde{M}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\widetilde{N}$
  (sheaf tensor product `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensor`). Its underlying morphism
  `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorHom` is adjoint, for the tilde–global-sections
  adjunction, to $m\otimes n\mapsto m\otimes n$ on global sections. It is an
  isomorphism on stalks because both sides are localizations of $M\otimes_R N$.
- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso_hom_app_tmul`, `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorHom_app_tmul`:
  on every open, the section $m\otimes n$ goes to $m\otimes n$.
- `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso_hom_naturality`: naturality in both arguments.
- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorMap`, `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorMap_bilinear`.
- `Hartshorne.tildeHom_ext`, `Hartshorne.tilde_map_toOpen_apply`: morphisms out of
  a tilde sheaf are determined by the global sections coming from the module.

Module `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSheafMonoidal` (the missing prerequisite,
for any ringed space $(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$ given by a sheaf `R` of commutative rings):

- `Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory`: the monoidal category structure on
  `SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf R)` (an instance), with unit the structure sheaf
  (`Hartshorne.Ringed.tensorUnit_eq`). `Hartshorne.Ringed.schemeModulesMonoidalCategory`
  is the same structure on `X.Modules` for a scheme `X`.
- Construction: Mathlib's monoidal localization (`LocalizedMonoidal`) of the
  monoidal category of presheaves of modules along sheafification
  (`Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationFunctor`, a localization at
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW`). Stability of `sheafificationW` under
  tensor products (`Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW_isMonoidal`) is checked on
  stalks, using `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW_iff` and the stalk isomorphism
  `Hartshorne.ModuleSheafTensor.presheafTensorStalkIso`.
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationFunctor_monoidal`: sheafification is monoidal.
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.forget_laxMonoidal`: the forgetful functor to presheaves is
  lax monoidal (as a right adjoint). Its tensor comparison is
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.tensorπ`, and the sections it produces are
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.tmulSection F G f g` ("$f\otimes g$").
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor`: `moduleSheafTensor F G ≅ F ⊗ G`.
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor_hom_bilinear` says it sends
  $f\otimes g$ to $f\otimes g$.
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.tensor_hom_ext`: morphisms out of `F ⊗ G` are determined on
  the sections $f\otimes g$.
- Section formulas: `tensorHom_tmulSection`, `whiskerLeft_tmulSection`,
  `whiskerRight_tmulSection`, `associator_hom_tmulSection`,
  `leftUnitor_hom_tmulSection`, `rightUnitor_hom_tmulSection` (namespace
  `Hartshorne.Ringed`), plus `scheme_*` restatements for `X.Modules` (needed to
  rewrite terms built from the scheme instance).

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineTildeMonoidal` (the completion criterion):

- `Hartshorne.tildeCoreMonoidal` and the instance `Hartshorne.tildeFunctorMonoidal`:
  `tilde.functor R` is strong monoidal.
- `Hartshorne.tildeμIso`: the tensor comparison
  $\widetilde M\otimes\widetilde N\cong\widetilde{M\otimes N}$. It is the inverse
  of `affineTildeTensorIso`, transported along `moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor`.
  `Hartshorne.tilde_μ_eq` and `Hartshorne.tilde_δ_eq` identify the functor's μ
  and δ with it (by `rfl`; δ is exactly
  `affineTildeTensorIso.hom ≫ moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor.hom`).
- `Hartshorne.tildeεIso`: the unit comparison, Mathlib's `tildeSelf` (an identity).
- `Hartshorne.tildeμIso_inv_app_tmul`, `Hartshorne.tildeμIso_hom_app_tmulSection`,
  `Hartshorne.tilde_tensor_hom_ext`, `Hartshorne.tilde_tensor₃_hom_ext`: the
  section formulas and the global-section extensionality used for the coherence
  squares.

## Needed

A prerequisite article stating the monoidal structure on module sheaves of a
ringed space (tensor product, unit $\mathcal{O}_X$, associator, unitors,
coherence), for this article to depend on. Its Lean is
`Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory`, with `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor`
comparing it to the tensor product of module sheaves.

## Recording, once the prerequisite exists

- New monoidal-structure article: `Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor`.
- This article: `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso` (the isomorphism),
  `Hartshorne.affineTildeTensorIso_hom_naturality`, `Hartshorne.tildeCoreMonoidal`
  (strong monoidal), and `Hartshorne.tilde_δ_eq` (its comparison is the isomorphism).

## Lean friction

- For a scheme, `X.Modules` agrees with `SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf X.sheaf)`
  only at default transparency. Instance search for the generic monoidal
  instance fails on it, which is why there is a separate scheme instance, the
  `scheme_*` lemma restatements, and `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false`
  in the tilde file.
- Instance search does not find `IsIso` of the sheafification counit through the
  abbreviation `sheafificationAdj`, so `sheafificationCounitIso` passes it
  explicitly.
- Writing `SheafOfModules.forget (ringCatSheafOf R)` without `.{u}` leaves a
  universe metavariable, and instance search then fails or hangs.
