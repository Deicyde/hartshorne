# Differentials characterize nonsingular varieties

## Status

Statement retracted after review: the article now assumes `X` irreducible (the book's
hypothesis) instead of integral, and concludes that `X` is a nonsingular variety, so reducedness
is part of the conclusion.

## Lean change required

`Hartshorne.isRegular_iff_relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank` must take
`[IrreducibleSpace X]` instead of `[IsIntegral X]`, keeping the conclusion
`X.IsRegular ↔ (Scheme.relativeDifferentials f).IsLocallyFreeOfRank n` (regular plus irreducible
gives integral). Forward direction: regular local rings are domains, so `X` is reduced, hence
integral, and the existing proof applies. Converse: local freeness of rank `n` gives fibre
dimension `n` at every point without integrality; at a closed point `dim_k m/m² = n`, and
`dim 𝒪_{X,x} = dim X = n` must be obtained without `IsIntegral` (for instance through `X_red`),
so `𝒪_{X,x}` is regular; then generize as before.
