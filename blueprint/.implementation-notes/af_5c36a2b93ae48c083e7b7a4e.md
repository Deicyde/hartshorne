# Ideals of definition

## Lean shape

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.IdealOfDefinition`.

- `Hartshorne.IdealSubsheaf R`, for a sheaf of commutative rings `R` on a
  topological space `T`, is the ideal subsheaf as an actual submodule sheaf:
  a `PresheafOfModules.Submodule` of `SheafOfModules.unit (ringCatSheafOf R)`
  (the unit module `R` over itself) together with locality of its sections.
  `IdealSubsheaf.toModules` is the resulting `SheafOfModules`, and
  `IdealSubsheaf.ι` its inclusion into the unit module (a `Mono`).
- Representation bridge to ideal families: `IdealSubsheaf.ideal J V` reads off
  the ideal of sections; `IdealSubsheaf.ofIdeals` builds an ideal subsheaf from
  a restriction-stable local family of ideals (`ideal_ofIdeals` is `rfl`).
- `IdealSubsheaf.quotient J` is `idealQuotientSheaf R J.ideal J.ideal_le_comap`
  (reused from the affine-formal-thickenings module); `quotientπ` is the
  projection, `quotientAb` the underlying abelian sheaf (composite of the
  forgetful functors through `RingCat`), and `quotientSheafedSpace` the ringed
  space `(T, R / J)`.
- `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition X J` for `X : NoetherianFormalScheme` and
  `J : IdealSubsheaf X.toLocallyRingedSpace.sheaf`: `sheafSupport J.quotientAb = Set.univ`
  and there exist a scheme `Y` with `IsNoetherian Y` and an isomorphism of
  sheafed spaces `Y.toSheafedSpace ≅ J.quotientSheafedSpace`.
- `IsIdealOfDefinition.quotientScheme h` transports the scheme structure along
  that isomorphism (`PresheafedSpace.IsOpenImmersion.toScheme`); its sheafed
  space is `J.quotientSheafedSpace` by `rfl`, and it carries an `IsNoetherian`
  instance.
- Extra API for Prop. 9.5: a `PartialOrder` on ideal subsheaves (`le_iff`),
  powers `J ^ k` via `sheafIdealPow` (`ideal_pow`), and `pow_one`.

## Design choices

- The book's "the locally ringed space is a Noetherian scheme" is stated as an
  isomorphism of ringed spaces with a Noetherian scheme. Locality of the stalks
  of the quotient is then a consequence (stalks are isomorphic to those of a
  scheme), so no separate `LocallyRingedSpace` structure on the quotient is
  required up front.
- `IdealSubsheaf` is stated on a general ringed space, matching the project's
  convention for module-sheaf notions; the scheme-specific
  `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf` uses the same storage (submodule of the unit
  module plus locality) but requires a scheme.
- The sheaf condition for a local submodule family is a private copy of the
  scheme-only `Scheme.isSheaf_submodule_of_local`, generalized to any sheaf of
  rings on a topological space.
