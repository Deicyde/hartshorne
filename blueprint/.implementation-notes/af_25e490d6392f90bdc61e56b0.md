# Projective dimension over a local ring is bounded by that of the residue field

## Status

New article from the regular-localization split (roadmap revision). The
project already proves the result, sorry-free, but the article records no
declaration yet: a later Formalize pass records it after independent review.

## Candidate Lean

In the module `Hartshorne.Scheme.LocalRingGlobalDimension`:

- `Hartshorne.LocalGlobalDimension.hasProjectiveDimensionLE_of_residueField`:
  for `[IsLocalRing R] [IsNoetherianRing R]`, `n : ℕ`, a bound
  `HasProjectiveDimensionLE (ModuleCat.of R (ResidueField R)) n` and a
  finitely generated `M : ModuleCat R`, gives `HasProjectiveDimensionLE M n`.
  This is the article statement.

Helpers in the same module: `subsingleton_ext_of_iso`,
`subsingleton_ext_of_forall_prime` (prime filtrations),
`isSMulRegular_quotient_of_notMem`, `subsingleton_ext_quotient_prime` (the
descending induction with Nakayama). The reduction from Ext vanishing against
finitely generated modules to the projective-dimension bound is Mathlib's
`ModuleCat.hasProjectiveDimensionLT_of_forall_finite`
(`Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/ProjectiveDimension.lean`). The Ext criterion
itself is the Mathlib-backed article projective-dimension-ext-criterion
(`CategoryTheory.hasProjectiveDimensionLT_iff`).

## Source

Matsumura is not among the blueprint sources, so the theorem number in
*Commutative Ring Theory* §19 for "gl.dim A = pd_A k for a Noetherian local
ring" was not verified; the article cites the section only. Hartshorne does
not state this result; it serves the localization theorem he cites in II.8,
Theorem 8.14A (p.177), and the finitely generated case of III.6,
Proposition 6.11A(a) (p.237).
