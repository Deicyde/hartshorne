# Normality of a local complete intersection

## Implementation route (w30, frontier/w30i)

Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.LocalCompleteIntersectionNormal`.

Target statement: `Hartshorne.isNormal_iff_of_isLocalCompleteIntersectionIn`. Given `X.IsRegular`,
a closed immersion `i : Y ⟶ X` and `IsLocalCompleteIntersectionIn i`, it states that `IsNormal Y`
holds if and only if every local ring of `Y` of dimension at most one is regular.

"Regular in codimension one" is read as `(R1)` of Theorem 8.22A, which covers local rings of
dimension at most one. It is deliberately not `Scheme.IsRegularInCodimensionOne`, which only
constrains the points of coheight one. With that reading the statement fails: `Spec k[x]/(x²)`
in `𝔸¹` is a local complete intersection that satisfies the coheight-one condition vacuously and
is not normal.

The proof goes through `Hartshorne.isNormal_iff_of_isCohenMacaulay`, which applies Serre's
criterion (`SerreNormality.normal_iff_R1_and_S2`) on affine opens. `(S2)` comes from
depth = dimension in the Cohen–Macaulay local rings given by Proposition 8.23(a).

The module compiles and is imported from the library root. Recording was waiting on the full
build and the r9-44 impact checks, which the host load blocked.
