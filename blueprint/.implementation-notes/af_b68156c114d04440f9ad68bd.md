# Geometric vector bundles from locally free sheaves

## Status: not started in Lean; Roadmap return

No Lean has been written for this node.

## Why it is returned

- **No bridge from sheaves of algebras to `QuasiCoherentAlgebra`.** In the relative-spec node,
  quasi-coherent algebras are formalized as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentAlgebra Y`: a coequifibered
  functor of rings on the affine Zariski site (Scheme/RelativeSpec.lean). `Sym(E)` is a module sheaf
  with a `ModuleSheafAlgebra` structure: `moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebra`,
  `moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebraStructure`, quasi-coherent by `symmetricAlgebra_isQuasicoherent`. No
  declaration and no roadmap node turns a quasi-coherent module sheaf with a sheaf-of-algebras
  structure into a `QuasiCoherentAlgebra`. The coequifibered condition needs
  `A(D(f)) = A(U)_f` as rings on affine `U`. Without this bridge, `Spec_Y Sym(E)` cannot be
  written down with the existing relative Spec. `QuasiCoherentAlgebra` is used only in
  RelativeSpec.lean.
- **Geometric vector bundle is not defined anywhere.** The notion is a `Y`-scheme with an open
  cover `U_i`, isomorphisms `f⁻¹(U_i) ≅ 𝔸ⁿ_{U_i}` over `U_i`, and transition automorphisms of
  `𝔸ⁿ_{U_i ∩ U_j}` that are linear. This node must define it, or a node for it must be added
  (vector-bundle-equivalence and vector-bundle-sections depend on this node). The project has no
  notion of a linear automorphism of `𝔸ⁿ_U`.

## Available ingredients once the bridge exists

- `symmetricAlgebra_restrict_iso_polynomial` (Scheme/SymmetricAlgebraSheaf.lean): where `E` is free,
  `Sym(E)|_U` is the polynomial algebra sheaf `𝒪_U[T_i]`, multiplicatively and unitally, with the
  variables in degree one. This gives the local identification `V(E)|_U ≅ 𝔸ⁿ_U` through
  `QuasiCoherentAlgebra.restrictIso` and `relSpecIso`.
- `Scheme.relativeDifferentials_affineSpaceOver_free` and the affine-space API, for `𝔸ⁿ_U`.
