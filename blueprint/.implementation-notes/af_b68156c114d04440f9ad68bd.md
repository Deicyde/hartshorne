# Geometric vector bundles from locally free sheaves

## Status: formalized (candidate, pending review)

Candidate `lean:` declarations:

- `AlgebraicGeometry.GeometricVectorBundle` (the notion, Ex. 5.18 preamble);
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.vectorBundle` (`V(E) = Spec_Y Sym(E)`);
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleAtlas` (`V(E)` is a geometric vector bundle of
  rank `n`).

`nonempty_geometricVectorBundle_vectorBundle` is the `Nonempty` corollary.

## Modules

- `Hartshorne/Scheme/QuasiCoherentAlgebraOfModule.lean`: the bridge from sheaves of algebras to
  `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentAlgebra`. For `M : Y.Modules` quasi-coherent with
  `S : ModuleSheafAlgebra M` and `hS : S.IsCommutative`, `ModuleSheafAlgebra.toQuasiCoherentAlgebra hS`
  has rings of sections `sectionsRing hS U` (`Γ(M, U)` with the multiplication of `S`, through the
  type synonym `SectionsType`), structure maps `unitRingHom` (`r ↦ r • 1`), restriction
  `sectionsRestrict`. The coequifibered condition (`coequifibered_structureMap`) is Lemma 5.3 for
  `M`: `exists_pow_smul_extension_of_isAffineOpen` and `exists_pow_smul_eq_zero_of_isAffineOpen`
  give the three `IsLocalization` axioms directly. `toQuasiCoherentAlgebra_restriction`: the
  general restriction of the relative-Spec API is `sectionsRestrict`.
- `Hartshorne/RingedSpace/PolynomialSheafSections.lean` (ringed space): if a presheaf of modules is
  `𝒪(W)[T_i]` over every open `W ⊆ U` compatibly with restriction, the sheafification unit is
  injective over `W ⊆ U` and bijective over `U` quasi-compact
  (`sheafifyUnit_bijective_of_polynomial`; local polynomials, finite subcover, coefficientwise
  gluing in `𝒪_X`). Applied to `W ↦ S(E(W))` with a frame over `U`
  (`moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebra_sheafifyUnit_bijective`).
- `Hartshorne/Scheme/SymmetricAlgebraAffineSections.lean`: `chartRingHom U e : Γ(U)[x_k] → Γ(U, Sym E)`,
  `x_k ↦ e_k` in degree one (`symDegreeOne`); bijective for a frame over an affine `U`
  (`chartRingHom_bijective`), natural in restriction (`chartRingHom_naturality`), and a change of
  frame is a linear substitution (`chartRingHom_transition`, from the basis `frameBasis` of the
  frame).
- `Hartshorne/Scheme/GeometricVectorBundle.lean`: the definition, `V(E)`, the trivializations
  `frameTrivialization` (`restrictIso`, `Spec` of `frameChartIso`, `isoOfIsAffine⁻¹`), over the base
  (`frameTrivialization_hom_over`), the chart formula
  `affineSpaceChart_frameTrivialization_inv` (uses `AffineSpace.isoOfIsAffine_inv_map_hom`:
  `𝔸ⁿ_S ⟶ 𝔸ⁿ_T` over affine bases is `Spec` of the polynomial map), the atlas over `AffineFrame E n`
  (all affine opens with a frame; no choices), and `GeometricVectorBundle.trivial`.

## Encoding of the definition

Hartshorne's condition on `ψ_i ∘ ψ_j⁻¹` over an affine `V ⊆ U_i ∩ U_j` is stated as an equation of
morphisms `Spec A[x] ⟶ X` through the standard charts `affineSpaceChart n V h`
(`(isoOfIsAffine V).inv ≫ AffineSpace.map (homOfLE h)`), with `A = Γ(V, ⊤)`:
`chart_j ≫ ψ_j⁻¹ ≫ ι = Spec θ ≫ chart_i ≫ ψ_i⁻¹ ≫ ι`, `θ` an `A`-algebra automorphism with
`θ(x_k) = ∑ a_{kl} x_l` (`IsLinearPolynomialAlgHom`). Variables are indexed by `ULift (Fin n)`, as
in `IsLocallyFreeOfRank`. The affine opens are taken as `Y.affineOpens` so that the `IsAffine`
instance is the Mathlib one (with `hV : IsAffineOpen V` as instance, rewriting failed on instance
type mismatches).

## Friction

- `Y.sheaf` (a `TopCat.Sheaf`) versus `Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology Y)` and
  `(Y.sheaf.obj ⋙ forget₂ …).obj U` versus `Γ(Y, U)` make `rw` fail its type check;
  `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` (as in `RelativeSpec.lean`) and
  term-mode `congrArg`/`.trans` were needed. `AffineZariskiSite` anonymous constructors also
  mis-elaborate as subtypes; the abbreviation `affineSite` avoids it.
- Ring-valued statements on `Γ(M, U)` need explicit `HSMul.hSMul (α := Γ(Y, U)) (β := Γ(M, U))`
  because the module instance of `M.val.obj U` is over `(Y.sheaf.obj ⋙ forget₂ …).obj U`.
