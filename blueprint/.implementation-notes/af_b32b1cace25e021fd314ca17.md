# The inverse of a point blowup has the center as a fundamental point

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Surface.PointBlowupFundamentalPoint`. Throughout, `S : Hartshorne.Surface k`
and `P : S.Point`; `π = S.pointBlowupProjection P`, `E = S.pointBlowupExceptional P`.

- `Hartshorne.Surface.pointBlowupPartialIso`: Mathlib's `Scheme.PartialIso` `X̃ ⇢ X` built from
  `pointBlowupIsoOffCenter` (`X̃ - E ≅ X - P`); density of both opens from
  `exists_ne_point` and irreducibility.
- `Hartshorne.Surface.toRationalMap_pointBlowupProjection`: `π.toRationalMap` equals the rational
  map of that partial isomorphism, so `π` is a birational transformation in the sense used by
  `Hartshorne.contractedCurves_finite` (rational map represented by a partial isomorphism).
- `Hartshorne.Surface.pointBlowupInverse`: the inverse birational transformation `T`, the rational
  map of `pointBlowupPartialIso.symm`; `pointBlowupInverse_compHom` says `π ∘ T = id`.
- `Hartshorne.Surface.dom_toRationalMap_pointBlowupProjection`: `Dom(π) = ⊤`.
- `Hartshorne.Surface.dom_pointBlowupInverse`: `Dom(T) = X - P`, with
  `coe_fundamentalLocus_pointBlowupInverse` and `fundamentalPoints_pointBlowupInverse`
  (`= {P}`), using `SchemeRationalMap.dom`, `fundamentalLocus`, `fundamentalPoints`.
- `Hartshorne.Surface.totalTransform_pointBlowupInverse`: `T(Z) = π⁻¹(Z)` for every `Z`, with
  `SchemeRationalMap.totalTransform` over `k`; `totalTransform_pointBlowupInverse_singleton`:
  `T({P})` is the support of `E`.

The `k`-structures used for the total transform are the local instances
`Hartshorne.Surface.overSpec` (`S.hom`) and `pointBlowupOverSpec` (`π ≫ S.hom`).

## Proof route

- `Dom(T) ⊇ X - P` is `le_dom`; on `X - P` the glued morphism is the inverse isomorphism
  (`homOfLE_domHom_pointBlowupInverse`), and `domHom_pointBlowupInverse_comp`
  (`π ∘ domHom T = ι`) follows by `ext_of_isDominant` since `X` is separated.
- `not_isIso_pointBlowupProjection`: if `π` were an isomorphism, the support of `E`, which is
  `π ⁻¹' {P}`, would be the single point `e = π⁻¹(P)`. The helper
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.coheight_le_one_of_minimal_mem_support` (Krull's
  principal ideal theorem `Ideal.height_le_one_of_isPrincipal_of_mem_minimalPrimes` applied
  to a local equation from `isLocallyPrincipalNonZeroDivisor_idealSheafData`) gives
  `coheight e ≤ 1`, while `coheight e = coheight P = dim 𝒪_{X,P} = 2` by
  `coheight_eq_of_isOpenImmersion` (an isomorphism is an open immersion),
  `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight` and `Hartshorne.dimension_at_closed_point` with `S.dim_eq`.
  The helper is the codimension half of
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.codim_range_subschemeι`, stated for a point
  minimal in the support instead of an irreducible subscheme.
- If `P ∈ Dom(T)` then `Dom(T) = ⊤`, and `σ = topIso.inv ≫ homOfLE ≫ domHom T` is inverse to
  `π`: `σ ≫ π = 𝟙` directly from `domHom_pointBlowupInverse_comp`, and `π ≫ σ = 𝟙` by
  `ext_of_isDominant` along the dense open `X̃ - E` (`X̃` separated:
  `isSeparated_pointBlowup`). This contradicts `not_isIso_pointBlowupProjection`.
- Total transform: the transposed graph `τ = (π, 𝟙) : X̃ ⟶ X ×_k X̃` is a closed immersion
  (Mathlib's instance for `pullback.lift (𝟙 _) f _` with separated target, composed with
  `pullbackSymmetry`). `graphHom k T = domHom T ≫ τ`, so `Γ_T ⊆ range τ`; conversely
  `τ(X̃ - E)` lies in the graph and `X̃ - E` is dense, so `range τ ⊆ closure (graph) = Γ_T`
  (`range_graphClosureι`).

## Scope

"Exceptional curve" is the support of the effective Cartier divisor `E` (equivalently the fibre
`π ⁻¹' {P}`); that it is an irreducible curve isomorphic to `ℙ¹` is not proved here (it needs
`X̃` to be a surface, Proposition V.3.1).
