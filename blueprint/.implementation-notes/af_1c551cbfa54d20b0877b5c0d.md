# Implementation note: exactness of affine formal global sections (Ex. II.9.3(a,b))

Status: complete candidate, pending review. Main declaration
`Hartshorne.affineFormal_globalSections_shortExact` (module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineFormalGlobalSectionsExact`): for `X : NoetherianFormalScheme` with
`IsAffineNoetherianFormalScheme X.toLocallyRingedSpace`, a short exact `S` of
`SheafOfModules (structureRingCatSheaf X.toLocallyRingedSpace)` and `IsCoherentFormalModule S.X₁`,
`(S.map (SheafOfModules.evaluation _ (op ⊤))).ShortExact`. Sorry-free; axioms propext,
Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

## Route

Injectivity and exactness in the middle are left exactness of sections
(`Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_sections_exact`). Surjectivity follows the local-lift-and-correct
argument of Proposition II.5.6 with a Čech/Mittag-Leffler step for the coherent kernel `F'`:

1. A global section of `F''` lifts on the opens of a finite cover `U` of the quasi-compact `X`.
2. The differences of the local lifts form a Čech 1-cocycle of `F'` on `U`; if it is a coboundary
   the lifts can be corrected to glue (`Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_exists_lift_of_cechOneVanishes`).
3. `F' = lim_n F'/J^(n+1)F'` (Prop. 9.6(a), `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.reconstruction`). The
   sequences `0 -> Γ(Q_n) -> ∏ Q_n(U_i) -> Z¹(U, Q_n) -> 0` are exact when each `Q_n` has vanishing
   Čech H¹ on `U`, and Prop. 9.1(b) gives vanishing for the limit when `Γ(Q_n)` is Mittag-Leffler
   (`Hartshorne.cechOneVanishes_of_isLimit`, using
   `Hartshorne.InverseSystem.inverseLimit_exact_of_isMittagLeffler`).
4. Čech H¹ of `Q_n = F'/J^(n+1)F'` vanishes when the thickening `X_n` is affine: `Q_n` is the
   pushforward of the coherent `F'_n`, and on an affine scheme global sections lift along every
   monomorphism out of a quasi-coherent module (Prop. 5.6). The trick: for a cocycle `c` take
   `P = ∏_k (V ↦ G(V ∩ U_k))`, glue the classes of `(c_ki)_k` in `P/G`, lift globally and read off
   a trivialization (`Hartshorne.cechOneVanishes_of_surjective_cokernel`).
5. Mittag-Leffler: `Γ(Q_(n+1)) -> Γ(Q_n)` is onto when `X_(n+1)` is affine. On `X_(n+1)`, `Q_n`
   (a module killed by `J^(n+2)`, `IsIdealOfDefinition.quotientModule`) is the pushforward of the
   coherent `F'_n` along the closed immersion `X_n -> X_(n+1)`, hence quasi-coherent (Prop. 5.8(c),
   `isQuasicoherent_pushforward`); the kernel of the epimorphism `F'_(n+1) -> Q_n` is
   quasi-coherent (`isQuasicoherent_kernel`) and Prop. 5.6 (`affine_globalSections_shortExact`) on
   `X_(n+1)` gives the surjectivity, carried back along the fully faithful `quotientPushforward`.
6. Affine thickenings: for `e : X ≅ Spf_I(A)`, `J` is the inverse image along `e` of
   `K = ker (𝒪_{Spf} -> 𝒪_{Y_1})`. Transport along `e` gives `𝒪_X / e⁻¹K ≅ e⁻¹_*(𝒪_{Spf}/K)` (via
   `idealQuotientSheafIso`), hence `J` is an ideal of definition and `(X, 𝒪_X/J^(n+1))` is
   isomorphic to `(Spf, 𝒪/K^(n+1))` (using `comap_pow_of_iso`), which is `Y_(n+1)` restricted to
   `|Y_1|` (`formalCompletionQuotientIso`), and `Y_(n+1) ≅ Spec (A/I^(n+1))`
   (`specIdealSubsheafNeighborhoodIso`) is affine.

## Friction

- Kernel defeq between the carrier of `Scheme.restrict` along `Y_1 ⟶ Y_(n+1)` and `|Y_1|` times
  out for symbolic `n` (it is fast for `n = 0`); the identification is made once for an arbitrary
  scheme (private `restrictSheafedSpaceIso`). Likewise surjectivity of `Scheme.ofRestrict` is
  proved by rewriting with `Scheme.ofRestrict_toLRSHom_base` rather than by defeq.
- Modules over `structureRingCatSheaf X` and over `ringCatSheafOf X.sheaf` are defeq but not
  reducibly so; instances (`Epi`, `IsIso`, `Category.assoc` in `simp`) do not transfer and are
  supplied explicitly.
- `IdealSubsheaf.quotientπ_app_eq_zero_iff` exists only as private copies; another private copy
  lives in `Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineFormalIdealOfDefinition`.
