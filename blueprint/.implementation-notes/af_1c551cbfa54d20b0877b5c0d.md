# Implementation note: exactness of affine formal global sections (Ex. II.9.3(a,b))

Status: partial. No `lean:` recorded. Everything below is sorry-free and builds.

## Route

Injectivity and exactness in the middle are left exactness of sections
(`Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_sections_exact`). Surjectivity follows the local-lift-and-correct
argument of Proposition II.5.6 with a Čech/Mittag-Leffler step for the coherent kernel `F'`:

1. A global section of `F''` lifts on the opens of a finite cover `U` of the quasi-compact `X`.
2. The differences of the local lifts form a Čech 1-cocycle of `F'` on `U`; if it is a coboundary
   the lifts can be corrected to glue (`Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_exists_lift_of_cechOneVanishes`).
3. `F' = lim_n F'/J^(n+1)F'` (Prop. 9.6(a), `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.reconstruction`). The
   sequences `0 -> Γ(Q_n) -> ∏ Q_n(U_i) -> Z¹(U, Q_n) -> 0` are exact when each `Q_n` has vanishing
   Čech H¹ on `U`, and Prop. 9.1(b) gives vanishing for the limit when `Γ(Q_n)` is Mittag-Leffler
   (`Hartshorne.cechOneVanishes_of_isLimit`, using
   `Hartshorne.InverseSystem.inverseLimit_exact_of_isMittagLeffler`).
4. Čech H¹ of `Q_n = F'/J^(n+1)F'` vanishes when the thickening `X_n` is affine: `Q_n` is the
   pushforward of the coherent `F'_n`, and on an affine scheme global sections lift along every
   monomorphism out of a quasi-coherent module (Prop. 5.6). The trick: for a cocycle `c` take
   `P = ∏_k (V ↦ G(V ∩ U_k))`, glue the classes of `(c_ki)_k` in `P/G`, lift globally and read off
   a trivialization (`Hartshorne.cechOneVanishes_of_surjective_cokernel`).

## Remaining goals

R1. On an affine formal `X` (an iso `e : X ≅ Spf_I(A)`), produce an ideal of definition `J` with
every thickening affine: take the inverse image along `e` of the kernel ideal of
`Spf_I(A) -> Spec(A/I)` (`QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionIdealSubsheaf`);
`IsIdealOfDefinition` comes from `isIdealOfDefinition_formalCompletionIdealSubsheaf` transported
along `e` (no public transport lemma exists yet), and `X_n` is affine because its ringed space is
isomorphic to `Spec(A/I^(n+1))` (`formalCompletionQuotientIso`, `specIdealSubsheafNeighborhoodIso`);
an iso of underlying sheafed spaces of schemes gives an iso of schemes.

R2. Mittag-Leffler (`hML`), given `X_(n+1)` affine. Planned route: build the closed immersion
`k : X_n -> X_(n+1)` (identity on spaces, quotient map `O/J^(n+2) -> O/J^(n+1)`; needs
functoriality of `IdealSubsheaf.quotient` in the ideal, which does not exist yet). Then
`F'/J^(n+1)F'` as an `X_(n+1)`-module (`IsIdealOfDefinition.quotientModule`) is `k_* F'_n`, which
is quasi-coherent by Prop. 5.8(c) (`isQuasicoherent_pushforward`, `X_n` Noetherian); the kernel of
`F'_(n+1) -> k_* F'_n` is quasi-coherent (`isQuasicoherent_kernel`); Prop. 5.6 on `X_(n+1)` gives
surjectivity, transported along the fully faithful `quotientPushforward` (sections unchanged).
The map `F'_(n+1) -> k_* F'_n` is the preimage of `powQuotientMap` under that full functor; it is
an epimorphism because faithful functors reflect epimorphisms.

R3. Final statement: `X : NoetherianFormalScheme` with
`IsAffineNoetherianFormalScheme X.toLocallyRingedSpace`, `S` a short exact complex of
`SheafOfModules (structureRingCatSheaf X.toLocallyRingedSpace)`, `IsCoherentFormalModule S.X₁`,
concluding `(S.map (SheafOfModules.evaluation _ (op ⊤))).ShortExact`; assemble from
`sheafOfModules_sections_exact` and `globalSections_surjective_of_isAffine` with R1, R2.
