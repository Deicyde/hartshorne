# Implementation note: the tautological sheaf need not be very ample

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveBundleRankOne`.

## What is proved

General rank-one statements, for an invertible `E` on a Noetherian scheme `X` (these generalize
the book's example):

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.projectiveBundleIsoOfIsInvertible`: `P(E) ≅ X`, with
  `projectiveBundleIsoOfIsInvertible_hom` saying its forward map is the projection, so the
  isomorphism is over `X`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.nonempty_pullback_projectiveBundleIsoOfIsInvertible_inv_iso`:
  the pullback of `𝒪_{P(E)}(1)` along the inverse `X ⟶ P(E)` is isomorphic to `E`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isIso_projectiveBundleTautologicalQuotient_of_isInvertible`:
  the tautological quotient `π^* E ⟶ 𝒪(1)` is an isomorphism.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.not_isVeryAmpleRelative_projectiveBundleTwistingSheaf`:
  if moreover `X` is nonempty and every global section of `E` vanishes, `𝒪_{P(E)}(1)` is not
  very ample relative to `X` (the project's `Hartshorne.IsVeryAmpleRelative`, unchanged).

The book's example: `Hartshorne.projectiveBundle_projectiveLine_twist_neg_one`, for
`X = Proj k[x₀, x₁]` over a field `k` and `E = projTwistingSheaf _ (-1)`, bundles the three
claims (isomorphism over `X`, `𝒪(1)` corresponds to `E`, not very ample). The proofs that `E` is
locally free of finite rank are the helpers `Hartshorne.isLocallyFree_projectiveLine_twist_neg_one`
and `Hartshorne.rankAt_projectiveLine_twist_neg_one_lt`.

## Route

- The section `s` of `π` is the image of the identity quotient `E ⟶ E` under the inverse of the
  Exercise 7.8 bijection (`projectiveBundleSectionEquiv`); `projectiveBundleSectionEquiv_apply`
  gives `s^* 𝒪(1) ≅ E`.
- `π ≫ s = 𝟙` by the uniqueness half of Proposition 7.12 (`projectiveBundle_hom_eq_of_equiv`):
  any two invertible quotients of the invertible sheaf `π^* E` are equivalent
  (`InvertibleQuotient.equiv_of_isInvertible`), using Exercise 7.1
  (`Hartshorne.isIso_of_epi_of_isInvertible`, recorded on the blowup universal-property article,
  hence the link-only Proof depends on edge to it).
- Not very ample: `AlgebraicGeometry.isGloballyGenerated_of_isVeryAmpleRelative` (very ample
  implies generated by global sections, the pullback of the surjection from a free sheaf onto
  `𝒪(1)` on projective space), `AlgebraicGeometry.isGloballyGenerated_pullback` along `s`,
  `Hartshorne.isGloballyGenerated_of_iso`, and then
  `not_isGloballyGenerated_of_isInvertible` (an invertible sheaf with only the zero global
  section on a nonempty scheme is not globally generated). These generation lemmas are
  unrecorded helpers in other modules.
- `Γ(ℙ¹_k, 𝒪(-1)) = 0`: `Hartshorne.ProjectiveSpaceSectionRing.projTwistingSheaf_global_eq_zero`
  (any commutative base ring, `r ≥ 1`, negative twist), an unrecorded helper of the module of the
  section-ring article (Proposition II.5.13).

## Divergences

- The Picard group of projective space (a Proof depends on edge) is not used: the sketch's
  global-generation argument does not need it.
