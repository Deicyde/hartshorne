## Setting

`k` a field (`[IsAlgClosed k]` for the theorem), `q : X ⟶ Spec k`, `L` invertible (`hL`),
`s : Fin (n + 1) → Γ(L, ⊤)`, `φ : X ⟶ ℙⁿ_k` with `hφ : φ ≫ projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection n _ = q`
and `e : φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L` with `he : e(φ^* xᵢ) = sᵢ` (the characterization of the morphism of
Theorem 7.1, the same convention as the chart criterion, Proposition 7.2). The existence of `e`
already forces the `sᵢ` to generate `L`, so no separate generation hypothesis is taken.
`V = linearSystem q s` is the set of `∑ aᵢ sᵢ`, the span of the `sᵢ` (from the finite-dimensional
linear system module). "X projective over k" is `Projective q`.

## Definitions

- `SectionsSeparatePoints V`: for closed `P ≠ Q` there is `t ∈ V` with `t_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P`,
  `t_Q ∉ 𝔪_Q L_Q`, or vice versa. `𝔪_P L_P` is `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (X.presheaf.stalk P) • ⊤`
  in the stalk module `L_P`.
- `SectionsSeparateTangentVectors q V`: for every closed `P` and `z ∈ 𝔪_P L_P` there are `cᵢ ∈ k`,
  `tᵢ ∈ V` with `(tᵢ)_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P` and `z - ∑ cᵢ (tᵢ)_P ∈ 𝔪_P² L_P`. This is "the classes of
  `{s ∈ V : s_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P}` span the `k`-vector space `𝔪_P L_P / 𝔪_P² L_P`" written out: a
  quotient is spanned by the classes of a subset `S` of `𝔪_P L_P` iff every element of
  `𝔪_P L_P` is a `k`-combination of `S` modulo `𝔪_P² L_P`. `k` acts on `L_P` through
  `Hartshorne.stalkConst q P : k →+* 𝒪_{X,P}` (germs of `kConst q`). This avoids building a
  `k`-module structure on the quotient.

- `moduleFinite_stalk_of_sectionsSeparatePoints`: `φ` is projective (`Projective.of_comp`),

  (`finite_stalk_of_isCoherent`), and since `φ` is a topological embedding the germ map
  `(φ_* 𝒪_X)_{φ(P)} → 𝒪_{X,P}` (private, built as a colimit cocone) is a surjective
  `𝒪_{ℙⁿ,φ(P)}`-linear map.
- `residueField_map_bijective_of_isClosed`: `κ(φ(P)) → κ(P)` bijective at closed `P`, via
  Mathlib's `residueFieldIsoBase` (identified with evaluation of constants).

  (`localRingHom_surjective_of_residueField_bijective_of_cotangent_surjective`) at closed points;

  `isClosedImmersion_of_sectionsSeparatePoints_of_moduleFinite` assembles this. Mathlib's
  `IsClosedImmersion` is "closed embedding + surjective on stalks", Hartshorne's definition.

(⇒):

- `sectionsSeparatePoints_of_injective` (only injectivity of `φ` is used): for a closed `P`,
  `pointOfClosedPoint q P hP ≫ φ` is a `k`-point of `ℙⁿ_k`; in `D₊(xⱼ)` it is `projectivePoint a`
  with `aⱼ = 1` (private `exists_eq_projectivePoint`, via `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` and
  `eq_projectivePoint`). Normalizing both points at the same `j`, distinct coordinates give a
  linear form vanishing at exactly one of them; if `φ(Q) ∉ D₊(xⱼ)`, `sⱼ` separates.
- `sectionsSeparateTangentVectors_of_isClosedImmersion`: `φ^#_P` is surjective; at the closed
  point `z = φ(P) ∈ D₊(xᵢ)` with `xₗ/xᵢ ≡ cₗ` modulo `𝔪_z`, every element of `𝔪_z` is, modulo
  `𝔪_z²`, a `k`-combination of the germs of `xₗ/xᵢ - cₗ` (private
  `exists_sub_sum_mem_maximalIdeal_sq`: the chart ring is generated by the `xₗ/xᵢ` over constants,
  `projectiveSpaceChartPolynomialMap_surjective`, and the stalk is its localization). These
  pull back to the sections `sₗ - cₗ sᵢ ∈ V`.
