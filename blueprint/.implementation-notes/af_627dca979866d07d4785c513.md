# Separation of points and tangent vectors

## Status (partial: the direction (⇐) is proved, (⇒) is not)

Module: `Hartshorne/Scheme/SeparatesPointsTangentVectors.lean` (imported by the library root).
No declaration is recorded yet: the article states an equivalence, and only the direction
"separates points and tangent vectors ⇒ closed immersion" is formalized.

### Setting

`k` a field, `q : X ⟶ Spec k`, `L` invertible (`hL`), `s : Fin (n + 1) → Γ(L, ⊤)`,
`φ : X ⟶ ℙⁿ_k` with `hφ : φ ≫ projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection n _ = q` and
`e : φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L` with `he : e(φ^* xᵢ) = sᵢ` (the characterization of the morphism of
Theorem 7.1, as in the chart criterion, Proposition 7.2). `V = linearSystem q s` is the set of
`∑ aᵢ sᵢ` (the span of the `sᵢ`, from the finite-dimensional linear system article). "X projective
over k" is `Projective q`.

### Definitions (the book's, p. 152)

- `Hartshorne.SectionsSeparatePoints V`: for closed `P ≠ Q` there is `t ∈ V` with
  `t_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P`, `t_Q ∉ 𝔪_Q L_Q`, or vice versa. `𝔪_P L_P` is
  `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (X.presheaf.stalk P) • ⊤` in the stalk module `L_P`.
- `Hartshorne.SectionsSeparateTangentVectors q V`: for every closed `P` and `z ∈ 𝔪_P L_P` there
  are `cᵢ ∈ k`, `tᵢ ∈ V` with `(tᵢ)_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P` and `z - ∑ cᵢ (tᵢ)_P ∈ 𝔪_P² L_P`. This is
  "the classes of `{s ∈ V : s_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P}` span the `k`-vector space `𝔪_P L_P / 𝔪_P² L_P`"
  written out (a quotient is spanned by the classes of a subset `S` of `𝔪_P L_P` iff every element
  of `𝔪_P L_P` is a `k`-combination of `S` modulo `𝔪_P² L_P`); `k` acts on `L_P` through
  `Hartshorne.stalkConst q P : k →+* 𝒪_{X,P}` (germs of `kConst q`). This avoids building a
  `k`-module structure on the quotient.
- `germ_mem_maximalIdeal_smul_top_iff`: `t_P ∈ 𝔪_P L_P ↔ P ∉ nonvanishingLocus L t` (true by
  definition of `nonvanishingLocus`).

### Proved (sorry-free)

- `mem_nonvanishingLocus_linearSystemSection_iff`: `X_{∑ aᵢ sᵢ} = φ⁻¹(D₊(∑ aᵢ xᵢ))` (uses
  `iso_app_linearSection`, `mem_nonvanishingLocus_pullbackGlobalSection_iff`).
- `eq_of_sectionsSeparatePoints`: separation of points ⇒ `φ` injective on closed points.
- `injective_of_sectionsSeparatePoints`, `isClosedEmbedding_of_sectionsSeparatePoints`: with
  `Projective q`, `φ` is proper, hence closed; `X` is a Jacobson space, and a closed map from a
  Jacobson `T₀` space injective on closed points is injective (private topological lemma). So `φ`
  is a homeomorphism onto a closed subset.
- `maximalIdealToCotangent_surjective_of_sectionsSeparateTangentVectors`: separation of tangent
  vectors ⇒ `𝔪_{φ(P)} → 𝔪_P / 𝔪_P²` surjective at closed `P` (on a chart `X_{sᵢ} ∋ P`,
  `(∑ aⱼ sⱼ)_P = φ^#(∑ aⱼ xⱼ/xᵢ) · (sᵢ)_P`, and `(sᵢ)_P` generates `L_P` freely).
- `residueField_map_bijective_of_isClosed` (`k` algebraically closed, `q` locally of finite type):
  `κ(φ(P)) → κ(P)` bijective at closed `P`, via Mathlib's `residueFieldIsoBase` (identified with
  evaluation of constants).
- `moduleFinite_stalk_of_sectionsSeparatePoints`: `𝒪_{X,P}` is a finite `𝒪_{ℙⁿ,φ(P)}`-module for
  every `P`: `φ` is projective (`Projective.of_comp`), `φ_* 𝒪_X` is coherent (Corollary 5.20,
  `isCoherent_pushforward_of_projective_of_locallyOfFiniteType`), its stalks are finite
  (`finite_stalk_of_isCoherent`), and since `φ` is a topological embedding the germ map
  `(φ_* 𝒪_X)_{φ(P)} → 𝒪_{X,P}` is a surjective `𝒪_{ℙⁿ,φ(P)}`-linear map.
- `stalkMap_surjective_of_sectionsSeparateTangentVectors`: Lemma 7.4
  (`localRingHom_surjective_of_residueField_bijective_of_cotangent_surjective`) at closed points.
- `isClosedImmersion_of_sectionsSeparatePoints_of_moduleFinite` and the direction (⇐):
  `isClosedImmersion_of_sectionsSeparatePoints_of_sectionsSeparateTangentVectors`
  (`[IsAlgClosed k]`, `hL`, `hφ`, `he`, `Projective q`, separation of points and tangent vectors
  ⇒ `IsClosedImmersion φ`). Stalk surjectivity at a non-closed `x` comes from a closed point `P`
  of `closure {x}` (private `stalkMap_surjective_of_specializes`: `𝒪_{X,x}` is a localization of
  `Γ(U, 𝒪_X)` for an affine `U ∋ P`). Mathlib's `IsClosedImmersion` is "closed embedding +
  surjective on stalks", which is Hartshorne's definition.

## Remaining goal: (⇒)

`IsClosedImmersion φ → SectionsSeparatePoints (linearSystem q s) ∧
SectionsSeparateTangentVectors q (linearSystem q s)` (same hypotheses; `[IsAlgClosed k]`,
`q` locally of finite type). Then the article's theorem is the conjunction of both directions.

Route (the book: "pull back hyperplanes"):

1. Points. A closed point `z` of `ℙⁿ_k` is `projectivePoint k a ha (pt)` for some `a ≠ 0`:
   take Mathlib's `pointOfClosedPoint π z hz : Spec k ⟶ ℙⁿ_k`; `σ^* 𝒪(1)` on `Spec k` is
   generated by the `σ^* xᵢ`, one of which trivializes it on `⊤` (one point); write
   `σ^* xᵢ = rᵢ • σ^* xⱼ`, build `σ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ 𝒪` with the frames lemma as in
   `eq_comp_coordinatePoint`, and conclude with `eq_projectivePoint`. Also needed:
   `projectivePoint (c • a) = projectivePoint a` for `c ≠ 0` (not in the project). For `φ(P) ≠ φ(Q)`
   with coordinates `a`, `b` not proportional pick `v` with `v ⬝ a = 0 ≠ v ⬝ b`; then
   `projectivePoint_mem_nonvanishingLocus_linearSection_iff` and
   `mem_nonvanishingLocus_linearSystemSection_iff` give the separating section `∑ vᵢ sᵢ`.
   Only injectivity of `φ` is used.
2. Tangent vectors. `φ^#_P` is surjective (closed immersion), so `𝔪_P = φ^#(𝔪_{φ(P)})`. At the
   rational point `z = φ(P)` with `z ∈ D₊(xᵢ)` and coordinates `cⱼ = (xⱼ/xᵢ)(z) ∈ k`, the maximal
   ideal of `Γ(D₊(xᵢ), 𝒪) = k[xⱼ/xᵢ]` (`projectiveSpaceChartPolynomialMap_surjective`) is generated
   by the `xⱼ/xᵢ - cⱼ`, hence so is `𝔪_z` (`IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`), and modulo
   `𝔪_z²` every element of `𝔪_z` is a `k`-combination of them. Each `xⱼ/xᵢ - cⱼ` is the ratio
   `(xⱼ - cⱼ xᵢ)/xᵢ`, pulled back to `(sⱼ - cⱼ sᵢ)/sᵢ`, with `sⱼ - cⱼ sᵢ ∈ V` vanishing at `P`; the
   chart computation `hgerm` in the proof of
   `maximalIdealToCotangent_surjective_of_sectionsSeparateTangentVectors` gives the germ identity.
