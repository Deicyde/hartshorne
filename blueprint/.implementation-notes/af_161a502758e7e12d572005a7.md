# Multiplicity of a point on a plane curve

Added for faithfulness item 15 (Hartshorne Ex. I.5.3, definition only), because the formalized
local plane-curve intersection multiplicity article (`af_5ffc461f2f07008cebf36805`) states its
bounds with `mult_P` and now links this article under "Depends on".

## Existing Lean (record in a Formalize pass)

- `Hartshorne.planeCurveMultiplicity (f : MvPolynomial (Fin 2) k) (P : Fin 2 → k) : ℕ` in
  `src/Hartshorne/Intersection/LocalPlaneCurveMultiplicity.lean` (docstring cites Hartshorne I,
  Exercise 5.3): `sInf` of the total degrees of the monomials of `affineTranslateEquiv P f`
  (= `f(x + P)`), i.e. the least `r` with a nonzero degree-`r` homogeneous part. It is already
  listed in the local intersection multiplicity article's `lean:`; it should move to (or also
  appear in) this article's `lean:` as its `def` target.
- Supporting facts in the same file: `mem_affinePointIdeal_pow_planeCurveMultiplicity`
  (`f ∈ 𝔪_P ^ mult_P f`) and the translation `affineTranslateEquiv`.

Check before recording: for `f = 0` the `sInf` of the empty set gives `0`, whereas the article
is about a curve (`f` nonconstant), so this edge case is outside the article's statement. The
characterization "`P ∈ Y` iff `mult_P(Y) > 0`" is part of the article's text; if a Formalize pass
wants it as a lemma, it is `planeCurveMultiplicity f P = 0 ↔ MvPolynomial.eval P f ≠ 0` for
`f ≠ 0` (constant coefficient of `f(x + P)` is `f(P)`); no such lemma was found in `src/`.
