# Affineness is invariant under reduction

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ReductionAffineIff`, namespace `Hartshorne.ReductionAffine`.

- `isAffine_iff_isAffine_reduced (X) [IsNoetherian X] : IsAffine X ↔ IsAffine X.nilradical.subscheme`:
  the exercise. `X_red` is Mathlib's closed subscheme cut out by the nilradical ideal sheaf.
- `isReduced_reduced`: `X.nilradical.subscheme` is reduced (it is the reduced induced closed
  subscheme on all of `X`, via `vanishingIdeal_top` and `Hartshorne.isReduced_reducedInducedSubscheme`).
- `isAffine_reduced_of_isAffine`: the easy direction (closed subscheme of an affine scheme,
  Mathlib's `IsClosedImmersion.isAffine_surjective_of_isAffine`).
- `isAffine_of_forall_isZero_H1`: filtration step. Through `Hartshorne.isAffine_tfae_sheafCohomology`
  ((iii) ⇒ (i)), on a Noetherian `X` it suffices that `H^1(X, Q) = 0` for every quasi-coherent `Q`
  killed by the nilpotent sections of `𝒪_X`. Uses `exists_nilradical_pow_eq_bot` (`𝒩^m = 0`,
  from a finite affine cover and `IsNoetherianRing.isNilpotent_nilradical`), the submodule sheaves
  `𝒩^k J = ArtinReesKernelSheaf.powSmul X.nilradical k J.carrier` (ideal-power-product article),
  the short exact sequences `0 ⟶ 𝒩^{k+1} J ⟶ 𝒩^k J ⟶ cokernel ⟶ 0` (`filtSC`), and
  `SerreAffineness.isZero_H1_of_shortExact`.
- `isZero_H1_of_isAffine_red`: if `X_red` is affine and locally Noetherian, `H^1(X, Q) = 0` for such
  `Q`. `redModules hQ : X_red.Modules` has underlying abelian presheaf `(Opens.map (redInv X)).op ⋙
  Q.presheaf`, where `redInv X` is the inverse of the homeomorphism `X_red ⟶ X` (`redHomeo`); a
  section `a` of `𝒪_{X_red}` acts by gluing `b • s` over affine opens with `b` a lift of `a`
  (`redSMul`, `exists_glue`, `IsLift`). Quasi-coherence (`isQuasicoherent_redModules`) is checked
  on the affine cover `X_red ∩ U`, `U` affine in `X`, with `Hartshorne.isIso_fromTildeΓ_of_pow_smul`.
  The vanishing is `Hartshorne.sheafCohomology_isZero_of_isQuasicoherent_of_isAffine` (III.3.5)
  transported along `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyPushforwardIsoOfIso`.

## Route and divergence

The book's quotients `𝒩^k F / 𝒩^{k+1} F` are modules over `X_red`; the printed hint compares
their cohomology on `X` and on `X_red` with Lemma III.2.10 (closed pushforward). Here the closed
immersion `X_red ⟶ X` is a homeomorphism, so the comparison is the homeomorphism invariance of
sheaf cohomology (proved alongside III.3.5) applied to the same abelian sheaf; Lemma III.2.10 is not
needed. The filtration is of a coherent ideal sheaf `J`, the form Serre's criterion (iii) takes.

The general fact "for a closed immersion `i` with ideal `I` and quasi-coherent `M` with `I·M = 0`,
the unit `M ⟶ i_* i^* M` is an isomorphism" is not used: the `𝒪_{X_red}`-module structure is
built directly, which only works because `X_red ⟶ X` is a homeomorphism. Ex. III.3.2 (reduced
components) needs the general closed-immersion version.
