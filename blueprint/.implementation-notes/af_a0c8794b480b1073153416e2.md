# Noetherian formal schemes

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.NoetherianFormalScheme` (imports only `Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalCompletion`).

- `Hartshorne.IsNoetherianFormalScheme X` for `X : LocallyRingedSpace.{u}`:
  `∃ (ι : Type) (_ : Finite ι) (U : ι → Opens X.carrier), iSup U = ⊤ ∧ ∀ i, ∃ (Y : Scheme.{u})
  (_ : IsNoetherian Y) (I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf Y),
  Nonempty (X.restrict (U i).isOpenEmbedding ≅ I.formalCompletion)`.
  Closed subschemes are represented by quasi-coherent ideal subsheaves (as in the
  formal-completion article); the open subspaces are Mathlib's `LocallyRingedSpace.restrict`
  along `Opens.isOpenEmbedding`; the isomorphism is in `LocallyRingedSpace`. The ambient schemes
  are arbitrary Noetherian schemes, not required affine. The index type lives in `Type` (it is
  finite, so this loses nothing).
- `Hartshorne.IsNoetherianFormalScheme.of_iso (e : X ≅ Y)` and `.iso_iff`: invariance under
  isomorphisms of locally ringed spaces (the first proof-depends-on bullet). The cover of `Y` is
  pulled back along `e.hom.base`; each restricted chart is identified with the corresponding
  restriction of `Y` by a private helper building an isomorphism between two open immersions with
  equal ranges from `LocallyRingedSpace.IsOpenImmersion.lift` (Mathlib has `isoOfRangeEq` only for
  presheafed spaces and schemes).
- `Hartshorne.NoetherianFormalScheme`: structure extending `LocallyRingedSpace.{u}` with the field
  `isNoetherianFormalScheme`. Its `Category` instance `NoetherianFormalScheme.category` has `Hom X Y := X.toLocallyRingedSpace ⟶
  Y.toLocallyRingedSpace` (morphisms are exactly morphisms of locally ringed spaces), with
  `NoetherianFormalScheme.forgetToLocallyRingedSpace` and
  `NoetherianFormalScheme.fullyFaithfulForgetToLocallyRingedSpace`.

## Not done: refinement to a finite affine formal atlas

The third proof-depends-on bullet (refining each chart by a finite affine cover of its ambient
Noetherian scheme) is not formalized; the Noetherian-scheme dependency is therefore not yet used.
It needs a restriction-to-open compatibility of formal completion that the project does not have:

- (R1) For `Y : Scheme.{u}`, `I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf Y` and `V : Y.Opens`, with the
  restricted ideal `I.inverseImage V.ι` (defined in `Hartshorne.Scheme.ReesAlgebraBlowup`), an
  isomorphism of locally ringed spaces
  `(I.inverseImage V.ι).formalCompletion ≅ I.formalCompletion.restrict
  ((Opens.map (I.infinitesimalNeighborhoodι 1).base).obj V).isOpenEmbedding`.
  Route: the `n`-th infinitesimal neighbourhood of the restricted ideal is the open subscheme of
  `Y_n` over `V` (pullback of a closed immersion along an open immersion, whose kernel is the
  inverse-image ideal), compatibly with the transition maps; then the inverse limit of sheaves
  commutes with restriction to an open.
- (R2) With (R1), a Noetherian ambient scheme's finite affine open cover
  (`AlgebraicGeometry.isNoetherian_iff_of_finite_affine_openCover`) and restriction of a
  restriction (an open-immersion range argument like the private helper), every Noetherian formal
  scheme has a finite open cover by completions of affine Noetherian schemes along closed
  subschemes.
- (R3) To phrase the atlas with `Hartshorne.IsAffineNoetherianFormalScheme` one further needs
  invariance of `formalCompletion` under isomorphisms of schemes (carrying ideal subsheaves) and
  that every quasi-coherent ideal subsheaf of `Spec A` is `specIdealSubsheaf A J` for its ideal of
  global sections `J`.
