# Morphisms over k preserve points with residue field k

Added for faithfulness item 14 (Hartshorne Ex. II.2.15(b)). No Lean states this yet; nothing in
`src/` proves preservation of residue field `k` under a morphism over `k`.

## Candidate formulation

`X Y : Scheme`, `[X.Over (Spec (.of k))]`, `[Y.Over (Spec (.of k))]`, `f : X ⟶ Y` with
`[f.IsOver (Spec (.of k))]`, `P : X`. Hypothesis: the composite
`k ≅ Γ(Spec k) → κ(unique point) → κ(P)` (equivalently `(X ↘ Spec k).residueFieldMap P`, after
identifying the residue field of `Spec k` with `k` via `Scheme.Spec.residueFieldIso` and the identification of the residue field of the zero ideal of `k` with `k`) is bijective /
an isomorphism; conclusion: the same for `f.base P`. Prefer `IsIso` / `Function.Bijective` over a
`Scheme.Hom.residueDegree … = 1` formulation, which is less direct.

## Mathlib and project declarations to use

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.residueFieldMap` and
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueFieldMap_comp` (`(f ≫ g).residueFieldMap x =
  g.residueFieldMap (f x) ≫ f.residueFieldMap x`), applied with `g` the structure morphism of
  `Y` and `f ≫ (Y ↘ Spec k) = X ↘ Spec k`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.residueFieldMap_congr` to rewrite along that equality.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Spec.residueFieldIso` for the residue field of `Spec k` at a prime (its target is the residue field of that prime, so one more identification, the residue field of the zero ideal of a field `k` is `k`, is needed).
- `RingHom.injective` (field homomorphisms are injective); then surjectivity of the outer map
  from surjectivity of the composite.
- Project prior art for the variety case: `Hartshorne.varietyAssociatedSchemeResidueConstants`
  (the map `k → κ(z)` on an associated scheme) and
  `Hartshorne.varietyAssociatedScheme_isClosed_singleton_iff` (Ex. 2.15(a)) in
  `src/Hartshorne/Scheme/VarietyClosedPoints.lean`.

The fully-faithful Lean (`src/Hartshorne/Scheme/VarietyFunctorFullyFaithful.lean`) proves that
closed points go to closed points with separating regular functions instead
(`exists_alpha_eq_of_comp_structureMorphism`), so it does not use this result; the fully-faithful
article links this one under "Informally uses".
