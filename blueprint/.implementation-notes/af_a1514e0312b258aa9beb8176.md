# The sheaf of a graded module on Proj

## Status

Partially formalized. The construction of $\widetilde{M}$ and the stalk formula
are done and sorry-free. The chart isomorphism, quasi-coherence and coherence are
not done, so nothing is recorded in the article frontmatter yet.

## Done (sorry-free, axioms only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`)

Module `Hartshorne.Projective.HomogeneousLocalizedModule` (no project imports):

- `Hartshorne.HomogeneousLocalizedModule 𝒜 ℳ x`: Hartshorne's $M_{(T)}$ for a
  submonoid $T$ of an $\mathbb{N}$-graded ring $S$ and a $\mathbb{Z}$-graded
  $S$-module $M$. It is the submodule
  `Hartshorne.homogeneousLocalizedSubmodule` of `LocalizedModule T M` made of the
  fractions $m/s$ with $m \in M_n$, $s \in S_n \cap T$. It is a module over
  Mathlib's `HomogeneousLocalization 𝒜 T`, with scalars restricted along
  $S_{(T)} \to T^{-1}S$. API: `mk`, `val`, `val_injective`, `ext`, `mk_surjective`,
  `val_add/neg/sub/zero/smul`.
- The graded action is `SetLike.GradedSMul 𝒜 ℳ`, using the scoped instance
  `Hartshorne.instVAddNatInt : VAdd ℕ ℤ` (translation). Mathlib has no
  `VAdd ℕ ℤ` instance.

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjGradedModuleSheaf`:

- `Hartshorne.projGradedModuleSheaf 𝒜 ℳ : (Proj 𝒜).Modules`. Its sections are
  locally fractional dependent functions into $M_{(\mathfrak p)}$
  (`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.isLocallyFraction`, `sheafInType`). They form a module
  over the sections of Mathlib's `Proj.structureSheaf`, acting pointwise. This is
  the same construction as Mathlib's structure sheaf of Proj and its Spec `tilde`.
- `ProjGradedModuleSheaf.stalkLinearEquiv 𝒜 ℳ x`: the stalk of $\widetilde{M}$ at
  $x$, as a module over the stalk of $\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Proj} S}$, is
  semilinearly equivalent to $M_{(x)}$. The equivalence is semilinear over
  `Proj.stalkIso'`, and `stalkLinearEquiv_germ` says the germ of $s$ goes to
  $s(x)$. Helpers: `stalkToFiber`, `sectionInBasicOpen` (the section $m/f$ on
  $D_+(f)$), `stalkToFiber_injective/surjective/smul`.

The construction needs no `DirectSum.Decomposition ℳ`. The decomposition is first
needed for the chart isomorphism (step 1 below).

Module `Hartshorne.Projective.HomogeneousLocalizedModuleAway` (algebra for steps 1 and 5):

- `decompose_smul_of_mem_add`, `decompose_smul_of_mem_of_lt`: homogeneous components of
  $c \cdot m$ for homogeneous $m$.
- `HomogeneousLocalizedModule.mapOfLE h : M_(T) →+ M_(T')` for `h : T ≤ T'`
  (`mapOfLE_mk`, `mapOfLE_smul` against `HomogeneousLocalization.mapId`), the linear version
  `mapOfLELinear` for the restricted-scalars instance `moduleOfLE`.
- Step 1 done: `isLocalizedModule_mapOfLE f_deg hd P hP hfP Q hQ`, where `hQ` characterizes
  `Q ⊆ S_(f)` by `Away.mk 𝒜 f_deg n a ha ∈ Q ↔ a ∉ P`.
- Step 5 algebra done: `finite_away f_deg hd` gives `Module.Finite (Away 𝒜 f) M_(f)` for
  Noetherian `A` and finite `M`. Proof: the submodule spanned by $\bigcup_k M_{kd}$ is finitely
  generated by homogeneous $b_l \in M_{k_l d}$, and $m/f^k = \sum_l (c_l)_{(k-k_l)d}/f^{k-k_l}
  \cdot b_l/f^{k_l}$. This avoids the EGA monomial argument.

## Remaining goals

1. **Localization lemma (algebra).** DONE (see above). For $f \in S_d$ with $d > 0$ and a homogeneous
   prime $\mathfrak p \not\ni f$, let $\mathfrak q \subset S_{(f)}$ be the preimage
   of the maximal ideal of $S_{(\mathfrak p)}$. Then the map
   $M_{(f)} \to M_{(\mathfrak p)}$, $m/f^k \mapsto m/f^k$, is a localization at
   $S_{(f)} \setminus \mathfrak q$ (`IsLocalizedModule`). The proof:
   - units: $a/f^k \notin \mathfrak q$ means $a \notin \mathfrak p$, and then
     $f^k/a$ inverts it;
   - surjectivity: $m/s = (m s^{d-1}/f^{n}) / (s^d/f^{n})$ for $m \in M_n$,
     $s \in S_n$;
   - kernel: if $u m = 0$ with $u \notin \mathfrak p$, some homogeneous component
     $u_e \notin \mathfrak p$ has $u_e m = 0$. This uses the decomposition of $M$.
     Then $u_e^d/f^e$ kills $m/f^k$.

   It needs a map between degree-zero localizations for $T \le T'$, which can be
   built with `IsLocalizedModule.lift` on `LocalizedModule`.
2. **Global sections map.** An $S_{(f)}$-linear map from $M_{(f)}$ to
   $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec} S_{(f)}, \widetilde{M}|)$, where $\widetilde{M}|$ is
   the restriction along `Proj.awayι 𝒜 f f_deg hm`, sending $m/f^k$ to the section
   $x \mapsto m/f^k$. It has to be checked against the $S_{(f)}$-action, which goes
   through `Scheme.ΓSpecIso`, the appIso of the restriction, `basicOpenToSpec_app_top`
   and `Proj.awayToSection`. The adjunction `tilde.adjunction` then gives
   $\widetilde{M_{(f)}} \to \widetilde{M}|$.
3. **Iso on stalks.** Show this morphism is an isomorphism on stalks, using:
   - the stalk of `tilde` (`IsLocalizedModule` instance for `tilde.toStalk`);
   - the stalk of a restriction along an open immersion. Mathlib seems to lack
     this API for `Scheme.Modules`; restriction along `(basicOpen f).ι` is a
     direct computation on sections;
   - `stalkLinearEquiv` and step 1;
   - the point correspondence: the image of $x \in D_+(f)$ under
     `basicOpenToSpec` (toSpecΓ, then comap along `awayToSection`) is the
     $\mathfrak q$ of step 1.

   Then an isomorphism on all stalks is an isomorphism, via the underlying
   abelian sheaves and conservativity of the forgetful functor from sheaves of
   modules. Statement target: `(Scheme.Modules.restrictFunctor (Proj.awayι 𝒜 f f_deg hm)).obj (projGradedModuleSheaf 𝒜 ℳ) ≅ tilde (ModuleCat.of (Away 𝒜 f) (HomogeneousLocalizedModule 𝒜 ℳ (Submonoid.powers f)))`.
4. **Quasi-coherence.** Transport the presentation of the tilde along the chart
   isomorphism, then move it to `M.over (basicOpen f)` with `overEquiv` and
   `overFunctorEquiv`, as in the project's proof of the affine-local criterion
   (2 ⇒ 1). Then apply `SheafOfModules.IsQuasicoherent.of_coversTop` over the
   cover by $D_+(f)$, $f$ homogeneous of positive degree (`Proj.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top'`).
5. **Coherence.** The algebra ($M_{(f)}$ finite over $S_{(f)}$) is done (`finite_away`).
   Remaining: unfold `Hartshorne.IsCoherent` on the cover by the $D_+(f)$ (affine opens),
   using quasi-coherence (step 4, which gives the tilde description on every affine open via
   `quasicoherent_affine_local_tfae`) and $\Gamma(D_+(f), \widetilde M) \cong M_{(f)}$
   (from the chart isomorphism), transported along `Proj.basicOpenIsoAway`.

## Blockers

- **Missing dependency edge.** The coherence clause needs `Hartshorne.IsCoherent`,
  which is owned by
  `schemes/modules-and-quasicoherent/coherent-affine-local-criterion`. The
  transitive dependencies of this article do not reach that article. Its
  closure is proj-structure-sheaf, proj-basic-open, proj-topology,
  sheaves-of-modules, quasicoherent-local-presentations,
  affine-quasicoherent-equivalence, tilde-gamma-adjunction,
  quasicoherent-principal-open-localization, tilde-localization and the basics
  below them. The article needs a Depends on edge to
  coherent-affine-local-criterion.
- **Mathlib gap.** No degree-zero homogeneous localization of graded modules.
  The partial Lean above supplies it. Mathlib also has no stalk API for
  restrictions of `Scheme.Modules` along open immersions, and no module analogue
  of `Proj.basicOpenIsoSpec`. Steps 1–3 are that analogue, a construction of
  several hundred lines on the scale of Mathlib's `ProjectiveSpectrum/Scheme.lean`.
