# The Picard group of projective space

## Status

No Lean yet (bounded attempt, 2026-11-09). The abstract isomorphism is immediate; the missing
piece is the identification of the twisting sheaves with divisor classes.

## Suggested target

With `𝒜 = homogeneousSubmodule (Fin (n + 1)) k` and `X = Proj 𝒜` (as in
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveSpaceClassGroup`), `hn : 0 < n`:

- `∀ L : X.Modules, L.IsInvertible → ∃! l : ℤ, Nonempty (L ≅ Hartshorne.projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 l)`;
- `Additive (Pic X.sheaf) ≃+ ℤ` sending the class of `projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 1` to `1`.

## Available pieces

- `Cl ≃ Pic`: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.weilClassEquivPicard` with local factoriality from
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isLocallyFactorial_of_isRegularLocalRing` and
  `Hartshorne.projectiveSpace_isRegular`; `Cl ≃ ℤ`: `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceClassGroupEquiv`.
  Composing gives `Pic X ≃+ ℤ` at once, but nothing yet says where `O(l)` goes.
- `Pic.mk_eq_mk_iff` (classes equal iff isomorphic), `Hartshorne.ProjTwistingSheaf.isInvertible`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.totalQuotientSheafEquivFunctionField` (`K_X(U) ≃ K(X)` for `U` containing
  the generic point) with its `_map` and `_toTotalQuotientSheaf` compatibilities.
- `CartierDivisor.toInvertibleSubmodule_surjective`: every invertible sub-`O_X`-module of `K_X`
  is `O_X(D)` for a Cartier divisor `D`; `InvertibleTotalQuotientSubmodule.exists_generator`.
- `Hartshorne.functionFieldToLocalization` (injective), `homogeneousFraction`,
  `ord_homogeneousFraction_eq_zero`, `ord_homogeneousFraction_eq_one`,
  `projectiveSpace_eq_of_coheight_eq_one`.

No tensor identity `O(l) ⊗ O(m) ≅ O(l+m)` is needed (Proposition 5.12(b) is unformalized and not
a dependency): the route below computes the class of each `O(l)` directly.

## Route (remaining goal)

Every step below reduces to existing API; the work is plumbing (estimated several hundred lines).

1. Embedding `O(l) ⟶ K_X` as `O_X`-modules. Sections of `projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 l` are locally
   fractional dependent functions; send `s` on `U ∋ η` (generic point) to
   `(totalQuotientSheafEquivFunctionField U h).symm (ψ (s η))` (and `0` on the empty open), where
   `ψ : S(l)_(0) → K(X)` sends `m / f` (`m ∈ S_{l+d}`, `f ∈ S_d`) to the rational function
   `m x₀^a / (f x₀^b)` with `a = (-l).toNat`, `b = l.toNat`; define `ψ` through `val` into
   `Localization ⊥.primeCompl` times `x₀^{-l}`, and pull back along the injective
   `functionFieldToLocalization`. Cleanest: characterize `ψ y` as the unique `κ` with
   `ι κ * x₀^b = ν (y.val) * x₀^a`, where `ι = functionFieldToLocalization` and
   `ν : LocalizedModule ⊥.primeCompl S ≃ₗ Localization ⊥.primeCompl` is `IsLocalizedModule.iso`
   for `Algebra.linearMap`; existence from `HomogeneousLocalizedModule.mk_surjective`. Linearity
   uses the pointwise action on sections (`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.sheafInType_smul_apply`) plus
   `totalQuotientSheafEquivFunctionField_toTotalQuotientSheaf`; on the empty open `K_X` is
   terminal, which gives naturality there.
2. Injective on sections: a section with `s η = 0` vanishes, since near any point it is `m / f`
   and `m / f = 0` at the zero prime forces `m = 0`.
3. Its image is an `InvertibleTotalQuotientSubmodule` isomorphic to `O(l)`; by surjectivity it is
   `O_X(D_l)` for a Cartier divisor `D_l`, so `Pic.mk O(l) = Pic.mk D_l.sheaf`.
4. `cartierToWeil D_l = l • H` for the hyperplane `H = V(x₀)`
   (`projectiveHyperplanePrimeDivisor`): at a prime divisor `P` choose `x_i ∉ P`; on `D₊(x_i)`
   the image is generated by `φ(x_i^l) = (x_i / x₀)^l` (every section of `O(l)` on `W ⊆ D₊(x_i)` is
   `r • x_i^l` with `r(x) = s(x) / x_i^l` locally fractional), so a local equation `f` of `D_l`
   there satisfies `f⁻¹ = u (x_i / x₀)^l` with `u` a unit (both generate the same invertible
   submodule, compare the proof of `CartierDivisor.toInvertibleSubmodule_injective`); then
   `CartierDivisor.weilCoeff_eq` and `ord_homogeneousFraction_eq_one`/`_eq_zero` give order `l`
   at `P = H` and `0` otherwise.
5. Hence `O(l)` goes to `l` under `Pic ≃ Cl ≃ ℤ`; existence and uniqueness of `l` follow from
   `Pic.mk_eq_mk_iff` and injectivity of the composite.
