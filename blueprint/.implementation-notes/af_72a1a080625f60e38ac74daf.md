# Linear systems

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.LinearSystem`.

- `Hartshorne.globalSectionsOverField q L`: `Γ(X, L)` as a `k`-vector space through
  `((Scheme.ΓSpecIso k).inv ≫ q.appTop)`, the same scalar map as Theorem 5.19
  (`Hartshorne.finiteDimensional_globalSections_of_projective`) and Proposition 7.7(c)
  (`Scheme.globalScalar`).
- `Hartshorne.LinearSystem q L`: structure with fields `isInvertible`, `sections` (a
  `k`-submodule `V`), `finiteDimensional`.
- `LinearSystem.divisors`: Cartier divisors `D` with `IsDivisorOfZeros L s D` for some nonzero
  `s ∈ V`. `isEffective_of_mem_divisors`, `exists_mem_divisors` (integral `X`);
  `divisor_eq_iff` (Proposition 7.7(c): same divisor iff `s' = c • s`, `c : kˣ`).
- `LinearSystem.complete hq hL` (`V = ⊤`, finite by Theorem 5.19; coherence of `L` from
  finite presentation of a finite locally free sheaf); `mem_divisors_complete_iff` (Cartier
  `|D₀|` on an integral projective `X`) and `mem_divisors_complete_iff_weil` (Weil `|D₀|` on a
  regular integral projective `X`).
- Partial order by inclusion of `sections` (`le_iff`, `divisors_mono`, `le_complete`).
- `LinearSystem.dim : ℤ := finrank V - 1`, `dim_complete`, `dim_mono`.
- `LinearSystem.IsBasePoint` (every section of `V` vanishes at `x`);
  `mem_support_baseLocusIdeal_iff` (agreement with `Hartshorne.baseLocusIdeal` of the
  finite-dimensional linear system module), `isBasePoint_iff` (integral `X`: `x` lies in the
  support of every divisor of the system, Hartshorne's definition).
- `LinearSystem.ofFamily`, `mem_ofFamily_sections_iff`: the span of a family is
  `Hartshorne.linearSystem q s`.
- `LinearSystem.restrictionMap i q L` (`k`-linear `s ↦ i^* s`), `LinearSystem.trace 𝔡 i`
  (image of `V`), `mem_trace_sections_iff`, `pullbackGlobalSection_ne_zero_iff` (reduced `Y`).
- `Hartshorne.IsDivisorIntersection i hD E`: `E = D·Y`, i.e. `E` effective with
  `idealSheafData hE = (idealSheafData hD).comap i` (the locally principal subscheme `D ∩ Y`);
  `IsDivisorIntersection.unique`.
- `LinearSystem.mem_trace_divisors_iff` (integral `X`, `Y`, any morphism `i`): the divisors of
  the trace are exactly the `D·Y` for `D` in the system with `range i ⊄ Supp D`.
- Bridge: `Scheme.CartierDivisor.IsDivisorOfZeros.idealSheafData_eq_invertibleZeroIdeal`
  (the ideal of an effective `(s)₀` is the zero-scheme ideal of `s`), with corollary
  `IsDivisorOfZeros.mem_support_iff`, and `Scheme.CartierDivisor.exists_isDivisorOfZeros_isEffective`.

## Scope relative to the book

Hartshorne works on a nonsingular projective variety over an algebraically closed field and,
for the trace, with a closed immersion of nonsingular projective varieties. The Lean definitions
need only a scheme over a field; the trace is defined for any morphism and the trace-divisor
comparison needs only `X`, `Y` integral. `D·Y` is formalized as the effective Cartier divisor
whose subscheme is the scheme-theoretic inverse image of `D`; the ambient hypersurface version
(`Hartshorne.projectiveIntersectionDivisor`, Exercise 6.2(a)) is not used.

## Proof notes

The bridge builds, from a trivialization `ψ : L|_U ≅ 𝒪_U`, the trivializing section `ψ⁻¹(1)`;
if `s|_U = g ψ⁻¹(1)` then `ψ(s|_U) = g`, so both ideals are `(g)` on affine opens inside `U`
(`idealSheafData_ideal_eq_span`, `invertibleZeroIdeal_ideal_eq_span`). The trace comparison is
`invertibleZeroIdeal_pullbackGlobalSection` plus `eq_zero_of_forall_notMem_nonvanishingLocus`.
