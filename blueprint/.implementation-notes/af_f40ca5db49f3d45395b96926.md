# The class group of a nonsingular quadric surface

## Partial progress (not yet recorded)

Model: `Hartshorne.quadricSurface k` is `Proj` of the Segre ring of `ℙ¹ × ℙ¹`, realised as the
monoid algebra over the exponent pairs `(e, g)` of bidegree `(d, d)`, graded by `d`
(`quadricExponents`, `QuadricRing`, `quadricGrading`). The four degree-one monomials `sᵢuⱼ`
(`quadricChart`) are the coordinates `x, y, z, w` with `xy = zw`.

Checked, sorry-free, axioms `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`, `unusedArguments` clean:

- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurface`: `quadricChartAwayEquiv` (each chart `D₊(sᵢuⱼ)` has
  coordinate ring `k[X₀, X₁]`), `quadricSurface_iSup_basicOpen`, `quadricSurface_isRegular`,
  `quadricSurface_isIntegral`, `quadricSurface_isNoetherian`,
  `quadricSurface_satisfiesConditionStar`.
- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceClassGroup`: the lines `L₁ = {s₀ = 0}` and `L₂ = {u₀ = 0}`
  (`quadricLineOne`, `quadricLineTwo`, prime divisors `quadricLineOneDivisor`,
  `quadricLineTwoDivisor`), `coheight_quadricLineOne`, `coheight_quadricLineTwo` (Krull's
  principal ideal theorem on the chart `D₊(s₁u₁)`), `eq_quadricLine_of_notMem_basicOpen` (the
  only prime divisors off the chart `D₊(s₀u₀)` are `L₁`, `L₂`), `quadricClassGroupHom`,
  `quadricClassGroupHom_surjective`, `quadricClassGroupHom_injective`,
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv`, `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineOne` (`(1, 0)`),
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineTwo` (`(0, 1)`),
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineOne_add_lineTwo` (the divisor `L₁ + L₂` has class
  `(1, 1)`). This is not a hyperplane-class normalization: nothing in the Lean defines the
  divisor that a hyperplane of `ℙ³_k` (even `s₀u₀ = 0`) cuts on `Q`, so the hyperplane class is
  not yet computed. Set-theoretically, `quadricLine_specializes_of_notMem_basicOpen` shows the
  zero set of `s₀u₀` is `L₁ ∪ L₂`, nothing about multiplicities.

The proof is Hartshorne's: the chart `D₊(s₀u₀) = Q - L₁ - L₂` is a UFD plane, which gives
surjectivity; injectivity uses that a rational function with divisor supported on `L₁ ∪ L₂` is a
unit of `k[X₀, X₁]`, hence a constant, and constants (pulled back along `Q → Spec 𝒬₀`) have
trivial divisor. It does not use the restriction exact sequence itself, `𝔸¹`-invariance, `Cl ℙⁿ`
or the Segre embedding, though it uses helpers from the modules of the first two (see Edges
below). Hartshorne's own surjectivity route is the open-restriction sequence plus `Cl 𝔸² = 0`;
switching to it (via the open-restriction module) would remove a hand-written surjectivity proof.

## Remaining goal

The statement normalizes the isomorphism by the rulings and the hyperplane class. Still missing:

1. Every line of each ruling has class `(1, 0)` resp. `(0, 1)`, not only `L₁`, `L₂`.
2. The class of a hyperplane section is `(1, 1)`. Nothing is done here yet: there is no
   definition of the divisor a hyperplane cuts on `Q`, not even for `s₀u₀ = 0`; only the class
   of the divisor `L₁ + L₂` is computed.
3. Both identifications and the pullback form of the isomorphism:
   (a) `quadricSurface k ≅ ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k` over `k`, with projections `p₁, p₂ : Q → ℙ¹_k`;
   (b) `quadricSurface k ≅ V(xy - zw)`, the closed subscheme of `ℙ³_k`;
   (c) pullback maps `pᵢ^* : Cl ℙ¹_k → Cl Q` (Weil-divisor pullback along the flat projections,
   or the Picard pullback of Exercise II.6.8(a) with the `Cl ≅ Pic` comparison on the locally
   factorial `Q` and `ℙ¹`);
   (d) the isomorphism `Cl ℙ¹_k × Cl ℙ¹_k → Cl Q` is `(a, b) ↦ p₁^* a + p₂^* b`, i.e. under
   `Cl ℙ¹ ≅ ℤ` it is the inverse of `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv` (so `p₁^*` of a point is
   the class of a fibre of `p₁`, a ruling line of class `(1, 0)`, and symmetrically).

## Why 1 and 2 are not one-step lemmas here

Both need the divisor of a rational function whose zeros and poles are not all visible in one
chart, e.g. `s₀u₀ / s₁u₀` (divisor `L₁ - {s₁ = 0}`) or `ℓ / s₀u₀` for a linear form `ℓ`. The
present proof only ever pulls rational functions back to the single chart `D₊(s₀u₀)` (and uses the
global constants). What is missing is a comparison of rational functions across charts: for a
homogeneous fraction `g / h` of the Segre ring, its pullback to every chart `D₊(sᵢuⱼ)` is the
dehomogenized fraction in `k[X₀, X₁]` (germs of `awayToSection` sections at the generic point,
`Proj.awayι` restricted to basic opens, `Proj.awayMap_awayToSection`). With that tool, order of
vanishing of `g / h` at any prime divisor is computed on a chart. The roadmap revision added this
tool as the article af_4123cecf1fd671d5bed8d63c, and goal 3 as the article
af_84a1fe08857c7719e03e20fc. Goal 1 also needs the line over
`[α : β] ∈ ℙ¹` as a prime divisor (homogeneous prime = kernel of `(e, g) ↦ α^{e₀} β^{e₁} u^g`),
and goal 2 needs a notion of the divisor cut on `Q` by a hyperplane (Exercise II.6.2(a), which
exists for morphisms to `ℙⁿ_k` and would apply to `Q → ℙ³_k` once 3 gives that morphism).

## Statement versus the present Lean

The article statement is unchanged and asks for more than the checked Lean:

1. "the two ruling classes are (1,0) and (0,1)" means every line of each
   ruling, not only `L₁`, `L₂`. Missing: the line over `[α : β] ∈ ℙ¹` as a
   prime divisor and its class, via the homogeneous-fraction article (for
   example the divisor of `s₀u₀ / s₁u₀` is `L₁ - {s₁ = 0}`).
2. "the hyperplane class is (1,1)" means the class of a hyperplane section. The
   Lean computes only the class of the divisor `L₁ + L₂`; it never defines the
   divisor cut on `Q` by a hyperplane (not even `s₀u₀ = 0`). Missing: that
   divisor. The Lean
   of Exercise II.6.2(a) (`Hartshorne.projectiveIntersectionDivisor`, for any
   morphism from a `(*)` scheme to `ℙⁿ_k` over any field, although its article
   is phrased over an algebraically closed field) would apply along the closed
   immersion `Q → ℙ³_k` from the split article; using it would need an edge to
   the ambient-hypersurface-divisor article. Then compare with `L₁ + L₂`
   through `ℓ / s₀u₀` and the homogeneous-fraction article.
3. "`Q ⊆ ℙ³` the quadric `xy = zw`, identified with ℙ¹ × ℙ¹ ... pullback
   from the two factors" needs goal 3 above in full: both identifications
   (with `ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k` and with `V(xy - zw) ⊆ ℙ³_k`), the pullback maps
   `pᵢ^*`, and the isomorphism `Cl ℙ¹ × Cl ℙ¹ → Cl Q` shown to be
   `p₁^* + p₂^*`. No Weil-divisor pullback along the projections exists in the
   roadmap; one route is the recorded Picard pullback (Exercise II.6.8(a))
   with the locally factorial `Cl ≅ Pic` comparison, which would need edges
   added (a Roadmap return).
