# The class group of a nonsingular quadric surface

## Partial progress (not yet recorded)

Model: `Hartshorne.quadricSurface k` is `Proj` of the Segre ring of `ℙ¹ × ℙ¹`, realised as the
monoid algebra over the exponent pairs `(e, g)` of bidegree `(d, d)`, graded by `d`
(`quadricExponents`, `QuadricRing`, `quadricGrading`). The four degree-one monomials `sᵢuⱼ`
(`quadricChart`) are the coordinates `x, y, z, w` with `xy = zw`.

  coordinate ring `k[X₀, X₁]`), `quadricSurface_iSup_basicOpen`, `quadricSurface_isRegular`,
  `quadricSurface_isIntegral`, `quadricSurface_isNoetherian`,
  `quadricSurface_satisfiesConditionStar`.
- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceClassGroup`: the lines `L₁ = {s₀ = 0}` and `L₂ = {u₀ = 0}`
  (`quadricLineOne`, `quadricLineTwo`, prime divisors `quadricLineOneDivisor`,
  `quadricLineTwoDivisor`), `coheight_quadricLineOne`, `coheight_quadricLineTwo` (Krull's
  principal ideal theorem on the chart `D₊(s₁u₁)`), `eq_quadricLine_of_notMem_basicOpen` (the
  only prime divisors off the chart `D₊(s₀u₀)` are `L₁`, `L₂`), `quadricClassGroupHom`,
  `quadricClassGroupHom_surjective`, `quadricClassGroupHom_injective`,
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv`, `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineOne` (`(1, 0)`),
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineTwo` (`(0, 1)`),
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_lineOne_add_lineTwo` (`L₁ + L₂`, the section `s₀u₀ = 0`, is
  `(1, 1)`).

The proof is Hartshorne's: the chart `D₊(s₀u₀) = Q - L₁ - L₂` is a UFD plane, which gives
surjectivity; injectivity uses that a rational function with divisor supported on `L₁ ∪ L₂` is a
unit of `k[X₀, X₁]`, hence a constant, and constants (pulled back along `Q → Spec 𝒬₀`) have
trivial divisor. It does not use the restriction exact sequence, `𝔸¹`-invariance, `Cl ℙⁿ` or the
Segre embedding.

## Remaining goal

The statement normalizes the isomorphism by the rulings and the hyperplane class. Still missing:

1. Every line of each ruling has class `(1, 0)` resp. `(0, 1)`, not only `L₁`, `L₂`.
2. The class of an arbitrary hyperplane section is `(1, 1)` (currently only `s₀u₀ = 0`).
3. "Pullback from the two factors": an identification of `quadricSurface k` with
   `ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k` or with the closed subscheme `V(xy - zw)` of `ℙ³_k`.

## Why 1 and 2 are not one-step lemmas here

Both need the divisor of a rational function whose zeros and poles are not all visible in one
chart, e.g. `s₀u₀ / s₁u₀` (divisor `L₁ - {s₁ = 0}`) or `ℓ / s₀u₀` for a linear form `ℓ`. The
present proof only ever pulls rational functions back to the single chart `D₊(s₀u₀)` (and uses the
global constants). What is missing is a comparison of rational functions across charts: for a
homogeneous fraction `g / h` of the Segre ring, its pullback to every chart `D₊(sᵢuⱼ)` is the
dehomogenized fraction in `k[X₀, X₁]` (germs of `awayToSection` sections at the generic point,
`Proj.awayι` restricted to basic opens, `Proj.awayMap_awayToSection`). With that tool, order of
vanishing of `g / h` at any prime divisor is computed on a chart. Goal 1 also needs the line over
`[α : β] ∈ ℙ¹` as a prime divisor (homogeneous prime = kernel of `(e, g) ↦ α^{e₀} β^{e₁} u^g`),
and goal 2 needs a notion of the divisor cut on `Q` by a hyperplane (Exercise II.6.2(a), which
exists for morphisms to `ℙⁿ_k` and would apply to `Q → ℙ³_k` once 3 gives that morphism).

## Split proposal

- New article "The split quadric surface is ℙ¹ × ℙ¹" (Hartshorne I, Exercise 2.15, and the setup of
  II.6.6.1): over a field `k`, the Segre morphism `ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k → ℙ³_k` is a closed immersion
  onto `V(xy - zw)`, whose homogeneous coordinate ring is the Segre ring. Depends on the Segre
  embedding article and the relative Segre morphism; it would record a graded isomorphism
  `k[x, y, z, w]/(xy - zw) ≅ QuadricRing k` and the identification of `quadricSurface k` with the
  image. Edge: quadric-surface-class-group depends on it.
- New article "Divisors of homogeneous fractions on Proj of a graded domain" (supporting the
  computations in II.6.4 and II.6.6.1): for a graded domain `S` generated in degree one whose charts
  satisfy `(*)`, the order of `g / h` at a prime divisor is computed on any chart containing it by
  the dehomogenized fraction. It would serve goals 1 and 2 here; `ProjectiveSpaceClassGroup` has
  the polynomial-ring special case.
