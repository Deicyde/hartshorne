# The class group of a nonsingular quadric surface

## Partial progress (not yet recorded)

Model: `Hartshorne.quadricSurface k` is `Proj` of the Segre ring of `ℙ¹ × ℙ¹`, realised as the
monoid algebra over the exponent pairs `(e, g)` of bidegree `(d, d)`, graded by `d`
(`quadricExponents`, `QuadricRing`, `quadricGrading`). The four degree-one monomials `sᵢuⱼ`
(`quadricChart`) are the coordinates `x, y, z, w = s₀u₀, s₁u₁, s₀u₁, s₁u₀` with `xy = zw`.

Checked, sorry-free, axioms `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`, `unusedArguments` clean:

  coordinate ring `k[X₀, X₁]`), `quadricSurface_iSup_basicOpen`, `quadricSurface_isRegular`,
  `quadricSurface_isIntegral`, `quadricSurface_isNoetherian`,
  `quadricSurface_satisfiesConditionStar`.
- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceClassGroup`: the lines `L₁ = {s₀ = 0}` and `L₂ = {u₀ = 0}`
  (`quadricLineOne`, `quadricLineTwo`, prime divisors `quadricLineOneDivisor`,
  `quadricLineTwoDivisor`), `coheight_quadricLineOne`, `coheight_quadricLineTwo` (Krull's
  principal ideal theorem on the chart `D₊(s₁u₁)`), `eq_quadricLine_of_notMem_basicOpen` (the
  only prime divisors off the chart `D₊(s₀u₀)` are `L₁`, `L₂`), `quadricClassGroupHom`,
  `quadricClassGroupHom_surjective`, `quadricClassGroupHom_injective`,

- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceSegre` (shared with the split article
  af_84a1fe08857c7719e03e20fc): `quadricSurfaceι : Q ⟶ Proj k[x, y, z, w]`, the closed immersion
  `Proj.map` of the graded surjection `x, y, z, w ↦ s₀u₀, s₁u₁, s₀u₁, s₁u₀`
  (`quadricCoordinateHom`), with kernel `(xy - zw)` (`ker_quadricCoordinateAlgHom`).
- `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceHyperplane`, **the hyperplane class**:
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_hyperplaneSection`: for every nonzero linear form `ℓ`, the
  divisor `H · Q` cut on `Q` by `H = V(ℓ)` (`projectiveIntersectionDivisor quadricSurfaceι`,
  Exercise II.6.2(a)) has class `(1, 1)`. Steps: `quadricHyperplaneSection_apply` (the
  coefficient at `x` is the order of `ℓ / X_i` for any coordinate `X_i` not vanishing at `x`),
  `quadricHyperplaneSection_X` (`(x = 0) · Q = L₁ + L₂`, from
  `ord_quadricLineOne_projHomogeneousFraction` and `ord_quadricLineTwo_projHomogeneousFraction`:
  `x / w` resp. `x / z` is a chart coordinate generating the line's height-one prime), and
  `quadricHyperplaneSection_eq_add_principalDivisor` (`H · Q = (x = 0) · Q + div(ℓ / x)`);
  `quadricSurface_not_subset_hyperplane` (no hyperplane contains `Q`, from the kernel).
- Same module, **the rulings**: `quadricRulingOneDivisor hα β` (`α ≠ 0`), the line
  `{[α : β]} × ℙ¹ = V(βs₀ - αs₁)` (prime divisor over the prime `(β - α s₁/s₀)` of the chart
  `D₊(s₀u₀)`; `quadricRulingFormOne_mem_rulingOneDivisor`: the forms `βs₀uⱼ - αs₁uⱼ` vanish on
  it), with `quadricRulingOneDivisor_sub_lineOne` (`= L₁ + div((βs₀ - αs₁)/s₀)`) and
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_rulingOne` (class `(1, 0)`); symmetrically
  `quadricRulingTwoDivisor`, `quadricRulingTwoDivisor_sub_lineTwo`,
  `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv_rulingTwo` (class `(0, 1)`). The lines over `[0 : 1]` are
  `L₁`, `L₂`. These are the lines over `k`-points of `ℙ¹`; over a non-closed field a fibre over
  a closed point of degree `e` has class `(e, 0)`, which is what `p₁^*` would give.

The class-group proof is Hartshorne's: the chart `D₊(s₀u₀) = Q - L₁ - L₂` is a UFD plane, which
gives surjectivity; injectivity uses that a rational function with divisor supported on `L₁ ∪ L₂`
is a unit of `k[X₀, X₁]`, hence a constant, and constants (pulled back along `Q → Spec 𝒬₀`) have
trivial divisor. It does not use the restriction exact sequence itself, `𝔸¹`-invariance, `Cl ℙⁿ`
or the Segre embedding, though it uses helpers from the modules of the first two (see Edges
below). Hartshorne's own surjectivity route is the open-restriction sequence plus `Cl 𝔸² = 0`;
switching to it (via the open-restriction module) would remove a hand-written surjectivity proof.

   (c) pullback maps `pᵢ^* : Cl ℙ¹_k → Cl Q`;
   (d) the isomorphism `Cl ℙ¹_k × Cl ℙ¹_k → Cl Q` is `(a, b) ↦ p₁^* a + p₂^* b`, i.e. under
   `Cl ℙ¹ ≅ ℤ` it is the inverse of `quadricSurfaceClassGroupEquiv` (so `p₁^*` of a point is
   the class of a fibre of `p₁`, a ruling line of class `(1, 0)`, and symmetrically).
   For (c) no Weil-divisor pullback along flat morphisms exists in the roadmap or the Lean
   (`affineLinePullback` only covers `𝔸¹_X → X`); the alternative is the recorded Picard
   pullback (Exercise II.6.8(a)) with the locally factorial `Cl ≅ Pic` comparison. Either way
   this is a missing prerequisite for the Roadmap.
