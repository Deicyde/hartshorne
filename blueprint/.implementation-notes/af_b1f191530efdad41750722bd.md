## Lean gaps (planned route)

1. `𝒪_X(0) ≅ 𝒪_X`: show that `twistHom 0 1` is an isomorphism, stalkwise. At each stalk, every
   element of `S(0)_(x)` is `c · 1/1`, and `c · 1/1 = 0` forces `c = 0`. Drafted (not yet
   compiled) as `twistZeroIso`, with `untwist n : F(n) ⊗ 𝒪_X(-n) ⟶ F`, `invSection` (`1 / g` in
   `𝒪_X(-k)` over `D₊(g)`) and `fracSection` (`m / g ∈ F(D₊(g))` for `m ∈ Γ(X, F(k))`,
   `g ∈ S_k`).
2. Value identities for `fracSection`: `(t m) / (t g) = (m / g)|_{D₊(tg)}`, additivity, and
   `(b / h) · (m / g) = (b m) / (h g)`. These reduce, through `tensor₃_hom_ext`, to pointwise
   identities of `twistMulHom` values.
3. The evaluation morphism `β : Γ_*(F)~ ⟶ F`. On a section `s` of `Γ_*(F)~` over `U`, glue the
   local values `m / g` of `s` using the sheaf property of `F`. Independence of the chosen
   representation follows from item 2: equality in a homogeneous localization at a prime gives
   a homogeneous `h ∉ 𝔭` (a homogeneous component of the witness) with `h g' m = h g m'`. Then
   check linearity and naturality.
4. Translations needed to apply Lemma 5.14 with `L = 𝒪_X(1)` and `f ∈ S_1`:
   - `𝒪_X(1)^{⊗ n} ≅ 𝒪_X(n)`, sending `f^{⊗ n}` to `f^n`;
   - the nonvanishing locus of `f / 1` is `D₊(f)`;
   - `sectionPowSMul` corresponds to the `S`-action on `Γ_*(F)`;
   - the finite affine cover `D₊(fᵢ)` by generators `fᵢ ∈ S_1`, with `𝒪_X(1)` trivial on each
     piece and quasi-compact overlaps.
5. `β` is an isomorphism, stalkwise as in `isIso_twistTensorHom`. The stalk of `Γ_*(F)~` at `x`
   is `Γ_*(F)_(x)` (`stalkLinearEquiv`). Surjectivity uses 5.14(b) on `D₊(f) ∋ x`, `f ∈ S_1`.
   Injectivity uses 5.14(a) with `L = 𝒪_X(e)` and the section `g h`.

Items 3 and 5 are each substantial. Item 4 is a set of separate bridging lemmas.
