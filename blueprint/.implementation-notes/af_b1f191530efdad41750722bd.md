# Reconstruction from graded global sections

## Status

Proved, pending review. Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjGammaStarReconstruction`, namespace
`Hartshorne.GammaStarReconstruction`.

- `gammaStarGrading F`: the grading of `GammaStar F = ⨁ₙ Γ(X, F(n))` by its summands (the range of
  `DirectSum.of _ n`), with `SetLike.GradedSMul` from `smul_of`.
- `gammaStarTilde F`: `projGradedModuleSheaf 𝒜 (gammaStarGrading F)`, i.e. `Γ_*(F)~`.
- `gammaStarEval F : gammaStarTilde F ⟶ F`: the evaluation morphism.
- `isIso_gammaStarEval hs hgen F`: Proposition 5.15, for `s : Finset A` with `s ⊆ S_1` and
  `Algebra.adjoin (𝒜 0) s = ⊤` (Hartshorne's "finitely generated by `S_1` as an
  `S_0`-algebra") and `[F.IsQuasicoherent]`.
- `gammaStarIso hs hgen F : gammaStarTilde F ≅ F`, with `hom = gammaStarEval F`.

## Construction of the evaluation morphism

- `isTrivializingSection_twistSection`: for `g ∈ S_d`, `g / 1` trivializes `𝒪_X(d)` on `D₊(g)`
  (explicit inverse `divFraction`, `t ↦ t / g`).
- `secMap F d g : F ⟶ F ⊗ 𝒪_X(d)` (`s ↦ s ⊗ g / 1`) is bijective on sections over opens of
  `D₊(g)` (`secMap_app_bijective`, via `tensorSectionHom_app_bijective` from the Serre
  global-generation module, an unrecorded helper there).
- `frac F d g m ∈ F(D₊(g))` for `m ∈ Γ(X, F(d))`: the preimage of `m`; identities
  `frac_res_mulGlobal` (`m / g = a m / a g`), `frac_add`, `frac_res_mulGlobal_eq_smul`
  (`c m / h g = (c / h) · (m / g)`, with `ringFrac` the section `c / h` of `𝒪_X`).
- `fracM` (through degree components of `Γ_*(F)`), `decompose_smul_eq_zero` (a homogeneous
  component of a witness `u ∉ 𝔭` with `u D = 0` also kills `D`), `germ_fracM_eq_of_mk_eq`, then
  `stalkEval` (`Γ_*(F)_(x) → F_x`), `stalkEval_add`, `stalkEval_smul`.
- `existsUnique_section_of_germ` (gluing prescribed germs), `evalSection`, `gammaStarEval`
  via `Ringed.modulesHomMk`.

## Proof of the isomorphism

- `stalk_gammaStarEval`, `isIso_gammaStarEval_of_bijective`: stalkwise criterion.
- `mem_nonvanishingLocus_twistSection`: under `S = S_0[S_1]`, the nonvanishing locus of
  `h / 1 ∈ Γ(X, 𝒪_X(e))` is `D₊(h)`.
- `powMap e N : 𝒪_X(e)^{⊗ N} ⟶ 𝒪_X(eN)` (recursion through `succIsoX`/`zeroIsoX`, scheme-typed
  copies of `Ringed.moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`/`ZeroIso`: with the generic instances `rw`
  cannot match, hence the copies and some `erw`), `powMap_tprod`, `powTwistMap`,
  `powTwistMap_sectionPowSMul` (`h^N s ↦ s ⊗ h^N / 1`).
- `stalkEval_injective` uses `exists_sectionPowSMul_eq_zero` (Lemma 5.14(a)) for `F(n)` and
  `L = 𝒪_X(e)`; `stalkEval_surjective` uses `exists_sectionPowSMul_extension` (Lemma 5.14(b))
  on the cover `D₊(xᵢ)` by the generators `xᵢ ∈ S_1`.

## Divergence from the printed proof

Hartshorne compares `Γ(D₊(f), F)` with `Γ_*(F)_(f)` for `f ∈ S_1` (Lemma 5.14 with
`L = 𝒪_X(1)`) and concludes on each chart through `(M~)|_{D₊(f)} ≅ (M_(f))~` (Proposition 5.11).
The Lean makes the same comparison on stalks and applies Lemma 5.14 to `L = 𝒪_X(e)` and the
section `h / 1` for an arbitrary homogeneous basic open `D₊(h)`; it uses the stalk formula of
`M~`, not the chart isomorphism.
