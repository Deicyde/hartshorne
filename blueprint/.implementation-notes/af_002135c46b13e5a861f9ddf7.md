# Formal spectrum is invariant under adic completion

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalSpectrumCompletion`.

- `Hartshorne.spfCompletionIso A I : Spf A I ≅ Spf (AdicCompletion I A) (I.map (algebraMap A _))`,
  the explicit isomorphism of locally ringed spaces.
- `Hartshorne.spec_map_algebraMap_spfCompletionIso_hom_base`: on points it lies over `Spec A`.
- `Hartshorne.spfCompletionIso_hom_c_app_π`: on sections, followed by the `n`-th projection of the
  limit sheaf, it is the `n`-th projection followed by pullback along
  `Hartshorne.spfCompletionNeighborhoodIso A I n`.
- `Hartshorne.spf_completion_invariant`: the bundled statement (existence of an isomorphism lying
  over `Spec A` on points).
- `Hartshorne.spfCompletionNeighborhoodIso`, `Hartshorne.spfCompletionNeighborhoodIso_hom_comp_succ`,
  `Hartshorne.spfCompletionNeighborhoodIso_hom_comp_ι_comp`: the isomorphisms
  `Y_(n+1) ≅ Y'_(n+1)` of the infinitesimal neighbourhoods, their compatibility with the
  transition inclusions, and that they lie over `Spec A`.

General tool (no completion involved), in namespace `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf`:
`formalCompletionIsoOfNeighborhoodIso` builds `X̂_Y ≅ X̂'_{Y'}` from isomorphisms
`φ_n : Y_(n+1) ≅ Y'_(n+1)` compatible with `infinitesimalNeighborhoodSucc`; it is `φ_0` on spaces
(`formalCompletionIsoOfNeighborhoodIso_hom_base_apply`, by `rfl`) and the induced isomorphism of
limit sheaves on sections (`formalCompletionIsoOfNeighborhoodIso_hom_c_app_π`). Helpers:
`neighborhoodIso_comp_infinitesimalNeighborhoodFromOne`, `neighborhoodIsoBase`,
`functor_obj_map_neighborhoodIso`, `neighborhoodIsoApp`, `neighborhoodIsoCompare`,
`neighborhoodIsoSheafIso`, `neighborhoodIsoSheafIso_hom_π`.

## Route

1. `Y_(n+1) ≅ Spec (B ⧸ J ^ (n + 1))` over `Spec B` (private `specNeighborhoodIso`): the ideal data
   of `(specIdealSubsheaf B J) ^ (n + 1)` is the kernel of `Spec (B ⧸ J ^ (n + 1)) ⟶ Spec B`,
   checked on global sections via `Scheme.IdealSheafData.ext_of_isAffine`, `Scheme.Hom.ker_apply`
   and `Scheme.ΓSpecIso_naturality`.
2. `Â ⧸ Î ^ (n + 1) ≃+* A ⧸ I ^ (n + 1)` from `exists_adicCompletionQuotientRingEquiv`
   (adic-completion-quotients) via `Classical.choose`, after `Ideal.map_pow`.
3. Compatibility in `n` is checked after composing with the closed immersion into `Spec Â` (a
   monomorphism), reducing to `factor ∘ evalₐ (n+2) = evalₐ (n+1)`.
4. The general tool then gives the limit-sheaf isomorphism, following the pattern of
   `formalCompletionIsoOfSupportEq` (comparison of the diagrams by `NatTrans.ofOpSequence`,
   pushforward preserving limits, `PresheafedSpace.isoOfComponents`).

## Friction

- `rw [Scheme.Hom.appLE_comp_appLE]` inside the naturality goal hit a kernel deterministic timeout;
  a fully generic lemma (private `appLE_comp_appLE_eq`) applied by `exact` avoids it.
- `IsIso (f.app U)` for an isomorphism `f` was not found by instance search when `U` is an image
  open `IsOpenMap.functor.obj W`; it is obtained from `PresheafedSpace.c_isIso_of_iso`.
- `PreservesLimit _ (sheafToPresheaf _ _)` was not synthesized for these diagrams; the private
  `sheafLimitIso` goes through `PreservesLimitsOfShape.preservesLimit` explicitly.
