# Implementation note: smooth-hypersurfaces-dense

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.SmoothHypersurfacesDense`, namespace
`Hartshorne.SmoothHypersurface` (shared with the Example 8.20.3 module
`CanonicalSheafSmoothHypersurface`; no names overlap).

## Statement and model

`Hartshorne.SmoothHypersurface.smooth_hypersurfaces_dense` (`[IsAlgClosed k]`, `2 ≤ n`, `1 ≤ d`):
there is `U ⊆ projectiveSpaceScheme k N`, `N = Veronese.dupleN n d`, open and dense, such that for
every closed point `H` and every `a ≠ 0` with `BertiniIncidence.Represents a H`,
`H ∈ U ↔ V(f_a).IsRegular ∧ IsIntegral V(f_a)`.

- `|dH|` is the projective space of coefficient vectors, coordinates indexed by
  `Fin (dupleN n d + 1)`, identified with the degree-`d` monomials through `Veronese.dupleEquiv`
  (the Veronese module's enumeration). Closed points `[a]` use `Represents`, as in Theorem 8.18.
- `dupleMonomial k n d j = x^{mⱼ}`, `hypersurfacePolynomial a = ∑ⱼ aⱼ x^{mⱼ} : 𝒮 d`.
- `hypersurfaceIdeal f` (for `f : 𝒮 d`, `ℙⁿ_k = Proj 𝒮`): `invertibleZeroIdeal` of the global
  section `hypersurfaceSection f = twistSection d f ⊤` of `projTwistingSheaf 𝒮 d`. Justification:
  `hypersurfaceIdeal_ideal_basicOpen` (ideal on `D₊(h)`, `h ∈ S_d`, `d > 0`, is
  `span {ringFrac d f h}` = `(f / h)`) and `mem_support_hypersurfaceIdeal_iff` (support = primes
  containing `f`). This is the same zero-scheme model as linear-system-api; the Example 8.20.3
  module uses the divisor of zeros, bridged by
  `Scheme.CartierDivisor.IsDivisorOfZeros.idealSheafData_eq_invertibleZeroIdeal`.
- "Nonsingular variety" = `IsIntegral ∧ IsRegular` of the subscheme.

## Proof route

1. `dupleMorphism k n d : Proj 𝒮 ⟶ ℙᴺ_{Spec k}`: the morphism of Theorem 7.1(b)
   (`existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`) for the sections `x^m` of `𝒪(d)`
   (`isGeneratedBySections_dupleSections`: `xᵢ^d` trivializes `𝒪(d)` on `D₊(xᵢ)`), over the
   structure morphism `baseMorphism k n` (an abbreviation of
   `BertiniIncidence.dualProjectiveSpaceStructure k n`, used instead of
   `projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism` so that no edge to the Chow-cycle article recording the latter
   is needed).
2. `hyperplaneSectionIdeal_dupleMorphism`: `hyperplaneSectionIdeal dupleMorphism a =
   hypersurfaceIdeal (hypersurfacePolynomial a)` via `invertibleZeroIdeal_map_iso`,
   `iso_app_linearSection` and `kConst_smul_hypersurfaceSection`.
3. `isClosedImmersion_dupleMorphism` (`0 < d`): Proposition 7.2 in polynomial form
   (`isClosedImmersion_iff_isAffineOpen_and_surjective_chartSectionPolynomialMap`). On
   `X_{x^m} = D₊(x^m)` the ratios `x^{m'}/x^m` and constants hit `ringFrac`; every `a / g^r`,
   `a ∈ S_{rd}`, is reached by induction on `r` (`homogeneous_induction`, splitting monomials with
   `Veronese.exists_le_degree_eq`), and `Proj.basicOpenIsoAway` gives all sections.
4. Bertini (`BertiniIncidence.bertini_regular_hyperplane_section`) for `dupleMorphism`, with
   `projectiveSpace_isRegular`; the clause "`H ⊉ X`" holds automatically since `f_a ≠ 0`
   (`hypersurfacePolynomial_ne_zero`, generic point via `notMem_genericPoint_proj`).
5. `isIntegral_hypersurface_of_isRegular` (`n ≥ 2`): if `f` is not irreducible,
   `exists_eq_mul_mem_mem` gives `f = p w` (`p` prime, `w` the degree-`(d - j)` component of the
   cofactor) and a point `x` (the homogeneous vanishing ideal of a classical point `P`) with
   `p, w ∈ x`; `P` comes from `projective_inter_nonempty_of_dim` (Theorem I.7.2) applied to
   `V(p)`, `V(q)` of dimension `n - 1` (`projective_codimension_one_iff_hypersurface`,
   `isProjVariety_projZeroSet_asHomogeneousIdeal`; relevance by `not_irrelevant_le_span`).
   `germ_mem_maximalIdeal_sq` puts the germ of the section in `𝔪ₓ² 𝒪(d)ₓ`, and
   `BertiniIncidence.germ_mem_maximalIdeal_sq_smul_top_iff` makes `V(f)` singular
   (`not_isRegular_of_eq_mul`). So `f` is prime, the support is the closure of the point `(f)`,
   and the reduced (regular local rings are domains, `RegularLocal.isDomain`) irreducible
   subscheme is integral.

## Divergences from the article's proof sketch

- The closed immersion is proved by the chart criterion (Proposition 7.2) for the morphism
  defined by the monomial sections, not by identifying it with `Veronese.dupleMap` /
  `Veronese.dupleEmbedding` (that identification needs the pullback of `𝒪(1)` along `dupleMap`,
  which the Veronese module does not compute). The Veronese module supplies the monomial index
  (`DupleIndex`, `dupleN`, `dupleEquiv`) and the monomial splitting `exists_le_degree_eq`; none of
  its recorded declarations is used.
- The integrality step follows the article's plain-text route (two components meet by the
  dimension theorem; a regular local ring is a domain) in the factorized form above: components
  are replaced by prime factors of `f`.
