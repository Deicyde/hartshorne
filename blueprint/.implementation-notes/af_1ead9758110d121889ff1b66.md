# Blowups are invariant under powers of the center

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.BlowupPowerInvariance`.

- Main result: `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupPowIso` (the isomorphism
  `Bl_{I^d} X ≅ Bl_I X` for a coherent `I` on a Noetherian `X` and `0 < d`) and
  `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupPowIso_hom_blowupProjection` (it is over `X`).
- Coherence of `I^d`: `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isCoherent_pow` (any power of a
  quasi-coherent ideal sheaf on a Noetherian scheme), from `isCoherent_powSmul` and coherence of
  the structure sheaf. The statement uses this proof for the blowup of `I ^ d`; the blowup does
  not depend on the coherence proof.
- Rees algebra of `J^d` as a Veronese subring: `Hartshorne.reesVeronese` (graded ring map
  `p(t) ↦ p(t^d)`, i.e. `Polynomial.expand`), its graded inverse `Hartshorne.reesVeroneseInv`
  (built from `RingEquiv.ofBijective`; surjectivity uses `Polynomial.contract`), and
  `Hartshorne.projReesVeroneseIso`.
- Veronese naturality (general graded rings): `Hartshorne.Veronese.veroneseMap`,
  `Hartshorne.Veronese.toProj_toSpecZero`, `Hartshorne.Veronese.map_comp_toProj_comp_map`.
- Gluing: `projReesPowIso` (chart isomorphism over an affine open), `projReesPowIso_hom_naturality`,
  `projReesPowIso_hom_projToSpec`, `projFunctorPowIso` (natural isomorphism of the relative gluing
  functors); the glued isomorphism is `HasColimit.isoOfNatIso`.

## Route

Follows the printed hint: the Rees algebra of `I^d` is the `d`-th Veronese subalgebra of the
Rees algebra of `I`, and `Proj` of a Veronese subring is `Proj` of the ring (II Ex. 5.13). The
recorded Veronese result is for affine graded rings only, so the proof glues: relative `Proj`
is the colimit of the functor `U ↦ Proj Γ(U, S)` on affine opens, and a natural isomorphism of
these functors compatible with the maps to `Spec Γ(U, 𝒪_X)` gives the isomorphism over `X`.

Naturality of the Veronese isomorphism is checked on the standard affine cover of the target
`Proj` after cancelling the (invertible) Veronese morphism, reducing to an identity of maps of
degree-zero localizations. The form `Proj.map φ ≫ toProj ≫ Proj.map θ = toProj ≫ Proj.map
(veroneseMap φ ∘ θ)` avoids needing the irrelevant-ideal hypothesis for `veroneseMap φ` alone.

## Friction

- Instance search does not find `HasColimit S.relativeGluingData.functor` (the instance
  `(d.functor ⋙ Scheme.forget).IsLocallyDirected` is not found by discrimination-tree lookup for
  this concrete gluing datum); the module supplies it as a local instance
  `hasColimit_relativeGluingData` by naming
  `Scheme.Cover.RelativeGluingData.instIsLocallyDirectedI₀CompFunctorForgetOfIsThin`.
- Rewriting inside composites whose objects are written as `S.grading U` versus `reesGrading
  (idealOn J U)` often fails with motive type mismatches; stating the irrelevant-ideal facts in
  the `S.grading U` form (`irrelevant_le_map_reesPowVeronese`) and passing explicit arguments to
  the general naturality lemma keeps elaboration fast.

## Possible upstreaming

`Veronese.veroneseMap`, `Veronese.toProj_toSpecZero` and `Veronese.map_comp_toProj_comp_map` fit
the Veronese Proj module; `hasColimit_relativeGluingData` fits the relative Proj module;
`isCoherent_pow` fits the ideal-power modules. They are kept here to avoid editing shared modules.
