# Vector bundles and locally free sheaves

  LocallyFreeIsoClasses Y n ≃ IsoClasses Y n`, with `toFun := vectorBundleClass` (`[E] ↦ [V(E)]`)

## Isomorphisms of geometric vector bundles (Exercise 5.18 preamble)

- `GeometricVectorBundle.chart`, `IsLinearTransition`: the charts `ψ_i⁻¹ : 𝔸ⁿ_{U_i} ⟶ X` and the
  linearity condition of the exercise for two charts at an affine open.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.transport` (the trivializations `ψ'_j ∘ g`), `unionU`, `unionψ`,
  `Compatible` (the union of two atlases on the same `X` satisfies the linearity condition),
  `union` (the combined structure).
- `GeometricVectorBundle.BundleIso G G'`: an isomorphism `g : X ≅ X'` over `Y` with
  `Compatible G (G'.transport g _)`, which is Hartshorne's definition. `BundleIso.refl`,
  `BundleIso.symm`, `BundleIso.trans`; `isLinearTransition_of_locally` (linearity of a transition
  is local on the base) makes the composite an isomorphism.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.Bundled Y n`, `isoSetoid`, `IsoClasses Y n`;
  `Scheme.Modules.LocallyFreeOfRank Y n`, `locallyFreeIsoSetoid`, `LocallyFreeIsoClasses Y n`.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.isAffineHom`: a geometric vector bundle is an affine morphism.

## The direction `E ↦ V(E) ↦ 𝒮(V(E)/Y)^∨`

- `BundleIso.sectionsModuleIso`: isomorphic bundles have isomorphic sheaves of sections
  (`s ↦ g ∘ s`), so `sectionsDualClass` is well defined.
- `Scheme.Modules.symmetricAlgebraIso`, `toQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso`,
  `symmetricQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso`, `vectorBundleIso`, `vectorBundleBundleIso`: `V(α)` for an
  isomorphism `α : E ≅ E'` is an isomorphism of geometric vector bundles, so `vectorBundleClass`
  is well defined.
- `sectionsDualClass_vectorBundle`: `𝒮(V(E)/Y)^∨ ≅ E^∨∨ ≅ E` (`vectorBundleSectionsModuleIso`
  plus `moduleSheafDoubleDualIso`).

## The direction `X ↦ 𝒮(X/Y)^∨ ↦ V(𝒮^∨)`

`nonempty_bundleIso_vectorBundleAtlas G hF : Nonempty (BundleIso (vectorBundleAtlas hF) G)`.

Route: chart-by-chart gluing with `Hartshorne.exists_iso_of_openImmersions_over`
(`ProjectiveBundleGluing`), rather than an isomorphism of quasi-coherent algebras
`Sym(𝒮^∨) ≅ f_* 𝒪_X`. The gluing route needs no sheaf-level universal property of `Sym` and no
gluing of "fibrewise linear functions"; the only computation is one matrix identity on an affine
open, and compatibility of the atlases is then immediate on charts.

- Index: `GlueIndex G` = a chart `i` and an affine `W ⊆ U_i`. `glueFrame`: the dual frame
  `e^{(i)∨}|_W` of `𝒮^∨` (`isLocalFrame_frameSection` + `IsLocalFrame.dual`), an index of
  `vectorBundleAtlas`.
- `glueMap`: on `p⁻¹(W) ⊆ V(𝒮^∨)`, the trivialization by `e^{(i)∨}|_W` followed by `ψ_i⁻¹`.
- `dualFrame_eval`: `e^{(j)∨}_k(s) = s^{(j)}_k` (chart coordinate). `chartCoord_transition_of`: the
  chart coordinates transform by the matrix of the given linear transition `θ` (the proof of
  `exists_chartCoord_transition`, with `θ` named). `dualFrame_expand`: hence
  `e^{(j)∨}_k = ∑_l m_{kl} e^{(i)∨}_l` on `W`, with `m` the matrix of `θ`.
- `frameChartIso_algEquiv`, `frameChartIso_transition`, `glue_trivialization_change`,
  `glueMap_compat`: the pieces agree on overlaps.
- `exists_iso_vectorBundle`: the glued isomorphism over `Y`; `transport_chart_glue`,
  `bundleIsoOfGlue`: on an affine `V ⊆ U_j` the chart `ψ_j⁻¹` is the chart of `V(𝒮^∨)` for the
  frame `e^{(j)∨}|_V`, so all transitions are linear.

## Notes

- The dual of a locally free module of rank `n` is reproved privately
  (`isLocallyFreeOfRank_moduleSheafDual`). The public
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.IsLocallyFreeOfRank.moduleSheafDual` is recorded on the II.8 tangent-sheaf
  article, which this article's dependencies do not reach.
- Three private helpers (`chartCoord_eq_appTop`, `chartLift_appTop_C'`, `chartCoord_transition_of`)
  repeat private material of `VectorBundleSections`; exposing a version of
  `exists_chartCoord_transition` that names `θ` there would remove the copy.
- The sheaf-level functoriality of `Sym` on isomorphisms (`symmetricAlgebraPresheafMap`,
  `symmetricAlgebraIso`) and `toQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso` are general. They could move to
  `SymmetricAlgebraSheaf` / `QuasiCoherentAlgebraOfModule` if other articles need them.
