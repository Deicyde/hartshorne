# Vector bundles and locally free sheaves

## Status

Partial result in Lean, not recorded. Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.VectorBundleEquivalence`.
Both maps on isomorphism classes are defined and one composite is proved to be the identity. The
other composite (a bundle is determined by its sheaf of sections) is the remaining goal, so the
bijection is not yet stated.

## What is proved

Isomorphisms of geometric vector bundles (Exercise 5.18 preamble):

- `AlgebraicGeometry.GeometricVectorBundle.chart`, `IsLinearTransition`: the charts
  `ψ_i⁻¹ : 𝔸ⁿ_{U_i} ⟶ X` and the linearity condition of the exercise for two charts at an affine
  open.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.transport` (the trivializations `ψ'_j ∘ g`), `unionU`, `unionψ`,
  `Compatible` (the union of two atlases on the same `X` satisfies the linearity condition),
  `union` (the combined structure).
- `GeometricVectorBundle.BundleIso G G'`: an isomorphism `g : X ≅ X'` over `Y` with
  `Compatible G (G'.transport g _)`, which is Hartshorne's definition. `BundleIso.refl`,
  `BundleIso.symm`, `BundleIso.trans`.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.isLinearTransition_of_locally`: linearity of a transition at an affine
  `V` follows from linearity on affine neighbourhoods of the points of `V`. This is what makes
  the composite of two bundle isomorphisms a bundle isomorphism.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.Bundled Y n` (`Σ X : Over Y, GeometricVectorBundle X.hom n`),
  `isoSetoid`, `IsoClasses Y n`; `Scheme.Modules.LocallyFreeOfRank Y n`, `locallyFreeIsoSetoid`,
  `LocallyFreeIsoClasses Y n`.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.isAffineHom`: a geometric vector bundle is an affine morphism.

Invariance of the sheaf of sections:

- `GeometricVectorBundle.BundleIso.sectionsModuleIso : G.sectionsModule ≅ G'.sectionsModule`,
  with `s ↦ g ∘ s` on sections. It is built from `sectionsModuleIsoOfCoordY` (atlases on the
  same `f` whose coordinates are linear for each other's operations give the same module, applied
  to `union`) and `transportSectionsModuleIso`.

`V` on isomorphisms:

- `Scheme.Modules.symmetricAlgebraIso` (`Sym(α)`, sheafified from `symmetricAlgebraMap`),
  `toQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso` (a multiplicative unital iso of commutative module-sheaf algebras
  gives an iso of quasi-coherent algebras), `symmetricQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso`,
  `vectorBundleIso` (via `QuasiCoherentAlgebra.relSpecIso`).
- `Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleBundleIso`: `V(α)` is a `BundleIso` between the atlases
  `vectorBundleAtlas`. On an affine `V` with frames `e` of `E` and `e'` of `E'`, the transition is
  the linear substitution expressing `α(e)` in the frame `e'` (`chartChange`).

The maps on classes:

- `GeometricVectorBundle.vectorBundleClass : LocallyFreeIsoClasses Y n → IsoClasses Y n`,
  `[E] ↦ [V(E)]`.
- `GeometricVectorBundle.sectionsDualClass : IsoClasses Y n → LocallyFreeIsoClasses Y n`,
  `[X] ↦ [𝒮(X/Y)^∨]`.
- `sectionsDualClass_vectorBundle`, `sectionsDualClass_vectorBundleClass`: the class of
  `𝒮(V(E)/Y)^∨` is that of `E` (`vectorBundleSectionsModuleIso` plus `moduleSheafDoubleDualIso`).
  Hence `sectionsDualClass_surjective` and `vectorBundleClass_injective`.

## Remaining goal

```lean
theorem vectorBundleClass_sectionsDualClass (q : IsoClasses Y n) :
    vectorBundleClass (sectionsDualClass q) = q
```

Equivalently, for `G : GeometricVectorBundle f n` with `F := moduleSheafDual G.sectionsModule`,
`Nonempty (BundleIso (vectorBundleAtlas hF) G)`. With it, the final statement is
`(Equiv.ofBijective vectorBundleClass ⟨vectorBundleClass_injective, surjectivity⟩)`, or the
`Equiv` with `toFun := vectorBundleClass` and `invFun := sectionsDualClass`. That is the article's
bijection, with `[E] ↦ [V(E)]` and `[X] ↦ [𝒮(X/Y)^∨]` as the two directions.

## Suggested route

Both schemes are affine over `Y` (`isAffineHom`, and `vectorBundleProj` is affine), so it suffices
to give an isomorphism of quasi-coherent algebras `Sym(F) ≅ QuasiCoherentAlgebra.ofHom f`, then
compose `relSpecIso` with `relSpecOfHomIso f`. Compatibility of the atlases can then be checked
on affine opens that carry a frame of `F` and lie in a chart of `G`. Use
`isLinearTransition_of_locally` for the other affine opens.

1. **Fibrewise linear functions.** Build an `𝒪_Y`-linear map `λ : F = 𝒮^∨ → f_* 𝒪_X`. On a chart
   `i` over an affine `W ⊆ U_i`, a linear form `φ` maps to the function
   `ψ_i^*(∑_k φ(e^{(i)}_k|_W) x_k)` on `f⁻¹(W) ≅ Spec Γ(W)[x]`, where `e^{(i)} = frameSection i`.
   Glue over `f⁻¹(W)` using the sheaf property of `𝒪_X`. The functions agree on overlaps because
   of the linear transitions. If `θ(x_k) = ∑ a_{kl} x_l`, the frame sections transform by the same
   matrix (`exists_chartCoord_transition`: `chartCoord_i = a · chartCoord_j`, evaluated on
   `frameSection j`). The private range lemma `range_affineSpaceChart_chart` and
   `affineSpaceChart_restrict` give the identifications `f⁻¹(W) ≅ Spec Γ(W)[x]` and their
   restrictions.
2. **Extend to `Sym`.** On affine opens with a frame of `F`, `Γ(W, Sym F)` is a polynomial ring
   (`chartRingHom_bijective`), so `λ` gives ring maps `Γ(W, Sym F) → Γ(f⁻¹W, 𝒪_X)` there. Extend
   them to all affine opens with the sheaf property of `𝒪_X` on `f⁻¹(U)`, then check
   multiplicativity and the structure maps locally. Alternatively, prove the universal property of
   the sheafified `Sym` (presheaf level: `SymmetricAlgebra.lift`; sheaf level: the sheafification
   adjunction, as `PresheafAlgebra.sheafifyMap_mul` does for multiplicativity).
3. **Iso.** On a framed affine `W` inside a chart, the map is the polynomial substitution given
   by the matrix of `β` in frames, so it is bijective. Bijectivity on all affine opens then follows
   by locality.
4. **Compatibility.** On such `W`, the chart of `V(F)` (`affineSpaceChart_frameTrivialization_inv`)
   and the chart of `G` differ by that substitution, which is linear.

The other approach, gluing a morphism `X ⟶ V(F)` over the cover `{f⁻¹(W)}` with
`Scheme.Cover.glueMorphisms`, needs the same matrix bookkeeping on overlaps. It also needs equality
of morphisms into `Spec Γ(W, Sym F)`, checked on rings of global sections.

  `Hartshorne.Ringed.IsLocallyFreeOfRank.moduleSheafDual` is recorded on the II.8 tangent-sheaf
  article, which this article's dependencies do not reach.

  `symmetricAlgebraIso`) and `toQuasiCoherentAlgebraIso` are general. They could move to
  `SymmetricAlgebraSheaf` / `QuasiCoherentAlgebraOfModule` if other articles need them.
