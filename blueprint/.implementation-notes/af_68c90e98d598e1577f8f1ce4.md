# Pushforwards of projective-bundle twists

## Status

Modules: `Hartshorne/Scheme/RelativeProjPushforwardTwist.lean` (general relative Proj part) and
`Hartshorne/Scheme/ProjectiveBundlePushforward.lean` (the projective bundle), both imported by
the library root. Proposition 7.11(a) is proved, sorry-free.

Candidate declarations to record (namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules`):

- `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom`: the natural map `S^l(E) ⟶ π_* 𝒪(l)`, `l ≥ 0`.
- `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom_app_restrict`: over an affine open `U ⊆ V` it is
  `s ↦ s|_U / 1` on `π⁻¹(U) = Proj Γ(U, S(E))` (this is what makes the map canonical).
- `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom_mul`: compatibility with the products
  `S^a(E) × S^b(E) → S^{a+b}(E)` and `𝒪(a) ⊗ 𝒪(b) → 𝒪(a+b)` on each affine piece. The product
  `u = s t` is given as a section `u` of `S^{a+b}(E)` with `ι u = ι s · ι t` in the sheaf of
  algebras `S(E)` (`ι` is injective, so `u` is determined); this avoids elaborating the
  presheaf-bilinear product `moduleSheafSymmetricPowerMul` on scheme sections.
- `isIso_projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom`, `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistIso`: the map is
  an isomorphism when `E` has rank at least two at every point.
- `isZero_pushforward_projectiveBundleTwistingSheaf`: `π_* 𝒪(l) = 0` for `l < 0`.
- `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistZeroIso`, `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistOneIso`:
  `π_* 𝒪(0) ≅ 𝒪_X` and `π_* 𝒪(1) ≅ E`.

## How the statement is modelled

- Hypotheses: `X` Noetherian, `E` locally free (`IsLocallyFree`) of finite rank (`rankAt < ℵ₀`),
  as in the projective-bundle article, and rank at least two as `∀ x, 2 ≤ E.rankAt x`.
- The graded isomorphism `Sym(E) ≅ ⊕_{l ∈ ℤ} π_* 𝒪(l)` is stated degreewise: for `l ≥ 0` a
  natural isomorphism `S^l(E) ≅ π_* 𝒪(l)`, for `l < 0` vanishing (`S^l(E) = 0` there), plus the
  multiplicativity lemma. The library has no sheaf of graded algebras `⊕_l π_* 𝒪(l)` and no
  glued multiplication `𝒪(a) ⊗ 𝒪(b) → 𝒪(a+b)` on a relative Proj, so multiplicativity is
  stated on each affine piece `π⁻¹(U) = Proj Γ(U, S(E))`, using the multiplication
  `twistMulHom` of the twisting sheaves of `Proj Γ(U, S(E))`, by comparing values at points
  (these lie in the same localization of `Γ(U, S(E))` for all twists, which avoids casts
  `↑a + ↑b = ↑(a + b)`).
- `π_* 𝒪` is stated as `π_* 𝒪(0)`, the zeroth twisting sheaf `S(0)~`; the library has no
  isomorphism `𝒪_{Proj_X S}(0) ≅ 𝒪_{Proj_X S}`.
- The map is canonical: it is glued from the sections `s / 1`
  (`GradedGlobalSections.twistSection`) on the affine pieces, with no choices; it is not an
  isomorphism chosen per open.

## Proof route

1. General construction (`RelativeProjPushforwardTwist`). For a quasi-coherent graded algebra
   `S`, a module `M` on `Proj_X S` with isomorphisms `α U : M|_{Proj S(U)} ≅ 𝒪_{Proj S(U)}(l)`
   compatible with the transition maps (`TwistCompatible`, satisfied by `relTwistingSheaf` and
   `relTwistingSheafRestrictIso` via `DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr.ψ_hom_app_top`), and a
   `GradedSectionsMap` `τ : Γ(U, F) → S(U)_l` compatible with restriction and the
   `𝒪_X`-action, `pushforwardTwistHom M α τ hα : F ⟶ π_* M` sends `s ∈ Γ(V, F)` to the section
   glued (`DirectedModuleGluing.mod_glue`) from the sections `α⁻¹(τ(s|_U) / 1)` over the affine
   `U ⊆ V`. Compatibility on overlaps uses basic opens contained in both
   (`exists_basicOpen_le_affine_inter`) and `twistRestrictHom_app_twistSection`
   (`s / 1 ↦ φ(s) / 1`). Linearity uses `appIso_hom_relProjToBase_app`: `π^# r` restricts on the
   piece over `U` to the global function `r|_U / 1` (`degreeZeroToGlobal`), computed through
   `projToOpen_ι` and `IsAffineOpen.fromSpec_app_of_le`.
2. Bijectivity is local (`isIso_of_forall_exists_bijective`, a basis/stalk criterion), and the
   affine opens with a frame of rank `n + 1 ≥ 2` form a basis
   (`exists_affine_isLocalFrame_of_two_le_rankAt`). On such `U`, `Γ(U, S^l(E)) ≅ Γ(U, S(E))_l`
   (`symmetricPowerGradedSectionsMap_bijective`, from `sheafProj_ι_self`), and
   `frameGradedHom : A[x_0, …, x_n] ≅ Γ(U, S(E))` transports Proposition II.5.13
   (`ProjectiveSpaceSectionRing.twistSection_top_bijective`,
   `projTwistingSheaf_global_eq_zero`) along `twistRestrictHom`, which is an isomorphism for a
   bijective graded map (a localization away from `1`, `isIso_twistRestrictHom`).
3. Vanishing for `l < 0`: `isZero_pushforward_of_twist_eq_zero`, the same basis argument.

This is the article's "affine-local descent of the standard projective-space calculation"
(Stacks 01OA): the book's argument is local on `X` where `E` is free, reducing to II.5.13.

## Friction

- `projectiveBundlePushforwardTwistHom_mul` needs `set_option maxHeartbeats 1000000`: the kernel
  check of its instantiation of the abstract `pushforwardTwistHom_mul` unfolds the projective
  bundle to the relative `Proj` of `S(E)`. The abstract lemma itself is cheap.
- The second module takes several minutes to elaborate; the abstract statements are fast, the
  cost is in unfolding `symmetricGradedAlgebra`.

- Comparing zeros of `Γ(M.restrict f, ⊤)` and `Γ(M, f ''ᵁ ⊤)` (definitionally equal) made
  elaboration and kernel checks very slow; the vanishing lemma is phrased with `Subsingleton`
  and transfers it along `restrictAppIso`, never equating zeros across the two types.
- `obtain … := h _ V hp` with `hp : p ∈ π ⁻¹ᵁ V` timed out unifying `?x ∈ V`; state
  `π p ∈ V` first.
- `toSectionsRing _ (…)` does not elaborate on sections of `moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebra`; use
  `show E.symSections U from …`.
