# The conormal sheaf of an immersion

## Status

Not formalized. The construction is committed in
`Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/ImmersionConormalSheaf.lean`; the library root does not import
it yet. The identification `i'_* N^*_i ≅ I/I²` is open. It rests on two general facts that are not
nodes of the roadmap (see "Missing prerequisites").

## Done

All of these are in `Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/ImmersionConormalSheaf.lean`.

Multiplication by an ideal:
- `Hartshorne.idealMulBilinear ι M`: for `ι : I ⟶ 𝒪_X`, the bilinear map `(a, m) ↦ ι(a) m`.
- `Hartshorne.idealTensor`: `I ⊗ M` typed as an `X.Modules`.
- `Hartshorne.idealMul ι M : I ⊗ M ⟶ M`, with `idealMul_bilinear` (its value on `a ⊗ m`).
- Typing everything as `X.Modules` and writing scalars as `X.ringCatSheaf.obj.obj V` avoids the
  instance failure noted in the first attempt.

The closed-immersion case, for `i : Z ⟶ X`:
- `Hartshorne.idealSheafSqQuotient i := cokernel (idealMul ι I)`, where `I` is
  `closedImmersionIdealSheaf i` and `ι` is `closedImmersionIdealSheafι i`. This is `I/I²`.
- `Hartshorne.idealMul_comp_unit`: the multiplication followed by the unit `I ⟶ i_* i^* I` is
  zero. The `𝒪_X`-action on `i_*` goes through `i^♯`, and `i^♯ ∘ ι = 0` by `kernel.condition`.
- `Hartshorne.idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward i : I/I² ⟶ i_* i^* I`, the induced map.

The immersion case, for `f : Z ⟶ X`:
- `Hartshorne.immersionIdealSheaf f := closedImmersionIdealSheaf f.liftCoborder`.
- `Hartshorne.immersionConormalSheaf f := (Scheme.Modules.pullback f.liftCoborder).obj (immersionIdealSheaf f)`.

## Remaining goal

`IsIso (idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward i)` for a closed immersion `i`. Apply it to
`i = f.liftCoborder` to get `i'_* N^*_f ≅ I/I²`.

## Missing prerequisites

1. **Tensor products and restriction.** Two related facts:
   - For an open immersion `j : V ⟶ X`, `(F ⊗ G)|_V ≅ F|_V ⊗ G|_V`, with `f ⊗ g ↦ f| ⊗ g|`.
   - Consequently, together with `affineTildeTensorIso`, the tensor product of quasi-coherent
     modules is quasi-coherent.

   Neither is a node. The tensor-product node records only the construction, the universal
   property and the stalk isomorphism. Without these facts, nothing is known about `I ⊗ I` on an
   affine open, so the cokernel `I/I²` cannot be computed there.
2. **Pullback and pushforward along a closed immersion.** For a closed immersion `i` with ideal
   sheaf `I` and an `𝒪_X`-module `M`, the unit `M ⟶ i_* i^* M` is an epimorphism with kernel
   `IM`, the image of `I ⊗ M ⟶ M`. That is, `i_* i^* M ≅ M/IM`. The remaining goal is the case
   `M = I`. The first attempt already suggested this split.

## Routes for fact 2, once fact 1 is available

- **Affine route.**
  1. Let `V = Spec A` be an affine open of `X`, `I|_V = J~`, and `B = Γ(Z, i⁻¹V) ≅ A/J`.
  2. Identify `(i_* i^* M)|_V` with `(B ⊗_A M(V))~`, using:
     - `QcqsPushforward.pushforwardRestrictIso`,
     - `PullbackQuasicoherent.specRestrictionPullbackIso`,
     - `tildePushforwardIso`.
  3. Show the unit becomes `m ↦ 1 ⊗ m`, by
     `PullbackQuasicoherent.specRestrictionPullbackIso_inv_unit` (in
     `RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange.lean`) and the analogous unit formula for
     `tildePushforwardIso`.
  4. Conclude with Mathlib's `TensorProduct.quotTensorEquivQuotSMul` (`(A/J) ⊗ M ≃ M/JM`).
  5. Glue with `Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_restrictFunctor_map`.
- **Stalk route.** Use `modules_isIso_of_stalk_bijective`. This needs:
  - stalks of cokernels of module sheaves (`QuotientSheafStalks` covers abelian sheaves only; the
    bridge from `SheafOfModules` cokernels is missing);
  - stalks of `I` (a kernel);
  - stalks of `i_*` for a closed immersion (`N_z` on the image, `0` off it);
  - `pullbackStalkIso`, together with its compatibility with the unit.
