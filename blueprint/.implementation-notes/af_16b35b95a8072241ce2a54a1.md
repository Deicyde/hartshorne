# The conormal sheaf of an immersion

## Status

Not recorded yet. The whole construction, including `i'_* N^*_i ≅ I/I²`, is drafted in
`Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/ImmersionConormalSheaf.lean`. The two general facts it needs are
proved there and in `Hartshorne/Scheme/ModuleSheafTensorRestrict.lean`, as unrecorded helpers.
The library root imports neither module yet.

## Done (compiled and committed)

`Hartshorne/Scheme/ModuleSheafTensorRestrict.lean`:
- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorRestrictIso f F G : (F ⊗ G)|_Y ≅ F|_Y ⊗ G|_Y`, for an open
  immersion `f : Y ⟶ X`, with `moduleSheafTensorRestrictIso_bilinear` (`a ⊗ b ↦ a ⊗ b`).
- `Hartshorne.isQuasicoherent_moduleSheafTensor`: the tensor product of quasi-coherent modules is
  quasi-coherent.

`Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/ImmersionConormalSheaf.lean`:
- `idealMulBilinear`, `idealTensor` and `idealMul` (with `idealMul_bilinear`): the map
  `I ⊗ M ⟶ M`.
- `idealSheafSqQuotient i := cokernel (idealMul ι I)`, which is `I/I²`.
- `idealMul_comp_unit`, and `idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward i : I/I² ⟶ i_* i^* I`.
- `immersionIdealSheaf f`, and `immersionConormalSheaf f := f.liftCoborder^* I`.

## Drafted (committed as work in progress)

These are in the leaf module. The last compile reported no errors except three tactic steps in
`bijective_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward_app`, and `immersionConormalPushforwardIso` had not
been added yet. All three steps have been rewritten but not rechecked:
- the `Mono` instance for the short complex `I ⟶ 𝒪_X ⟶ i_* 𝒪_Z`;
- the rewrite by `idealMul_bilinear`;
- the additivity step of `Submodule.smul_induction_on`.

Declarations:
- `isIso_of_bijective_moduleSpecΓ`: on `Spec R`, a map between modules of the form `M̃` is an
  isomorphism if it is bijective on global sections.
- `surjective_cokernel_π_app`: for a map `F ⟶ G` of quasi-coherent modules, `G ⟶ coker` is
  surjective on affine sections.
- `exists_one_tmul` and `mem_ker_smul_top_of_one_tmul_eq_zero`. For a surjective ring map
  `A ⟶ B` with kernel `J`:
  - every element of `B ⊗_A M` is `1 ⊗ m`;
  - `1 ⊗ m = 0` implies `m ∈ JM`.
- `closedImmersion_unit_toOpen` (compiles): on `i⁻¹ V`, the unit sends `s ∈ I(V)` to `1 ⊗ s`.
- `idealSectionsModule_smul` (compiles): `Γ(V)` acts on `I(V)` by restriction.
- `bijective_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward_app`: the comparison is bijective on affine
  sections.
- `isIso_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward`: `I/I² ≅ i_* i^* I`.
- `immersionConormalPushforwardIso f : i'_* N^*_f ≅ I/I²`.

## Helpers a future Roadmap pass might promote to articles

- Tensor products commute with restriction to open subschemes (`moduleSheafTensorRestrictIso`).
- The tensor product of quasi-coherent modules is quasi-coherent
  (`isQuasicoherent_moduleSheafTensor`).
- For a closed immersion `i` with ideal sheaf `I`, `i_* i^* I ≅ I/I²`
  (`isIso_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward`). The general `i_* i^* M ≅ M/IM` has the same
  proof.
- On `Spec R`, a map of quasi-coherent modules is an isomorphism if it is bijective on global
  sections (`isIso_of_bijective_moduleSpecΓ`).
- Two helpers from `RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange.lean`:
  - isomorphisms of module sheaves are local (`Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_restrictFunctor_map`);
  - the unit formula for `specRestrictionPullbackIso` (`specRestrictionPullbackIso_inv_unit`).
