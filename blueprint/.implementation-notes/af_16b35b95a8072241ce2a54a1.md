# The conormal sheaf of an immersion

## Status

Not formalized. A bounded attempt fixed the route below; no Lean was committed.

## Ingredients available

- The factorization of an immersion: Mathlib's `Scheme.Hom.liftCoborder`, a closed immersion
  into the open subscheme `f.coborderRange`, together with `Scheme.Hom.liftCoborder_ι`.
- The ideal sheaf of a closed immersion: `Hartshorne.closedImmersionIdealSheaf` and
  `closedImmersionIdealSheafι`, the kernel of `structureSheafToPushforward`.
- Pullback and pushforward with their adjunction: `Scheme.Modules.pullback`,
  `Scheme.Modules.pushforward`, `pullbackPushforwardAdjunction`.
- Tensor products of module sheaves: `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensor`, with its universal
  property for bilinear maps (`moduleSheafTensorHomEquiv`, `ModuleSheafBilinear`).

## Route

1. Definitions. Put `I := closedImmersionIdealSheaf f.liftCoborder` and
   `N^*_f := (Scheme.Modules.pullback f.liftCoborder).obj I`.
2. Multiplication. Build `I × I → I`, `(a, b) ↦ ι(a) • b`, as a `ModuleSheafBilinear`. Its
   tensor map is `μ : I ⊗ I ⟶ I`. Define `I/I² := cokernel μ`.
3. The comparison map. Let `η : I ⟶ i_* i^* I` be the adjunction unit, with
   `i := f.liftCoborder`.
   - `μ ≫ η = 0`: through `moduleSheafTensorHomEquiv_comp`, its bilinear map is
     `(a, b) ↦ ι(a) • η(b) = (i.app ι(a)) • η(b) = 0`, because sections of `I` are killed by
     `i^#` and the `𝒪_X`-action on `i_*` goes through `i^#`.
   - Then `cokernel.desc μ η _ : I/I² ⟶ i_* N^*_f` is the canonical map.
4. The remaining goal: this map is an isomorphism. Both sides are quasi-coherent. Two ways to
   check it:
   - On affine opens `Spec A` with `I = J~`: use `tildePullbackIso` and `tildePushforwardIso`
     to identify `i_* i^* J~` with `(A/J ⊗_A J)~`, show that `η` corresponds to
     `m ↦ 1 ⊗ m`, and use `J/J² ≅ A/J ⊗_A J` (Mathlib's quotient-tensor equivalences). The
     compatibility of the tilde isomorphisms with the unit, and of restriction to affine opens
     with `i_*` and `i^*`, is the main work.
   - On stalks: `pullbackStalkIso` gives `(i^*I)_z ≅ I_z ⊗ 𝒪_{Z,z}`; tensor stalks are given by
     `moduleSheafTensorStalkIso`. This also needs the stalks of `i_*` for a closed immersion:
     `(i_*N)_x ≅ N_x` for `x ∈ Z`, and `0` off `Z`.

## Pitfall

Defining the multiplication through `ModuleSheafBilinear (R := X.sheaf)` on `X.Modules` makes
instance search fail. It looks for `Module ((X.sheaf.obj ⋙ forget₂ _ _).obj U)` on sections that
carry `Module (X.ringCatSheaf.obj.obj U)`. Either state everything over a general
`R : Sheaf _ CommRingCat` (as in `RingedSpace/`) and specialize, or write the scalars with the
`X.ringCatSheaf` form.

## Suggested split (Roadmap)

The identification `i'_* N^*_i ≅ I/I²` rests on a general fact: for a closed immersion `i` and an
`𝒪_X`-module `M`, `i_* i^* M ≅ M / I M`. This could be its own node feeding this one.
