# Cohomology under an affine morphism

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.AffineMorphismCohomologyPushforward`.

- `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoPushforwardOfIsAffineHom`: for `f : X ⟶ Y` with
  `[IsAffineHom f] [IsNoetherian X] [IsNoetherian Y] [Y.IsSeparated]` and a
  quasi-coherent `F : X.Modules`, an isomorphism between the sheaf cohomology
  of the underlying abelian sheaves of `F` and of `(Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).obj F`,
  in every degree `p`.
- `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoPushforwardOfIsAffineHom_hom_naturality`:
  naturality in `F` (for morphisms between quasi-coherent modules).
- The cover-level versions `sheafCohomologyIsoPushforwardOfAffineCover` and
  `..._hom_naturality` take any linearly ordered open affine cover of `Y`;
  the cover-free statement uses the cover by all affine opens with a
  well-ordering (`affineOpensLinearOrder`).

`X.IsSeparated` is not a hypothesis: it follows from `Y.IsSeparated` because
affine morphisms are separated (Mathlib `IsSeparated.of_isAffineHom`), packaged
as `scheme_isSeparated_of_isAffineHom`.

## Route

As in the book: the inverse image of an affine cover of `Y` is an affine cover
of `X`; the ordered Čech complexes of `f_* F` on `U` and of `F` on `f⁻¹U` are
isomorphic (`AffineMorphismCohomologyPushforward.cechComplexIso`, for any
continuous map of topological spaces and any abelian sheaf; in each degree it
is the identity on sections up to the equality of opens
`f⁻¹(⋂ U_{i_j}) = ⋂ f⁻¹U_{i_j}`); `f_* F` is quasi-coherent
(`isQuasicoherent_pushforward`, Noetherian-source case); Theorem III.4.5
(`cechCohomologyIsoOfIsQuasicoherent`) on `X` and on `Y`.

The abelian sheaf underlying the module pushforward is definitionally the
topological pushforward of the underlying abelian sheaf
(`AffineMorphismCohomologyPushforward.toSheafPushforwardIso` has identity
components).

The book uses a finite affine cover; the formalized Theorem III.4.5 needs no
finiteness, so the Lean uses all affine opens and avoids universe lifting of a
`Fin n`-indexed cover.
