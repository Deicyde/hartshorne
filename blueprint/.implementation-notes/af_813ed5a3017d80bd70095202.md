# Open extension by zero adjunction

Pinned Mathlib has `TopCat.Sheaf.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction`, but that is the
opposite adjunction `j^* ⊣ j_*`. It also has
`Topology.IsOpenEmbedding.sheafPullbackIso`, which compares standard pullback
with naive restriction. The generic construction
`CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback` supplies an
abstract left adjoint to naive restriction by sheafifying a left Kan
extension, but no reached theorem identifies that left Kan extension with the
roadmap's concrete conditional presheaf.

Prove either the displayed Hom equivalence directly for the conditional
presheaf or a natural isomorphism from its sheafification to that generic left
adjoint. The resulting unit is used by the source-stated properties article
`af_0bfcbd42273250dba6a2230e`.
