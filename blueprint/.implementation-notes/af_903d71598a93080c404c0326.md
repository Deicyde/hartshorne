# Projective dimension of the residue field

## Status

Complete in Lean, not yet recorded. The regular-localization split revision
removed `not_ready` and the dependence on the all-modules global-dimension
article: the proof uses a regular sequence generating the maximal ideal, not
the global-dimension theorem. A later Formalize pass records the declarations
below after independent review.

## Candidate Lean

In the module `Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalGlobalDimension`, namespace
`Hartshorne.RegularLocal`:

- `projectiveDimension_residueField_eq_ringKrullDim`:
  `projectiveDimension (ModuleCat.of R (ResidueField R)) = ringKrullDim R`
  for `[IsRegularLocalRing R]`. This is the article statement; Mathlib's
  `ringKrullDim` is the article's $\dim A$. Record it first.
- `projectiveDimension_residueField`: the same with
  `(maximalIdeal R).spanFinrank` on the right. Record it second.

The proof uses Mathlib's `ModuleCat.projectiveDimension_quotient_eq_length`
(projective dimension of the quotient by a regular sequence, the unlinked
Proof depends on item) and
`Hartshorne.RegularLocal.exists_isWeaklyRegular_of_spanFinrank_eq` from
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalCohenMacaulay` (a weakly regular sequence of
length $\dim A$ generating $\mathfrak m$).

## Route difference

The article's proof sketch obtains the regular sequence from the
Cohen–Macaulay property and the dimension criterion for regular sequences
(the two linked articles). The Lean helper
`exists_isWeaklyRegular_of_spanFinrank_eq` instead inducts on the number of
generators, using `Hartshorne.RegularLocal.isDomain` (article
regular-local-ring-domain) and
`Hartshorne.RegularLocal.isRegularLocalRing_quotient_of_notMem_sq` (the forward
direction of regular-hypersurface-local-criterion, proved separately in that
module). When recording, either accept the helper as covered by the linked
articles or add Proof depends on edges to regular-local-ring-domain and
regular-hypersurface-local-criterion.
