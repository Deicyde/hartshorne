# Completion at a closed point

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalCompletionClosedPoint`.

- `Hartshorne.closedPointIdealSubsheaf P hP`: the ideal sheaf of the closed point, taken as
  `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv.symm` of Mathlib's `Scheme.IdealSheafData.vanishingIdeal` of
  the closed set `{P}` (the reduced closed subscheme structure; the same ideal data as the
  project's reduced induced subscheme, without depending on that article).
- `Hartshorne.onePointLocallyRingedSpace R` (for `[IsLocalRing R]`): carrier
  `Hartshorne.onePointSpace` (`TopCat.of PUnit`), presheaf Mathlib's `skyscraperPresheaf` with
  value `R` (decidability of membership taken classically); stalks are local through
  `skyscraperPresheafStalkOfSpecializes`.
- `Hartshorne.formalCompletionClosedPointIso P hP U hPU` (def, `[IsNoetherian X]`, `U` an affine
  open containing `P`) and `Hartshorne.formalCompletion_closedPoint_iso` (theorem: `Nonempty` of
  the isomorphism with the one-point space of
  `AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal (X.presheaf.stalk P)) (X.presheaf.stalk P)`).
  `IsNoetherian X` is used to make that completion a local ring (Mathlib's instance for
  Noetherian local rings), so the target is a locally ringed space.

## Proof route

- Supporting lemmas: `range_closedPointIdealSubsheaf_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι` (support is
  `{P}`), the carrier is a subsingleton, `formalCompletionClosedPointCarrierIso` (carrier is the
  one-point space), `ideal_closedPointIdealSubsheaf` (on an affine `U ∋ P` the ideal is
  `primeIdealOf P`, maximal by `IsAffineOpen.primeIdealOf_isMaximal_of_isClosed`),
  `preimage_closedPointIdealSubsheaf_eq_top`.
- Global sections: `formalCompletionClosedPointSectionsEquiv` composes restriction along
  `U ∩ |Y| = ⊤`, `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso`, and `adicCompletionLocalizationEquiv`
  (the map of adic completions induced by `A → A_𝔪 = 𝒪_{X,P}`, bijective by
  `adicCompletionMap_bijective` because each `A ⧸ 𝔪 ^ n → A_𝔪 ⧸ (𝔪A_𝔪) ^ n` is Mathlib's
  `IsLocalization.AtPrime.equivQuotMaximalIdealPow`).
- Sheaf: `isoSkyscraperPresheafPUnit` shows any sheaf of rings on the one-point space is the
  skyscraper sheaf of its global sections (Mathlib's `toSkyscraperPresheaf` is an isomorphism on
  every open: germs on the one-point space are bijective, and over the empty open both sides are
  terminal). The locally ringed space isomorphism is assembled with
  `PresheafedSpace.isoOfComponents`, `SheafedSpace.isoMk`, `LocallyRingedSpace.isoOfSheafedSpaceIso`.
- The project formal spectrum is not used: the affine-sections isomorphism already gives the
  ring over the whole one-point space.
