# The conormal criterion for nonsingularity

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.ConormalCriterionNonsingular`, namespace
`Hartshorne.ConormalCriterion`.

- `conormal_criterion_nonsingular`: the equivalence. `X ⟶ Spec k` of finite type, `X`
  irreducible and regular, `i : Y ⟶ X` a closed immersion with `Y` irreducible; then
  `Y.IsRegular ↔ (relativeDifferentials (i ≫ p)).IsLocallyFree ∧ Mono (conormalToPullback i p)`.
  "Exact on the left" is `Mono` of the first map of the conormal sequence of Proposition 8.12
  (the sequence of the closed-immersion-conormal-sequence article, whose first term is the
  conormal sheaf `i^* I`). This matches the conventions of the forward-direction article.
- `isRegular_of_isLocallyFree_of_mono`: the converse direction.

Separatedness of `X` (part of "variety") is not used, as in the forward-direction theorem and in
Theorem 8.15.

## Route

Forward direction: `NonsingularConormal.nonsingular_closedImmersion_conormal` (left exactness),
plus `isRegular_iff_relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank` for local freeness of `Ω_{Y/k}`.
The codimension `r` that the forward theorem needs comes from the dimension-codimension formula
(`exists_topologicalKrullDim_eq`).

Converse, along the printed proof: constant rank `q` of `Ω_{Y/k}` (`rankAt_eq_of_connectedSpace`);
at a closed point the stalk conormal sequence is `0 → C → B^n → B^q → 0`, so `C` is free of rank
`s = n - q` (`exists_linearEquiv_of_exact`); Nakayama along the local surjection
`𝒪_{X,y} → 𝒪_{Y,y}` (`span_eq_top_of_span_tmul_eq_top`, using `B ⊗_A M ≅ M/(ker ρ)M`) gives
`s` generators of the ideal stalk; Mathlib's `ringKrullDim_le_ringKrullDim_quotient_add_card`
gives `dim A ≤ dim B + s` (`ringKrullDim_stalk_le_add`). On the other side
`ringKrullDim_stalk_le_finrank_fibre` (Proposition 8.7 in an affine chart, then
`ringKrullDim_le_spanFinrank_maximalIdeal`). Then `q = dim Y` and Theorem 8.15 applies.

In the printed proof the comparison subscheme `Y'` cut out by local generators of `J` appears in
the forward direction; the converse only needs the generator count and the dimension inequality.
