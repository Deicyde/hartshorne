# Schemes as formal schemes: implementation note

## Status

Complete candidate (pending review). Lean in `Hartshorne.Scheme.SchemeAsFormalScheme`, namespace
`Hartshorne`. Sorry-free.

## Declarations

- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.instBot`: the zero ideal subsheaf $0 \subseteq \mathcal{O}_X$,
  defined as the subsheaf of the zero ideal data (`quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv.symm ⊥`);
  `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.ideal_bot` shows its ideal of sections is zero over every open.
  The project had no `Bot` on quasi-coherent ideal subsheaves before.
- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionCanonicalHom I`: the canonical morphism
  $\widehat{X}_Y \to X$ of ringed spaces (as a `PresheafedSpace` morphism), for any
  quasi-coherent ideal subsheaf $I$: the closed immersion $|Y| \to |X|$ on spaces and the
  existing canonical section maps `formalCompletionSectionsMap` (with their naturality) on
  sheaves. It is not shown to be a morphism of locally ringed spaces in general.
- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionBotIso X : (⊥).formalCompletion ≅ X.toLocallyRingedSpace`,
  for every scheme $X$ (Hartshorne states it for Noetherian $X$). Its underlying presheafed-space
  morphism is `formalCompletionCanonicalHom ⊥` (`formalCompletionBotIso_hom_toPresheafedSpace`,
  `rfl`), with `formalCompletionBotIso_hom_base_apply` and `formalCompletionBotIso_hom_c_app`.
- `isNoetherianFormalScheme_toLocallyRingedSpace`: a Noetherian scheme is a Noetherian formal
  scheme (via `isNoetherianFormalScheme_formalCompletion` and `IsNoetherianFormalScheme.of_iso`).
- `NoetherianFormalScheme.ofScheme X`, `NoetherianFormalScheme.toNoetherianFormalSchemeBotIso X`
  (the completion of $X$ along itself is isomorphic to `ofScheme X` in the category of Noetherian
  formal schemes).
- `NoetherianFormalScheme.fromNoetherianScheme`: the functor from
  `ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory (fun X : Scheme => IsNoetherian X)` to
  `NoetherianFormalScheme`, and `NoetherianFormalScheme.fullyFaithfulFromNoetherianScheme`
  (from `Scheme.fullyFaithfulForgetToLocallyRingedSpace`, since morphisms of Noetherian formal
  schemes are morphisms of locally ringed spaces).

## Proof route

Following the article's proof sketch. Along $0$, the ideal data of $0^{n+1}$ is $\bot$, so every
closed immersion $Y_{n+1} \to X$ is an isomorphism (`Scheme.isIso_subschemeι_iff_eq_bot`), hence
so are the inclusions $Y_{n+1} \to Y_{n+2}$ and the transition maps of
`formalCompletionDiagram`. Mathlib's `Functor.IsEventuallyConstantTo.isIso_π_of_isLimit` (over
the cofiltered $\mathbb{N}^{op}$) then makes the projection of the limit to the first term an
isomorphism; composing with restriction to $Y_1 \cong X$ shows each canonical section map
`formalCompletionSectionsMap U` is an isomorphism. The isomorphism of locally ringed spaces is
`PresheafedSpace.isoOfComponents` with the homeomorphism of $Y_1 \to X$ and the inverse of the
canonical sheaf map.

## Friction

- The instance `IsIso (limit.π (⊥).formalCompletionDiagram (op 0))` stated in this module was not
  found by instance search at its use sites (implicit-argument mismatch in the `limit` term), so
  it is a plain theorem `isIso_limit_π_formalCompletionDiagram_bot` and is passed explicitly.
