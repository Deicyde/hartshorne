# Diagonal conormal comparison

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RelativeDifferentialsDiagonalConormal`, namespace
`Hartshorne.DiagonalConormal`. Sorry-free.

## Recorded declarations

- `relativeDifferentialsDiagonalConormalIso f : relativeDifferentials f ≅
  immersionConormalSheaf (pullback.diagonal f)`. It is `asIso` of the comparison morphism
  `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal f`. `isIso_relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal`
  proves that the comparison morphism is an isomorphism.
- The universal derivation goes to the difference of the two pullbacks:
  - `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal_app_d`: `d a ↦ conormalD … U a`, the image under the
    unit `I ⟶ j_* j^* I` of the section `p₁^* a - p₂^* a` of the diagonal ideal.
  - `relativeDifferentialsDiagonalConormalIso_d`: through `immersionConormalPushforwardIso`, the
    image of `d a` is the class of that section in `I/I²`.
- Independence of the neighbourhood: `factorizationComparison f F` is the same construction for
  any `ConormalIndependence.ClosedFactorization` `F` of the diagonal.
  `factorizationComparison_toCanonical` says it composes with `toCanonical F` to the canonical
  morphism. `isIso_factorizationComparison` and `factorizationComparison_comparison` say it is an
  isomorphism and compatible with `comparison F G`.

Sign: our class is `a ⊗ 1 - 1 ⊗ a` (first pullback minus second). Mathlib's
`KaehlerDifferential.D a` is the class of `1 ⊗ a - a ⊗ 1`.

## Proof structure

- General derivation: `retractionsDerivation` works for any `j : X ⟶ W` with retractions
  `q₁, q₂` (`j ≫ qᵢ = 𝟙`, `q₁ ≫ f = q₂ ≫ f`). The difference section lifts uniquely to the ideal
  sheaf (`idealSection`). Leibniz only uses that the unit is linear through `j^♯`.
- Surjectivity on affine sections (`relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal_app_surjective`):
  - `chart_le_coborderRange`: `U ×_V U` lies in `coborderRange`, because the diagonal of an affine
    morphism is closed and `coborder` commutes with open embeddings.
  - `mem_closure_nbhd`: from `baseChange_isPushout` and the general `mem_closure_of_isPushout`,
    functions on it are sums of products `p₁^* a · p₂^* b`.
  - `mem_span_conormalD` then spans `N^*_Δ(U)` by the `conormalD b`, using
    `unit_app_surjective_of_isAffineOpen` (from `bijective_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward_app`).
- Left inverse (`diagonalConormalToRelativeDifferentials`):
  - Start from `ClosedConormal.conormalToPullback j p₂`.
  - Then `inv (squareMap f _)`. `squareMap` is the base-change map for the square on `W`, and is
    an isomorphism because it is the restriction of `baseChangeRelativeDifferentialsMap f f`
    (`squareMap_comp_diagRestrictIso`).
  - Then `j^* p₁^* ≅ 𝟙` (`pullbackPullback_unit`).
  - It sends `conormalD a` to `d a`, so it is a left inverse.
- Epi plus split mono gives the isomorphism. Epi comes from local surjectivity, via
  `TopCat.Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_epi` and faithfulness of `SheafOfModules.toSheaf`.
- Independence: `conormalRestrictIso_conormalD` (through `idealSheafRestrictIso_inv_idealSection`)
  and the transport `eqToHom_conormalD`.

Do not discharge the comparison by defining the diagonal conormal sheaf as `Ω_{X/Y}`. The target
is `immersionConormalSheaf (pullback.diagonal f)`.
