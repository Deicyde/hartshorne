# Diagonal conormal comparison

## Status: partial (comparison morphism built, isomorphism open)

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RelativeDifferentialsDiagonalConormal`, namespace
`Hartshorne.DiagonalConormal`. Sorry-free. Nothing is recorded on the article yet.

Proved:

- General setting: `j : X ⟶ W` with retractions `q₁ q₂ : W ⟶ X` (`j ≫ qᵢ = 𝟙`) and
  `q₁ ≫ f = q₂ ≫ f`. On `U`, `diffSection q₁ q₂ U a` is `q₁^* a - q₂^* a` on
  `nbhd q₁ q₂ U = q₁⁻¹ U ⊓ q₂⁻¹ U` (`preimage_nbhd`: `j⁻¹ (nbhd U) = U`). It is killed by `j^♯`
  (`app_diffSection`), so it lifts uniquely to the ideal sheaf `closedImmersionIdealSheaf j`
  (`idealSection`, `ι_idealSection`, `idealSection_eq`). Additivity, Leibniz, restriction and
  vanishing on `f^♯` sections are `idealSection_add`, `idealSection_mul`, `idealSection_map` and
  `idealSection_app_eq_zero`.
- `conormalD h₁ h₂ U a` is the image of `idealSection` under the unit `I ⟶ j_* j^* I`, restricted
  to `U`. Leibniz only uses that the unit is linear with `𝒪_W` acting through `j^♯`
  (`unitApp_smul`) and that `j^♯ (qᵢ^* a) = a` (`app_res_app`). It does not need `I²`.
- `retractionsDerivation h₁ h₂ f hq` is a relative derivation `𝒪_X ⟶ j^* I` over `f`, built with
  `RelDiffLocal.derivationOfKills`.
- Diagonal: `diagFst f` and `diagSnd f` are the projections restricted to
  `(pullback.diagonal f).coborderRange`. `diagonalConormalDerivation f` takes values in
  `immersionConormalSheaf (pullback.diagonal f)`, which is definitionally `j^* I` for
  `j = (pullback.diagonal f).liftCoborder`. The comparison morphism
  `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal f : Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ N^*_Δ` comes from
  `relativeDifferentialsHomEquiv`. `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal_d` and
  `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal_app_d` give `d a ↦ [p₁^* a - p₂^* a]`.

Sign: our class is `a ⊗ 1 - 1 ⊗ a`. Mathlib's `KaehlerDifferential.D a` is the class of
`1 ⊗ a - a ⊗ 1` in `(KaehlerDifferential.ideal A B).Cotangent`. The two differ by `-1`, which does
not affect bijectivity.

## Exact remaining goal

`IsIso (Hartshorne.DiagonalConormal.relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal f)` for every
`f : X ⟶ Y`. The article's isomorphism is then `asIso` of it. The last sentence of the article
(independence of the neighbourhood) is then a corollary. Compose with
`ConormalIndependence.ClosedFactorization.toCanonical`. Then check that `retractionsDerivation`,
built from a factorization `F` with `q₁, q₂` the projections restricted to `F.U`, corresponds to
`diagonalConormalDerivation` under `toCanonical F`. Both are given by the same difference
sections, restricted.

## Next route

1. Locality: reduce to sections over affine `U = Spec B ⊆ f⁻¹ V` with `V = Spec A` affine. Use
   `Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_restrictFunctor_map` or the stalk criterion
   `Hartshorne.Ringed.modules_isIso_of_stalk_bijective`.
2. The open `T = fst⁻¹ U ⊓ snd⁻¹ U` of `X ×_Y X` is affine (`IsAffineOpen.pullback_inf` in
   `RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange`). Its preimage under the diagonal is `U`. It lies in
   `coborderRange`, because the diagonal is closed in `T`;
   `ConormalIndependence.ClosedFactorization.le_coborderRange` is the template. Its ring of
   sections must still be identified with `B ⊗_A B`, compatibly with the two projections and with
   the multiplication map to `B`.
3. On `T`, `I(T) = J = ker (B ⊗_A B → B) = KaehlerDifferential.ideal A B`.
   `bijective_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward_app` and `surjective_cokernel_π_app` identify
   `N^*_Δ(U)` with `J/J²`. That the image of `I ⊗ I` on affine sections is `J²` needs the sections
   of `moduleSheafTensor` on an affine open, via the tilde–tensor compatibility.
4. `RelDiffLocal.affineIso` and `RelDiffLocal.affineIso_hom_d` identify `Ω_{X/Y}(U)` with
   `Ω_{B/A}`. Through these identifications, `relativeDifferentialsToDiagonalConormal_app_d` sends
   `d b` to `-(D b)`. So the map on sections is `-1` on `(KaehlerDifferential.ideal A B).Cotangent`.
   It is `B`-linear and `Ω_{B/A}` is generated by the `d b`, so it is bijective.

A route through the conormal sequence of `j` over `q₁` (`ClosedImmersionConormalSequence`) plus
base change of differentials only gives a surjection `N^*_Δ ⟶ Ω_{X/Y}`. Left injectivity of the
conormal sequence is not proved in the project, so that route does not close the goal by itself.

Do not discharge the comparison by defining the diagonal conormal sheaf as `Ω_{X/Y}`. The target
must stay `immersionConormalSheaf (pullback.diagonal f)`.
