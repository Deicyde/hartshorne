# Projective-line point-count blocker

The arithmetic endpoint is available: pinned Mathlib proves
`Projectivization.card_of_finrank_two`, so the classical projectivization of a
two-dimensional vector space over a finite field has `q + 1` points.

The current dependency DAG does not supply the representation bridge needed to
apply that theorem to the article's scheme-valued point count. In particular:

- `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceScheme` defines the bare `Proj` scheme, but its
  article does not expose a structure morphism to `Spec k` or a finite-type
  proof suitable for `Hartshorne.pointCountSequence`.
- No pinned declaration or reached roadmap article gives an equivalence between
  morphisms over `Spec k`
  from `Spec K` to the projective-line scheme and
  `Projectivization K (Fin 2 → K)`.
- `Hartshorne.rationalPointEquivSchemePoint` only repackages morphisms in the
  over-category as commuting underlying scheme morphisms; it does not identify
  them with homogeneous coordinates.
- The planned article "The tautological quotient and functor of points" states
  a sufficiently general projective-bundle functor-of-points theorem, but the
  selected article does not depend on it and it is not yet formalized.

Roadmap should add and connect a projective-line functor-of-points bridge (or
activate the general projective-bundle theorem), together with the relevant
relative-projective-space structure morphism. Once that equivalence is proved,
`Projectivization.card_of_finrank_two` and
`Hartshorne.finiteFieldExtension_natCard` give the required `q^r + 1` formula.
Counting coordinate tuples before proving this equivalence would not establish
the scheme-valued statement.
