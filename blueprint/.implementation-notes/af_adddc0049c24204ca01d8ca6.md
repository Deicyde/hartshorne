# isomorphism-off-center (Hartshorne II Prop 7.13(b), p.164)

Module: `src/Hartshorne/Scheme/BlowupIsoOffCenter.lean` (imports only
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ReesAlgebraBlowup`; the shared Rees/blowup module is unchanged).

## Declarations to record

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.centerCompl`: the open `X \ Y`, defined as the
  complement of the set-theoretic image of the closed immersion
  `(quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv I).subschemeι : Y ⟶ X` (Mathlib's closed subscheme of the
  ideal sheaf data of `I`).
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isIso_blowupProjection_restrict_centerCompl`:
  `IsIso (I.blowupProjection hI ∣_ I.centerCompl)` for `X` Noetherian and `I` coherent (the
  hypotheses under which `blowup` is defined).
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupIsoOffCenter` and `blowupIsoOffCenter_hom`: the
  explicit iso `(π ⁻¹ᵁ (X \ Y)).toScheme ≅ (X \ Y).toScheme` whose `hom` is `π ∣_ (X \ Y)`.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.coe_blowupProjection_preimage_centerCompl`
  (`rfl`): the carrier of `π ⁻¹ᵁ (X \ Y)` is `(π ⁻¹' Set.range Y.ι)ᶜ`, i.e. the source is
  `Bl_I(X) \ π^{-1}(Y)` with the genuine set-theoretic preimage.

## Proof (follows the book)

- `isIso_toSpecZero_of_forall_mem_eq_mul_pow`: for an `ℕ`-graded ring `A` with a degree-one
  non-zero-divisor `t` such that every degree-`n` element is `b tⁿ` with `b ∈ A₀`, the map
  `Proj.toSpecZero` is an iso: `t` spans the irrelevant ideal, so `D₊(t) = Proj A` and
  `Proj.awayι` is an iso; `A₀ → A_(t)` is bijective; then `Proj.awayι_toSpecZero`.
- `isIso_toSpecZero_reesGrading_of_eq_top`: the book's local identity
  `Proj O_U[T] ≅ U` in affine form: for `I = ⊤` the Rees algebra is `R[T]` (`t = X`).
- `ideal_eq_top_of_le_centerCompl`: `I|_U = O_U` in the form "`Γ(V, I) = ⊤` for each affine
  open `V ⊆ X \ Y`", via `IdealSheafData.mem_support_iff_of_mem` and
  `IsAffineOpen.self_le_iSup_basicOpen_iff`.
- `isIso_blowupProjection_restrict_of_isAffineOpen`: over such `V`, `π ∣_ V` is
  `restrictIso.hom ≫ projToOpen V` (`blowupRestrictIso_hom_projToOpen`, the compatibility of
  blowup formation with restriction recorded on the Rees/blowup article), and `projToOpen V` is
  `toSpecZero ≫ Spec.map (toZero V)` followed by the iso `Spec Γ(V) ≅ V`; `toZero V` is
  bijective by `S₀ = O_X`.
- `isIso_morphismRestrict_of_forall_exists` (generic helper): isomorphism is local on the
  target (`IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.of_forall_exists_morphismRestrict` plus
  `morphismRestrictRestrict`), giving the main theorem from the affine opens inside `X \ Y`.

## Notes

- The book's unlinked proof-dependency bullet (`Proj O_U[T] ≅ U`) is proved here as the affine
  statement `isIso_toSpecZero_reesGrading_of_eq_top`; there is no roadmap article for it.
- The book's statement does not mention the Noetherian/coherence hypotheses; they are the
  hypotheses of the blowup construction itself (Rees/blowup article), not extra assumptions.
