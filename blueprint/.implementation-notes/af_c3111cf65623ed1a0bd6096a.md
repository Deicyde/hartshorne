# The monoidal category of sheaves of modules

## Status

Added by a roadmap revision for the tilde-tensor article, whose completion
criterion (tilde is strong monoidal) needs this structure. The Lean below already
exists in a sorry-free module imported by the library root; nothing is recorded
in the frontmatter, so that a later Formalize pass records it after independent
review.

## Candidate declarations

Module `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSheafMonoidal`, for a ringed space given by
a topological space `X` and a sheaf `R` of commutative rings:

- `Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory` (an `instance`): the monoidal category
  structure on `SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf R)`. The structure is monoidal
  only: the module has no braided or symmetric instance, so the article does not
  claim symmetry.
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.tensorUnit_eq`: the unit object is the structure sheaf
  `SheafOfModules.unit (ringCatSheafOf R)` (by `rfl`).
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor`: `moduleSheafTensor F G ≅ F ⊗ G`,
  comparing the structure's tensor product with the sheafified tensor product of
  the module-tensor-product article; `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor_hom_bilinear`
  says it sends `f ⊗ g` to `tmulSection F G f g`.
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.schemeModulesMonoidalCategory` (an `instance`): the same
  structure on `Y.Modules` for a scheme `Y`.
- Section formulas backing the statement's description of the associator and
  unitors: `Hartshorne.Ringed.associator_hom_tmulSection`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.leftUnitor_hom_tmulSection`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.rightUnitor_hom_tmulSection` (the unitors send `r ⊗ f` and
  `f ⊗ r` to `r • f`), with `Hartshorne.Ringed.scheme_associator_hom_tmulSection` (and the whiskering formulas `scheme_whiskerLeft_tmulSection`, `scheme_whiskerRight_tmulSection`) restated for `Y.Modules`; the unitor formulas have no scheme restatement.
- Supporting: `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW_iff`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationW_isMonoidal`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationFunctor`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafificationFunctor_monoidal`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.forget_laxMonoidal`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.tensorπ`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.tmulSection`,
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.tensor_hom_ext`.

Recording suggestion: `lean: Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory
Hartshorne.Ringed.tensorUnit_eq Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor
Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor_hom_bilinear`, optionally with the
scheme instance and the three section formulas. The article's `declaration:` is
`instance` because the main target is declared with `instance`; audit checks the
kind.

## Construction

Mathlib's monoidal localization (`LocalizedMonoidal`, in
`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Localization/Monoidal/Basic.lean`) of the monoidal
category of presheaves of modules (`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Presheaf/Monoidal.lean`)
along sheafification. Stability of `sheafificationW` under tensor products is
checked on stalks (`Hartshorne.Ringed.W_iff_forall_isIso_stalk` and
`Hartshorne.ModuleSheafTensor.presheafTensorStalkIso`).
