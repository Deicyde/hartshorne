# Local rings of a fibre product

## Status

Not formalized. Neither the pinned Mathlib nor the project has it. Mathlib's
`Scheme.Pullback.Triplet` covers residue fields only, and its stability of
flat morphisms under base change is proved affine-locally without this
statement.

## Lean route

Candidate statement: for `f : X ⟶ Y`, `g : Y' ⟶ Y` and
`x' : pullback f g`, the ring map
`X.presheaf.stalk x ⊗[Y.presheaf.stalk y] Y'.presheaf.stalk y' → (pullback f g).presheaf.stalk x'`
induced by the stalk maps of `pullback.fst f g` and `pullback.snd f g`
(compatible by `pullback.condition` and `Scheme.Hom.stalkMap_congr_hom`)
satisfies `IsLocalization.AtPrime` for the comap of the maximal ideal; flatness
then follows from `IsLocalization.flat`. Mind the `ringSheafStalk` versus
`presheaf.stalk` instance-transparency issue if the statement must meet the
ringed-space layer used by `module-pullback-stalk`.

Route: choose affine opens `W ∋ y`, `V ∋ x` over `W` and `V' ∋ y'` over `W`.
The fibre product of `V` and `V'` over `W` is an open of `X'` containing `x'`
(`pullbackRestrictIsoRestrict`; project `Hartshorne.isPullback_fiberProductRestrictOpen`)
and is `Spec (Γ(V) ⊗_{Γ(W)} Γ(V'))` (`AlgebraicGeometry.pullbackSpecIso`).
Mathlib's `Scheme.stalkMapIsoOfIsPullback` (stalk maps across a pullback along
an open immersion) handles the open-immersion step,
`IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk` (or `StructureSheaf.IsLocalization.to_stalk`)
identifies stalks on an affine open with localizations, and a tensor product of
localizations is a localization of the tensor product
(transitivity of localization, `IsLocalization.localization_localization_isLocalization` in
`Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/LocalizationLocalization.lean`).

## Source note

Origin is `bridged`, grounded in the affine construction and open restriction
of Theorem II.3.3 (pp.87–88), which the source summary records. The earlier
split proposal also located this in the proof of Proposition III.9.2(b)
(p.254); `blueprint/sources/hartshorne-iii-9.md` does not record that proof's
steps, so that locator stays unverified and is not cited in the article.
