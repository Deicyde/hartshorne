# Projection from a point is a morphism

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectionFromPoint` (imports the coordinate-chart module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.CoordinateNonvanishingOpen`, `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.PicardGroup` for the
structure sheaf as an invertible module, and
`Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange` for
`Scheme.Modules.pullbackComp_hom_unit` and `Scheme.Modules.pullback_map_unit`).
`ℙⁿ_k` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme n (Spec (CommRingCat.of k))`; `k` is any field.

### General point and hyperplane

- Data: `p : Fin (n + 2) → k` (the point `P = projectivePoint k p _`) and `h : Fin (n + 2) → k`
  (the hyperplane `H = {linearSection k (n + 1) h = 0}`), with `h ⬝ᵥ p ≠ 0` (`P ∉ H`).
- `linearSection k m v = ∑ kConst _ (vⱼ) • xⱼ`; `kConst q` is the constant ring map
  `k → Γ(X, ⊤)` through the structure morphism `q`.
- `projectionVector k p h i = (h ⬝ᵥ p) • eᵢ − pᵢ • h`, `projectionLinearSections` the linear
  forms `sᵢ = h(P) xᵢ − pᵢ h`; `pointProjectionDomain` the union of their nonvanishing loci;
  `pointProjectionTwist`, `pointProjectionSections` the restrictions to it;
  `pointProjection k p h` the morphism given by Theorem 7.1(b)
  (`existsUnique_pointProjection`, `eq_pointProjection_iff`).
- `pointProjectionDomain_eq_compl_range`: `U_P = (range P)ᶜ`. Hard direction
  `eq_comp_projectivePoint_of_notMem`: for `g : T ⟶ ℙⁿ⁺¹_k` from a reduced scheme avoiding
  `U_P`, the pulled-back `sᵢ` vanish (`eq_zero_of_forall_notMem_nonvanishingLocus`), so
  `g^* xⱼ = (pⱼ / h(P)) g^* h`, `g^* h` trivializes `g^* 𝒪(1)`, and Theorem 7.1 uniqueness
  gives `g = (g ≫ proj) ≫ P`. Easy direction from
  `projectivePoint_mem_nonvanishingLocus_linearSection_iff` (the value of `∑ vⱼ xⱼ` at
  `(a)` is `v ⬝ᵥ a`, `iso_app_linearSection_projectivePoint`).
- `pullbackGlobalSection_pointProjection_linearSection`: `π^* h = 0` (from
  `sum_smul_projectionLinearSections`, `∑ hᵢ sᵢ = 0`, and `iso_app_linearSection`);
  `pointProjection_notMem_nonvanishingLocus`: the image misses the nonvanishing locus of `h`.
- `exists_lift_projectivePoint_comp_pointProjection`: for `h(P) q − h(q) p ≠ 0` (equivalently
  `q` is not a multiple of `p`, `projection_eq_zero_iff`), `(q)` lifts to `U_P` and goes to
  `(h(P) q − h(q) p)`.
- `pointProjection_isProjection`: the summary.
- `mem_nonvanishingLocus_pullbackGlobalSection_iff`: the nonvanishing locus of `f^* s` is
  `f⁻¹` of that of `s`.

### Coordinate case

`P = (0 : ⋯ : 0 : 1)`, `H = {x_{n+1} = 0}`: `projectionDomain n Y = ⨆_{i ≤ n} D₊(xᵢ)`,
`projectionFromPoint n Y : U ⟶ ℙⁿ_Y` defined by `x₀, …, xₙ` (any base `Y`),
`coordinatePoint k n`, `hyperplaneInclusion n Y` defined by `x₀, …, xₙ, 0`,
`projectionDomain_eq_compl_range`, `exists_lift_projectivePoint_comp_projectionFromPoint`
(`π(a) = (a₀ : ⋯ : aₙ)`), and `pointProjection_single_last`: the general projection for this
`P`, `H` is `isoOfEq ≫ projectionFromPoint ≫ hyperplaneInclusion`. Summary:
`projectionFromPoint_isProjection`.

### Helpers

`exists_iso_of_comp`, `exists_iso_of_comp_all` (if `φ` is defined by `(L, s)` then `f ≫ φ` is
defined by `(f^* L, f^* s)`); `pullbackGlobalSection_add/_sub/_sum/_smul/_zero`;
`structureModuleOf X` (`𝒪_X` as a module) and `structureModuleSection`.

## Partial

- The target of the general projection is `ℙⁿ⁺¹_k`, with image in `H` (scheme-theoretically,
  `π^* h = 0`). That `H ≅ ℙⁿ_k`, e.g. that `hyperplaneInclusion` is a closed immersion onto
  `H`, is not proved; it needs Hartshorne II, Proposition 7.2 (the projective closed-immersion
  chart criterion article). With it, `π_P` factors through `H` as a morphism to `ℙⁿ_k`.
- The geometric description `π(Q) = PQ ∩ H` is stated on `k`-rational points.
