# Pluricanonical rational maps and their image dimensions

## Lean

Modules: `Hartshorne.Scheme.LinearSystemRationalMap` (general linear-system layer) and
`Hartshorne.Surface.PluricanonicalMap` (the pluricanonical specialization).

Setting: `f : X ⟶ Spec k`, `hf : Projective f`, `hω : (canonicalSheaf f).IsInvertible`; the map
itself needs `[IsIntegral X]` (so that the complement of the base locus is nonempty, hence
dense). Hartshorne's nonsingular projective variety over an algebraically closed field satisfies
all of these; algebraic closedness is not used.

- `pluricanonicalSheaf f n = moduleSheafTensorPower n (canonicalSheaf f)` (`ω_X^{⊗ n}`),
  `pluricanonicalSystem f hf hω n = LinearSystem.complete hf _` (`|nK|`).
- Projective-space convention: the project identifies `P(V)` with `ℙᴺ_k` through an ordered
  spanning family (Theorem II.7.1 in the quotient convention, as in `linearSystemMorphism` and
  `LinearSystem.morphism`). Here the family is a chosen basis `pluricanonicalBasis` of
  `H⁰(X, ω_X^{⊗ n})`, `N = pluricanonicalProjDim f n = finrank - 1`. There is no intrinsic
  `Proj Sym V` target in the library for a vector space; a basis change composes with a linear
  automorphism of `ℙᴺ_k` (not formalized).
- The nonzero hypothesis is `h : ∃ s : CanonicalSectionRing.Component f n, s ≠ 0`, the same form
  as `kodairaDimension_nonneg_iff`. No map is defined without it.
- `pluricanonicalMap` is `linearSystemRationalMap`, the class (Mathlib `Scheme.RationalMap`) of
  `linearSystemPartialMap` = `linearSystemMorphism` on `linearSystemDomain`;
  `mem_pluricanonicalDomain_iff` identifies that domain with the non-base points of `|nK|`.
  The scheme-level rational map is Mathlib's, as in `Hartshorne.Rational.SchemeMaximalDomain`;
  the Chapter I `RatMap` of the rational-map article is for classical varieties and is not used.
- Image: `linearSystemImage` = closure of `Set.range (linearSystemMorphism ...)` as
  `Closeds ℙᴺ_k`; dimension `linearSystemImageDim` = `topologicalKrullDim` of that subspace.
  The reduced closed subscheme structure on the image is not constructed.
- Constructions are for every `n`; `n > 0` is the case of interest and is not a hypothesis.

## Facts

- Empty system: `pluricanonicalSystem_divisors_eq_empty_iff` (integral `X`),
  `pluricanonicalSystem_dim_eq_neg_one_iff`, from `LinearSystem.divisors_eq_empty_iff` and
  `LinearSystem.dim_eq_neg_one_iff`.
- One-dimensional: `pluricanonicalImageDim_eq_zero` (finrank `1`), from
  `linearSystemImageDim_eq_zero` (one nonzero section, target `ℙ⁰_k`) and
  `subsingleton_projectiveSpaceOverScheme_zero` (`ℙ⁰_k ≅ Proj k[x₀]` is the chart
  `D₊(x₀) ≅ Spec k[x₀]_{(x₀)}`, and `chartAwayEquiv` plus `MvPolynomial.isEmptyAlgEquiv` make that
  ring `k`).
- Fixed part: stated without divisor machinery, for a morphism `m : L' ⟶ L` of invertible sheaves
  and sections `sᵢ = m(s'ᵢ)` (multiplication by the canonical section of `𝒪(F)` for a fixed part
  `F` is the case in the article): `linearSystemDomain_map_le`, `linearSystemMorphism_map`
  (uniqueness in Theorem II.7.1 on the smaller domain, using `isIso_pullback_map_linearSystemDomain`:
  an epimorphism of invertible sheaves is an isomorphism, `isIso_of_epi_of_isInvertible`),
  `linearSystemRationalMap_map`, `linearSystemImage_map`; pluricanonical forms
  `pluricanonicalMap_eq_of_fixedPart`, `pluricanonicalImage_eq_of_fixedPart`. What is not
  formalized: producing `m`, `L' = 𝒪(nK - F)` and the `s'ᵢ` from the fixed component of `|nK|`
  (needs the fixed part of a linear system as a divisor and its section).

## Friction

- `Submodule.smul_mem_smul` on `𝔪_x • ⊤ ⊆ L_x` fails to find `IsScalarTower` unless
  `moduleSheafStalkModule L x` is put in context: the module instance on stalks synthesized in a
  late module differs from the one used in the definition of `nonvanishingLocus`.
- `Scheme.Modules` vs `SheafOfModules` category instances: `Epi` and `≫` must be stated in
  `X.Modules` (`@Quiver.Hom X.Modules _ ...`) to match `IsGeneratedBySections.epi` and
  `isIso_of_epi_of_isInvertible`.
