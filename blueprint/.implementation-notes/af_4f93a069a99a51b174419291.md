# A very ample power implies ampleness

## Lean

`Hartshorne.isAmple_of_isVeryAmpleRelative_moduleSheafTensorPower` in
`Hartshorne.Scheme.VeryAmplePowerImpliesAmple`. Hypotheses: `f : X ⟶ Spec A`
with `A` Noetherian and `FiniteType f`, `L` invertible, `0 < m`, and
`IsVeryAmpleRelative f (moduleSheafTensorPower m L)`. The conclusion is stated
under the Noetherian instance from `finiteType_isNoetherian f hf`, as in
`IsAmple.exists_isAffineOpen_nonvanishingLocus_of_finiteType`.

The finite-type hypothesis only supplies that instance. An open subscheme of a
scheme projective over a Noetherian ring is already Noetherian, so the
hypothesis could be dropped in favour of `[IsNoetherian X]`; it is kept to
match the book.

## Proof route

Unpack the immersion as `o : X ⟶ Z` (open) and `c : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_A` (closed). `Z` is
projective (`Projective.mk`), hence proper, hence finite type and Noetherian;
`c^* 𝒪(1)` is very ample on `Z` with the identity as open part. Extend a
coherent `F` to `Z` (`VeryAmplePower.exists_isCoherent_restrict_iso_of_isOpenImmersion`,
which transports along `o.isoOpensRange` and calls
`exists_isCoherent_restrict_iso_of_isNoetherian`), apply
`exists_isGeneratedBySections_tensorPowerTwist_of_projective` on `Z`, restrict
back with `moduleSheafTensorRestrictIso` and `moduleSheafTensorPowerRestrictIso`,
and finish with `isAmple_tfae`.

## Helpers and duplication

- `VeryAmplePower.tensorPowerIsoOfIso`: tensor powers of isomorphic sheaves are
  isomorphic, by induction via `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`. No such
  congruence existed in the project.
- `VeryAmplePower.isGloballyGenerated_restrict` and the local instance
  `VeryAmplePower.opensMap_final` copy the proof of `isGloballyGenerated_pullback`
  and the instance `opensMap_final` from `Hartshorne.Scheme.NormalizationDivisorDescent`,
  which is too heavy to import here. These are candidates for raising into
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.GloballyGenerated`.
