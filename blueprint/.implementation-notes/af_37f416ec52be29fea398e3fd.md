# The canonical sheaf of projective space

## Status

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23e), not closable in about 1.5 h. No Lean
was written, and nothing is recorded. The Euler sequence, the determinant of a short exact
sequence, and `Pic ℙⁿ ≅ ℤ` all exist. Two inputs are missing: the determinant of a direct sum of
line bundles, and the vanishing of global sections of negative twists (the article's
proof-dependency item).

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.EulerSequence.eulerSequence`: the sequence `0 → Ω → 𝒪(-1)^{n+1} → 𝒪 → 0` on
  `Proj A[x₀,…,xₙ]` is short exact. Here `Ω = Scheme.relativeDifferentials (toBase A n)` and
  `E = ⨁_{Fin (n+1)} 𝒪(-1)` (abbrevs `Ω`, `E`, `O1` in
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.EulerSequence(Maps)`).
- `Hartshorne.ExteriorPowerExactFiltration.exteriorPower_exact_det`: for a short exact sequence
  of locally free modules of ranks `n', n, n''`, `⋀^n S.X₂ ≅ ⋀^{n'} S.X₁ ⊗ ⋀^{n''} S.X₃`.
- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafExteriorPowerOneIso : ⋀^1 F ≅ F`, `Hartshorne.twistMulIso`
  (`𝒪(a) ⊗ 𝒪(b) ≅ 𝒪(a+b)`), `Hartshorne.projTwistingSheaf_isInvertible`.
- `Hartshorne.exists_unique_iso_projTwistingSheaf`, `Hartshorne.projectiveSpacePicardEquiv`:
  `Pic ℙⁿ_k ≅ ℤ` with `𝒪(l) ↦ l`.
- `Hartshorne.topologicalKrullDim_projectiveSpace` (`dim ℙⁿ = n`), and `Hartshorne.canonicalSheaf`
  / `canonicalSheaf_eq` and `Hartshorne.geometricGenus` /
  `Hartshorne.GlobalSectionsOver`.

## Route (no dualizing needed)

1. Local freeness: `𝒪(-1)^{n+1}` is locally free of rank `n+1` and `𝒪` of rank 1. Then `Ω` is
   locally free of rank `n`, being the kernel of a surjection of locally free sheaves, or by
   regularity of `ℙⁿ` and Theorem 8.15.
2. `exteriorPower_exact_det` on the Euler sequence gives `⋀^{n+1} E ≅ ⋀^n Ω ⊗ ⋀^1 𝒪 ≅ ω`, using
   `canonicalSheaf_eq` with `dim ℙⁿ = n`. The structure morphism must be matched with
   `toBase A n` for `A = k`.
3. **Gap:** `⋀^{m+1}(F ⊕ L) ≅ ⋀^m F ⊗ L` for `F` locally free of rank `m` and `L` invertible.
   This is the split exact sequence `F → F ⊕ L → L` plus `exteriorPower_exact_det`; then induct
   over `Fin (n+1)` on the biproduct `E`. With `twistMulIso` this gives
   `⋀^{n+1} E ≅ 𝒪(-n-1)`. Alternatively compute in `Pic` through `projectiveSpacePicardEquiv`.
4. **Gap:** `Γ(ℙⁿ_k, 𝒪(l)) = 0` for `l < 0` and `n ≥ 1`. One route uses
   `Hartshorne.ProjectiveSpacePicard.twistToK` (injective) and `le_ord_twistToFunctionField`: a
   global section gives a rational function `φ` with `div φ ≥ -l·H`, which is effective of
   positive degree. That contradicts degree zero of principal divisors
   (`Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceWeilDivisorDegree_principalDivisor`) unless `φ = 0`. Then
   `p_g = finrank k 0 = 0`.
