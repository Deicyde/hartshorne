# Normal and conormal sheaves

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.NormalConormalSheaves`.

- The conormal sheaf reuses the conormal sheaf of an immersion,
  `Hartshorne.immersionConormalSheaf` (recorded on the immersion conormal sheaf article), defined
  through the closed immersion `f.liftCoborder` into `f.coborderRange`.
- `Hartshorne.immersionConormalSheafIso`: for any immersion `f`, `immersionConormalSheaf f` is
  `f^* I_f` with `I_f` the ideal sheaf of `f` itself (restriction along the open immersion
  `f.coborderRange.ι`, via `ConormalIndependence.conormalRestrictIso`).
- `Hartshorne.immersionConormalSheafPushforwardIso`: for a closed immersion `i` with ideal sheaf
  `J`, `i_* N^*_{Y/X} ≅ J/J²` (`idealSheafSqQuotient i`), the book's `N^* = J/J²`.
- `Hartshorne.normalSheaf`: `Ringed.moduleSheafDual` of the conormal sheaf, the dual of the
  finite-locally-free-duality article.
- `Hartshorne.immersionConormalSheaf_isLocallyFreeOfRank`: transport of the rank statement of
  `NonsingularConormal.nonsingular_closedImmersion_conormal` along `immersionConormalSheafIso`.
- `Hartshorne.normalSheaf_isLocallyFreeOfRank`: the rank statement for the normal sheaf, by
  `Ringed.IsLocallyFreeOfRank.moduleSheafDual` (recorded on the tangent sheaf article; hence the
  Proof depends on link to it).

Hypotheses follow the Theorem 8.17 formalization: `X` irreducible, regular, of finite type over
an algebraically closed `k`; `Y` irreducible and regular; `r = codim (Y, X)`. Separatedness is not
used.
