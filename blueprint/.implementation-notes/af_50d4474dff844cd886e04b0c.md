# Torsion étale cohomology

## Partial progress (cohomology groups, checked)

The cohomology-group clause is formalized, without `sorry`, in the module
`Hartshorne.Weil.TorsionEtaleCohomology`:

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.torsionEtaleCohomology X ℓ r i : Ab.{u}` is
  `((Sheaf.Γ X.smallEtaleTopology Ab.{u}).rightDerived i).obj
  (X.constantTorsionEtaleSheaf ℓ r)`, i.e. Mathlib's classical right derived
  functor of the global sections functor (the right adjoint of the constant
  sheaf functor), evaluated at the constant sheaf of the dependency.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.additive_sheafΓ_smallEtaleTopology`: global
  sections are additive (a right adjoint preserves binary products).
- Injective resolutions come from Mathlib: the sheaf category is Grothendieck
  abelian (transported from the essentially small affine étale site), hence has
  enough injectives.

Universe choice: for `X : Scheme.{u}` the sheaf category has morphisms in
`Type (u + 1)` (the small étale site is a large category), but global sections
land in `Ab.{u}`, so the groups are `u`-small. The coefficient group is the
dependency's `ULift`ed `ZMod (ℓ ^ r)`.

These declarations are not yet recorded in the article frontmatter, because the
second clause (functoriality in morphisms of schemes) is missing.

## Remaining goal

For `f : X ⟶ Y`, the pullback map
`H^i_et(Y, ℤ/ℓ^r) ⟶ H^i_et(X, ℤ/ℓ^r)` induced by the inverse image along the
morphism of small étale sites.

## Route

1. The base-change functor `Y.Etale ⥤ X.Etale`, `V ↦ V ×_Y X` (Mathlib's
   `MorphismProperty.Over.pullback`; at the pinned Mathlib the instance
   `HasPullbacksAlong @Etale f` it needs is not synthesized with the étale
   site imports), and its continuity for the small étale topologies. Mathlib
   proves continuity of `Over.pullback` only for the big slice topologies
   `J.over X`.
2. The inverse image `f⁻¹ : Sheaf Y.smallEtaleTopology Ab.{u} ⥤ Sheaf
   X.smallEtaleTopology Ab.{u}`, left adjoint to the pushforward
   `sheafPushforwardContinuous`. Mathlib's `Functor.sheafPullback` needs this
   adjoint to exist; the Kan-extension construction needs colimits over comma
   categories that are only essentially small, so in `Ab.{u}` it has to go
   through the essentially small affine étale site (or an essential smallness
   result for `X.Etale`, absent from Mathlib).
3. Exactness of `f⁻¹` (at least: it preserves monomorphisms), so that `f⁻¹`
   of an injective resolution is a resolution, or equivalently the pushforward
   preserves injectives.
4. `f⁻¹` of the constant sheaf `ℤ/ℓ^r` on `Y` is the constant sheaf on `X`:
   compare left adjoints, using that the pushforward commutes with global
   sections because base change preserves the terminal object.
5. The map on derived functors: unit `F ⟶ f_* f⁻¹ F`, then compare
   `f⁻¹` of an injective resolution with an injective resolution on `X`. The
   project article `cohomology/derived-functors/resolution-to-injective-comparison`
   supplies the comparison map, but this article's dependencies do not reach
   it.

## Missing prerequisites

No roadmap article provides the morphism of small étale sites induced by a
morphism of schemes (items 1 to 4). The article's "Proof depends on" names
"the coefficient sheaf's inverse-image functor on the small étale site" only
as prose, with no linked article. Needed new roadmap prerequisites (to be added
under `weil-conjectures/etale-cohomology/foundations/`):

- inverse image of abelian sheaves along a morphism of schemes on small étale
  sites, left adjoint to the pushforward, and its exactness;
- compatibility of this inverse image with constant sheaves;
- a dependency edge to
  `cohomology/derived-functors/resolution-to-injective-comparison` (or an
  equivalent) for the induced map on right derived functors.
