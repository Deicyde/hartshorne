# Blowups are invariant under invertible factors

## Status

Complete candidate (pending review). Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.BlowupInvertibleFactorInvariance`.

Headline: `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupMulIso` and
`Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupMulIso_hom_blowupProjection`: for `[IsNoetherian X]`,
`I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` with `hI : IsCoherent I.toModules` and
`L : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` with `hL : L.toModules.IsInvertible`, an isomorphism
`(I * L).blowup (isCoherent_mul I L) ≅ I.blowup hI` whose hom commutes with the two
`blowupProjection`s.

## Encoding

- The product `I * L` of quasi-coherent ideal subsheaves did not exist; it is a new `Mul`
  instance (`QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.instMul`) defined through
  `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv` from Mathlib's product of `IdealSheafData`. Its affine sections
  are `ideal_mul`: `(I * L).ideal U = I.ideal U * L.ideal U`. Coherence on a Noetherian scheme
  (`isCoherent_mul`) is the same argument as `isCoherent_pow` (`isCoherent_powSmul` over `⊤`).
- "Invertible sheaf of ideals" is `L.toModules.IsInvertible` (the ideal sheaf, as an
  `𝒪_X`-module, is invertible). This is the hypothesis Lemma 7.9's `lineBundleTwist` takes
  for `L.toModules`.

## Route

1. Algebra (`reesScale`, `reesScale_bijective`, `projReesScaleIso`, `projReesScaleIso_eq`): for
   `K = J * span {f}` with `f` a non-zero-divisor, `p(t) ↦ p(f t)` is a graded ring isomorphism
   `reesGrading J ≅ reesGrading K`; two non-zero-divisor generators of the same principal ideal
   differ by a unit `u`, and `reesScale (u f) = reesScale f ∘ SegreProduct.scale u`, which is the
   identity on `Proj` (`SegreProduct.projMap_scale`).
2. Frames (`ideal_eq_span_frameGen`, `frameGen_mem_nonZeroDivisors`): a frame `e` of
   `L.toModules` over `U` gives `L.ideal U = span {f}` with `f = (e default).1` a
   non-zero-divisor.
3. Local isomorphisms over framed affines: `projReesMulIsoOfFrame` (from step 1), frame
   independence, naturality along basic-open inclusions (`reesMap_comp_reesScaleRingHom`), and
   compatibility with `Proj.toSpecZero`. `projReesMulTwistIso` composes it with the inverse of
   Lemma 7.9's local isomorphism `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.framedIso`, giving
   `Proj Γ(U, Rees(I·L)) ≅ Proj Γ(U, Rees(I)·L)`.
4. `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.framedGlueIso` (generic): compatible isomorphisms
   `Proj A(U) ≅ Proj B(U)` over the framed affines glue along `framedCover` to
   `A.relProj ≅ B.relProj`, over `X` when the local ones are over `Spec Γ(U, 𝒪_X)`. This is the
   gluing pattern of `lineBundleTwistIso`, stated for arbitrary `A`, `B`.
5. `blowupMulIso = framedGlueIso … ≪≫ I.reesAlgebra.lineBundleTwistIso hL`; over `X` by
   `lineBundleTwistIso_hom_relProjToBase` and `framedGlueIso_hom_relProjToBase`.

## Notes

- The identification of `Rees(I·L)` with `Rees(I)·L` is made at the level of `Proj` over the
  framed affines, not as an isomorphism of quasi-coherent graded algebras on all affine opens: the
  sections of `Sym(L)` over an affine open on which `L` has no frame are only accessible through
  the sheafification, so a canonical map `Γ(U, Sym(L)) → Γ(U, 𝒪_X)[t]` on every affine open was
  not available. On a framed affine the underlying graded ring isomorphism is
  `a ⊗ fⁿ ↦ fⁿ a tⁿ`.
- Lemma 7.9 is used for its global isomorphism `lineBundleTwistIso` and its local pieces
  (`framedIso`, `framedIso_naturality`, `framedIso_hom_toSpec`, `framedCover`,
  `isLocalFrame_restrictFrame`).
