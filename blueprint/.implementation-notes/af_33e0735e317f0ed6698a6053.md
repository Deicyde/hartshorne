# Open extension by zero blocker

The source-shaped construction itself is viable.  Model the conditional
presheaf over `V : Opens X` by the additive subgroup of
`F(j⁻¹V)` consisting of sections satisfying `V ⊆ U ∨ s = 0`, then
sheafify it.  A checked scratch implementation also gives the canonical map
to `j_*F` and proves the claimed on- and off-open stalk isomorphisms using
`TopCat.Presheaf.exists_le_germ_eq`,
`TopCat.Presheaf.stalkFunctor_map_unit_toSheafify_isIso`, and
`TopCat.Presheaf.stalkPushforward.stalkPushforward_iso_of_isInducing`.

The remaining restriction and uniqueness clauses need a prerequisite not
reached by the current DAG: the ordinary extension-by-zero adjunction

`Hom_X(j_!F, G) ≃ Hom_U(F, j^*G)`.

Pinned Mathlib has `TopCat.Sheaf.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction`, but that is the
opposite adjunction `j^* ⊣ j_*`.  It also has
`Topology.IsOpenEmbedding.sheafPullbackIso`, which compares standard pullback
with naive restriction.  The generic construction
`CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback` supplies an
abstract left adjoint to naive restriction by sheafifying a left Kan
extension, but no reached theorem identifies that left Kan extension with the
article's concrete conditional presheaf.  Without that comparison, replacing
the stated construction by the abstract adjoint would not preserve the
definition.

Roadmap should add either the displayed Hom equivalence directly for the
conditional presheaf or a natural isomorphism from its sheafification to that
generic left adjoint.  Its unit must identify `j^*j_!F` with `F`; this yields
the restriction equivalence and the compatible uniqueness theorem.

The article also needs to make the compatibility data explicit.  As written,
"unique up to unique isomorphism" is false: when `U = X`, any sheaf `F` with a
nontrivial automorphism has both the identity and that automorphism as
self-isomorphisms while satisfying all listed stalk properties.  A correct
statement equips a candidate `G` with a fixed isomorphism `j^*G ≅ F` and
asserts a unique isomorphism `j_!F ≅ G` whose restriction is compatible
with that fixed identification.  This should be a theorem about the
construction, not a theorem stored as a structure field.
