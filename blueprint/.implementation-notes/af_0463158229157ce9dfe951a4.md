# Smoothness by tangent-map surjectivity: implementation note

## Route of the recorded proof

`Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.smoothOfRelativeDimension_tfae_of_isRegularAt` follows the printed
proof (pp. 270–271):

- (1) ⇒ (2): Example 10.0.2,
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`.
- (2) ⇒ (3): `Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.finrank_cotangentSpace_eq_of_isRegularAt` gives the
  cotangent dimensions from "X and Y are both smooth over k"
  (`Hartshorne.smoothOfRelativeDimension_iff_forall_isRegularAt`, Example 10.0.3), local freeness
  of `Ω_{X/k}` and `Ω_{Y/k}` (Example 10.0.2) and II.8.7 at closed points
  (`Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.moduleFiberDim_relativeDifferentials_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace`, from
  `Hartshorne.localCotangentToDifferentials_bijective`). The dimension count at a closed point is
  `Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.injective_localCotangentBaseChange_iff_of_isClosed`.
- (3) ⇒ (1): flatness from regular parameters and Lemma 10.3A
  (`Hartshorne.RegularCotangentFlat.flat_stalkMap_of_surjective_zariskiTangentMap`); the generic
  fibre bound from II.8.6A (`Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.le_finrank_kaehler_genericPoint`, through
  `Hartshorne.trdeg_le_rank_kaehlerDifferential`); freeness of `Ω_{𝒪_{X,x}/𝒪_{Y,y}}` at closed
  points from II.8.9 (`Hartshorne.free_of_finrank_residueField_eq_finrank_fractionField`); every
  fibre then has dimension `n` by
  `Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.moduleFiberDim_relativeDifferentials_eq_of_specializes`.

## Remaining divergence: the sequence of II.8.11

The closed-point count uses the transitivity sequence of the local rings
(`Hartshorne.CotangentKaehler.injective_localCotangentBaseChange_iff`), not the recorded
scheme-level sequence `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials_transitivity_exact`.
Using the latter needs, at a closed point `x` over `y`, a commutative square identifying the
fibre map `k(x) ⊗ (f^*Ω_{Y/k})_x → k(x) ⊗ (Ω_{X/k})_x` with the cotangent map
`k(x) ⊗ 𝔪_y/𝔪_y² → 𝔪_x/𝔪_x²`:

- the stalk of `relativeDifferentials` at `x` as the Kähler module of the stalk, compatibly with
  `d` (the library has only the equality of fibre dimensions,
  `Hartshorne.SmoothTangent.finrank_fibre_relativeDifferentials_eq_stalk`);
- the element formula for `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkLinearEquiv` on `1 ⊗ dg`, together with
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp_unit_d`;
- right exactness of `M ↦ k(x) ⊗ M_x` (the stalk functor and extension of scalars are left
  adjoints), to take the fibre of the cokernel presentation
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentialsTransitivityIsColimit`.
