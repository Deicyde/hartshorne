# Finitely generated modules over a regular local ring

## Status

New article from the regular-localization split (roadmap revision). The
project already proves the result, sorry-free, but the article records no
declaration yet: a later Formalize pass records it after independent review.

## Candidate Lean

In the module `Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalGlobalDimension`:

- `Hartshorne.RegularLocal.hasProjectiveDimensionLE_of_isRegularLocalRing`:
  for `[IsRegularLocalRing R]` and a finitely generated `M : ModuleCat R`,
  `HasProjectiveDimensionLE M (maximalIdeal R).spanFinrank`. The bound is the
  minimal number of generators of the maximal ideal, which equals
  `ringKrullDim R` by `isRegularLocalRing_iff`; the reviewer should accept
  this as the article's $\dim A$ or a Formalize pass should add a corollary
  stated with `ringKrullDim`.

Its proof combines `Hartshorne.RegularLocal.projectiveDimension_residueField`
(residue-field-projective-dimension) with
`Hartshorne.LocalGlobalDimension.hasProjectiveDimensionLE_of_residueField`
(local-projective-dimension-residue-bound), matching the article's Proof
depends on.

## Consumers

- regular-localization: `hasProjectiveDimensionLE_residueField_localization`
  applies it to `R ⧸ 𝔭`.
- regular-locally-factorial: the stable-freeness step of the UFD proof
  (`Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalFactorial`) uses it.
- regular-local-projective-test-by-base (Hartshorne III, Exercise 6.6(a))
  concerns finitely generated modules only, so this article replaces the
  all-modules global-dimension article (still not ready, source-blocked) as
  its proof prerequisite.
