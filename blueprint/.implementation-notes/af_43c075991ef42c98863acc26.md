# External products of cycles

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Intersection.ExternalProduct`.

- `Hartshorne.externalProduct pX pY r s` is the bilinear map
  `codimensionCycles X r →+ codimensionCycles Y s →+ codimensionCycles (pullback pX pY) (r + s)`.
  The setting is `k` algebraically closed, `X`, `Y` integral, and `pX`, `pY` locally of finite
  type. Separatedness and quasi-compactness are not needed. The product `X × Y` is the fibre
  product over `Spec k`, not the Chapter I Segre product.
- `Hartshorne.externalProductPoint pX pY x y` is the generic point of `V × W`, and
  `Hartshorne.closure_externalProductPoint` identifies its closure with
  `fst⁻¹ (closure {x}) ∩ snd⁻¹ (closure {y})`. `Hartshorne.codim_closure_externalProductPoint`
  gives `codim (V × W) = codim V + codim W`.
- `Hartshorne.externalProduct_apply_of_eq_single` gives the value on generators,
  `[V] ⊗ [W] ↦ [V × W]`, and `Hartshorne.coe_externalProduct` gives the bilinear formula.

## Route

The local computation is algebra on an affine chart. `ExternalProduct.exists_chart` identifies
`Spec (Γ(X, U) ⊗_k Γ(Y, V))` with an open of the fibre product, using `pullbackSpecIso` and
`pullback.map` of the two `fromSpec` maps. The `k`-algebra structure on `Γ(X, U)` is
`Spec.preimage (hU.fromSpec ≫ pX)`, as in `BaseExtensionComponentDimension.exists_baseExtension_chart`.

- `ExternalProduct.isDomain_tensorProduct`: over an algebraically closed field, `A ⊗_k B` is a
  domain for finitely generated domains `A`, `B`. It is transported from the coordinate ring of
  an affine product (Remark 1.4.6 and Exercise I.3.15(a),(b)). No Mathlib result gives this.
- `ExternalProduct.height_tensorKer`: `ht (ker (A ⊗ B → A/𝔭 ⊗ B/𝔮)) = ht 𝔭 + ht 𝔮`. It follows
  from Theorem 1.8A(b) in `A ⊗ B`, `A` and `B` together with `ringKrullDim_tensorProduct`.
- `ExternalProduct.exists_closure_eq_preimage_inter`: the chart-wise generic points from two
  charts have equal finite coheights and one specializes to the other, so they coincide. Hence
  `V × W` is irreducible.
