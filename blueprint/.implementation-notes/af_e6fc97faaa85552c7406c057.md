# Base change of a module sheaf flat over a base

## Status

Not stated or proved in Lean. The statement elaborates over the formalized
notion of flatness over a base: for `f : X ⟶ Y`, `g : Y' ⟶ Y` and
`F : X.Modules`, the hypothesis is `F.FlatOver f` and the conclusion is
`((Scheme.Modules.pullback (pullback.fst f g)).obj F).FlatOver (pullback.snd f g)`.
The proof needs two stalk computations. The first is now formalized (below).
The second, the local ring of a fibre product, is still missing and is the size
of an article.

## Partial progress (checked Lean, sorry-free, imported by the library root)

Module `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSkyscraper`, for a ringed space given by a
sheaf `R` of commutative rings on `X : TopCat`:

- `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSkyscraper x : ModuleCat (ringSheafStalk R x) ⥤ SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf R)`,
  the skyscraper module sheaf at `x` (object `moduleSkyscraperObj`, sheaf
  condition `skyscraperAbPresheaf_isSheaf`, morphisms `moduleSkyscraperMap`);
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleStalkFunctor x`, the stalk as an
  `𝒪_{X,x}`-module, functorially (`moduleStalkFunctor_map_germ`);
- `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleStalkSkyscraperAdjunction x : moduleStalkFunctor x ⊣ moduleSkyscraper x`,
  with `moduleStalkSkyscraperAdjunction_homEquiv_apply` (a linear map
  `F_x → N` goes to the morphism sending a section to the image of its germ).

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ModulePullbackStalk`, for a morphism of schemes
`h : X' ⟶ X` and `x' : X'`:

- `Hartshorne.pushforwardSkyscraperIso h x'`: `h_*` of the skyscraper at `x'`
  is the skyscraper at `h x'` of the restriction of scalars along the stalk map;
- `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkIso h x' : Scheme.Modules.pullback h ⋙ moduleStalkFunctor x' ≅ moduleStalkFunctor (h x') ⋙ ModuleCat.extendScalars (h.stalkMap x').hom`,
  i.e. `(h^*F)_{x'} ≅ 𝒪_{X',x'} ⊗_{𝒪_{X,h x'}} F_{h x'}` naturally in `F`, by
  uniqueness of left adjoints (`pullbackStalkAdjunction`,
  `stalkExtendScalarsAdjunction`);
- `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkLinearEquiv h x' F`, the same as an
  `𝒪_{X',x'}`-linear equivalence.

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.Flatness.FlatTensorFlatAlgebra`:

- `Hartshorne.flat_tensorProduct_of_flat_algebra`: for ring maps
  `B' → S → C` with `C` flat over `S`, and an `S`-module `N` flat over `B'`,
  `C ⊗_S N` is flat over `B'`. This is the flatness step of the final algebra
  below, with `S = A ⊗_B B'` and `N = B' ⊗_B F_x`.

## Remaining goal

- Local rings of a fibre product. For `x' ∈ X' = X ×_Y Y'` over `x ∈ X`,
  `y' ∈ Y'` and `y ∈ Y`, the ring `𝒪_{X',x'}` is a localization (in particular
  a flat algebra) of `𝒪_{X,x} ⊗_{𝒪_{Y,y}} 𝒪_{Y',y'}`, compatibly with the stalk
  maps of both projections. Neither the pinned Mathlib nor the roadmap has it.
  Mathlib's `Scheme.Pullback.Triplet` covers residue fields only. Its stability
  of flat morphisms under base change is proved affine-locally. Route: choose
  affine opens `W ∋ y`, `V ∋ x` over `W` and `V' ∋ y'` over `W`. The fibre
  product of `V` and `V'` over `W` is an open of `X'` containing `x'`
  (`pullbackRestrictIsoRestrict`, project `fiber-product-restrict-open`), and
  is `Spec (Γ(V) ⊗_{Γ(W)} Γ(V'))`. Open immersions preserve stalks, the stalks
  of `Spec` are localizations, and a tensor product of localizations is a
  localization of the tensor product.
- Final algebra. Write `A = 𝒪_{X,x}`, `B = 𝒪_{Y,y}`, `B' = 𝒪_{Y',y'}`,
  `C = 𝒪_{X',x'}` and `R = A ⊗_B B'`. If `F_x` is flat over `B` and `C` is flat
  over `R`, then `C ⊗_A F_x ≅ C ⊗_R (B' ⊗_B F_x)` is flat over `B'`, by
  `flat_tensorProduct_of_flat_algebra` with `N = B' ⊗_B F_x` (flat over `B'`
  by `Module.Flat.baseChange`). Still to build: the `C`-linear isomorphism
  `C ⊗_A F_x ≅ C ⊗_R (B' ⊗_B F_x)`, which sends `c ⊗ m` to `c ⊗ (1 ⊗ m)`. Here
  `B' ⊗_B F_x` is an `R`-module through the tensor-product action of
  `A ⊗_B B'` on `F_x ⊗_B B'`. Then transport along
  `pullbackStalkLinearEquiv`, which is `C`-linear and hence `B'`-linear for the
  structure through the stalk map of `pullback.snd f g`. This also needs
  `pullback.condition` on stalk maps (`Scheme.Hom.stalkMap_congr_hom`).

## Split proposal (Roadmap)

0. New small article "Skyscraper module sheaves and the stalk adjunction", in
   `schemes/modules-and-quasicoherent`. Statement: for a ringed space, a point
   $x$ and an $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$-module $N$, the skyscraper sheaf with sections
   $N$ over opens containing $x$ and $0$ otherwise is an $\mathcal{O}_X$-module,
   and the stalk functor $F \mapsto F_x$ (to $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$-modules) is left
   adjoint to the skyscraper functor. Depends on: sheaves-of-modules,
   stalks-and-germs. Records `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSkyscraper`,
   `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleStalkFunctor`,
   `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleStalkSkyscraperAdjunction`. Already formalized here.
1. New article "Stalks of the pullback of a module sheaf", in
   `schemes/modules-and-quasicoherent`. Statement: for a morphism of schemes
   $h : X' \to X$, an $\mathcal{O}_X$-module $F$ and $x' \in X'$, there is an
   isomorphism of $\mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$-modules
   $(h^*F)_{x'} \cong \mathcal{O}_{X',x'} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,h(x')}} F_{h(x')}$,
   natural in $F$. Source: Hartshorne II.5, p.110 (definition
   $h^*F = h^{-1}F \otimes_{h^{-1}\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{O}_{X'}$, with stalks
   of inverse images and of tensor products). Depends on:
   module-push-pull-adjunction, schemes/sheaves/stalks-and-germs. Proof depends
   on: (0) and extension/restriction of scalars along a ring map (Mathlib
   `ModuleCat.extendRestrictScalarsAdj`). The module tensor product article is
   not used by the Lean proof, so it is dropped. Records
   `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkIso` (and `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkLinearEquiv`).
   Already formalized here.
2. New article "Local rings of a fibre product", in `schemes/fiber-products`.
   Statement: for $x' \in X \times_Y Y'$ over $x$, $y'$, $y$, the local ring
   $\mathcal{O}_{X \times_Y Y', x'}$ is the localization of
   $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{Y,y}} \mathcal{O}_{Y',y'}$ at a prime
   ideal, compatibly with the two projections. Source: Hartshorne II.3,
   Theorem 3.3 (construction of fibre products, p.87), local form, together
   with the proof of III.9.2(b) (p.254), which uses this localization
   statement. The p.254 locator was not confirmed against the summary source
   file `blueprint/sources/hartshorne-iii-9.md`, so the article should be
   marked derived unless the locator is verified. Depends on:
   schemes-have-fiber-products, affine-fiber-product,
   fiber-product-restrict-open. Proof depends on:
   morphisms/local-ring-is-localization. Lean route: Mathlib's
   `Scheme.stalkMapIsoOfIsPullback` (stalk maps across a pullback along an open
   immersion) reduces the statement to affine opens, where it is the
   localization of `Γ(V) ⊗_{Γ(W)} Γ(V')`. Not formalized.
