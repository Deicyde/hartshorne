# Base change of a module sheaf flat over a base

## Status

Not stated or proved in Lean. The statement elaborates over the formalized
notion of flatness over a base: for `f : X ⟶ Y`, `g : Y' ⟶ Y` and
`F : X.Modules`, the hypothesis is `F.FlatOver f` and the conclusion is
`((Scheme.Modules.pullback (pullback.fst f g)).obj F).FlatOver (pullback.snd f g)`.
The proof is blocked on two stalk computations that neither the pinned Mathlib
nor the roadmap provides.

## Missing prerequisites

- Stalks of the module pullback. For a morphism of schemes $h : X' \to X$, an
  $\mathcal{O}_X$-module $F$ and $x' \in X'$, the stalk
  $(h^*F)_{x'}$ is $F_{h(x')} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,h(x')}} \mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$,
  as $\mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$-modules. Mathlib defines `Scheme.Modules.pullback`
  (through `SheafOfModules.pullback` and `PresheafOfModules.pullback`) as an
  abstract left adjoint of the pushforward, with no description of its
  sections or stalks. No roadmap article states this computation. The
  articles on restriction stalks and on stalks of the associated sheaf treat
  only sheaves of abelian groups, with no module structure. A
  plausible route is adjoint uniqueness: the stalk at $x'$ of $h^*$ and
  $(-)_{h(x')} \otimes \mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$ are both left adjoint to
  "skyscraper at $h(x')$ of an $\mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$-module, viewed as an
  $\mathcal{O}_{X,h(x')}$-module". This needs skyscraper sheaves of modules and
  a module-level stalk/skyscraper adjunction, which are also absent. Mathlib
  has these only for sheaves valued in a fixed category, in
  `Topology/Sheaves/Skyscraper.lean` and `CategoryTheory/Sites/Point/Skyscraper.lean`.
- Local rings of a fibre product. For $x' \in X' = X \times_Y Y'$ over
  $x \in X$, $y' \in Y'$ and $y \in Y$, the ring $\mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$ is a
  localization of $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{Y,y}} \mathcal{O}_{Y',y'}$
  at a prime, compatibly with the stalk maps of both projections. The pinned
  Mathlib does not have this. Its stability of flat morphisms under base change
  is proved affine-locally, not through stalks. No article under
  `schemes/fiber-products` states it.

Suggested new roadmap articles: one for the stalk of the module pullback, in
`schemes/modules-and-quasicoherent` and depending on the module push-pull
adjunction, and one for the local ring of a fibre product, in
`schemes/fiber-products`. This article would then depend on both.

## Proof route once the prerequisites exist

Write $B = \mathcal{O}_{Y,y}$, $A = \mathcal{O}_{X,x}$, $B' = \mathcal{O}_{Y',y'}$
and $C = \mathcal{O}_{X',x'}$. Then $(g'^*F)_{x'} \cong F_x \otimes_A C$ and
$C = S^{-1}(A \otimes_B B')$. So, with $R = A \otimes_B B'$,
$F_x \otimes_A C \cong (F_x \otimes_B B') \otimes_R C$. Here
$F_x \otimes_B B'$ is flat over $B'$ by `Module.Flat.baseChange`, and $C$ is
flat over $R$ by `IsLocalization.flat`. Tensoring a flat $B'$-module (which is
also an $R$-module) over $R$ with a flat $R$-algebra keeps it flat over $B'$,
because the two tensor functors compose. The
$B'$-module structure obtained this way must also be matched with the one that
flatness over a base uses, namely restriction of scalars along the stalk map of
$f'$.
