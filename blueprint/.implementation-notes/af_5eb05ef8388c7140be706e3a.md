# Base change for differential sheaves

## Status

Not formalized. A bounded attempt fixed the route below; no Lean was committed.

## Canonical map

Write `g' : X' ⟶ X` and `f' : X' ⟶ Y'` for the projections, and `h : Y' ⟶ Y`, so that
`g' ≫ f = f' ≫ h`. The map `g'^*Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ Ω_{X'/Y'}` is the composite of:

1. `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp g' f : g'^*Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ Ω_{X'/Y}`,
   where `Ω_{X'/Y}` is taken for `g' ≫ f`;
2. `relativeDifferentialsCongr` along `g' ≫ f = f' ≫ h`;
3. `relativeDifferentialsCompToLeft f' h : Ω_{X'/Y} ⟶ Ω_{X'/Y'}`.

Compatibility with the universal derivations, `d_{X'/Y'}(g'^♯ b) = image of 1 ⊗ d_{X/Y} b`, follows
from `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp_unit_d`, `relativeDifferentialsCongr_hom_d` and
`relativeDifferentialsCompToLeft_d`.

## Remaining goal: the map is an isomorphism

Both sides are quasi-coherent:
- `Ω` by `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials_isQuasicoherent`;
- the pullback by `Hartshorne.isQuasicoherent_pullback`.

So it suffices to check on an affine cover of `X'`. Take affine `V ⊆ Y`, `U ⊆ f⁻¹V`, and
`V' ⊆ h⁻¹V`. Then `U ×_V V' ⊆ X'` is affine with ring `B ⊗_A A'`, and these opens cover `X'`.
On such an open:

- `Ω_{X/Y}|_U ≅ (Ω_{B/A})~` by `AlgebraicGeometry.RelDiffLocal.affineIso`. That goes through
  `RelDiffLocal.restrictIso` (restriction to opens over opens) and
  `Scheme.relativeDifferentialsSpecIso`.
- `g'^*` of a tilde is a tilde by `Hartshorne.tildePullbackIso`, together with restriction of
  pullbacks to opens.
- `Ω_{(B⊗A')/A'} ≅ (B ⊗ A') ⊗_B Ω_{B/A}` by Mathlib's `KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquiv`
  (recorded on kahler-differential-base-change).

The main work is showing that, under these identifications, the canonical map becomes Mathlib's
base-change equivalence. It suffices to compare the two maps on the generators `1 ⊗ d b`, using
the derivation compatibilities above and `RelDiffLocal.affineIso` on `d`.

## Alternative

Prove that `g'^*Ω_{X/Y}` corepresents `Y'`-derivations of `𝒪_{X'}`, as
`RelDiffLocal.restrictCorepresentable` does for open restrictions. This needs a sheaf-level
description of derivations on a fibre product, which the project does not have.
