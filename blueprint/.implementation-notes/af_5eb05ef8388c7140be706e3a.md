# Base change for differential sheaves

## Status

Not formalized. A second bounded attempt committed the canonical map and most of the local
ingredients in `Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange.lean`. The library
root does not import that module yet. The isomorphism statement is still open.

## Done (all in `RelativeDifferentialsBaseChange.lean`)

Notation: `g' = pullback.fst f h`, `f' = pullback.snd f h`.

The canonical map and its basic properties:
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.baseChangeRelativeDifferentialsMap f h : g'^*Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ Ω_{X'/Y'}`. It
  is `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp`, then `relativeDifferentialsCongr pullback.condition`,
  then `relativeDifferentialsCompToLeft`.
- `baseChangeRelativeDifferentialsMap_unit_d`: on any open `V ⊆ X`, the map sends the pulled-back
  section `unit (d b)` to `d (g'^♯ b)`. The unit here is
  `(Modules.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction g').unit`.
- `pullback_relativeDifferentials_hom_ext`: two maps out of `g^*Ω_{X/Y}` are equal if they agree
  on all the sections `unit (d b)`.

Locality:
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_restrictFunctor_map`: a morphism of module
  sheaves is an iso if it is one after restricting along open immersions covering the scheme.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.IsAffineOpen.pullback_inf`: for affine `U ⊆ X`, `V ⊆ Y` lying over an affine
  `W ⊆ Z`, the open `fst⁻¹U ⊓ snd⁻¹V` of `X ×_Z Y` is affine.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_pullback_inf`: on `X ×_Z Y`, `φ` is an iso if
  `(Hartshorne.QuasicoherentAbelianAux.specRestrictFunctor ⟨fst⁻¹U ⊓ snd⁻¹V, _⟩).map φ` is an iso
  for every such triple `(U, V, W)`.

Units of the standard isomorphisms (all morphism-level equations):
- `Scheme.Modules.unit_pullbackComp_inv`, `unit_pullbackCongr_hom` and
  `unit_restrictFunctorIsoPullback_hom`.
- `Hartshorne.tildePullbackIso_hom_unit`: `(tildePullbackIso φ).hom` composed with the unit of
  `tilde ⊣ Γ` followed by `pullback ⊣ pushforward` is the unit of `extendScalars ⊣ restrictScalars`
  followed by `tilde ⊣ Γ`. On elements this says `unit (m̃) ↦ (1 ⊗ m)~`.

Derivatives under the local identifications:
- `AlgebraicGeometry.RelDiffLocal.restrictIso_hom_d`: `restrictIso` sends `d (j.appIso W).hom c`
  to `d c`, on every open `W`.
- `RelDiffLocal.fromSpec_appIso_hom_res`.
- `RelDiffLocal.affineIso_hom_d`: `affineIso f U V h` sends `d` of the restriction of `b ∈ Γ(X, U)`
  to `toOpen (D b)`, on every open of `Spec Γ(X, U)`.

Algebra:
- `KaehlerDifferential.bijective_of_tmul_D`: for `Algebra.IsPushout R S A B`, every `B`-linear map
  `B ⊗[A] Ω[A⁄R] → Ω[B⁄S]` with `1 ⊗ D a ↦ D (algebraMap a)` is bijective. The proof shows it equals
  `tensorKaehlerEquiv`.

## Remaining goal

`IsIso (baseChangeRelativeDifferentialsMap f h)`. By `isIso_of_isIso_pullback_inf` it suffices to
treat one triple at a time. Fix affine `U ⊆ X`, `V' ⊆ Y'` and `V ⊆ Y` with `U ≤ f⁻¹V` and
`V' ≤ h⁻¹V`. Let `W = g'⁻¹U ⊓ f'⁻¹V'`, `B = Γ(X, U)`, `A = Γ(Y, V)`, `A' = Γ(Y', V')` and
`B' = Γ(X', W)`. The goal is `IsIso ((specRestrictFunctor W).map φ)`. Write
`Ψ = SRPI.hom ≫ (specRestrictFunctor W).map φ ≫ (affineIso f' W V' _).hom`, where
`SRPI = Hartshorne.PullbackQuasicoherent.specRestrictionPullbackIso g' Ω_{X/Y} U W _ Ω[B⁄A] (affineIso f U V _).symm`.
Then `Ψ : (B' ⊗_B Ω_{B/A})~ ⟶ (Ω_{B'/A'})~`, and it suffices that `Ψ` is an iso.

1. **Element formula for `SRPI`.** On global sections, `SRPI.hom` sends `toOpen (1 ⊗ m)` to the
   restriction to `W` of `unit_{g'} (e.hom (toOpen m))`, with `e = (affineIso f U V _).symm`. The
   chase goes through, in order:
   - `tildePullbackIso.inv`, via `tildePullbackIso_hom_unit` and `Adjunction.comp_unit_app`;
   - naturality of the unit of `Spec.map (g'.appLE U W _)`;
   - `restrictFunctorIsoPullback`, via `unit_restrictFunctorIsoPullback_hom`;
   - `pullbackComp`, via `unit_pullbackComp_inv`, then `pullbackCongr`, via
     `unit_pullbackComp_inv` and `unit_pullbackCongr_hom`;
   - `restrictFunctorIsoPullback.inv` for `W`.

   The work is bookkeeping: opens such as `p⁻¹ᵁ (p ''ᵁ ⊤)` that equal `⊤` only propositionally,
   and composites of restriction maps that collapse in the thin category. A scratch statement of
   the first step, `tildePullbackIso_hom_unit_apply`, had not yet been compiled when the attempt
   ended. It should follow from `congr_hom` of `tildePullbackIso_hom_unit` after `comp_unit_app`.
2. **Value of `Ψ` on generators.** Combine step 1 with `affineIso_hom_d` for `f` (at `⊤`, inverted)
   and `baseChangeRelativeDifferentialsMap_unit_d`. Then use naturality of `φ` under restriction
   and `affineIso_hom_d` for `f'`. The result: `Γ(⊤, Ψ) (toOpen (1 ⊗ D b)) = toOpen (D (ρ b))`,
   with `ρ = g'.appLE U W _ : B → B'`.
3. **`Ψ = tilde.map L`.** Take `L = toOpen ⊤ ≫ Γ(⊤, Ψ) ≫ (toOpen ⊤)⁻¹`, using
   `tilde.toTildeΓNatIso`, the unit of `tilde.adjunction`. Get `Ψ = tilde.map L` from full
   faithfulness of tilde. Then `L (1 ⊗ D b) = D (ρ b)` by step 2.
4. **Pushout.** Show `Algebra.IsPushout A A' B B'` for the algebra structures given by `appLE`.
   `W` is the image of the open immersion `pullback (f.resLE V U _) (h.resLE V V' _) ⟶ X'` used in
   the proof of `IsAffineOpen.pullback_inf`. Mathlib's `isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback` gives
   `IsPushout` for affine pullbacks. Then `KaehlerDifferential.bijective_of_tmul_D` makes `L`
   bijective, so `tilde.map L` and hence `Ψ` are isos.

After that, record the theorem, import the module in the library root, and delete this note.
