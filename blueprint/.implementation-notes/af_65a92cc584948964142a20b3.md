# Implementation note: very ample linear systems and the Hilbert polynomial

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.VeryAmpleHilbertPolynomial`.

## Statements

- `Hartshorne.LinearSystem.exists_dim_complete_nsmul_eq_projectiveHilbertPolynomial`:
  Cartier-divisor form. `k` algebraically closed, `X` integral and projective over `k`
  (`hq : Projective q`), `ι : X ⟶ Proj (homogeneousSubmodule (Fin (N + 1)) k)` a closed
  immersion with `ι ≫ projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism = q`, and
  `φ : ι^* 𝒪(1) ≅ D.sheaf`. Conclusion: there is `n₀` with
  `((complete hq (n • D).isInvertible_sheaf).dim : ℚ) = P_Y(n) - 1` for `n ≥ n₀`, where
  `P_Y = projectiveHilbertPolynomial Y` and
  `Y = projZeroSet (ι.base (genericPoint X)).asHomogeneousIdeal`.
- `Hartshorne.LinearSystem.exists_dim_complete_weil_nsmul_eq_projectiveHilbertPolynomial`:
  Hartshorne's form, for a Weil divisor on a regular integral projective `X`, through
  `weilCartierEquiv` (same pattern as the torsion sibling, Exercise 7.6(b)).
- `Hartshorne.VeryAmpleHilbert.mem_projZeroSet_iff_classicalPoint_mem_range` (with the point
  `Hartshorne.VeryAmpleHilbert.classicalPoint P`, homogeneous prime `J({P})`): `Y` is exactly the
  set of classical points whose scheme point lies in `ι(X)`, so `P_Y` is the Hilbert polynomial of
  the embedded variety.

The Lean is more general than the article in two ways: the Cartier form needs only an integral
projective `X` (no regularity), and any closed embedding over `k` with `ι^* 𝒪(1) ≅ 𝒪_X(D)` is
allowed, not only the one given by the complete system `|D|`.

## Route

1. `ker_projQuotientι_eq`, `exists_iso_projQuotientι`: with `I` the homogeneous prime `𝔭` of the
   image `y` of the generic point, both `ι` and `Proj (S/I) ⟶ ℙᴺ` have reduced source, so their
   kernels are the vanishing ideals of the closures of their images
   (`ker_eq_vanishingIdeal_closure_range`); both closures are `closure {y}` (the point of
   `Proj (S/I)` with ideal `0` maps to `y`). `IsClosedImmersion.lift` gives `X ≅ Proj (S/I)`.
   Reducedness of `Proj` of a graded domain is a private helper (stalks are subrings of
   localizations).
2. `exists_finrank_pullback_twist_eq`: transport along `e.inv` with
   `SectionAlgebraModel.pullbackPullbackTwistIso` and
   `SectionAlgebraModel.bijective_iso_comp_pullbackTopAddHom`; `s ↦ s / 1` on `(S/I)_n` is bijective
   for large `n` by `SectionAlgebraModel.exists_bijective_twistSection_quotient` (Exercise 5.9(b)
   for `M = S/I`). `k`-linearity: `SectionAlgebraModel.iso_comp_pullbackTopAddHom_smul` on the
   `X` side and `SectionAlgebraModel.toSpecZero_appTop_smul_twistSection` on the `Proj (S/I)` side,
   after `projMap_toSpecZero` identifies the structure maps.
3. `nonempty_nsmul_sheaf_iso`: classes in `Pic`: `[𝒪_X(nD)] = [𝒪_X(D)]^n` (`cartierDivisorToPicard`),
   `= (ι^*[𝒪(1)])^n = ι^*[𝒪(n)]` using `twistMulIso` and `Ringed.Pic.pullback`. Only `n ≥ 1` is
   needed, so `𝒪(0) ≅ 𝒪` is never used.
4. Main theorem: `dim_complete`, `finrank_globalSectionsOverField_congr`, the Nullstellensatz
   `J(Z(𝔭)) = 𝔭` (`isProjVariety_projZeroSet_asHomogeneousIdeal`), and
   `projectiveHilbertPolynomial_isHilbertPolynomial`.

## Friction

- `HomogeneousIdeal 𝒮` depends on the `GradedRing` instance: the bundled `projVanishingIdeal Y`
  is built with a different instance path from `MvPolynomial.gradedAlgebra`, and
  `GradedRing (gradedQuotient 𝒮 (projVanishingIdeal Y))` then fails to synthesize. The module
  rebundles `J(Y)` locally and states the section comparison for a general `I` with a hypothesis
  on `I.toIdeal`.
- `rw` through `(e.inv ≫ q).appTop` and `CommRingCat.comp_apply` on the composite structure map of
  `Proj (S/I)` times out; the proof states the morphism equation separately and uses a general
  `appTop`/`Spec.map` lemma.
- The used `SectionAlgebraModel` helpers (in `BirationalSectionAlgebraModel` and
  `SectionAlgebraSheafComparison`) are not recorded on any article.
