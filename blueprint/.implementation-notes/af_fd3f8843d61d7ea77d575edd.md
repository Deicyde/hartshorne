# Quotients of completed coherent modules: implementation note

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.SchemeCompletionQuotient`.

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.powQuotientToModuleCompletionObj I F n`: the canonical map
  `F̂ / J ^ (n + 1) F̂ ⟶ (F / I ^ (n + 1) F)|_Y`, the cokernel descent of the projection
  `moduleCompletionπ I F n`; `powQuotientπ_powQuotientToModuleCompletionObj` is its defining
  equation.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isIso_powQuotientToModuleCompletionObj`: the map is an
  isomorphism, for `X` locally Noetherian and `F` quasi-coherent.

Reading of the statement.

- The completed ideal `J` is `formalCompletionIdealSubsheaf I`, the ideal subsheaf of `𝒪_{X̂}` with
  sections `formalCompletionIdeal I V = ker (𝒪_{X̂}(V) → 𝒪_Y(V))`, which the affine-formal-thickenings
  article records and identifies on affine opens with the completion of `I(U)`
  (`formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso_formalCompletionIdeal`). Its powers are computed by
  `formalCompletionIdealSubsheaf_pow_ideal`.
- "`F / I^{n+1} F` on the common closed support" is `moduleCompletionObj I F n`: the quotient
  `F / I ^ (n + 1) F` pulled back to `Y_(n+1)`, transported to `|Y|` and viewed as an
  `𝒪_{X̂}`-module through `𝒪_{X̂} ⟶ 𝒪_{Y_(n+1)}`, which is the `n`-th term of the inverse system
  defining `F̂`. The isomorphism is of `𝒪_{X̂}`-modules on `|Y|`.
- The isomorphism is canonical: it is the map induced by the projection `F̂ ⟶ (F / I^{n+1} F)|_Y`.

Proof. The map agrees with `powQuotientModuleCompletionIso I F n` (in
`Hartshorne.Scheme.CoherentFormalModuleReconstruction`, proved for the reconstruction article),
which comes from the exactness of `J ^ (n + 1) F̂ ⟶ F̂ ⟶ (F / I^{n+1} F)|_Y`
(`moduleCompletionPowShortComplex_exact`) and surjectivity of the projection
(`epi_moduleCompletionπ'`), both checked on the opens `U ∩ |Y|` with `U` affine in `X`: a
compatible sequence of sections of `F` over `U` whose `n`-th term lies in `I(U)^{n+1} Γ(U, F)` is
rewritten as `∑ fᵢ yᵢ` with `fᵢ` finitely many generators of `I(U)^{n+1}` and `yᵢ` sections of
`F̂` (`mem_sectionsSmul_of_moduleCompletionπ_eq_zero`).
