# Powers of an ideal of definition

## Lean

- Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.PowersOfIdealOfDefinition`.
- Main theorem: `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.pow_succ` (`IsIdealOfDefinition X J →
  IsIdealOfDefinition X (J ^ (n + 1))`).
- Support part: `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.sheafSupport_quotientAb_pow`, from
  `J ^ (n + 1) ⊆ J ⊆ J_red` and the general
  `Hartshorne.sheafSupport_quotientAb_of_le_vanishingIdealSubsheaf`.
- Reusable public helpers: `IdealSubsheaf.pow_le_pow_right`, `IdealSubsheaf.pow_succ_le_self`,
  `IdealSubsheaf.ideal_pow_le_ideal_pow`, `IdealSubsheaf.stalkIdeal` (germs of sections of `J`)
  with `germ_mem_stalkIdeal_iff`, `stalkIdeal_mono`, `stalkIdeal_pow` (`(J ^ k)_x = J_x ^ k`),
  `IdealSubsheaf.IsKernelChart` with `isKernelChart_iff`, and the gluing criterion
  `IdealSubsheaf.exists_isNoetherian_iso_quotientSheafedSpace_of_charts` (Noetherian affine
  kernel charts on a quasi-compact space give a Noetherian quotient scheme). The criterion
  generalizes the chart construction of the largest-ideal leaf.

## Route

Follows the article's proof dependencies: Proposition 9.5(a) (`exists_pow_le_and_pow_le`) and
9.5(b) (`isIdealOfDefinition_vanishingIdealSubsheaf`, `isGreatest_vanishingIdealSubsheaf`) give
`J_red ^ a ⊆ J`. Charts: an affine open `Spec B` of the thickening `Y_(m+1)` of a formal chart
`U ≅ Ŷ` maps to `X` with stalk kernels `(ker π₀)_y ^ (m+1)` (from
`mem_formalCompletionIdealPow_iff`, Proposition 9.4), and `ker π₀` lies in `J_red`. The
"coherence of the power on the thickening" input is proved by a closed-immersion argument: the
open subscheme `g(Spec B)` of the scheme `(X, 𝒪_X / J)` maps to `Spec B` (lifted through the open
immersion `Spec B ⟶ (X, 𝒪_X / (J ∩ ker g))`), this is a closed immersion, hence `Spec (B ⧸ c)`
(Mathlib `IsClosedImmersion.Spec_iff`), and the localizations of `c` are the images of `J_x`.
Then `Spec (B ⧸ c ^ (n+1))` is a kernel chart of `J ^ (n+1)`.

## Friction

Stating the `Y_(m+1) ⟶ X̂` morphism directly with `I.infinitesimalNeighborhoodFromOne m` for a
variable `m` makes the kernel time out (deterministic timeout even at 1000000 heartbeats) when it
checks the restricted structure sheaf against `formalCompletionSheafObj m`. Stating it for an
arbitrary open embedding `f : Y_1 ⟶ Z` and morphism `π` and instantiating afterwards avoids this.
