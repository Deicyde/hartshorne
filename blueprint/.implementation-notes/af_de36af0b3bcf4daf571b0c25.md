# The relative twisting sheaf

## Status

Statement phase not started in Lean: no declaration recorded. A bounded attempt
stopped at the inventory below, because the construction needs two pieces of
infrastructure that neither Mathlib (pinned) nor the project has, and the first
is a prerequisite the roadmap does not list.

## Available

- `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.relProj` is glued with Mathlib's
  `Scheme.Cover.RelativeGluingData` over `X.AffineZariskiSite` (affine opens,
  arrows = basic-open inclusions `D(r) ⊆ U`). The pieces are
  `Proj (S.grading U)`, the transition maps are
  `Proj.map (S.restrict f) (S.irrelevant_le f)` (open immersions onto
  `D₊(r)`, `isOpenImmersion_projMap`, `range_projMap`), and the open immersions
  into the glued scheme are `S.projι U` with `projFunctor.map f ≫ projι V =
  projι U`.
- On each piece: `Hartshorne.projTwistingSheaf (S.grading U) 1`, invertible by
  `ProjTwistingSheaf.isInvertible` once `Algebra.adjoin (S(U)₀) (S(U)₁) = ⊤`,
  which follows from `GeneratedInDegreeOne` (closure of `S(U)₀ ∪ S(U)₁` as a
  subring is the subalgebra generated by `S(U)₁` over `S(U)₀`).
- `Hartshorne.SheafGluing.GluingData` (Exercise II.1.22) glues sheaves valued in
  a fixed category `C` on a topological space; it does not handle sheaves of
  modules over a sheaf of rings (`Scheme.Modules = SheafOfModules
  X.ringCatSheaf`).

## Missing

1. **Gluing sheaves of modules** (not in Mathlib: no `IsStack` instance for
   `Scheme.Modules`, no `SheafOfModules` gluing or dense-subsite equivalence;
   not in the project). Most convenient form here: given a scheme `Y`, a
   functor `F : I ⥤ Scheme` with open immersions `ι i : F i ⟶ Y` compatible
   with `F.map`, jointly covering and locally directed (as for
   `X.AffineZariskiSite`), modules `M i` on `F i` and isomorphisms
   `restrict (F.map f) (M j) ≅ M i` satisfying identity and composition
   compatibilities, produce `M` on `Y` with `restrict (ι i) M ≅ M i`
   compatible with the transition isomorphisms. Route: `M := lim_{i ∈ Iᵒᵖ}
   (ι i)_* (M i)` in `Y.Modules` (limits exist and are sectionwise), then
   prove `restrict (ι i) M ⟶ M i` is an isomorphism on sections using local
   directedness and the sheaf condition of each `M j`. Equivalently, a module
   version of Exercise II.1.22. Estimated well over a thousand lines.
2. **Compatibility of twisting sheaves with localization on degree zero**
   (the article's unlinked "Proof depends on" bullet): for `B = A[1/s]` with
   `s ∈ A₀` and the graded map `φ : A → B`,
   `restrict (Proj.map φ) (projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 n) ≅ projTwistingSheaf ℬ n`,
   natural enough that the composite along `D(r') ⊆ D(r) ⊆ U` agrees with the
   direct one (the cocycle condition for item 1). Route: fibrewise isomorphism
   `S(n)_(𝔭) ≅ S[1/s](n)_(𝔮)` of homogeneous localizations for `𝔭 = φ⁻¹ 𝔮`,
   shown to preserve the locally-fraction predicate of
   `ProjGradedModuleSheaf.isLocallyFraction` both ways and to be linear over
   the structure-sheaf map of `Proj.map`; the cocycle then follows pointwise.

## Remaining goal

With items 1 and 2: glue the `projTwistingSheaf (S.grading U) 1` along
`X.AffineZariskiSite` to a module on `S.relProj`; invertibility is local and
follows from `ProjTwistingSheaf.isInvertible` on each piece; the restriction
compatibility over an affine open `U` is the gluing isomorphism composed with
`restrictIso` (`(Proj_X S)|_U ≅ Proj S(U)`).
