# Pullback injects canonical sections

## Status

Partial (frontier/w20d). The pullback map on top exterior powers exists and the
invertible-sheaf half of the injectivity argument is proved; nothing is recorded. The missing
input is the compatibility of relative differentials with open immersions, which the roadmap does
not have as an article (Roadmap return).

## Lean so far (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.CanonicalPullback`, sorry-free)

- `Hartshorne.ModuleSheafMultilinear.pushforward` (with `pushforward_app`,
  `IsAlternating.pushforward`): a multilinear morphism of `𝒪_V`-modules pushed forward along
  `f : V ⟶ Y` is `𝒪_Y`-multilinear through `f^♯`; `IsAlternating.precompHom`.
- `Hartshorne.exteriorPowerPullback f p n :
  moduleSheafExteriorPower n Ω_{Y/k} ⟶ f_*(moduleSheafExteriorPower n Ω_{V/k})` for
  `p : Y ⟶ Spec k`, where `Ω_{V/k} = Scheme.relativeDifferentials (f ≫ p)`; on sections
  `ω_1 ∧ ⋯ ∧ ω_n ↦ f^*ω_1 ∧ ⋯ ∧ f^*ω_n`, via `relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp f p`.
  Its component at `⊤` is the map `Γ(Y, ⋀^n Ω_Y) → Γ(V, ⋀^n Ω_V)`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.presheaf_map_injective_of_isInvertible`: on an integral
  scheme, restriction of sections of an invertible sheaf to a nonempty open is injective.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.injective_app_top_of_isInvertible`: a morphism `φ : L ⟶ M`
  with `L` invertible on an integral scheme is injective on `Γ(⊤)` if it is injective on the
  sections over some nonempty open `U`.

## Remaining goal

With `Y = X'` integral, `ω_Y = canonicalSheaf p` invertible (`canonicalSheaf_isInvertible`),
`topologicalKrullDim Y = n = topologicalKrullDim V`, and `W ⊆ V` a nonempty open on which `f`
restricts to an open immersion `g = W.ι ≫ f` with image `W'`, apply
`injective_app_top_of_isInvertible` to `φ = exteriorPowerPullback f p n` and `U = W'`. What is left
is that `φ.val.app (op W')`, followed by restriction from `f⁻¹ W'` to `W`, is injective. That is
the statement that for an open immersion `g : W ⟶ Y` over `k`, the map
`Ω_{Y/k}(U) → Ω_{W/k}(g⁻¹ U)` of `relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp g p` is bijective for
`U ⊆ g(W)` (equivalently `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp g p : g^*Ω_{Y/k} ≅ Ω_{W/k}`), plus
its compatibility with `moduleSheafExteriorPowerRestrictIso` to pass to `⋀^n`.

Route for the missing fact: compare universal properties. For an open immersion,
`Der_k(𝒪_W, M) ≅ Der_k(𝒪_Y, g_*M)` (derivations are local, and `transitivityDerivationPushforward`
already gives one direction), so `Hom(Ω_{W/k}, M) ≅ Hom(g^*Ω_{Y/k}, M)` naturally in `M`, and the
comparison is precomposition with `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp g p`.

Then the article's statement: for `X, X'` nonsingular of the same dimension `n` over an
algebraically closed `k`, `V ⊆ X` dense open and `f : V ⟶ X'` over `k` restricting to an open
immersion on a nonempty open `W ⊆ V`, the map `(exteriorPowerPullback f pX' n).val.app (op ⊤)`,
transported to `Γ(V, canonicalSheaf (V.ι ≫ pX))` along `f ≫ pX' = V.ι ≫ pX` and
`canonicalSheaf_eq`, is injective (and `k`-linear). The dimension of `V` equals that of `X`
because `V` is a dense open of an irreducible scheme of finite type over a field.
