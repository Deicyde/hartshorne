# Pullback injects canonical sections

## Status

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23d), not closable in budget. No Lean was
written, and nothing is recorded. The article needs a pullback map on top exterior powers of
differential sheaves, and the project has neither functoriality of the exterior power of module
sheaves nor its compatibility with pullback.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.canonicalSheaf f = moduleSheafExteriorPower n (Scheme.relativeDifferentials f)`
  (`Hartshorne.canonicalSheaf_eq`), and `Hartshorne.canonicalSheaf_isInvertible` for an
  irreducible regular scheme of finite type over an algebraically closed field.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp f g :
  Ω_{Y/Z} ⟶ f_* Ω_{X/Z}` and its adjoint `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp :
  f^* Ω_{Y/Z} ⟶ Ω_{X/Z}` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RelativeDifferentialsTransitivity`).
- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafExteriorPowerHomEquiv`: maps `⋀^r F ⟶ H` are alternating
  `Hartshorne.ModuleSheafMultilinear r F H` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.SheafTensorOperations`).
- `Hartshorne.birational_iff_hasIsomorphicOpenSubsets` for the dense open where the map is an
  isomorphism.

## Lean gaps (suggested route)

1. **Map on canonical sheaves.** Build `⋀^n Ω_{X'/k} ⟶ f_*(⋀^n Ω_{V/k})` from the universal
   property. The alternating multilinear map `(ω_1, …, ω_n) ↦ f^*ω_1 ∧ ⋯ ∧ f^*ω_n` over `U ⊆ X'`
   is the wedge map of `Ω_V` over `f⁻¹U`, composed with `relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp`.
   Its scalars must be restricted along `f^♯ : 𝒪_{X'}(U) ⟶ 𝒪_V(f⁻¹U)`, a
   `MultilinearMap.restrictScalars` through the pushforward module structure. Taking global
   sections gives the map `Γ(X', ω_{X'}) ⟶ Γ(V, ω_V)`. This avoids needing `f^*⋀^n ≅ ⋀^n f^*`.
2. **Compatibility with open immersions.** On the dense open `W ⊆ V` where `f` is an
   isomorphism onto an open `W' ⊆ X'`, the map is the restriction `Γ(X', ω) ⟶ Γ(W', ω)`
   transported along the isomorphism. This needs naturality of step 1 under restriction to opens
   and under isomorphisms.
3. **Sections of an invertible sheaf on an integral scheme.** Restriction to a dense open is
   injective: locally `L ≅ 𝒪`, and `𝒪(U) ⟶ 𝒪(U ∩ W')` is injective on an integral scheme. No
   such lemma was found for `Scheme.Modules`.

Then injectivity follows: a section killed by the pullback is killed on `W`, hence restricts to
zero on `W'` by step 2, hence is zero by step 3.
