# Homogeneous ideals define projective closed subschemes

## Status

Statement and proof complete (candidate, pending review). Module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveClosedSubschemeHomogeneousIdeal`, namespace
`Hartshorne.ProjectiveClosedSubscheme`.

## Encoding

- Projective space: `Proj 𝒮` with `𝒮 = MvPolynomial.homogeneousSubmodule (Fin (r + 1)) R`,
  as in the section-ring article.
- "The closed subscheme defined by a homogeneous ideal $I$": the closed immersion
  `Hartshorne.projQuotientι 𝒮 I : Proj (gradedQuotient 𝒮 I) ⟶ Proj 𝒮` of Exercise II.3.12
  (recorded on proj-closed-subscheme-truncation), compared through its ideal sheaf
  `Scheme.Hom.ker`.
- Module ideal sheaf of $Y$: `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv.symm f.ker`
  (`QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf`, module-ideal-sheaf-data); its inclusion into
  $\mathcal{O}_X$ is `idealι`.
- $\Gamma_*(I_Y)\cap S$: `gammaStarIdeal R r ℐ`, the preimage under
  `ProjectiveSpaceSectionRing.sectionRingMap` ($S \to \Gamma_*(\mathcal{O}_X)$) of the range of
  `gammaStarLinear (idealι ℐ)` ($\Gamma_*(\mathcal{I}) \to \Gamma_*(\mathcal{O}_X)$, the
  degreewise map `gammaStarMap`, $S$-linear by naturality of `mulMap`). Homogeneity is proved
  in the definition; `mem_gammaStarIdeal_iff` gives the degreewise description.

## Declarations

- `exists_iso_projQuotientι_gammaStarIdeal` (main): for a closed immersion
  `f : Y ⟶ Proj 𝒮` and `0 < r`, `Y ≅ Proj (S/I)` over `Proj 𝒮` with
  `I = gammaStarIdeal R r (quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv.symm f.ker)`.
- `ker_projQuotientι_gammaStarIdeal_eq_ker`: the same ideal-sheaf equality for any morphism
  `f` (the closed-immersion instance is not needed for the equality of kernels, so it was
  dropped after the `unusedArguments` lint).
- `ker_projQuotientι_gammaStarIdeal`: for any quasi-coherent ideal sheaf `ℐ`,
  `(projQuotientι 𝒮 (gammaStarIdeal R r ℐ)).ker = quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv ℐ`.

## Proof outline (follows the printed proof)

- `le_ker_projQuotientι`: Prop. 5.15 (`GammaStarReconstruction.isIso_gammaStarEval`) makes
  `stalkEval` surjective (`stalkEval_surjective_of_isIso`), so a section of `ℐ` is locally
  `m / s`; its image in $\mathcal{O}_X$ is `b / s` with `b` from Prop. 5.13
  (`sectionRingMap_bijective`), using `app_frac` (naturality of the fractions) and
  `frac_sectionRingMap`; then `b ∈ I` and `awayToSection_mem_ker_iff` puts `b / s` in the
  kernel ideal. Locality of the ideal subsheaf of the kernel finishes.
- `ker_projQuotientι_le`: on the charts `D₊(xᵢ)`, `awayToSection_mem_ker_iff` gives
  `xᵢᵏ a ∈ I` for a kernel section `a / xᵢⁿ`, and `ringFrac_mem_ideal` puts it in `ℐ`;
  `Scheme.IdealSheafData.le_of_iSup_eq_top` globalizes.

## Notes

- Hypothesis `0 < r` is kept: it is the hypothesis of Prop. 5.13
  ($\Gamma_*(\mathcal{O}_X) = S$), which the printed proof uses and which is what makes
  "$I = \Gamma_*(I_Y)$" an ideal of $S$. The article's statement does not mention $r \ge 1$.
- `isIso_gammaStarEval` on `ℙʳ_R` needs `synthInstance.maxHeartbeats 200000` (sheaf instances
  behind `IsQuasicoherent`); the generators are the `X i` (private `adjoin_X_eq_top`).
- Arithmetic in `Γ_*(𝒪_X)` with `map_add` timed out (instance unification on the
  `ModuleCat` carriers); `sectionRingMap_decompose` is proved through `twistValue` instead.
