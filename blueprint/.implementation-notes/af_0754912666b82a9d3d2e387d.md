# Implementation note: Sections of a geometric vector bundle

Status: partial (statement phase). No `lean:` is recorded; the second assertion of the
article is proved as an isomorphism of sheaves of sets, the first assertion (module structure
and local freeness for an arbitrary geometric vector bundle) is open.

## Lean (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.VectorBundleSections`)

- `AlgebraicGeometry.sectionsPresheaf f`: Hartshorne's `𝒮(X/Y)` for `f : X ⟶ Y`, as a presheaf of
  sets on `Y`; sections over `U` are `s : U ⟶ X` with `s ≫ f = U.ι`, restriction is
  precomposition with `Y.homOfLE`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.isSheaf_sectionsPresheaf`: it is a sheaf (unique gluing; gluing uses
  `Hartshorne.glueOpens` on the cover `W.ι ⁻¹ᵁ U i` of `W = ⨆ U i`).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.RelSpecSections.equiv`: for a quasi-coherent algebra `A` and an affine
  open `U`, sections of `Spec_Y A ⟶ Y` over `U` are equivalent to ring maps `φ : A(U) ⟶ Γ(Y, U)`
  with `A.structureMap.app (op U) ≫ φ = 𝟙` (the `𝒪_Y(U)`-algebra maps);
  `RelSpecSections.restriction_equiv` is naturality for affine `U ⊆ V` (through
  `QuasiCoherentAlgebra.restriction`). No affineness of `U` is needed for the Spec–Γ step; it is
  used to cancel `U.toSpecΓ`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleSectionsIso E hE :
  sectionsPresheaf (vectorBundleProj E hE) ≅ (moduleSheafDual E).val.presheaf ⋙ forget _`,
  the natural isomorphism `𝒮(V(E)/Y) ≅ E^∨` of sheaves of sets, Hartshorne's convention
  `V(E) = Spec_Y Sym(E)`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleSectionsIso_hom_eval`: the explicit description:
  for a section `s` over an open `W` and an affine `V ⊆ W`, the image of `s` evaluated at
  `x ∈ E(V)` is `sectionAlgHom (s|_V) (symDegreeOne x)`, the algebra map of `s|_V` on `x` in
  degree one. This pins the isomorphism down and is the article's "an algebra map
  `Sym(E) → 𝒪_Y` is determined by an `𝒪_Y`-linear map `E → 𝒪_Y`".

Route: `sectionLinearForm` (the linear form `E(V) → 𝒪(V)` of a section over affine `V`,
natural in affine `V`); `sectionDual c s` (the morphism `E|_V → 𝒪_V` with these values on a
frame `c` of `E` over an affine `V`, `IsLocalFrame.homOfValues`); `sectionDual_eval` (its value on
any affine `V' ⊆ V` is the linear form of `s|_{V'}`, by expanding in the restricted frame);
naturality on the basis of framed affine opens (`isBasis_affineFrame`, indexed by
`Scheme.Modules.AffineFrame`); bijectivity on framed affines (`sectionDual_bijective`:
`RelSpecSections.equiv`, then `chartRingHom_bijective` identifies `Γ(V, Sym(E))` with a polynomial
ring on the frame, then `homOfValues` is inverse to `localFrameEval`); extension from the basis
with `TopCat.Sheaf.restrictHomEquivHom` and `TopCat.Sheaf.isIso_iff_isIso_basis` (both sides are
sheaves of sets: `isSheaf_sectionsPresheaf`, and `dualTypeSheaf`).

## Remaining goals

1. First assertion, for an arbitrary `G : GeometricVectorBundle f n`: an `𝒪_Y`-module structure
   on `𝒮(X/Y)` given by the linear structure of the charts, locally free of rank `n`. A faithful
   statement: there is `M : Y.Modules` with `M.IsLocallyFreeOfRank n` and an isomorphism
   `M.presheaf ⋙ forget _ ≅ sectionsPresheaf f` such that, for every `i` and every affine open
   `V ⊆ U_i`, the composite `M(V) ≅ 𝒮(V) ≅ Γ(V, 𝒪)^n` (chart coordinates through `ψ_i`) is
   `Γ(V, 𝒪)`-linear. Route: for a section `s` over an affine `V ⊆ U_i`, factor
   `s|` through `f⁻¹(U_i)`, apply `ψ_i`, and lift to `t_i : V ⟶ Spec A[x_1, …, x_n]` along
   `affineSpaceChart n V hi` (use `AffineSpace.isPullback`-type facts for `AffineSpace.map` of the
   open immersion `V ⟶ U_i`; then `t_i ≫ affineSpaceChart ≫ ψ_i⁻¹ ≫ ι = s`). The field
   `GeometricVectorBundle.linear` and monicity of `affineSpaceChart ≫ ψ_i⁻¹ ≫ ι` give
   `t_i = t_j ≫ Spec θ`, so the coordinates `t_i^*(x_k)` transform by the matrix of `θ`. The module
   structure then glues along these linear transitions; alternatively build the sheaf `E` of
   fibrewise-linear functions on `X` (Exercise 5.18(d) direction), identify `X ≅ V(E)` over `Y`,
   and transport `vectorBundleSectionsIso`.
2. Upgrade of the second assertion to `𝒪_Y`-modules: show `vectorBundleSectionsIso` matches the
   chart-wise linear structure of `vectorBundleAtlas`: for a frame `c` and affine `W ⊆ c.V`, the
   coordinates of `s` in the chart `frameTrivialization` (via `AffineSpace.homOverEquiv`) are
   `localFrameEval` of the image of `s`, which is `k ↦ sectionLinearForm (s|_W) (e_k|_W)`
   (`localFrameEval_sectionDual`, `vectorBundleSectionsIso_hom_eval`). The missing step is the
   computation of the pullback of the coordinate `x_k` along `frameTrivialization`, which is
   `symDegreeOne e_k` through `frameChartIso`.

## Notes

- `Scheme.Modules` has a lemma named `map_smul`; inside `namespace Scheme.Modules` use
  `LinearMap.map_smul` explicitly.
- Morphisms in `Type u` are now a structure (`TypeCat.ofHom`), so a type-valued presheaf's
  `map` must be built with `TypeCat.ofHom`, and `ext` on such a functor law already applies
  `Subtype.ext`.
- In `TopCat.Presheaf` over a scheme, field notation `U.toScheme` on `U : Opens Y` fails;
  write `Scheme.Opens.toScheme (X := Y) U`.
