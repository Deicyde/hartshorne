# Implementation note: Sections of a geometric vector bundle

Status: candidate (statement phase), pending review. Both assertions are formalized in the
module `Hartshorne.Scheme.VectorBundleSections`.

## Declarations

- `AlgebraicGeometry.sectionsPresheaf f`: Hartshorne's `𝒮(X/Y)` for `f : X ⟶ Y`, a presheaf of sets
  on `Y`; sections over `U` are `s : U ⟶ X` with `s ≫ f = U.ι`, restriction is precomposition
  with `Y.homOfLE`. `AlgebraicGeometry.isSheaf_sectionsPresheaf`: it is a sheaf (unique gluing,
  via `Hartshorne.glueOpens` on the cover `W.ι ⁻¹ᵁ U i` of `W = ⨆ U i`).
- First assertion, for `G : GeometricVectorBundle f n`:
  `AlgebraicGeometry.GeometricVectorBundle.sectionsModule G : Y.Modules`, whose sections over `W`
  are the sections of `f` over `W` (`sectionsModuleForgetIso`), with addition and the
  `𝒪_Y(W)`-action of the chart coordinates (`coordY_add`, `coordY_smul`); and
  `GeometricVectorBundle.sectionsModule_isLocallyFreeOfRank`: it is locally free of rank `n`.
- Second assertion, for `E` locally free of rank `n`:
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleSectionsModuleIso hE :
  (vectorBundleAtlas hE).sectionsModule ≅ moduleSheafDual E`, an isomorphism of `𝒪_Y`-modules
  whose map on sections is `Scheme.Modules.vectorBundleSectionsIso hE :
  sectionsPresheaf (vectorBundleProj E hE) ≅ (moduleSheafDual E).val.presheaf ⋙ forget _`
  (`vectorBundleSectionsModuleIso_hom_apply`). `vectorBundleSectionsIso_hom_eval` describes it:
  for a section `s` over `W`, an affine `V ⊆ W` and `x ∈ E(V)`, the image of `s` evaluated at `x`
  is `sectionAlgHom (s|_V) (symDegreeOne x)`, the `𝒪_Y(V)`-algebra map
  `Γ(V, Sym(E)) ⟶ 𝒪_Y(V)` of `s|_V` on `x` in degree one (the article's "an algebra map
  `Sym(E) → 𝒪_Y` is determined by an `𝒪_Y`-linear map `E → 𝒪_Y`").

## Route

- Relative Spec: `RelSpecSections.equiv` identifies sections of `Spec_Y A ⟶ Y` over an affine `U`
  with ring maps `φ : A(U) ⟶ Γ(Y, U)` such that `A.structureMap.app (op U) ≫ φ = 𝟙`
  (`𝒪_Y(U)`-algebra maps), natural for affine `U ⊆ V` (`RelSpecSections.restriction_equiv`). The
  project's relative Spec had no universal property for sections; this supplies the affine case.
- `𝒮(V(E)/Y) ≅ E^∨` as sheaves of sets: `sectionLinearForm` (the linear form `E(V) → 𝒪(V)` of a
  section over affine `V`); `sectionDual c s` (the morphism `E|_V → 𝒪_V` with these values on a
  frame `c`, via `IsLocalFrame.homOfValues`); naturality and bijectivity on the basis of framed
  affine opens (`isBasis_affineFrame`, `sectionDual_bijective`, using `chartRingHom_bijective`);
  extension by `TopCat.Sheaf.restrictHomEquivHom` and `TopCat.Sheaf.isIso_iff_isIso_basis`.
- Chart coordinates of a GVB: `chartLift` lifts `ψ_i ∘ s` through
  `AffineSpace.isPullback_map (Y.homOfLE hi)`; `chartCoord`/`coordY` are its coordinates.
  `chartCoord_bijective`, `chartCoord_restrict`, and `exists_chartCoord_transition` (on an affine
  `V ⊆ U_i ∩ U_j`, the polynomial-ring charts satisfy `t_i = t_j ≫ Spec θ` since
  `affineSpaceChart ≫ ψ_i⁻¹ ≫ ι` is mono, so coordinates change by the matrix of `θ`).
- Module structure: `coords s` records the coordinates of `s` on all `ChartPiece`s (affine opens
  inside some `U_i`); it is injective, and families compatible with `transfer` (restrict, then
  change chart) glue to sections (`exists_coords_eq`, from `isSheaf_sectionsPresheaf`). The group
  and module operations are defined by gluing pointwise operations on coordinates
  (`secAdd`, `secSmul`, ...), and the axioms are transported with `Function.Injective.addCommGroup`
  and injectivity of `coords`. Local freeness: `frameSection i` (chart coordinates the standard
  basis) is a frame over `U_i` (`isLocalFrame_frameSection`), then
  `Ringed.isLocallyFreeOfRank_of_isLocalFrame`.
- Module isomorphism for `V(E)`: `vectorBundleAtlas_chartCoord` (chart coordinates are the values
  of the linear form on the frame, computed through `affineSpaceChart_frameTrivialization_inv`),
  so on framed affines the linear form is `∑ x_k s_k`; linearity of `vectorBundleSectionsIso` is
  checked on framed affines by separation of `𝒪_Y`, and `modulesHomMk` plus
  `modules_isIso_of_locally_bijective` give the isomorphism.

Hartshorne's exercise has no printed proof; the route follows the article's sketch (relative-Spec
universal property, then the universal property of `Sym`).

## Notes

- The module structure depends on the atlas `G` (Hartshorne's definition includes the covering
  and trivializations as data); for `V(E)` the canonical atlas `vectorBundleAtlas` is used.
- `Scheme.Modules` has a lemma named `map_smul`; inside `namespace Scheme.Modules` write
  `LinearMap.map_smul`.
- Morphisms in `Type u` are a structure (`TypeCat.ofHom`); a type-valued presheaf's `map` must be
  built with `TypeCat.ofHom`, and `ext` on its functor laws already applies `Subtype.ext`.
- In `TopCat.Presheaf` over a scheme, field notation `U.toScheme` on `U : Opens Y` fails; write
  `Scheme.Opens.toScheme (X := Y) U`.
- `rw` with lemmas stating `Y.presheaf.map _ (moduleSheafHomEval …)` can fail to match because the
  structure sheaf appears as `Y.sheaf.obj`; `Eq.trans` with the lemma term works.
