# Projection formula for finite locally free sheaves

## Partial progress: the scheme case is proved

The module `Hartshorne.Scheme.FiniteLocallyFreeProjectionFormula` proves the
projection formula for a morphism of **schemes** `f : X ⟶ Y`, with no `sorry`.
Its declarations are:

- `Hartshorne.projectionFormulaMap`: the canonical map
  `f_*F ⊗ E ⟶ f_*(F ⊗ f^*E)`, induced by `s ⊗ x ↦ s ⊗ η(x)`, where `η` is the
  unit of the pullback–pushforward adjunction (`Hartshorne.pullbackUnit`);
- `Hartshorne.projectionFormulaMap_naturality_left` and
  `Hartshorne.projectionFormulaMap_naturality_right`: naturality in `F` and in
  `E`;
- `Hartshorne.projectionFormulaMap_isIso`: the map is an isomorphism when `E`
  is locally free (`Scheme.Modules.IsLocallyFree`) and has finite rank at
  every point (`Scheme.Modules.rankAt E y < ℵ₀`);
- `Hartshorne.projectionFormulaIso`: the resulting isomorphism
  `f_*(F ⊗ f^*E) ≅ f_*F ⊗ E`.

The proof also adds two reusable lemmas:

- `Hartshorne.IsLocalFrame.pullback`: if `e` is a frame of `E` over `U`, then
  the sections `η(e_i)` form a frame of `f^*E` over `f⁻¹U`;
- `Hartshorne.IsLocalFrame.tensorFrameMap_bijective`: if `b` is a frame of `A`
  over `W`, then for every open `V ⊆ W` the map `F(V)^ι → (F ⊗ A)(V)`,
  `(s_i) ↦ ∑ s_i ⊗ b_i`, is bijective.

The proof is local on frames and does not use Yoneda. The frame-pullback
lemma uses the adjunction `f^*(-)|_{f⁻¹U} ⊣ f_* j_*`, obtained by composing
Mathlib's pullback–pushforward adjunction with `restrictAdjunction`. It shows
that `𝒪^ι → (f^*E)|_{f⁻¹U}` is an isomorphism by writing down its inverse. The
tensor lemma compares `F ⊗ A` with `ℋom(A^∨, F)`.

None of these declarations is in the article's `lean:` frontmatter.

## Gap

The article is stated for morphisms of **ringed spaces**. The project's
notions of locally free module, rank, duals and local frames, together with
the duality results this leaf depends on (finite-locally-free-duality), exist
only for schemes (`Scheme.Modules`). The tensor product of module sheaves is
already defined for ringed spaces; duality is not. To prove the stated form,
locally free modules, rank and the duality file would first have to be
generalised to ringed spaces.

## Options for Roadmap

- (a) Generalise local freeness, rank and finite-locally-free duality to
  ringed spaces, then adapt the scheme proof. The argument itself only uses
  the ringed-space structure, the adjunction, restriction to opens and
  stalks.
- (b) Restate the article for morphisms of schemes. This is the setting used
  where the result is applied later in the book. The existing module then
  formalizes it as is.

## Lean friction

Statements that instantiate the duality file's generic lemmas at a nested
module such as `moduleSheafDual (moduleSheafDual A)`, and then apply
`presheaf.map` to its sections, cause a kernel deterministic timeout, even
though the types agree syntactically. The workaround is to state lemmas
generically over a pairing `p : A ⟶ moduleSheafDual B` and to instantiate
`B := moduleSheafDual A` only inside proofs (see
`Hartshorne.pairingTensorMap` and `Hartshorne.tensorFrameMap_bijective_of_pairing`).
For sections of pushforward or composite-functor objects, abbreviations with
declared section types (`etaSec`, `PullbackFrame.uSec`) avoid slow `whnf`
unification.

## Ringed-space generalization plan

The project owner chose option (a): generalize, keeping the article's ringed-space statement.
Setting: `{X : TopCat.{u}} {R : Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) CommRingCat.{u}}`, modules `SheafOfModules.{u} (ringCatSheafOf R)`, new general declarations in namespace `Hartshorne.Ringed` under `src/Hartshorne/RingedSpace/`. Schemes are the case `R := X.sheaf`, for which `SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf X.sheaf)` is definitionally `X.Modules`. A morphism of ringed spaces is `f : X ⟶ Y` in `TopCat` with
`φ : ringCatSheafOf S ⟶ ((Opens.map f).sheafPushforwardContinuous _ _ _).obj (ringCatSheafOf R)`,
using Mathlib's `SheafOfModules.pushforward φ`, `SheafOfModules.pullback φ` and
`SheafOfModules.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction φ`. Schemes use `φ := f.toRingCatSheafHom`.

This article is stage 6 and needs stage 3 (general duality and frames) and stage 2
(`Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree`). Stage 6: `RingedSpace/FiniteLocallyFreeProjectionFormula.lean`
records `Ringed.projectionFormulaMap`, `Ringed.projectionFormulaMap_naturality_left`,
`Ringed.projectionFormulaMap_naturality_right`, `Ringed.projectionFormulaMap_isIso` (hypothesis
`Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree E`), `Ringed.projectionFormulaIso`. The frame-tensor lemma
(`tensorFrameMap_bijective_of_pairing`) carries over unchanged. The main risk is the
frame-pullback lemma, which needs `(f^*E)|_{f⁻¹U} ≅ f_U^*(E|_U)` in general. Route: open
restriction is the pullback along the open inclusion, then `SheafOfModules.pullbackComp` and
Mathlib's `SheafOfModules.pullbackObjFreeIso` (`f^*𝒪^n ≅ 𝒪^n`, the site functor `Opens.map f`
preserves the terminal object). Land that comparison first as its own commit (pure addition,
no claims) if it is long. The scheme module has no users and is deleted in the same commit as
the general one. Claims: this article plus the `lean/...` targets of the deleted scheme
declarations.

The full staged plan (setting, route, all stages, claim sets) is in the implementation note of the Modules and quasi-coherent sheaves container (`af_cef7a5b34bf9a0c78dc51155`).
