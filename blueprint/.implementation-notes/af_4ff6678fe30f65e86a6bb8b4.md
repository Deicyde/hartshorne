# The normalization is an isomorphism over a normal open

## Candidate declarations (not recorded; for a later Formalize pass after review)

- A theorem in `Hartshorne.Scheme.AbsoluteNormalizationUniversal` (or a new module next to it),
  e.g. `Hartshorne.isIso_absoluteNormalizationMap_resLE_of_isNormal`: for `X` integral and an
  open `U` with `IsNormal U`, the restriction of `Hartshorne.absoluteNormalizationMap X` over
  `U` (`Scheme.Hom.resLE` / `morphismRestrict`) is an isomorphism.

## Prior art

- The case `U = X` is the project instance `Hartshorne.isIso_fromNormalization_of_isNormal`
  (`src/Hartshorne/Scheme/AbsoluteNormalizationUniversal.lean`).
- Pinned Mathlib `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.normalizationPullback`
  (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization.lean`) compares the normalization of a base change
  with the base change of the normalization and is an isomorphism along a smooth `g`; an open
  immersion is smooth. One route: identify the restriction over `U` with the normalization of
  `U` (the generic point of `X` lies in `U`), then apply the `U = X` case. A direct route via
  `Hartshorne.absoluteNormalizationObjIso` on affine opens of `U` (integral closure of an
  integrally closed domain is itself) and locality of `IsIso` on the target also works.
