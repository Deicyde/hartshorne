# The section-algebra model of a birational projective morphism

## Status

Partial, sorry-free, not recorded (no `lean:`). Module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`, namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`. Only the affine-chart part of Step 2 is
proved; the global statement over `X` is open.

## Infrastructure survey

- Relative Proj exists as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (sections
  over affine opens, graded, localizing on basic opens) with
  `relProj`, `relProjToBase`, `restrictIso`, and the hypothesis predicates
  `DegreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`, `DegreeOneCoherent`, `GeneratedInDegreeOne`,
  bundled in `RelativeProjData`. Degree-one generation of a sheaf of graded
  algebras is therefore already expressible.
- Missing for the global statement:
  1. tensor powers `L^d` of an invertible module on `Z` with the graded
     multiplication `L^a ⊗ L^b → L^{a+b}` and its associativity;
  2. the graded ring `⨁_d Γ(f⁻¹ U, L^d)` for affine `U ⊆ X`, with `Γ(U, 𝒪_X)`
     in degree zero, packaged as a `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X` (the
     localization condition is quasi-coherence of `f_* L^d`, Prop. 5.8(c), at
     the level of rings of sections);
  3. for a closed immersion `i : Z → ℙⁿ_A`, the identification `Z ≅ Proj T`
     with `T` the (saturated) homogeneous coordinate ring under which
     `i^* 𝒪(1)` is `𝒪_{Proj T}(1)`; `projective_iff_exists_iso_proj` gives the
     isomorphism of schemes only;
  4. the comparison morphism `Z → Proj_X S`, glued from the chart isomorphisms.
- No ring structure on `Γ_*(𝒪_{Proj T})` exists in general (only the
  `T`-module structure `GradedGlobalSections.gammaStarModule`). For a graded
  domain `T`, `ProjectiveSectionRing.sectionToFraction` embeds the sections of
  `𝒪(n)` in `Frac T` multiplicatively (`sectionToFraction_sectionMul`); this
  is the multiplication used here.

## What is proved

- `SectionAlgebraModel.exists_bijective_twistSection`: for `T` generated over
  `T₀` (finite type over a field) by finitely many elements of degree one,
  `T_n → Γ(Proj T, 𝒪(n))`, `s ↦ s / 1` (`GradedGlobalSections.twistSection`),
  is bijective for `n ≫ 0`. From
  `EventualSectionRecovery.exists_bijective_alphaComponent` with
  `M = T`, graded by `gradedTwist 𝒜 0`, through `bijective_alphaComponent_iff`,
  `bijective_constHom_iff`, `bijective_constHom0_congr`.
- `SectionAlgebraModel.gradedImage`, `SectionAlgebraModel.sectionAlgebraPiece`:
  for a graded domain `T`, the degree-`m` part of the section algebra of
  `𝒪(d)` inside `Frac T` (`T₀` for `m = 0`, the values of sections of
  `𝒪(md)` for `m ≥ 1`).
- `SectionAlgebraModel.exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese`: for a graded
  domain `T` generated by `x₀, …, xᵣ ∈ T₁` over `T₀` of finite type over a
  field, with `T₊ ≠ 0`, there is `d₀` such that for all `d ≥ d₀`: `d > 0`;
  every piece `m` equals `T_{md}`; every piece lies in the subring generated by
  pieces `0` and `1`; and the subring generated by all pieces is the image of
  the Veronese subring `T^{(d)}`. With `Veronese.projIso` this is the
  affine-chart form of the article: the section algebra of `L^d` is
  `≅ T^{(d)}`, generated in degree one, with `Proj ≅ Proj T`.

## Remaining goal

For `f : Z ⟶ X` projective and birational between varieties over `k` (Hartshorne
assumes `X` quasi-projective; the chartwise argument here needs only a finite
affine cover of `X`), and the closed
immersion `i : Z ⟶ ℙⁿ_X` with `L = i^* 𝒪(1)`: there is `d₀` such that for
`d ≥ d₀` the graded `𝒪_X`-algebra `S` with `S₀ = 𝒪_X`, `S_m = f_* L^{md}`
(`m ≥ 1`) is a `RelativeProjData X` (degree one coherent by
`isCoherent_pushforward_of_projective`, generated in degree one) and
`(RelativeProjData.relativeProj) ≅ Z` over `X`.

## Next route

1. Over each affine `U = Spec A`, identify `f⁻¹ U ≅ Proj T_U` with `T_U` the
   saturated homogeneous coordinate ring of `f⁻¹ U ⊆ ℙⁿ_A` and `L ↦ 𝒪(1)`
   (item 3 above). For a variety, `T_U` is a domain and `(T_U)₀ = A`.
2. Take a finite affine cover of `X` and `d₀` the maximum of the bounds of
   `exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese`; the bound holds for every larger
   `d`, and degree-one generation is local on `X`, so it holds on every affine.
3. Build `S` (items 1–2 above). In the integral case one can avoid general
   tensor-power associativity by realizing every `Γ(f⁻¹ U, L^{md})` inside a
   fixed field, as `sectionToFraction` does chartwise.
4. Glue `Z_U ≅ Proj T_U ≅ Proj T_U^{(d)} ≅ Proj S(U)` using
   `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.restrictIso`, checking compatibility on basic
   opens.
