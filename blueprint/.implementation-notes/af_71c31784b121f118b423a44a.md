# The section-algebra model of a birational projective morphism

## Status

Partial, sorry-free, not recorded (no `lean:`). Modules
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeHomogeneousCoordinateAlgebra` (namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`), `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjDesc` and
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjVeronese` (namespace
`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`). The quasi-coherent graded algebra,
Hartshorne's hypotheses for it, and `Proj_X S ≅ Z` over `X` are proved; the
identification of its graded pieces with `f_* L^{md}` is proved on the members of
a finite affine cover (not yet on every affine open, and not canonically).

## Design choice (third pass)

Design (D): `S = T^{(d)}` for the relative homogeneous coordinate algebra
`T(U) = Γ(X, U)[x₀, …, xᵣ] / I_U`. Chosen because with (D) the algebra, its
quasi-coherence, generation in degree one, coherence of `S₁` and
`Proj_X S ≅ Z` hold for every `d ≥ 1` and are independent of the uniform bound;
the bound only enters the comparison `S(U)_m ≅ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})`. Design (K)
would have needed the uniform bound (on every affine open) before even
defining a graded algebra generated in degree one, plus multiplicative frames
for `L^a ⊗ L^b → L^{a+b}`. Consequently the order (b), (d), (c), (a) was used:
(a) under (D) is only the degreewise comparison.

The ideal `I_U` (`chartIdeal`) is defined as the homogeneous prime of the image
of the generic point of `Z` under `f⁻¹(U) ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec Γ(X, U)} ≅ Proj Γ(X, U)[x]`
(`⊤` if `f⁻¹(U)` is empty). For integral `Z` this is the saturated ideal of
`f⁻¹(U)` (the proof of `exists_chartIso` identifies it with the ideal of
`exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing`), and restriction/localization
become point computations: `I_V = I_U ∩ Γ(X, V)[x]` for `U ⊆ V`.

## Infrastructure survey

- Relative Proj exists as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (rings of
  sections `obj` over affine opens, restriction along basic opens, graded,
  coequifibered = localizing on basic opens) with `relProj`, `relProjToBase`,
  `restrictIso`, and the predicates `DegreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`,
  `DegreeOneCoherent`, `GeneratedInDegreeOne`, bundled in `RelativeProjData`.
- Relative projective space `ℙʳ_X` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme r X`
  (`ℙʳ_ℤ ×_ℤ X`), its `𝒪(n)` is `projectiveSpaceTwistingSheaf r X n` (pulled
  back from `ℙʳ_ℤ`), a projective morphism is `Hartshorne.Projective`
  (closed immersion into some `ℙʳ_X` over `X`).
- Birational is encoded as in the Step 4 Lean (`BirationalDenominatorIdeal`):
  `f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` plus bijectivity of
  `f.genericFunctionFieldMap`.
- Tensor powers: `moduleSheafTensorPower`, with `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`,
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerAddIso`, `moduleSheafTensorPowerRestrictIso`;
  pullback and tensor: `pullbackTensorIso` (invertible sheaves).
- No ring structure on `Γ_*(𝒪_{Proj T})` exists in general (only the
  `T`-module structure). For a graded domain `T`,
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.sectionToFraction` embeds the sections of `𝒪(n)` in
  `Frac T` multiplicatively; this is the multiplication used on charts.

## What is proved

Chart algebra (`BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`):

- `exists_bijective_twistSection`: `T_n → Γ(Proj T, 𝒪(n))`, `s ↦ s / 1`, is
  bijective for `n ≫ 0` (`T` generated in degree one over `T₀` of finite type
  over a field; Exercise 5.9(b) for `M = T`).
- `gradedImage`, `sectionAlgebraPiece`, `exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese`:
  for a graded domain `T`, the section algebra of `𝒪(d)` inside `Frac T`
  equals the Veronese ring `T^{(d)}` in every degree and is generated in degree
  one, for `d ≫ 0`.

Affine identification (`ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing`):

- `pullbackProjMapTwistIso`: `(Proj.map φ)^* 𝒪(n) ≅ 𝒪(n)` (Prop. 5.12(c),
  everywhere-defined case, from `ProjTwistPullback.pullbackTwistIso`).
- `pullbackProjectiveSpaceOverSpecTwistIso`: under
  `projectiveSpaceOverSpecIso A r (Equiv.refl _)`, `𝒪_{ℙʳ_A}(n)` is
  `𝒪_{Proj A[x]}(n)`.
- `exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing`: for a closed immersion
  `i : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec A}`, a saturated homogeneous `I`
  (`gammaStarIdeal`, Ex. 5.10) and `e : Z ≅ Proj (A[x]/I)` over `ℙʳ_A` with
  `i^* 𝒪(n) ≅ e^* 𝒪(n)` for all `n`, and `A[x]/I` a domain when `Z` is
  integral (the private `isDomain_of_isIntegral_proj` reproves the Ex. 5.14(a)
  argument over any base ring, using
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.forall_pow_mul_eq_zero_or`).
- `tensorPowerPullbackTwistIso`, `tensorPowerPullbackProjectiveSpaceTwistIso`:
  for `L ≅ ψ^* 𝒪(1)`, `L^{⊗(N+1)} ≅ ψ^* 𝒪(N+1)` (recursive
  `noncomputable def`).
- `chartEmbedding i hU`: for `i : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_X`, `f = π ∘ i` and an affine open
  `U` (`hU : IsAffineOpen U`), the morphism `f⁻¹(U) ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec Γ(X, U)}`;
  `chartEmbedding_fst`, `chartEmbedding_projection`, `chartEmbedding_comp`

## Remaining goal

The article's degreewise identification on every affine open, canonically:
for `d ≥ d₀` (uniform), every affine `U ⊆ X` and `m ≥ 1`, an isomorphism
`T(U)^{(d)}_m = T(U)_{md} ≅ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{⊗md})` compatible with restriction to
basic opens (and with multiplication, `L^{a} ⊗ L^{b} ≅ L^{a+b}`), i.e.
`S_m ≅ f_* L^{md}` as quasi-coherent sheaves. Currently it is proved only for
the members of a finite affine cover, through non-canonical `AddEquiv`s.
Once it holds, record `exists_sectionAlgebraModel_of_finiteCover` strengthened
to all affine opens (or the sheaf form), with `sectionAlgebraRelativeProjData`,
`sectionAlgebraRelativeProjIso`, `sectionAlgebraRelativeProjIso_hom_comp`.

## Next route

1. Canonical comparison `α_U : T(U)_n → Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{⊗n})`: build it as
   `s ↦` (pullback along `chartIso` of `twistSection n s`) transported by
   `pullbackTwistIsoOfFac`, `pullbackChartEmbeddingTwistIso` and
   `tensorPowerPullbackProjectiveSpaceTwistIso`, and prove naturality in `U`
   for `D(g) ⊆ U` (the chart isomorphisms are compatible by
   `projMap_chartPoint`/`coordToZ_map`; the twist isomorphisms need a
   naturality lemma for `pullbackTwistIsoOfFac` under `Proj.map (polyMap ρ)`).
2. Localization of the target: `Γ(f⁻¹D(g), L^{n}) = Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{n})_g`. Proved
   in elementwise form (Lemma 5.3 for the quasi-coherent `f_* F`, `f` qcqs):
   `exists_pow_smul_eq_zero_of_isAffineOpen_preimage`,
   `exists_pow_smul_extension_of_isAffineOpen_preimage`; `T(D(g))_n = T(U)_n[1/g]`
   follows degreewise from `coordinateAlgebra_isLocalizationAway`.
3. Local-global: each affine `U` is covered by opens that are basic in both
   `U` and a member of the finite cover (`exists_basicOpen_le_affine_inter`);
   `α` on those is the localization of `α` on the cover member (bijective
   for `n ≥ d₀`), so `α_U` is bijective by Mathlib's
   `bijective_of_isLocalized_span` (needs `α_U` to be `Γ(X, U)`-linear).

The crux is step 1: the comparison in
`exists_addEquiv_sections_tensorPower_of_fac` is now canonical given
`chartIso` (no `Nonempty.some` left), but its naturality for `D(g) ⊆ U` needs
naturality of `sectionsPullbackIso`, `pullbackTwistIsoOfFac` (through
`ProjTwistPullback.pullbackTwistIso`, cf. `psiAdj_etaSec`) and `twistSection`
under `Proj.map` of the localization `T(U) → T(D(g))`.
