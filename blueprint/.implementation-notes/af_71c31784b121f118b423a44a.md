# The section-algebra model of a birational projective morphism

## Status

Partial, sorry-free, not recorded (no `lean:`). Modules
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalSectionAlgebraModel` (namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`), and the supporting new modules
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing` (same namespace) and
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjDesc` (namespace
`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`). Steps 1–2 of the book's proof are
proved on every affine open of the base; the global statement over `X` (the
quasi-coherent graded algebra and the identification of its relative Proj) is
open.

## Infrastructure survey

- Relative Proj exists as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (rings of
  sections `obj` over affine opens, restriction along basic opens, graded,
  coequifibered = localizing on basic opens) with `relProj`, `relProjToBase`,
  `restrictIso`, and the predicates `DegreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`,
  `DegreeOneCoherent`, `GeneratedInDegreeOne`, bundled in `RelativeProjData`.
- Relative projective space `ℙʳ_X` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme r X`
  (`ℙʳ_ℤ ×_ℤ X`), its `𝒪(n)` is `projectiveSpaceTwistingSheaf r X n` (pulled
  back from `ℙʳ_ℤ`), a projective morphism is `Hartshorne.Projective`
  (closed immersion into some `ℙʳ_X` over `X`).
- Birational is encoded as in the Step 4 Lean (`BirationalDenominatorIdeal`):
  `f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` plus bijectivity of
  `f.genericFunctionFieldMap`.
- Tensor powers: `moduleSheafTensorPower`, with `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`,
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerAddIso`, `moduleSheafTensorPowerRestrictIso`;
  pullback and tensor: `pullbackTensorIso` (invertible sheaves).
- No ring structure on `Γ_*(𝒪_{Proj T})` exists in general (only the
  `T`-module structure). For a graded domain `T`,
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.sectionToFraction` embeds the sections of `𝒪(n)` in
  `Frac T` multiplicatively; this is the multiplication used on charts.

## What is proved

Chart algebra (`BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`):

- `exists_bijective_twistSection`: `T_n → Γ(Proj T, 𝒪(n))`, `s ↦ s / 1`, is
  bijective for `n ≫ 0` (`T` generated in degree one over `T₀` of finite type
  over a field; Exercise 5.9(b) for `M = T`).
- `gradedImage`, `sectionAlgebraPiece`, `exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese`:
  for a graded domain `T`, the section algebra of `𝒪(d)` inside `Frac T`
  equals the Veronese ring `T^{(d)}` in every degree and is generated in degree
  one, for `d ≫ 0`.

Affine identification (`ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing`):

- `pullbackProjMapTwistIso`: `(Proj.map φ)^* 𝒪(n) ≅ 𝒪(n)` (Prop. 5.12(c),
  everywhere-defined case, from `ProjTwistPullback.pullbackTwistIso`).
- `pullbackProjectiveSpaceOverSpecTwistIso`: under
  `projectiveSpaceOverSpecIso A r (Equiv.refl _)`, `𝒪_{ℙʳ_A}(n)` is
  `𝒪_{Proj A[x]}(n)`.
- `exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing`: for a closed immersion
  `i : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec A}`, a saturated homogeneous `I`
  (`gammaStarIdeal`, Ex. 5.10) and `e : Z ≅ Proj (A[x]/I)` over `ℙʳ_A` with
  `i^* 𝒪(n) ≅ e^* 𝒪(n)` for all `n`, and `A[x]/I` a domain when `Z` is
  integral (the private `isDomain_of_isIntegral_proj` reproves the Ex. 5.14(a)
  argument over any base ring, using
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.forall_pow_mul_eq_zero_or`).
- `tensorPowerPullbackTwistIso`, `tensorPowerPullbackProjectiveSpaceTwistIso`:
  for `L ≅ ψ^* 𝒪(1)`, `L^{⊗(N+1)} ≅ ψ^* 𝒪(N+1)` (recursive
  `noncomputable def`).
- `chartEmbedding i hU`: for `i : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_X`, `f = π ∘ i` and an affine open
  `U` (`hU : IsAffineOpen U`), the morphism `f⁻¹(U) ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec Γ(X, U)}`;
  `chartEmbedding_fst`, `chartEmbedding_projection`, `chartEmbedding_comp`
  (followed by `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeMap r hU.fromSpec` it is
  `f⁻¹(U) ⊆ Z ⟶ ℙʳ_X`), `isPullback_chartEmbedding`,
  `isClosedImmersion_chartEmbedding`, `pullbackChartEmbeddingTwistIso`
  (`𝒪(n)` pulls back to `(i^* 𝒪(n))|_{f⁻¹(U)}`).

Step 2 on charts (`BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`):

- `exists_iso_proj_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese` (closed subscheme of
  `ℙʳ_A`, `A` of finite type over `k`, `Z` integral) and
  `exists_iso_proj_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese_of_isAffineOpen` (over an
  affine open `U` of `X`, `f⁻¹(U)` nonempty): `f⁻¹(U) ≅ Proj T`, `T` a domain,
  `L` ↔ `𝒪(1)`, and the `exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese` conclusions.
- `exists_addEquiv_sections_tensorPower_of_isAffineOpen` (no integrality):
  `Γ(f⁻¹(U), (L|_{f⁻¹(U)})^{⊗(N+1)}) ≃+ T_{N+1}` for `N ≫ 0`, through
  `twistSection`.

Gluing (`RelativeProjDesc`):

- `relProjDesc`, `projι_relProjDesc`, `relProjDesc_unique`,
  `relProjDesc_comp`, `isIso_relProjDesc`, `relProjIsoOfProj`: compatible
  morphisms `Proj S(U) ⟶ W` induce `Proj_X S ⟶ W`; if they are open
  immersions over `U` with image `g⁻¹(U)` it is an isomorphism over `X`
  (the pattern of `QuasiCoherentAlgebra.isIso_descOfι` for relative Spec).

## Remaining goal

For `X` integral, separated, of finite type over `k` (Hartshorne: a
quasi-projective variety), `Z` integral, `f : Z ⟶ X` birational (encoding
above), `i : Z ⟶ ℙʳ_X` a closed immersion with `i ≫ π = f`, and
`L = i^* 𝒪(1)`: there is `d₀` such that for `d ≥ d₀` there is a
`RelativeProjData X` whose algebra `S` has `S(U)₀ = Γ(X, U)`,
`S(U)_m ≅ Γ(f⁻¹(U), L^{⊗md})` (`m ≥ 1`, compatibly with restriction and
multiplication), generated in degree one (`S₁` coherent by
`isCoherent_pushforward_of_isInvertible`), and
`RelativeProjData.relativeProj ≅ Z` over `X`.

## Next route

1. Build `S` as a `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X`. Two designs:
   - (K) Realize every piece inside `K[t]`, `K = K(Z)`: `S(U)` the subring
     with `t^m`-coefficients in the image of `Γ(f⁻¹(U), L^{md})` under a
     rational trivialization `s ↦ s / x_j^{md}` (`x_j` a coordinate section
     not vanishing at the generic point). Restriction maps are then
     inclusions, so the functor is immediate; coequifibered is
     `S_m(D(g)) = S_m(U)[1/g]` inside `K`, i.e. quasi-coherence of
     `f_* L^{N}` (`isQuasicoherent_pushforward`) in sections form. Needs the
     multiplication `L^{a} ⊗ L^{b} → L^{a+b}` to send `x_j^a ⊗ x_j^b` to
     `x_j^{a+b}` (frames through `pullbackTensorIso` and `twistMulIso`), and
     compatibility with `sectionToFraction` on the charts.
   - (D) `S = T^{(d)}` for the relative homogeneous coordinate algebra
     `T(U) = Γ(X, U)[x]/I_U`; needs `I_{D(g)} = (I_U)_g` (`Γ_*` of the ideal
     sheaf commutes with localization on the base) and the degreewise
     identification with `f_* L^{md}` from
     `exists_addEquiv_sections_tensorPower_of_isAffineOpen`.
2. Uniform `d₀`: take a finite affine cover (`X` quasi-compact), the maximum
   of the chart bounds; for an arbitrary affine `U` the comparison is an
   isomorphism of quasi-coherent sheaves checked on the cover.
3. Glue with `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.relProjIsoOfProj`, taking
   `j U : Proj S(U) ≅ Proj T_U^{(d)} ≅ Proj T_U ≅ f⁻¹(U) ⊆ Z`
   (`Veronese.projIso`, `exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing` applied
   to `chartEmbedding`). Compatibility with restriction can be checked after
   composing with the mono `i` (closed immersion); it needs the naturality of
   `Veronese.toProj` with respect to `Proj.map` (not yet in the project).
