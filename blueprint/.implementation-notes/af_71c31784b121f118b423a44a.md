# The section-algebra model of a birational projective morphism

## Status

## Design choice (third pass)

Design (D): `S = T^{(d)}` for the relative homogeneous coordinate algebra
`T(U) = Γ(X, U)[x₀, …, xᵣ] / I_U`. Chosen because with (D) the algebra, its
quasi-coherence, generation in degree one, coherence of `S₁` and
`Proj_X S ≅ Z` hold for every `d ≥ 1` and are independent of the uniform bound;
the bound only enters the comparison `S(U)_m ≅ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})`. Design (K)
would have needed the uniform bound (on every affine open) before even
defining a graded algebra generated in degree one, plus multiplicative frames
for `L^a ⊗ L^b → L^{a+b}`. Consequently the order (b), (d), (c), (a) was used:
(a) under (D) is only the degreewise comparison.

The ideal `I_U` (`chartIdeal`) is defined as the homogeneous prime of the image
of the generic point of `Z` under `f⁻¹(U) ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec Γ(X, U)} ≅ Proj Γ(X, U)[x]`
(`⊤` if `f⁻¹(U)` is empty). For integral `Z` this is the saturated ideal of
`f⁻¹(U)` (the proof of `exists_chartIso` identifies it with the ideal of
`exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing`), and restriction/localization
become point computations: `I_V = I_U ∩ Γ(X, V)[x]` for `U ⊆ V`.

## Infrastructure survey

- Relative Proj exists as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (rings of
  sections `obj` over affine opens, restriction along basic opens, graded,
  coequifibered = localizing on basic opens) with `relProj`, `relProjToBase`,
  `restrictIso`, and the predicates `DegreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`,
  `DegreeOneCoherent`, `GeneratedInDegreeOne`, bundled in `RelativeProjData`.
- Relative projective space `ℙʳ_X` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme r X`
  (`ℙʳ_ℤ ×_ℤ X`), its `𝒪(n)` is `projectiveSpaceTwistingSheaf r X n` (pulled
  back from `ℙʳ_ℤ`), a projective morphism is `Hartshorne.Projective`
  (closed immersion into some `ℙʳ_X` over `X`).
- Birational is encoded as in the Step 4 Lean (`BirationalDenominatorIdeal`):
  `f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` plus bijectivity of
  `f.genericFunctionFieldMap`.
- Tensor powers: `moduleSheafTensorPower`, with `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`,
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerAddIso`, `moduleSheafTensorPowerRestrictIso`;
  pullback and tensor: `pullbackTensorIso` (invertible sheaves).
- No ring structure on `Γ_*(𝒪_{Proj T})` exists in general (only the
  `T`-module structure). For a graded domain `T`,
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.sectionToFraction` embeds the sections of `𝒪(n)` in
  `Frac T` multiplicatively; this is the multiplication used on charts.

## Fifth pass: route for the sheaf form

Route chosen: (A'), a variant of route (A). The comparison is defined *globally* from data pulled
back from `ℙʳ_ℤ`, so naturality in `U` and `Γ(X, U)`-linearity hold by construction; the chart
chain is only used per affine open, to prove bijectivity, and only on pulled-back monomials.
Chosen over (B) (embedding into `K(Z)`) because (B) needs a trivialization of `L^{md}` at the
generic point and the injection of sections of a pulled-back tensor power into its generic stalk,
none of which exists in the project, while the `etaSec` calculus for (A') already existed
(`pullbackComp_hom_pullbackGlobalSection`, `pullbackCongr`/`pullbackId`/`restrictFunctorIsoPullback`
unit lemmas, `psiAdj_etaSec`).

Proved (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.SectionAlgebraSheafComparison`, namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`):

- `projMapTwistIso`, `projMapTwistIso_hom_pullbackGlobalSection`: `(Proj.map φ)^* 𝒪(d) ≅ 𝒪(d)`
  sends the pullback of `s / 1` to `φ(s) / 1`; `pullbackPullbackTwistIso`,
  `pullbackPullbackTwistIso_pullbackTop` (the same through `j^* ε^*` for `j ≫ ε = Proj.map φ`).

  function pulled back from `t ∈ S₀` (or from `Γ(X, U)` through `Spec S₀ ⟶ Spec Γ(X, U) ≅ U`)
  multiplies `s / 1` by `t`.
- `bijective_app_of_iso`, `bijective_map_of_eq`, `bijective_pullbackSection_of_le`,
  `pullbackTopAddHom`, `bijective_iso_comp_pullbackTopAddHom`, `iso_comp_pullbackTopAddHom_smul`.

## Sixth pass: the sheaf form (route A', finished)

Proved (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.SectionAlgebraSheafComparison`, namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`):

- `chartTwistSection_smul`: `Γ(X, U)`-scalars pass through the chart map (the scalar becomes the
  function pulled back along `Proj T(U) ⟶ Z ⟶ X`).
- `coordHom_monomial`, `mk_homogeneousComponent_mem`, `twistSection_add`, `twistSection_zero`,
  `twistSection_mk_homogeneousComponent_eq_zero`.
- `chartTwistSection_polyTwistSection` (key identity, every `p`):
  `chartTwistSection (polyTwistSection p) = (p_n mod I_U) / 1`, and
  `chartTwistSection_polyTwistSection_of_mem` for `p` homogeneous of degree `n`.
- `coordLift` (a homogeneous lift of `x ∈ T(U)_n`), `coordTwistSection U n : T(U)_n →+
  Γ(f⁻¹U, 𝒪(n)|_Z)` (`[p] ↦ polyTwistSection p`, well defined by the key identity and injectivity
  of the chart map) with `chartTwistSection_coordTwistSection`, `coordTwistSection_mk`,
  `coordTwistSection_restrict` (natural for `V ⟶ U`), `coordTwistSection_structureMap_mul`
  (`Γ(X, U)`-linear), `coordTwistSection_bijective` (bijective whenever `s ↦ s / 1` is, i.e. for
  `n ≥ d₀` by `exists_bound_bijective_twistSection`).
- `tensorPowerL`, `tensorPowerTwistOnZIso` (`L^{n} ≅ 𝒪(n)|_Z`, `n > 0`, global),
  `veroneseTwistSection d U hm : T(U)^{(d)}_m →+ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})` with `_bijective`, `_restrict`,
  `_structureMap_mul`.
- Headline `exists_sectionAlgebraModel_natural`: for `d ≥ d₀` there is a `RelativeProjData X`
  `S` (`S₀ = 𝒪_X`, `S₁` coherent, generated in degree one, `Proj_X S ≅ Z` over `X`) and additive
  isomorphisms `E_U : Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md}) ≃+ S(U)_m` (`U` affine, `m ≥ 1`) compatible with restriction
  along every inclusion of affine opens and `Γ(X, U)`-linear through the structure map, i.e. an
  isomorphism of `𝒪_X`-modules `f_* L^{md} ≅ S_m` on the affine opens (which determine both
  quasi-coherent sheaves). The fourth-pass `exists_sectionAlgebraModel` (per-affine `Nonempty`) is
  kept as a weaker form.

Not addressed: compatibility of `E` with multiplication (`L^{a} ⊗ L^{b} → L^{a+b}` against the
product of `S`); the article's algebra structure on `⊕ f_* L^{md}` is then transported from `S`.
