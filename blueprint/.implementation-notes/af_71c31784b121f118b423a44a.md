# The section-algebra model of a birational projective morphism

## Status

Fourth pass: candidate for recording (pending review). Headline
`exists_sectionAlgebraModel` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.SectionAlgebraUniformBound`):
for `d ≥ d₀` (one bound) there is a `RelativeProjData X` (quasi-coherent graded
`𝒪_X`-algebra with `S₀ = 𝒪_X`, `S₁` coherent, generated in degree one) whose
degree-`m` part over every affine open `U` is additively isomorphic to
`Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})` (`m ≥ 1`), and whose relative Proj is `Z` over `X`. The
identification of the graded pieces is per affine open (a `Nonempty` additive
isomorphism, built canonically from `chartIso` but not proved compatible with
restriction or linear over `Γ(X, U)`); the sheaf form `S_m ≅ f_* L^{md}` is the
remaining strengthening (below). Other modules:
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing`,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeHomogeneousCoordinateAlgebra` (namespace
`Hartshorne.SectionAlgebraModel`), `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjDesc` and
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjVeronese` (namespace
`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`).

## Design choice (third pass)

Design (D): `S = T^{(d)}` for the relative homogeneous coordinate algebra
`T(U) = Γ(X, U)[x₀, …, xᵣ] / I_U`. Chosen because with (D) the algebra, its
quasi-coherence, generation in degree one, coherence of `S₁` and
`Proj_X S ≅ Z` hold for every `d ≥ 1` and are independent of the uniform bound;
the bound only enters the comparison `S(U)_m ≅ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})`. Design (K)
would have needed the uniform bound (on every affine open) before even
defining a graded algebra generated in degree one, plus multiplicative frames
for `L^a ⊗ L^b → L^{a+b}`. Consequently the order (b), (d), (c), (a) was used:
(a) under (D) is only the degreewise comparison.

The ideal `I_U` (`chartIdeal`) is defined as the homogeneous prime of the image
of the generic point of `Z` under `f⁻¹(U) ⟶ ℙʳ_{Spec Γ(X, U)} ≅ Proj Γ(X, U)[x]`
(`⊤` if `f⁻¹(U)` is empty). For integral `Z` this is the saturated ideal of
`f⁻¹(U)` (the proof of `exists_chartIso` identifies it with the ideal of
`exists_iso_proj_homogeneousCoordinateRing`), and restriction/localization
become point computations: `I_V = I_U ∩ Γ(X, V)[x]` for `U ⊆ V`.

## Infrastructure survey

- Relative Proj exists as `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra` (rings of
  sections `obj` over affine opens, restriction along basic opens, graded,
  coequifibered = localizing on basic opens) with `relProj`, `relProjToBase`,
  `restrictIso`, and the predicates `DegreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`,
  `DegreeOneCoherent`, `GeneratedInDegreeOne`, bundled in `RelativeProjData`.
- Relative projective space `ℙʳ_X` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme r X`
  (`ℙʳ_ℤ ×_ℤ X`), its `𝒪(n)` is `projectiveSpaceTwistingSheaf r X n` (pulled
  back from `ℙʳ_ℤ`), a projective morphism is `Hartshorne.Projective`
  (closed immersion into some `ℙʳ_X` over `X`).
- Birational is encoded as in the Step 4 Lean (`BirationalDenominatorIdeal`):
  `f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` plus bijectivity of
  `f.genericFunctionFieldMap`.
- Tensor powers: `moduleSheafTensorPower`, with `moduleSheafTensorPowerSuccIso`,
  `moduleSheafTensorPowerAddIso`, `moduleSheafTensorPowerRestrictIso`;
  pullback and tensor: `pullbackTensorIso` (invertible sheaves).
- No ring structure on `Γ_*(𝒪_{Proj T})` exists in general (only the
  `T`-module structure). For a graded domain `T`,
  `ProjectiveSectionRing.sectionToFraction` embeds the sections of `𝒪(n)` in
  `Frac T` multiplicatively; this is the multiplication used on charts.

## What is proved

Chart algebra (`BirationalSectionAlgebraModel`):

- `exists_bijective_twistSection`: `T_n → Γ(Proj T, 𝒪(n))`, `s ↦ s / 1`, is
  bijective for `n ≫ 0` (`T` generated in degree one over `T₀` of finite type
  over a field; Exercise 5.9(b) for `M = T`).
- `gradedImage`, `sectionAlgebraPiece`, `exists_sectionAlgebraPiece_eq_veronese`:
  for a graded domain `T`, the section algebra of `𝒪(d)` inside `Frac T`
  equals the Veronese ring `T^{(d)}` in every degree and is generated in degree
  one, for `d ≫ 0`.

Affine identification (`ProjectiveChartCoordinateRing`):

  `projectiveSpaceOverSpecIso A r (Equiv.refl _)`, `𝒪_{ℙʳ_A}(n)` is

## Remaining goal

The sheaf form of the identification: `S_m ≅ f_* L^{md}` as `𝒪_X`-modules, i.e.
the per-affine isomorphisms `T(U)_{md} ≅ Γ(f⁻¹U, L^{md})` chosen compatibly with
restriction to basic opens (and linear over `Γ(X, U)`). Now that the bound is
uniform, this is only a naturality statement: each comparison is the composite of
`s ↦ s / 1` (`twistSection`) with the sections of the sheaf isomorphism `γ_U` of
`nonempty_addEquiv_sections_tensorPower_of_bijective` and `sectionsPullbackIso`
along `chartIso`.
