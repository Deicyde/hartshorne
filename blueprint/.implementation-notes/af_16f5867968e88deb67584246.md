# Adjunction for an effective Cartier divisor

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.AdjunctionEffectiveCartierDivisor` (candidate, pending
review). Both the explicit isomorphism and the `Nonempty` form are proved.

- `Hartshorne.adjunctionEffectiveCartierDivisorIso`: for `p : X ⟶ Spec k` of finite type, `X`
  irreducible and regular, `D` effective with associated closed subscheme
  `i = subschemeι hD : Y ⟶ X`, `Y` irreducible and regular:
  `canonicalSheaf (i ≫ p) ≅ i^* (moduleSheafTensor (canonicalSheaf p) D.sheaf)`.
- `Hartshorne.adjunction_effectiveCartierDivisor`: the `Nonempty` form.
- `Hartshorne.normalSheafIsoPullbackCartierDivisorSheaf`: `normalSheaf i ≅ i^* D.sheaf`, for any
  effective Cartier divisor (no regularity needed). Chain: dual of
  `immersionConormalSheafIso` composed with `i^*` of `idealSheafIso`, then `pullbackDualIso`
  (inverse), then `i^*` of `moduleSheafDualIsoOfIso (negIsoDual D)` and the inverse of
  `moduleSheafDoubleDualIso`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.codim_range_subschemeι`: on a locally Noetherian
  scheme, an irreducible effective Cartier divisor has codimension one. Proof: the range is the
  closure of the image `x` of the generic point; on an affine `W ∋ x` where the ideal is
  `(g)`, `g` a non-zero-divisor, the prime of `x` is minimal over `(g)` (any smaller prime
  containing `g` is a point of `Y` specializing to `x`, hence equal to `x`), so its height is
  one by Mathlib's Krull principal ideal theorem
  (`Ideal.height_le_one_of_isPrincipal_of_mem_minimalPrimes`,
  `Ideal.one_le_height_span_singleton_of_mem_nonZeroDivisors`).

The main isomorphism is `adjunctionDeterminantNormalSheafIso` with `r = 1`, then
`moduleSheafExteriorPowerOneIso` and `normalSheafIsoPullbackCartierDivisorSheaf` in the second
tensor factor, then the inverse of `pullbackTensorIso` (both `ω_X` and `𝒪_X(D)` invertible).
