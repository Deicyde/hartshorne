# Constructible tangent-rank strata

## Status: partial; no `lean:` recorded; Roadmap return (split the node)

The Lean is in `Hartshorne/Cohomology/SmoothMorphisms/TangentRankLocus.lean`, namespace
`Hartshorne.TangentRank`. It is sorry-free and uses only the standard axioms. The constructibility
half of the article is fully proved. Most of the stratification half is proved in one precise
reading. The article's stratification sentences are not precise enough to fix one reading, so no
`lean:` is set.

Throughout, `X Y : Over (Spec k)` with `k` algebraically closed and `f : X ⟶ Y`, with
`[LocallyOfFiniteType X.hom] [LocallyOfFiniteType Y.hom]`. `[QuasiCompact X.hom]` is added
wherever a finite-type `X` is needed. Quasi-compactness of `Y` is never used. The rank of the
tangent map at `x` is
`finrank κ(x) (LinearMap.range (zariskiTangentMap f.left x))`. The statements that mention it
carry `haveI := LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian Y.hom`, which supplies the
finite-dimensionality instance that `zariskiTangentMap` needs.

## Proved

- `exists_isConstructible_tangentRank_le` (the constructibility half): for every `r : ℕ` there is
  a constructible `C ⊆ X` (Mathlib's `Topology.IsConstructible`) with
  `{x | IsClosed {x} ∧ rank ≤ r} = C ∩ {x | IsClosed {x}}`. Here `C` is
  `{x | moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/k} x ≤ moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/Y} x + r}`. A finite-type `X` is a
  Noetherian space, so Mathlib's constructible sets are the finite unions of locally closed sets.
- `moduleFiberDim_add_finrank_range_zariskiTangentMap`: at a closed point,
  `moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/Y} x + rank = moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/k} x`. This is the sheaf-level bridge
  that the old note said was missing. It is assembled from existing results:
  `SmoothTangent.stalk_kaehler_transitivity_exact` (the stalk of II.8.11),
  `SmoothTangent.exact_mapBaseChange_map_of_subsingleton`,
  `SmoothTangent.bijective_residue_germ_appTop`,
  `SmoothTangent.finrank_fibre_relativeDifferentials_eq_stalk`,
  `SmoothTangent.moduleFiberDim_relativeDifferentials_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace` (II.8.7 at a
  closed point) and `CotangentKaehler.finrank_fiber_add_finrank_range`. The hypothesis setup
  repeats the one in `SmoothTangent.injective_localCotangentBaseChange_iff_of_isClosed`. That
  file belongs to another article, so it was not refactored. This is a candidate for raising
  copied code.
- `finrank_range_zariskiTangentMap`: the tangent map and the scalar-extended cotangent map have
  the same rank (the dual of a map has the same rank).
- `isConstructible_setOf_moduleFiberDim_le`, `exists_forall_moduleFiberDim_le`,
  `isConstructible_of_isClosed`: generic helpers on coherent fibre dimensions and Noetherian
  spaces.
- `exists_isOpen_subset_tangentRank_eq`: for `Z` irreducible and closed and an open `O` meeting
  `Z`, there is an open `U ⊆ O` meeting `Z` with three properties. The rank is constant at the
  closed points of `U ∩ Z`. The reduced induced closed subscheme on `Z` (`reducedInducedSubscheme`)
  is regular at every point over `U`. The reduced induced closed subscheme on `closure (f '' Z)`
  is regular at every point of `f '' (U ∩ Z)`. The regular loci come from
  `exists_nonempty_dense_isRegularAt` through `exists_isOpen_isRegularAt_reducedInducedSubscheme`.
- `exists_isOpen_tangentRank_eq_of_maximal`: let `Z` be an irreducible component of the closure
  of `X_r`, stated as `Maximal (fun T ↦ IsIrreducible T ∧ T ⊆ closure X_r) Z`. Then there is an
  open `U` meeting `Z` with these properties:
  - the rank is a constant `n ≤ r` at the closed points of `U ∩ Z`;
  - the closed points of `U ∩ Z` are dense in `Z`;
  - their images are closed points, dense in `closure (f '' Z)`;
  - both reduced structures are regular as above.
- `exists_finite_tangentRank_stratification`: a finite, pairwise disjoint family of pairs
  `(Z, U)` with `Z` irreducible and closed, `U` open and `U ∩ Z ≠ ∅`. The strata `U ∩ Z` cover
  `X`, and each has the properties of `exists_isOpen_subset_tangentRank_eq`. The proof is
  Noetherian induction over `Closeds X` (`WellFoundedLT`). At each step it removes a stratum on an
  irreducible component that is open in the remaining closed set
  (`exists_isIrreducible_isOpen_inter_subset`).

DAG reachability with a trial `lean:` of the three main theorems shows no gaps, after the
link-only edges to coherent-fiber-dimension-upper-semicontinuous and
relative-differentials-transitivity.

## Remaining goals

Only the stratification sentences of the article are open. Each needs a decision on its reading
before it can be proved.

1. **Coupling of the two stratification statements.** The article asks that each irreducible
   component of the closure of `X_r` contain a dense stratum *of the stratification*. The proved
   stratification and the proved dense strata are separate. Route: run the Noetherian induction
   first on `T₀ = closure X_r`. A component `Z'` of `T₀` that has not yet been chosen is never cut
   by a step that chooses another component `Z`, because `U ∩ T ⊆ Z` forces `U ∩ Z' = ∅`. So
   strengthen the induction claim to say "every irreducible component of `T` is the `Z` of some
   stratum". Then stratify the open remainder `X \ T₀`. For that, generalise the induction to
   sets of the form (closed) ∩ (open), or apply it to `closure (X \ T₀)` and intersect with
   `T₀ᶜ`.
2. **"Cotangent presentations nonsingular".** The phrase is undefined. The proof already shows
   that the fibre dimensions of `Ω_{X/k}` and `Ω_{X/Y}` are constant on each stratum (they equal
   their values at the generic point). Exposing this in the statements is a small change. If the
   phrase means local freeness of the restrictions of these sheaves to the reduced strata, the
   missing input is "a coherent sheaf of constant fibre rank on a reduced scheme is locally free"
   (Ex. II.5.8), applied to the pullbacks to the strata.
3. **"The relevant stratum of `Y`".** The proved form makes the reduced structure on
   `closure (f(Z))` regular along `f(U ∩ Z)`. It does not stratify `Y`.

The image-dimension node (tangent-rank-image-dimension) needs only
`exists_isOpen_tangentRank_eq_of_maximal`, which is proved.

  with `Hartshorne.TangentRank.exists_isConstructible_tangentRank_le`.
- "Tangent-rank stratification and dense strata": a precise statement is needed, for example the
  one proved in `exists_finite_tangentRank_stratification` and
  `exists_isOpen_tangentRank_eq_of_maximal`. These could be recorded once the statement settles
  the three points above.
- The prose "Proof depends on" items "Generic freeness and flattening stratifications … local
  matrix-minor rank strata" and "Chevalley constructibility for their images" are not used. The
  constructibility comes from upper semicontinuity of coherent fibre dimension and the transitivity
  sequence. The strata come from generic points and the dense nonsingular locus.
