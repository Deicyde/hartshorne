# Constructible tangent-rank strata

## Status: not started in Lean; Roadmap return

No Lean has been written for this node. The constructibility half of the route below is mostly
unblocked by existing results. The stratification half rests on results that have no node in the
roadmap and are not in the project or in Mathlib.

## Available ingredients

- **Semicontinuity of fibre rank for coherent sheaves.** This is available, with no Noetherian
  hypothesis: `Hartshorne.exists_nhds_moduleFiberDim_le_of_isCoherent` and
  `Hartshorne.isClosed_setOf_le_moduleFiberDim_of_isCoherent`
  (Cohomology/Semicontinuity/CoherentFiberDimension.lean). Coherence of the differentials is
  `Hartshorne.IsCoherent (Scheme.relativeDifferentials f)`, from
  `Scheme.relativeDifferentials_isCoherent_of_locallyOfFiniteType`
  (Scheme/Differentials/RelativeDifferentialsQuasicoherent.lean).
- **Constructibility on schemes.** Mathlib has scheme-level Chevalley:
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isLocallyConstructible_image`,
  `Scheme.Hom.isConstructible_image` and `Scheme.Hom.isConstructible_preimage`
  (Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FinitePresentation.lean).
- **The transitivity sequence** `f^*Ω_{Y/k} → Ω_{X/k} → Ω_{X/Y} → 0`:
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials_transitivity_exact`.
- **The local cotangent space via differentials** at the ring level:
  `Hartshorne.localCotangentToDifferentials_bijective`.

## Missing prerequisites

- Generic freeness / flattening stratification for the coherent cotangent presentations.
- A Noetherian-induction stratification into finitely many locally closed strata.
- The reduced induced subscheme structure on locally closed subsets, and the comparison of tangent
  maps along such strata.
- Dense nonsingular strata on these reduced locally closed subschemes. The dense nonsingular locus
  node covers integral schemes over a perfect field.

## Suggested route for the constructibility half

For a closed point `x` over an algebraically closed field, `m_x/m_x² ≅ Ω_{X/k} ⊗ k(x)`, and the
cotangent map `m_y/m_y² ⊗ k(x) → m_x/m_x²` is the fibre at `x` of `f^*Ω_{Y/k} → Ω_{X/k}`, whose
cokernel is `Ω_{X/Y}`. By right exactness of the fibre,
`rank(Tf_x) = moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/k} x − moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/Y} x`. Both terms are upper
semicontinuous, so `{rank ≤ r}` is a finite union of the locally closed sets
`{moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/k} = i} ∩ {moduleFiberDim Ω_{X/Y} = j}` with `i − j ≤ r`.

What still has to be written for this half:
- The sheaf-level bridge `(Ω_{X/k})_x ⊗ k(x) ≅ m_x/m_x²` at closed points. The ring-level
  statement exists, but the stalk of `Scheme.relativeDifferentials` has no general API; it is
  computed through charts in individual files.
- The compatibility of that bridge with the Zariski tangent map and with the fibre of the
  transitivity sequence.

The stratification half could be split into its own node so that the constructibility statement
can land first.
