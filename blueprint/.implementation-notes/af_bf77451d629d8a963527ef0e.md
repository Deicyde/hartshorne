# Constructible tangent-rank strata

## Status (42981-w29, frontier/w29b): not started in Lean; Roadmap return

No Lean has been written for this node. Its proof rests on results that have no node in the
roadmap and are not in the project or in Mathlib, so it is returned to Roadmap rather than
expanded during Formalize.

## Missing prerequisites

- **Semicontinuity of fibre rank for coherent sheaves.** On a locally Noetherian scheme,
  `x ↦ dim_{k(x)} F ⊗ k(x)` is upper semicontinuous for coherent `F`, so its level sets are
  locally closed. The coherent free-locus node covers only the free-stalk criterion, not the
  fibre-rank function.
- **Constructibility on schemes.** Finite unions of locally closed sets, Noetherian induction,
  and Chevalley's theorem for the images of strata under finite-type morphisms. Mathlib has
  `IsConstructible` on topological spaces and Chevalley for prime spectra, but there is no
  scheme-level bridge in the project or the DAG.
- **Generic freeness / flattening stratification for coherent sheaves.** This is listed under
  "Proof depends on" as plain text, with no linked node.
- **Nonsingular dense strata on reduced locally closed subschemes.** The dense nonsingular locus
  node covers integral schemes over a perfect field. The strata here need the reduced
  induced structure on locally closed subsets and the comparison of tangent maps along them.

## Suggested route once the prerequisites exist

For a closed point `x` over an algebraically closed field, `m_x/m_x² ≅ Ω_{X/k} ⊗ k(x)`. This is
the local cotangent via differentials node, `Hartshorne.localCotangentToDifferentials_bijective`.
The cotangent map `m_y/m_y² ⊗ k(x) → m_x/m_x²` is then the fibre at `x` of
`f^*Ω_{Y/k} → Ω_{X/k}`, whose cokernel is `Ω_{X/Y}`. By right exactness,
`rank(Tf_x) = dim Ω_{X/k} ⊗ k(x) − dim Ω_{X/Y} ⊗ k(x)`. Both terms are upper semicontinuous, so
`{rank ≤ r}` is a finite union of the locally closed sets `{dim Ω_{X/k} ⊗ k(x) = i} ∩
{dim Ω_{X/Y} ⊗ k(x) = j}` with `i − j ≤ r`, which gives constructibility. The stratification part
is a separate Noetherian-induction argument. It could be split into its own node so that the
constructibility statement can land first.
