## Route

Charts `D₊(a t)` of the Rees `Proj` (Stacks 0806 style), not the book's/roadmap's embedding into
`ℙⁿ_U` via local generators and Theorem 7.1 (`existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`). On
a chart the ring map `A[It]_(a t) → Γ(V, 𝒪_Z)` over `f^♯` is unique when `f^♯ a` is a
non-zero-divisor (`aⁿ · y/(a t)ⁿ = y/tⁿ`) and exists when `f^♯ a` also divides `f^♯(I)`. Two lifts
are compared on a basic open `V` inside a common chart (`le_preimage_chartι_image`: `(a t)/(b t)`
pulls back to a unit), using `ι_comp_eq_of_appLE_eq` (maps into an affine open agree if their
`appLE` agree). Local lifts are glued with `glueOpens`; compatibility on overlaps is the
uniqueness theorem applied to the open subscheme, whose inverse image ideal is the pull-back
(`comap_comp` and the open-immersion lemma). `Scheme.hom_ext_of_forall` gives global equality.

Exercise 7.1: local generators from `isLocalFrame_of_over_iso` (frames indexed by `ULift (Fin 1)`),
local surjectivity of an epimorphism (a private ringed-space copy of `modules_exists_local_lift`),
then `modules_isIso_of_locally_bijective`.
