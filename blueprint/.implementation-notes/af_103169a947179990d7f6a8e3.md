# Products of nonsingular varieties: implementation note

Lean: `Hartshorne.product_nonsingular_variety` in
`src/Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/ProductOfNonsingularVarieties.lean`.

Statement shape: `k` algebraically closed, `pX : X ⟶ Spec k`, `pY : Y ⟶ Spec k` with `X`, `Y`
integral, `FiniteType`, `IsSeparated`, and `Scheme.IsRegular`. Conclusion: the pullback
`X ×_k Y` is integral, `pullback.fst ≫ pX` is separated and of finite type, the pullback is
regular, and `topologicalKrullDim (X ×_k Y) = topologicalKrullDim X + topologicalKrullDim Y`
(sum in `WithBot ℕ∞`, no finiteness hypothesis needed).

Helpers (namespace `Hartshorne.ProductNonsingular`, unrecorded):

- `isLocallyFreeOfRank_pullback`: pullback preserves local freeness of rank `r`, via the
  ringed-space local frames (`Ringed.exists_isLocalFrame_of_isLocallyFreeOfRank`,
  `Ringed.IsLocalFrame.pullback`, `Ringed.isLocallyFreeOfRank_of_isLocalFrame`), the same
  route as `Hartshorne.isInvertible_pullback` with rank `r` in place of `1`.
- `restrictFreeOfLE`, `isLocallyFreeOfRank_biprod`: ranks add under `⊞`, using Mathlib's
  `restrictFunctorComp`, `PreservesColimitPair.iso`, `SheafOfModules.freeSumIso`.
- `exists_chart`: the open immersion `Spec (A ⊗_k B) ⟶ X ×_k Y` for nonempty affine opens,
  copied from the chart inside `Hartshorne.ExternalProduct.exists_chart` (that lemma does not
  expose the chart itself). Candidate for raising into a shared lemma.
- `isReduced_pullback` (stalks are stalks of `Spec` of a domain), `irreducibleSpace_pullback`
  (`ExternalProduct.exists_closure_eq_preimage_inter` at the two generic points),
  `isIntegral_pullback`, `finiteType_pullback`, `topologicalKrullDim_pullback` (one chart plus
  `DimensionCodimension.exists_ringKrullDim_eq` on the product and on each factor),
  `isRegular_pullback` (Theorem II.8.15 both ways and `relativeDifferentialsProductIso`).

The domain property of `A ⊗_k B` comes from the unrecorded
`Hartshorne.ExternalProduct.isDomain_tensorProduct`, which goes through the Chapter I
coordinate-ring results; hence the links to the tensor-product-dimension,
affine-product-coordinate-ring and coordinate-ring-realization articles.

The Chapter I dependency "Products of varieties" is not used by the Lean proof: that article
is about quasi-projective products via the Segre embedding, not scheme fibre products.
