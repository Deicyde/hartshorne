# proj-eventual-section-recovery (Hartshorne II Ex. 5.9(a,b))

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjEventualSectionRecovery`, namespace
`Hartshorne.EventualSectionRecovery`.

## (a) The map

- `constSection ℳ n hm`: the global section `x ↦ m / 1` of `M(n)~` for `m ∈ M_n`.
- `alphaComponent ℳ hgen n : ℳ n →+ Γ(X, M~ ⊗ 𝒪(n))`: image of `constSection` under
  `(ProjTwist.twistIso ℳ hgen n).inv` (Prop. 5.12(b)); needs `S` generated by `S_1`.
- `alpha ℳ hgen : M →ₗ[A] GammaStar (projGradedModuleSheaf 𝒜 ℳ)` (with
  `gammaStarModule` as local instance), `alpha_of_mem` (graded), `alpha_mem_gammaStarGrading`.
  S-linearity reduces to `mulMap_twistIso_hom`: under the twist isomorphism, `mulMap` by `s / 1`
  is the graded map "multiply by `s`" `M(n)~ → M(n+e)~` (`smulTwistMap`).
- `gammaStarMap φ` (summand-wise `φ ▷ 𝒪(n)` on global sections) and naturality
  `gammaStarMap_alpha` from `twistIso_hom_naturality`.

## (b) Eventual bijectivity

`exists_bijective_alphaComponent`: `S_0` finite type over a field `k`, `S` generated over `S_0` by
a finset of degree-one elements (the project's form of "generated by `S_1`, `S_1` finite over
`S_0`", as in 5.15/5.19), `M` finite with a decomposition.

Route (the book's hint: "use the methods of the proof of (5.19)"):

- `constHom ℳ n : ℳ n →+ Γ(M(n)~)`, `bijective_alphaComponent_iff`, `EventuallyBijective`.
- `eventuallyBijective_of_exact` (short exact sequences, using the 5.19 left exactness lemma
  `ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite.exists_sections_of_gradedLocMap_eq_zero` for the twisted
  modules), `eventuallyBijective_of_bijective`.
- `eventuallyBijective_of_primeFactors`: graded prime filtration (`exists_gradedPrimeFiltration`
  via `intGrading` / `intModuleGrading`, as in 5.19).
- Factor `(S/𝔭)(l)`: `constHom0`/`constSection0` (degree-zero sections), `bijective_constHom_iff`,
  `bijective_constHom0_congr` (regrading via `regradeIso`), `exists_bijective_constHom0_primeFactor`,
  using the new uniform bound `exists_bound_of_chartCond` (finitely generated span of all
  chart-condition elements inside the finite integral closure; homogeneous comparison with
  `eq_zero_of_sum_isHomFrac_eq_zero`).

Copied private helpers (private in `ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`, copied verbatim and kept
private): `prod_pow_mem`, `exists_mem_mul_monomial`, `exists_mem_mul_of_le`, `chartCond_choose`,
`isIntegral_of_chartCond`, `isHomFrac_zero/add/smul/of_chartCond`, and the whole `PrimeFactor`
section (`toFracQ*`, `primeIdeal`, `primePoint`, `globalValue_val_eq_zero`, `primeValue*`,
`eq_zero_of_primeValue_eq_zero`, `exists_primeValue_chart`, `chartCond_primeValue`), plus
`decompose_map`. Candidates for raising to public in the 5.19 module.
