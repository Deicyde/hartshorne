# Constant fibre dimension forces local freeness

## Restatement for reduced X (faithfulness item 17)

The article was restated from integral to reduced Noetherian `X` (Hartshorne II Ex. 5.8(c)
assumes only that `X` is reduced). The existing Lean below covers only the integral case, so it
no longer states the article. To record, a Formalize pass must prove the reduced version:

- Local algebra: over a reduced local ring `R`, a finite module `M` whose localization at each
  minimal prime `P` has `finrank R_P (R_P ⊗ M) = dim_k (k ⊗ M)` is free of that rank (Nakayama
  surjection `R^n → M`; its kernel dies at every minimal prime since `R_P` is a field,
  `Ring.KrullDimLE.of_isLocalization` + `Ring.KrullDimLE.isField_of_isReduced`; the nilradical
  `sInf (minimalPrimes R)` is zero). No Noetherian hypothesis is needed here.
- Scheme side: replace `[IsIntegral X]` by `[IsReduced X]` in
  `free_stalk_of_finrank_fibre` and `isLocallyFreeOfRank_iff_forall_finrank_fibre`
  (in-place generalization; callers with `[IsIntegral X]` still elaborate since integral
  schemes are reduced, so the integral case stays available, e.g. for
  `relative-differentials-rank-of-smooth`). On an affine open `Spec A` the minimal primes of
  the stalk at `y` are localizations of primes `q ≤ y` of `A`, and the fibre hypothesis at the
  point `q` gives the local-algebra hypothesis.

## Candidate declarations (already in the library, not yet recorded)

Added by the roadmap revision that split this criterion out of
`relative-differentials-rank-of-smooth` (reviewer r58). The Lean exists, compiles and is
imported by the library root; a Formalize pass should independently check it states this
article before recording `lean:` / `statement:` / `proof:`.

- `Hartshorne.isLocallyFreeOfRank_iff_forall_finrank_fibre`
  (`Hartshorne.Scheme.CoherentFibreRank`): currently for `X` with `[IsIntegral X]` (to be
  generalized as above) and `[IsLocallyNoetherian X]`, coherent `F` and `n : ℕ`,
  `F.IsLocallyFreeOfRank n ↔ ∀ x, finrank κ(x) (κ(x) ⊗[𝒪_{X,x}] F_x) = n`.
  Assuming only locally Noetherian makes this stronger than the article's Noetherian
  statement; it is exactly the weakening the article's last paragraph allows. This should be the first `lean:` name.
- Supporting public declarations in the same module that may also be listed:
  `Hartshorne.finrank_fibre_of_isLocallyFreeOfRank` (forward direction, any scheme),
  `Hartshorne.free_stalk_of_finrank_fibre` (constant fibre dimension gives free stalks of
  rank `n` on an integral locally Noetherian scheme).
- Local algebra input (the plain-text `Proof depends on` bullet):
  `Hartshorne.free_of_finrank_residueField_eq_finrank_fractionRing`
  (`Hartshorne.Scheme.FibreDimensionFree`). No article of its own; Mathlib has no
  equivalent at the pinned revision (`RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/FreeLocus.lean` and
  `RingTheory/Flat/LocallyFree.lean` need flatness or finite presentation plus constant
  `rankAtStalk`, not a fibre comparison over a local domain).

## Free-locus dependency (plan for `coherent-free-locus`)

The proof of `isLocallyFreeOfRank_iff_forall_finrank_fibre` uses
`Hartshorne.isLocallyFree_iff_forall_free_stalk_of_isLocallyNoetherian` and the
`Hartshorne.FreeLocusAux` helpers (`free_stalk_restrict_iff` and friends) from
`Hartshorne.Scheme.CoherentFreeLocus`, not the two names `coherent-free-locus` records
(`Hartshorne.exists_isFree_restrict_of_free_stalk`,
`Hartshorne.isLocallyFree_iff_forall_free_stalk`), which assume `[IsNoetherian X]`.

The roadmap revision deliberately did not change the statement of `coherent-free-locus`
(Hartshorne II Ex. 5.7(a,b) is stated for Noetherian `X`, and changing it would retract a
proved article). Instead, when this article is recorded, Formalize should also append to
`coherent-free-locus`'s `lean:` list (after the existing two, so its progress credit is
unchanged) the locally Noetherian variants
`Hartshorne.exists_isFree_restrict_of_free_stalk_of_isLocallyNoetherian` and
`Hartshorne.isLocallyFree_iff_forall_free_stalk_of_isLocallyNoetherian`. They are stronger
than the article's statement (they imply the recorded Noetherian forms, which are proved
from them), so recording them needs no statement change or retraction. This plan lives here
because `coherent-free-locus` is proved, so a note on it would be flagged stale.
