# The differential criterion for smoothness over a field

## Candidate declarations (existing, checked, sorry-free; not yet recorded)

Created by the split of hartshorne-smoothness-criterion. Module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.DifferentialCriterionField`:

- `Hartshorne.isSmoothAt_of_finrank_le`: `T` of finite type over a field `K`, `q` prime,
  `finrank κ(q) (κ(q) ⊗_T Ω_{T/K}) ≤ n ≤ dim T_q + dim T ⧸ q` give `Algebra.IsSmoothAt K q`
  (Mathlib's `IsSmoothAt` is formal smoothness of `Localization.AtPrime q`).
- `Hartshorne.smooth_of_forall_finrank_le`: the same at every prime gives `Algebra.Smooth K T`
  (via `Algebra.smoothLocus_eq_univ_iff`).
- Supporting: `Hartshorne.exists_linearIndependent_D_ker`,
  `Hartshorne.exists_surjective_fibre_kaehler_quotient`,
  `Hartshorne.linearIndependent_one_tmul_of_ringHom`,
  `Hartshorne.exists_localization_tensor_kaehler_toTensor`,
  `Hartshorne.injective_of_span_eq_top_of_linearIndependent`,
  `Hartshorne.eq_bot_of_ringKrullDim_le_quotient`, `Hartshorne.injective_of_ringKrullDim_le`.

## Lean uses behind the proof edges

- Regular quotient theorem: `Hartshorne.isRegularLocalRing_localization_quotient_of_linearIndependent_D`,
  `Hartshorne.ringKrullDim_localization_quotient_add_card_of_linearIndependent_D`.
- Dimension formula: `Hartshorne.height_add_ringKrullDim_quotient_eq`.
- Regular local rings are domains: `Hartshorne.RegularLocal.isDomain`.
- Jacobian criterion: Mathlib's `Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_cotangentComplexBaseChange`
  (the case `K = κ(S)` is `..._residueField`), recorded on the Mathlib article
  local-jacobian-criterion-formally-smooth.
