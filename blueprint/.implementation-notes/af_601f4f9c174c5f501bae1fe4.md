# Cohomological dimension from an affine cover

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.AffineCoverCohomologicalDimension`.

- `Hartshorne.isCohomologicalDimensionBound_of_affine_cover`: the bound form,
  `IsCohomologicalDimensionBound X r`.
- `Hartshorne.cohomologicalDimension_le_of_affine_cover`: `cohomologicalDimension X ≤ r`.

Both take `[IsNoetherian X] [X.IsSeparated]`, a cover `U : ι → X.Opens` with
`IsOpenCover U`, `∀ i, IsAffineOpen (U i)`, and `Nat.card ι = r + 1`; `ι` lives in an
arbitrary universe and finiteness follows from the cardinality hypothesis.

## Route

As in the book: Theorem 4.5 (`Hartshorne.cechCohomologyIsoOfIsQuasicoherent`) identifies
`H^i(X, F)` with ordered Čech cohomology. Its index type must be in the scheme's universe and
linearly ordered, so the proof reindexes the cover by `ULift (Fin (r + 1))` through
`Finite.equivFinOfCardEq`. The ordered Čech complex has no strictly increasing `(i + 1)`-tuples
from a set of at most `i` elements (`Hartshorne.OrderedCechIndex.isEmpty_of_card_le`), so its
degree-`i` cochains are zero and so is its cohomology
(`Hartshorne.orderedCechCohomology_isZero_of_card_le`). These two helpers are the unlinked
"no terms above degree r" proof dependency of the article.
