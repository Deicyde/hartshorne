# Twisting sheaves under localization in degree zero

## Status

Not recorded: the article was split off the relative twisting sheaf
(`af_de36af0b3bcf4daf571b0c25`) by a roadmap revision, and new articles get no `lean:`
until a Formalize pass reviews the declarations below against the statement.

## Candidate declarations

In `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjTwistLocalization` (namespace `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap`),
sorry-free and imported by the library root:

- `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap.twistRestrictHom`: for a graded map `φ : A → B` with
  `B₊ ≤ φ(A₊)B` whose `Proj.map φ` is an open immersion, the morphism
  `(projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 n).restrict (Proj.map φ hf) ⟶ projTwistingSheaf ℬ n`
  (pointwise formula `twistRestrictHom_app_apply`, rfl).
- `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap.isIso_twistRestrictHom`,
  `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap.twistRestrictIso`: for `[IsLocalization.Away s B]`,
  `s ∈ A₀`, `φ = algebraMap`, the isomorphism of the article.
- `Hartshorne.ProjTwistMap.twistFiber_comp` (with `twistFiber_id`): functoriality of
  the fiber maps `m/t ↦ φm/φt`, stated with a pointwise hypothesis `φ'' = φ' ∘ φ`.
  This is the fiber-level form of the compatibility with composites. The sheaf-level
  identity and cocycle conditions are proved only in the relative Proj setting
  (`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.twistTransitionHom_id`,
  `twistTransitionHom_comp` in `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProjTwist`). Those are stated for
  `S.restrict f` along arrows of `X.AffineZariskiSite`, not for an arbitrary chain
  `A → B = A[1/s] → C = B[1/t]`, so they do not state the article's compatibility clause.
  A Formalize pass must add a sheaf-level `twistRestrictHom_comp` / `twistRestrictHom_id`
  for degree-zero localizations; the proof of `twistTransitionHom_comp` carries over,
  using `twistFiber_comp`, `twistFiber_id` and Mathlib's `Proj.map_comp`, `Proj.map_id`.
- Supporting: `twistFiber`, `twistFiber_mk`, `twistFiber_smul`, `twistHom`,
  `twistFiber_injective`, `twistFiber_surjective`, `fiberEquiv`.

That the localization's `Proj.map` is an open immersion onto `D₊(s)` is
`Hartshorne.isOpenImmersion_projMap` with `Hartshorne.range_projMap`, and `B₊ ≤ A₊B` is
`Hartshorne.irrelevant_le_map_of_isLocalizationAway`, all in `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeProj`,
which is why the article has Relative Proj under "Proof depends on".

## Relation to Pullback of twisting sheaves

`af_0a85053c2012b17661490eb8` (II.5.12(c), `f^* 𝒪(n) ≅ 𝒪(n)|_U`) assumes both rings
are generated in degree one; for `B = A[1/s]` this gives such an isomorphism.
This article stays separate because it needs no generation hypothesis and records the
compatibility with composites used for gluing. `twistHom`
(`𝒪_{Proj A}(n) ⟶ f_* 𝒪_{Proj B}(n)` for any graded map) is an ingredient for that
article; alternatively this article can become its "Proof depends on".
