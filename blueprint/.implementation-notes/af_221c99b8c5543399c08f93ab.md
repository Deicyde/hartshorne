# The degree of cycles on projective space is invariant under rational equivalence

## Status

New article from the projective-cycle-rational-equivalent-linear-space split (roadmap
proposal). Lean for the degree map exists (below), but nothing is recorded in the frontmatter:
Formalize should record declarations only after independent review.

Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, §1.4 and §2.5 (degree of cycles on projective space);
the exact locator was not confirmed offline. The proof sketch is the elementary one through
Bézout (Hartshorne I.7.7) and the length formula (new article normalization-order-length), not
Fulton's Chern-class route.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree`, `Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree_pointCodimensionCycle`
  (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.ProjectiveLinearSubspaceCycle`): the article's `deg`, defined
  through `Hartshorne.projectiveSpectrumPointDegree` (module
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveWeilDivisorDegree`, the projective degree of the classical set
  cut out by the point's homogeneous prime). Candidates to record here once reviewed.
- `Hartshorne.projective_hypersurface_bezout` (module
  `Hartshorne.Intersection.ProjectiveHypersurfaceBezout`): needs `f` irreducible and the
  variety of positive dimension, and works with classical sets; for reducible `F` sum over the
  irreducible factors. `Hartshorne.projectiveIntersectionMultiplicity` is the graded
  multiplicity `μ_𝔭(S/(I(V)+I(H)))`.
- `Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_eq_closure_principalDivisor` for the generators.
- Special cases already covered: `m = 0` by complete-variety-zero-cycle-degree (planned), and
  codimension one by `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceWeilDivisorDegree_principalDivisor`
  (projective-space-class-group). A lemma relating `cycleProjectiveDegree` in codimension one to
  `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceWeilDivisorDegree` would discharge the codimension-one case.
- The `m = n` case is trivial (`Rat^0 = 0`).

## Candidate statement

`Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree_eq_zero_of_mem_rationalEquivalenceSubgroup`: for
`c ∈ rationalEquivalenceSubgroup projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism
finiteType_projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism topologicalKrullDim_projectiveSpace (n - m)`,
`cycleProjectiveDegree (n - m) c = 0`; plus the descended map on `Hartshorne.chowGroup`. The
first is exactly the first hypothesis of
`Hartshorne.rationallyEquivalent_degree_smul_linearSubspace_of_exists`.
