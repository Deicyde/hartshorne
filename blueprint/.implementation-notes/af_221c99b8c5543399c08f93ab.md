# The degree of cycles on projective space is invariant under rational equivalence

## Status

New article from the projective-cycle-rational-equivalent-linear-space split (roadmap
proposal). Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, §1.4 and §2.5 (degree of cycles on projective
space); the exact locator was not confirmed offline. The proof sketch is the elementary one through
Bézout (Hartshorne I.7.7) and the length formula (normalization-order-length), not Fulton's
Chern-class route.

The statement and proof are formalized sorry-free (third pass, 2026-11-12); the `lean:` frontmatter
is a candidate pending independent review.

## Main declarations

Module `Hartshorne.Intersection.ProjectiveCycleDegreeInvariance`:

- `Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree_eq_zero_of_mem_rationalEquivalenceSubgroup`: for `k`
  algebraically closed and every `r`, the degree `Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree r` (module
  `Hartshorne.Intersection.ProjectiveLinearSubspaceCycle`) vanishes on
  `rationalEquivalenceSubgroup projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism ... r`. Stated for every `r`, not
  only `r = n - m`; it is the first hypothesis of
  `Hartshorne.rationallyEquivalent_degree_smul_linearSubspace_of_exists`.
- `Hartshorne.chowGroupProjectiveDegree` (`QuotientAddGroup.lift`) with
  `Hartshorne.chowGroupProjectiveDegree_mk`: the descended map `A^r(ℙⁿ_k) → ℤ`.
- `Hartshorne.finsum_ord_mul_projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_eq_zero`: `∑_Y ord_Y(g) deg Y = 0` for
  a nonzero rational function `g` on an integral closed subvariety `V ⊆ ℙⁿ_k` (orders are Mathlib's
  length-based `Scheme.ord`, so `V` need not be normal).

## Proof structure

### Supporting modules (second pass)

Modules under `src/Hartshorne/Intersection/`, all imported by the library root:

- `GradedLocalizationLength`: for an `ℕ`-graded domain `A`, homogeneous prime `𝔮`, `f ∈ A₁ ∖ 𝔮`:
  `flat_atPrime` (`A_𝔮` flat over `A₍𝔮₎`, as a localization of `A₍𝔮₎[X]`, `X ↦ f`),
  `map_maximalIdeal_atPrime`, and `length_quotient_map_atPrime`
  (`ℓ_{A_𝔮}(A_𝔮 / I A_𝔮) = ℓ_{A₍𝔮₎}(A₍𝔮₎ / I)`). This is the "degree-zero versus homogeneous
  localization" comparison of the sketch.
- `ProjectiveSubvarietyOrder`: for a closed immersion `i : V ⟶ Proj A` of a (not necessarily
  normal) locally Noetherian integral scheme, `functionFieldQuot i hF hG` is `F / G` in `K(V)`
  (multiplicativity: `functionFieldQuot_mul`, `functionFieldQuot_eq_of_mul_eq`);
  `stalkMap_stalkQuot_eq_zero_iff` (kernel of `𝒪_{Proj, y} → 𝒪_{V, y}`); and the main result
  `ord_functionFieldQuot_pow`: for `y` of codimension one, `L ∈ A₁ ∖ 𝔮_y`, `F ∉ 𝔭` of degree `e`,
  `V.ord (F / Lᵉ) y = (gradedMultiplicity A (A ⧸ (𝔭 ⊔ span {F})) 𝔮_y).toNat`. Uses Mathlib's
  length-based `Scheme.ord`, so no normality is needed (compare the normal-case version
  `Hartshorne.projectiveIntersectionDivisor_apply_eq_length` in
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveLocalIntersectionMultiplicity`, which assumes `(*)`).
- `NormalizationLocalOrder`: `length_quotient_eq_finsum_primesOver` (for a Noetherian domain `D`
  with `O = integralClosure D (FractionRing D)` finite and `dim D_Q = 1`:
  `ℓ_{D_Q}(D_Q/a) = ∑ᶠ_{𝔔 ∩ D = Q} ord_{O_𝔔}(a) · 𝔔.inertiaDeg D`), derived from
  `NormalizationOrderLength.length_quotient_eq_finsum_ord_mul_inertiaDeg` via
  `IsLocalization.integralClosure`; `ringKrullDim_localization_of_comap_eq` (`dim O_𝔔 = 1`);
  helpers `inertiaDeg_eq_one_of_isLocalization`, `inertiaDeg_eq_of_isLocalization`,
  `ring_ord_localization_atPrime_eq`.
- `NormalizationPushforwardOrder`: the predicate `FibreOrdFormula ν hν y`
  (`∑ᶠ z ∈ ν⁻¹{y}, ord_z(ν^♯ g)·[κ(z):κ(y)] = ord_y(g)` for all `g ≠ 0`) and its transport
  `FibreOrdFormula.of_isOpenImmersion`; Spec facts `Spec.ord_algebraMap`,
  `Spec.stalkIso_hom_stalkMap`, `Spec.stalkMap_algebraMap`, `Spec.residueDegree_map_eq_inertiaDeg`;
  `Scheme.ord_div`, `Scheme.Hom.apply_genericPoint_of_isDominant`.

Route followed: the article's (length formula through the normalization on `𝒪_{V,Y}`, then graded
multiplicity, then Bézout), with `deg ν_* div g` computed as `∑_y ord_y(g) deg Y` on `V` itself.

### Supporting modules (third pass)

- `NormalizationFibreOrder`: `fibreOrdFormula_specMap_integralClosure` (goal 1),
  `fibreOrdFormula_absoluteNormalizationMap` (goal 2, for `V` integral of finite type over a field),
  `genericFunctionFieldMap_absoluteNormalizationMap_bijective` (`K(V) ≅ K(Ṽ)`), all through the
  private affine chart lemma `exists_chart_absoluteNormalization`. Instance pitfall fixed by a
  private `abbrev closureRing D := CommRingCat.of (integralClosure D (FractionRing D))` with private
  instances for `IsDomain`, `IsNoetherianRing`, the function-field and stalk algebras, the scalar
  tower, `IsFractionRing` and `x.asIdeal.IsPrime` (instances keyed on `↑R` are not found otherwise).
- `NormalizationPushforwardDivisor`: `coheight_apply_eq_of_isFinite` (finite morphisms of integral
  finite-type schemes preserve codimension) and
  `subvarietyNormalizationPushforward_principalDivisor_apply` (goal 3): the coefficient of
  `ν_* div(ν^♯ g)` at `x` is `∑ᶠ y ∈ i⁻¹{x}, V.ord g y`.

### Assembly (third pass), module `ProjectiveCycleDegreeInvariance`

Private helpers: `hypersurfaceMult i F y` is `μ_y(F)`, the graded multiplicity of `S / (𝔭 + F)` at
the prime of `y`; `ord_functionFieldQuot_eq_sub` (`ord_y(F/G) = μ_y(F) - μ_y(G)`, choosing a
coordinate `X_j ∉ 𝔮_y` via `exists_X_notMem_projectiveSpace`), `hypersurfaceMult_mul`,
`exists_eq_functionFieldQuot` (every rational function is `F / G`),
`finsum_hypersurfaceMult_irreducible` (Bézout for an irreducible form: the component
correspondence of `ProjectiveIntersectionDegreeFormula` rebuilt for an integral `V` without `(*)`,
with `projective_hypersurface_bezout` and `hypersurface_hilbertPolynomial_and_degree`), and
`finsum_hypersurfaceMult` (all forms, by `UniqueFactorizationMonoid.induction_on_prime`). The
generator case uses `genericFunctionFieldMap_absoluteNormalizationMap_bijective` to write the
rational function on `Ṽ` as `ν^♯ g`, then
`subvarietyNormalizationPushforward_principalDivisor_apply`. The cases where `V` has no
codimension-one point (then all orders vanish) and `n ≥ 1` (from `height_projectiveSpectrum_le`)
are handled inside `finsum_ord_mul_projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_eq_zero`.

## Divergence from the sketch

None of substance: orders are taken on `V` through the fibre order formula (the length formula
through the normalization, applied on affine charts) rather than written out on `𝒪_{V,Y}`
directly; the degree-zero versus homogeneous localization comparison is
`GradedLocalizationLength.length_quotient_map_atPrime`.
