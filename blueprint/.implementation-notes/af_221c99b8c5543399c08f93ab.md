# The degree of cycles on projective space is invariant under rational equivalence

## Status

New article from the projective-cycle-rational-equivalent-linear-space split (roadmap
proposal). Lean for the degree map exists (below), but nothing is recorded in the frontmatter:
Formalize should record declarations only after independent review.

Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, §1.4 and §2.5 (degree of cycles on projective space);
the exact locator was not confirmed offline. The proof sketch is the elementary one through
Bézout (Hartshorne I.7.7) and the length formula (new article normalization-order-length), not
Fulton's Chern-class route.

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23b): not closable without the two bridges
under "Lean gaps" below. A second attempt (2026-11-12) built most of gap (1) as reusable modules
(next section); nothing is recorded and the candidate statement is not yet proved.

## Candidate statement

`Hartshorne.cycleProjectiveDegree_eq_zero_of_mem_rationalEquivalenceSubgroup`: for
`c ∈ rationalEquivalenceSubgroup projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism
finiteType_projectiveSpaceStructureMorphism topologicalKrullDim_projectiveSpace (n - m)`,
`cycleProjectiveDegree (n - m) c = 0`; plus the descended map on `Hartshorne.chowGroup`. The
first is exactly the first hypothesis of
`Hartshorne.rationallyEquivalent_degree_smul_linearSubspace_of_exists`.

## Lean gaps

- Comparing the coefficient of `ν_* div(F/G)` at `Y` (orders on the normalization, pushed with
  residue degrees) with `i(V, Z(F); Y) - i(V, Z(G); Y)`: the length formula, plus the
  comparison of the length of `𝒪_{V,Y}/(F/L^e)` with the graded multiplicity at the homogeneous
  prime of `Y` (degree-zero versus homogeneous localization of `S(V)`).
- The scheme-to-classical bridge for subvarieties of `ℙⁿ_k` of arbitrary dimension (the
  existing bridge `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree` covers degrees only).

## Partial progress (2026-11-12): proved, sorry-free, not recorded

Modules under `src/Hartshorne/Intersection/`, all imported by the library root:

- `GradedLocalizationLength`: for an `ℕ`-graded domain `A`, homogeneous prime `𝔮`, `f ∈ A₁ ∖ 𝔮`:
  `flat_atPrime` (`A_𝔮` flat over `A₍𝔮₎`, as a localization of `A₍𝔮₎[X]`, `X ↦ f`),
  `map_maximalIdeal_atPrime`, and `length_quotient_map_atPrime`
  (`ℓ_{A_𝔮}(A_𝔮 / I A_𝔮) = ℓ_{A₍𝔮₎}(A₍𝔮₎ / I)`). This is the "degree-zero versus homogeneous
  localization" comparison of the sketch.
- `ProjectiveSubvarietyOrder`: for a closed immersion `i : V ⟶ Proj A` of a (not necessarily
  normal) locally Noetherian integral scheme, `functionFieldQuot i hF hG` is `F / G` in `K(V)`
  (multiplicativity: `functionFieldQuot_mul`, `functionFieldQuot_eq_of_mul_eq`);
  `stalkMap_stalkQuot_eq_zero_iff` (kernel of `𝒪_{Proj, y} → 𝒪_{V, y}`); and the main result
  `ord_functionFieldQuot_pow`: for `y` of codimension one, `L ∈ A₁ ∖ 𝔮_y`, `F ∉ 𝔭` of degree `e`,
  `V.ord (F / Lᵉ) y = (gradedMultiplicity A (A ⧸ (𝔭 ⊔ span {F})) 𝔮_y).toNat`. Uses Mathlib's
  length-based `Scheme.ord`, so no normality is needed (compare the normal-case version
  `Hartshorne.projectiveIntersectionDivisor_apply_eq_length` in
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveLocalIntersectionMultiplicity`, which assumes `(*)`).
- `NormalizationLocalOrder`: `length_quotient_eq_finsum_primesOver` (for a Noetherian domain `D`
  with `O = integralClosure D (FractionRing D)` finite and `dim D_Q = 1`:
  `ℓ_{D_Q}(D_Q/a) = ∑ᶠ_{𝔔 ∩ D = Q} ord_{O_𝔔}(a) · 𝔔.inertiaDeg D`), derived from
  `NormalizationOrderLength.length_quotient_eq_finsum_ord_mul_inertiaDeg` via
  `IsLocalization.integralClosure`; `ringKrullDim_localization_of_comap_eq` (`dim O_𝔔 = 1`);
  helpers `inertiaDeg_eq_one_of_isLocalization`, `inertiaDeg_eq_of_isLocalization`,
  `ring_ord_localization_atPrime_eq`.
- `NormalizationPushforwardOrder`: the predicate `FibreOrdFormula ν hν y`
  (`∑ᶠ z ∈ ν⁻¹{y}, ord_z(ν^♯ g)·[κ(z):κ(y)] = ord_y(g)` for all `g ≠ 0`) and its transport
  `FibreOrdFormula.of_isOpenImmersion`; Spec facts `Spec.ord_algebraMap`,
  `Spec.stalkIso_hom_stalkMap`, `Spec.stalkMap_algebraMap`, `Spec.residueDegree_map_eq_inertiaDeg`;
  `Scheme.ord_div`, `Scheme.Hom.apply_genericPoint_of_isDominant`.

Route followed: the article's (length formula through the normalization on `𝒪_{V,Y}`, then graded
multiplicity, then Bézout), with `deg ν_* div g` computed as `∑_y ord_y(g) deg Y` on `V` itself.

## Exact remaining goals and route

1. `fibreOrdFormula_specMap_integralClosure`: `FibreOrdFormula (Spec.map (algebraMap D O)) _ Q`
   for `D : CommRingCat` Noetherian domain, `O` its integral closure (finite), `coheight Q = 1`.
   All inputs are proved. Write `g = a / b`, use `Scheme.ord_div` on both sides and
   `finsum_mem_sub_distrib` (fibre finite by `Scheme.Hom.finite_preimage_singleton`, `Spec.map`
   of a finite map being `IsFinite`); for `a ∈ D`: `ν^♯(a) = a` via
   `Scheme.Hom.genericFunctionFieldMap_algebraMap` + `Spec.stalkMap_algebraMap`, then
   `Spec.ord_algebraMap` on both sides (codimension one of fibre points from
   `ringKrullDim_localization_of_comap_eq` + `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight`),
   `Spec.residueDegree_map_eq_inertiaDeg`, and `length_quotient_eq_finsum_primesOver`.
   Pitfalls met: instances keyed on `↑R` for `R : CommRingCat` (function-field algebra,
   `FreeLocusAux.freeLocusStalkAlgebra`, `specStalk_functionField_isScalarTower`) are not found for
   `R := CommRingCat.of O` (the carrier reduces); supply them as local instances
   (`instAlgebraCarrierFunctionFieldSpec (CommRingCat.of O)` etc.). Pin `Spec.map (R := D)`. Avoid
   mixing `PrimeSpectrum O` and `Spec (.of O)` in one statement (index sums by `PrimeSpectrum O`
   and convert the sets once); a draft mixing them hit `whnf` timeouts.
2. `FibreOrdFormula (absoluteNormalizationMap V)` at every codimension-one `y` for `V` integral of
   finite type over a field: affine open `W ∋ y`, `exists_isOpenImmersion_absoluteNormalization`
   (range is `ν⁻¹ W`), `W ≅ Spec Γ(W, ⊤)`, `exists_isIso_absoluteNormalization` and
   `exists_isIso_absoluteNormalization_spec` with `O` the integral closure (finiteness of `O` over
   `Γ(W, ⊤)` as in `pointCodimensionCycle_mem_rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_of_monic`); transport
   twice with `FibreOrdFormula.of_isOpenImmersion`.
3. Coefficient formula for generators: the coefficient at `i y` of
   `subvarietyNormalizationPushforward … (div g̃)` is `V.ord g y` (`g̃ = ν^♯ g`, `ν^♯` bijective as
   `K(Ṽ) ≅ K(V)`), zero off `range i`: `rawCyclePushforwardCoeff` is the residue degree (no
   dimension drop for finite maps, as in `hcoeff` of the companion leaf), residue degrees of closed
   immersions are `1`.
4. Represent `g ∈ K(V)` as `functionFieldQuot i hF hG` (surjectivity of `i.stalkMap` at the generic
   point and `HomogeneousLocalization.mk_surjective`); factor `F, G` into irreducible forms
   (`MvPolynomial` is a UFD; factors of forms are forms); per `y` pick a variable `L = X_j ∉ 𝔮_y`
   and use `functionFieldQuot_eq_of_mul_eq`, `Scheme.ord_div`, `ord_functionFieldQuot_pow`.
5. Bézout sum for an irreducible form `F ∉ 𝔭` of degree `e ≥ 1`:
   `∑ᶠ_y μ_{𝔮_y}(S/(𝔭+F)) · projectiveSpectrumPointDegree (i y) = e · deg V`. The
   scheme-to-classical bridge already exists for normal `X` in
   `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveIntersectionDegreeFormula` (`closedImmersionComponentEquiv`,
   `projectiveIntersectionDivisor_apply_eq_projectiveIntersectionMultiplicity`,
   `weilDivisorProjectiveDegree_projectiveIntersectionDivisor`); adapt it to integral `V`
   (the component correspondence should not need `(*)`), replacing its DVR-based orders by
   `ord_functionFieldQuot_pow`. `dim V = m + 1 ≥ 1` gives Bézout's positive-dimension hypothesis.
6. Assemble `cycleProjectiveDegree_eq_zero_of_mem_rationalEquivalenceSubgroup` from
   `rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_eq_closure_principalDivisor` (`AddSubgroup.closure_induction`) and
   the descended map on `Hartshorne.chowGroup` (`QuotientAddGroup.lift`); with
   `exists_rationallyEquivalent_zsmul_linearSubspace` this discharges both hypotheses of
   `rationallyEquivalent_degree_smul_linearSubspace_of_exists`.
