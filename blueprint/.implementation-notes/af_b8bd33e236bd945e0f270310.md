# Exactness of scheme-theoretic completion

## Status

Complete, candidate pending review. Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.SchemeCompletionExact`.

## Lean

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletion_exact`: for `X` locally Noetherian,
  `I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` and an exact `S : ShortComplex X.Modules` with `S.X₁`
  quasi-coherent and `S.X₂`, `S.X₃` coherent (`IsCoherent`), the image of `S` under
  `I.moduleCompletionFunctor` is exact in the category of sheaves of modules over
  `I.formalCompletionRingCatSheaf` (the structure sheaf of the formal completion).
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletion_shortExact`: short exact sequences of
  coherent modules go to short exact sequences (from the exact form applied to `0 ⟶ F' ⟶ F` and
  `F ⟶ F'' ⟶ 0`, as in `moduleCompletion_tilde_shortExact`).
- Helper `Hartshorne.modules_app_exact_of_isAffineOpen`: over an affine open `U`, sections of an
  exact sequence of quasi-coherent modules are exact. Proof: factor `F' ↠ K ↪ F` through the
  cycles `K = ker(F ⟶ F'')` (quasi-coherent by `isQuasicoherent_kernel`); sections are left exact
  (`modules_sections_exact`); `Γ(U, F') ⟶ Γ(U, K)` is onto by `affine_globalSections_shortExact`
  applied to `0 ⟶ ker ⟶ F' ⟶ K ⟶ 0` restricted to `U` (`restrictFunctor U.ι`, exact).

## Route

The main theorem applies `moduleCompletionHom_exact_of_isBasis` (the affine-basis criterion in
`Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineFormalModuleExact`, the core of the Prop 9.4(c) proof) with the set of
all affine opens of `X`, whose traces on `|Y|` form a basis (`Scheme.isBasis_affineOpens`,
`opens_isBasis_map_image`). Over each affine `U`: `Γ(U, 𝒪_X)` is Noetherian
(`IsLocallyNoetherian.component_noetherian`), sections of coherent modules are finite
(`forall_affine_finite_of_isCoherent`), and sections are exact (helper above).

This checks exactness locally on affine opens of `X` directly on `X̂`, rather than first
identifying `F̂|_{Û}` with the affine completion `(M~)^` on `Spf Γ(U, 𝒪_X)` and quoting
`moduleCompletion_tilde_exact`. The latter would need a comparison of the formal completion of
an open subscheme with the open subspace of `X̂`, and of the completion of `F|_U` with `F̂|_{Û}`;
neither exists yet. The local computation used is the same one (exactness of adic completion of
finite modules on the sections over affine opens).

## Not formalized

The target of the Lean is all modules over the structure sheaf of `X̂`; that `F̂` is a coherent
formal module (in the sense of `IsCoherentFormalModule`) is not proved here. A category-level
statement (the restriction of `moduleCompletionFunctor` to `coherentModules X` preserves finite
limits and colimits) would also need additivity of `moduleCompletionFunctor`, not yet proved.
