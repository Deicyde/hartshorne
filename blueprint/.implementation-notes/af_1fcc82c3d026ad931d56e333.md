## Remaining goal

The general case `0 < m < n - 1`. The Lean has no theory linking projective degree (from the
Hilbert polynomial) to rational equivalence of higher-codimension cycles.

1. **Cyclicity.** Every integral `m`-dimensional `Y ⊆ ℙⁿ` is rationally equivalent to an
   integer multiple of `[Lₘ]`.
2. **Degree invariance.** For an integral `(m+1)`-dimensional `V ⊆ ℙⁿ` with normalization
   `ν : Ṽ → ℙⁿ` and a rational function `f` on `Ṽ`, the cycle `ν_* div(f)` has degree 0. So
   degree descends to `A_m(ℙⁿ)`.

Given 1 and 2, `[Y] ~ a·[Lₘ]` and `d = deg Y = a·deg Lₘ = a`. This step is already checked as
`Hartshorne.rationallyEquivalent_degree_smul_linearSubspace_of_exists`. So once 1 and 2 are
formalized, the leaf is one application of that theorem.

## Split proposal (Roadmap return; adopted by the roadmap revision above)

Kept for reference. Where it differs from the revision: the localization sequence became its
own article; the leaf's projection-family sketch was kept rather than replaced (articles keep
the source's route), with the new route recorded in this note; no edge was added to
projective-space-chow-ring.


The article's proof sketch, a projection family degenerating `Y` to `deg(π)·π(Y)`, needs three
things:

- proper pushforward of rational equivalence from `ℙ¹ × ℙⁿ` (article
  proper-pushforward-preserves-rational-equivalence, not ready);
- the family form of rational equivalence (Exercise 6.1, currently OUT);
- the fact that a finite linear projection of `Y` onto `ℙᵐ` has generic degree `deg Y`.

None of these is in the article's Depends on list. A route that avoids families uses the
following new articles.

1. **The Chow groups of projective space are generated by linear subspaces.** Every integral
   `m`-dimensional subvariety of `ℙⁿ_k` is rationally equivalent to an integer multiple of a
   linear `m`-space.
   - Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, §1.9; the Roadmap should confirm the exact example
     number.
   - Proof: `ℙⁿ ∖ ℙⁿ⁻¹ ≅ Aⁿ`, the localization sequence `A_m(ℙⁿ⁻¹) → A_m(ℙⁿ) → A_m(Aⁿ) → 0`,
     and `A_m(Aⁿ) = 0` for `m < n`. The last fact has an elementary proof. After a linear change
     of coordinates, the projection `π : Aⁿ → Aⁿ⁻¹` is finite on `Y`. Then `Y` is a prime
     divisor of `C = π(Y) × A¹`, which has dimension `m + 1`. Let `g` be the monic minimal
     polynomial of the last coordinate over `K(π(Y))`; it has coefficients in the normalization.
     Then `ν_* div(g) = [Y]` on the normalization `ν` of `C`.
   - Depends on: rational-equivalence-via-normalizations; raw-cycle-pushforward; the
     closed-immersion case of proper-pushforward-preserves-rational-equivalence; and a
     localization sequence for the normalization-defined rational equivalence (Fulton Prop. 1.8),
     either as a new elementary article or inlined as the extension step. The existing
     chow-localization-exact-sequence and chow-affine-homotopy-invariance are stated for the
     axiomatic Chow groups of A.1 and are not ready, so they cannot serve here; the sketch above
     proves `A_m(Aⁿ) = 0` directly without homotopy invariance. Cross-link this article with
     projective-space-chow-ring, which states the axiomatic analogue.
   - Would record: the linear-subspace points and codimension lemmas above.
2. **The degree of cycles on ℙⁿ is invariant under rational equivalence.** For `k`
   algebraically closed, `deg : Z_m(ℙⁿ) → ℤ` vanishes on `ν_* div(f)` for every integral
   `(m+1)`-dimensional `V ⊆ ℙⁿ`, so it descends to `A_m(ℙⁿ) → ℤ`.
   - Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, §1.4 and §2.5 (degree of cycles on `ℙⁿ`); the
     Roadmap should confirm the locator.
   - Proof: write `f = F/G` with `F, G` forms of the same degree and use Theorem I.7.7 on each
     irreducible factor.
   - Depends on: rational-equivalence-via-normalizations; raw-cycle-pushforward;
     projective-hilbert-polynomial-and-degree; projective-hypersurface-bezout; and new article 3.
   - Already covered special cases: `m = 0` by complete-variety-zero-cycle-degree, and
     codimension one by projective-space-class-group (principal divisors have degree zero). A
     lemma relating the codimension-one cycle degree to the Weil-divisor degree of
     projective-space-class-group would discharge the degree hypothesis there.
3. **Orders via the normalization are lengths.** For a one-dimensional Noetherian local domain
   `A` with finite normalization `B` and `a ≠ 0`:
   `ℓ_A(A/aA) = Σ_𝔪 ord_{B_𝔪}(a)·[κ(𝔪):κ(A)]`.
   - Source: Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, Example 1.2.3 and Lemma A.3.
   - Use: it identifies the coefficients of `ν_* div(F|_V)` with the intersection multiplicities
     of I.7.7. Those also need comparing the homogeneous and degree-zero localizations of `S(V)`,
     a supporting step.
