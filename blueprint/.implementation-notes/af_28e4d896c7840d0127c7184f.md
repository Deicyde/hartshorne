# Coherence of projective pushforward

## Lean

- Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectivePushforwardCoherent`.
- Main theorem: `Hartshorne.isCoherent_pushforward_of_projective`, stated with `X` and `Y` as
  schemes over `Spec k` (`Scheme.Over`), both locally of finite type and quasi-compact over
  `Spec k`, and `f` a morphism over `Spec k`. It is a one-line corollary of the general form.
- General form: `Hartshorne.isCoherent_pushforward_of_projective_of_locallyOfFiniteType`, which
  assumes only a morphism `Y ⟶ Spec k` locally of finite type.
- Kept-but-unused hypotheses of the main theorem (anonymous instance binders, flagged by
  Batteries' `unusedArguments`): `LocallyOfFiniteType (X ↘ Spec k)`, `QuasiCompact (X ↘ Spec k)`,
  `QuasiCompact (Y ↘ Spec k)` and `f.IsOver (Spec k)`. The source states them; the proof needs
  only the finite type of `Y` (its affine opens have finitely generated `k`-algebras of sections).

## Route

Follows Hartshorne's proof. `f` is proper (`Projective.isProper`), hence quasi-compact and
quasi-separated, so `f_* F` is quasi-coherent (`isQuasicoherent_pushforward_of_qcqs`). `Y` is
locally Noetherian, so coherence is clause 4 of `coherent_affine_local_tfae`: on every affine open
`U`, `Γ(U, f_* F)` is a finite `Γ(Y, U)`-module (the `fromTildeΓ` isomorphism comes from
quasi-coherence via `quasicoherent_affine_local_tfae`). For the finiteness, apply Theorem 5.19
(`moduleFinite_globalSections_of_projective`) with `A = Γ(Y, U)` to
`f ∣_ U ≫ hU.isoSpec.hom : f⁻¹ U ⟶ Spec Γ(Y, U)` and `F.restrict (f⁻¹ U).ι`.

Private helpers:

- `projective_of_isPullback`: projectivity is stable under base change (from
  `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeMap_isPullback` and pasting of pullback squares). Applied to the
  pullback square `isPullback_morphismRestrict`.
- `finite_pushforward_sections`, `topIso_inv_morphismRestrict_appTop_map`, `map_app_map_map`:
  identify Theorem 5.19's `Γ(Y, U)`-module `Γ(f⁻¹ U, F|)` (through
  `(ΓSpecIso A).inv ≫ (f ∣_ U ≫ isoSpec.hom).appTop` and `appIso`) with `Γ(U, f_* F)` (through
  `f.app U`), via restriction along `(f⁻¹ U).ι ''ᵁ ⊤ = f⁻¹ U`.

Coherence of `F.restrict (f⁻¹ U).ι` comes from `isCoherent_pullback` and
`restrictFunctorIsoPullback`; this is why the article links Pullback preserves quasi-coherence.

## Friction

- Rewriting with `morphismRestrict_appTop` leaves terms that are not type-correct at implicit
  transparency (opens of `U` versus opens of `(Opens.toTopCat Y).obj U`), so later `rw` steps
  fail. Stating the naturality computation for arbitrary restriction maps (`map_app_map_map`) and
  closing with `exact` avoids this.
- `List.TFAE.out` with an anonymous-constructor `fun U => ⟨_, _⟩` argument fails to elaborate; use
  `refine (... .out 3 0).mp ?_; intro U`.
