# The canonical sheaf of a smooth hypersurface

## Status

Complete (candidate, pending review). Lean in
`Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.CanonicalSheafSmoothHypersurface`, namespace
`Hartshorne.SmoothHypersurface`. Sorry-free. Recorded: `canonicalSheaf_smoothHypersurface`
(the isomorphism), `geometricGenus_pos` ($p_g(Y) > 0$, unconditional), `not_rational`.

## Conventions

- A hypersurface of degree $d$ in $X = \mathbb{P}^n_k$ is the divisor of zeros $D = (s)_0$ of a
  nonzero global section $s$ of $\mathcal{O}_X(d)$ (`IsDivisorOfZeros`, II.7). $Y$ is the closed
  subscheme of $D$: `hypersurface s hD`, with closed immersion `hypersurfaceι s hD`
  (`subschemeι` of the effective divisor, `isEffective_of_isDivisorOfZeros`).
- "Nonsingular" is `[IrreducibleSpace Y]` plus `Y.IsRegular`, as in
  `adjunctionEffectiveCartierDivisorIso`.
- $\mathcal{O}_Y(m)$ is `(Scheme.Modules.pullback i).obj (projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 m)`.
- "Not rational": no `PartialIso` over $k$ from $Y$ to `Proj (grading k m)` for any $m$, the
  birational-equivalence convention of `geometricGenus_eq_of_birational`.

## Route notes

- Non-rationality for $m \ge 1$ uses the rank inequality behind Theorem 8.19
  (`rank_globalSections_exteriorPower_le_of_partialIso`, with `canonicalSheaf_eq`), not the
  equality of `geometricGenus` (a `finrank`): without finite-dimensionality, $p_g = 0$ does not
  give $\Gamma = 0$. Mathematically it is the book's argument: canonical sections inject under a
  birational map, and $\Gamma(\mathbb{P}^m, \omega) = 0$.
- The case $m = 0$ (not in the book's sketch, which only compares with $\mathbb{P}^{n-1}$):
  birational to $\mathbb{P}^0$ forces $\dim Y = 0$, so $Y$ is one point whose image is a closed
  point of codimension one (`codim_range_subschemeι`); `height_add_coheight_point` on
  $\mathbb{P}^n_k$ then gives $n = 1$. This is where $n \ge 2$ is used.
- Ringed-space smul lemmas on `localFrameMap` values did not find their instances over
  `Y.sheaf`; `smul_zero` was applied through `PresheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf Y.sheaf).obj`.
