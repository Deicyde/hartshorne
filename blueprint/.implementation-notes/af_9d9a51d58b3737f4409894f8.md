# A birational projective morphism is a blowup

## Status

Candidate `Hartshorne.exists_iso_blowup_of_birational_projective` (module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalProjectiveBlowup`), pending review. Hypotheses:
`p : X ⟶ Spec k` of finite type and quasi-projective, `X` and `Z` integral,
`f : Z ⟶ X` projective (`Hartshorne.Projective`), birational in the project's
encoding (`f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` and
`f.genericFunctionFieldMap` bijective). Conclusion: a coherent
`QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` `I`, nonzero over some open set, and
`e : Z ≅ I.blowup hI` with `e.hom ≫ I.blowupProjection hI = f`. Any field `k`;
of `Z` only integrality is used (`Z` separated of finite type follows from `f`
projective).

## Route (book's Steps 1–5)

- Steps 1–3: `exists_sectionAlgebraModel_multiplicative`, repackaged as the
  structure `BirationalBlowup.SectionModel` (data, `E`, restriction,
  linearity, multiplicativity, `Proj_X S ≅ Z` over `X`).
- `S_m` inside `K(X)` (`BirationalBlowupGenericCoefficient`,
  `BirationalBlowupSectionEmbedding`): a trivializing section `e` of
  `L = i^* 𝒪(1)` near the generic point of `Z`; `genericCoeff` = germ at the
  generic point of the coefficient w.r.t. `e^{⊗n}`; `coeffK` = its image under
  `θ = (K(X) ≅ K(Z))⁻¹`; `pieceCoeff`/`sectionToPoly : S(U) →+* K(X)[T]`,
  injective, natural (`sectionToPoly_restrict`), multiplicative via
  `SectionModel.mul`.
- Step 4 (`BirationalBlowupIdeal`): `fracHom : f_* L^{1·d} ⟶ K_X`;
  denominator ideal of the project's Step 4 (`denominatorIdealSheaf`,
  `exists_isGloballyGenerated_denominatorIdealSheaf_tensor`);
  `exists_dual_hom_denominator` (nonzero `t : (A^n)^∨ ⟶ 𝒪_X` with values in the
  denominator ideal); `blowupIdeal` = `idealOfMono` of `s ⊗ ψ ↦ t(ψ)·s`;
  `mem_blowupIdeal_iff` on framed opens. The multiplication/mono step is a
  private copy of the corresponding private lemmas of
  `BirationalDenominatorIdeal` (the recorded
  `exists_mono_moduleSheafDual_tensor_pushforward` hides `ι` and `φ`, which are
  needed explicitly here).
- Step 5 (`BirationalBlowupRees`, `BirationalProjectiveBlowup`):
  `reesHomOfEmbedding` (pure algebra: a graded ring generated in degrees 0, 1
  embedded in `K[T]` with degree-one image `κ(J)T` is the Rees algebra of `J`);
  `Setup` bundles all data; `Setup.ρ : S(U) ≅ ⊕ I(U)ᵐ` on framed affine `U`
  (`s ↦ t(ψ)ᵐ c(s)`), `ρ_restrict`, `ρ_smul`, `projIso_ρ_eq` (frame
  independence on `Proj` via `SegreProduct.projMap_scale`); `localIso` =
  `projIsoOfBijective ρ ≫ (framedIso)⁻¹` (empty schemes when `U = ∅`);
  glued by `framedGlueIso` (from `BlowupInvertibleFactorInvariance`, unrecorded)
  and composed with `lineBundleTwistIso` (Lemma 7.9): `Setup.blowupIso`.
- Nonzero: if all `I(U) = 0`, the irrelevant ideal of `Rees(I)(U)` is zero, so
  `Proj` is empty, contradicting `Z ≅ Bl_I X`.
