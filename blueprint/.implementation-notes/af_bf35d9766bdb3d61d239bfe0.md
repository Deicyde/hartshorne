# The denominator ideal of a birational line bundle

## Status

Partial result in Lean, not recorded (no `lean:` frontmatter). Module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.BirationalDenominatorIdeal`, imported by the library root. Everything below is
sorry-free and uses only the standard axioms.

What is proved is the first half of Step 4 of the proof of Theorem II.7.17: `f_* L` as a coherent
fractional ideal, its denominator ideal `J`, and global generation of `J ⊗ A^n` for some `n > 0`.
The conclusion of the article, an embedding of `A^{-n} ⊗ f_* L` into `O_X`, is not proved yet.

## Setting chosen for the Lean

- Varieties: integral schemes `X`, `Z`. `X` is locally of finite type over a field `k` (structure
  morphism `pX`). Coherence statements need `IsLocallyNoetherian X`; the ample step needs
  `[IsNoetherian X]` because `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsAmple` is defined only on
  Noetherian schemes.
- `f : Z ⟶ X` projective: `Hartshorne.Projective f`.
- Birational: `hf : f.base (genericPoint Z) = genericPoint X` together with
  `Function.Bijective (f.genericFunctionFieldMap hf)`, the pullback of rational functions
  `K(X) → K(Z)` (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.genericFunctionFieldMap`). For varieties over `k`,
  a projective birational morphism satisfies this. The final theorem may want a wrapper deriving it
  from whatever scheme-level notion of birational morphism the Thm 7.17 chain settles on; the
  chain has none yet.
- The constant sheaf `K` is `X.totalQuotientModule` (the sheaf `K_X` of total quotient rings as an
  `O_X`-module). On an integral scheme its sections over an open containing the generic point are
  `K(X)` (`totalQuotientSheafEquivFunctionField`).

## Declarations (namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme`)

- `totalQuotientPushforward f hf θ hθ : (Modules.pushforward f).obj Z.totalQuotientModule ⟶
  X.totalQuotientModule`, for a left inverse `θ` of the pullback of rational functions; injective
  on sections (`totalQuotientPushforward_app_injective`).
- `exists_pushforward_toTotalQuotientModule_injective`: for `L` invertible on `Z`, there is
  `ι : f_* L ⟶ K_X` injective on all sections. Uses Proposition II.6.15
  (`exists_invertibleTotalQuotientSubmodule_iso`) on `Z`.
- `isCoherent_of_isInvertible`, `isCoherent_pushforward_of_isInvertible`: `L` and `f_* L` are
  coherent (Corollary II.5.20, `Hartshorne.isCoherent_pushforward_of_projective_of_locallyOfFiniteType`).
- `exists_ne_zero_smul_mem_toK`: common denominators. For coherent `F` with `ι : F ⟶ K_X` and an
  affine open `U ∋ η`, there is `a ≠ 0` in `O_X(U)` with `ι(a • s) ∈ O_X(U)` for all `s ∈ F(U)`.
- `exists_res_smul_eq_toK`: clearing denominators over an affine `U` also clears them over every
  open `V ⊆ U`. Proof via stalks: `Hartshorne.FiberDimAux.isLocalizedModule_germₗ`.
- `denominatorIdealObj ι U`: the ideal `{a | ∀ s ∈ F(U), ι(a • s) ∈ O_X(U)}`.
- `denominatorIdeal hF ι : X.IdealSheafData`: these ideals on affine opens, compatible with basic
  opens.
- `denominatorIdealSheaf hF ι : Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X`, via
  `Hartshorne.quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv`. Its properties:
  - it is coherent (`isCoherent_denominatorIdealSheaf`);
  - its ideal is nonzero on every affine open containing `η` (`denominatorIdealSheaf_ideal_ne_bot`);
  - `J · F ⊆ O_X` over every open (`exists_smul_eq_toK_of_mem_denominatorIdealSheaf`).
- `exists_isGloballyGenerated_denominatorIdealSheaf_tensor`: for `A` ample there is `n > 0` with
  `moduleSheafTensor J.toModules (moduleSheafTensorPower n A)` globally generated.
- `exists_denominatorIdealSheaf_of_birational`: the package for `F = f_* L`: `ι` mono, `J`
  coherent, nonzero, and `J · f_* L ⊆ O_X`.

## Remaining goal

With `F = f_* L`, `ι`, `J`, and `n > 0` as above, `G := J ⊗ A^n` is globally generated. To prove (shape of the statement, not yet compiled):

```lean
∃ n : ℕ, 0 < n ∧ ∃ φ : moduleSheafTensor (Hartshorne.tensorZPow A (-(n : ℤ)))
    ((Modules.pushforward f).obj L) ⟶ SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf, Mono φ
```

The hypotheses are those of `exists_denominatorIdealSheaf_of_birational`, plus `[IsNoetherian X]`
and `hA : A.IsAmple`. Quasi-projectivity of `X` enters only through the existence of `A`.

Next route, following the book:

1. `G ≠ 0`, because `J ≠ 0` and `A^n` is invertible. So global generation gives a nonzero global
   section `σ` of `G`.
2. The book reads `σ` as an injection `A^{-n} ⟶ J`. Tensor `σ : O_X ⟶ J ⊗ A^n` with `A^{-n}` and
   compose with `A^n ⊗ A^{-n} ≅ O_X`.
   - Dual evaluation: `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafDualEvalIso`.
   - Unit and associativity isos: `moduleSheafUnitTensor`, `moduleSheafTensorAssoc`.
3. Multiplication `μ : J ⊗ F ⟶ O_X` from the bilinear family `(a, s) ↦ b` with `ι(a • s) = toK b`.
   - Build it with `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorHomEquiv`.
   - Each `b` exists by `exists_smul_eq_toK_of_mem_denominatorIdealSheaf`.
   - `toK` is injective (`toK_injective`).
4. `φ := (t ⊗ F) ≫ μ`. Mono is the main work, a local computation.
   - On an open `V ∋ η` with a frame `e` of `A^{-n}`, `φ(e ⊗ s) = t(e) · ι(s)` with
     `t(e) ∈ J(V)`.
   - `t(e)` is nonzero because `σ ≠ 0` and `X` is integral.
   - Multiplication by a nonzero element of the field `K(X)` is injective.
   - An equivalent repackaging may be easier: build a mono `χ : F ⟶ A^n`, `s ↦ σ · s`, then tensor
     it with `A^{-n}`. Tensoring with a module that has local frames preserves monos
     (`Hartshorne.Ringed.modulesTensorMap_mono_of_localFrames`).

The pieces still missing from the project: a nonzero global section from global generation of a
nonzero sheaf; the local frame description of `G ⊗ L` for invertible `L`; and torsion-freeness /
rank-one injectivity on an integral scheme. A quick search found none of them under an obvious
name. `Hartshorne.Scheme.AmplePowerEquivalence` has the frame-restriction iso for
tensoring with an invertible sheaf (`twistRestrictHom_bijective`).
