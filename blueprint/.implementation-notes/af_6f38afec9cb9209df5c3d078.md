# The global-to-sheaf Ext map

## Partial progress

The module `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.GlobalToSheafExt` is sorry-free, imported by
the library root, and not recorded in the article frontmatter. It works on a
general ringed space (namespace `Hartshorne.Ringed`, module sheaves
`SheafOfModules (ringCatSheafOf R)`); global Ext is Mathlib's
`CategoryTheory.Abelian.Ext.{w}` under an assumed `HasExt.{w}` instance, as in
the global-Ext articles. Checked declarations:

- `Hartshorne.Ringed.globalToSheafExtOf F I n : Ext F G n →+ Γ(X, ℰxtⁿ(F, G))`
  for an injective resolution `I` of `G` (`Γ(X, -)` is the value on `⊤`), and
  `globalToSheafExtOf_extMk`: the class of a cocycle `f : F ⟶ Iⁿ` goes to the
  section `cocycleSheafExtClass F I n f`, namely `f` as a morphism
  `𝒪_X ⟶ ℋom(F, Iⁿ)` (`homEquivStructureHom`, through
  `moduleSheafHomGlobalSectionsAddEquiv` and `moduleSectionsAddEquiv` on `⊤`),
  lifted to the cycles of `ℋom(F, I^•)`, projected to homology and transported
  by `sheafExtIsoHomology`. Well-definedness: `cocycleSheafExtClass_eq_of_extMk_eq`
  (positive degree via `extMk_eq_zero_iff`; degree zero via
  `Cocycle.fromSingleMk_mem_coboundaries_iff` and the zero object
  `I.cochainComplex.X (-1)`).
- Compatibility of the global-Hom and sheaf-Hom complexes:
  `sheafHomCochain_comp_d`, with naturality `sheafHomCochain_comp_f` (chain maps)
  and `sheafHomCochain_precomp` (first variable).
- `Hartshorne.extMk_resolutionAugmentation` (generic abelian category):
  `I.extMk (g ≫ I.ι.f 0) 1 = Ext.mk₀ g`. Mathlib has no such degree-zero
  comparison; the proof goes through `extEquivCohomologyClass`,
  `equivOfIsKInjective` and `SmallShiftedHom.equiv`. A candidate for upstreaming.
- The cochain identification is natural for the precomposition transformation
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafHomPrecomp` of
  `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.SheafExtContravariant`, whose derived maps are
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafExtPrecomp` (contravariant functoriality of sheaf Ext);
  naturality in `F` should be stated with `sheafExtPrecomp`.
- `sheafExtIsoSheafHomComplexHomology` is `sheafExtIsoHomology` with its target
  written as `HomologicalComplex.homology`; `RightDerivedLES.isoH` is the
  general version of the same retyping.

## Remaining goal

A complete draft of the following exists (lead has its location); it
elaborated apart from the local failures listed.

1. Degree zero, `globalToSheafExtOf_mk₀`: `globalToSheafExtOf F I 0 (Ext.mk₀ g)`
   is the image of `g` under `moduleSheafHomGlobalSectionsAddEquiv` and
   `(moduleSheafHomFunctor F).rightDerivedZeroIsoSelf.inv`. The draft first
   proves `cocycleSheafExtClass_augmentation`: the class of `g ≫ I.ι.f 0` is
   `homEquivStructureHom F G g ≫ toRightDerivedZero.app G`, by
   `InjectiveResolution.toRightDerivedZero_eq`, cancelling `iCycles` with
   `toRightDerivedZero'_comp_iCycles` and the naturality of
   `homEquivStructureHom`. It then rewrites `Ext.mk₀ g` with
   `extMk_resolutionAugmentation`. Open point: after rewriting with the
   augmentation lemma, `rw [moduleSectionsAddEquiv_naturality]` does not find
   its pattern; use `moduleSectionsAddEquiv_naturality` with explicit objects
   and `Eq.trans`, as in `homEquivStructureHom_naturality`.
2. Naturality in `G`, `globalToSheafExtOf_comp_mk₀`: for `g : G ⟶ G'` and
   resolutions `I`, `I'`, take `φ := ⟨InjectiveResolution.desc g I' I, _⟩`, use
   `InjectiveResolution.extMk_comp_mk₀`, then a naturality lemma for
   `sheafExtIsoSheafHomComplexHomology` (from `sheafExtIsoHomology_hom_naturality`
   via `Iso.inv_comp_eq` and `Iso.eq_comp_inv`), `homologyπ_naturality_assoc`,
   `liftCycles_comp_cyclesMap_assoc`, cancel `iCycles`, and
   `sheafHomCochain_comp_f`. Open point: the `Hom` condition of `φ` needs
   `desc_commutes_zero` together with `single₀_map_f_zero`; plain `simp`
   left it unsolved.
3. Naturality in `F`, with `sheafExtPrecomp`: `InjectiveResolution.mk₀_comp_extMk`,
   then `SheafExtContravariant.extMap_comp_isoE_hom` (or
   `InjectiveResolution.rightDerived_app_eq`), the same homology steps, and
   `sheafHomCochain_precomp`. In the draft, the version via `rightDerived_app_eq`
   hit a kernel deterministic timeout; the heterogeneous-equality
   substitution trick of `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.SheafExt` elaborated but its
   final `Iso.inv_hom_id_assoc` rewrite did not match.
4. Independence of the resolution (`globalToSheafExtOf_eq`, naturality in `G` at
   `𝟙 G` with `Ext.comp_mk₀_id`; pass the implicit resolution explicitly), then
   the canonical `globalToSheafExt F G n := globalToSheafExtOf F (injectiveResolution G) n`
   and its four public lemmas (cocycle formula for any resolution, degree zero,
   naturality in `G`, naturality in `F`), which the article's `lean:` should
   record.

## Blocker: kernel time

Every declaration that mentions the complex `ℋom(F, I^•)`
(`(moduleSheafHomFunctor F).mapHomologicalComplex _`) takes 12 s or more in
the kernel. The module already takes about 210 s to build, and the draft of the
remaining lemmas took 15 minutes, with single declarations at 100 to 125 s.
Isolated tests: `(sheafHomComplex F K).X p = (moduleSheafHomFunctor F).obj (K.X p)`
by `rfl` is fast, but any statement that combines `mapHomologicalComplex` terms
with morphisms built from `homEquivStructureHom` costs about 12 s. Neither removing the
local `Abelian.toPreadditive` priority nor adding an `Additive` instance of
`moduleSheafHomFunctor F` stated for the abelian preadditive structure (the
device of `SheafExtContravariant`) changed the build time (about 210 s either
way). Profiling: the slow declarations are those whose statements combine
`mapHomologicalComplex` terms or wrapper definitions with morphisms built from
`homEquivStructureHom`; `homEquivStructureHom F M' (f ≫ g) =
homEquivStructureHom F M f ≫ (moduleSheafHomFunctor F).map g` alone checks
instantly. Next route: keep every statement in terms of
`(moduleSheafHomFunctor F).obj`/`.map` and `homEquivStructureHom` directly (no
wrapper such as `sheafHomCochain`), convert to the complex's `X`/`d` only
through `rfl` lemmas used by `rw`, and measure each declaration with
`set_option profiler true` before adding the next.

## Technical notes

- `I.ι.f 0` has source `((single₀ C).obj Y).X 0`, which equals `Y` only at
  default transparency; writing `g ≫ I.ι.f 0` blocks `simp` and `rw`. The
  wrapper `resolutionAugmentation` avoids this, but it duplicates the existing
  `Hartshorne.RightDerivedLES.aug` (with `aug_d`, `aug_eq`) in
  `Hartshorne.Cohomology.RightDerivedLongExactSequence`; a successor should
  state everything with `RightDerivedLES.aug I` and delete the wrapper.
  Likewise `sheafExtIsoSheafHomComplexHomology` could be
  `RightDerivedLES.isoH (moduleSheafHomFunctor F) I n`.
- `homologyFunctor.obj` versus `HomologicalComplex.homology`, and
  `moduleSheafHom F M` versus `(moduleSheafHomFunctor F).obj M`, are not
  reducibly equal. `sheafExtIsoSheafHomComplexHomology` and the codomain of
  `homEquivStructureHom` restate the types so that rewriting works.
- In proof terms that depend on a morphism (the `hf` arguments), `rw` with
  `Preadditive.add_comp` matches inside the proof and fails on the motive. Take
  the sum's cocycle proof as a separate hypothesis (see `cocycleSheafExtClass_add`).
