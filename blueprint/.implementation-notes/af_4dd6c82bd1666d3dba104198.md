# Regular schemes are locally factorial

## Prior implementation guidance

The algebraic implication “regular local ring implies UFD” (Auslander–Buchsbaum)
is not available in the pinned Mathlib checkout. It remains a sourced
prerequisite rather than an asserted upstream fact. The later Weil–Cartier
comparison is deliberately not part of this node. Once that algebraic theorem
exists, this node is immediate: the stalks are regular local rings, regular
local rings are domains (already in the project), and the scheme-level
predicate asks exactly for domain and UFD stalks.

## Partial progress (bounded attempt)

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalFactorial` (sorry-free, imported by the
library root), namespace `Hartshorne.RegularLocalFactorial`. The proof of
“regular local implies UFD” is complete except for two inputs, which appear as
hypotheses of the final declaration:

- `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`: every regular
  local ring is a UFD, assuming (H1) prime localizations of regular local rings
  are regular and (H2) every finitely generated module over a regular local ring
  has finite projective dimension.

Supporting declarations (all checked, axioms propext/choice/Quot.sound only):

- `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_ringKrullDim_le_one`: dimension at most one.
- `prime_of_mem_maximalIdeal_of_notMem_sq`, `exists_mem_maximalIdeal_notMem_sq`,
  `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_iff_localizationAway`: for `x ∈ 𝔪 \ 𝔪²`, `x` is
  prime and `R` is a UFD iff `R[1/x]` is (Nagata, via Mathlib).
- `exists_mem_forall_exists_mul_eq_of_isPrincipal_map`,
  `isUnit_map_of_forall_exists_mul_eq`: a nonzero finitely generated ideal of a
  domain that is principal at every maximal ideal is an invertible fractional
  ideal (missing from Mathlib).
- `isPrincipal_of_linearEquiv`,
  `isPrincipal_of_isStablyFree_of_forall_isPrincipal_map`,
  `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_isStablyFree`: a Noetherian domain whose
  localizations at maximal ideals are UFDs and whose projective height-one
  primes are stably free is a UFD.
- `isStablyFree_of_exact`: in `0 → K → F → M → 0` with `F` finite free and `M`
  projective, `K` stably free implies `M` stably free.
- `isStablyFree_of_isLocalizedModule_of_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`,
  `isStablyFree_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`: over a Noetherian local
  ring, a projective localization of a finitely generated module of finite
  projective dimension is stably free; hence, under (H2), projective ideals of
  `R[1/x]` are stably free.
- `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_localization`: the induction on the
  dimension, with (H1) and stable freeness of projective ideals of `R[1/x]` as
  hypotheses.

## Remaining goal

Discharge (H1) and (H2):

- (H1) is the existing node regular-localization (Hartshorne Theorem 8.14A),
  in progress separately; its statement matches the hypothesis exactly.
- (H2) is one direction of Serre's theorem (Matsumura, *Commutative Ring
  Theory*, Theorem 19.2): a finitely generated module over a regular local ring
  has projective dimension at most its dimension. Next route: induction on
  `d = dim R`. For `d = 0` the ring is a field. For `d > 0` pick `x ∈ 𝔪 \ 𝔪²`
  (`R/(x)` is regular of dimension `d - 1`, already in the project); for a
  first syzygy `N` of `M`, `x` is `N`-regular (domain, `N` inside a free
  module), Mathlib gives `pd_R(N/xN) = pd_R N + 1`, the induction hypothesis
  bounds `pd_{R/(x)}(N/xN)`, and the missing change-of-rings lemma
  `pd_R L ≤ pd_{R/(x)} L + 1` for `R/(x)`-modules `L` (induction on
  `pd_{R/(x)} L`, using `pd_R R/(x) ≤ 1` and exactness of restriction of
  scalars) closes the step.

Once both exist, the algebraic theorem is a one-line application of
`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`, and this node
follows from it with `Hartshorne.RegularLocal.isDomain`.
