# Regular schemes are locally factorial

## Status

Complete in Lean, not yet recorded. The Roadmap blocker (missing proof edges
to the owners of the declarations the proof uses) is resolved by the
regular-locally-factorial edges roadmap revision; what remains is for those
owners to record their declarations, after which this article records its own.

Declaration to record (`lean:`), in module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalFactorial`:

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isLocallyFactorial_of_isRegularLocalRing`: a scheme
  all of whose local rings are regular local rings is locally factorial (every
  local ring is a domain and a UFD).

Its algebraic input, the article's cited Matsumura Theorem 48, is the

- regular-hypersurface-local-criterion (already recorded): the proof uses

  direction of this article's statement ($f \notin \mathfrak m^2$ implies $A/(f)$ regular), which records the parallel
  declaration `Hartshorne.isRegularLocalRing_quotient_span_singleton_iff_notMem_sq`
  (stated for a domain and $x \ne 0$, both available here). When recording,
  either accept the helper as covered by this edge or reroute through the
  recorded declaration.

No edge to regular-local-implies-cohen-macaulay: the proof uses only the
quotient helper from that article's module, not the Cohen–Macaulay theorem it
records.

The proof also uses the superseded special-case helper
`Hartshorne.RegularLocalization.eq_bot_or_eq_maximalIdeal_of_ringKrullDim_le_one`
(module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RegularLocalization`), which no
article records; it is the elementary fact that a local domain of dimension at
most one has no primes other than $0$ and $\mathfrak m$.

This note should be deleted when the article is recorded.

## Proof structure (supporting declarations, same module)

Induction on the dimension (`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_localization`,
`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`): base case
`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_ringKrullDim_le_one`; for `x ∈ 𝔪 \ 𝔪²`
(`exists_mem_maximalIdeal_notMem_sq`, `prime_of_mem_maximalIdeal_of_notMem_sq`)
the ring is a UFD iff `R[1/x]` is
(`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_iff_localizationAway`); `R[1/x]` is a UFD by the
descent lemma `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_isStablyFree` (locally principal
ideals are invertible, `isUnit_map_of_forall_exists_mul_eq`, missing from
Mathlib; invertible stably free ideals are principal), whose stable-freeness
input comes from `isStablyFree_of_exact`,
`isStablyFree_of_isLocalizedModule_of_hasProjectiveDimensionLE` and
`isStablyFree_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`.
