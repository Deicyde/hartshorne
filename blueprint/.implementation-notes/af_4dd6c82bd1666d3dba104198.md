## Proof structure (supporting declarations, same module)

Induction on the dimension (`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_localization`,
`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`): base case
`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_ringKrullDim_le_one`; for `x ∈ 𝔪 \ 𝔪²`
(`exists_mem_maximalIdeal_notMem_sq`, `prime_of_mem_maximalIdeal_of_notMem_sq`)
the ring is a UFD iff `R[1/x]` is
(`uniqueFactorizationMonoid_iff_localizationAway`); `R[1/x]` is a UFD by the
descent lemma `uniqueFactorizationMonoid_of_isStablyFree` (locally principal
ideals are invertible, `isUnit_map_of_forall_exists_mul_eq`, missing from
Mathlib; invertible stably free ideals are principal), whose stable-freeness
input comes from `isStablyFree_of_exact`,
`isStablyFree_of_isLocalizedModule_of_hasProjectiveDimensionLE` and
`isStablyFree_of_forall_hasProjectiveDimensionLE`.
