# Quasi-coherent sheaves are filtered unions of coherent subsheaves

## Partial progress

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.QuasicoherentCoherentSubobjects` (sorry-free, imported by the
library root, not recorded in the article frontmatter) models a coherent subsheaf of `F` as a
subobject of `F` in `X.Modules` whose underlying module is coherent
(`Hartshorne.CoherentSubobject F`, ordered as subobjects). Checked declarations:

- `Hartshorne.isCoherent_kernel_of_isQuasicoherent`,
  `Hartshorne.isCoherent_image_of_isQuasicoherent`: on a locally Noetherian scheme, kernel and
  image of a map from a coherent module to a quasi-coherent module are coherent;
- `Hartshorne.coherentSubobject_isFiltered`: the poset is filtered;
- `Hartshorne.subobjectFamilyDiagram`, `Hartshorne.subobjectFamilyCocone`: the diagram and the
  inclusion cocone of a family of subobjects given by a predicate;
  `Hartshorne.coherentSubobjectDiagram`, `Hartshorne.coherentSubobjectCocone` are these for
  the coherence predicate;
- `Hartshorne.nonempty_isColimit_subobjectFamilyCocone`: in a Grothendieck abelian category, a
  nonempty directed family of subobjects with supremum `⊤` has the inclusions as a colimit
  cocone (reindexes along `Shrink` and applies Mathlib's
  `IsGrothendieckAbelian.isColimitMapCoconeOfSubobjectMkEqISup`);
- `Hartshorne.subobject_eq_top_of_forall_mem_range`: in `X.Modules`, a subobject containing
  every member of a family is `⊤` once every local section of `F` is a section of a member;
- `Hartshorne.nonempty_isColimit_coherentSubobjectCocone_of_forall_mem`: on a locally
  Noetherian scheme, if every local section of the quasi-coherent `F` is a section of some
  coherent subsheaf, the coherent-subsheaf cocone is a colimit cocone.

The Grothendieck abelian structure of `X.Modules` is a private theorem in this module
(`modules_isGrothendieckAbelian`), a copy of the private
`sheafModules_isGrothendieckAbelian` of `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ModuleSheavesEnoughInjectives`
specialised to schemes. Making that one public there (another article's module) and deleting the
copy is a pending cleanup.

## Remaining goal

For `X` Noetherian and `F` quasi-coherent: every `s : Γ(F, U)` lies in the range of
`P.1.arrow.app U` for some `P : CoherentSubobject F`. With the reduction above this closes the
leaf.

## Missing prerequisite (split proposal)

This is Hartshorne's hint for Ex. II.5.15(e): apply 5.15(d) to the subsheaf of `F|_U`
generated by `s` (the image of `𝒪_U → F|_U`, coherent by the image lemma above). It needs the
subsheaf form of Ex. II.5.15(d): for `X` Noetherian, `U` open, `G` quasi-coherent on `X` and a
coherent subsheaf `F ⊆ G|_U`, there is a coherent subsheaf `F' ⊆ G` with `F'|_U = F`.

- The roadmap article `schemes/coherent-sheaves/coherent-extension` (af_e6085b5482d318aaf9e1af13)
  is the right home, but its second sentence only treats `F` identified with `G|_U`; Hartshorne
  5.15(c),(d) treat a subsheaf `F ⊆ G|_U`, which is the case used here. Same issue in
  `coherent-extension-affine` (af_0bbdf1efed6dcc5ffe5d91e9).
- This article's "Proof depends on" should list `coherent-extension`; its prose already says
  "extend", but the edge is missing.
- `coherent-extension-affine` needs `qcqs-pushforward-quasicoherent`
  (af_8e36cd2ce8a49bd91790411b), unformalized; the pushforward of a quasi-coherent module along
  a quasi-compact open immersion is not in the pinned Mathlib or in `src/`.

## Next route

1. Formalize `qcqs-pushforward-quasicoherent` at least for an open immersion from an affine
   (or quasi-compact) open into a Noetherian scheme.
2. A route to the remaining goal that skips gluing: take a finite affine cover `V_i = Spec A_i`
   with `F|_{V_i} ≅ M_i~`. Choose finitely generated `N_i ⊆ M_i` whose tilde contains `s` on
   `U ∩ V_i` (cover `U ∩ V_i` by finitely many basic opens; take the numerators of `s`). Let
   `P` be the meet over `i` of the kernels of `F → j_{i*}(M_i~ / N_i~)`. `P` is quasi-coherent
   by step 1 and abelian closure. `P|_{V_i} ⊆ N_i~` makes `P` coherent, and `s ∈ P(U)`.
3. Or follow Hartshorne: prove the subsheaf form of `coherent-extension-affine`, then
   `coherent-extension` by induction over a finite affine cover, gluing subsheaves.
