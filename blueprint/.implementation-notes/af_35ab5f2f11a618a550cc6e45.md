# Serre's affineness criterion

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.SerreAffinenessCriterion`.

- `Hartshorne.isAffine_tfae_sheafCohomology`: for `[IsNoetherian X]`, `List.TFAE` of `IsAffine X`;
  vanishing of `H^i` (cohomology of the underlying abelian sheaf) for quasi-coherent `F`, `i > 0`;
  vanishing of `H^1(X, J)` for every `J : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` with
  `IsCoherent J.toModules`.
- (iii) ⇒ (i) alone: `Hartshorne.SerreAffineness.isAffine_of_isZero_H1`.

## Route (Hartshorne's printed proof)

- (i) ⇒ (ii): `sheafCohomology_isZero_of_isQuasicoherent_of_isAffine` (III.3.5 for an affine
  Noetherian scheme, transported along `X ≅ Spec Γ(X, 𝒪_X)`).
- Local step `exists_affine_basicOpen_of_isClosed`: ideal sheaves `vI Z :=
  idealSheafDataToSubsheaf (vanishingIdeal Z)`; `vIIncl` for `I_{Y∪P} ⊆ I_Y`; the section of the
  cokernel is glued with `TopCat.Sheaf.existsUnique_gluing'` over the cover `{U, X ∖ P}`;
  `surjective_app_top` (from `rightDerived_exact₃` at degree 0) lifts it. Points of an affine open
  are handled through `IsAffineOpen.fromSpec` and `fromSpec_preimage_basicOpen`.
- Generation step `span_eq_top_of_cover`: `freeL l` (iterated biproducts of `𝒪_X`), `αL l`
  (built from `smulHom`), short exact sequences `kerSC` (`ker αL l ⟶ ker αL (f :: l) ⟶ image of the
  first coordinate`), the image turned into an ideal subsheaf by `idealOfMono`; induction gives
  `H^1(ker αL l) = 0`; `epi_αL` by local surjectivity.
- Combination `isAffine_of_local_of_generation`: closed points via
  `IsClosed.exists_closed_singleton`, compactness, `isAffine_of_isAffineOpen_basicOpen`.
- `isCoherent_toModules`: every quasi-coherent ideal subsheaf is coherent on a locally Noetherian
  scheme, so the coherence hypothesis in (iii) is always met.

## Friction

`rfl` for `((inclusion h₁ h₂ h).app V g).1 = g.1` times out in the kernel when the carriers are
`vI Z` (the kernel unfolds the carrier); stating it for abstract carriers
(`inclusion_app_val`) and instantiating is instant.
