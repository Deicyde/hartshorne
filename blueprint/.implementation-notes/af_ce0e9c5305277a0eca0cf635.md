# Localization sequence for rational equivalence via normalizations

## Status

New article from the projective-cycle-rational-equivalent-linear-space split (roadmap
proposal). No Lean exists for it; nothing is recorded in the frontmatter. Formalize should
record declarations here only after independent review.

This is Fulton's Proposition 1.8 for the normalization-defined rational equivalence of
Appendix A.1. It is deliberately separate from chow-localization-exact-sequence (Hartshorne's
A10), which is stated for nonsingular varieties through the axiomatic pushforward and pullback
of A2/A3 and is not ready. Only the closed-immersion pushforward and the open restriction are
needed here, and both act on generators directly; no proper-pushforward theorem is used.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.codimensionCycles` (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.CodimensionCycles`),
  `Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup`, `Hartshorne.RationallyEquivalent`,
  `Hartshorne.subvarietyNormalizationMap`, `Hartshorne.subvarietyNormalizationPushforward`
  (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.RationalEquivalence`), `Hartshorne.chowGroup`
  (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.ChowGroups`).
- `Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_eq_closure_principalDivisor`
  (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.RationalEquivalencePrincipal`): the generators as pushed
  principal divisors indexed by closed immersions `i : V ⟶ X` of integral `V`. The
  closed-immersion step is then composition `V ⟶ Y ⟶ X` of closed immersions.
- `Hartshorne.rawCyclePushforward` (module `Hartshorne.Intersection.RawCyclePushforward`); for a
  closed immersion every coefficient is 1.
- `Hartshorne.absoluteNormalization` and its open cover; the open-restriction step needs that
  the normalization of `V ∩ U` is the preimage of `U` (normalization is affine-local).
- Mathlib: no localization sequence for cycles.

## Candidate declarations (names are suggestions)

New module, say `Hartshorne.Intersection.RationalEquivalenceLocalization`:

1. `Hartshorne.codimensionCycles.restrictOpen` (the map `[W] ↦ [W ∩ U]`, zero for `W ⊆ Y`)
   and `Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_map_restrictOpen_le`.
2. `Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_map_closedImmersion_le` for the closed-immersion
   pushforward with the codimension shift `c`.
3. `Hartshorne.chowGroup_localization_exact` (exactness in the middle) and
   `Hartshorne.chowGroup_restrictOpen_surjective`.

The codimension bookkeeping (`codim_X W = codim_Y W + c`, `codim_U (W ∩ U) = codim_X W`) uses
the dimension formula for varieties over a field; the target application only needs `Y` a
hyperplane in `ℙⁿ`, so `Y` may be restricted to an integral closed subvariety.
