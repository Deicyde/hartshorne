# Affine sections of a formal completion

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalCompletionAffineSections`.

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso I U`, for
  `U : X.affineOpens`: a ring isomorphism from the sections of `I.formalCompletionSheaf` over
  `I.infinitesimalNeighborhoodι 1 ⁻¹ᵁ U` (the open `U ∩ |Y|` of `|Y|`) to Mathlib's
  `AdicCompletion (I.ideal U) Γ(X, U)`, whose ring structure is Mathlib's inverse limit of the
  `A ⧸ I(U) ^ n` (the `n = 0` term is the zero ring, so the limit agrees with the article's
  `lim_n A / I(U)^{n+1}`).
- `formalCompletionSheafObjAffineEquiv_evalₐ_formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso`: the
  `(n + 1)`-st component of the isomorphism, read through
  `formalCompletionSheafObjAffineEquiv I U n : A ⧸ I(U) ^ (n + 1) ≃+* Γ(U ∩ |Y|, 𝒪_{Y_(n+1)})`,
  is the limit projection to the `n`-th term of `formalCompletionDiagram`.
- `formalCompletionSectionsMap I U` (any open `U`): the canonical map
  `Γ(U, 𝒪_X) → Γ(U ∩ |Y|, 𝒪_{X̂_Y})`, lifted from the restrictions
  `formalCompletionSheafObjSectionsMap` (an `appLE` along `Y_(n+1) ⟶ X`);
  `formalCompletionSectionsMap_naturality` makes it natural in `U`.
- `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso_formalCompletionSectionsMap`: it goes to
  `AdicCompletion.of`.
- `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso_map`: for affine `W ≤ U`, restriction goes to
  `Hartshorne.adicCompletionMap (I.ideal U) (I.ideal W) ρ _`, the ring map of adic completions
  induced by `ρ = X.presheaf.map (homOfLE h).op` (built with `AdicCompletion.liftRingHom`;
  Mathlib only has the module-functorial `AdicCompletion.map` over one ring).

## Route

- Sections of the limit sheaf: `isLimitFormalCompletionSheafSections` (sheafToPresheaf and
  evaluation preserve the ℕᵒᵖ-limit; Mathlib's sheaf limit machinery, the article
  *Limits of sheaves are computed pointwise*).
- The open of `Y_(n+1)` corresponding to `U ∩ |Y|` is `ι_(n+1) ⁻¹ᵁ U`
  (`functor_obj_preimage_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι`), so the restriction
  `Γ(U, 𝒪_X) → Γ(U ∩ |Y|, 𝒪_{Y_(n+1)})` is `ι_(n+1).app U` followed by an isomorphism; Mathlib's
  `Scheme.IdealSheafData.subschemeι_app_surjective` and `ker_subschemeι_app` give surjectivity
  and kernel `I(U) ^ (n + 1)` (with `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.ideal_pow`). This is the
  "sections of affine quotient sheaves are ring quotients" step; the project's
  tilde-localization declaration is not used, since Mathlib's subscheme construction already
  computes the affine sections of the closed subscheme cut out by `I ^ (n + 1)`.
- The isomorphism is built from `AdicCompletion.liftRingHom` (forward) and the limit lift of the
  cone `formalCompletionAffineAdicCone` (backward).

## Friction

- `TopCat.Sheaf` vs `ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory`: `rw` on goals mentioning
  `(limit formalCompletionDiagram).obj` fails ("not type-correct under implicit transparency");
  use `exact`/`.trans` with the elementwise lemma instead (`NatTrans.naturality_apply`).
- Elaborating `Γ(Y_(n+1+1), …) =?= (formalCompletionSheafObj (n+1)).presheaf.obj (op V)` times
  out; giving `formalCompletionSheafObjSectionsMap` the `formalCompletionSheafObj` form as its
  declared type avoids it.
