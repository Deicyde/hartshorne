Route of the affine step: Exercise 9.3 (`affineFormal_globalSections_shortExact`, applicable since

## Remaining goal

```
theorem Hartshorne.IsCoherentFormalModule.of_shortExact {X : NoetherianFormalScheme.{u}}
    {S : ShortComplex (SheafOfModules.{u} (structureRingCatSheaf X.toLocallyRingedSpace))}
    (hS : S.ShortExact) (h₁ : IsCoherentFormalModule S.X₁) (h₃ : IsCoherentFormalModule S.X₃) :
    IsCoherentFormalModule S.X₂
```

Missing prerequisites, in order:

1. Chart independence on affine charts (the essential-surjectivity half of Theorem 9.7, article
   affine-formal-coherent-equivalence, itself waiting on coherent-system-effectivity): if `G` is
   coherent formal on `X` and `W ⊆ X` is an open with `W ≅ Spf_I(A)`, `A` Noetherian and
   `I`-adically complete, then `G|_W ≅ (M~)^` transported, for a finite `A`-module `M`. The data of
   `IsCoherentFormalModule` for `F'` and `F''` come with unrelated charts, so without this there is
   no single chart on which both are completed finite modules. The counit above is the natural
   comparison map: one would show `ε_{G|_W}` is an isomorphism.
2. Reduction from a chart over a non-complete Noetherian `A` to `Â`: `spfCompletionIso`
   (`Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalSpectrumCompletion`) identifies `Spf_I(A) ≅ Spf_{IÂ}(Â)`; one needs
   the module-level statement that `(M~)^` on `Spf_I(A)` corresponds to `((M ⊗ Â)~)^`.
3. Locality: (a) restriction of `IsCoherentFormalModule` to an open, using that opens of `Ŷ`
   are traces of opens `V ⊆ Y`, `Ŷ|_{V ∩ |Y|} ≅ V̂` and `Ĝ|_{V ∩ |Y|} ≅ (G|_V)^`; (b) a finite
   cover by opens on each of which the restriction is coherent gives coherence on `X` (charts of
   the opens compose to charts of `X`). Every point of a chart `Ŷ` has an affine neighbourhood
   `Spec B ⊆ Y`, giving `Spf_{I_B}(B)` charts.

With 1–3: cover `X` by finitely many affine formal opens `W` (quasi-compactness); on each, by 2 and
1, `F'|_W` and `F''|_W` are completions of finite modules over the complete ring; the restricted
sequence is short exact; `affineFormal_extension_isCoherentFormalModule` gives `F|_W` coherent;
conclude by 3(b).
