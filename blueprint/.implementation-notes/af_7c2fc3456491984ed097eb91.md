# Scheme-valued points of the projective line

Pinned Mathlib supplies the arithmetic endpoint
`Projectivization.card_of_finrank_two`, but the bridge itself needs two
geometric identifications:

1. specialize the relative-projective-space functor-of-points theorem for an
   invertible sheaf with two ordered generators to `Spec K`, identifying its
   points with one-dimensional quotients of `K²`; and
2. use affine quasi-coherent equivalence to express that quotient as linear
   algebra, then dualize and use the standard basis to identify `(K²)∨` with
   `Fin 2 → K`, producing `Projectivization K (Fin 2 → K)`.

`Hartshorne.rationalPointEquivSchemePoint` only repackages over-category
morphisms as commuting scheme morphisms and does not provide the homogeneous-
coordinate equivalence. The generated-line-bundle article supplies the
relative projective-space classification, but the quotient-line/classical-
projectivization identification remains a genuine focused bridge.
