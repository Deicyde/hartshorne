# Scheme-valued points of the projective line

Lean: `src/Hartshorne/Weil/ProjectiveLineSchemePoints.lean`, namespace
`Hartshorne.ProjectiveLinePoints`.

## Statement shape

- `Point k K`: morphisms `Spec K ⟶ ℙ¹_k` (`projectiveSpaceOverScheme 1 (Spec k)`) whose
  composite with `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection` is `Spec.map (algebraMap k K)`.
- `coordinateMap φ : (Fin 2 → K) →ₗ[K] Γ(Spec K, φ^* 𝒪(1))`, `a ↦ a₀ φ^* x₀ + a₁ φ^* x₁`, and
  `kernel φ` its kernel. A one-dimensional quotient of `K²` is recorded by its kernel, a
  subspace `W` with `finrank K ((Fin 2 → K) ⧸ W) = 1`.
- `equivQuotient k K : Point k K ≃ {W // finrank K ((Fin 2 → K) ⧸ W) = 1}`, with
  `equivQuotient_apply_coe` saying the map is `φ ↦ kernel φ`.
- `quotientEquivDualLine K V`: `W ↦ W.dualAnnihilator`, quotient lines of `V` to lines of
  `Module.Dual K V` (any finite-dimensional `V`).
- `equivProjectiveSpace k K : Point k K ≃ ProjectiveSpace K (Fin 2)`, the composite with
  `(Pi.basisFun K (Fin 2)).toDualEquiv.symm` and `Projectivization.equivSubmodule`.
- `equivProjectiveSpace_eq_mk`: if `φ^* xᵢ = cᵢ • t` with `c ≠ 0`, the point is
  `Projectivization.mk K c hc`, i.e. homogeneous coordinates `[c₀ : c₁]`.

The core lemmas (`finrank_quotient_kernel`, `eq_of_kernel_eq`, `exists_kernel_eq`) hold over
an arbitrary base scheme `Y` with any structure morphism `Spec K ⟶ Y`.

## Proof route

II Thm 7.1 (`existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`) with `X = Spec K`, `n = 1`.

- On `Spec K` every open containing the point is `⊤`, so a generating family of sections of
  an invertible sheaf contains a global trivializing section
  (`exists_isTrivializingSection_top`, from the nonvanishing-locus lemmas of the 7.1 file).
  The `K`-module structure on sections is Mathlib's `Γ(Spec R, -)`-module instance from
  `Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.Tilde`, re-exposed as `Module K Γ(M, U)`
  (`instModuleSections`), because instance search does not see `K` through
  `CommRingCat.of K`.
- Injectivity: two points with the same kernel have pulled-back data identified by
  `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` on the one-element cover `{⊤}`; then uniqueness in 7.1(b).
- Surjectivity: `W` is the kernel of a functional `f`; the structure sheaf with sections
  `f(eᵢ) • 1` is invertible and generated, and 7.1(b) gives the morphism. This uses the
  structure sheaf directly rather than `tilde (K² ⧸ W)`; the two agree (`tildeSelf` is `refl`)
  and no other use of the affine equivalence is needed.

## Naturality

`equivProjectiveSpace_map`: for `ι : K →ₐ[k] K'`, `Point.map ι φ` (precompose with
`Spec K' ⟶ Spec K`) goes to `Projectivization.map (coordinateBaseChange ι) _` of the image
of `φ`. Proof by the chart description: on `Spec K`, `φ` lands in some chart `D₊(x_k)`
(`exists_top_le_chartPreimage`), `φ^* x_l = chartCoordinate (toIntMap φ) k l _ • φ^* x_k`
(`pullbackGlobalSection_eq_chartCoordinate_smul`, from `restrict_pullbackGlobalSection_eq_smul`
of the 7.1 file), and `chartCoordinate_comp` (via `ΓSpecIso_naturality`) gives the
compatibility with composition; `equivProjectiveSpace_eq_mk` finishes. This avoids any
`pullbackComp` / `pullbackGlobalSection` compatibility lemma.
