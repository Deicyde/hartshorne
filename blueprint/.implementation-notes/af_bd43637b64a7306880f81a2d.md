# The graded symmetric-algebra sheaf

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.SymmetricAlgebraSheaf`.

## Supporting implementation references

- `SymmetricAlgebra.lift`
- `SymmetricAlgebra.equivMvPolynomial`

## Where each clause lives

- Construction and grading: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.symmetricAlgebra` /
  `symmetricAlgebraStructure` / `symmetricPower` are abbreviations of the sheaf-tensor-operations
  constructions `Hartshorne.moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebra` etc.; `symmetricAlgebra_graded` bundles
  the direct-sum decomposition, degree additivity of the product, and commutativity.
- Generated in degree one: `symmetricPowerOneIso` (`Sym^1 E ≅ E`),
  `Hartshorne.epi_moduleSheafTensorToSymmetricPower` (the maps `T^d E → Sym^d E`,
  `f₁ ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ f_d ↦ f₁ ⋯ f_d`, are epimorphisms), and the multiplicative epimorphism
  `T(E) → Sym(E)` (`moduleSheafTensorToSymmetricAlgebra`, `_mul`, `_one`,
  `moduleSheafTensorAlgebraι_comp_toSymmetricAlgebra`).
- Quasi-coherence: `symmetricAlgebra_isQuasicoherent`, `symmetricPower_isQuasicoherent`
  (no Noetherian hypothesis needed).
- Coherence: `symmetricPower_isCoherent` (Noetherian hypothesis used through
  `coherent_affine_local_tfae`; finite presentation alone already gives coherence via
  `isCoherent_of_isFinitePresentation`).
- Ranks: `symmetricPower_isLocallyFreeOfRank` (rank `C(n + d - 1, d)`),
  `symmetricPower_isLocallyFree`.
- Polynomial description: `Hartshorne.exists_polynomialAlgebraSheaf_iso_symmetricAlgebra` and
  `polynomialAlgebraSymmetricAlgebraIso` (`_mul`, `_one`, `_X`) for `E ≅ 𝒪_X^{⊕ ι}` globally,
  with `polynomialAlgebraSheaf` the sheafification of `U ↦ 𝒪_X(U)[T_i]`.

## Method

- Algebra: `symmetricMonomial_bijective` (monomials over monotone words form a basis of
  `Sym^d` of a free module; linear independence via the map to `MvPolynomial`), `MonotoneWord.card`
  (stars and bars through `Sym.card_sym_eq_choose`).
- Sheaves: `Ringed.isLocalFrame_sheafify` (a family restricting to bases on all opens of `U`
  becomes a local frame of the sheafification: injectivity by stalks, surjectivity by gluing
  coefficients in `𝒪_X`), then frames give `E|_U ≅ free` via
  `Ringed.nonempty_over_iso_free_of_isLocalFrame` (PicardGroup).
- Quasi-coherence of `Sym(E)`: over `U` with a frame, restriction (a left adjoint) preserves the
  coproduct decomposition, so `Sym(E)|_U ≅ free (Σ_d monomials)`; then
  `SheafOfModules.IsQuasicoherent.of_coversTop`.

## Known gap / next route

The local polynomial identification is proved for globally free `E`. Applied to an open
subscheme `U` it identifies `Sym(E|_U)` with `𝒪_U[T]`; the comparison `Sym(E)|_U ≅ Sym(E|_U)`
as sheaves of algebras is not formalized (for the powers it is
`moduleSheafSymmetricPowerRestrictIso`). Route: build the presheaf isomorphism for
`U ↦ S(F(U))` as for `symmetricPowerPresheafRestrictIso`, sheafify with
`sheafificationRestrictHom`, and prove multiplicativity in the style of
`PresheafAlgebra.sheafifyMap_mul`. The projective-bundle article will need it.

## Friction

- The scoped `Module` instance on `M.val.presheaf.obj U` (namespace `Hartshorne.Ringed`) clashes
  with `ModuleCat.isModule` on `M.val.obj U`; families typed as presheaf sections cause
  type-incorrect motives and `whnf` timeouts. Keep sections typed as `M.val.obj U`, and put
  constructions that do not need frames outside `namespace Ringed`.
- `SheafOfModules.IsQuasicoherent` / `IsFinitePresentation` sometimes elaborate with universe
  `0` inside proofs; pin with `.{u, u, u}`.
