# The graded symmetric-algebra sheaf

## Degree zero: `Sym^0(E) = O_X` (condition (†), p.160)

The statement's clause `Sym^0(E) ≅ O_X` as algebras is formalized in
`Hartshorne/Scheme/SymmetricAlgebraSheaf.lean`, in the style of the degree-one
identification `symmetricPowerOneIso`.

- Module level: `Hartshorne.SheafTensorOperations.symmetricPowerZeroEquiv`,
  `Sym^0_A M ≃ₗ[A] A`, sending the empty product to `1`, natural in semilinear
  maps (`symmetricPowerZeroEquiv_map`); the empty product maps to `1` in the
  symmetric algebra (`symmetricPowerToAlgebra_zero_tprod`).
- Ringed space (any site): `Hartshorne.moduleSheafSymmetricPowerZeroIso`,
  `moduleSheafSymmetricPower 0 F ≅ SheafOfModules.unit _`, the sheafification
  of the presheaf iso `symmetricPowerPresheafZeroIso` followed by the counit at
  the structure sheaf; its inverse sends `1` to the class of the empty product
  (`moduleSheafSymmetricPowerZeroIso_inv_one`).
- Algebra compatibility on a ringed space: the composite `O_X ≅ S^0 F → S(F)`
  sends `1` to the unit of `S(F)` (`Hartshorne.moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebraι_zero_one`),
  sends `a` to `a • 1` (`moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebraι_zero_apply`), and is
  multiplicative (`Hartshorne.moduleSheafSymmetricAlgebraι_zero_mul`).
- Scheme corollaries: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.symmetricPowerZeroIso`
  (`E.symmetricPower 0 ≅ SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf`),
  `symmetricPowerZeroIso_inv_one` and `symmetricPowerZeroIso_inv_mul`.
