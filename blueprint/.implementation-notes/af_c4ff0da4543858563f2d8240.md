# Scheme completions are formal schemes

## Status (candidate, pending review)

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.AlgebraizableFormalScheme`.

- `Hartshorne.IsAlgebraizable X`: a locally ringed space isomorphic to
  `I.formalCompletion` for some Noetherian scheme `Y` and
  `I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf Y`. This is the article's naming sentence.
- `Hartshorne.IsAlgebraizable.isNoetherianFormalScheme`: algebraizable implies
  `IsNoetherianFormalScheme`.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isAlgebraizable_formalCompletion` and
  `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isNoetherianFormalScheme_formalCompletion`
  (the article's theorem), for `[IsNoetherian X]`.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.toNoetherianFormalScheme`: the bundled
  object of the category `NoetherianFormalScheme`.

## Route

The recorded `IsNoetherianFormalScheme` (Hartshorne's p.194 definition) allows
non-affine Noetherian ambient schemes in its charts, so the one-member cover
`{⊤}` witnesses the property, via Mathlib's `LocallyRingedSpace.restrictTopIso`.
The article's affine-cover bullet and its affine-sections edge are not used by
this route; they belong to a future affine-atlas result (a finite cover by
`Spf`-type charts, cf. `Hartshorne.IsAffineNoetherianFormalScheme`).
