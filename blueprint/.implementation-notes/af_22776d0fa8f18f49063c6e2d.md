# The zeta function of the projective line

## Status

Candidate, pending review. Lean module `Hartshorne.Weil.ProjectiveLineZeta`. Sorry-free.

## Declaration

- `Hartshorne.hasseWeilZetaSeries_projectiveLine` (k a finite field, $q$ = `Nat.card k`), a
  conjunction matching the article's "both as a formal series and its displayed rational
  representative":
  1. `hasseWeilZetaSeries k ℙ¹ π hπ = ((1 - X) * (1 - C q * X))⁻¹` in `ℚ⟦X⟧` (the `Inv` of
     power series over a field);
  2. the image of the zeta series in `LaurentSeries ℚ` equals the image of
     `1 / ((1 - RatFunc.X) * (1 - RatFunc.C q * RatFunc.X)) : RatFunc ℚ` (Mathlib's coercion
     `RatFunc F → F⸨X⸩`). This is the sense in which the series is the expansion at the origin
     of the rational function.
  Here `ℙ¹` is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme 1 (Spec (.of k))` with
  `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection` and `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeProjection_finiteType`,
  as in `pointCountSequence_projectiveLine`.

## Route

The book's route. Private helpers:

- `negLogOneSub a` $=\sum_{r\ge 1} a^r t^r/r$ (that is, $-\log(1-at)$; Mathlib's
  `PowerSeries.log` is $\log(1+X)$ and Mathlib has no `exp ∘ log` identity, so the identity is
  proved directly);
- `exp_subst_negLogOneSub_mul`: $\exp(-\log(1-at))\,(1-at)=1$;
- `exp_subst_add`: $\exp(f+g)=\exp f\cdot\exp g$ for $f,g$ with zero constant term
  (with `exp_subst_mul_exp_subst_neg`).

All exponential identities go through `PowerSeries.derivative_subst` (chain rule),
`derivative_exp` and `PowerSeries.derivative.ext` (equal derivative and constant term), the same
technique as `traceExponential_eq_inv_det`. The log series of $\mathbb{P}^1$ is split as
`negLogOneSub 1 + negLogOneSub q` using `pointCountSequence_projectiveLine`. The Laurent-series
conjunct uses `RatFunc.coe_coe` on the polynomial $(1-X)(1-qX)$ and `map_inv₀`.
