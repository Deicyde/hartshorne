# Finiteness of projective global sections

## Lean statement

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`.

- `Hartshorne.moduleFinite_globalSections_of_projective`: for a field `k`, a finite-type
  `k`-algebra `A`, `f : X ⟶ Spec A` with `Projective f`, and `F : X.Modules` with `IsCoherent F`,
  `Γ(X, F)` is a finite `A`-module, the `A`-action being restriction of scalars along
  `((Scheme.ΓSpecIso (.of A)).inv ≫ f.appTop).hom`.
- `Hartshorne.finiteDimensional_globalSections_of_projective`: the case `A = k`, as
  `FiniteDimensional k`. The module is definitionally `GlobalSectionsOver f F` (geometric-genus
  article); that name is not used so that this article does not depend on geometric-genus.

## Proof route

1. Corollary 5.16(b) (`projective_iff_exists_iso_proj`): `X ≅ Proj 𝒜'` over `Spec A`,
   `φ : A ≃+* 𝒜' 0`, finitely many degree-one generators; the graded ring is a finite-type
   `k`-algebra. `F` is pushed forward along the isomorphism (coherent by
   `isCoherent_pushforward_of_isFinite`, Exercise 5.5(c)); the `A`-action matches the
   `𝒜' 0`-action through `degreeZeroToGlobal` (the ring map of `Proj.toSpecZero`).
2. `ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite.moduleFinite_sections_of_isCoherent` (coherent `F` on
   `Proj S`): `F ≅ Γ_*(F)~` (`GammaStarReconstruction.gammaStarIso`, Proposition 5.15); each chart
   module `Γ_*(F)_(fᵢ)` is finite over `S_(fᵢ)` (`finite_away_of_finite_sections`, the converse of
   `ProjGradedModuleSheaf.finite_sections_basicOpen`); the graded submodule `M` spanned by the
   numerators of finitely many chart generators (`homogeneousSpan`) is finitely generated and
   `Γ(M~) → Γ(Γ_*(F)~)` is surjective (`exists_sections_of_surjective_charts`).
3. `moduleFinite_sections_of_finite` (finitely generated graded `M`):
   `moduleFinite_sections_of_primeFactors` (graded prime filtration, Proposition I.7.4, plus left
   exactness `exists_sections_of_gradedLocMap_eq_zero` and `moduleFinite_sections_of_exact`) and
   `moduleFinite_sections_primeFactor` (each factor `(S/𝔭)(l)`, by integrality over `S/𝔭`:
   `exists_finset_of_chartCond`, determinant trick on `(S/𝔭)_{≥ N}`, Theorem I.3.9A via
   `moduleFinite_integralClosure_of_finiteType_of_field`, degree projection of coefficients).

## Divergence from the printed route

The finitely generated `M` with `Γ(M~) ↠ Γ(F)` is built from generators of the chart modules
`Γ(D₊(fᵢ), F)` and Proposition 5.15, not from Serre's global generation (Theorem 5.17 /
Corollary 5.18); the Lean does not use `exists_isGeneratedBySections_tensorPowerTwist_of_projective`.
Only surjectivity of `Γ(M~) → Γ(F)`, not `F ≅ M~`, is proved and used.

## Friction and prior art

- The graded prime filtration is stated for `ℤ`-graded algebras with `Submodule R`-valued
  gradings; `projGradedModuleSheaf` uses `ℕ`-graded rings with `SetLike` gradings. The bridge is
  `intGrading` (the grading extended by zero, as `ℤ`-submodules, with `GradedAlgebra`),
  `intModuleGrading`, and `gradedSMul_of_intGrading`. Two `ℤ`-module structures on a homogeneous
  submodule (`AddCommGroup.toIntModule`, `SubmoduleClass.module'`) are not definitionally equal,
  so each filtration factor is transported to `primeFactor` through its graded equivalence.
- `Γ_*(F)` had no `DirectSum.Decomposition` instance; `gammaStarGrading.decomposition` adds one.
- Reused unrecorded helpers: `ProjGradedModuleSheaf.exists_globalValue_eq_mapOfLE`,
  `gradedLocMap`, `projGradedModuleSheafMap`, `ProjectiveSectionRing.IsHomFrac`,
  `IsHomFrac.sub`, `eq_zero_of_sum_isHomFrac_eq_zero` (Exercise 5.14(a) module).
- The determinant-trick helpers of the Exercise 5.14(a) module are stated only for quotients of
  `k[x₀, …, xᵣ]`; this module proves the general graded version (`isIntegral_of_chartCond`).
