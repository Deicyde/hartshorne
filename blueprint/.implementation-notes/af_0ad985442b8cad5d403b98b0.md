# Finiteness of projective global sections (partial)

## Status

Partial. Theorem 5.19 is formalized sorry-free for `X = Proj S` and `F = M~` with `M` a
finitely generated graded module, in the module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`.
The article's statement (arbitrary `X` projective over `A`, arbitrary coherent `F`) is not yet
formalized; nothing is recorded in `lean:`.

## Proved (namespace `Hartshorne.ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`)

Setting: `𝒜 : ℕ → σ` with `GradedRing 𝒜` on `A` (Hartshorne's `S`), `A` a finite-type algebra
over a field `k`, `f : ι → A` a finite family (`Fintype ι`) of degree-one elements with
`Algebra.adjoin (𝒜 0) (Set.range f) = ⊤`. Global sections are an `𝒜 0`-module by restriction
of scalars along `degreeZeroToGlobal 𝒜 : 𝒜 0 →+* Γ(Proj 𝒜, ⊤)` (the ring map of
`Proj.toSpecZero`; pointwise value `degreeZeroToGlobal_val`).

- `moduleFinite_sections_of_finite`: for a graded module `ℳ : ℤ → σM`
  (`SetLike.GradedSMul 𝒜 ℳ`, `DirectSum.Decomposition ℳ`) with `Module.Finite A M`,
  `Γ(Proj 𝒜, projGradedModuleSheaf 𝒜 ℳ)` is a finite `𝒜 0`-module.
- `moduleFinite_sections_of_primeFactors`: the filtration step. Uses
  `exists_gradedPrimeFiltration` for the `ℤ`-graded algebra `intGrading 𝒜` (the grading extended
  by zero, as `ℤ`-submodules; `intGrading.gradedAlgebra`) and the module grading
  `intModuleGrading ℳ`. Factors are `primeFactor 𝒜 p hp l`
  (`gradedModuleTwist (gradedQuotientPiece …) l`, i.e. `(S/𝔭)(l)`).
- `moduleFinite_sections_primeFactor`: finiteness for each factor (the book's last step).
- `exists_sections_of_gradedLocMap_eq_zero`, `moduleFinite_sections_of_exact`,
  `moduleFinite_sections_of_injective`: left exactness of `M ↦ Γ(Proj S, M~)` and finiteness
  along left exact sequences / injective graded maps (`S₀` Noetherian).
- `exists_finset_of_chartCond`, `ChartCond`: the algebraic core (integrality by the determinant
  trick on `S_{≥ ∑ jᵢ}`, finiteness of the integral closure, degree projection of coefficients).
- `moduleFinite_projTwistingSheaf_of_isDomain`: the integral case `Γ(Proj S, 𝒪(n))` for a graded
  domain `S`, all `n : ℤ` (not needed by the general theorem; kept as the direct form of the
  book's `Γ(Y, 𝒪_Y(l))`).

Prior art reused (unrecorded helpers): from `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveSectionRingIntegralClosure`
(Exercise 5.14(a) module) `genericPoint`, `sectionValue`, `sectionValue_add`,
`eq_zero_of_sectionValue_eq_zero`, `toFraction_mk_smul`, `IsHomFrac`, `IsHomFrac.sub`,
`eq_zero_of_sum_isHomFrac_eq_zero` (used only by the integral case and the degree projection);
`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.exists_globalValue_eq_mapOfLE`, `projGradedModuleSheafMap`,
`gradedLocMap`, `gradedQuotient` (recorded on proj-closed-subscheme-truncation, reached through
projective-scheme-as-proj).

Friction: the graded prime filtration is stated for `ℤ`-graded algebras with
`Submodule R`-valued gradings, while `projGradedModuleSheaf` uses `ℕ`-graded rings with
`SetLike` gradings. `intGrading`/`intModuleGrading`/`gradedSMul_of_intGrading` bridge them. Two
`ℤ`-module structures on a homogeneous submodule (`AddCommGroup.toIntModule` and
`SubmoduleClass.module'`) are not definitionally equal, so the filtration factor is transported to
`primeFactor` through the given graded equivalence rather than used directly.

## Remaining goal

The article's statement: for `A` a finite-type `k`-algebra, `f : X ⟶ Spec A` projective
(`Projective f`) and `F : X.Modules` with `IsCoherent F`, `Γ(X, F)` is a finite `A`-module
(restriction of scalars along `((Scheme.ΓSpecIso (.of A)).inv ≫ f.appTop).hom`); over a field,
finite dimensional (`GlobalSectionsOver`).

Next route, following the book:

1. Corollary 5.16(b) (`projective_iff_exists_iso_proj`): `X ≅ Proj 𝒜` over `Spec A` with
   `φ : A ≃+* 𝒜 0` and a finite set of degree-one generators. `𝒜`'s ring is a finite-type
   `k`-algebra (finite-type over `𝒜 0 ≅ A`). Transport `F` along the isomorphism (pushforward
   along an isomorphism preserves `IsCoherent` and global sections); the `A`-action through
   `f` is the `𝒜 0`-action through `degreeZeroToGlobal` composed with `φ`.
2. `F ≅ M~` on `Proj 𝒜` for a finitely generated graded `M` (Hartshorne cites Corollary 5.18 /
   Proposition 5.15): `GammaStarReconstruction.gammaStarIso` gives `F ≅ Γ_*(F)~`. On each chart
   `D₊(fᵢ)`, `Γ(D₊(fᵢ), Γ_*(F)~) ≅ Γ_*(F)_(fᵢ)` (`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.toChartΓ_bijective`)
   is finitely generated over `S_(fᵢ)` (coherence: `forall_affine_finite_of_isCoherent`); write
   generators as `mⱼ / fᵢ^{kⱼ}` with `mⱼ` homogeneous in `Γ_*(F)` and let `M` be the graded
   submodule they span (a homogeneous submodule; grading via `gradedSubmodulePiece` over
   `intGrading`). Then `M~ → Γ_*(F)~` (`projGradedModuleSheafMap` of the inclusion) is injective
   (localization is exact) and bijective on each chart, hence an isomorphism. Alternatively use
   Serre global generation (`exists_isGeneratedBySections_tensorPowerTwist_of_projective`) for
   the generators, as in Corollary 5.18.
3. Conclude with `moduleFinite_sections_of_finite` (isomorphic sheaves have `𝒜 0`-isomorphic
   global sections).
