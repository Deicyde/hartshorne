# Finiteness of projective global sections (partial)

## Status

Partial. The last step of Hartshorne's proof (the integral case) is formalized
sorry-free in the module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`. The
statement of the article is not yet formalized; nothing is recorded in `lean:`.

## Proved (namespace `Hartshorne.ProjectiveGlobalSectionsFinite`)

- `moduleFinite_projTwistingSheaf_of_isDomain`: for a graded domain `S`
  (`𝒜 : ℕ → σ`, `GradedRing 𝒜`, `IsDomain A`) that is a finite-type algebra over a
  field `k` and is generated over `S₀` by a finite family `f : ι → A` of degree-one
  elements, `Γ(Proj S, 𝒪(n))` is a finite `S₀`-module for every `n : ℤ`. The
  `S₀`-module structure is restriction of scalars along `degreeZeroToGlobal`, the
  ring map `S₀ → Γ(Proj S, 𝒪)` of `Proj.toSpecZero` (computed pointwise in
  `degreeZeroToGlobal_val`). This is Hartshorne's `Γ(Y, 𝒪_Y(l))` for the integral
  closed subscheme `Y = Proj(S/𝔭)`.
- `exists_finset_of_chartCond` (pure algebra): for a Noetherian graded domain
  generated over `S₀` by finitely many `fᵢ ∈ S₁` (not all zero), whose integral
  closure in `K = Frac S` is a finite `S`-module, any set `V ⊆ K` of elements
  satisfying `ChartCond 𝒜 f n` (on each chart `D₊(fᵢ)`, `z = a / fᵢ^j` with
  `a ∈ S_{j+n}`) has a finite subset of which every element of `V` is an
  `S₀`-combination.
- `chartCond_pow_mul_sectionValue`, `sectionValue_degreeZeroToGlobal_smul`:
  the value in `K` of a section of `𝒪(n)` (at the generic point), multiplied by
  `f_{i₀}^m` with `n + m = d ≥ 0`, satisfies the chart condition in degree `d`;
  the value map is `S₀`-linear.

Route (Hartshorne's): integrality of each value by the determinant trick on the
ideal `S_{≥ ∑ jᵢ}` (Atiyah–Macdonald 5.1, `isIntegral_of_smul_mem_submodule`),
finiteness of the integral closure (`moduleFinite_integralClosure_of_finiteType_of_field`,
Theorem I.3.9A), Noetherianity, then degree projection of the coefficients using the
independence of homogeneous fractions of distinct degrees. Negative twists are
handled by multiplying by a power of a nonzero generator (injective in `K`).

Prior art reused (unrecorded helpers of the Exercise 5.14(a) module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveSectionRingIntegralClosure`): `genericPoint`,
`sectionValue`, `sectionValue_add`, `eq_zero_of_sectionValue_eq_zero`, `toFraction_mk_smul`,
`IsHomFrac`, `IsHomFrac.sub`, `eq_zero_of_sum_isHomFrac_eq_zero`; and
`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.exists_globalValue_eq_mapOfLE` (chart sections are single
fractions). The determinant-trick helpers there are stated only for quotients of
`k[x₀, …, xᵣ]`; this module proves the general graded version.

## Remaining goal

The article's statement: for `A` a finite-type `k`-algebra, `f : X ⟶ Spec A`
projective and `F` coherent (`IsCoherent F`), `Γ(X, F)` is a finite `A`-module
(restriction of scalars along `(ΓSpecIso A).inv ≫ f.appTop`); over `k`, finite
dimensional (`GlobalSectionsOver`).

Next route, following the book:

1. Corollary 5.16(b) (`projective_iff_exists_iso_proj`): `X ≅ Proj S` over
   `Spec A` with `S₀ ≅ A` and `S` generated by finitely many degree-one elements;
   `S` is then a finite-type `k`-algebra. Transport `F` along the isomorphism
   (pushforward along an isomorphism preserves `IsCoherent` and global sections,
   `A`-linearly).
2. `F ≅ M~` on `Proj S` for a finitely generated graded `S`-module `M`: from
   `GammaStarReconstruction.gammaStarIso` (`F ≅ Γ_*(F)~`) and Serre global generation
   (`exists_isGeneratedBySections_tensorPowerTwist_of_projective`) take `M` the
   submodule of `Γ_*(F)` generated by finitely many sections of `F(n)`; needs that
   `M~ → Γ_*(F)~` is an isomorphism (stalkwise: localization is exact, and the
   generated sections span every stalk of `F(n)`).
3. Filtration (`exists_gradedPrimeFiltration`). Gap: that theorem is stated for
   `ℤ`-graded algebras `𝓐 : ℤ → Submodule R A` with factors
   `gradedModuleTwist (gradedQuotientPiece …) l`, whereas `projGradedModuleSheaf`
   uses an `ℕ`-graded `𝒜 : ℕ → σ` and `ℳ : ℤ → σM` with `SetLike.GradedSMul`; a
   bridge between the two gradings is needed. Then left exactness of
   `M ↦ Γ(Proj S, M~)` on graded short exact sequences (localization is exact;
   a section of `M~` locally in the image of `M'` is locally a fraction of `M'`
   after multiplying by a homogeneous element outside the point) reduces to
   `M = (S/𝔭)(l)`, since `A` is Noetherian.
4. `Γ(Proj S, ((S/𝔭)(l))~) ≅ Γ(Proj (S/𝔭), 𝒪(l))`, compatibly with
   `S₀ → (S/𝔭)₀` (surjective, so finiteness over `(S/𝔭)₀` gives finiteness over
   `A`): the points of `Proj (S/𝔭)` are the points of `Proj S` containing `𝔭`, the
   localizations agree there and vanish elsewhere. If `𝔭 ⊇ S₊`, both sides are
   zero. Then apply `moduleFinite_projTwistingSheaf_of_isDomain` to `S/𝔭` (a
   finite-type `k`-algebra domain generated by the images of the `fᵢ`).
