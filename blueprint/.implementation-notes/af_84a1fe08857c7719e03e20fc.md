## Remaining goal

The isomorphism `relativeSegreSource 1 1 (Spec (.of k)) ≅ quadricSurface k`
whose composite with a closed immersion `Q → ℙ³_{Spec k}` (with
`ℙ³_{Spec k} = projectiveSpaceOverScheme 3 (Spec k)`, the target of
`relativeSegre 1 1 (Spec k)`) is `relativeSegre 1 1 (Spec k)`, after the
coordinate reordering `(x, y, z, w) = (z₀₀, z₁₁, z₀₁, z₁₀)`.

Routes considered:

1. Kernels. `GeneratedProj.toProjectiveSpace` (module
   `Hartshorne.Scheme.GeneratedProjProjective`) with `v l = s_i u_j` for
   `l = segreIndex 1 1 (i, j)` gives a closed immersion `Q → ℙ³_{Spec 𝒬₀}`;
   compose with `projectiveSpaceOverSchemeMap 3` along `Spec 𝒬₀ ≅ Spec k`.
   Then Mathlib's `IsClosedImmersion.lift` / `IsClosedImmersion.isIso_lift`
   reduce the iso to equality of the two kernels as ideal sheaves on
   `ℙ³_{Spec k}`, to be checked on the charts `D₊(z_l) ×_ℤ Spec k`; the chart
   rings of the fibre products (tensor products) have no explicit description in
   the project yet, which is the main cost.
2. Morphism `Q → ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k`. Glue the two projections `Q → ℙ¹_ℤ` from the
   partial Proj maps of `x_i ↦ s_i u_0` (on `D₊(u₀)`) and `x_i ↦ s_i u_1` (on
   `D₊(u₁)`), lift to the fibre product, and show it is an isomorphism chart by
   chart (`quadricChartAwayEquiv` against `segreChartSpec`); also needs the
   chart rings of `ℙ¹_k ×_k ℙ¹_k` as polynomial rings.

A comparison `Proj k[x₀, …, xₙ] ≅ projectiveSpaceOverScheme n (Spec k)` (the
article's unlinked "Proof depends on" bullet) does not exist in the project and
would let `quadricSurfaceι` be compared with `relativeSegre` directly.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.quadricSurface k` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurface`):
  `Proj` of `Hartshorne.QuadricRing k`, the monoid algebra over the exponent
  pairs `(e, g)` of bidegree `(d, d)` (`quadricExponents`), graded by `d`
  (`quadricGrading`). `quadricChart k i j` is the degree-one monomial `sᵢuⱼ`,
  and `quadricChartAwayEquiv` identifies each chart `D₊(sᵢuⱼ)` with
  `k[X₀, X₁]`. This is the Segre ring of the article; the coordinates are
  `x = s₀u₀`, `y = s₁u₁`, `z = s₀u₁`, `w = s₁u₀`.
- `Hartshorne.relativeSegre r s Y`, `Hartshorne.relativeSegre_spec`,
  `relativeSegreSource`, `relativeSegre_fst`, `relativeSegre_isPullback`, the
  charts `segreChartSpec` and their closed-immersion instance (module
  `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeSegre`). The source is
  `ℙʳ_Y ×_Y ℙˢ_Y` with `ℙⁿ_Y = ℙⁿ_ℤ ×_ℤ Y` (`projectiveSpaceOverScheme`).
  The coordinate index is `segreIndex`, lexicographic: `z₀₀, z₀₁, z₁₀, z₁₁`.
- `Hartshorne.isClosedImmersion_projPartialMap_comp_top_of_surjective`
  (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjSurjectionClosedImmersion`): a surjective
  graded map `S → T` gives a closed immersion `Proj T → Proj S`. Also
  `Hartshorne.projQuotientι` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjClosedSubschemeTruncation`).
- `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceScheme` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveSpace`)
  for `ℙ³_k = Proj k[x₀, …, x₃]`.
- The classical `Hartshorne.segre_embedding` is about classical points over an
  algebraically closed field and is not used by the Lean proof; the article
  links it under Informally uses (the quadric leaf therefore no longer reaches
  it, which is fine: its own Lean does not use it either).

## Candidate declarations (names are suggestions)

A new module, say `Hartshorne.Scheme.QuadricSurfaceSegre`, importing
`QuadricSurface`, `RelativeSegre` and `ProjSurjectionClosedImmersion`:

1. `Hartshorne.quadricCoordinateHom : MvPolynomial (Fin 4) k →ₐ[k] QuadricRing k`,
   `X 0, X 1, X 2, X 3 ↦ s₀u₀, s₁u₁, s₀u₁, s₁u₀`, degree-preserving;
   `quadricCoordinateHom_surjective`;
   `ker_quadricCoordinateHom : RingHom.ker _ = Ideal.span {X 0 * X 1 - X 2 * X 3}`
   (dimension count `(d+1)²` in each degree, or a direct normal-form argument);
   packaged as a graded `k`-algebra equivalence
   `quadricRingEquivQuotient : (MvPolynomial (Fin 4) k ⧸ Ideal.span {…}) ≃ₐ[k] QuadricRing k`.
2. `Hartshorne.quadricSurfaceι : quadricSurface k ⟶ projectiveSpaceScheme k 3`,
   with `IsClosedImmersion` from the surjection in 1.
3. `Hartshorne.quadricSurfaceIsoSegreSource :
   quadricSurface k ≅ relativeSegreSource 1 1 (Spec (.of k))` together with
   the compatibility of `quadricSurfaceIsoSegreSource.hom ≫ relativeSegre 1 1 _`
   with `quadricSurfaceι`, up to the identification of `ℙ³_{Spec k}` with
   `projectiveSpaceScheme k 3` and the coordinate reordering
   `(x, y, z, w) = (z₀₀, z₁₁, z₀₁, z₁₀)`. Chartwise: `quadricChartAwayEquiv`
   against `segreChartSpec`.
4. Convenience: the two projections `quadricSurface k ⟶ ℙ¹_k` obtained from
   3 and `relativeSegre_fst`/the second projection. These are what the quadric
   class-group leaf needs for "pullback from the two factors".

Universe care: `relativeSegre` lives in `Scheme.{u}` and `quadricSurface` is
`Scheme.{u}` for `k : Type u`.
