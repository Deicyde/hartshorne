# Twisting a graded algebra by a line bundle

## Status

Formalized (candidate, pending review). The construction of `S · L` (with `Sym^d L ≅ L^{⊗ d}` for
invertible `L`), the global `X`-isomorphism `φ : Proj_X(S · L) ≅ Proj_X(S)`, and the formula
`𝒪_{Proj_X(S·L)}(1) ≅ φ^* 𝒪_{Proj_X S}(1) ⊗ π^* L` (under `S.GeneratedInDegreeOne`, the part of
(†) the proof uses) are sorry-free. Candidate `lean:`: `lineBundleTwist`, `lineBundleTwistIso`,
`lineBundleTwistIso_hom_relProjToBase`, `nonempty_relTwistingSheaf_one_iso` (namespace
`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`) and
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.tensorPowerIsoSymmetricPower`.

## Modelling choice

`(S · L)_d` is modelled as the image of `S(U)_d ⊗ Γ(U, Sym^d L)` on affine `U`, i.e. `L^d` is
taken to be `Sym^d L`; `tensorPowerIsoSymmetricPower` identifies this with `L^{⊗ d}` canonically
(rebuilding `S · L` from tensor powers would need the tensor-algebra multiplication as a graded
algebra on affine sections, which is more work for no gain).

## Gluing infrastructure

`Hartshorne.DirectedModuleGluing.Data.liftIso` (in `BlowupExceptionalIdeal.lean`) is the gluing
lemma needed: a module with isomorphisms to the pieces of a descent datum, compatible with the
transitions (`Data.CompatibleIsos`), is isomorphic to the glued module. It would be better placed
in `ModuleGluingDirected.lean`. A generic "isomorphisms of modules glue over a locally directed
cover" lemma (descent datum of the restrictions `G|_{U_i}`) was attempted; its cocycle condition
reduces to composites of `M.presheaf.map (eqToHom _)` whose intermediate objects are only
definitionally equal, and `eqToHom_trans`/`Functor.map_comp` do not fire syntactically
(`erw` times out), so it was dropped in favour of using `liftIso` directly.

Attempted, not landed: the descent datum `framedTwistData : DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr
(framedCover hL A)` (`M U := A.twistingSheaf n U.obj`, `tr f := Proj.map (A.restrict f.hom) _`,
`tr_eq f := (framedCover_trans A f).symm`, `θ f := A.twistTransition n f.hom`, cocycle from
`twistTransitionHom_comp` via a `subst`-generalized copy taking `h = f ≫ g` with
`ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory.comp_hom`), and
`relTwistingSheafFramedIso : A.relTwistingSheaf n ≅ (framedTwistData hL A n).glue` by
`Data.liftIso` from `(A.twistData n).ψ_compat f.hom _` (the extra factor
`(restrictFunctorCongr (tr_eq f)).inv.app _` is the identity since `tr f` and
`(framedCover hL A).trans f` are definitionally equal). It elaborates, but the kernel needs about
90 s for the data fields of `framedTwistData` (even with every proof field `sorry`), 90 s more for
`θ_comp`, and times out on the closing `rfl` of the compatibility proof. Each field checks
quickly in isolation (`twistTransition` at framed indices, `(framedCover hL A).X U`), a first try
at defining the framed cover's pieces through `A.relativeGluingData.cover` was inconclusive (it
did not elaborate as written); the cause is not found. A route around it: state the descent datum and compatibility for a general full
subcategory of `X.AffineZariskiSite` closed under restriction (with the cover built from
`A.relativeGluingData.cover`), or prove the compatibility with `Scheme.Modules.hom_ext` on
sections instead of `rfl`.
