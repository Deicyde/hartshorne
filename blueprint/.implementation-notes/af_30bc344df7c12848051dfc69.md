# Twisting a graded algebra by a line bundle

## Status

Not formalized. A first bounded attempt found that the statement needs
several constructions the project does not yet have; no Lean was committed.

## Encoding constraints

Relative Proj takes a `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X`: graded rings
of sections `S(U)` over the affine opens of `X` (the small affine Zariski site),
an `𝒪_X`-algebra structure in degree zero, and the condition
`S(D(r)) = S(U)_r` (`NatTrans.Coequifibered`). An invertible sheaf is an
`X.Modules` object satisfying `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible`
(or `Hartshorne.Ringed.IsInvertible`). So `S · L` has to be built as a
`QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X` from `S` and `L`.

## Missing pieces (in dependency order)

1. Sections of a quasi-coherent module along basic opens. For `L` invertible
   (hence quasi-coherent) and an affine `U`, `Γ(L, D(r))` is the localization
   of `Γ(L, U)` at `r`, functorially on `X.AffineZariskiSite`. Invertible
   implies quasi-coherent exists for the ringed-space notion
   (`Hartshorne.Ringed.IsInvertible.isFiniteLocallyFree`, then
   `Hartshorne.Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree.isQuasicoherent`), but no bridge from
   `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible` was found. The functorial
   localization statement was not found packaged for reuse.
2. The affine graded ring `(S · M)` for a graded `A`-algebra `S` and an
   invertible `A`-module `M`, with `(S · M)_d = S_d ⊗_A M^{⊗d}`.
   Commutativity of `⊕_d M^{⊗d}` needs `M` invertible: Mathlib's
   `TensorPower` graded ring is not commutative in general. One workable
   encoding is the diagonal subring `⊕_d S_d ⊗ Sym^d M` of
   `S ⊗_A Sym_A M`, together with `Sym^d M ≅ M^{⊗d}` for invertible `M`.
   Mathlib's `SymmetricAlgebra` has no grading yet.
3. `S · L` as a `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra`: restriction maps, and
   coequifibered from 1 and base change of the construction in 2.
4. The local isomorphism and its independence (the article's Proof depends on
   bullet). For a generator `t` of a free rank-one `M`, the graded iso
   `S ≅ S · M`, `s ↦ s ⊗ t^d`. Changing `t` to `u t` (with `u` a unit)
   composes with `σ_u : s_d ↦ u^d s_d`. Then:
   - `Proj.map σ_u = 𝟙`, via `Proj.awayι_comp_map` on the cover
     `Proj.affineOpenCover`, since `σ_u` is the identity on every
     `HomogeneousLocalization.Away`.
   - On `𝒪(1)` (`projTwistingSheaf _ 1`), `σ_u` acts as multiplication by
     `u`.
5. The global `X`-isomorphism `Proj_X(S · L) ≅ Proj_X(S)`. Natural
   isomorphisms of the gluing functors (`QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.projFunctor`)
   over `projToSpec` would give it through `HasColimit.isoOfNatIso`, as in the
   functoriality of relative Spec (`Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentAlgebra.relSpecIso`).
   But `L` is not trivial on every affine open, so the per-`U` isomorphism
   must itself be glued from basic opens trivializing `L`, using 4.
6. `𝒪_{Proj(S·L)}(1) ≅ φ^* 𝒪_{Proj S}(1) ⊗ π^* L`. Glue local module
   isomorphisms with `Hartshorne.DirectedModuleGluing` (as in
   `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.twistData`), using the transition factor from 4
   to match `π^* L`'s transition functions.

## Suggested split (Roadmap)

Pieces 2–3 (the twisted graded algebra) and 4 (independence of the local Proj
isomorphism, with its `𝒪(1)` factor) are self-contained statements. Each
would be a natural separate node feeding this one.
