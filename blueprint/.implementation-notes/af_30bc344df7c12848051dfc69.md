# Twisting a graded algebra by a line bundle

## Status

Partial. The construction of `S · L`, the local isomorphisms with their independence of the
trivialization and their naturality, and the global `X`-isomorphism
`φ : Proj_X(S · L) ≅ Proj_X(S)` are formalized, sorry-free. The twisting-sheaf formula
`𝒪_{Proj(S·L)}(1) ≅ φ^* 𝒪_{Proj S}(1) ⊗ π^* L` is not yet formalized, so no `lean:` is recorded.

## Lean (all sorry-free, axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound)

Affine algebra, `Hartshorne/Scheme/SegreProductGradedAlgebra.lean` (namespace
`Hartshorne.SegreProduct`):

- `component`, `diagonal`, `grading`, `gradedRing`, `coe_decompose`: for graded `A`-algebras
  `S`, `T` with `A` in degree zero, the subalgebra `⊕_d S_d ⊗_A T_d` of `S ⊗_A T` with its grading.
- `tensorMap`, `diagonalMap`, `diagonalMap_id`, `diagonalMap_comp`: functoriality along graded
  maps over `A → A'`.
- `isLocalizationAway_diagonalMap`: if `A' = A[1/r]`, `S' = S[1/r]`, `T' = T[1/r]`, then the
  diagonal product of the primed rings is the localization at `r` (the quasi-coherence condition).

Local isomorphism, `Hartshorne/Scheme/SegreProductTrivialization.lean`:

- `ofGenerator` (`s ↦ s ⊗ t^d`), `ofGenerator_bijective` when every `T_d` is free on `t^d`
  (hypothesis `∀ d, ∀ x ∈ T_d, ∃! a, a • t^d = x`), `projIsoOfGenerator` (via the project's
  `projIsoOfBijective`).
- `scale` (`σ_u : s ↦ u^d s`), `ofGenerator_smul`, `projMap_scale` (`Proj.map σ_u = 𝟙` for a unit

- `diagonalGradedMap`, `diagonalMap_ofGenerator`, `projIsoOfGenerator_naturality` (compatibility
  with base change, e.g. restriction to a basic open).
- `existsUnique_smul_pow_of_bijective`: a bijective graded `A[x] → T` gives a generator.

## Modelling choice to review

`(S · L)_d` is modelled as the image of `S(U)_d ⊗ Γ(U, Sym^d L)` on affine `U`, i.e. `L^d` is
taken to be `Sym^d L`. For invertible `L` this is canonically `L^{⊗ d}`, but the project has no
lemma `Sym^d L ≅ L^{⊗ d}` yet (`Hartshorne.epi_moduleSheafTensorToSymmetricPower` gives the
surjection `T^d L → Sym^d L`; injectivity can be checked on a frame, where both sides are free of
rank one). A reviewer may want that comparison, or `S · L` rebuilt from tensor powers, before
the statement is accepted.

## Remaining goal

`𝒪_{Proj_X(S·L)}(1) ≅ (pullback φ.hom) 𝒪_{Proj_X S}(1) ⊗ (pullback π) L`, with `𝒪(1)` the
relative twisting sheaf `relTwistingSheaf 1` and `π = (S.lineBundleTwist L hL).relProjToBase`.

Route:

1. Over a framed affine `U` with frame `e`, under `framedIso U`:
   `𝒪_{Proj (S·L)(U)}(1) ≅ (Proj.map (ofGenerator t))^* 𝒪_{Proj S(U)}(1)` from
   `Hartshorne.pullbackProjMapTwistIso` (this needs `S` generated in degree one, so take `S` from
   `RelativeProjData`, which is Hartshorne's (†)), and `π^* L|_U ≅ 𝒪` from the frame.
2. Compute how these local isomorphisms change with the frame: replacing `e` by `u e` changes the
   first by multiplication by `u` (`σ_u` acts on `𝒪(1)` by `u`) and the trivialization of `L` by
   `u⁻¹`, so their tensor product does not depend on the frame. This is the article's "transition
   factor" bullet.
3. Glue the local module isomorphisms over `framedCover`. The project has no lemma for gluing
   morphisms of sheaves of modules on an open cover; `Hartshorne.DirectedModuleGluing` glues
   modules but has no universal property for maps. Either prove such a lemma (morphisms of
   sheaves of modules form a sheaf) or show both sides are the glued module of the same descent
   datum `twistData` up to the local isomorphisms.
