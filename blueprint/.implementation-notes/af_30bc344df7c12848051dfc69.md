# Twisting a graded algebra by a line bundle

## Status

Partial. The construction of `S · L` (with `Sym^d L ≅ L^{⊗ d}` for invertible `L`), the local
isomorphisms with their independence of the trivialization and their naturality, and the global
`X`-isomorphism `φ : Proj_X(S · L) ≅ Proj_X(S)` are formalized, sorry-free. Towards the
twisting-sheaf formula: `φ^* 𝒪_{Proj S}(n)` restricts on each framed piece to
`𝒪_{Proj (S·L)(U)}(n)`. For the formula `𝒪_{Proj(S·L)}(1) ≅ φ^* 𝒪_{Proj S}(1) ⊗ π^* L` the
frames-and-transition-functions route below is set up (frames on a cover, restriction of charts,
base scalars on the pieces); the same-piece transition computation and the final assembly remain,
so no `lean:` is recorded.

`Hartshorne.SegreProduct`):

- `component`, `diagonal`, `grading`, `gradedRing`, `coe_decompose`: for graded `A`-algebras
  `S`, `T` with `A` in degree zero, the subalgebra `⊕_d S_d ⊗_A T_d` of `S ⊗_A T` with its grading.
- `tensorMap`, `diagonalMap`, `diagonalMap_id`, `diagonalMap_comp`: functoriality along graded

- `isLocalizationAway_diagonalMap`: if `A' = A[1/r]`, `S' = S[1/r]`, `T' = T[1/r]`, then the

## Modelling choice

`(S · L)_d` is modelled as the image of `S(U)_d ⊗ Γ(U, Sym^d L)` on affine `U`, i.e. `L^d` is
taken to be `Sym^d L`; `tensorPowerIsoSymmetricPower` identifies this with `L^{⊗ d}` canonically
(rebuilding `S · L` from tensor powers would need the tensor-algebra multiplication as a graded
algebra on affine sections, which is more work for no gain).

## Gluing infrastructure

`Hartshorne.DirectedModuleGluing.Data.liftIso` (in `BlowupExceptionalIdeal.lean`) is the gluing
lemma needed: a module with isomorphisms to the pieces of a descent datum, compatible with the
transitions (`Data.CompatibleIsos`), is isomorphic to the glued module. It would be better placed
in `ModuleGluingDirected.lean`. A generic "isomorphisms of modules glue over a locally directed
cover" lemma (descent datum of the restrictions `G|_{U_i}`) was attempted; its cocycle condition
reduces to composites of `M.presheaf.map (eqToHom _)` whose intermediate objects are only
definitionally equal, and `eqToHom_trans`/`Functor.map_comp` do not fire syntactically
(`erw` times out), so it was dropped in favour of using `liftIso` directly.

Attempted, not landed: the descent datum `framedTwistData : DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr
(framedCover hL A)` (`M U := A.twistingSheaf n U.obj`, `tr f := Proj.map (A.restrict f.hom) _`,
`tr_eq f := (framedCover_trans A f).symm`, `θ f := A.twistTransition n f.hom`, cocycle from
`twistTransitionHom_comp` via a `subst`-generalized copy taking `h = f ≫ g` with
`ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory.comp_hom`), and
`relTwistingSheafFramedIso : A.relTwistingSheaf n ≅ (framedTwistData hL A n).glue` by
`Data.liftIso` from `(A.twistData n).ψ_compat f.hom _` (the extra factor
`(restrictFunctorCongr (tr_eq f)).inv.app _` is the identity since `tr f` and
`(framedCover hL A).trans f` are definitionally equal). It elaborates, but the kernel needs about
90 s for the data fields of `framedTwistData` (even with every proof field `sorry`), 90 s more for
`θ_comp`, and times out on the closing `rfl` of the compatibility proof. Each field checks
quickly in isolation (`twistTransition` at framed indices, `(framedCover hL A).X U`), a first try
at defining the framed cover's pieces through `A.relativeGluingData.cover` was inconclusive (it
did not elaborate as written); the cause is not found. A route around it: state the descent datum and compatibility for a general full
subcategory of `X.AffineZariskiSite` closed under restriction (with the cover built from
`A.relativeGluingData.cover`), or prove the compatibility with `Scheme.Modules.hom_ext` on
sections instead of `rfl`.

## Remaining goal

`Nonempty (𝒪_{Proj_X(S·L)}(1) ≅ moduleSheafTensor ((pullback φ.hom).obj 𝒪_{Proj_X S}(1))
((pullback π).obj L))`, `𝒪(1) = relTwistingSheaf 1`, `π = (S.lineBundleTwist L hL).relProjToBase`,
under `S.GeneratedInDegreeOne` (part of Hartshorne's (†)). Route (frames file):

1. Same-piece transition (`i = ⟨V, e, he, s, hs⟩`, `j = ⟨V, e', he', t, ht⟩`): on `i.opens`,
   `MT|(pieceDegreeOneSection (j.gen_mem)) = c • aSec i` is `pieceDegreeOneSection_eq_smul` with
   `c = ((projι V).appIso _).inv (awayToSection g (g'/g))`, `g = gen_i s`, `g' = gen_j t`. For the
   `b`'s: write `e' default = v • e default` (`exists_eq_smul_section` on
   `IsTrivializingSection.of_isLocalFrame he`), `σ_t = c_S • σ_s` on the image of `D₊(s)`
   (`pieceDegreeOneSection_eq_smul` for `S`), push through `Ringed.etaSec_restrict`,
   `Ringed.etaSec_smul`, `Scheme.Modules.map_smul`, bilinearity of `moduleSheafTensorBilinear`, to
   get `bTop j| = (rφ * rπ) • bSec i` with `rφ = φ^# c_S`, `rπ = π^# v` restricted. Then show
   `c = rφ * rπ`: apply `(projι V).appIso _ .hom`; `appIso_hom_map_app`, `appLE_eq_of_eq`
   (`projι_lineBundleTwistHom_eq`), `Scheme.Hom.appLE_comp_appLE`, `Scheme.Hom.appIso_hom'`,
   Mathlib's `Proj.awayToSection_comp_appLE` give `φ`-part `awayToSection g (Away.map gen (t/s))`;
   `projι_relProjToBase_appLE` gives `π`-part `awayToSection g (v/1)`; finally
   `g' = (v/1)·gen_i t` in `Away g` from `frameGenerator e' = unitRingHom v * frameGenerator e`
   (`chartRingHom_X`, `symDegreeOne_smul`) and `TensorProduct.tmul_smul`.
2. Overlaps: for `y ∈ i.opens ⊓ j.opens`, `exists_basicOpen_le_affine_inter` on `π y` gives an
   affine `V` basic in both `i.U` and `j.U`, `f : V ⟶ i.U`, `f' : V ⟶ j.U`; `y` lies in the
   piece over `V` (`relProjToBase_preimage`), hence in `(i.restrictChart f).opens` and
   `(j.restrictChart f').opens` (`mem_opens_restrictChart`). On `O` = their intersection combine
   `restrict_pieceDegreeOneSection_gen`/`restrict_bTop` (restriction, `c = 1`) with step 1 for the
   two restricted charts (same `V`), merging restrictions with `Scheme.Modules.map_map_homOfLE`.
3. Assemble with `nonempty_iso_of_isTrivializingSection_local` (cover `iSup_opens`, frames
   `isTrivializingSection_aSec`/`bSec`), instantiate `MT, αT, MS, αS` with the relative twisting
   sheaves (`pieceIsosCompatible_relTwistingSheaf`), and note `φ.hom = lineBundleTwistHom` by `rfl`.
