# A generic finite-module frame

## Status

In progress in `Hartshorne/Cohomology/ChevalleyGenericFrame.lean`. The library root does not
import it.

## Statement as formalized

`Hartshorne.exists_genericFiniteModuleFrame`: for a finite surjective `f : X ⟶ Y` of Noetherian
schemes with `Y` integral, there are:
- a coherent `M`;
- `r > 0`;
- `α : 𝒪_Y^{(ULift (Fin r))} ⟶ f_* M`, whose stalk map at the generic point of `Y` is an
  isomorphism.

Integrality of `X` is not used, so it is dropped.

## Proof route

1. `f_* 𝒪_X` is coherent, and its stalk at `η` is free (a vector space over the function field).
   So it is free on an affine open `W ∋ η` (`exists_isFree_restrict_of_free_stalk`). Its sections
   `B` over `W` are a finite free `A = Γ(W)`-module (`exists_affine_free`).
2. Take a basis `bᵢ` of `B`. These are global sections of `E = j_* (𝒪_X|_U)` with `U = f⁻¹ W`.
   `M` is their span `spanSheaf` in `E`, which is coherent (`isCoherent_spanSheaf`). `α` is
   `frameMap`.
3. Over `W`:
   - `restrictFreeIso_inv_comp_map_freeHomEquiv_symm` turns `α|_W` into the map given by the
     restricted sections;
   - `freeHomEquiv_symm_isIso_of_basis` shows it is an isomorphism once those sections form a
     basis of `Γ(W, f_* M) = M(U)`;
   - this holds because `M(U) ⊆ E(U) = B`, and that inclusion is onto since it contains the
     basis (`isIso_restrict_frameMap`).
4. Take the stalk at `η` through `Scheme.Modules.restrictStalkNatIso`.
