# Flasque sheaves are acyclic

## Implementation guidance

State it in the derived-functor model of the `sheaf-cohomology` article:
for `F : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X` with `[F.IsFlasque]` and `n : ℕ`,
`IsZero ((sheafCohomology X (n + 1)).obj F)` (equivalently
`Hartshorne.IsRightAcyclic (sheafSectionsOver ⊤) F`).

The proof needs no Ext comparison: the long exact sequence is
`Hartshorne.rightDerivedδ` / `Hartshorne.rightDerived_exact₁` (the roadmap
revision repointed the proof dependency from the Ext-model long-exact-sequence
article to the right-derived one). The dimension-shifting lemma
`Hartshorne.isRightAcyclic_of_shortExact_injective`
(`Hartshorne/Cohomology/CohomologyWithSupports.lean`) together with
`Hartshorne.injectiveAbelianSheaf_isFlasque`,
`TopCat.Sheaf.IsFlasque.of_shortExact_of_isFlasque₁₂` and surjectivity of
global sections on a short exact sequence with flasque kernel is the whole
argument; compare `Hartshorne.isRightAcyclic_supportedSections_of_isFlasque`,
which proves the supported analogue the same way.
