# The largest ideal of definition: implementation note

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.LargestIdealOfDefinition`.

## Declarations

- `Hartshorne.vanishingIdealSubsheaf X`: for any locally ringed space `X`, the ideal subsheaf of
  sections whose germ at every point lies in the maximal ideal of the stalk. This is the Lean
  `J_red`; it is defined intrinsically (no charts), so it needs no gluing.
- `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.isGreatest_vanishingIdealSubsheaf X`: `J_red` is the greatest
  element of the set of ideals of definition, and for every ideal of definition `J`,
  `IsReduced h.quotientScheme ↔ J = J_red`. Uniqueness of the largest ideal of definition is
  `IsGreatest.unique`.
- `Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition.isIdealOfDefinition_vanishingIdealSubsheaf X`: `J_red` is an
  ideal of definition (so ideals of definition exist).
- Supporting public lemmas: `mem_vanishingIdealSubsheaf_iff`,
  `sheafSupport_quotientAb_vanishingIdealSubsheaf`,
  `IsIdealOfDefinition.le_vanishingIdealSubsheaf_of_support_eq_univ`,
  `IsIdealOfDefinition.le_vanishingIdealSubsheaf`, `IsIdealOfDefinition.isReduced_quotientScheme_iff`.

## Route

1. `J ⊆ J_red` for every ideal subsheaf with `Supp (𝒪_X / J) = X`, and the reducedness
   characterization: on `(X, 𝒪_X / J)` the image of a section of `J_red` has non-unit germs, hence
   empty basic open, hence is zero when the scheme is reduced (Mathlib `basicOpen_eq_bot_iff`,
   the reduced form of Hartshorne II, Exercise 2.18(a)); conversely nilpotents of `𝒪_X / J_red`
   lift locally to sections with a power in `J_red`, and maximal ideals are prime.
2. Existence. A morphism of locally ringed spaces `g : W ⟶ X` from a reduced scheme kills `J_red`
   (`LocallyRingedSpace.preimage_basicOpen` plus `basicOpen_eq_bot_iff`), so it factors through
   `(X, 𝒪_X / J_red)` via `sheafifyLift`. When `g` is an open embedding with surjective stalk maps,
   the factorization has bijective stalk maps, hence is an open immersion
   (`SheafedSpace.IsOpenImmersion.of_stalk_iso`). Such charts `Spec R ⟶ X` with `R` reduced
   Noetherian through every point make `(X, 𝒪_X / J_red)` a locally ringed space and a scheme
   (`LocallyRingedSpace.IsOpenImmersion.scheme`), locally Noetherian by
   `isLocallyNoetherian_of_affine_cover`; quasi-compactness of a Noetherian formal scheme gives
   `IsNoetherian`.
3. Charts on a Noetherian formal scheme: on a chart `U ≅ Ŷ = I.formalCompletion`, take
   `Z = reducedInducedSubscheme ⊤` of `Y_1`, the closed immersion `Z ⟶ Y_1` (a homeomorphism with
   surjective stalk maps), the identification of `Y_1` with the restriction carrying the target of
   `π₀`, and the morphism `Y_1 ⟶ Ŷ` with identity base and sheaf map `π₀`, whose stalk maps are
   surjective by `isLocallySurjective_formalCompletionπ`. Compose with `fromSpec` of an affine open
   of `Z`.

## Differences from the article's proof sketch

The article's proof dependencies describe a local construction on affine formal charts glued by
uniqueness. Here `J_red` is defined globally by pointwise vanishing, which makes
the gluing step unnecessary; the local work is only to show that `(X, 𝒪_X / J_red)` is locally
the reduced induced subscheme of `Y_1`. The cofinality statement of Proposition 9.5(a) is not used.
