# Localizations of regular local rings

## Status

Not formalized. The general statement needs homological infrastructure that
neither the pinned Mathlib nor the project provides. The faithful target is

`[IsRegularLocalRing A] (p : Ideal A) [p.IsPrime] : IsRegularLocalRing (Localization.AtPrime p)`

with Mathlib's `IsRegularLocalRing`. The pinned Mathlib defines regular rings by
regularity of all prime localizations and lists "regular local implies regular"
as a TODO in `Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean`.

## Partial progress (sorry-free, not recorded in the frontmatter)

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RegularLocalization` proves the cases
that need no homological algebra:

- `𝔭 = 𝔪` (`A_𝔪 ≅ A`): `isRegularLocalRing_localization_atPrime_maximalIdeal`;
- `𝔭 = 0` (a regular local ring is a domain, so `A_(0)` is a field):
  `isRegularLocalRing_localization_atPrime_of_eq_bot`;
- `dim A ≤ 1` (every prime is then `0` or `𝔪`):
  `isRegularLocalRing_localization_atPrime_of_ringKrullDim_le_one`.

All are in namespace `Hartshorne.RegularLocalization`. They use the
domain property from the regular-local-implies-Cohen–Macaulay module, which no
article records as a Lean target.

## Route (Serre)

1. A regular local ring has finite global dimension. In particular
   `pd_A(A/𝔭) < ∞`.
2. Projective dimension does not increase under localization, so
   `pd_{A_𝔭}(κ(𝔭)) < ∞`.
3. A Noetherian local ring whose residue field has finite projective
   dimension is regular.

## Available in the pinned Mathlib

- Projective dimension in abelian categories, `HasProjectiveDimensionLT` and
  its Ext criterion (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/Projective/Dimension.lean`).
- Behaviour under localization: `projectiveDimension_le_projectiveDimension_of_isLocalizedModule`
  and `projectiveDimension_eq_iSup_localizedModule_prime`
  (`Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalProperties/ProjectiveDimension.lean`). This covers step 2.
- Behaviour under quotients by regular sequences:
  `ModuleCat.projectiveDimension_quotient_eq_length` and
  `projectiveDimension_quotSMulTop_eq_succ_of_isSMulRegular`
  (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/ProjectiveDimension.lean`). With a regular
  system of parameters that is a regular sequence generating `𝔪`, this gives
  `pd_A(k) = dim A`.
- `hasProjectiveDimensionLT_of_forall_finite`: over a Noetherian ring, a finite
  module's projective dimension is detected by Ext against finite modules.

## Missing infrastructure (candidate prerequisite articles)

- Step 1: the global dimension of a regular local ring. The roadmap already has
  this as `cohomology/ext-groups/homological-dimension/regular-local-global-dimension`
  (not ready). Only the finitely generated case is needed here. One route is
  `pd_A(k) = dim A` (the roadmap's `residue-field-projective-dimension`, not
  ready), plus "for a finite module `M` over a Noetherian local ring,
  `pd M ≤ n` iff `Ext^{n+1}(M, k) = 0`", plus the bound `Ext^{n+1}(M, k) = 0`
  for `n ≥ pd k`. That bound needs either balanced Tor
  (`Tor_i(M, k) ≅ Tor_i(k, M)`) or minimal free resolutions. Mathlib has neither
  in a usable form.
- Step 3, not in the roadmap: Serre's converse. A Noetherian local ring `B`
  with `pd_B(k) < ∞` (equivalently, finite global dimension) is regular.
  Matsumura, Theorem 19.2, needs:
  - minimal free resolutions, with Betti numbers read off from `Ext(-, k)`;
  - `depth B > 0` when `pd_B(k) < ∞` and `dim B > 0`;
  - Nagata's lemma: for a non-zero-divisor `x ∈ 𝔪 \ 𝔪²`, `k` is a direct
    summand of `𝔪/x𝔪`, so `pd_{B/xB}(k) < ∞`;
  - induction through the project's lift lemma, which says regularity of
    `B/xB` gives regularity of `B`.

  An alternative route uses the Koszul complex, which Mathlib also lacks.

## Elementary extension not attempted

When `𝔭` is generated by part of a regular system of parameters, the quotients
by initial segments are regular local domains. They form a chain of primes of
length `r` below `𝔭`, so `ht 𝔭 = r` and `A_𝔭` is regular. This is
straightforward from the project's quotient lemma but does not reach the
general case.
