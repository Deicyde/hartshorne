# Pullback of divisors along finite curve morphisms

## Partial progress

The Lean module `Hartshorne.Scheme.FiniteCurveDivisorPullback` is complete and
sorry-free. It has passed independent review for faithfulness and proof
integrity, but it is not yet recorded in this article's frontmatter. All
declarations live in the namespace `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme`:

- `IsNonsingularCurve` (Hartshorne II.6, p. 136): over `k`, separated, locally
  of finite type, quasi-compact, `topologicalKrullDim X = 1`, and all stalks
  regular local rings, with integrality supplied as `[IsIntegral X]`.
  `IsNonsingularCurve.satisfiesConditionStar` shows that such a curve satisfies
  condition $(*)$. `IsNonsingularCurve.isClosed_singleton_iff_coheight_eq_one`
  shows that its closed points are exactly its prime divisors.
- `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveFunctionFieldMap` ($f^\#$) and
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveDegree` ($[K(X):K(Y)]$).
- `IsNonsingularCurve.divisorDegree` (the sum of the coefficients).
- `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurvePointPullback` ($f^*Q$),
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurvePointPullback_apply` (independence of the local
  parameter),
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurvePointPullback_support_subset` (finite fibre
  sum), and
  `IsNonsingularCurve.weilDivisorAddEquivFinsupp_finiteCurvePointPullback` (the
  explicit finite-sum formula).
- `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveDivisorPullback` ($f^*$ on $\operatorname{Div}$),
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveDivisorPullback_primeDivisor` (the additive
  extension of $Q \mapsto f^*Q$), and
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveDivisorPullback_principalDivisor`
  ($f^*((g)) = (f^\#g)$).
- `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveClassGroupPullback` and
  `IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveClassGroupPullback_weilDivisorClassHom` (the map
  on $\operatorname{Cl}$).

The headline theorems carry `[_hk : IsAlgClosed k]` and
`[_hfk : f.IsOver (Spec (.of k))]`. These are Hartshorne's standing setting and
are not used by the proofs. If the frontmatter records these theorems, the
article needs one sentence saying so.

## Blocker (resolved by the roadmap revision)

The blocker was that the scheme-level notion of a nonsingular curve over $k$
(Hartshorne II.6, p. 136) had no article. The roadmap revision resolved it:

- It added the definition article
  `schemes/divisors/curve-divisors/nonsingular-curve.md`. That article owns the
  curve predicate, the result that such a curve satisfies $(*)$, and the
  identification of closed points with prime divisors. Its implementation note
  names the three curve declarations above as candidates and compares the Lean
  definition with Hartshorne's wording.
- This article now lists that article and
  `../weil-divisors/weil-linear-equivalence.md` under Depends on. The descent
  to $\operatorname{Cl}$ uses the Weil class group.
- Propositions 6.7 and 6.8
  (`complete-nonsingular-curve-projective.md` and
  `nonconstant-complete-curve-map-finite.md`) also list the new article under
  Depends on.

The statement of this article was not changed.
