# Pullback of divisors along finite curve morphisms

## Revision (supervisor review r9, item 37)

The degree of a finite morphism is now defined for finite morphisms of curves (Hartshorne p.137:
"If f: X -> Y is a finite morphism of curves, we define the degree of f to be the degree of the
field extension [K(X):K(Y)]"), not only of nonsingular curves. The rest of the article (divisor
degree, pullback of points and divisors, principal divisors, class groups) is unchanged and stays
on nonsingular curves. The source page was corrected to p.137.

## Lean needed to re-record

- The curve predicate `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsCurve k X`, which
  `Scheme.IsNonsingularCurve` extends.
- The degree and function-field map for curves: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsCurve.finiteCurveDegree`
  and `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsCurve.finiteCurveFunctionFieldMap`. They replace the nonsingular
  versions as the article's degree and `f^#`.
- The nonsingular versions stay as corollaries:
  `Scheme.IsNonsingularCurve.finiteCurveDegree_eq` and `finiteCurveFunctionFieldMap_eq`.
