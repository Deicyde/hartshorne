# Relative Spec of a quasi-coherent algebra

## Revision (review r9 item 49)

The statement now gives all of Exercise II.5.17(c) as printed on p.128: besides
`f^{-1}(V) ≅ Spec A(V)`, the compatibility of `f^{-1}(U) -> f^{-1}(V)` with the
restriction `A(V) -> A(U)` for every inclusion of open affines, and the
uniqueness of `(X, f)`. The article was retracted for this revision.

Encoding: as for relative Proj (II.7 source note, convention paragraph), a
quasi-coherent algebra is `Hartshorne.QuasiCoherentAlgebra`, a ring-valued
functor on Mathlib's small affine Zariski site (arrows: basic-open inclusions
`D(r) ⊆ U`) with the localization condition. For a general inclusion `U ⊆ V` of
affine opens the restriction `A(V) -> A(U)` is not part of the data; it is the
unique ring map compatible with the given restrictions to every affine open
that is basic in both `U` and `V` (such opens cover `U`).

To re-record, a Formalize pass must add to `Hartshorne/Scheme/RelativeSpec.lean`:
- the restriction homomorphism for an arbitrary inclusion of affine opens, its
  compatibility with the given restrictions on common basic opens, its
  uniqueness for that property, its agreement with the given restriction on
  basic-open inclusions, and the correspondence of `f^{-1}(U) -> f^{-1}(V)` with
  it under `restrictIso`;
- uniqueness of `(X, f)`: compatible open immersions `Spec A(U) -> X` onto
  `g^{-1}(U)` over `U` (compatibility along basic-open inclusions suffices)
  give a unique morphism `Spec_Y A -> X` restricting to them, and it is an
  isomorphism over `Y`;
- record `map_ι` (compatibility along basic opens) with the above.
