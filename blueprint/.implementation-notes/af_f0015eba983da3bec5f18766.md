# The Artin–Rees kernel system is pro-zero

## Partial progress (module level, checked)

The module-level assertions are formalized, without `sorry`, in the module
`Hartshorne.Cohomology.ArtinReesProZeroKernel`, namespace
`Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernel`:

- `system a R : ℕᵒᵖ ⥤ AddCommGrpCat` is the inverse system with term
  `K n = (R ⊓ a ^ (n + 1) • ⊤) ⧸ (a ^ (n + 1) • R)` (stage `n` uses the power
  `n + 1`), with transition maps induced by inclusion of submodules
  (`transition`, a linear map).
- `exists_transition_eq_zero`: for every `n` there is `n' ≥ n` with the linear
  transition map `K n' → K n` equal to zero.
- `system_isProZero`: the same statement for the morphisms of the inverse
  system (the pro-zero assertion, the article's main result).
- `system_sections_eq_zero` and `system_sections_subsingleton`: every
  compatible section vanishes, so the inverse limit (represented, as in the
  Mittag–Leffler modules, by compatible sections of the underlying Type-valued
  functor) is trivial.

Hypotheses: `IsNoetherianRing A`, `Module.Finite A E`; `R` is an arbitrary
submodule (finiteness of `R` is automatic over a Noetherian ring). The proof
uses only `Hartshorne.artinRees_inducedTopology`, applied to the index `n + 1`.

Algebraic core of the uniformity step of the sheaf clause (same module):

- `transition_eq_zero_iff`: the transition `K m → K n` vanishes exactly when
  `R ∩ a ^ (m + 1) E ⊆ a ^ (n + 1) R`.
- `localized'_pow_smul`, `localized'_numerator`: localization (any
  `IsLocalization` / `IsLocalizedModule` pair) commutes with `a ^ k • N` and
  with the numerator `R ∩ a ^ (n + 1) E`.
- `exists_transition_localized_eq_zero`: for every `n` one `n' ≥ n` makes the
  transition `K n' → K n` vanish simultaneously for the data localized at every
  multiplicative subset (so for every basic open of an affine chart and every
  stalk at a point of the chart). Over a finite affine cover the sheafwise
  `n'` is then the maximum of the chart indices.

## Remaining goal

The sheaf clause: for a quasi-compact Noetherian scheme, a coherent ambient
module sheaf `E`, a coherent submodule sheaf `R` and a coherent ideal sheaf
`I`, the kernel sheaves `(R ∩ I ^ (n + 1) E) / I ^ (n + 1) R` form a pro-zero
inverse system (and hence have zero limit). Route: on each affine chart of a
finite affine cover the stalks (or the sections over basic opens) of the
kernel sheaves are the module-level kernels of the localized chart data, so
`exists_transition_localized_eq_zero` gives an `n'` per chart; take the
maximum over the cover and conclude stalkwise.

## Blocker: missing article-sized prerequisite

Coherence (`Hartshorne.IsCoherent`) and the abelian closure of
(quasi-)coherent modules are now formalized, but the sheaf objects of the
clause are not definable from the roadmap:

- No article states the product `I ^ k · F` of an ideal sheaf with a module
  sheaf or a submodule sheaf, nor its affine description
  `Γ(U, I · N) = Γ(U, I) · Γ(U, N)` for quasi-coherent data on an affine
  open `U`. The same object is needed, and also undefined, in
  `schemes/formal-schemes/coherent-formal-modules/completed-coherent-module`
  and `scheme-completion-quotient-comparison` (`F / I ^ (n + 1) F`).
- `schemes/coherent-sheaves/module-ideal-sheaf-data` (module ideal sheaves)
  is not formalized and is not reached from this article.
- Stalks of a quasi-coherent module on an affine open are localizations in
  Lean (`Hartshorne.FiberDimAux.isLocalizedModule_germₗ`, under a
  tilde-isomorphism hypothesis), but the submodule-sheaf analogue (stalks of a
  quasi-coherent submodule sheaf are the localized submodules) is not
  available.

## Split proposal (Roadmap return)

1. Restrict this article to the module-level statement (the pro-zero
   assertion and `lim K n = 0`), recording `Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernel.system`,
   `Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernel.system_isProZero`,
   `Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernel.system_sections_eq_zero`.
2. New article "Products of ideal sheaves with module sheaves": for an ideal
   sheaf `I` and a submodule sheaf `N ⊆ F` of an `O_X`-module, define the
   submodule sheaf `I · N` (image of `I ⊗ N → F`); for quasi-coherent `I`,
   `N`, `F` it is quasi-coherent with `Γ(U, I · N) = Γ(U, I) · Γ(U, N)` on
   affine `U`, and coherent when `F` is coherent on a Noetherian scheme.
   Source: Hartshorne II.9, p.194 (the quotients `F / I ^ (n + 1) F` in the
   completion of a coherent sheaf). Depends on: module-ideal-sheaf-data,
   module-tensor-product, quasicoherent-abelian-closure,
   coherent-affine-local-criterion.
3. New article "The Artin–Rees kernel sheaves are pro-zero" (the sheaf clause
   of this article, same source locator). Depends on: this article, article 2,
   quasicoherent-principal-open-localization. Proof: the uniform localized
   index `Hartshorne.ArtinReesKernel.exists_transition_localized_eq_zero` on a
   finite affine cover. The theorem on formal functions then depends on
   article 3 (Hartshorne's proof takes cohomology of the kernel sheaves).
4. Add edges from article 2 to `completed-coherent-module` and
   `scheme-completion-quotient-comparison`.
