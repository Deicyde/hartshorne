# Relative differential sheaf

The universal-sheaf construction is viable with pinned Mathlib. Pull the
commutative structure sheaf of the target back to the source, transpose the
scheme's structure morphism across the sheaf pullback/pushforward adjunction,
apply
`PresheafOfModules.DifferentialsConstruction.relativeDifferentials'` on the
source site, and sheafify the resulting presheaf of modules. The
sheafification adjunction composed with
`DifferentialsConstruction.isUniversal'` gives the required equivalence
between module-sheaf morphisms out of the result and relative derivations.
A scratch elaboration verified this construction and its canonical
derivation without axioms or open statements.

The remaining article statement is not represented by the current dependency
DAG. It also requires a canonical comparison with the conormal sheaf of the
diagonal for an arbitrary scheme morphism, including independence of the open
neighborhood in which the diagonal immersion is closed. Pinned Mathlib has the
`IsImmersion` instance for a diagonal and the canonical `liftCoborder`
factorization into a closed immersion followed by an open immersion. It has
`IdealSheafData` on affine opens, but no associated module sheaf `J/J^2` for an
arbitrary immersion and no comparison between such a conormal sheaf and the
sheafification of relative Kähler differentials. The existing presheaf
differentials file explicitly leaves the general different-site construction
as a TODO.

Do not discharge the comparison by defining “diagonal conormal sheaf” to be
the relative differential sheaf: that would make the claimed theorem
tautological without relating it to the diagonal ideal. Roadmap should either
split the conormal comparison and neighborhood-independence theorem into its
own leaf with a conormal-sheaf prerequisite, or add those missing prerequisites
to this leaf. The already planned base-change and closed-immersion conormal
sequence articles are downstream and cannot be used here under the current
DAG.
