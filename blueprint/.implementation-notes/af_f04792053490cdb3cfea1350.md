# Sheaf Ext for a Koszul-regular immersion

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Cohomology.KoszulRegularImmersion`.

## Status (partial)

The article bundles three claims. Claim 1 is proved; claims 2 and 3 are open.

### Definitions

- `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular f M`: the Koszul complex `K_•(f; M)`
  (`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex`) is exact in every positive degree.
  A regular sequence (Mathlib's `RingTheory.Sequence.IsWeaklyRegular`) is Koszul-regular by
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex_exactAt_of_isWeaklyRegular`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r`: `i : X ⟶ Y` is a closed immersion and every point
  of `Y` has an affine open `U` and `f : Fin r → Γ(Y, U)` with `i.ker.ideal U = Ideal.span (range f)`
  and `IsKoszulRegular f Γ(Y, U)`. This is the affine-local form of the Stacks definition of a
  Koszul-regular immersion. The ideal is Mathlib's `Scheme.Hom.ker`; on affine opens it is
  `RingHom.ker (i.app U)` (`Scheme.Hom.ker_apply`), i.e. the sections of the project's
  `Hartshorne.closedImmersionIdealSheaf i`.

### Claim 1 (the defining sequence is `M`-regular): proved

- `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_sections_of_isFiniteLocallyFree`: for a Koszul-regular `f` over an
  affine open `U` and finite locally free `M`, every `y ∈ U` has a basic open `D(g) ⊆ U`, `y ∈ D(g)`,
  on which the restricted sequence is Koszul-regular on `Γ(Y, D(g))` and on `Γ(M, D(g))`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_isKoszulRegular_sections`: the immersion form
  (defining sequences near every point that are Koszul-regular on both `Γ(Y, U)` and `Γ(M, U)`).
- Helpers: `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular.of_iso`, `.of_flat`, `.baseChange`,
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.isKoszulRegular_iff_exact`, `Hartshorne.module_free_sections_of_iso_free`,
  `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_basicOpen`.

Possible strengthening: Koszul-regularity on `Γ(M, U)` for the whole affine `U` follows from
`IsKoszulRegular.of_flat` once `Γ(M, U)` is known to be a flat `Γ(Y, U)`-module (quasi-coherence
of `M` on `U` gives `Γ(M, D(g)) = Γ(M, U)_g`; then Mathlib's `Module.flat_of_isLocalized_span`).

### Claim 2 (`ℰxtⁱ_Y(i_* 𝒪_X, M) = 0` for `i < r`): open

Remaining goal:
`IsZero ((Ringed.sheafExt (R := Y.sheaf) ((Scheme.Modules.pushforward i).obj (SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf)) n).obj M)`
for `n < r`, given `IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r` and `Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree M`.

Route: vanishing is local; on the basic opens of claim 1 (with `M` free there),
`ℰxtⁿ_Y(i_*𝒪_X, M)|_V ≅ ℰxtⁿ_V(𝒪_V/I_V, M|_V)` needs sheaf Ext restricted to opens
(Hartshorne III.6.2, blueprint article "Sheaf Ext restricts to open subsets", not formalized
and not a dependency of this article). Then the tilde of the Koszul complex `K_•(f; Γ(Y, V))` is a
finite free resolution of `(i_*𝒪_X)|_V` and `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafExtIsoHomologyOfLocallyFreeResolution`
computes the Ext as the cohomology of `ℋom(K~, M|_V) ≅ Hom_A(K, Γ(M, V))~`. The algebraic input
is Koszul self-duality: `Hom_A(⋀^p A^r, P) ≅ ⋀^{r-p} A^r ⊗ P` via the wedge pairing into
`⋀^r A^r ≅ A`, under which the dual of the contraction is the contraction up to sign
(`ι_φ(ω) ∧ η = (-1)^p ω ∧ ι_φ(η)` for `ω ∈ ⋀^{p+1}`, `η ∈ ⋀^{r-p}`), so
`Hⁱ(Hom_A(K_•(f; A), P)) ≅ H_{r-i}(K_•(f; P))`, which vanishes for `i < r` by claim 1.

### Claim 3 (top Ext and the determinant): open

Remaining goal: an isomorphism `ℰxt^r_Y(i_*𝒪_X, M) ≅ i_*(i^*M ⊗ (⋀^r(I/I²))^∨)` of
`𝒪_Y`-modules. Locally `ℰxt^r ≅ P/(f)P` through the top Koszul dual; the local isomorphisms
change by `det` of the transition matrix under a change of generators
(`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulChangeOfBasisIso`), which is what the factor
`(⋀^r(I/I²))^∨` absorbs; gluing then needs module-sheaf gluing along these determinant cocycles.
