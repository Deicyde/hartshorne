# Sheaf Ext for a Koszul-regular immersion

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Cohomology.KoszulRegularImmersion`.

## Status (partial)

The article bundles three claims. Claims 1 and 2 are proved. For claim 3 the commutative-algebra
core (Koszul self-duality, the local Ext calculation on sections, and the determinant transition
law) is proved; the sheaf-level statement is open.

### Definitions

- `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular f M`: the Koszul complex `K_•(f; M)`
  (`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex`) is exact in every positive degree.
  A regular sequence (Mathlib's `RingTheory.Sequence.IsWeaklyRegular`) is Koszul-regular by
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex_exactAt_of_isWeaklyRegular`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r`: `i : X ⟶ Y` is a closed immersion and every point
  of `Y` has an affine open `U` and `f : Fin r → Γ(Y, U)` with `i.ker.ideal U = Ideal.span (range f)`
  and `IsKoszulRegular f Γ(Y, U)`. This is the affine-local form of the Stacks definition of a
  Koszul-regular immersion. The ideal is Mathlib's `Scheme.Hom.ker`; on affine opens it is
  `RingHom.ker (i.app U)` (`Scheme.Hom.ker_apply`), i.e. the sections of the project's
  `Hartshorne.closedImmersionIdealSheaf i`.

### Claim 1 (the defining sequence is `M`-regular): proved

- `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_sections_of_isFiniteLocallyFree`: for a Koszul-regular `f` over an
  affine open `U` and finite locally free `M`, every `y ∈ U` has a basic open `D(g) ⊆ U`, `y ∈ D(g)`,
  on which the restricted sequence is Koszul-regular on `Γ(Y, D(g))` and on `Γ(M, D(g))`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_isKoszulRegular_sections`: the immersion form
  (defining sequences near every point that are Koszul-regular on both `Γ(Y, U)` and `Γ(M, U)`).
- Helpers: `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular.of_iso`, `.of_flat`, `.baseChange`,
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.isKoszulRegular_iff_exact`, `Hartshorne.module_free_sections_of_iso_free`,
  `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_basicOpen`.

Possible strengthening: Koszul-regularity on `Γ(M, U)` for the whole affine `U` follows from
`IsKoszulRegular.of_flat` once `Γ(M, U)` is known to be a flat `Γ(Y, U)`-module (quasi-coherence
of `M` on `U` gives `Γ(M, D(g)) = Γ(M, U)_g`; then Mathlib's `Module.flat_of_isLocalized_span`).

### Algebraic core of claims 2 and 3: proved

Let `V = A^r`, `[-]` the coefficient of `e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r` (`Hartshorne.Koszul.topCoord`).

- `Hartshorne.Koszul.contractLeft_mul_of_mem`: contraction is a graded derivation.
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.wedgeDual_bijective`: `⋀^q V → (⋀^p V)^∨`, `η ↦ (ω ↦ [η ∧ ω])`, is bijective
  for `q + p = r` (from `Hartshorne.ExteriorPerfectPairing.bijective_μ_basis`).
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualEquiv`: `⋀^q V ⊗ P ≃ Hom(⋀^p V, P)`;
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.lcomp_contraction_comp_koszulDualEquiv`: transpose of the contraction is the
  contraction up to `(-1)^q`.
- On the dual of the Koszul resolution `Hom_A(K_•(f; A), P)` (differentials
  `LinearMap.lcomp A P (K.d (p+1) p).hom`):
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.injective_lcomp_sequenceKoszulComplex_d` (degree 0, `r > 0`),
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.exact_lcomp_sequenceKoszulComplex_d` (degrees `1, …, r-1`), both for `f`
  Koszul-regular on `P`; `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap` with
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.exact_koszulDualTopMap` (top cohomology `≅ P / (f) P`, no regularity needed)
  and `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap_apply` (it is `ψ ↦ [ψ(e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r ⊗ 1)]`, stated for
  `r = s + 1`).
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap_changeOfBasis`: for invertible `Q`, precomposing with the
  change-of-basis map `K_•(f Q; A) → K_•(f; A)` (`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulChangeOfBasis`)
  multiplies the top map by `det Q` (via `Hartshorne.Koszul.exteriorPower_map_topForm`).
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_koszulDual`: for a Koszul-regular immersion and
  finite locally free `M`, near every point the dual Koszul complex with coefficients `Γ(M, U)` is
  exact below `r` with top cohomology `Γ(M, U) / I(U) Γ(M, U)`.

### Claim 2 (`ℰxtⁱ_Y(i_* 𝒪_X, M) = 0` for `i < r`): proved

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.KoszulRegularImmersionExt`.

- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.isZero_sheafExt`: for `IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r`,
  `Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree (R := Y.sheaf) M` and `n < r`,
  `IsZero ((Ringed.sheafExt (R := Y.sheaf) ((Scheme.Modules.pushforward i).obj
  (SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf)) n).obj M)`.
- Local form: `Hartshorne.KoszulImmersion.isZero_moduleRestrict_sheafExt` (on an affine `V₀`
  with Koszul-regular generators of the ideal, inside an open where `M` is free);
  `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_le` chooses such `V₀` inside any neighbourhood.
- Locality: `Hartshorne.Ringed.isZero_of_isZero_moduleRestrict`.

Infrastructure built for it (all general ringed spaces):

- `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.SheafExtRestrict` (Hartshorne III.6.2): `Ringed.restrictRingSheaf`,
  `Ringed.moduleRestrict` (`= moduleSheafRestrict`, restriction to the subspace `U`),
  `Ringed.homRestrict`/`Ringed.homRestrictIso` (`ℋom_X(F, -)|_U ≅ ℋom_U(F|_U, (-)|_U)`, natural),
  exactness instances for `moduleRestrict`, `Ringed.restrictInjectiveResolution`, and
  `Ringed.sheafExtRestrictIso : (ℰxtⁿ_X(F, G))|_U ≅ ℰxtⁿ_U(F|_U, G|_U)` (objectwise; naturality
  in `G` not yet proved). The ℋom comparison uses the equivalence of the sites of opens of `U` and
  of `X` contained in `W ⊆ U` (`Ringed.SheafExtRestrict.overEquiv`, a dense-subsite equivalence)
  and Mathlib's `SheafOfModules.pushforwardPushforwardEquivalence`.
- `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ScalarExtensionSheaf`: for `φ : A → 𝒪_X(X)`, the presheaf
  `V ↦ 𝒪_X(V) ⊗_A N` (`Ringed.ScalarExtension.presheaf`, functorial), a sheaf for finite free `N`
  (`Ringed.ScalarExtension.sheaf`) and locally free of finite rank
  (`isFiniteLocallyFree_sheaf`); `overHomEquiv : ((𝒪 ⊗_A N)|_W ⟶ G|_W) ≃ (N →ₗ[A] G(W))` with
  `overHomEquiv_comp`; global `homOfLinear`.
- In `KoszulRegularImmersionExt`, namespace `Ringed.KoszulSheaf`: the sheafified Koszul complex
  `complex φ f`, `isZero_homology_homComplex` (the dual complex `ℋom(𝒪 ⊗_A K_•, G)` has zero
  cohomology below `r` if `f` is Koszul-regular on `G(W)` over a basis of opens), and
  `quasiIso_augmentation` (the augmentation to `F₀[0]` is a quasi-isomorphism given flatness of
  `𝒪(W)` over `A` and exactness in degree zero over a basis).

Route taken: the one sketched before (restriction of `ℰxt` to opens, sheafified Koszul resolution,
`sheafExtIsoHomologyOfLocallyFreeResolution`, `exists_koszulDual`-style algebra via
`exact_lcomp_sequenceKoszulComplex_d`, exactness over the basis of basic opens with
`sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections`). The restriction isomorphism is proved directly from
injective resolutions (exactness of restriction plus Lemma 6.1), not from the universal property
of derived functors.

### Claim 3 (top Ext and the determinant): open

Remaining goal: an isomorphism `ℰxt^r_Y(i_*𝒪_X, M) ≅ i_*(i^*M ⊗ (⋀^r(I/I²))^∨)` of
`𝒪_Y`-modules (Lean statement not yet written).

Route: on each Koszul affine `V₀` (as in claim 2), `ℰxt^r|_{V₀} ≅ h^r(ℋom(𝒪 ⊗_A K_•, M|_{V₀}))`
(`sheafExtRestrictIso` + `sheafExtIsoHomologyOfLocallyFreeResolution`); `h^r` is the cokernel of
`ℋom(L_{r-1}, M) → ℋom(L_r, M)`, and on sections over basic opens `overHomEquiv` and
`koszulDualTopMap`/`exact_koszulDualTopMap` identify it with `M(W) / I M(W)`, i.e. with
`(i_* i^* M)|_{V₀}` (needs a sheaf map `h^r ⟶ (i_* i^*M)|_{V₀}` built from `koszulDualTopMap`,
exact over the basis). Under a change of generators `f ↦ f Q` the local isomorphisms change by
`det Q` (`koszulDualTopMap_changeOfBasis`), which is the transition of the local bases
`f₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ f_r` of `⋀^r(I/I²)`; gluing (e.g. `Hartshorne.ModuleGluing`) then gives the twist by
`(⋀^r(I/I²))^∨`. Missing pieces: naturality of `sheafExtRestrictIso` in `G` and its compatibility
with iterated restriction (to compare the local isomorphisms on overlaps), the conormal sheaf
`I/I²` of a Koszul-regular immersion as a locally free module with local frames given by `f`, and
`i^*M`, `⋀^r`, dual and their identifications on `V₀`.
