# Sheaf Ext for a Koszul-regular immersion

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Cohomology.KoszulRegularImmersion`.

## Status (partial)

The article bundles three claims. Claim 1 is proved. For claims 2 and 3 the commutative-algebra
core (Koszul self-duality, the local Ext calculation on sections, and the determinant transition
law) is proved; the sheaf-level statements are open.

### Definitions

- `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular f M`: the Koszul complex `K_•(f; M)`
  (`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex`) is exact in every positive degree.
  A regular sequence (Mathlib's `RingTheory.Sequence.IsWeaklyRegular`) is Koszul-regular by
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulComplex_exactAt_of_isWeaklyRegular`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r`: `i : X ⟶ Y` is a closed immersion and every point
  of `Y` has an affine open `U` and `f : Fin r → Γ(Y, U)` with `i.ker.ideal U = Ideal.span (range f)`
  and `IsKoszulRegular f Γ(Y, U)`. This is the affine-local form of the Stacks definition of a
  Koszul-regular immersion. The ideal is Mathlib's `Scheme.Hom.ker`; on affine opens it is
  `RingHom.ker (i.app U)` (`Scheme.Hom.ker_apply`), i.e. the sections of the project's
  `Hartshorne.closedImmersionIdealSheaf i`.

### Claim 1 (the defining sequence is `M`-regular): proved

- `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_sections_of_isFiniteLocallyFree`: for a Koszul-regular `f` over an
  affine open `U` and finite locally free `M`, every `y ∈ U` has a basic open `D(g) ⊆ U`, `y ∈ D(g)`,
  on which the restricted sequence is Koszul-regular on `Γ(Y, D(g))` and on `Γ(M, D(g))`.
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_isKoszulRegular_sections`: the immersion form
  (defining sequences near every point that are Koszul-regular on both `Γ(Y, U)` and `Γ(M, U)`).
- Helpers: `Hartshorne.Koszul.IsKoszulRegular.of_iso`, `.of_flat`, `.baseChange`,
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.isKoszulRegular_iff_exact`, `Hartshorne.module_free_sections_of_iso_free`,
  `Hartshorne.isKoszulRegular_basicOpen`.

Possible strengthening: Koszul-regularity on `Γ(M, U)` for the whole affine `U` follows from
`IsKoszulRegular.of_flat` once `Γ(M, U)` is known to be a flat `Γ(Y, U)`-module (quasi-coherence
of `M` on `U` gives `Γ(M, D(g)) = Γ(M, U)_g`; then Mathlib's `Module.flat_of_isLocalized_span`).

### Algebraic core of claims 2 and 3: proved

Let `V = A^r`, `[-]` the coefficient of `e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r` (`Hartshorne.Koszul.topCoord`).

- `Hartshorne.Koszul.contractLeft_mul_of_mem`: contraction is a graded derivation.
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.wedgeDual_bijective`: `⋀^q V → (⋀^p V)^∨`, `η ↦ (ω ↦ [η ∧ ω])`, is bijective
  for `q + p = r` (from `Hartshorne.ExteriorPerfectPairing.bijective_μ_basis`).
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualEquiv`: `⋀^q V ⊗ P ≃ Hom(⋀^p V, P)`;
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.lcomp_contraction_comp_koszulDualEquiv`: transpose of the contraction is the
  contraction up to `(-1)^q`.
- On the dual of the Koszul resolution `Hom_A(K_•(f; A), P)` (differentials
  `LinearMap.lcomp A P (K.d (p+1) p).hom`):
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.injective_lcomp_sequenceKoszulComplex_d` (degree 0, `r > 0`),
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.exact_lcomp_sequenceKoszulComplex_d` (degrees `1, …, r-1`), both for `f`
  Koszul-regular on `P`; `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap` with
  `Hartshorne.Koszul.exact_koszulDualTopMap` (top cohomology `≅ P / (f) P`, no regularity needed)
  and `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap_apply` (it is `ψ ↦ [ψ(e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ e_r ⊗ 1)]`, stated for
  `r = s + 1`).
- `Hartshorne.Koszul.koszulDualTopMap_changeOfBasis`: for invertible `Q`, precomposing with the
  change-of-basis map `K_•(f Q; A) → K_•(f; A)` (`Hartshorne.Koszul.sequenceKoszulChangeOfBasis`)
  multiplies the top map by `det Q` (via `Hartshorne.Koszul.exteriorPower_map_topForm`).
- `Hartshorne.IsKoszulRegularImmersion.exists_koszulDual`: for a Koszul-regular immersion and
  finite locally free `M`, near every point the dual Koszul complex with coefficients `Γ(M, U)` is
  exact below `r` with top cohomology `Γ(M, U) / I(U) Γ(M, U)`.

### Claim 2 (`ℰxtⁱ_Y(i_* 𝒪_X, M) = 0` for `i < r`): open

Remaining goal:
`IsZero ((Ringed.sheafExt (R := Y.sheaf) ((Scheme.Modules.pushforward i).obj (SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf)) n).obj M)`
for `n < r`, given `IsKoszulRegularImmersion i r` and `Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree M`.

Route: vanishing is local; on the opens `U` of `exists_koszulDual`,
`ℰxtⁿ_Y(i_*𝒪_X, M)|_U ≅ ℰxtⁿ_U((i_*𝒪_X)|_U, M|_U)` needs sheaf Ext restricted to opens
(Hartshorne III.6.2, blueprint article "Sheaf Ext restricts to open subsets", not formalized
and not a dependency of this article). Then the sheafified Koszul complex `K_•(f; Γ(Y, U))~` is a
finite free resolution of `(i_*𝒪_X)|_U`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.sheafExtIsoHomologyOfLocallyFreeResolution`
computes the Ext as the cohomology of `ℋom(K~, M|_U)`, and on a basic open where `M` is free this
is `Hom_A(K, Γ(M, U))~`, whose cohomology vanishes below `r` by `exists_koszulDual` and exactness
of `~`.

### Claim 3 (top Ext and the determinant): open

Remaining goal: an isomorphism `ℰxt^r_Y(i_*𝒪_X, M) ≅ i_*(i^*M ⊗ (⋀^r(I/I²))^∨)` of
`𝒪_Y`-modules. Locally `ℰxt^r ≅ (Γ(M, U) / I Γ(M, U))~` through `koszulDualTopMap`; under a change
of generators the local isomorphisms change by `det Q` (`koszulDualTopMap_changeOfBasis`), and
`det Q` is also the transition of the local bases `f₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ f_r` of `⋀^r(I/I²)`, so the local maps
glue to the stated isomorphism with `(⋀^r(I/I²))^∨`. Needs, beyond claim 2: the identification of
`I/I²|_U` as free on the classes of `f` (conormal of a Koszul-regular immersion) and module-sheaf
gluing of the local isomorphisms.
