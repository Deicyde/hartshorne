# Completion depends only on the closed support

## Status

Statement and proof complete, in `Hartshorne.Scheme.FormalCompletionSupport`; pending review.

## Declarations

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletion_support_invariant`: the article's
  statement. For quasi-coherent ideal subsheaves `I`, `J` of a Noetherian scheme with equal
  supports (Mathlib `IdealSheafData.support` of `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv`): positive
  powers `(I ^ a).carrier ≤ J.carrier`, `(J ^ b).carrier ≤ I.carrier`, and an isomorphism of
  locally ringed spaces `I.formalCompletion ≅ J.formalCompletion` which is the identity on
  points of `X` and compatible on sections with the projections to the infinitesimal
  neighbourhoods.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionIsoOfSupportEq`: the isomorphism.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.infinitesimalNeighborhoodι_formalCompletionIsoOfSupportEq_hom_base`:
  identity on points (commutes with `infinitesimalNeighborhoodι 1`).
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionIsoOfSupportEq_hom_c_app_π`: for
  `(J ^ (m + 1)).carrier ≤ (I ^ (n + 1)).carrier`, the iso followed by the projection to
  `𝒪_{Y_(n+1)}` equals the projection to `𝒪_{Y'_(m+1)}` followed by restriction along the
  inclusion of subschemes. Since such `m` exist for every `n`, these equations determine the
  isomorphism on sections (this is the sense of "canonical").
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.exists_pow_carrier_le_of_support_le` and
  `Hartshorne.idealSheafData_exists_pow_le_of_le_radical`: the power containments.

## Route

- Powers: `J ≤ K.radical` (from the supports, via Mathlib `vanishingIdeal_support` and
  `le_support_iff_le_vanishingIdeal`); on each affine of a finite affine cover the ring is
  Noetherian, `Ideal.exists_pow_le_of_le_radical_of_fg` gives an exponent, the maximum works,
  and `IdealSheafData.le_of_iSup_eq_top` globalizes.
- Isomorphism: rather than comparing the two inverse systems with each other directly, both are
  compared with the limit over the poset of all ideal sheaf data with support `Z`
  (`FormalCompletionSupport.SupportIdeal`), on the reduced subscheme on `Z` (subscheme of
  `vanishingIdeal Z`, a closed subscheme of each of them, homeomorphic to each). The powers of `I`
  are an initial (cofinal) chain there by the Noetherian power lemma
  (`FormalCompletionSupport.initial_powChain`, `Functor.Initial.limitIso`); the chain is
  identified with the formal-completion diagram transported along the homeomorphism
  (`compare`); `sheafedSpaceIso` packages this. The final iso is
  `(sheafedSpaceIso I).symm ≪≫ sheafedSpaceIso J`.
- Hartshorne's coherence hypothesis is not used: on a Noetherian scheme every quasi-coherent
  ideal sheaf is coherent, so the Lean statement over quasi-coherent ideal subsheaves is the same
  statement.

## Friction

- `TopCat.Sheaf` is a semireducible `def`, so terms like `(limit F).obj` and
  `K.subscheme` versus `I.infinitesimalNeighborhood (n + 1)` are only defeq at default
  transparency; `rw` fails with "motive is not type correct" and `simp` does not fire. The proofs
  use `erw` and `refine`/`exact` chains (`appLE_map`, `map_appLE`) instead.
- Instance search does not see `PreservesLimit` for `sheafToPresheaf` on the formal-completion
  diagram; `limitPreserved` extracts it from the shape instance explicitly.
