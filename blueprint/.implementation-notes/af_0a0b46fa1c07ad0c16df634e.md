# The tangent morphism and dual plane curve

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Intersection.PlaneCurveDual`. Main theorem
`Hartshorne.planeCurve_tangentLine_morphism`; definitions
`Hartshorne.planeCurveRegularLocus`, `Hartshorne.planeCurveTangentLine`,
`Hartshorne.planeDualCurve`; the regular locus is a quasi-projective variety by
`Hartshorne.isQuasiProjVariety_planeCurveRegularLocus`, and the curve is a
projective variety by `Hartshorne.isProjVariety_planeCurve`.

## Modelling choices

- The curve is `projZeroSet {F}` with `F` irreducible and homogeneous, as in the
  local intersection multiplicity article. Lines are points `a` of the dual plane
  (`ProjectiveSpace k (Fin 3)`), the line being `projectivePlaneLineForm a.rep`,
  following Hartshorne's identification of lines with `(a₀, a₁, a₂)`.
- "Regular point" is Hartshorne's Chapter I nonsingularity:
  `Variety.NonsingularAt` (regular local ring) on `Variety.ofProjective` of the
  curve. The article's listed dependency on scheme regular points is not used by
  the Lean; the classical article (intrinsic nonsingularity) is linked instead.
- "Morphism" is `VarietyHom` from `Variety.ofQuasiProjective` of the regular locus
  to `Variety.ofProjective` of `Set.univ` (the dual plane).
- The tangent line is defined by the gradient `(∂F/∂xⱼ)(P)`, with a junk value off
  the regular locus; the theorem characterizes it as the unique line of
  multiplicity greater than one.
- The dual curve is the Zariski closure of the image of the regular locus; the
  theorem also identifies it with the closure of the range of the morphism.

## Reusable helpers

- `nonsingularAt_planeCurve_iff`: Jacobian criterion for plane curves, via the
  chart isomorphism, `vanishingIdeal_chartMap_image_planeCurve` and the affine
  Jacobian criterion.
- `exists_varietyHom_of_homogeneous`: a map into `ℙ²` given by homogeneous
  polynomials of one degree without common zero is a morphism (stated for source
  and target in `ℙ²`; the proof does not use the dimension).
- `one_lt_projectivePlaneLocalIntersectionMultiplicity_iff`,
  `derivative_homogeneousLineRestrict`, `projectivePlaneLineForm_dvd_of_vanish`.
- `exists_varietyHom_iff_locally_chart_regular` (product-variety file) was not
  usable because it requires the index type in the universe of `k`.
