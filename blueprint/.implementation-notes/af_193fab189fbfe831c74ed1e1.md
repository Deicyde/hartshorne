# The differential criterion for a scheme over a field

## Status

No Lean yet. Created by the split of hartshorne-smoothness-criterion as the scheme form of the
differential criterion (review amendment). It is the step the leaf's converse still needs,
applied to the fibres `X_y → Spec κ(y)`.

## Suggested target

For `K` a field, `s : Z ⟶ Spec (.of K)` with `LocallyOfFiniteType s` (and quasi-compact `Z` if
convenient), `n : ℕ`, every `C ∈ irreducibleComponents Z` with `topologicalKrullDim C = n`, and
`finrank κ(z) (κ(z) ⊗ (relativeDifferentials s)_z) ≤ n` for every `z`, conclude `Smooth s`.

## Route

Smoothness is local on the source (`IsZariskiLocalAtSource`); on an affine open `Spec T`, apply
`Hartshorne.smooth_of_forall_finrank_le`.

- Dimension clause: `coheight z + height z = n` for every point by
  `Hartshorne.FlatEquidimensional.coheight_add_height_eq_of_equidim` with
  `Hartshorne.FlatEquidimensional.exists_le_height_eq` and
  `Hartshorne.FlatEquidimensional.catenaryAt_of_finiteType`; transport to `T` with
  `Hartshorne.FlatEquidimensional.height_apply_of_isOpenImmersion`,
  `Hartshorne.FlatEquidimensional.height_spec_eq`, `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight` and
  `IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk` (as in
  `Hartshorne.topologicalKrullDim_of_mem_irreducibleComponents_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`).
- Differential clause: compare `κ(q) ⊗_T Ω_{T/K}` with the fibre of `Ω_{Z/K}` at the point through
  `RelDiffLocal.affineIso`, `Hartshorne.rank_fibre_restrict` and
  `Hartshorne.FreeLocusAux.isLocalizedModule_germTopLinear`.
