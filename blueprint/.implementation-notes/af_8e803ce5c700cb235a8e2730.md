# The tangent-normal exact sequence

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.TangentNormalExactSequence`.

- `Hartshorne.tangentNormal_shortExact`: for `p : X ⟶ Spec k` of finite type with `k`
  algebraically closed, `X` irreducible and regular, and a closed immersion `i : Y ⟶ X` with `Y`
  irreducible and regular, the short complex
  `tangentSheaf (i ≫ p) ⟶ moduleSheafDual (i^* Ω_{X/k}) ⟶ normalSheaf i` is short exact.
- `Hartshorne.tangentToPullbackDual`, `Hartshorne.pullbackDualToNormal`: its two maps, the duals
  (precomposition, `Ringed.moduleSheafHomPrecomp`) of the maps of the conormal sequence; the
  second is composed with the dual of `immersionConormalSheafIso`, which identifies the conormal
  sheaf with `i^* J`.
- `Hartshorne.tangentToPullbackDual_comp_pullbackDualToNormal`: the composite vanishes.

Hypotheses match `NonsingularConormal.nonsingular_closedImmersion_conormal` (Theorem 8.17), except
that the codimension `r` is not a parameter: a private lemma shows `codim (Y, X)` is a natural
number (bounded by the dimension of a Noetherian local ring of `X` at a point of `Y`).

## Proof route

As in the book: the conormal sequence is short exact (8.17), `Ω_{Y/k}` is locally free of rank
`dim Y` (8.15, via `isRegular_iff_relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank`), and the dual of a
short exact sequence with finite locally free last term is short exact
(`Ringed.dualShortComplex_shortExact`, recorded on the canonical sheaf of projective space article,
proved from the contravariant `ℰxt` sequence and `ℰxt¹(L, -) = 0` for finite locally free `L`).

## Gap: middle term

The middle term is `(i^* Ω_{X/k})^∨`, the dual of `Ω_X ⊗ 𝒪_Y`. Hartshorne writes `T_X|_Y = i^* T_X`.
Identifying the two needs `i^*(E^∨) ≅ (i^* E)^∨` for finite locally free `E`, which is not on main
(only the invertible case, through the Picard group, in `Hartshorne.Scheme.PicardPullback`; and
`Hartshorne.pullbackTensorIso` is stated for invertible sheaves only). Route: build the map
`i^*(E^∨) ⟶ (i^* E)^∨` adjoint, under the tensor–Hom adjunction, to
`i^*(E^∨) ⊗ i^* E ≅ i^*(E^∨ ⊗ E) ⟶ i^* 𝒪_X ≅ 𝒪_Y` (needs the pullback tensor comparison for
locally free `E`), then check it is an isomorphism on an open cover trivializing `E`.

The general dualizing lemma lives on the Example 8.20.1 article; a standalone article for it
would let this article depend on it directly.
