# The tangent-normal exact sequence

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.TangentNormalExactSequence`.

- `Hartshorne.exists_tangentNormal_shortExact`: for `p : X ⟶ Spec k` of finite type with `k`
  algebraically closed, `X` irreducible and regular, and a closed immersion `i : Y ⟶ X` with `Y`
  irreducible and regular, there is a short exact sequence
  `tangentSheaf (i ≫ p) ⟶ i^* (tangentSheaf p) ⟶ normalSheaf i`. Existential in the maps, like
  the Example 8.20.1 tangent sequence on main; the explicit maps are those below composed with
  `pullbackDualIso`.
- `Hartshorne.tangentNormal_shortExact`: the dual of the conormal sequence with explicit maps,
  `tangentSheaf (i ≫ p) ⟶ moduleSheafDual (i^* Ω_{X/k}) ⟶ normalSheaf i`.
- `Hartshorne.tangentToPullbackDual`, `Hartshorne.pullbackDualToNormal`: its two maps, the duals
  (precomposition, `Ringed.moduleSheafHomPrecomp`) of the maps of the conormal sequence; the
  second is composed with the dual of `immersionConormalSheafIso`, which identifies the conormal
  sheaf with `i^* J`. `Hartshorne.tangentToPullbackDual_comp_pullbackDualToNormal`: the composite
  vanishes.
- `Hartshorne.pullbackDualMap`, `Hartshorne.pullbackDualMap_isIso`, `Hartshorne.pullbackDualIso`:
  for any morphism of schemes `f : X ⟶ Y` and `E` locally free of rank `r`, the canonical map
  `f^*(E^∨) ⟶ (f^*E)^∨` and the proof that it is an isomorphism.
- `Hartshorne.pullbackTensorMap_isIso_of_isLocallyFreeOfRank`: `pullbackTensorMap` is an
  isomorphism for locally free sheaves of finite rank (the Picard-pullback article proves it for
  invertible sheaves). It rests on `Ringed.IsLocalFrame.tensor_prod`: frames `a` of `A` and `b` of
  `B` give the frame `a i ⊗ b k` of `A ⊗ B`.

Hypotheses match `NonsingularConormal.nonsingular_closedImmersion_conormal` (Theorem 8.17), except
that the codimension `r` is not a parameter: a private lemma shows `codim (Y, X)` is a natural
number (bounded by the dimension of a Noetherian local ring of `X` at a point of `Y`).

## Proof route

As in the book: the conormal sequence is short exact (8.17), `Ω_{Y/k}` is locally free of rank
`dim Y` (8.15, via `isRegular_iff_relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank`), and the dual of a
short exact sequence with finite locally free last term is short exact
(`Ringed.dualShortComplex_shortExact`, recorded on the canonical sheaf of projective space article,
proved from the contravariant `ℰxt` sequence and `ℰxt¹(L, -) = 0` for finite locally free `L`).

The identification `(i^* Ω_{X/k})^∨ ≅ i^* T_X`: `pullbackDualMap` is adjoint, under the tensor–Hom
adjunction, to `f^*E ⊗ f^*(E^∨) ≅ f^*(E ⊗ E^∨) ⟶ f^*𝒪_Y ≅ 𝒪_X` (inverse of `pullbackTensorMap`,
pullback of the evaluation pairing, Mathlib's `SheafOfModules.pullbackObjUnitToUnit`). Near every
point, it sends the pullback `η(e_i^∨)` of the dual frame of a frame `e` of `E` to the dual frame
of the pullback frame `η(e_i)`, and both are frames, so it is bijective on sections over small
opens (`Ringed.modules_isIso_of_locally_bijective`).

The comparison is stated for scheme morphisms, because `pullbackTensorMap` is; a ringed-space
version would follow the same argument.

The general dualizing lemma lives on the Example 8.20.1 article; a standalone article for it
would let this article depend on it directly. Likewise the tensor and dual comparisons for
pullbacks would sit naturally on their own article next to Picard pullback.
