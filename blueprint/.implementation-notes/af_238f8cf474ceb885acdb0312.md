# The graded global-sections module

## Status

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23d), not closable in budget. No Lean was
written, and nothing is recorded. The twist infrastructure exists. What is missing is the global
section of `𝒪_X(d)` attached to `s ∈ S_d`, and the graded-module construction itself. The latter
means module axioms across degrees, with `ℤ`-index casts. Estimated at several hours.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `F : X.Modules` with the monoidal structure of module sheaves (`⊗`, `α_`, `◁`, `▷`; module
  `Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSheafMonoidal`), and `Hartshorne.tmulSection` for the tensor of two
  sections.
- `Hartshorne.projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 n = 𝒪_X(n)` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjTwistingSheaf`).
- `Hartshorne.twistMulIso 𝒜 hgen n m : 𝒪(n) ⊗ 𝒪(m) ≅ 𝒪(n + m)`, with
  `Hartshorne.twistMulIso_assoc`, the pointwise formula `Hartshorne.twistMulIso_hom_tmulSection`
  (`(a/s, b/t) ↦ ab/(st)`), and `Hartshorne.tensor₃_hom_ext`: maps out of `(F ⊗ G) ⊗ H` are
  determined on sections `(f ⊗ g) ⊗ h` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjTwistCompatibility`). These
  need `S` generated by `S_1` over `S_0` (`hgen`).

## Lean gaps (suggested route)

1. **Global sections from `S_d`.** For `s ∈ S_d`, the section `x ↦ s/1` of `𝒪(d) = S(d)~` over
   `⊤` (an `IsFraction` with constant numerator `s ∈ S(d)_0` and denominator `1 ∈ S_0`). Prove
   `twistMulIso(s̃ ⊗ t̃) = (st)~` from `twistMulIso_hom_tmulSection` and `section_ext`.
2. **Multiplication maps as sheaf morphisms.** Take
   `μ_s : F(n) ⟶ F(n + d)`, the composite `F(n) ≅ F(n) ⊗ 𝒪 ⟶ F(n) ⊗ 𝒪(d) ≅ F ⊗ (𝒪(n) ⊗ 𝒪(d))
   ⟶ F(n + d)`, where `F(n) = F ⊗ 𝒪(n)` and the middle map is tensoring with `s̃ : 𝒪 ⟶ 𝒪(d)`.
   Prove additivity in `s`, `μ_{st} = μ_s ≫ μ_t ≫ eqToHom` (from step 1 and `twistMulIso_assoc`),
   and `μ_1 = eqToHom`. Prove these as equalities of morphisms via `tensor₃_hom_ext`, not on
   global sections.
3. **The graded module.** Define `Γ_*(F) = ⨁_{n : ℤ} Γ(X, F(n))`. One route is Mathlib's
   `DirectSum.Gmodule` with `ι_A = ℕ` acting on `ι_M = ℤ` (it needs a `VAdd ℕ ℤ`), with
   `GSMul S_d × Γ(F(n)) → Γ(F(n + d))` given by `μ_s` on global sections. The `Gmodule` axioms are
   step 2 transported through `Sigma.ext`. Then transfer `Module (⨁ S_d) Γ_*(F)` along
   `DirectSum.decompose 𝒜`, and record the grading as `SetLike.GradedSMul`-style membership.
