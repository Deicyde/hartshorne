# Eliminating the base locus of a linear system — implementation note

Module: `Hartshorne/Scheme/BaseLocusBlowup.lean` (imported by the library root). Sorry-free,
standard axioms only.

## Statement shape

- Setting: `X` Noetherian (the book's hypothesis; the blowup needs it), `L` invertible,
  `s : Fin (n + 1) → Γ(L, ⊤)`. Projective space is `projectiveSpaceOverScheme n Y` over an
  arbitrary base `p : X ⟶ Y` (the book's `ℙⁿ` with an unnamed base; `Y = Spec ℤ` recovers it).
- The base ideal `Hartshorne.baseIdeal hL s` is `baseLocusIdeal hL (Set.range s)` (from the III.10
  linear-system article) transported to a `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf`;
  `baseLocusIdeal_range_ideal_eq_span` is the book's local description (generated by the `ψ(sᵢ)`
  where `ψ` trivializes `L`). Coherence is `SerreAffineness.isCoherent_toModules` (every
  quasi-coherent ideal on a locally Noetherian scheme is coherent).
- `L'` (`baseBlowupSheaf`) is the subsheaf `π^* L · I𝒪_{X̃}` of `π^* L`, built as
  `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.toModules (powSmul I𝒪_{X̃} 1 ⊤)` with `E = π^* L`; `baseBlowupSheafι` is the
  inclusion (with a `Mono` instance). This is the book's "image of `π^* L ⊗ I𝒪_{X̃}` in `π^* L`";
  no tensor product is formed.
- The morphism `φ` on `U = linearSystemDomain hL s` enters `baseBlowupMorphism_restrict` through
  its Theorem 7.1 characterization (any `φ` with `φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L|_U` carrying `xᵢ` to `sᵢ|_U`), so it
  applies in particular to the unique such morphism.
- The linear-system sentence is `isBasePointFree_baseBlowup`: for `s` a family in
  `globalSectionsOverField q L`, the linear system of `L'` (structure morphism `π ≫ q`) spanned by
  the `π^* sᵢ` is base-point-free.

## Proof route

Follows the book. Key private lemma `isTrivializingSection_powSmul`: if `ε` trivializes `E` on
`W`, `h` is a unit of the total quotient ring on `W` and generates `I` on every affine open of `W`,
then `h ε` trivializes `I · E` on `W`. Surjectivity glues local quotients through
`Scheme.exists_toK_eq_of_locally` (the total quotient sheaf), so no hand-written gluing.
`exists_localData` produces, near each point, the affine `W₀ ⊆ π⁻¹(V)` with the exceptional ideal
generated both by the `hᵢ = π^♯ gᵢ` and by one `a` (from
`IsLocallyPrincipalNonZeroDivisor.exists_isUnit` applied to Proposition 7.13(a)). From `hᵢ = dᵢ a`
and `a = ∑ cᵢ hᵢ` one gets `∑ cᵢ dᵢ = 1`, so some `D(dᵢ)` contains the point and `π^* sᵢ`
trivializes `L'` there; this gives invertibility and generation at once.

Compatibility: over `π⁻¹(U)` every section of `π^* L` lies in `L'` (the base ideal is the unit
ideal over `U = centerCompl`), so the restriction of `baseBlowupSheafι` is bijective on sections
(`ModuleGluing.isIso_of_bijective`), and its pullback along `(π⁻¹ U).ι` is an isomorphism via
`restrictFunctorIsoPullback`. Both `(π⁻¹ U).ι ≫ φ̃` and `π|_{π⁻¹U} ≫ φ` are then defined by the
sections `(ι ≫ π)^* sᵢ` (`exists_iso_of_comp`, `pullbackComp` section lemmas), and Theorem 7.1's
uniqueness identifies them. That `π|_{π⁻¹U}` is an isomorphism is Proposition 7.13(b) after
`linearSystemDomain_eq_centerCompl`.
