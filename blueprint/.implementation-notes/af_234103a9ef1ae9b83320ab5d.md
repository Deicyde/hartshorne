# Embedded degree equals divisor degree: implementation note

## Status

Partial (blocked). Lean in `Hartshorne.Scheme.EmbeddedCurveDegree`, namespace `Hartshorne`.
Sorry-free. Nothing is recorded in `lean:`: the article's theorem itself is not yet proved.

Setting used: $k$ algebraically closed, $X$ a nonsingular curve over $k$
(`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsNonsingularCurve k X`, with `[X.Over (Spec k)]` and `[IsIntegral X]`),
$\iota : X \to \mathbb{P}^n_k = \operatorname{Proj} k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ a closed immersion,
$\deg \iota(X)$ = `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree (ι.base (genericPoint X))` (the degree of the
closure of the image of the generic point, as in the projective-intersection-degree article),
$\deg D$ = `hX.divisorDegree D`, $\mathcal{O}(1)$ = `projTwistingSheaf 𝒜 1`, and
$\mathcal{O}_X(D)$ = `(Scheme.weilCartierEquiv hlf D).sheaf` with `hlf` from
`Scheme.isLocallyFactorial_of_isRegularLocalRing`. "$D$ very ample with embedding $\iota$" is the
hypothesis $\iota^*\mathcal{O}(1) \cong \mathcal{O}_X(D)$ (Hartshorne's definition of very ample).

## Proved declarations

- `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_of_isClosed`: a closed point of $\mathbb{P}^n_k$ has degree 1.
- `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_hyperplane`: a coordinate hyperplane $V(x_i)$ ($n \ge 1$) has
  degree 1.
- `weilDivisorProjectiveDegree_eq_divisorDegree`: on an embedded nonsingular curve, the
  embedding degree of a divisor equals its divisor degree.
- `nontrivial_of_isNonsingularCurve`, `isSeparated_of_isNonsingularCurve`,
  `pos_of_isClosedImmersion_of_isNonsingularCurve` (no curve embeds in $\mathbb{P}^0$).
- `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_eq_divisorDegree_hyperplane`: if $x_i$ does not vanish on
  $\iota(X)$, then $\deg \iota(X) = \deg(H \cdot X)$ for $H = V(x_i)$ (Exercise II.6.2(c) with
  $\deg H = 1$).
- `cartierToWeil_eq_projectiveIntersectionDivisor_hyperplane`: if $C$ is the divisor of zeros of
  the pulled-back coordinate section $\iota^* x_i$ of $\iota^*\mathcal{O}(1)$ (and
  $\iota^*\mathcal{O}(1)$ is isomorphic to some $\mathcal{O}_X(D_0)$), then the Weil divisor of
  $C$ is $H \cdot X$. Proof: on $\iota^{-1} D_+(x_j)$ the section $\iota^* x_i$ is
  $\iota^\#(x_i/x_j)$ times the frame $\iota^* x_j$
  (`ProjTwistingSheaf.restrict_degreeOneSection_eq_smul`, pulled back with `Ringed.etaSec_smul`),
  and any trivialization sends the frame to a local unit.
- `exists_linearEquivalent_projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_eq_divisorDegree`: if
  $\iota^*\mathcal{O}(1) \cong \mathcal{O}_X(D)$, there is an effective $E \sim D$ with
  $\deg \iota(X) = \deg E$ (namely $E = H \cdot X$, the divisor of zeros of $\iota^* x_i$, by
  II.7.7 in the form `Scheme.weil_isDivisorOfZeros`).

## Remaining goal

From `exists_linearEquivalent_projectiveSpectrumPointDegree_eq_divisorDegree`, the article's
theorem `projectiveSpectrumPointDegree (ι.base (genericPoint X)) = hX.divisorDegree D` needs only
`hX.divisorDegree E = hX.divisorDegree D` for $E \sim D$, i.e. principal divisors on a complete
(here projective) nonsingular curve have degree zero (Corollary II.6.10). That is the article
`schemes/divisors/curve-divisors/principal-divisor-degree-zero`, which is not formalized (its
prerequisite `complete-nonsingular-curve-projective` is not formalized either) and is not a
dependency of this article. Next route: once II.6.10 lands, state the final theorem with the same
hypotheses as the existence theorem above and finish with that corollary applied to $E - D$.
