# Thickenings of an affine formal scheme

## Status

Statement and proof written in Lean (candidate, pending review), module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.AffineFormalThickenings`. Sorry-free; axioms are the
standard three.

## Lean meaning

- $\mathcal{O}_{\widehat X}$ is `formalCompletionSheaf` of
  `specIdealSubsheaf A I` (definitionally the presheaf of `Spf A I`), and
  $\pi_n : \mathcal{O}_{\widehat X} \to \mathcal{O}_{Y_{n+1}}$ is
  `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionπ n` (the limit projection).
- $J$ is `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.formalCompletionIdeal`: over $V$, the
  kernel of $\pi_0$. It is a family of ideals stable under restriction
  (`formalCompletionIdeal_le_comap`) and local
  (`mem_formalCompletionIdeal_of_locally`). It is the completion of the
  ideal sheaf: on $U \cap |Y|$, $U$ affine, it is the image of the completed
  ideal $\widehat{I(U)}$ in $\widehat{B}$
  (`formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso_formalCompletionIdeal_eq_range`, via
  `completedIdealMap_range`). Since the book defines $J$ as
  $\varprojlim (\widetilde{I}/\widetilde{I}^{n})$, which equals
  $\ker(\mathcal{O}_{\widehat X} \to \mathcal{O}_{Y})$ by left exactness of
  inverse limits, this is used as the definition. The ideal is stored as a
  family of ideals of the section rings, not as a `PresheafOfModules.Submodule`.
- $J^{k}$ is `formalCompletionIdealPow k`, an instance of the generic
  `Hartshorne.sheafIdealPow`: sections lying locally in $J(W)^{k}$ (the ideal
  subsheaf generated by the presheaf $V \mapsto J(V)^{k}$).
- $\mathcal{O}_{\widehat X}/J^{k}$ is `formalCompletionQuotient k`, an
  instance of the generic `Hartshorne.idealQuotientSheaf`: the sheafification
  (Mathlib `presheafToSheaf`, valued in `CommRingCat`) of
  $V \mapsto \mathcal{O}(V)/J^{k}(V)$. So the isomorphism is one of sheaves of
  rings.
- $\widetilde{A/I^{n+1}}$ is $\mathcal{O}_{Y_{n+1}}$ transported to $|Y|$
  (`formalCompletionSheafObj n`), together with the scheme isomorphism
  $Y_{n+1} \cong \operatorname{Spec} A/I^{n+1}$ over $\operatorname{Spec} A$
  (`specIdealSubsheafNeighborhoodIso`, `specIdealSubsheafNeighborhoodIso_hom_comp`,
  from `specIdealSubsheaf_pow`).

## Proof route

On an affine open $U = \operatorname{Spec} B$, the affine-sections iso
`formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso` carries $\pi_n$ to reduction
$\widehat B \to B/I(U)^{n+1}$, whose kernel is $I(U)^{n+1}\widehat B$ by
`exists_adicCompletionQuotientRingEquiv` (`ker_evalₐ_eq_map`). Hence
$J(U\cap|Y|)^{n+1} = \ker \pi_n$ there (`formalCompletionIdeal_pow_eq_ker`)
and $\pi_n$ is surjective there (`surjective_formalCompletionπ_app`). The
opens $U \cap |Y|$ form a basis (`exists_affineOpens_preimage_le`), giving
`mem_formalCompletionIdealPow_iff` ($J^{n+1} = \ker\pi_n$ on every open) and
`isLocallySurjective_formalCompletionπ`. The generic
`idealQuotientSheafIso` (injective and locally surjective map from the
quotient presheaf, Mathlib `W_of_isLocallyBijective`) then gives
`formalCompletionQuotientIso`. These sheaf-level results hold for every
locally Noetherian scheme; `affineFormalThickenings` is the
$\operatorname{Spf}$ case.

Tilde exactness is not invoked directly: the surjectivity and kernel of
$\Gamma(U,\mathcal{O}_X) \to \Gamma(U\cap|Y|,\mathcal{O}_{Y_{n+1}})$ come
through the affine-sections module.
