# Affineness of reduced irreducible components

## Status

Proved, sorry-free, in `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ReducedComponentsAffine`. The article's theorem is
`Hartshorne.isAffine_iff_forall_irreducibleComponents_isAffine` (axioms: `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).

## Structure of the proof

- Easy direction: `Hartshorne.isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_of_isAffine` (closed subscheme of
  an affine scheme is affine, via `isAffine_of_isAffineHom`) and
  `Hartshorne.forall_irreducibleComponents_isAffine_of_isAffine` (components written as
  `⟨Z, isClosed_of_mem_irreducibleComponents Z hZ⟩ : Closeds X`).
- Closed pushforward cohomology for scheme closed immersions:
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.pushforwardCohomologyIso` gives `H^n(X, i_* G) ≅ H^n(Z, G)` by
  transporting `closedPushforwardCohomologyIso` (III Lemma 2.10) along the homeomorphism
  `Z ≅ range i`; `ReducedComponentsAffine.isZero_H_pushforward` (vanishing for `n > 0` when `Z` is
  affine locally Noetherian and `G` quasi-coherent, III.3.5);
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.isZero_H1_of_shortExact_pushforward`.
- Induction on components: the two-piece case `ReducedComponentsAffine.TwoPieceAffine` implies
  the hard direction (`isAffine_of_forall_irreducibleComponents_of_twoPiece`, via
  `isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_finset_sup`, `isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_preimageCloseds`,
  `finite_irreducibleComponents_of_isNoetherian`).
- Serre's criterion: `twoPieceAffine_of_twoPieceSequences` (iii ⇒ i of
  `isAffine_tfae_sheafCohomology`) reduces the two-piece case to `TwoPieceSequences`;
  `isAffine_iff_of_twoPieceSequences` derives the iff from it.
- `ReducedComponentsAffine.twoPieceSequences`: `vanishingIdeal_mul_eq_bot` (`I_B I_A ⊆ I_A ⊓ I_B
  = vanishingIdeal (A ⊔ B) = nilradical = 0` on a reduced scheme); the sequence
  `prodSC I J : 0 ⟶ I J ⟶ J ⟶ J / I J ⟶ 0` with `I J = powSmul I 1 J.carrier` (the existing
  `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.powSmul` with exponent 1, quasi-coherent by `isQuasicoherent_powSmul`);
  `smul_eq_zero_of_mem_powSmul_one` (`I_B` kills `I_A J`) and `smul_eq_zero_cokernel_prodι`
  (`I_A` kills `J / I_A J`).
- `ReducedComponentsAffine.ClosedDescent.exists_isQuasicoherent_iso_pushforward`: for
  `I : Y.IdealSheafData` and a quasi-coherent `Q` killed by `I` (on affine opens), `Q ≅ i_* G` with
  `G` quasi-coherent on `I.subscheme`. Construction (`descent`): `G(V) = Q(up V)`, where
  `up V = (i '' Vᶜ)ᶜ` is the largest open of `Y` meeting `Z` in `V`. Sheaf condition
  (`isSheaf_upPresheaf`) by gluing in `Q` over the `up (V k)` together with `up ⊥ = Y ∖ Z`, where
  `Q` has no nonzero sections (`eq_zero_of_le_up_bot`). Module structure (`upModule`) by gluing
  the actions of local lifts over affine opens, as in Ex. 3.1's `redModules`.
  `descentHom : i_* G ⟶ Q` is restriction `Q(up (i⁻¹ U)) ⟶ Q(U)`, bijective because
  `up (i⁻¹ U) ≤ U ⊔ (Y ∖ Z)` (`res_injective_of_le_sup`, `res_surjective_of_le_sup`).
  Quasi-coherence via `isIso_fromTildeΓ_of_pow_smul` on the affine opens `i⁻¹ U`.

## Route notes

- Hartshorne prints no proof for Ex. 3.2. The article's last Proof depends on bullet describes the
  exact sequence comparing a union, its two pieces and their intersection; the Lean instead uses
  `0 ⟶ I_A J ⟶ J ⟶ J / I_A J ⟶ 0`, whose outer terms are pushforwards from the reduced induced
  subschemes on `B` and `A` (reported to Roadmap; the bullet is unchanged).
- The descent construction was chosen over `G = i^* Q` with the unit of
  `pullbackPushforwardAdjunction`: that route needs the compatibility of
  `pullbackStalkLinearEquiv` with the unit, which is not available. `ClosedDescent` generalizes
  Ex. 3.1's `ReductionAffine.redModules` (the case `I = nilradical`, where `up` is the inverse
  image along the homeomorphism); Ex. 3.1 could be refactored onto it.
