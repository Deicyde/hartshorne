# Affineness of reduced irreducible components

## Status

Partial. Everything below is sorry-free in `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ReducedComponentsAffine`;
the exercise itself (the `iff`) is not yet proved unconditionally, so no `lean:` is recorded.

## Proved

- Easy direction: `Hartshorne.isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_of_isAffine` (closed subscheme of
  an affine scheme is affine, via `isAffine_of_isAffineHom`) and
  `Hartshorne.forall_irreducibleComponents_isAffine_of_isAffine` (the article's forward
  direction, components written as `⟨Z, isClosed_of_mem_irreducibleComponents Z hZ⟩ : Closeds X`).
- Closed pushforward cohomology for scheme closed immersions:
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.pushforwardCohomologyIso` gives `H^n(X, i_* G) ≅ H^n(Z, G)` for
  `i : Z ⟶ X` a closed immersion and any `G : Z.Modules`, by transporting
  `closedPushforwardCohomologyIso` (III Lemma 2.10, closed subspace inclusion) along the
  homeomorphism `Z ≅ range i` (`sheafCohomologyPushforwardIsoOfIso`). The underlying abelian sheaf
  of `Scheme.Modules.pushforward i G` is definitionally the topological pushforward along
  `i.base`. `ReducedComponentsAffine.isZero_H_pushforward`: these vanish for `n > 0` when `Z` is
  affine locally Noetherian and `G` quasi-coherent (III.3.5).
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.isZero_H1_of_shortExact_pushforward`: `H^1` of the middle term of a
  short exact sequence whose outer terms are such pushforwards vanishes. These are general
  helpers that the sibling Ex. 3.1 can also use.
- Induction on components: `ReducedComponentsAffine.TwoPieceAffine` is the two-piece case
  (`Y` reduced Noetherian, `A ⊔ B = ⊤`, reduced induced subschemes on `A`, `B` affine ⇒ `Y`
  affine). `ReducedComponentsAffine.isAffine_of_forall_irreducibleComponents_of_twoPiece` derives
  the hard direction from it by induction over a finset of closed subsets
  (`isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_finset_sup`), finitely many components
  (`finite_irreducibleComponents_of_isNoetherian`). The key transfer
  `isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_preimageCloseds`: for `A ≤ W`, the reduced induced subscheme
  on `A` inside the one on `W` is isomorphic to the one in `X` (factor through it by
  `existsUnique_reducedInducedSubschemeι_factorization`; the factor is a surjective closed
  immersion onto a reduced scheme, so an isomorphism by `isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_of_surjective`).
  The reduced induced subscheme on `⊤` of a reduced scheme is the scheme itself
  (`isAffine_of_isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_top`).
- `ReducedComponentsAffine.TwoPieceSequences` and
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.twoPieceAffine_of_twoPieceSequences`: Serre's criterion (iii ⇒ i of
  `isAffine_tfae_sheafCohomology`) reduces the two-piece case to producing, for every
  quasi-coherent ideal sheaf `J`, a short exact sequence `0 ⟶ F' ⟶ J ⟶ F'' ⟶ 0` with `F'` a
  pushforward of a quasi-coherent module from the reduced induced subscheme on `B` and `F''` one
  from that on `A`. `ReducedComponentsAffine.isAffine_iff_of_twoPieceSequences` is the article's
  statement conditional on `TwoPieceSequences`.

## Remaining goal

Prove `ReducedComponentsAffine.TwoPieceSequences`; then the article's theorem is
`isAffine_iff_of_twoPieceSequences twoPieceSequences X` (rename/restate as the final declaration).

Intended route (the book's): `I_A ⊓ I_B = vanishingIdeal (A ⊔ B) = ⊥` since `Y` is reduced, so
`I_B · I_A = 0`. Take `F' = I_A · J ⊆ J` and `F'' = J / I_A J`. Then `F'` is killed by `I_B` and
`F''` by `I_A`. Missing infrastructure (not in Mathlib or the project as of this note):

1. For a closed immersion `i : Z ⟶ Y` with ideal `I` and a quasi-coherent `𝒪_Y`-module `M`, the
   unit `M ⟶ i_* i^* M` of `Scheme.Modules.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction` is an epimorphism with
   kernel `I · M`; in particular an isomorphism when `I · M = 0`. A stalk check is the natural
   route: `Hartshorne.pullbackStalkLinearEquiv` gives `(i^* M)_z ≅ 𝒪_{Z,z} ⊗ M_{i z}`, but its
   compatibility with the unit is not recorded; stalks of `i_*` at points off the image vanish
   (`closedExtensionByZeroOffImageStalkIso` for the topological closed inclusion).
2. Products `I · M` of a quasi-coherent ideal subsheaf with a module, and their quasi-coherence.

Quasi-coherence of `i^* M` is `Hartshorne` II.5.8(a) (`Scheme/PullbackQuasicoherent.lean`).
