# The regular-local differential criterion

## Status

Blocked. A sorry-free partial module,
`Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.RegularLocalDifferentialCriterion`, proves
Lemma 8.9 and both directions of Hartshorne's Theorem 8.8 except for one
missing input: that a regular local ring is an integral domain. None of its
declarations is recorded in the article frontmatter. The module is not
imported by the library root yet.

## Blockers

1. **The article statement is false as written.** It says that $B$ is
   regular of dimension $r$ if and only if $\Omega_{B/k}$ is free of rank $r$.
   The backward implication fails. Take $k$ perfect of characteristic
   $p > 0$ and $B = k[x]/(x^p)$. This $B$ is local with residue field $k$ and
   is a quotient of a finitely generated algebra. Since $d(x^p) = p x^{p-1}dx = 0$,
   $\Omega_{B/k} = B\,dx$ is free of rank $1$. But $\dim B = 0$ while
   $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2 = k\bar x$ has dimension $1$, so $B$ is not
   regular. Hartshorne's Theorem 8.8 actually reads: $\Omega_{B/k}$ is a free
   $B$-module of rank $\dim B$ if and only if $B$ is a regular local ring.
   The article needs a revision to that statement. The forward implication of
   the current wording (regular of dimension $r$ implies free of rank $r$) is
   true, but only because regular local rings are domains (blocker 2).

2. **Missing prerequisite: regular local rings are domains.** Hartshorne's
   proof of "regular implies free" passes to the fraction field of $B$ and
   applies Lemma 8.9, which needs a domain. He cites Matsumura for the fact
   that a regular local ring is an integral domain (see also Matsumura,
   *Commutative Ring Theory*, Theorem 14.3).
   The pinned Mathlib only has the definition of a regular local ring
   (`Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean`) and no domain result. No
   roadmap article states it either. The schemes/divisors/cartier-picard
   regular-locally-factorial article mentions "every regular local ring is a
   UFD" only inside its own proof. The usual proof inducts on $\dim A$. Pick
   $x \in \mathfrak m$ outside $\mathfrak m^2$ and outside every minimal prime
   (prime avoidance). Then $A/(x)$ is regular of dimension $\dim A - 1$, hence
   a domain by induction. So $(x)$ is prime and contains a minimal prime
   $\mathfrak p$. Since $x \notin \mathfrak p$, we get $\mathfrak p = x\mathfrak p$,
   and Nakayama gives $\mathfrak p = 0$.

3. **Unreached dependency: dimension theory.** The step $\dim B =
   \operatorname{trdeg}_k K(B)$ uses Theorem I.1.8A. It needs part (a), owned
   by affine-varieties/dim-fg-domain, and the height of a maximal ideal, which
   follows from part (b), owned by affine-varieties/dim-formula-catenary.
   The article's Depends on lists do not reach either one, even transitively.
   The partial module already uses `Hartshorne.exists_ringKrullDim_eq_trdeg`
   from dim-fg-domain and `Hartshorne.height_eq_ringKrullDim_of_isMaximal`.
   The latter is an unlisted helper in the dim-formula-catenary module. Both
   articles need to be added as proof dependencies.

## Checked Lean (partial module)

- `Hartshorne.free_of_finrank_residueField_eq_finrank_fractionField`:
  Lemma 8.9, without the Noetherian hypothesis, which the proof does not use.
  It uses the fibres $\kappa \otimes M$ and $K \otimes M$, in Mathlib's
  tensor order. For the article it should be restated with an unused
  `_`-named Noetherian instance, or the article sentence about it adjusted.
- `Hartshorne.finrank_cotangentSpace_eq_finrank_residueField_tensor_kaehlerDifferential`:
  $\dim_\kappa \mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2 = \dim_\kappa(\kappa \otimes \Omega_{B/k})$,
  from `Hartshorne.localCotangentEquivDifferentials`.
- `Hartshorne.isRegularLocalRing_of_free_kaehlerDifferential`: if
  $\Omega_{B/k}$ is free of rank $\dim B$, then $B$ is regular. This needs
  neither perfectness nor a domain.
- `Hartshorne.exists_ringKrullDim_eq_trdeg_of_essFiniteType`: for a local
  domain $B$, essentially of finite type over $k$, with $k \to B/\mathfrak m$
  surjective, $\dim B = \operatorname{trdeg}_k \operatorname{Frac} B$, a
  natural number. It is stated with $k$ and $B$ in one universe, because the
  dimension-theory declarations are single-universe.
- `Hartshorne.free_kaehlerDifferential_of_isRegularLocalRing_of_isDomain`: a
  regular local domain $B$, essentially of finite type over a perfect field
  $k \cong B/\mathfrak m$, has $\Omega_{B/k}$ free of rank $\dim B$. It uses
  Mathlib's `exists_isTranscendenceBasis_and_isSeparable_of_perfectField`,
  `Hartshorne.trdeg_le_rank_kaehlerDifferential` and
  `KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale`.

All five depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The
`unusedArguments` lint is clean.

## Next route

Once blockers 1–3 are resolved, the article theorem is
`IsRegularLocalRing B ↔ Module.Free B Ω[B⁄k] ∧ (Module.finrank B Ω[B⁄k] : WithBot ℕ∞) = ringKrullDim B`,
under `[PerfectField k] [IsLocalRing B] [Algebra.EssFiniteType k B]` and
bijectivity of `algebraMap k (ResidueField B)`. The forward direction is
`free_kaehlerDifferential_of_isRegularLocalRing_of_isDomain`, given the domain
instance from blocker 2. The backward direction is
`isRegularLocalRing_of_free_kaehlerDifferential`. Removing the
single-universe restriction would need ULift transport of the dimension
theory, or universe-polymorphic versions of the I.1.8A declarations.
