# The Euler sequence on projective space

## Route

Hartshorne's chartwise maps `φ_i : Ω|_{U_i} → M~|_{U_i}`, `d(x_j/x_i) ↦ x_i⁻²(x_i e_j − x_j e_i)`,
are realised by a single global morphism `α : Ω ⟶ 𝒪(-1)^{n+1}` obtained from the universal
property of `Ω_{X/Y}` applied to the derivations `f ↦ ∂f/∂x_j` (quotient rule on degree-zero
fractions). Gluing is then automatic. `β = ∑ x_j` is assembled from multiplication by the
coordinates `𝒪(-1) ⟶ 𝒪`. `𝒪(-1)^{n+1}` is the biproduct in `X.Modules`
(`Abelian.hasFiniteBiproducts` as a local instance).

## Done (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.EulerSequenceMaps`)

- `Hartshorne.EulerSequence.pD`, `pD_mk`, `pD_mul`: `∂/∂x_j` on localizations of the polynomial
  ring, via `SpecKaehler.pointD`.
- `ptD`, `ptMul`, `sum_ptMul_ptD` (Euler's identity at a point), `toLoc`.
- `secD`, `secMul`, `mulX`, `toBase` (`Proj.toSpecZero ≫ Spec.map (A → S₀)`),
  `toBase_appTop_val` (global functions from `A` are constants), `secD_kills`, `derivD`,
  `alphaj`, `alphaj_d`, `alpha`, `beta`, `alpha_beta : alpha ≫ beta = 0`.

## Done (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.EulerSequenceChainRule`)

- Sections of the biproduct: `comp`, `incl`, `eq_sum_incl_comp`, `E_ext`, `comp_alpha`,
  `beta_app`.
- `exists_X_notMem` (the charts `D₊(x_i)` cover), `fracSec` (`r / x_i^k`), `invX` (`1 / x_i`),
  `cstSec` (constants from `A`), `epi_beta : Epi beta`.
- `cD`, `yS`, `cD_yS` (`x_i ∂(x_m/x_i)/∂x_l = δ_lm` for `l ≠ i`), `PCR` and its closure lemmas,
  `chain_rule`: `d f = ∑_l (x_i ∂f/∂x_l) d(x_l/x_i)` on any open `W ⊆ D₊(x_i)`.
  The abstract `chain_div` avoids slow elaboration over the module-sheaf section types.

## Remaining

- Exactness in the middle and injectivity of `α`, sectionwise on `V ⊆ D₊(x_i)`:
  the inverse on the kernel is `ψ(s) = ∑_{j≠i} (x_i s_j) d(x_j/x_i)`. `α ∘ ψ = id` on the
  kernel is pointwise algebra. `ψ ∘ α ∘ d = d` is the chain rule
  `d f = ∑_{j≠i} x_i ∂f/∂x_j · d(x_j/x_i)` (local fractions `f = a/b`, dehomogenise `a, b` as
  polynomials in the `x_j/x_i`). Injectivity of `α` then uses that every section of `Ω` is
  locally the image of the Kähler presheaf (local surjectivity of `toSheafify`). Glue over the
  cover `D₊(x_i)` and conclude with a kernel-fork argument (`ShortComplex.exact_of_f_is_kernel`).
- The final statement on `projectiveSpaceScheme A n`.
