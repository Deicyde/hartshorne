# Reconstruction from infinitesimal reductions — implementation note

Status: partial progress, no `lean:` target recorded (second partial pass). Module:
`Hartshorne/Scheme/CoherentFormalModuleReconstruction.lean` (imported by the library root).
Hartshorne II.9, Proposition 9.6(a).

## Infrastructure findings (before this leaf)

- There was no product `J F` of an ideal subsheaf with a module on a general ringed space. The
  existing `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.powSmul` is scheme-only (quasi-coherent ideal data on affine
  opens), so it does not apply on a formal scheme.
- There was no way to view a module killed by `J ^ (n + 1)` as a module on the scheme
  `(X, 𝒪_X / J ^ (n + 1))`. The quotient sheaf of rings `𝒪_X / J` is a sheafification, so a direct
  module structure over it would have to be glued by hand. (Resolved in the second pass with
  Mathlib's `PresheafOfModules.sheafify`: `IdealSubsheaf.Annihilates.quotientModule`.) The morphism
  `(X, 𝒪_X / J) ⟶ X` exists only as a private definition in `PowersOfIdealOfDefinition.lean`
  (`quotientSchemeHom`).
- Instance synthesis does not see that the underlying spaces of `h.quotientScheme` and `X` are the
  same (they agree only after unfolding `quotientScheme`). So the generic instances for
  `Opens.map`, continuity, and `PresheafOfModules.pushforward ... IsRightAdjoint` do not fire.
  They are supplied by hand (`isContinuous_opensMap_quotientScheme`, an instance that is found in
  type signatures but has to be passed with `letI` in bodies;
  `isRightAdjoint_presheafPushforward_quotientRingCatSheafHom`;
  `isRightAdjoint_quotientPushforward`).
- `ringCatSheafOf R` expects a `Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology T) CommRingCat`, but ideal
  subsheaves are stated over `TopCat.Sheaf CommRingCat T`, which is a semireducible `def`. So every
  goal mentioning a module over `ringCatSheafOf R` is type-incorrect at instance transparency, and
  `rw` fails, for example with `Category.assoc` or `_assoc` lemmas. `simp only` and term-mode
  `congrArg`/`.trans` proofs work. Making `TopCat.Sheaf` locally reducible is refused without an
  unsafe-reducibility option, which was not used. The same mismatch separates
  `I.formalCompletionRingCatSheaf` from `ringCatSheafOf I.formalCompletionSheaf` (equal only at
  default transparency); the primed `moduleCompletion'` abbreviations below retype the completion
  objects so that zero morphisms, cokernels and limits are found in one category.

## Proved (sorry-free, standard axioms only)

General ringed space `(T, R)`, ideal subsheaf `J`, `R`-module `M` (`IdealSubsheaf` namespace):

- `sectionsSmul J M V = J(V) M(V)`, `map_mem_sectionsSmul`. The submodule sheaf
  `smulSubmodule J M` (sections lying locally in `J(V) M(V)`, the image of `J ⊗ M ⟶ M`), with
  `mem_smulSubmodule_iff`, `sectionsSmul_le_smulSubmodule`, `smulSubmodule_isLocal`,
  `smulSubmodule_mono`. Also `smulModules`, `smulι`, `smulInclusion`, `smulInclusion_smulι`, and
  `powSubmodule J M k = J ^ (k + 1) M`.
- Ideal powers: `pow_le_pow_left` (`J ⊆ J'` gives `J ^ k ⊆ J' ^ k`), `pow_mul_le_pow_pow`
  (`J ^ (a m) ⊆ (J ^ a) ^ m`), and `exists_pow_succ_le_pow_succ`.
- `powQuotient J M n = M / J ^ (n + 1) M` (a cokernel), with `powQuotientπ`, `powQuotientMap`,
  `powQuotientπ_powQuotientMap`, `powQuotientDiagram`, `powQuotientCone`, and the canonical map
  `toLimitPowQuotient : M ⟶ lim_n M / J ^ (n + 1) M` (`toLimitPowQuotient_π`).
- Cofinality: `powQuotientDesc` (the induced map when `J ^ (k + 1) M ⊆ J' ^ (n + 1) M`) with
  `_self`, `_comp`, `powQuotientDiagram_map_eq`, `powQuotientMap_eq_powQuotientDesc`,
  `limit_π_powQuotientDesc`. Also `limitPowQuotientMap` (with `_π`, `_comp`, `_self`,
  `toLimitPowQuotient_limitPowQuotientMap`), `limitPowQuotientIsoOfCofinal`, and
  `isIso_toLimitPowQuotient_iff_of_cofinal`.

Noetherian formal scheme `X`, `h : IsIdealOfDefinition X J` (`IsIdealOfDefinition` namespace):

- `isIso_toLimitPowQuotient_iff`: for two ideals of definition, `F ⟶ lim F / J₁ ^ (n + 1) F` is an
  isomorphism iff `F ⟶ lim F / J₂ ^ (n + 1) F` is. Proved from Prop. 9.5(a)
  (`exists_pow_le_and_pow_le`). This is the "replace `J` by the completed ideal" step of the book's
  proof.
- `quotientRingCatSheafHom h : 𝒪_X ⟶ 𝒪_X / J`, onto `h.quotientScheme.ringCatSheaf` (identity on
  spaces). Also `quotientPushforward`, `quotientPullback`, and `quotientAdjunction`.
- `reduction h F n`: `F_n` as a module on `(h.pow_succ n).quotientScheme`. It is the pullback of
  `F / J ^ (n + 1) F` along `𝒪_X ⟶ 𝒪_X / J ^ (n + 1)`, by analogy with
  `moduleCompletionNeighborhood`. `toReduction h F n : F / J ^ (n + 1) F ⟶ (F_n pushed to X)` is
  the unit.

Modules killed by an ideal subsheaf (general ringed space, `IdealSubsheaf` namespace):

- `Annihilates J M` (`J(V) M(V) = 0` for all `V`), `Annihilates.smul_eq_zero`,
  `annihilates_cokernel_smulι` (`J` kills `M / J M`; uses that an epimorphism of sheaves of
  modules is locally surjective and that a locally vanishing section vanishes).
- `isLocallySurjective_quotientπ` (`R ⟶ R / J` is locally surjective).
- `Annihilates.quotientModule`: a module killed by `J` as a module over `R / J`, built with
  Mathlib's `PresheafOfModules.sheafify` from the presheaf of `R(V) ⧸ J(V)`-modules
  (`Annihilates.sectionsModule`, `Annihilates.presheafModule`, `quotientPresheafRingCat`,
  `quotientPresheafToSheaf`); its underlying sheaf is that of `M`, and
  `Annihilates.quotientπ_smul` says the class of `r` acts as `r`.

For an ideal of definition (`IsIdealOfDefinition` namespace):

- `isLocallySurjective_quotientRingCatSheafHom`; `quotientPushforward` is `Faithful` and `Full`
  (fullness from Mathlib's `PresheafOfModules.restrictHomEquivOfIsLocallySurjective`).
- `quotientModule h hQ`, `quotientPushforwardQuotientModuleIso`, and
  `isIso_quotientAdjunction_unit_app`: for `Q` killed by `J` the unit `Q ⟶ i_* i^* Q` is an
  isomorphism (Mathlib's `Adjunction.isIso_unit_app_of_iso`).
- `isIso_toReduction`: `toReduction h F n` is an isomorphism for every `F` and `n`. This is the
  former missing piece 1, and the middle conjunct of the planned statement.

Chart computation (`QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf` namespace; `X` locally Noetherian, `G`

## Remaining goal (the article's statement)

Planned statement, for `X` Noetherian, `h : IsIdealOfDefinition X J`, and
`hF : IsCoherentFormalModule F`:

```
(∀ n, IsCoherent (h.reduction F n)) ∧ (∀ n, IsIso (h.toReduction F n)) ∧
  IsIso (J.toLimitPowQuotient F)
```

The middle conjunct is `isIso_toReduction` (done, for every `F`). Missing:

1. Locality (former piece 2). Restriction to an open `U` (`structureModuleRestrict`) and transport
   along a chart isomorphism `e : U ≅ Ŷ` (`structureModulePushforward e.inv`) must commute with
   `smulSubmodule` (with the ideal restricted, resp. transported: needs `IdealSubsheaf`
   restriction and transport constructions, none exist yet), with cokernels (restriction to an
   open and pushforward along an isomorphism are exact) and with limits (they are right
   adjoints), compatibly with `toLimitPowQuotient`. Then `IsIso` of a morphism of sheaves of
   modules can be checked on the finite chart cover (e.g. via `SheafOfModules.toSheaf` reflecting
   isomorphisms and stalks).
2. Cofinality on a chart (former part of piece 3). On `Ŷ = I.formalCompletion` the transported
   `J|_U` and `J₀ = formalCompletionIdealSubsheaf I` must be adically cofinal
   (`∃ a, J'^a ≤ J₀` and `∃ b, J₀^b ≤ J'`), so that `isIso_toLimitPowQuotient_moduleCompletion`
   gives the iso for `J'` through `IdealSubsheaf.isIso_toLimitPowQuotient_iff_of_cofinal`. The
   global `IsIdealOfDefinition.isIso_toLimitPowQuotient_iff` does not apply directly, since it
   compares two ideals of definition of one formal scheme. Route: show that `J|_U` transported
   is an ideal of definition of `Ŷ` (full support and the scheme structure restrict to opens and
   transport along isomorphisms) and that `J₀` is one (`formalCompletionQuotientIso`, full
   support of `𝒪_Ŷ / J₀`), then apply Proposition 9.5(a) (`exists_pow_le_and_pow_le`) on `Ŷ`
   viewed as a `NoetherianFormalScheme` (Noetherian `Y`).
3. Coherence of `F_n = h.reduction F n` for an arbitrary ideal of definition `J`. On a chart,
   `F / J^(n+1) F` is a quotient of `Ĝ / J₀^m Ĝ ≅ G_(m-1)` for `m` with `J₀^m ⊆ J^(n+1)`
   (cofinality, item 2), which is coherent on `Y_m`; `(X, 𝒪_X / J^(n+1))` restricted to `U` is a
   closed subscheme of `Y_m` and `F_n` is the corresponding quotient. Needs: pullback along
   `𝒪_X ⟶ 𝒪_X / J^(n+1)` commutes with restriction to opens, comparison of the open subscheme of
   `(h.pow_succ n).quotientScheme` over `U` with a closed subscheme of `Y_m`, and coherence of a
   quotient of a coherent module on a Noetherian scheme by a quasi-coherent submodule.
