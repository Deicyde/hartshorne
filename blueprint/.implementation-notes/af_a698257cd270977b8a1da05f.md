# On a normal variety codimension-one rational equivalence is linear equivalence

## Partial progress

A complete draft is in `src/Hartshorne/Intersection/RationalEquivalenceCodimOne.lean` (module
`Hartshorne.Intersection.RationalEquivalenceCodimOne`). Its first focused build failed only on
trivial points (the identity of a scheme applied to a point is closed by `rfl`, `zero_le` takes no
explicit argument, a deprecated `ENat` lemma name), which have been edited but not yet rebuilt.

Planned recorded declarations:
`Hartshorne.rationalEquivalenceSubgroup_one_eq_map_principalDivisorSubgroup` (the rational-equivalence
subgroup of codimension-one cycles is the image of the principal divisors),
`Hartshorne.rationallyEquivalent_weilDivisorToCodimOneCycles_iff`,
`Hartshorne.codimOneCycleClassEquivWeilClassGroup` (codimension-one cycles modulo the
rational-equivalence subgroup are additively equivalent to the Weil class group, stated without the
graded Chow group) and `Hartshorne.codimOneCycleClassEquivWeilClassGroup_mk`.

Helpers: transport of normality, function fields, orders of vanishing and principal divisors along an
isomorphism (open immersion); the raw pushforward along an isomorphism is the identity on
coefficients; a closed immersion into an integral scheme with image of codimension zero is an
isomorphism; a normal integral separated scheme of finite type over a field satisfies `(*)`; Weil
divisors are exactly the codimension-one cycles. The normalization map of a normal integral scheme
is an isomorphism by the existing `isIso_fromNormalization_of_isNormal`.
