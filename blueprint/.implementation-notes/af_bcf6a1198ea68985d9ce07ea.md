# Hartshorne's smoothness criterion

## Target

For `f : X ⟶ Y` of finite type over a field `k` (objects of `Over (Spec k)` with
`LocallyOfFiniteType ∧ QuasiCompact`, as in the sibling articles), Mathlib's
`SmoothOfRelativeDimension n f.left` is equivalent to: `Flat f.left`; every irreducible
component of every `f.left.fiber y` has `topologicalKrullDim = n`; and
`finrank κ(x) (κ(x) ⊗ (relativeDifferentials f.left)_x) = n` for every `x`.

## Remaining goal

The converse needs `Smooth (f.left.fiberToSpecResidueField y)` from the fibre-dimension and
differential clauses: the scheme form of the differential criterion, applied to the fibres,
plus the identification of the differential fibres of `X_y` with those of `Ω_{X/Y}`.

## Next route

Work on affine charts as Mathlib's `Smooth.of_smooth_fiberToSpecResidueField` does: reduce to
`Spec B → Spec A` and show `Algebra.FormallySmooth κ(p) (p.Fiber B)` for every prime `p` of `A`
with `smooth_of_forall_finrank_le` on `T = κ(p) ⊗_A B`.

- Differential clause: `Ω_{T/κ(p)} ≅ T ⊗_B Ω_{B/A}` (`KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquiv`
  for the pushout), so `κ(P) ⊗_T Ω_{T/κ(p)} ≅ κ(P) ⊗_B Ω_{B/A}`; compare with the fibre of
  `Ω_{X/Y}` at the image point through `RelDiffLocal.affineIso`, `Hartshorne.rank_fibre_restrict`
  and `Hartshorne.FreeLocusAux.isLocalizedModule_germTopLinear`.
- Dimension clause: `Spec T` is an open subscheme of `X_y`
  (`isPullback_fiberToSpecResidueField_of_isPullback`, `Spec.fiberToSpecResidueFieldIso`). On
  `X_y`, `coheight z + height z = n` for every point by
  `FlatEquidimensional.coheight_add_height_eq_of_equidim` with `exists_le_height_eq` and
  `catenaryAt_of_finiteType`; transport with `height_apply_of_isOpenImmersion`,
  `height_spec_eq`, `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight` and `IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`
  (as in `topologicalKrullDim_of_mem_irreducibleComponents_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`).
- Then `Scheme.smoothOfRelativeDimension_of_flat_of_smooth_fiber` gives the converse, and the
  iff combines it with `flat_and_fiber_dim_and_finrank_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`.

Instance pitfalls met: quotient rings and `Localization.AtPrime` carry instance paths that are
not reducibly equal to the `CommRing`-derived ones. Build ring equivalences with
`IsLocalization.ringEquivOfRingEquiv` inside the proof that consumes them, give implicit ring
arguments explicitly (`IsRegularLocalRing.of_ringEquiv (R := _)`), avoid putting a custom
algebra structure on a quotient ring (present `T_q` through `Localization.localRingHom`
instead), and use `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` where Mathlib does.

## Split (resolved by the roadmap revision)

The split proposal and its review amendments are implemented as new articles in
`cohomology/smooth-morphisms/criteria/`, each with its own implementation note naming the
candidate declarations above (none recorded in `lean:` yet; a later Formalize pass reviews and
records them):

1. independent-differentials-regular-quotient (background): `RegularQuotientCotangentIndependent`
   and `JacobianRegularQuotient` declarations. Cites regular-hypersurface-local-criterion as its
   one-element case.
2. smooth-over-field-component-dimension (background, Stacks 10.140):
   `topologicalKrullDim_of_mem_irreducibleComponents_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension` and the
   `SubmersivePresentation` declarations. Proof depends on (1), dim-formula-catenary,
   flat-equidimensional-total-space-iff-fibers.
3. differential-criterion-smooth-over-field (background, Stacks 00TQ), only the direction
   "fibre rank at most the local dimension implies smooth": `isSmoothAt_of_finrank_le`,
   `smooth_of_forall_finrank_le`. Proof depends on (1), dim-formula-catenary,
   regular-local-ring-domain, the conormal exact sequence, and (6).
4. flat-smooth-fibers-smooth (Mathlib article, Stacks 01V8,
   `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth.of_smooth_fiberToSpecResidueField`); smooth-iff-geometrically-regular-fibers
   now links it.
5. differential-criterion-smooth-scheme-over-field (bridged): the scheme form of (3), no Lean yet;
   its note carries the dimension-transport and differential-fibre route (also kept under "Next
   route" here).
6. local-jacobian-criterion-formally-smooth (Mathlib article,
   `Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_cotangentComplexBaseChange`), used by (3).

This article's `## Proof depends on` gained (2), (3), (4) and (5); the converse now reads: apply
(5) to each fibre `X_y` (identifying its differential fibres with those of `Ω_{X/Y}`), then
`Scheme.smoothOfRelativeDimension_of_flat_of_smooth_fiber`, then combine with
`flat_and_fiber_dim_and_finrank_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`.
