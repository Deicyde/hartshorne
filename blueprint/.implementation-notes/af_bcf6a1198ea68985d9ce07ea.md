# Hartshorne's smoothness criterion

## Target

For `f : X ⟶ Y` of finite type over a field `k` (objects of `Over (Spec k)` with
`LocallyOfFiniteType ∧ QuasiCompact`, as in the sibling articles), Mathlib's
`SmoothOfRelativeDimension n f.left` is equivalent to: `Flat f.left`; every irreducible
component of every `f.left.fiber y` has `topologicalKrullDim = n`; and
`finrank κ(x) (κ(x) ⊗ (relativeDifferentials f.left)_x) = n` for every `x`.

## Remaining goal

The converse needs `Smooth (f.left.fiberToSpecResidueField y)` from the fibre-dimension and
differential clauses. This is the differential criterion for smoothness over a field (Stacks,
Section 10.140, tag 00TQ), an article-sized result the DAG lacks, plus the identification of
the differential fibres of `X_y` with those of `Ω_{X/Y}`.

## Next route for the differential criterion (algebra form)

`K` a field, `T` of finite type over `K`, `q` prime, `dim_{κ(q)} κ(q) ⊗ Ω_{T/K} ≤ n ≤
dim T_q + dim T ⧸ q`; goal `Algebra.IsSmoothAt K q`.

1. Present `T = R ⧸ I` with `R = K[x₁, …, x_N]`; `Q` the preimage of `q`.
2. `κ(q) ⊗_T Ω_T` is a quotient of `κ(q) ⊗_R Ω_R` (dimension `N`) by the span `W` of the
   `1 ⊗ d f`, `f ∈ I` (construct the derivation `t ↦ [1 ⊗ d(lift t)]` into the quotient by
   `W` and lift through `liftKaehlerDifferential` and `liftBaseChange`). Pick `f₁, …, f_c ∈ I`
   with independent differentials, `N ≤ c + n`; move the independence to `κ(Q)` along the
   residue map `κ(Q) → κ(q)`.
3. `JacobianRegularQuotient`: `D = R_Q ⧸ (f)` is a regular local domain of dimension
   `ht Q - c`; by `height_add_ringKrullDim_quotient_eq`, `dim D ≤ dim T_q`.
4. `T_q ≅ R_Q ⧸ I R_Q` (as in `SubmersivePresentation.localization_quotient_transfer`), a
   quotient of the domain `D` of the same dimension, so `I R_Q = (f) R_Q`.
5. Mathlib's `Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_cotangentComplexBaseChange` with
   `P = R_Q` and the field `κ(Q)`: the kernel is generated by the `fᵢ`, whose differentials
   are independent (compare `κ(Q) ⊗_{R_Q} Ω_{R_Q}` with `κ(Q) ⊗_R Ω_R` through
   `tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale`), so the map is injective.

Scheme glue: on an affine chart `Spec B → Spec A` of `f`, apply the criterion to
`κ(p) ⊗_A B`; its differential fibres agree with those of `Ω_{B/A}` by Kähler base change
(`KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquiv`), and its local dimensions come from the fibre
clause through `FlatEquidimensional.catenaryAt_of_finiteType`,
`coheight_add_height_eq_of_equidim`, `height_apply_of_isOpenImmersion`, `height_spec_eq`,
`ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight`. Then Mathlib's `Algebra.Smooth.of_formallySmooth_fiber`
(or `Smooth.of_smooth_fiberToSpecResidueField`) and
`Scheme.smoothOfRelativeDimension_of_flat_of_smooth_fiber` finish.

Instance pitfalls met: quotient rings and `Localization.AtPrime` carry instance paths that are
not reducibly equal to the `CommRing`-derived ones, so build ring equivalences with
`IsLocalization.ringEquivOfRingEquiv` inside the proof that consumes them (stating a
`Nonempty (_ ≃+* _)` lemma fixes the wrong instance path), and give the source ring of
`IsRegularLocalRing.of_ringEquiv` explicitly.

## Split proposal

- New article "Elements with independent differentials cut out a regular local ring":
  for a `k`-algebra `P`, a prime `Q` with `P_Q` regular, and `f₁, …, f_c ∈ Q` with linearly
  independent differentials in `κ(Q) ⊗ Ω_{P/k}`, the ring `P_Q ⧸ (f₁, …, f_c)` is regular of
  dimension `ht Q - c`. Records the four declarations of `RegularQuotientCotangentIndependent`
  and `JacobianRegularQuotient` named above. Depends on: regular local rings; the conormal
  exact sequence.
- New article "Dimension of a scheme smooth over a field": if `Z → Spec k` is of finite type
  and smooth of relative dimension `n`, every irreducible component of `Z` has dimension `n`
  (algebra form: local rings of a standard smooth algebra are regular and
  `dim S_q + dim S ⧸ q = n`). Records
  `topologicalKrullDim_of_mem_irreducibleComponents_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension` and the
  three `SubmersivePresentation` declarations. Depends on: the previous new article; the
  dimension–codimension formula; the dimension of a scheme. Source: Stacks Section 10.140.
- New article "The differential criterion for smoothness over a field": a scheme of finite
  type over `k` all of whose irreducible components have dimension `n` and whose differential
  fibres all have dimension `n` is smooth over `k`. Source: Stacks Section 10.140, tag 00TQ.
  Depends on: the first new article; the dimension–codimension formula; Kähler base change.
  Nothing recorded yet.
- A Mathlib article "Flat morphisms with smooth fibres are smooth" (Stacks 01V8,
  `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth.of_smooth_fiberToSpecResidueField`); also usable by
  smoothness-and-geometrically-regular-fibres.
- Edges: this article's Proof depends on gains the second, third and fourth new articles;
  the third gains the first.
