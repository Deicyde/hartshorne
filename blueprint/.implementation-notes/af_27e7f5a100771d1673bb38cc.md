# Generic coherent pushforward approximation

## Status

Not formalized. No Lean has been added for this article. The proof is blocked on missing
prerequisites (below), not on the Lean of the frame.

## Intended statement

Encoding: the frame data are hypotheses and the conclusion is for every coherent `F`. This keeps
the article's order (`r` and the frame fixed before `F`), ties `r` to a specific frame, and the
Exercise 4.2(c) proof gets its frame from `Hartshorne.exists_genericFiniteModuleFrame` once and
then applies this result to each `F`. Draft (not yet elaborated):

```
theorem exists_genericCoherentApproximation {X Y : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ Y) [IsFinite f]
    [IsIntegral Y] [IsNoetherian Y] [IsNoetherian X] (M : X.Modules) (hM : IsCoherent M)
    (r : ℕ) (α : (SheafOfModules.free (ULift.{u} (Fin r)) : Y.Modules) ⟶
      (Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).obj M)
    (hα : IsIso ((Scheme.Modules.toPresheaf Y ⋙
      TopCat.Presheaf.stalkFunctor AddCommGrpCat.{u} (genericPoint Y)).map α))
    (F : Y.Modules) (hF : IsCoherent F) :
    ∃ (G : X.Modules) (_ : IsCoherent G)
      (β : (Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).obj G ⟶ ⨁ fun _ : Fin r => F),
      IsIso ((Scheme.Modules.toPresheaf Y ⋙
        TopCat.Presheaf.stalkFunctor AddCommGrpCat.{u} (genericPoint Y)).map β)
```

`F^r` is written as a finite biproduct so that additive functors (cohomology in 4.2(c)) split it.
The hint's argument does not use `r > 0`, surjectivity of `f`, or integrality of `X`. Then the
Lean drops them, as the frame's Lean drops integrality of `X`. The 4.2(c) proof takes `r > 0` from
the frame.

## Route (the book's hint)

Apply sheaf Hom into `F` to `α`: `Hom(f_*M, F) → Hom(𝒪_Y^r, F) ≅ F^r`. It is an isomorphism at
the generic point because `α` is and because Hom out of a coherent (locally finitely presented)
module commutes with stalks. `Hom(f_*M, F)` is a coherent `O_Y`-module with an `f_*O_X`-module
structure through the action on `f_*M`. By Exercise II.5.17(e) it is `f_*G` for a coherent `G`.

## Missing prerequisites

Neither the project nor pinned Mathlib has these. Searches: `autoform search` for affine-morphism
module equivalences, pushforward essential surjectivity, sheaf Hom stalks and Hom coherence. Grep
of `src` and Mathlib's `AlgebraicGeometry/Modules` and `Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf`.

1. Exercise II.5.17(e), essential surjectivity part: for a finite (affine) morphism `f`, every
   quasi-coherent (coherent, under Noetherian hypotheses) `O_Y`-module with a compatible
   `f_*O_X`-module structure is isomorphic to `f_*G`, compatibly with the action, for some
   quasi-coherent (coherent) `O_X`-module `G`. The source notes list 5.17(e) as not an
   independent target, and no article states it. `Hartshorne.relativeSpecEquiv` and
   `RelativeSpec.lean` handle algebras only. Possible constructions: glue the tilde modules
   `Hom(...)(V)~` on the affine charts `f⁻¹ V` (`DirectedModuleGluing` over the affine opens of
   `Y`, as for relative Proj); or the right adjoint `f^!` of `f_*`. Either way the work is well
   beyond a leaf: base change of `Hom` along `A_V → A_{V'}`, the cocycle, and `f_*` of the glued
   module.
2. Stalks of sheaf Hom out of a coherent module: `Hom(E, F)_y ≅ Hom_{O_{Y,y}}(E_y, F_y)`. This is
   the degree-zero case of the unformalized sheaf-Ext-stalk article.
3. Coherence of `Hom(E, F)` for coherent `E`, `F` on a Noetherian scheme. This is the degree-zero
   case of the unformalized coherent-sheaf-Ext article.

The article's Proof depends on links do not reach articles for 2 or 3. Item 1 is only named in
prose.

## Routes considered and rejected

- `G = M ⊗ f^*F` with the projection formula needs a generically invertible map `f_*M → 𝒪_Y^r`.
  Global maps out of `f_*M` need not exist (for example `f_*M = 𝒪(1)` on `ℙ¹`).
- `G = f^*Hom(f_*M, F)`: the map `f_*f^*H → H` is surjective at the generic point but not
  injective. Cutting it down needs the descent of an `f_*O_X`-submodule, which is item 1 again.
- Extending a chart-level map from an affine `W ∋ η` by twisting with powers of the ideal of
  `Y \ W` needs Hom coherence (item 3), extension of morphisms of coherent sheaves, and `f_*` of
  ideal multiples. It is no smaller and leaves the printed route.
