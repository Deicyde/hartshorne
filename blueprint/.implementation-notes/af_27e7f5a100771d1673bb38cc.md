# Generic coherent pushforward approximation

## Status

Candidate Lean, pending review: `Hartshorne.exists_genericCoherentApproximation` in
`Hartshorne.Cohomology.ChevalleyGenericApproximation`.

## Statement

```
theorem exists_genericCoherentApproximation {X Y : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ Y) [IsFinite f]
    [IsIntegral Y] [IsNoetherian Y] (M : X.Modules) (hM : IsCoherent M)
    (r : ℕ) (α : (SheafOfModules.free (ULift.{u} (Fin r)) : Y.Modules) ⟶
      (Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).obj M)
    (hα : IsIso ((Scheme.Modules.toPresheaf Y ⋙
      TopCat.Presheaf.stalkFunctor AddCommGrpCat.{u} (genericPoint Y)).map α))
    (F : Y.Modules) (hF : IsCoherent F) :
    ∃ (G : X.Modules) (_ : IsCoherent G)
      (β : (Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).obj G ⟶ ⨁ fun _ : Fin r => F),
      IsIso ((Scheme.Modules.toPresheaf Y ⋙
        TopCat.Presheaf.stalkFunctor AddCommGrpCat.{u} (genericPoint Y)).map β)
```

Encoding: the frame data are hypotheses and the conclusion is for every coherent `F`, so the
Exercise 4.2(c) proof gets its frame from `Hartshorne.exists_genericFiniteModuleFrame` once and
applies this result to each `F`. `F^r` is a finite biproduct so that additive functors split it.
The proof does not use `r > 0`, surjectivity of `f`, integrality of `X`, or `X` Noetherian (which
follows from `f` finite and `Y` Noetherian), so the Lean drops them. Integrality of `Y` is only
used to name the generic point; the stalk statement is proved at every point
(`Hartshorne.ChevalleyApprox.isIso_stalk_evalSum`).

## Route taken (diverges from the book's hint)

The book applies sheaf Hom into `F` to `α` and descends `ℋom(f_*M, F)` along `f` with Exercise
II.5.17(e). The Lean builds the descended module directly:

1. `Hartshorne.RelSpecModuleData` (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.RelativeSpecModuleGluing`): modules
   `N_U` over the sections `A(U)` of a quasi-coherent algebra on the affine opens `U` of `Y`, with
   restriction maps that are localizations along basic opens, glue to a module on
   `Spec_Y A` (`RelSpecModuleData.glue`, with `RelSpecModuleData.ψ` identifying its restriction
   to the chart over `U` with `N_U~`). This is the module half of II.5.17(e) in the form needed
   here; no article states it.
2. `Hartshorne.ChevalleyApprox.homData` takes `N_U = Hom_{Γ(U,𝒪_Y)}(Γ(U, f_*M), Γ(U, F))` with
   the `Γ(f⁻¹U, 𝒪_X)`-action by precomposition with multiplication (`homSec`); restriction maps
   are localizations because `Γ(U, f_*M)` is finitely presented
   (`isLocalizedModule_homResHom`, from Mathlib's
   `Module.FinitePresentation.isLocalizedModule_mapExtendScalars`).
3. `G = approx f M F` is the glued module transported along `Spec_Y(f_*𝒪_X) ≅ X`;
   `isCoherent_approx` proves coherence (finite Hom modules over Noetherian rings), and
   `pushforwardApproxIso` identifies `f_*G` with the pushforward of the glued module.
4. `evalHom f M F m` is evaluation at a global section `m` of `f_*M`, glued from the affine
   pieces; `evalSum` combines the sections `mᵢ` defining `α` into `f_*G ⟶ F^r`.
5. Near a point where `α` is a stalk isomorphism, the `mᵢ|_U` form a basis of `Γ(U, f_*M)` on
   every basic open of an affine neighbourhood (`exists_bijective_app`, `exists_basis_near`),
   so `evalSum` is bijective on those sections (`bijective_evalSum_app`), hence an isomorphism on
   the stalk (`isIso_stalk_of_forall_exists_bijective`).

So the Lean never forms `ℋom(f_*M, F)` as a sheaf on `Y`, does not use the module-sheaf-hom
article, and needs neither the stalk formula for sheaf Hom nor coherence of sheaf Hom.

## Prior failed routes (for the record)

- `G = M ⊗ f^*F` with the projection formula needs a generically invertible map `f_*M → 𝒪_Y^r`,
  and global maps out of `f_*M` need not exist.
- `G = f^*ℋom(f_*M, F)`: the map `f_*f^*H → H` is not injective at the generic point.
