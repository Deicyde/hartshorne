## Proved (compiling)

- `Hartshorne.moduleSheafTensorPowerMul_multilinear`: in `T^{r+s} F` the product of local tensors
  is their concatenation (`Fin.append`).
- `Hartshorne.tensorPower_bilinear_ext`: bilinear morphisms `T^r F × T^s F → H` into a sheaf are
  determined by their values on pairs of local tensors.
- `Hartshorne.SheafAlgebraOperations.PresheafBilinear.pushforward` (with `pushforward_app`): the
  pushforward of a bilinear morphism along a morphism of schemes.
- `Hartshorne.tensorPowerPushforwardMap_mul`: the comparison maps `T^m F ⟶ f_*(T^m G)` from
  plurigenus invariance (`tensorPowerPushforwardMap`) preserve products of sections. This is the
  multiplicativity point of the article.
- `Hartshorne.TensorPowerSectionRing.gradedRingHom` (with `_of`, `coe_gradedRingHom`,
  `_injective`, `_surjective`): degreewise additive maps that preserve the unit and products give
  a graded ring homomorphism of tensor-power section rings. It is injective or surjective when
  every degree is.

## Remaining

1. Unit: `(tensorPowerPushforwardMap f α 0)` sends `oneSec` to `oneSec`. The draft proof uses
   `oneSec F U = (moduleSheafTensorPowerMultilinear 0 F).app U Fin.elim0` (by `rfl`, from
   `TensorPower.gOne_def`) and `tensorPowerPushforwardMap_tprod`. It is written but not compiled.
2. Degreewise map: `ψ_m := (restr_m)⁻¹ ∘ pull_m`.
   - `pull_m` is `(tensorPowerPushforwardMap φ.hom (exteriorPowerPullback φ.hom pX' n) m)` on
     global sections.
   - `restr_m` is the bijective restriction `(tensorPowerPushforwardMap V.ι
     (exteriorPowerPushforwardMap V.ι (relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp V.ι pX) n) m)`.
   - Both are set up as in `rank_globalSections_le_of_partialIso` (PlurigenusBirational.lean).
   - `ψ` is injective, and it preserves unit and products by step 1 and
     `tensorPowerPushforwardMap_mul` (apply the injectivity of `restr`). Feed it to
     `gradedRingHom`, after rewriting `canonicalSheaf_eq` in `CanonicalSectionRing`.
3. Surjectivity in each degree. This is not available from the plurigenus proof, which compares
   only cardinal ranks through injections both ways; `H⁰` is not yet known to be
   finite-dimensional in the library. Two routes:
   - (a) prove `Γ(X, ω_X^{⊗m})` is finite-dimensional over `k` (coherent sheaf on a proper
     scheme). Then injective plus equal `finrank` (plurigenus invariance) gives bijective.
   - (b) show `ψ' ∘ ψ = id` for the map `ψ'` built from `e.symm`. This needs functoriality of
     `relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp` and `tensorPowerPushforwardMap` under
     composition. It also needs sections of an invertible sheaf on an integral scheme to be
     determined on a nonempty open (`presheaf_map_injective_of_isInvertible`).
4. Then state the graded `k`-algebra isomorphism `R(X, K_X) ≅ R(X', K_X')`, with `k`-linearity
   in each degree via `globalSectionsLinearMap`, and record.
