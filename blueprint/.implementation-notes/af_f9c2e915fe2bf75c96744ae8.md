# Canonical rings of birational varieties

## Status

Statement and proof compile, sorry-free, in
src/Hartshorne/Surface/CanonicalRingBirational.lean (imported by the library root). Pending
independent review and recording.

Declaration to record: `Hartshorne.canonicalSectionRing_ringEquiv_of_birational`. It asserts a
ring isomorphism `CanonicalSectionRing pX ≃+* CanonicalSectionRing pX'` together with `k`-linear
isomorphisms `ψ m` between the degree-`m` pieces such that the ring isomorphism sends
`DirectSum.of _ m x` to `DirectSum.of _ m (ψ m x)`. Hypotheses: `k` algebraically closed, `X`,
`X'` integral, regular, proper over `k`, and a partial isomorphism `X ⤏ X'` over `k`. Proper
replaces Hartshorne's projective.

Optional companion declarations describing the construction:
`Hartshorne.pluricanonicalTransfer`, `Hartshorne.pluricanonicalTransfer_source`,
`Hartshorne.pluricanonicalTransfer_mul`, `Hartshorne.pluricanonicalTransfer_symm_apply`.

## Source note

The source page for V.6 (p. 421) says only that the Kodaira dimension `trdeg_k R(X,K) - 1` is a
birational invariant. It does not state that canonical rings are isomorphic, so the docstrings
say the result "supplements" V.6.

## Construction (Lean names)

- `differentialsPullback f h`: `Ω_{q_B} ⟶ f_* Ω_{q_A}` for `h : f ≫ q_B = q_A`, defined as a
  transport of `relativeDifferentialsToPushforwardComp`. Its value on `d b` is
  `differentialsPullback_d`, and it is compatible with composition
  (`differentialsPullback_comp`). Keeping the structure morphisms general avoids casts between
  `relativeDifferentials ((f ≫ g) ≫ q)` and `relativeDifferentials (f ≫ g ≫ q)`.
- `tensorPowerPushforwardMap_comp`, `exteriorPowerPushforwardMap_comp`: functoriality of the
  induced maps on `T^m` and `⋀^n`, relative to `pushforwardComp`.
- `pluricanonicalPullback n m f h := tensorPowerPushforwardMap f (exteriorPowerPushforwardMap f
  (differentialsPullback f h) n) m`. On global sections it is compatible with composition
  (`pluricanonicalPullback_app_top_comp`, where the composite can be any morphism equal to
  `f ≫ g`), and it preserves the unit and products (`pluricanonicalPullback_oneSec`,
  `pluricanonicalPullback_mul`). It is injective on global sections when it is an open immersion
  on a nonempty open (`pluricanonicalPullback_app_top_injective`).
- `birationalDomainMap pX' e := e.toRationalMap.toPartialMap`. Mathlib's
  `PartialMap.toPartialMap_toRationalMap_restrict` gives `homOfLE_birationalDomainMap_hom`: on
  `e.source` this map is `e.iso.hom ≫ e.target.ι`.
- `pluricanonicalTransfer e hX he hnX m`: `(restriction to V)⁻¹ ∘ (pullback along V ⟶ X')` as a
  `k`-linear map. `GlobalSectionsOver.castLinearMap` handles the two equal structure morphisms on
  `V`. Bijectivity of restriction to `V` is `pluricanonicalPullback_domain_bijective`, which
  follows the plurigenus proof.
- `pluricanonicalTransfer_source`: on `U = e.source`,
  `P_{U.ι}(ψ s) = P_{e.iso.hom ≫ e.target.ι}(s)`. `pluricanonicalTransfer_symm_apply` then follows
  by composition and injectivity of pullback to `e.target`.
- `pluricanonicalTransferRingHom` comes from `TensorPowerSectionRing.gradedRingHom`, and
  `pluricanonicalTransferEquiv` from `LinearEquiv.ofLinearMap`. The final theorem generalises
  over sheaves `F = ⋀^n Ω` and `subst`s, which moves from `⋀^n Ω` to `canonicalSheaf`.
