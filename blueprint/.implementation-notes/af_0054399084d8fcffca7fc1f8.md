# Infinitesimal neighbourhoods

## Prior implementation guidance

The definition is made on the module ideal sheaf. It is transported to and
from Mathlib's affine-open `IdealSheafData` through the existing bridge; these
representations are not treated as definitionally equal.

## Lean (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.InfinitesimalNeighborhoods`)

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.pow` (with a `Pow _ ℕ` instance): the power
  `I ^ k` taken in the ideal sheaf, defined as `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.powSmul` of the
  bridged ideal data on the top submodule of `𝒪_X`; quasi-coherent by
  `isQuasicoherent_powSmul`.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.ideal_pow`: `Γ(U, I ^ k) = Γ(U, I) ^ k` on affine
  `U` (from `sub_powSmul_eq`), and `Hartshorne.quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv_pow`: the
  bridge sends `I ^ k` to Mathlib's `IdealSheafData` power. The two powers are equal by
  this theorem, not by `rfl`.
- `Hartshorne.infinitesimalNeighborhood I n := (equiv (I ^ (n + 1))).subscheme`, with
  `Hartshorne.infinitesimalNeighborhoodι` (a closed immersion) and
  `ker_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι` / `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv_symm_ker_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι`
  (cut out by `I ^ (n + 1)`).
- `Hartshorne.infinitesimalNeighborhoodZeroIso f : Y_0 ≅ Y` for a closed immersion
  `f : Y ⟶ X` with module ideal sheaf `equiv.symm f.ker`, with
  `infinitesimalNeighborhoodZeroIso_hom_comp` (compatible with the maps to `X`).
- The Noetherian hypothesis of the article is not used; the Lean is stated for every scheme.
- Not formalized here because the article does not claim them: the transition closed
  immersions `Y_n ⟶ Y_{n+1}` (Mathlib's `IdealSheafData.inclusion` with
  `powSmul_antitone`) and the equality of underlying spaces (Mathlib's
  `IdealSheafData.support_pow_succ` after `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv_pow`).
