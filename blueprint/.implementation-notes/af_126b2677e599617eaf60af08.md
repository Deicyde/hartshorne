# The projective section ring is the integral closure

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.ProjectiveSectionRingIntegralClosure`
(namespace `Hartshorne.ProjectiveSectionRing`).

## Statement shape

The closed subscheme `X ⊆ ℙʳ_k` is presented as `Proj S` for `S = k[x₀,…,xᵣ]/I`
(`gradedQuotient`, closed immersion `projQuotientι`), with `I` saturated
(`hsat : ∀ s, (∀ i, ∃ n, X i ^ n * s ∈ I) → s ∈ I`). By Corollary 5.16 and
Exercise 5.10 this is the homogeneous coordinate ring `S = k[x]/Γ_*(ℐ_X)`; the
identification of `𝒪_X(n)` on `Proj S` with the restriction of `𝒪(n)` (Prop. 5.12(c))
is not formalized in the project, so `𝒪_X(n)` is `projTwistingSheaf` of `Proj S`.

`S' = ⨁_{n ≥ 0} Γ(X, 𝒪_X(n))` is `DirectSum ℕ (fun n => twistGlobal 𝟙_ n)` (the
nonnegative part of `Γ_*(𝒪_X)` of the graded-global-sections article).
"`S'` is the integral closure" is stated through the natural map
`sectionRingToFraction : S' →+ Frac S` (value at the generic point `(0)` of
`Proj S`): it is injective, multiplicative (`sectionToFraction_sectionMul`),
sends `s · 1` to `s` (`sectionToFraction_mulGlobal_unitSection`), and its range is
`integralClosure S (FractionRing S)`. `IsAlgClosed k` is kept and unused.

## Proof outline (Lean names)

- Values of sections in `Frac S` agree at all points (`sectionValue_eq_genericPoint`),
  so `S' → K` is injective (`sectionRingToFraction_injective`, via
  `eq_zero_of_sum_isHomFrac_eq_zero`: homogeneous fractions of distinct degrees are
  independent).
- Integrality (book: `s · S_m ⊆ S` for large `m`): `exists_mem_eq_pow_mul_sectionToFraction`
  (chart fraction `a / xᵢ^j`) and `isIntegral_of_forall_coord` (determinant trick on the
  ideal `S_{≥N}` via `isIntegral_of_smul_mem_submodule`).
- Integrally closed (book: sheaf of integrally closed domains `⊕ 𝒪_X(n)`): on a chart,
  `S_(xᵢ)` is integrally closed (`isIntegrallyClosed_away`, from normality), so
  `S_(xᵢ)[xᵢ]` (a polynomial ring, `chartPoly`) is integrally closed with fraction
  field `K` and contains `S` (`exists_chartPoly_eq_of_isIntegral`); the degree parts
  agree across charts and glue (`exists_sectionToFraction_eq`), giving
  `mem_range_sectionRingToFraction`.
- `X` integral: `isIntegral_of_connectedSpace` (connected Noetherian with domain stalks
  is irreducible, `irreducibleSpace_of_connectedSpace`). `S` a domain:
  `forall_pow_mul_eq_zero_or` (homogeneous zero divisors on integral `Proj`) plus
  saturation, `isDomain_of_isIntegral`.
