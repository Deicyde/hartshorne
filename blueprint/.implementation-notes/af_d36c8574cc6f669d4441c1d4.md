# Implementation note: bertini-avoid-finite-singular-set

Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.BertiniAvoidFiniteSingularSet`.

## Candidate declarations

- `Hartshorne.BertiniIncidence.bertini_avoid_finite_singular_set`: part (1). Closed immersion
  `φ : X ⟶ ℙⁿ_k` of an integral scheme, `k` algebraically closed, hypothesis
  `{x | IsClosed {x} ∧ ¬ X.IsRegularAt x}.Finite`. Conclusion in the shape of
  `bertini_regular_hyperplane_section`: a dense open `U` of `projectiveSpaceScheme k n` whose
  closed points `H` representing `a ≠ 0` are exactly those with every singular closed point off
  `(hyperplaneSectionIdeal φ a).support`, some point off the support, and the hyperplane-section
  subscheme regular.
- `Hartshorne.BertiniIncidence.hyperplanes_avoiding_finite_set_dense`: part (2), for a finite set
  of closed points of `projectiveSpaceOverScheme n (Spec k)`; "avoiding `P`" is `P` not in the
  support of the zero scheme `invertibleZeroIdeal _ (linearSection k n a)`.
- `Hartshorne.BertiniIncidence.bertini_regular_hyperplane_section_avoiding`: the last sentence
  of the article, the intersection of the part (2) open with the open of Theorem 8.18 (uses the
  recorded `bertini_regular_hyperplane_section`).

## Route

- Universal hyperplane: `universalHyperplane φ` is the intersection over charts
  `X_{sᵢ} ×_k D₊(a_β)` of the complements of `basicOpen (incidenceChartEquation …)` with the
  one-row matrix `μ = sectionRatio`; `mem_universalHyperplane_iff` identifies its points with
  `fst z ∈ (hyperplaneSectionIdeal φ a).support` via `mem_chartIncidence_iff` and
  `mem_support_hyperplaneSectionIdeal_iff_chart`.
- `hyperplanesThrough φ x` is the `snd`-image of the fibre over `x`; `hyperplanesThrough_spec`
  gives closed (proper image), proper (the coordinate hyperplane `xᵢ = 0` misses `x`), and the
  closed-point description. `exists_isOpen_dense_avoiding` takes the complement of a finite union
  (`biUnion_ne_univ_of_irreducibleSpace`).
- Incidence over the regular locus: `finrank_badHyperplaneSubspace_of_isRegularAt` and
  `exists_local_incidence_equations_of_isOpen` are the recorded incidence lemmas with global
  regularity replaced by regularity at closed points of an open `O` (the neighbourhood `V` is
  shrunk into `O` by one more basic-open factor). `bertini_bad_hyperplane_incidence_of_isOpen`
  re-runs the chart gluing over `O`: `Z = closure B_O`, `Z ⊆ universalHyperplane φ`, and
  `dim Z ≤ n - 1`.
- The dimension bound uses `topologicalKrullDim_le_inter_of_isIrreducible`: with the reduced
  induced structure a closed irreducible `Z` in a finite-type `k`-scheme is integral, and
  `topologicalKrullDim_opens_eq` (Exercise II.3.20) gives `dim Z = dim (Z ∩ W)` for an open `W`
  meeting `Z`. This is needed because `Z` may have points over the singular set.
- Points of `Z` over a singular point `x` lie on the universal hyperplane, so a hyperplane in the
  image of `Z` that avoids the singular set is bad at a closed regular point.
- `forall_not_isBadHyperplaneAt_iff_of_isLocallyNoetherian` is the recorded good-iff-no-bad
  lemma with `X.IsRegular` weakened to local Noetherianity (only that was used).

## Prior art and reuse

The recorded incidence modules are unchanged; the generalized lemmas are new declarations that
follow the recorded proofs. Abstraction candidate: the recorded `exists_local_incidence_equations`,
`finrank_badHyperplaneSubspace`, `bertini_bad_hyperplane_incidence` and
`forall_not_isBadHyperplaneAt_iff` could be re-derived from the `_of_isOpen` /
`_of_isRegularAt` / `_of_isLocallyNoetherian` versions with `O = univ`.
