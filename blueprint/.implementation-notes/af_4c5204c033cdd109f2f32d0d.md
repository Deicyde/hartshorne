## Lean (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.PlurigenusBirational`)

- `Hartshorne.geometricGenus_eq_of_birational`: `X`, `X'` integral, regular, proper over an
  algebraically closed `k`, with `∃ e : X.PartialIso X', e.IsOver pX pX'`; then
  `geometricGenus pX = geometricGenus pX'`. It is `plurigenus_eq_of_birational` at `m = 1`
  combined with `geometricGenus_eq_plurigenus_one`.
- `geometricGenus_eq_plurigenus_one` comes from `tensorPowerOneHom : T^1 F ⟶ F`, which is bijective on
  sections (`bijective_tensorPowerOneHom_app`, with inverse `tensorPowerOneInv`), and
  `globalSectionsLinearMapOfHom`.
