# Ideals of definition are cofinal: implementation note

## Status

Complete, sorry-free. Lean in `Hartshorne.Scheme.IdealsOfDefinitionCofinal`, namespace
`Hartshorne.IsIdealOfDefinition`.

- `exists_pow_le_and_pow_le` (the article's statement): for ideals of definition `J₁`, `J₂` on
  `X : NoetherianFormalScheme`, `∃ a b : ℕ, 0 < a ∧ 0 < b ∧ J₂ ^ a ≤ J₁ ∧ J₁ ^ b ≤ J₂`. Here
  `≤` is the inclusion order on `IdealSubsheaf` (`IdealSubsheaf.le_iff`) and `J ^ k` is the
  ideal subsheaf of sections lying locally in `J(V) ^ k`.
- `exists_pow_le`: one direction, `∃ a, 0 < a ∧ J₂ ^ a ≤ J₁`, assuming only that `J₁` is an
  ideal of definition and `sheafSupport J₂.quotientAb = Set.univ`. The two-sided theorem applies
  it twice.

## Route

Follows the book's argument on the Noetherian scheme $Y = (X, \mathcal{O}_X/J_1)$
(`IsIdealOfDefinition.quotientScheme`, whose presheaf is `J₁.quotient.presheaf` by `rfl`).

1. Private API for the projection `π = J.quotientπ : R ⟶ R / J` (the sheafification of
   `V ↦ R(V) ⧸ J(V)`): `π.hom = quotientPresheafπ ≫ toSheafify` (via `toSheafify_naturality`);
   kernel of `π_U` is `J(U)` (local injectivity of `toSheafify`, `Presheaf.equalizerSieve_mem`,
   plus locality of `J`); `π` is locally surjective (`Presheaf.isLocallySurjective_toSheafify'`);
   naturality in elementwise form. If `1 ∈ J(V)` then every stalk of `R / J` at points of `V`
   is trivial, so the point is not in `sheafSupport J.quotientAb`.
2. A section of an ideal subsheaf with full support has non-unit germs (else it is a unit near
   the point, putting `1` in `J`), using `RingedSpace.isUnit_res_of_isUnit_germ`.
3. For `g ∈ J₂(V)`, the germ of `π g` in `𝒪_{Y,x}` is never a unit: otherwise `π g · t = 1` near
   `x`, lift `t` locally to `t'`, so `g t' - 1 ∈ J₁`; its germ is a non-unit (step 2 for `J₁`), so
   the germ of `g t'` is a unit in the local ring `𝒪_{X,x}`, contradicting step 2 for `J₂`.
4. Hence on an affine open `W` of `Y` the restriction of `π g` has empty basic open, so it is
   nilpotent by `IsAffineOpen.fromSpec_preimage_basicOpen` and
   `PrimeSpectrum.basicOpen_eq_bot_iff` (the basic-open-empty-iff-nilpotent article).
5. Uniform exponent: finite affine subcover of the compact space `Y`; each `Γ(Y, U)` is
   Noetherian (`IsLocallyNoetherian.component_noetherian`), so
   `IsNoetherianRing.isNilpotent_nilradical` gives `N U`. With `a = sup N + 1`, the nilradical of
   each stalk `𝒪_{Y,x}` has `a`-th power zero, since the stalk is a localization of `Γ(Y, U)`
   (`IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`) and `IsLocalization.map_radical` gives
   `nil(stalk) = map (nil Γ(Y, U))`.
6. A section `s` of `J₂ ^ a` lies locally in `J₂(W) ^ a`; its image in `𝒪_{Y,x}` lies in
   `(map J₂(W)) ^ a ⊆ nil ^ a = 0`, so `π s = 0` by `TopCat.Presheaf.section_ext`, i.e.
   `s ∈ J₁`.

## Friction

`X.toLocallyRingedSpace.sheaf.presheaf` and `X.presheaf` (and `J.quotient.presheaf` versus the
scheme presheaf of `quotientScheme`) are defeq but not reducibly so; mixing them in one
multiplication or `rw` fails instance synthesis or motive checks. The proofs keep every ring
element in the sheaf's presheaf and state the needed restriction lemmas in that form, passing
to the scheme presheaf only by `exact`/type ascription.
