# Multiplicity of a divisor at a point

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Surface.PointMultiplicity`:

- `Hartshorne.Surface.pointMultiplicity D P : ℕ` is `sSup {r | f ∈ 𝔪_P^r}` for the chosen local
  equation `f = (exists_isLocalEquation D P).choose`, the same choice as
  `localIntersectionMultiplicity`. Defined at every point of the surface.
- `pointMultiplicity_eq`: the same `sSup` for any local equation (independence).
- `pointMultiplicity_eq_iff`: `μ_P(D) = r ↔ f ∈ 𝔪_P^r ∧ f ∉ 𝔪_P^{r+1}` for any local equation
  (the maximum exists, by Krull's intersection theorem).
- `pointMultiplicity_add`: `μ_P(D + E) = μ_P(D) + μ_P(E)` at every point, the sum curve written
  as `⟨D.1 + E.1, _⟩` (there is no `Add` instance on `Surface.Curve`).
- `pointMultiplicity_eq_iff_exists_initialForm`: at a closed point, for `x, y` with
  `span {x, y} = 𝔪_P`, `μ_P(D) = r` iff there is a nonzero homogeneous `F ∈ k[X, Y]` of degree
  `r` with `f - F(x, y) ∈ 𝔪_P^{r+1}` (coefficients mapped by `stalkStructureMap`). This is the
  form the total-transform formula (Prop. 3.6) consumes: `f = F_r(x, y) + h`, `h ∈ 𝔪^{r+1}`.
- Helper `IsLocalEquation.mul`: a product of local equations is a local equation of the sum.

Module `Hartshorne.Scheme.RegularLocalInitialForm` (commutative algebra, unrecorded helpers):

- `RegularLocal.eval_notMem_pow_succ`: `A` Noetherian local with infinite residue field, `𝔪`
  generated by `x : ι → A` with `dim A = #ι`; if `F` is homogeneous of degree `s` with nonzero
  reduction mod `𝔪`, then `F(x) ∉ 𝔪^{s+1}` (Matsumura 14.4, infinite residue field case).
- `RegularLocal.eval_mem_pow_succ_of_map_eq_zero`: coefficients in `𝔪` give `F(x) ∈ 𝔪^{s+1}`.
- `RegularLocal.mul_notMem_pow_add_succ`: additivity of the `𝔪`-adic order in a regular local
  ring with infinite residue field.

## Route

No associated graded ring is built. Proof of `eval_notMem_pow_succ`: pick `a ∈ κ^d` with
`a_j ≠ 0`, `F̄(a) ≠ 0` (`MvPolynomial.funext`, infinite `κ`); lift to `ã`; with `I` generated by
the `d - 1` elements `ã_j x_i - ã_i x_j`, homogeneity (`isHomogeneous_eval₂_mul_left`) gives
`ã_j^s G(x) ≡ G(ã) x_j^s mod I`, hence `𝔪^s ⊆ I + 𝔪^{s+1}`, so `𝔪^s ⊆ I` by Nakayama and
Krull's height theorem (`Ideal.height_le_card_of_mem_minimalPrimes_span_finset`) contradicts
`ht 𝔪 = d`. Mathlib's `Ideal.mem_span_pow_iff_exists_isHomogeneous` writes elements of `𝔪^s`
as homogeneous forms in the generators. The infinite residue field holds on a surface because
`κ(P)` contains `k`. The finite-residue-field case (general regular local rings) is not needed
here; it would need the `A[t]_{𝔪A[t]}` extension or Hilbert–Samuel theory.
