# Multiplicity of a divisor at a point

## Status

Assessed in a bounded Formalize attempt (frontier/w23b), not closable in budget. No Lean was
written for this article, and nothing is recorded in the frontmatter. The article bundles a
definition with three claims (independence of the local equation, additivity, initial-form
degree). Additivity and the initial-form claim both need the associated graded ring of a
regular local ring to be a polynomial ring (a domain), and neither Mathlib nor the project has
that. Independence and the definition are within reach on their own (see below), but the
statement-and-proof policy rules out recording only part of the article.

## Existing Lean to build on

- `Hartshorne.Surface.IsLocalEquation D P f`, `Hartshorne.Surface.exists_isLocalEquation`,
  `Hartshorne.Surface.IsLocalEquation.associated` (module `Hartshorne.Surface.LocalEquation`):
  local equations of an effective divisor at a point exist and differ by a unit of `𝒪_{X,P}`.
  These are the article's "local equation" and give independence: `m_P^r` is an ideal, so
  `f ∈ m_P^r ↔ u f ∈ m_P^r` for a unit `u`.
- The surface's stalks are regular local rings of dimension two at closed points
  (surface-curve-convention); `IsRegularLocalRing` is in Mathlib
  (`Mathlib.RingTheory.RegularLocalRing.Defs`). By Krull's intersection theorem the maximum is
  finite for `f ≠ 0`.
- The local-intersection-multiplicity module uses the same chosen local equation, so a
  definition here via `Classical.choose (exists_isLocalEquation D P)` would match it.

## Lean gaps

1. **Associated graded ring.** For a regular local ring `(A, m)` of dimension `d` with residue
   field `κ`, `gr_m(A) = ⊕ m^r/m^{r+1} ≅ κ[x_1, …, x_d]` (Matsumura, Thm 14.4; Atiyah–Macdonald
   Ch. 11). In particular `gr_m(A)` is a domain, so the order function `ord_m(f) = max{r | f ∈ m^r}`
   satisfies `ord_m(fg) = ord_m(f) + ord_m(g)`. Additivity `μ_P(D + E) = μ_P(D) + μ_P(E)`
   follows, because a product of local equations is a local equation of the sum. No associated
   graded ring of an ideal-adic filtration, and no such isomorphism, was located in Mathlib or the
   project.
2. **Initial form.** "Degree of the initial homogeneous part in regular local parameters" needs
   either the isomorphism above (the initial form of `f` is its image in `m^r/m^{r+1}`, a
   homogeneous polynomial of degree `r` in the images of `x, y`) or, more concretely, the
   completion `Â ≅ κ[[x, y]]` (Cohen structure theorem, Hartshorne I.5.5A), which Mathlib also
   lacks. One possible statement avoids completions: the image of `f` in
   `m^r/m^{r+1} ≅ Sym^r(m/m^2)` is nonzero exactly when `r = μ_P(D)`.

## Suggested plan

Split the article. (a) A definition-and-independence leaf, closable now from
`Hartshorne.Surface.LocalEquation` plus Krull's intersection theorem (for finiteness at
`f ≠ 0`). (b) A commutative-algebra leaf `gr_m(A)` is a polynomial ring for a regular local
ring, or at least `ord_m` is additive (this needs only that `gr_m(A)` is a domain). (c)
Additivity and the initial-form statement, depending on (b).
