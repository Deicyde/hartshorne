# Exactness of affine module completion — implementation note

Status: partial progress, no `lean:` target recorded. Module:
`Hartshorne/Scheme/AffineFormalModuleExact.lean` (imported by the library root).

## Proved (sorry-free, standard axioms only)

- `Hartshorne.sheaf_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: a `ShortComplex` of sheaves of abelian groups on a
  topological space is exact if, over every open of a basis, sections of the middle term killed by
  the second map lift to the first term. Proof via `TopCat.Sheaf.exact_iff_stalkFunctor_map_exact`
  (the stalk criterion) and `ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff`, using `exists_germ_eq`, `germ_eq` and a
  basis refinement.
- `Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: the same for `SheafOfModules R` over any
  `R : Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) RingCat`, through the faithful exact functor
  `ModuleCohomology.toTopSheaf R` (`ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful`). This is the route
  Hartshorne's proof takes (sections over basic opens `D(f) ∩ V(I)`, then stalks), and it uses the
  stalkwise-exactness dependency.
- Levelwise functoriality of `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletion`:
  `powSmulHom` (`I^(n+1)F ⟶ I^(n+1)G`, with `powSmulHom_comp_powSmulι`),
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom` (`F/I^(n+1)F ⟶ G/I^(n+1)G`, with `π_moduleCompletionQuotientHom`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_id`, `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp_moduleCompletionQuotientMap`), and `moduleCompletionObjHom`
  (`(F/I^(n+1)F)|_Y ⟶ (G/I^(n+1)G)|_Y` over the completed structure sheaf, with `_id`, `_comp`).

## Remaining goals

1. Naturality of the transition maps:
   `moduleCompletionObjHom φ (n+1) ≫ moduleCompletionMap G n =
    moduleCompletionMap F n ≫ moduleCompletionObjHom φ n`.
   Mathematically it is the naturality of the adjunction unit for `Y_(n+1) ⟶ Y_(n+2)`, of
   `Scheme.Modules.pullbackComp` and `Scheme.Modules.pullbackCongr`, of the restriction map
   `sheafOfModulesPushforwardCompRestrict` (a one-line `ext` proof in the abstract setting), and
   `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp_moduleCompletionQuotientMap`. With it, `NatTrans.ofOpSequence`
   and `limMap` give `moduleCompletionFunctor : X.Modules ⥤ SheafOfModules _`.
   Obstacle met: every proof that compares a freshly elaborated term with the body of
   `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` (as elaborated in `FormalCompletionModule.lean`) times out in
   the elaborator or the kernel. The body mixes `infinitesimalNeighborhood (n+1+1)` (from the type
   of `moduleCompletionNeighborhood F (n+1)`) with `infinitesimalNeighborhoodι (n+2)`, has middle
   objects of the form `Ps.obj (P2.1 Q)` produced by unification, and uses a private proof for
   `pullbackCongr`; when Lean compares e.g. `(P₂ ⋙ Ps).obj Q` with `Ps.obj (P₂.obj Q)` it tries
   unfolding `Scheme.Modules.pullback` first. Things that did work: `unfold
   moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` (kernel-cheap); generalising the private proof with
   `generalize_proofs`; abstract diagram-chase lemmas with functors as variables (cheap in
   themselves). Things that failed: `unfold moduleCompletionNeighborhood` (kernel timeout), `show`
   or `rfl` equation lemmas for the body (kernel timeout), `dsimp` on the goal (kernel timeout),
   instantiating an abstract lemma (elaborator `isDefEq` timeout). Next route: restate
   `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` once, in a new module, as a natural transformation built with
   `Functor.whiskerLeft`/`whiskerRight` over functors written with `n + 1 + 1` throughout, prove
   `moduleCompletionMap F n` equals its component through a single equation that the kernel can
   check, and get naturality for free. Alternatively ask for `FormalCompletionModule.lean` to
   define the transition maps as components of natural transformations, so that functoriality
   comes from the definitions.
2. Affine sections. For `X = Spec A`, `I = specIdealSubsheaf A I`, `F = M~` and a basic open
   `U = D(f) ∩ V(I)` of `|Y|`, identify `Γ(U, moduleCompletionObj (M~) n)` with
   `M_f / I^(n+1) M_f` naturally in `M` and compatibly with the transition maps. That makes
   `Γ(U, moduleCompletion (M~))` (via `isLimitModuleCompletionSections`) the `I A_f`-adic
   completion of `M_f`. Ingredients: sections of `powSmul` on affines (`sub_powSmul_eq`), `tilde`
   preserving cokernels, `tildePullbackIso` along `Spec (A/I^(n+1)) ⟶ Spec A` with
   `specIdealSubsheafNeighborhoodIso`, and localisation of tilde sections. No module analogue of
   `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso` exists in the project. This is a missing prerequisite for
   the article (the module version of "Affine sections of a formal completion").
3. Conclusion. For a short complex `M' ⟶ M ⟶ M''` of finite `A`-modules that is exact, the
   sections over each `D(f) ∩ V(I)` form `AdicCompletion.map` of the localised maps over the
   Noetherian ring `A_f` with ideal `I A_f`, exact by `AdicCompletion.map_exact`; then
   `sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections` with the basis of basic opens of `|Y| ≅ Spec (A/I)`
   gives exactness of the completed sheaves. Planned statement: preservation of exactness of
   composable pairs of finite `A`-modules (this implies preservation of short exact sequences,
   which is what "exact functor" means for an additive functor from the abelian category of finite
   modules over a Noetherian ring).
