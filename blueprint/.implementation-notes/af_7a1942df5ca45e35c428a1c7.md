# Exactness of affine module completion — implementation note

Status: complete candidate (pending review). Modules:
`Hartshorne/Scheme/AffineFormalModuleExact.lean` (the leaf) and
`Hartshorne/Scheme/FormalCompletionModuleAffineSections.lean` (the shared affine-sections API, also
needed by 9.4(b) and 9.6(a)); both imported by the library root.

## Recorded candidate

- `Hartshorne.moduleCompletion_tilde_exact`: for `A : CommRingCat` Noetherian, `I : Ideal A` and an
  exact `S : ShortComplex (ModuleCat A)` with `S.X₂`, `S.X₃` finite,
  `(S.map (tilde.functor A ⋙ (specIdealSubsheaf A I).moduleCompletionFunctor)).Exact`.
  `S.X₁` need not be finite and `S` need not be short exact (stronger than the book's form).
- `Hartshorne.moduleCompletion_tilde_shortExact`: the short exact form (all three terms finite).
The statement is phrased with `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletionFunctor`, the
functor `F ↦ F̂` defined in the leaf module (not recorded: the article is a theorem); the
completion of `M~` is `moduleCompletion (specIdealSubsheaf A I) (M~)`, the module sheaf of
completed-coherent-module over the structure sheaf of `Spf A I`.

## Proof route (Hartshorne's)

Exactness is checked on stalks through sections over a basis: `sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections`
(stalk criterion), phrased through the pushforward `i_*` along `i : |Y| ⟶ X` in
`sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_pushforward_sections`. The basis is `{D(g) ∩ V(I)}`
(`opens_isBasis_map_image`, `PrimeSpectrum.isBasis_basic_opens`, closed embedding of `Y ⟶ X`).
Over an affine open `U` with `Γ(U, 𝒪)` Noetherian, `Γ(U, F)`, `Γ(U, F'')` finite and
`Γ(U, F') → Γ(U, F) → Γ(U, F'')` exact, `moduleCompletionHom_exists_preimage` lifts sections: the
components of a section of `F̂` lift to `Γ(U, F)` (`moduleCompletionSectionsMap_surjective`), form an
`I(U)`-adic Cauchy sequence (transition compatibility and `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_eq_zero_iff`),
map into `I(U)^(n+1) Γ(U, F'')`, and `exists_lift_of_exact` (from `adicCompletion_map_exact`, which
derives middle exactness for non-short sequences from Mathlib's `AdicCompletion.map_exact`,
`map_injective`, `map_surjective`) gives a lift, assembled by `exists_moduleCompletion_sections`.
`moduleCompletionHom_exact_of_isBasis` is the general-scheme statement. For `Spec A` and `D(g)`:
`tilde_map_app_basicOpen_exact` (`IsLocalizedModule.map_exact` with `tilde.toOpen`),
`tilde_finite_basicOpen` (`CoherentAux.finite_basicOpen_of_finite`, `isLocalizing_tilde`), and
`IsLocallyNoetherian.component_noetherian`.

## The canonical maps (route ii of the earlier diagnosis)

`moduleCompletionLevelMap I χ hχ F n : F ⟶ i_* (F / I^(n+1) F)|_Y` (sections module), for any
`χ : 𝒪_X ⟶ i_* 𝒪_{X̂_Y}` compatible with the projections (`hχ`); the leaf instantiates `χ` with a
private copy of the canonical map (`toFormalCompletionRingCatSheaf`). On sections it is
`moduleCompletionSectionsMap` by `rfl`. Its compatibility with the transition maps is an equation
of morphisms, `moduleCompletionLevelMap_comp_moduleCompletionMap`, from the generic
`unit_restrictToComp_pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict` (proved on sections over abstract schemes and
sites from `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app_unit`) and `moduleCompletionMap_eq`. Naturality in
`F`: `moduleCompletionLevelMap_naturality`.

This morphism-level compatibility is what affine-formal-module-tensor (9.4(b)) is stuck on: its
private `toModuleCompletionObjPushforward F n` is `moduleCompletionLevelMap` for
`χ = structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat` (with the same restriction maps; its private
`pushforwardRestrictToComp` is `sheafOfModulesPushforwardRestrictToComp`), so
`moduleCompletionLevelMap_comp_moduleCompletionMap` gives its remaining goal 1 once it imports the
sections module and restates its components through `moduleCompletionLevelMap`.

## Friction (instance paths): what works

The formal-completion sheaves live on the opens of `|Y|` with the
`SheafedSpace.instTopologicalSpaceCarrierCarrier` instance, while `Opens.map (ι 1).base` and
`IsOpenEmbedding.functor` use `TopCat.str`. The comparison itself is cheap, but terms where the same
object appears with both instance paths in implicit arguments often time out (elaborator `whnf` or
kernel). Patterns that worked:

- Work at the morphism level and take sections only through generic lemmas whose instantiation is
  syntactic: `sheafOfModules_val_app_apply_of_comp_eq` (`f ≫ g = h` gives
  `g (f x) = h x`) and `sheafOfModules_val_app_apply_of_comp_eq_comp`. Rewriting
  `(f ≫ g).val.app V x` to `g.val.app V (f.val.app V x)` directly times out.
- Keep section-level statements on the `X` side (sections of `i_*` objects over opens of `X`).
- Limits: `exists_sections_isLimit_mapCone` / `sections_isLimit_mapCone_ext` take
  `IsLimit (G.mapCone c)` (built as `isLimitOfPreserves (pushforward χ) (limit.isLimit _)`), the
  transition maps already pushed forward, and the compatibility as a morphism equation
  `θ (n + 1) ≫ f n = θ n` plus a section equation involving only `θ n`. Versions that construct
  `limit (Functor.ofOpSequence f)` inside the lemma, or that take section-level compatibility with
  `f n` applied, time out (kernel or `whnf`).
- `(SheafOfModules.pushforward χ).IsRightAdjoint` is not found by `inferInstance`; build it from
  `PresheafOfModules.pullbackObjIsDefined_eq_top` and `SheafOfModules.PullbackConstruction.adjunction`.
- `rw [Category.assoc]` and `rw [← Functor.map_comp]` fail on composites mixing `X.Modules` with
  `SheafOfModules X.ringCatSheaf`; chain `Category.assoc`, `whisker_eq`, `eq_whisker` with `Eq.trans`.
- On `Spec R`, use `CoherentAux.D g` rather than `PrimeSpectrum.basicOpen g` for the
  `Module`/`IsScalarTower` instances on `Γ(Spec R, D g)` and `Γ(M~, D g)`.
