# Exactness of affine module completion — implementation note

Status: partial progress, no `lean:` target recorded. Modules:
`Hartshorne/Scheme/AffineFormalModuleExact.lean` and
`Hartshorne/Scheme/FormalCompletionModuleAffineSections.lean` (the shared affine-sections API, also
needed by 9.4(b) and 9.6(a)); both imported by the library root.

## Proved (sorry-free, standard axioms only)

- `Hartshorne.sheaf_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: a `ShortComplex` of sheaves of abelian groups on a
  topological space is exact if, over every open of a basis, sections of the middle term killed by
  the second map lift to the first term. Proof via `TopCat.Sheaf.exact_iff_stalkFunctor_map_exact`
  (the stalk criterion) and `ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff`, using `exists_germ_eq`, `germ_eq` and a
  basis refinement.
- `Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: the same for `SheafOfModules R` over any
  `R : Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) RingCat`, through the faithful exact functor
  `ModuleCohomology.toTopSheaf R` (`ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful`). This is the route
  Hartshorne's proof takes (sections over basic opens `D(f) ∩ V(I)`, then stalks), and it uses the
  stalkwise-exactness dependency.
- Levelwise functoriality of `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletion`:
  `powSmulHom` (`I^(n+1)F ⟶ I^(n+1)G`, with `powSmulHom_comp_powSmulι`),
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom` (`F/I^(n+1)F ⟶ G/I^(n+1)G`, with `π_moduleCompletionQuotientHom`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_id`, `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp_moduleCompletionQuotientMap`), and `moduleCompletionObjHom`
  (`(F/I^(n+1)F)|_Y ⟶ (G/I^(n+1)G)|_Y` over the completed structure sheaf, with `_id`, `_comp`).
- Naturality of the transition maps and the completion functor:
  `moduleCompletionObjHom_comp_moduleCompletionMap` (the square
  `moduleCompletionObjHom φ (n+1) ≫ moduleCompletionMap G n =
  moduleCompletionMap F n ≫ moduleCompletionObjHom φ n`), `moduleCompletionDiagramHom` (via
  `NatTrans.ofOpSequence`, with `_app`, `_id`, `_comp`), `moduleCompletionHom φ : F̂ ⟶ Ĝ` (`limMap`,
  with `moduleCompletionHom_π`, `_id`, `_comp`) and `moduleCompletionFunctor : X.Modules ⥤
  SheafOfModules I.formalCompletionRingCatSheaf`.
- Affine sections of the levels (module `FormalCompletionModuleAffineSections`, general scheme `X`,
  quasi-coherent `F`; no affineness of `X` needed):
  - `Hartshorne.closedImmersion_unit_app_surjective`, `Hartshorne.closedImmersion_unit_app_injective`:
    for a closed immersion `i` and quasi-coherent `G`, the unit `G ⟶ i_* i^* G` is surjective on
    sections over an affine open `V`, and injective there when `ker(Γ(V, 𝒪_X) → Γ(i⁻¹V, 𝒪_Z))` kills
    `Γ(V, G)` (generalizes the ideal-sheaf computation in `ImmersionConormalSheaf`, via
    `specRestrictionPullbackIso`, `exists_one_tmul`, `mem_ker_smul_top_of_one_tmul_eq_zero`).
  - `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletionSectionsMap I F n U : Γ(F, U) ⟶ Γ((F/I^(n+1)F)|_Y,
    U ∩ |Y|)` (quotient map, unit of `ι_(n+1)^* ⊣ ι_(n+1)*`, restriction along
    `functor_obj_preimage_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι`; target type is that of the sections of
    `moduleCompletionObj I F n` over `ι_1⁻¹ U`), with `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_apply` (rfl).
  - `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_surjective` and `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_eq_zero_iff`
    (kernel `I.ideal U ^ (n + 1) • ⊤`) for `U` affine. Helpers: `cokernel_π_powSmulι_app_eq_zero_iff`,
    `sub_powSmul_top_eq`, `surjective_cokernel_π_powSmulι_app`,
    `isQuasicoherent_moduleCompletionQuotient`.
  - Generic transition lemmas over abstract schemes and sites: `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app`
    (moved here from the leaf module), `pullbackUnitTransition_app_unit` (on a pulled-back section
    `a^* x`, `pullbackUnitTransition q` gives the restriction of `b^* (q x)`), and
    `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app_unit` (the same through the restriction along `α`).
  The sub-step "the ideal of `specIdealSubsheaf A I` over `D(f)` is `I A_f`" turned out to be
  unnecessary for this route: work with the affine open `U` itself, `R = Γ(U, 𝒪_X)`, `I(U)` and
  `Γ(U, F)`.
- Morphism-level transition square (route B, first half):
  `pushforward_pullback_cokernelπ_comp_moduleCompletionMap` (in `FormalCompletionModuleAffineSections`):
  `(φ_(n+1))_* (ι_(n+2))^* π_(n+1) ≫ moduleCompletionMap F n =
  pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict … (𝟙 F) ≫ (φ_n)_* (ι_(n+1))^* π_n`, proved by
  `moduleCompletionMap_eq` and `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_naturality` (elaborates in seconds).
  Generic section formula `Hartshorne.pushforward_pullback_map_val_app_unit` for `(φ₂)_* a^* u`.
- Supporting revision of `FormalCompletionModule.lean` (recorded on completed-coherent-module; lead
  approved; recorded names and types unchanged): generic constructions
  `Hartshorne.pullbackUnitTransition` (pull back `q` along `a`, unit of `s^* ⊣ s_*`, then
  `pullbackComp` and `pullbackCongr`) and `Hartshorne.pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict` (its transport
  along `φ₂` followed by `sheafOfModulesPushforwardCompRestrict`), with
  `pullbackUnitTransition_naturality`, `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_naturality` and
  `sheafOfModulesPushforwardCompRestrict_naturality`, all over abstract schemes and sites. The bodies
  of `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` and `moduleCompletionMap` were restated as instances of these
  (same composite, index `n + 1 + 1` throughout), with the `rfl` lemmas
  `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap_eq` and `moduleCompletionMap_eq`.

## Remaining goals

1c. Transition compatibility (the one missing piece of goal 1):
   `(I.moduleCompletionMap F n).val.app (op (ι_1 ⁻¹ᵁ U)) (I.moduleCompletionSectionsMap F (n+1) U s)
   = I.moduleCompletionSectionsMap F n U s` for every open `U`. Mathematically it is
   `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app_unit` with `q = moduleCompletionQuotientMap F n`, or
   equivalently the square above applied to the section `ι_(n+2)^* s` restricted to `ι_1⁻¹ U`
   (`congrArg (fun φ => φ.val.app _ y)`; that extraction and the rewrite of its left-hand side with
   `pushforward_pullback_map_val_app_unit` both elaborate fast), followed by
   `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app_unit` with `q = 𝟙 F` on the right. What fails is
   matching any instance of the generic section lemmas against concrete section terms.
   Diagnosis (explicit-term diffs of goal against instance, plus `diagnostics`):
   - Instance paths. `formalCompletionRingCatSheaf` is declared over
     `Opens.grothendieckTopology (I.infinitesimalNeighborhood 1 : TopCat)`, which elaborates with
     `TopologicalSpace` instance `SheafedSpace.instTopologicalSpaceCarrierCarrier`, while
     `IsOpenEmbedding.functor`, `Scheme.Opens` and the lemma
     `functor_obj_preimage_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι` use `TopCat.str` of the carrier. Generic
     variables (`C`, `F₂ : C ⥤ Opens Y`, the `≤` in `h₂`) pick one of the two, so every
     instantiation differs from the concrete term in hundreds of `Opens` instance arguments.
     Unifying `instTopologicalSpaceCarrierCarrier Y =?= (carrier Y).str` inside these terms makes
     `whnf` reduce the projection through `infinitesimalNeighborhood` (gluing, ideal powers,
     `powSmul`), which is the timeout (elaborator and, after `erw`, the kernel).
   - Indices. `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict` instantiated with `s = infinitesimalNeighborhoodSucc n`
     infers `Y = Y_(n+2)`, while the sections of `moduleCompletionObj F (n+1)` live over
     `Y_(n+1+1)`; forcing `(Y := I.infinitesimalNeighborhood (n + 1 + 1))` removes these diffs.
   Tried and rejected (each leaves a timeout): forcing `Y`, `T` or both; restating
   `formalCompletionRingCatSheaf`'s declared site with the `TopCat.str` instance and the generic
   variables with `Y.Opens` (this removes the site diffs but the `≤`/category instances in
   restriction maps still differ); named constants for the composites (`rfl` between a constant
   and its body is fast, but a statement written identically to the generic instance still fails
   `exact`); `attribute [local irreducible] infinitesimalNeighborhood` (breaks projections:
   "invalid projection"). The closest: with `Y` forced in `moduleCompletionMap`'s body,
   `rw [moduleCompletionMap_eq, moduleCompletionSectionsMap_apply']` (sections stated with
   `(pullback ι).obj (moduleCompletionQuotient F n)` instead of `moduleCompletionNeighborhood`),
   then `erw [pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app_unit]` succeeds in the elaborator and leaves
   the expected goal `ρ(unit(q(π_(n+1) s))) = ρ(unit(π_n s))`, but the kernel times out on the
   cast (also at 1000000 heartbeats).
   Next routes: (i) make the instance path uniform at the source, i.e. state the formal-completion
   sheaves (`formalCompletionSheaf`, `formalCompletionRingCatSheaf`) and
   `functor_obj_preimage_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι` over one carrier form, so that all section

   `unit_comp_pushforward_transition`, then take `.val.app` of that equation with `θ_n` defined

   With 1c, `isLimitModuleCompletionSections` identifies `Γ(U ∩ |Y|, F̂)` with the `I(U)`-adic

2. Conclusion. For an exact composable pair `M' ⟶ M ⟶ M''` of finite `A`-modules, use
   `sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections` with the basis `{ι_1⁻¹ U | U affine open of Spec A}`
   of `|Y|`. For `x` in the sections of the completion of `M~` over `ι_1⁻¹ U` killed by the map,
   write its components `x_n = θ_n(s_n)` (`moduleCompletionSectionsMap_surjective`); by 1c and
   `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_eq_zero_iff`, `(s_n)` is an element of `AdicCompletion (I.ideal U)
   Γ(U, M~)` mapping to zero in that of `Γ(U, M''~)`; `AdicCompletion.map_exact` over the Noetherian
   ring `Γ(U, 𝒪)` (`isNoetherianRing_affineOpen`) with finite modules `Γ(U, M~)` (coherence of `M~`)
   and the exactness of `Γ(U, -)` on the tilde sequence (affine, quasi-coherent kernels:
   `modules_sections_exact`, `surjective_cokernel_π_app`) gives a preimage; naturality of θ in `F`

   transfer it back. No localization or `I A_f` identification is needed.

## Friction (elaboration and kernel)

- Comparing a term built with `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` (or the old composite body) against
  a freshly elaborated composite times out in the elaborator or the kernel, even when every piece
  is definitionally equal and small probes (`n + 1 + 1` against `n + 2` inside
  `infinitesimalNeighborhood`, `moduleCompletionNeighborhood` against its pullback form) are fast.
  What works: state a lemma once over abstract schemes, sites and functors, restate the concrete
  definition as a literal instance of the generic definition, and rewrite with the `rfl` lemma
  (`moduleCompletionMap_eq`) before applying the generic lemma with all explicit arguments.
- `rw [← Functor.map_comp]` fails on composites mixing `X.Modules` and
  `SheafOfModules X.ringCatSheaf` (the goal is not type-correct at instance transparency); use the
  generic lemmas instead.
- An `rfl` proof of `moduleCompletionMap F n = pushforward.map (moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap F n)
  ≫ restriction` still times out in the elaborator, so that form was not added.
- Section-level goals involving `Scheme.Modules` pushforwards are often not type-correct at
  instance transparency (`Γ(a_* P, U)` versus `Γ(P, a⁻¹ U)`), so `rw` with `ConcreteCategory.comp_apply`
  or `Hom.app_smul` fails; `erw`, or `Eq.trans` with the lemma instantiated explicitly, works.
- A proof of `f ≫ a = b` passed to the generic lemmas must be the same term as in the goal
  (`Scheme.IdealSheafData.inclusion_subschemeι _`, not `infinitesimalNeighborhoodSucc_comp_ι`,
  whose type has `n + 2`), or keyed rewriting fails.
