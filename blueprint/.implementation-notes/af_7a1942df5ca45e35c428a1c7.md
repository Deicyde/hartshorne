# Exactness of affine module completion — implementation note

Status: partial progress, no `lean:` target recorded. Module:
`Hartshorne/Scheme/AffineFormalModuleExact.lean` (imported by the library root).

## Proved (sorry-free, standard axioms only)

- `Hartshorne.sheaf_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: a `ShortComplex` of sheaves of abelian groups on a
  topological space is exact if, over every open of a basis, sections of the middle term killed by
  the second map lift to the first term. Proof via `TopCat.Sheaf.exact_iff_stalkFunctor_map_exact`
  (the stalk criterion) and `ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff`, using `exists_germ_eq`, `germ_eq` and a
  basis refinement.
- `Hartshorne.sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_sections`: the same for `SheafOfModules R` over any
  `R : Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) RingCat`, through the faithful exact functor
  `ModuleCohomology.toTopSheaf R` (`ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful`). This is the route
  Hartshorne's proof takes (sections over basic opens `D(f) ∩ V(I)`, then stalks), and it uses the
  stalkwise-exactness dependency.
- Levelwise functoriality of `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.moduleCompletion`:
  `powSmulHom` (`I^(n+1)F ⟶ I^(n+1)G`, with `powSmulHom_comp_powSmulι`),
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom` (`F/I^(n+1)F ⟶ G/I^(n+1)G`, with `π_moduleCompletionQuotientHom`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_id`, `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp`,
  `moduleCompletionQuotientHom_comp_moduleCompletionQuotientMap`), and `moduleCompletionObjHom`
  (`(F/I^(n+1)F)|_Y ⟶ (G/I^(n+1)G)|_Y` over the completed structure sheaf, with `_id`, `_comp`).
- Naturality of the transition maps and the completion functor:
  `moduleCompletionObjHom_comp_moduleCompletionMap` (the square
  `moduleCompletionObjHom φ (n+1) ≫ moduleCompletionMap G n =
  moduleCompletionMap F n ≫ moduleCompletionObjHom φ n`), `moduleCompletionDiagramHom` (via
  `NatTrans.ofOpSequence`, with `_app`, `_id`, `_comp`), `moduleCompletionHom φ : F̂ ⟶ Ĝ` (`limMap`,
  with `moduleCompletionHom_π`, `_id`, `_comp`) and `moduleCompletionFunctor : X.Modules ⥤
  SheafOfModules I.formalCompletionRingCatSheaf`. Also the generic section formula
  `Hartshorne.pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_val_app`.
- Supporting revision of `FormalCompletionModule.lean` (recorded on completed-coherent-module; lead
  approved; recorded names and types unchanged): generic constructions
  `Hartshorne.pullbackUnitTransition` (pull back `q` along `a`, unit of `s^* ⊣ s_*`, then
  `pullbackComp` and `pullbackCongr`) and `Hartshorne.pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict` (its transport
  along `φ₂` followed by `sheafOfModulesPushforwardCompRestrict`), with
  `pullbackUnitTransition_naturality`, `pullbackUnitTransitionRestrict_naturality` and
  `sheafOfModulesPushforwardCompRestrict_naturality`, all over abstract schemes and sites. The bodies
  of `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` and `moduleCompletionMap` were restated as instances of these
  (same composite, index `n + 1 + 1` throughout), with the `rfl` lemmas
  `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap_eq` and `moduleCompletionMap_eq`.

## Friction (elaboration and kernel)

- Comparing a term built with `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap` (or the old composite body) against
  a freshly elaborated composite times out in the elaborator or the kernel, even when every piece
  is definitionally equal and small probes (`n + 1 + 1` against `n + 2` inside
  `infinitesimalNeighborhood`, `moduleCompletionNeighborhood` against its pullback form) are fast.
  What works: state a lemma once over abstract schemes, sites and functors, restate the concrete
  definition as a literal instance of the generic definition, and rewrite with the `rfl` lemma
  (`moduleCompletionMap_eq`) before applying the generic lemma with all explicit arguments.
- `rw [← Functor.map_comp]` fails on composites mixing `X.Modules` and
  `SheafOfModules X.ringCatSheaf` (the goal is not type-correct at instance transparency); use the
  generic lemmas instead.
- An `rfl` proof of `moduleCompletionMap F n = pushforward.map (moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap F n)
  ≫ restriction` still times out in the elaborator, so that form was not added.
