# Infinitesimal extensions of a nonsingular affine scheme

## Status

Statement and proof compiled, sorry-free, in
`Hartshorne/Scheme/Differentials/AffineNonsingularInfinitesimalExtensions.lean`; pending review.
Main theorem: `Hartshorne.InfinitesimalExtension.nonempty_trivialIso`.

## Encoding

* `Hartshorne.InfinitesimalExtension pX F` (for `pX : X ⟶ Spec k`, `F : X.Modules`): a scheme
  `X'` with `X' ⟶ Spec k`, a closed immersion `ι : X ⟶ X'` over `k` (this is
  `(X', 𝒪_{X'}/I) ≅ X`, with `I = Hartshorne.closedImmersionIdealSheaf ι`), the condition
  `I² = 0` on sections, and an isomorphism `ι_* F ≅ I` of `𝒪_{X'}`-modules. Since `ι` turns out to
  be a homeomorphism and `I` is a module over `𝒪_{X'}/I = ι_* 𝒪_X`, this is the book's
  "`I ≅ F` as `𝒪_X`-modules". The `I² = 0` field is implied by the module isomorphism, but it is
  part of the book's definition and is used in the proof.
* `Hartshorne.InfinitesimalExtension.trivialExtensionPresheaf F`: the presheaf of rings
  `U ↦ Γ(U, 𝒪_X) ⊕ Γ(U, F)` (Mathlib's `TrivSqZeroExt`, with the right action of `Γ(U, 𝒪_X)`
  equal to the left one via the local instance `opModule`) on the space of `X`.
* `Hartshorne.InfinitesimalExtension.TrivialIso E`: an isomorphism of presheaves of rings
  `𝒪_{X'} ≅ ι_* (𝒪_X ⊕ F)` compatible with `ι^#` and the projection to `𝒪_X` (field `comp_fst`),
  with the `k`-structures (field `app_over`, stated on global sections, which determines it on every
  open by naturality), and restricting to the given `I ≅ F` (field `app_ideal`).
* The theorem concludes `IsHomeomorph ι.base ∧ Nonempty E.TrivialIso`, so the isomorphism is an
  isomorphism of ringed spaces `(X', 𝒪_{X'}) ≅ (X, 𝒪_X ⊕ F)`.
* "Affine nonsingular of finite type over `k`" is `[IsAffine X] [LocallyOfFiniteType pX]` and
  `X.IsRegular` (the hypothesis of the recorded Ex. 8.6 lifting theorem).

## Proof route

1. `I` lies in the nilradical, so `ι` is surjective (`Scheme.Hom.support_ker`), hence a
   homeomorphism (`isHomeomorph`).
2. For affine `U ⊆ X'`, `Γ(U, 𝒪_{X'})` is Noetherian (`isNoetherianRing_sections`): the exact
   sequence `0 → Γ(ι⁻¹U, F) → Γ(U, 𝒪_{X'}) → Γ(ι⁻¹U, 𝒪_X) → 0` of `Γ(U, 𝒪_{X'})`-modules has
   Noetherian ends (coherence through `Hartshorne.coherent_affine_local_tfae`, and restriction of
   scalars along a surjection). So `X'` is Noetherian, and its reduction lifts through `ι`
   (`IsClosedImmersion.lift`) to a closed subscheme of the affine `X`; Hartshorne III Ex. 3.1
   (`Hartshorne.ReductionAffine.isAffine_iff_isAffine_reduced`) gives `X'` affine
   (`isAffine_scheme`).
3. `Γ(X', 𝒪) → Γ(X, 𝒪)` is surjective with square-zero kernel; Ex. 8.6
   (`Hartshorne.AffineNonsingularLifting.exists_lift_of_isRegular`) gives a `k`-section `s`, and
   `ρ := X'.toSpecΓ ≫ Spec.map s ≫ X.isoSpec.inv` is a retraction of `ι` over `k`
   (`exists_retraction`).
4. `splitHom`: `(a, m) ↦ ρ.appLE a + (ι ∘ φ)(m)` on every open; bijective because it is a section
   of `ι^#` plus the inclusion of `ker ι^#` (sections of the kernel sheaf are the kernel on
   sections: `exists_closedImmersionIdealSheafι_app`, `closedImmersionIdealSheafι_app_injective`).
   These assemble to the isomorphism `split : ι_* (𝒪_X ⊕ F) ⟶ 𝒪_{X'}`.

## Divergence from the roadmap edges

The sheafification step uses the `Spec`/global-sections adjunction (the retraction `ρ`) rather
than the tilde equivalence, so the Lean does not use the affine quasi-coherent equivalence
article's declaration. Affineness of `X'` is not stated in the article's sketch; it uses
Hartshorne III Ex. 3.1, linked as a proof dependency.

## Lean friction

* `closedImmersionIdealSheaf` and `closedImmersionIdealSheafι` are typed in the `SheafOfModules`
  category, not `X.Modules`; dot notation `.app` does not resolve on them and `kernel`/`Mono`
  instances must be taken in `SheafOfModules X.ringCatSheaf` (`ShortComplex.mk (C := ...)`,
  `equalizer.ι_mono`).
* Sections of `SheafOfModules.unit` are defeq but not syntactically `Γ(X, U)`; the structure
  fields use `show Γ(X', U) from _` to multiply them.
