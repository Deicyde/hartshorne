# Implementation note: the canonical sheaf of a product

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.CanonicalSheafOfProduct`.

## Declarations

- `Hartshorne.canonicalSheaf_product`: the book statement (Hartshorne II, Ex. 8.3(b)). Setting: `k`
  algebraically closed, `X`, `Y` integral, separated, of finite type over `k`, regular. Conclusion:
  `Nonempty (canonicalSheaf (pullback.fst pX pY ≫ pX) ≅ moduleSheafTensor (p_X^* canonicalSheaf pX)
  (p_Y^* canonicalSheaf pY))`, with the tensor product `moduleSheafTensor` of module sheaves.
  Separatedness is only passed on to `product_nonsingular_variety`; it is kept because the book's
  varieties are separated.
- `Hartshorne.nonempty_exteriorPower_relativeDifferentials_product_iso`: the general form behind it.
  For schemes `X`, `Y` over any `S` with `Ω_{X/S}`, `Ω_{Y/S}` locally free of ranks `m`, `n`,
  `⋀^{m+n} Ω_{(X ×_S Y)/S} ≅ p_X^*(⋀^m Ω_{X/S}) ⊗ p_Y^*(⋀^n Ω_{Y/S})`.
- `Hartshorne.pullbackExteriorPowerMap` / `pullbackExteriorPowerMap_etaSec` /
  `pullbackExteriorPowerMap_isIso` / `pullbackExteriorPowerIso`: pullback commutes with exterior
  powers of a sheaf locally free of finite rank. The map `f^*(⋀^r F) ⟶ ⋀^r (f^*F)` is adjoint to
  `exteriorPowerPushforwardMap f (pullbackUnit F) r` (from the canonical-pullback module). It is
  an isomorphism because it carries the pulled-back exterior frame `η(e_K)` to the exterior frame
  of the pulled-back frame `η(e)` (the same pattern as `pullbackTensorMap_isIso` in the
  Picard-pullback module, using `Ringed.IsLocalFrame.app_bijective_of_map`,
  `Ringed.IsLocalFrame.pullback`, `ExteriorPerfectPairing.isLocalFrame_exteriorPowerFrame`).

## Route

The book's hint: Ex. 8.3(a) (`relativeDifferentialsProductIso`) gives the split short exact
sequence `0 → p_X^*Ω_X → Ω_{X×Y} → p_Y^*Ω_Y → 0` (built as the biproduct sequence conjugated by
the iso, so that its middle term is literally `Ω_{X×Y}` and no functoriality of `⋀^r` is needed);
Ex. II.5.16(d) (`ExteriorPowerExactFiltration.exteriorPower_exact_det`) gives
`⋀^{m+n} Ω_{X×Y} ≅ ⋀^m p_X^*Ω_X ⊗ ⋀^n p_Y^*Ω_Y`; pullback-exterior-power commutation and
`Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoOfIso` finish. The book setting uses Theorem II.8.15
(`isRegular_iff_relativeDifferentials_isLocallyFreeOfRank`) for the ranks of `Ω_X`, `Ω_Y`, and
`product_nonsingular_variety` for `dim (X ×_k Y) = dim X + dim Y`, so that
`canonicalSheaf_eq` identifies `ω_{X×Y}` with `⋀^{m+n} Ω_{X×Y}`.

## Gaps

Pullback commuting with exterior powers had no blueprint article; it is proved here as a helper.
