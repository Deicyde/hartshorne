# Constant fibre dimension of relative differentials

## Prior implementation guidance

This is a statement about the scheme differential sheaf and the finite-module
criterion for local freeness; it is not definitionally the modern Mathlib
smoothness predicate.

## Supporting implementation references

- `Algebra.IsStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension.rank_kaehlerDifferential`

## Blocked attempt: missing prerequisites

The statement was not recorded: under the strict policy it needs a complete
proof, and the proof uses results the Markdown dependencies do not reach.

- Reverse direction (constant fibre dimension implies locally free of rank
  $n$) needs coherence of $\Omega_{X/Y}$ (article
  `schemes/differentials/scheme-differentials/relative-differentials-quasicoherent`)
  and the statement that a coherent sheaf with a free stalk is free nearby
  (article `schemes/coherent-sheaves/coherent-free-locus`). Neither is reached.
- It also needs Hartshorne II, Exercise 5.8(c): on an integral (or reduced)
  Noetherian scheme a coherent sheaf of constant fibre dimension is locally
  free. II.5's source note records Exercise 5.8 as not adopted, there is no
  roadmap article for it, and Mathlib has no reduced-ring fibre-dimension
  criterion. The "Proof depends on" bullet naming it is prose, not a link.
- Forward direction (locally free of rank $n$ implies fibre dimension $n$)
  needs the stalk of a locally free sheaf of rank $n$ to be free of rank $n$.
  Freeness of stalks is in the coherent-free-locus module (helpers
  `Hartshorne.free_stalk_free_spec`, `Hartshorne.free_stalk_of_isLocallyFree`);
  the rank still has to be tracked through the localization.
- The corollary with Mathlib's `AlgebraicGeometry.SmoothOfRelativeDimension`
  needs the affine description $\Omega_{X/Y}|_V \cong (\Omega_{B/A})^\sim$
  (article `schemes/differentials/scheme-differentials/affine-relative-differentials`,
  plus the unrecorded helper `AlgebraicGeometry.RelDiffLocal.affineIso` in the
  quasi-coherence module) and the Mathlib fact
  `Algebra.IsStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension.rank_kaehlerDifferential`.
  Hartshorne's own smoothness definition is the open article
  `cohomology/smooth-morphisms/criteria/hartshorne-smoothness-criterion`,
  which uses this leaf, so it cannot supply the corollary.

## Checked partial Lean

`Hartshorne.free_of_finrank_residueField_eq_finrank_fractionRing` (module
`Hartshorne.Scheme.FibreDimensionFree`, no `sorry`, standard axioms only): over
a local domain $R$ with residue field $k$ and fraction field $K$, a finitely
generated module $M$ with $\dim_k(k\otimes M)=\dim_K(K\otimes M)$ has a basis
indexed by $\operatorname{Fin}(\dim_k(k\otimes M))$. This is the local core of
Exercise 5.8(c) for integral schemes, and could be the Lean for a new
Exercise 5.8(c) article.

## Remaining route

1. At $x\in X$, write $M=(\Omega_{X/Y})_x$, a finite module over the local
   domain $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$. On an affine open around $x$, quasi-coherence
   makes the stalk at the generic point $\eta$ the localization of $M$ at
   $(0)$, so $\Omega_\eta\otimes k(\eta)\cong K\otimes M$ with
   $K=\operatorname{Frac}\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$.
2. Constant fibre dimension $n$ gives equal special and generic fibre
   dimensions, so the lemma above makes $M$ free of rank $n$. Then the
   coherent free-locus result makes $\Omega_{X/Y}$ free on a neighbourhood,
   with rank $n$ by comparing with the stalk.
3. For the converse, pass the rank-$n$ presentation to stalks and tensor with
   $k(x)$.
4. For the corollary, use the affine identification and the rank of Kähler
   differentials of a standard-smooth algebra.
