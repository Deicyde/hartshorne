# A nowhere-vanishing section of a high-rank generated bundle

## Status: complete (candidate, pending review)

Modules (sorry-free; axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound):
`Hartshorne.Scheme.NowhereVanishingSectionCount` (commutative algebra),
`Hartshorne.Scheme.LocalFrameGerms` (local frames and stalks),
`Hartshorne.Scheme.NowhereVanishingSection` (the result).

### Statement

`Hartshorne.exists_nonvanishing_section_shortExact`: `k` algebraically closed,
`q : X ⟶ Spec k`, `[IsIntegral X]`, `FiniteType q`, `[IsSeparated q]`,
`topologicalKrullDim X = n`, `E.IsLocallyFreeOfRank r`, `n < r`, global sections
`v : Fin (N + 1) → Γ(E, ⊤)` with `E.IsGeneratedBySections (fun i : ULift (Fin (N + 1)) => v i.down)`.
Conclusion: some `s ∈ linearSystem q v` (the `k`-span `V` of the `v_i`) has
`nonvanishingLocus E s = Set.univ` (i.e. `s_x ∉ 𝔪_x E_x` for all `x`), and there are `E'`,
`p : E ⟶ E'` with `ShortComplex.mk (sectionHom E s) p _` short exact and
`E'.IsLocallyFreeOfRank (r - 1)`. Here `Hartshorne.NowhereVanishingSection.sectionHom E s` is the
morphism `𝒪_X ⟶ E` sending `1` to `s` (`sectionHom_app`: on sections over `U` it is `b ↦ b s|_U`).

"V finite-dimensional, generating E" is rendered by a finite spanning family of `V` that
generates `E`; the two are equivalent since germs of `k`-combinations lie in the span of the
germs. `[IsSeparated q]` is unused (Batteries `unusedArguments` reports it); it is kept because
it is part of the definition of a variety. The general forms, without separatedness and over any
infinite field, are the two lemmas below.

### General lemmas

- `Hartshorne.NowhereVanishingSection.exists_mem_linearSystem_nonvanishingLocus_eq_univ`
  (`[Infinite k]`, integral, finite type, no separatedness): the nowhere-vanishing section.
- `Hartshorne.NowhereVanishingSection.shortExact_and_isLocallyFreeOfRank_cokernel`
  (`[IsReduced X] [IsLocallyNoetherian X]`, `E` locally free of rank `r`, `s` nowhere vanishing):
  `0 → 𝒪_X → E → coker → 0` short exact with the cokernel locally free of rank `r - 1`.

### Route

Part 1, the section (incidence dimension count, chart by chart, as in the proof of II.8.18):

- `exists_embedding_isLocalFrame` (LocalFrameGerms): near each point some `r` of the `v_l` form a
  local frame. The germs generate the free stalk `E_x`; Mathlib's
  `Module.exists_basis_of_span_of_flat` extracts a basis; `exists_isLocalFrame_basicOpen_of_span_germ`
  turns sections whose germs generate `E_x` into a frame on `D(det C)`, `C` the matrix of
  coordinates in a given frame (invertible at `x` because the frame's germs are linearly
  independent: `linearIndependent_germ_of_isLocalFrame`).
- `exists_open_polynomial`: on an affine `W` inside such a neighbourhood, `A = Γ(X, W)` (with the
  `k`-algebra structure `sectionsAlgebra q W`, restriction of `kConst q`) is a domain of finite
  type with `ringKrullDim A ≤ n` (`DimensionCodimension.exists_ringKrullDim_eq`). The coordinates
  `a_{j l}` of `v_l|_W` in the frame `v_{σ j}|_W` have an identity block, so
  `NowhereVanishingSection.exists_ne_zero_forall_span_eq_top` (NowhereVanishingSectionCount) gives
  `P ≠ 0` with `(∑_l t_l a_{j l})_j` generating the unit ideal whenever `P(t) ≠ 0`; that algebraic
  lemma substitutes `T_{σ j} ↦ -∑_{l ∉ σ} a_{j l} T_l` into `A[T_l : l ∉ σ]`, whose transcendence
  degree is its Krull dimension `≤ n + (N + 1 - r) < N + 1` (`exists_ringKrullDim_eq_trdeg`,
  Theorem I.1.8A), so the substitution is not injective. Then the germ of `s` at `y ∈ W` has frame
  coordinates generating the unit ideal, hence is not in `𝔪_y E_y`.
- Compactness of `X` gives finitely many `W`; `exists_forall_eval_ne_zero` (infinite field,
  `MvPolynomial.funext`) gives a common non-root `t`.

Part 2, the exact sequence:

- `mono_sectionHom`: on sections `b ↦ b s|_U` is injective since `s_y` is unimodular in the free
  `E_y` (`eq_zero_of_smul_eq_zero_of_notMem`) and sections are determined by germs.
- `shortExact_and_isLocallyFreeOfRank_cokernel`: `E' = cokernel`. `E` coherent
  (`Ringed.IsFiniteLocallyFree.isFinitePresentation` + `coherent_affine_local_tfae`), so `E'`
  coherent (`isCoherent_cokernel`). Stalks are right exact
  (`NonsingularConormal.stalk_exact_and_surjective`), the image of `𝒪_{X,x}` in `E_x` is
  `𝒪_{X,x} s_x`, and `finrank_residueField_tensor_eq_sub_one` gives fibre dimension `r - 1` at every
  point; `isLocallyFreeOfRank_iff_forall_finrank_fibre` (II, Exercise 5.8(c)) concludes.

### Relation to the article's dependencies

The proof follows the method of the proof of Theorem II.8.18 (incidence correspondence, fibres of
codimension `rank E`, dimension count, image not dense) but in affine coordinates on charts, with
the dimension count done algebraically through transcendence degree. It does not use the
declarations recorded on bertini-bad-hyperplane-incidence (those are specific to hyperplane
sections of a projective embedding). Of the Bertini chart machinery, only the idea of solving the
linear system on a chart (`bertiniQuotEquiv`, private there) is reused, in the simpler form of an
identity block.

### Friction

- `Ringed.localFrameMap_apply` and similar Ringed lemmas do not rewrite in goals elaborated with
  `R := X.sheaf` (the `Sheaf`/`TopCat.Sheaf` mismatch makes the motive ill-typed at reducible
  transparency); `localFrameMap_apply_scheme` restates it in scheme notation, and binder type
  ascriptions (`fun (a : κ → Γ(X, V)) => _`) avoid the Ringed types.
- The module structure on `moduleStalk (SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf).val x` is not found
  by instance search over `X.ringCatSheaf`; working with the types produced by
  `Ringed.moduleStalkFunctor (R := X.sheaf) x` avoids it.
- `SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf` lives in the `SheafOfModules` category, so cokernels of maps
  out of it need `structureModuleOf X` (typed `X.Modules`) as the source.
