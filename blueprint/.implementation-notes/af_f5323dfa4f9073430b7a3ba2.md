# Morphisms from generated invertible sheaves

## Status: both directions proved in Lean, sorry-free (pending review)

Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.MorphismFromGeneratedLineBundle`.

Declarations for the article's `lean:` field:

- `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceIntCoordinate`: the coordinate `xᵢ` as a global section of
  `𝒪(1)` on `ℙⁿ_ℤ` (the degree-one section `xᵢ / 1`).
- `Hartshorne.pullbackGlobalSection`: `φ^* e` for a global section `e` of an `𝒪_Y`-module
  (built from `pullbackSection`, restricted to `⊤`).
- `Hartshorne.projectiveSpaceCoordinate`: `xᵢ` on `ℙⁿ_Y`, the pullback of
  `projectiveSpaceIntCoordinate` along `ℙⁿ_Y ⟶ ℙⁿ_ℤ`.
- `Hartshorne.isInvertible_pullback_projectiveSpaceTwistingSheaf`: (a), `φ^* 𝒪(1)` is invertible.
- `Hartshorne.isGeneratedBySections_pullback_projectiveSpaceCoordinate`: (a), the `φ^* xᵢ`
  generate `φ^* 𝒪(1)`.
- `Hartshorne.existsUnique_morphism_of_isGeneratedBySections`: (b), existence and uniqueness of
  the `Y`-morphism with an isomorphism `φ^* 𝒪(1) ≅ L` carrying each `φ^* xᵢ` to `sᵢ`.

The Lean works over an arbitrary base scheme `Y` with structure morphism `p : X ⟶ Y`; the book's
case is `Y = Spec A`. Part (a) is stated for every morphism `φ : X ⟶ ℙⁿ_Y` (being over `Y` is
not needed). Families of sections are indexed by `ULift (Fin (n + 1))` inside
`IsGeneratedBySections`, whose index type lives in the universe of `X`.

## Proof outline (follows p.150)

- Charts: on `Vₖ = φ⁻¹(D₊(xₖ))`, `φ^* xₖ` trivializes `φ^* 𝒪(1)`
  (`isTrivializingSection_chartPreimage`) and `φ^* xₗ = φ^#(xₗ/xₖ) · φ^* xₖ`
  (`restrict_pullbackGlobalSection_eq_smul`). Part (a) follows since the `Vₖ` cover `X`.
- `isTrivializingSection_nonvanishingLocusOpens`: a section `s` of an invertible sheaf
  trivializes it on `X_s` (local statement glued by `IsTrivializingSection.of_local`).
- Construction (the book's chart maps): `sectionRatio k l = sₗ/sₖ` on `X_{sₖ}`;
  `chartMorphism k : X_{sₖ} ⟶ D₊(xₖ)` is `Spec` of `xₗ/xₖ ↦ sₗ/sₖ` (via `projChartMap`);
  the charts agree on overlaps by the cocycle identity (`chartMorphism_compat`) and glue with
  `glueOpens` to `morphismToProjInt : X ⟶ ℙⁿ_ℤ`; then `φ = pullback.lift` with `p`.
- Uniqueness: any `φ` with such an isomorphism has `Vₖ = X_{sₖ}` (`chartPreimage_eq_locus`) and
  `φ^#(xₗ/xₖ) = sₗ/sₖ` there (`appLE_ratioInt_of_iso`); `projInt_hom_ext` says a morphism into
  `ℙⁿ_ℤ` is determined by these data.
- Pullback data: `exists_iso_of_chartData` glues the local isomorphisms `φ^* xₖ ↦ sₖ` with
  `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames`.

## Abstraction candidates

- `Ringed.exists_iso_of_frames` repeats the proof of `Ringed.nonempty_iso_of_frames`
  (Scheme/VeryAmpleTensorProduct.lean) while also recording that the isomorphism carries the
  frames to each other. `nonempty_iso_of_frames` could be derived from it.
- `exists_mem_nonvanishingLocus_of_isGeneratedBySections` is the specific-sections form of
  `exists_mem_nonvanishingLocus_of_isGloballyGenerated` (Scheme/NormalizationDivisorDescent.lean).
- `projInt_hom_ext` and `morphismToProjInt_appLE` follow the same pattern as the Segre-morphism
  chart lemmas (Scheme/RelativeSegre.lean).
