# Projective-space smoothness over an arbitrary base

## Viable proof route

The arbitrary-base theorem has a complete chart proof in the current Lean and
Mathlib APIs. For the total-degree grading on
`MvPolynomial (Fin (n + 1)) R`, the coordinate-variable basic opens cover
`Proj`; the irrelevant-ideal inclusion follows from the description of the
ideal generated by all variables. Dehomogenization and homogenization give a
ring equivalence from the degree-zero localization away from `X i` to the
polynomial ring in the remaining coordinates. The injectivity argument works
over the universe-lifted integers by mapping to the fraction field, while the
surjectivity argument is the explicit homogenization construction.

For projective space over `Spec (ULift Z)`, this identifies every coordinate
chart with affine `n`-space. Source locality for smooth morphisms and the
existing affine-space theorem prove that the absolute projective-space map is
smooth of relative dimension `n`; stability under base change then proves the
same assertion for relative projective space over an arbitrary scheme.

## Source-domain boundary

The source inventory fixes a base field `k` and reads the schemes in
Hartshorne III.10 as finite type over `k`. The roadmap now keeps this
arbitrary-base theorem as an `origin: bridged` helper. The separate cited
article is the fixed-field, finite-type corollary. Attach the chart proof to
this helper, not directly to the cited corollary.

## Adopted Roadmap split

The roadmap splits the work into two leaves:

1. an `origin: bridged` helper for the arbitrary-scheme theorem proved by the
   chart and base-change route above; and
2. an `origin: cited` corollary stated in the fixed-field, finite-type context
   adopted for Hartshorne III.10.

The cited corollary depends on this bridged helper. Formalize should first
package the verified arbitrary-base chart proof here, then discharge the cited
corollary as its specialization.

## Module organization

Do not place the full projective-chart construction in the affine-space
module. Put the ring-general coordinate-chart equivalence in a reusable
projective-space chart module, and put the public arbitrary-base smoothness
helper and the source-shaped corollary in a dedicated projective-space
smoothness module. Keep implementation-only lemmas private where possible.
Adding those modules to the library root is an import change and requires the
full import-impact, skeleton, conflict, and module-claim protocol.
