# The conormal sequence of a closed immersion

Target statement: `Hartshorne.ClosedConormal.closedImmersion_conormal_exact`. For a closed
immersion `i : Z ⟶ X` and a morphism `f : X ⟶ Y`, the sequence
`i^* I ⟶ i^* Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ Ω_{Z/Y}` is exact and the second map is an epimorphism.

- The left term is the conormal sheaf `i^* I` of the closed immersion. Its pushforward is
  `I/I²` (`isIso_idealSheafSqQuotientToPushforward`).
- The second map is the transitivity map `pullbackRelativeDifferentialsToComp i f`.
- The first map `conormalToPullback` is the adjoint of `I ⟶ i_* i^* Ω_{X/Y}`, `a ↦ 1 ⊗ d a`
  (`idealToPushforward`).

The proof shows that the second map is a cokernel of the first; this is not checked on affine
charts. A map `τ : i^*Ω_{X/Y} ⟶ M` that kills the conormal image gives `a ↦ τ(1 ⊗ d a)`, a
derivation `𝒪_X ⟶ i_* M` that kills `I`. It descends to a derivation `𝒪_Z ⟶ M` over `Y`
(section `Descent`): sections of `𝒪_Z` lift locally to `𝒪_X` because the stalk maps of `i` are
surjective, and two lifts differ by a section of `I` near each point. The descended values are
glued with the sheaf property of `M`.
