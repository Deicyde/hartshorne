# Degree-one basic opens cover Proj of a degree-one-generated ring

## Candidate Lean declarations

Not recorded yet (no `lean:` on purpose); a later Formalize pass should record these after
independent review:

- `Hartshorne.ProjTwistingSheaf.exists_mem_one_notMem` in `Hartshorne/Scheme/ProjTwistingSheaf.lean`:
  every point of `Proj 𝒜` lies outside some `f ∈ 𝒜 1`, under
  `Algebra.adjoin (𝒜 0) (𝒜 1 : Set A) = ⊤`.
- Its helper `Hartshorne.ProjTwistingSheaf.irrelevant_le_span_one` (same module): the irrelevant
  ideal lies in the ideal spanned by `𝒜 1`.

`exists_mem_one_notMem` is used directly by the proof of
`Hartshorne.ProjTwistingSheaf.isInvertible` (proj-twisting-sheaf) and by
`Hartshorne/Scheme/GloballyGenerated.lean`; `irrelevant_le_span_one` is used only through
`exists_mem_one_notMem`. Related library API: `AlgebraicGeometry.Proj.affineOpenCoverOfIrrelevantLESpan`
takes the hypothesis `irrelevant_le_span_one` provides. The general
cover `AlgebraicGeometry.Proj.affineOpenCover` (proj-basic-open-cover) is not used by them.
