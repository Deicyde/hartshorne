# The exceptional ideal is invertible

## Status (partial, sorry-free)

Lean module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.BlowupExceptionalIdeal`. Proved:

- Chart algebra, for `S = A[It]` (`reesAlgebra I`, graded by `reesGrading I`), `a ∈ I` and
  `f = a t` (`Hartshorne.reesDegOne`), with `A → S_(f)` written `Hartshorne.reesAwayMap`:
  - `Hartshorne.reesAway_map_ideal_eq_span`: `I · S_(f) = (a)` (for `b ∈ I`, `b = a · (b t)/(a t)`);
  - `Hartshorne.reesAway_algebraMap_mem_nonZeroDivisors`: `a` is a non-zero-divisor of `S_(f)`;
  - `Hartshorne.reesTwistAwayToAway` (`S(1)_(f) → S_(f)`), `reesTwistAwayToAway_injective`,
    `range_reesTwistAwayToAway` (image `I · S_(f)`), `reesTwistAwayToAway_twistGenerator`
    (`f ↦ a`) and `reesTwistAwayToAway_mk` (the chart-free formula `y / s ↦ (y / t) / s`,
    with `y / t` = `Hartshorne.reesShift`, i.e. `Polynomial.divX`). This is the article's
    "Proof depends on" item.
- Charts of a relative Proj (generic in `S : QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X`):
  `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.chartι` (`Spec S(U)_(f) ⟶ Proj_X S`), `chartMap`,
  `chartι_relProjToBase`, `chartSectionsIso`, `inverseImageIdeal_ideal_chart` (over `D₊(f)` the
  inverse image ideal is the image of `J(U)`), `exists_chartι_eq`.
- `Hartshorne.ReesAlgebraSheaf.isLocallyPrincipalNonZeroDivisor_inverseImageIdeal`: for any
  `J : X.IdealSheafData`, `π⁻¹J · 𝒪` on `Proj_X (⨁ Jᵈ)` is locally generated by one
  non-zero-divisor (no Noetherian or coherence hypothesis needed).
- Blowup level (`I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X`, `[IsNoetherian X]`, `hI` coherent):
  `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.exceptionalIdeal` (`inverseImageIdeal π I`, equal to
  `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv (I.inverseImage π)` by
  `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv_inverseImage`), `isLocallyPrincipalNonZeroDivisor_exceptionalIdeal`,
  `exceptionalDivisor` and `exceptionalDivisor_isEffective` (through
  `Scheme.effectiveCartierDivisorEquiv`), `idealSheafData_exceptionalDivisor`,
  `exceptionalIdealSheaf` (`closedImmersionIdealSheaf` of the subscheme), the explicit iso
  `exceptionalIdealSheafIsoDivisorSheaf : I_E ≅ 𝒪(-E)` (Proposition 6.18), and
  `isInvertible_exceptionalIdealSheaf`.

- On `Proj A[It]` (module section `ProjMap`): pointwise division by `t`,
  `Hartshorne.twistDivX` (`S(1)_T → S_T`, `m / s ↦ (m / t) / s`, `twistDivX_mk`; additive,
  `S_T`-linear and injective when `T` meets `S_1`), assembled into the morphism
  `Hartshorne.twistDivXHom : projTwistingSheaf (reesGrading I) 1 ⟶ 𝒪`, a monomorphism
  (`mono_twistDivXHom`). Pointwise, so it needs no gluing over the charts `D₊(a t)`.
- Generic: `Hartshorne.imageIdealSheafIso` (with `toImageIdealSheaf`, `toImageIdealSheaf_ι`).
  A mono `φ : F ⟶ 𝒪_X` from a quasi-coherent `F` gives an explicit

## Next route

The local isomorphism on each piece `Proj S(U)` is in hand up to identifying ideals:
`projTwistingSheafIsoImageIdealSheaf`. Missing steps:

1. **Ideal identification on a piece.** `idealSheafDataOfHom (twistDivXHom I)` equals the inverse
   image of `I` under `Proj A[It] ⟶ Spec A`. For the blowup piece this is
   `(I.exceptionalIdeal hI).comap (projι U)`, since `projι U ≫ π = projToOpen U ≫ U.ι`.
   Use `IdealSheafData.ext_of_iSup_eq_top` with the charts `D₊(a t)`. Over a chart the
   inverse-image side is `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.inverseImageIdeal_ideal_chart`, or its
   `Proj` analogue. The image side needs `Γ(D₊(f), 𝒪(1)) ≅ S(1)_(f)` and
   `Γ(D₊(f), 𝒪) ≅ S_(f)` (`ProjGradedModuleSheaf.toChartΓ` / `chartIso`, Mathlib's
   `Proj.awayToSection`), with `twistDivX` at `powers f`. That map equals
   `reesTwistAwayToAway` by `twistDivX_mk` and `reesTwistAwayToAway_mk`, so its range is
   `I · S_(f)` (`range_reesTwistAwayToAway`).
2. **Restriction of ideal sheaves.** `closedImmersionIdealSheaf K.subschemeι` restricted along an
   open immersion `j` is `closedImmersionIdealSheaf (K.comap j).subschemeι`. Not in the project.
3. **Compatibility with the transitions** `QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra.twistTransitionHom`
   (`ProjTwistMap.twistRestrictHom`, pointwise `twistFiber`). Pointwise `twistFiber` sends
   `m / s ↦ φ(m) / φ(s)`, and `φ` acts on coefficients, so it commutes with `divX`: the check is
   pointwise.
4. **Into the glue.** `relTwistingSheaf` is `DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr.glue`, a subsheaf of
   compatible families in `∏ (projι U)_* 𝒪_U(1)`. Isomorphisms
   `e_U : I_E|_{Proj S(U)} ≅ 𝒪_U(1)` from 1–2, compatible with `θ` by 3, give a morphism
   `I_E ⟶ glue`: its `U`-component is `e_U`, read through restriction to `projι U ''ᵁ (projι U ⁻¹ᵁ W)`.
   This needs no sheaf gluing, since the glue's sections are families. It is an isomorphism by
   `Scheme.Modules.isIso_of_isIso_restrictFunctor_map`, because its restriction followed by
   `DataTr.ψ U` is `e_U`. This is the analogue of `ModuleGluing.θ`, which the project lacks for the
   directed gluing.

Mapping into the glue avoids gluing morphisms out of it, for which the project has no lemma for
modules (only `ModuleGluingDirected.mod_glue` for sections).

A different approach to the local map: `S(1)_(x) → S_(x)` is multiplication by the degree `-1` element
`t⁻¹ = a / (a t)` of `S_x`. It is a global section of `𝒪(-1)` on `Proj A[It]`, so one could use
`𝒪(1) ⊗ 𝒪(-1) ≅ 𝒪(0)`. That needs `𝒪(0) ≅ 𝒪`, which is not in the project; the pointwise
`twistDivX` avoids it.

The optional bridge from `inverseImageIdeal` (`IdealSheafData.comap`) to the literal image of
`f^*I → 𝒪_Y`, which the reviewer of the Rees article flagged, was not needed.
