## How the proof goes

- Chart algebra, for `S = A[It]` (`reesAlgebra I`, graded by `reesGrading I`), `a ∈ I` and
  `f = a t` (`Hartshorne.reesDegOne`), with `A → S_(f)` written `Hartshorne.reesAwayMap`:
  `reesAway_map_ideal_eq_span` (`I · S_(f) = (a)`), `reesAway_algebraMap_mem_nonZeroDivisors`,
  `reesTwistAwayToAway` (`S(1)_(f) → S_(f)`, injective, image `I · S_(f)` by
  `range_reesTwistAwayToAway`, formula `y / s ↦ (y / t) / s` by `reesTwistAwayToAway_mk`). This is
  the article's "Proof depends on" item.
- Invertibility and `E`: `ReesAlgebraSheaf.isLocallyPrincipalNonZeroDivisor_inverseImageIdeal`
  (over the charts `D₊(a t)` the inverse image ideal is generated by the non-zero-divisor `a`),
  then `Scheme.effectiveCartierDivisorEquiv` and `idealSheafIso` (Proposition 6.18) give `E` and
  `I_E ≅ 𝒪(-E)`.
- `I_E ≅ 𝒪(1)`:
  1. On `Proj A[It]`, pointwise division by `t`: `twistDivX`, assembled into the monomorphism
     `twistDivXHom : 𝒪(1) ⟶ 𝒪`.
  2. Its image on a chart `D₊(a t)` is the ideal generated by `I`
     (`homRangeIdeal_twistDivXHom_chart`, via the chart sections `ProjGradedModuleSheaf.toChartΓ`
     and `awayι_appIso_inv_top`), so its image ideal sheaf is the inverse image of `I`
     (`ReesAlgebraSheaf.idealSheafDataOfHom_pieceDivX`, by `IdealSheafData.ext_of_iSup_eq_top`).
  3. Generic module facts: the image ideal of the ideal sheaf inclusion of `K` is `K`
     (`idealSheafDataOfHom_closedImmersionIdealSheafι`); restricting a map into `𝒪` along an open
     immersion (`restrictToUnit`) pulls the image ideal back (`idealSheafDataOfHom_restrictToUnit`);
     two monomorphisms into `𝒪` with the same image ideal have isomorphic sources
     (`isoOfIdealSheafDataOfHomEq`, built on the Proposition 6.18 kernel-lift `imageIdealSheafIso`).
     Together: `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.exceptionalIdealSheafRestrictIso`, `I_E|_{Proj A[It]} ≅
     𝒪(1)`, compatible with the inclusions into `𝒪`.
  4. Division by `t` commutes with the transition maps (`twistRestrictHom_comp_twistDivXHom`;
     the graded maps act on coefficients, so they commute with `divX`, `reesShift_reesMap`), so the
     piece isomorphisms are compatible (`DirectedModuleGluing.Data.compatibleIsos_of_comp_unit`,
     `DirectedModuleGluing.DataTr.compatibleIsos_of_comp_unit`)
     and glue (`DirectedModuleGluing.Data.liftIso`) to `exceptionalIdealSheafIsoTwistingSheaf`.
- To avoid expensive unification between `I.blowup hI` and `(I.blowupData hI).algebra.relProj`
  (and between `reesGrading (idealOn J U)` and `(reesAlgebraSheaf J).grading U`), the gluing step
  works with `exceptionalIdealRel` / `exceptionalIdealSheafRel` (the same objects typed over
  `relProj`) and `pieceDivX` (division by `t` typed over the piece's grading).
  `homRangeIdeal_twistDivXHom_chart` is given `maxHeartbeats 800000` and the piece-level ideal
  lemmas and the compatibility theorem `400000`: the elaborator spends most of that time on
  unifying the sections of `𝒪(1)` over a chart with the chart sections of the Proj module sheaf.
  Stating lemmas over the cover's index category, or with `respectTransparency false` and a
  closing `rw`, produced kernel deterministic timeouts; the final proofs avoid both.

## Friction

- Rewriting in `X.Modules` with morphisms into `SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf` often fails the
  motive check at implicit transparency (`TopCat.Sheaf` vs `Sheaf`); the proofs use
  `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false in` locally.
- `toChartΓ_bijective` lives in `ProjectiveSpaceSectionRing`, which this module does not import;
  the surjectivity is reproved privately from `awayModuleIsoChartSections`.
