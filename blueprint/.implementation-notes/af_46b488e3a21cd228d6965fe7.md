# Clearing denominators with an invertible section

## Status

Blocked before stating. No Lean has been written for this leaf yet.

## Blockers

1. **Missing prerequisite (dependency edge).** You cannot even state the
   statement without the sheaf tensor product. The expression $f^n s$ lives in
   $\Gamma(X, F \otimes L^{\otimes n})$, and the existence clause extends
   $f^n t$ to a global section of $F \otimes L^{\otimes n}$. The project's
   tensor product of module sheaves is owned by
   `schemes/modules-and-quasicoherent/module-tensor-product`. The
   Depends on / Proof depends on lists of this article do not reach that
   article, even transitively. The transitive closure contains only
   locally-free-and-invertible, quasicoherent-local-presentations,
   quasicoherent-principal-open-localization, tilde-localization,
   sheaves-of-modules, separated-affine-intersection,
   noetherian-space-open-compact and the scheme basics below them.
   The article needs at least a Depends on edge to module-tensor-product.

2. **No tensor powers or nonvanishing locus.** Neither the project nor the
   pinned Mathlib has:
   - tensor powers $L^{\otimes n}$ of a module sheaf. The roadmap article
     `schemes/module-exercises/sheaf-tensor-operations` (tensor algebra,
     degree-$n$ pieces) would provide them, but it is unformalized. So is the
     Picard-group article `schemes/divisors/cartier-picard/invertible-sheaf-group`;
   - the multiplication map $F \otimes L^{\otimes n} \to F \otimes L^{\otimes (n+1)}$,
     $s \mapsto s \otimes f$, for a global section $f$ of $L$;
   - the nonvanishing locus $X_f$ of a section of an invertible sheaf, i.e.
     the points where the germ of $f$ generates the stalk, and its openness.
   Mathlib has the presheaf-level monoidal structure on presheaves of modules,
   but no monoidal structure on sheaves of modules.

3. **Local computations missing.** The proof restricts to a trivializing
   affine open $U = \operatorname{Spec} A$, where $L|_U \cong \mathcal{O}_U$,
   $F|_U \cong \widetilde{M}$, $f \leftrightarrow g \in A$ and
   $X_f \cap U = D(g)$. It then uses
   $\Gamma(D(g), \widetilde{M}) = M_g$. This needs the following facts about
   the project's sheaf tensor product. None of them exists yet:
   - compatibility with restriction to open subschemes:
     $(F \otimes G)|_U \cong F|_U \otimes G|_U$. Sheafification has to commute
     with restriction;
   - the unit isomorphism $F \otimes \mathcal{O}_X \cong F$, and invariance
     under isomorphism in each variable;
   - compatibility of these isomorphisms with the multiplication-by-$f$ maps,
     so that, after trivializing, multiplication by $f^n$ becomes
     multiplication by $g^n$ on $M$.
   The principal-open localization ($\Gamma(D(g),\widetilde M) = M_g$) and the
   separated affine-intersection inputs are available through Mathlib
   (`AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_fromTildeΓ_iff_isLocalizing`,
   `AlgebraicGeometry.IsAffineOpen.inf`).

## Suggested route

- Add the dependency on module-tensor-product. Add a new definition leaf,
  under projective-sheaves or modules-and-quasicoherent, that gives tensor
  powers of an invertible sheaf, the section-multiplication maps and the
  nonvanishing locus $X_f$ together with its openness. Alternatively, depend
  on the tensor-power part of sheaf-tensor-operations once it is formalized.
- Add a lemma leaf for the restriction and unit compatibilities of the sheaf
  tensor product listed above.
- With those in place, the lemma itself follows the textbook proof. On each
  affine of a finite trivializing cover, the uniqueness clause is the
  localization kernel statement for $M \to M_g$, with a common exponent taken
  over the finite cover. The existence clause lifts $t$ on each
  $D(g_i)$ after multiplication by $g_i^{n}$. The local lifts are then glued,
  using the uniqueness clause on the quasi-compact overlaps $U_i \cap U_j$ to
  kill the discrepancies after a further power of $f$.
