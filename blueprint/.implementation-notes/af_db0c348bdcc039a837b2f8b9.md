# Completion and ordinary sheaf tensor product

## Status

Complete, candidate pending review. Module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.SchemeCompletionTensor`.

## Lean

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.isIso_pullbackToModuleCompletion`: for `X` locally
  Noetherian, `I : QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` and `F : X.Modules` with `IsCoherent F`, the
  canonical map `I.pullbackToModuleCompletion F : f^* F ⟶ I.moduleCompletion F` is an
  isomorphism.
- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.pullbackIsoModuleCompletion`: the isomorphism
  `(SheafOfModules.pullback I.structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat).obj F ≅
  I.moduleCompletion F`, defined as `asIso` of the canonical map.

The tensor product `F|_{|Y|} ⊗_{𝒪_X|_{|Y|}} 𝒪_X̂` is modelled, as on affine-formal-module-tensor
(review r111, F4), by the module pullback along `f : (|Y|, 𝒪_X̂) ⟶ (X, 𝒪_X)` given by the closed
embedding `i : |Y| ⟶ X` and `𝒪_X ⟶ i_* 𝒪_X̂` (`structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat`);
Mathlib's `SheafOfModules.pullback` is `i⁻¹ F ⊗_{i⁻¹ 𝒪_X} 𝒪_X̂`. The isomorphism is the canonical
map `pullbackToModuleCompletion` (adjoint of `toModuleCompletionPushforward : F ⟶ i_* F̂`).

All helpers are private.

## Route

An explicit inverse `F̂ ⟶ f^* F` is built on the basis of `|Y|` formed by the traces
`U ∩ |Y|` of the affine opens `U` of `X` (`isBasis_affineTrace`, from `opens_isBasis_map_image`).

1. Sections (`toAdic`): for `U` affine and `F` quasi-coherent, a section `x` of `F̂` over
   `U ∩ |Y|` is sent to the class of the Cauchy sequence of lifts (`Function.surjInv` of
   `moduleCompletionSectionsMap_surjective`) of its projections to the levels, in
   `AdicCompletion (I.ideal U) Γ(F, U)`. It is additive, injective
   (`moduleCompletionSectionsMap_eq_zero_iff` and `sections_isLimit_mapCone_ext`), sends the
   image of `s ∈ Γ(F, U)` to `AdicCompletion.of s`, and is semilinear over
   `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso` (`toAdic_smul`; the action on the level `n` factors
   through `formalCompletionRingCatSheafπ n`).
2. Inverse on a trace (`completionToPullback`, needs `Γ(X, U)` Noetherian and `Γ(F, U)` finite):
   `toAdic`, then the inverse of `AdicCompletion.ofTensorProductEquivOfFiniteNoetherian`, then
   `b ⊗ s ↦ b · η(s)` (`tensorToPullback`, via `TensorProduct.liftAddHom`; `η` the unit
   `F ⟶ f_* f^* F`). It is `Γ(U ∩ |Y|, 𝒪_X̂)`-linear, sends the image of `s` to `η(s)`, and is a
   right inverse of the canonical map on sections (`θ ∘ η = ψ` from the adjunction, then
   injectivity of `toAdic`). Every section over the trace is `θ(κ t)` for a tensor `t`, which
   gives compatibility with restriction between nested affine opens by tensor induction.
3. Gluing (`sigmaApp`, `sigmaBasis`, `sigmaAb`, `completionToPullbackHom`): compatibility for an
   arbitrary inclusion of traces `U' ∩ |Y| ⊆ U ∩ |Y|` (affine opens not necessarily nested) is
   checked on a cover by traces of affine opens inside `U ∩ U'`. The family is then a morphism of
   presheaves on the induced basis category and extends by `TopCat.Sheaf.restrictHomEquivHom` to
   a morphism of abelian sheaves; linearity over every open is checked locally on the basis, and
   `PresheafOfModules.homMk` makes it a morphism of `𝒪_X̂`-modules.
4. Inverse: `σ ≫ θ = 𝟙` on sections over the basis; `θ ≫ σ = 𝟙` by injectivity of the adjunction
   `homEquiv`, since `ψ ≫ f_* σ = η` (checked on sections over affine opens of `X`).

## Divergence from the printed proof

Hartshorne derives the statement from Proposition 9.4(b) and locality on an affine cover. The
recorded affine result (`isIso_tildePullbackToModuleCompletion`) is stated on `Spf_I(A)` for a
ring `A`; quoting it on an affine open `U` of `X` would need (i) the pullback `f^*` to commute
with restriction to `U ∩ |Y|` (conjugate of the pushforward composition), (ii) the formal
completion of the open subscheme `U` to be identified with the open subspace of `X̂`, together
with `(F|_U)^ ≅ F̂|_{U ∩ |Y|}` compatibly with the canonical maps, and (iii) transport along
`U ≅ Spec A`. None of these exist yet. The present proof uses the same algebraic input as 9.4(b),
`Â ⊗_A M ≅ M̂` for finite `M` over Noetherian `A` (Mathlib), directly on the sections over the
affine traces, and does the local-to-global step by gluing the inverse. The Lean uses
`pullbackToModuleCompletion` from affine-formal-module-tensor but not its affine isomorphism.

## Generality

`X` is assumed locally Noetherian (Hartshorne: Noetherian); `F` coherent is used through
`IsCoherent.isQuasicoherent` and `forall_affine_finite_of_isCoherent`.
