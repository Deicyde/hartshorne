# Rees algebras and blowups

## Prior implementation guidance

Keep the module pullback `f^*I` distinct from the inverse-image ideal, which is
the image of `f^*I -> O` and is represented by `IdealSheafData.comap`.

## Lean (module `Hartshorne.Scheme.ReesAlgebraBlowup`)

- Affine algebra. `reesGrading I n` is the degree-`n` part of Mathlib's
  `reesAlgebra I ⊆ R[t]` (elements that are monomials of degree `n`), with
  `reesGrading_gradedRing` built from `DirectSum.Decomposition.ofAddHom`
  (decomposition by coefficients). `reesMap φ h` acts on coefficients;
  `reesMap_isLocalizationAway`: if `S = R[1/r]` and `J = I S` then
  `S[Jt] = R[It][1/r]` (checked degree by degree via
  `IsLocalization.mem_map_algebraMap_iff` and `Ideal.map_pow`).
- `reesAlgebraSheaf J : QuasiCoherentGradedAlgebra X` for `J : X.IdealSheafData`
  (sections over affine `U` are `reesAlgebra (J.ideal U)`; restriction along
  basic opens is `reesMap` of the presheaf map; quasi-coherence is
  `coequifibered_iff_forall_isLocalizationAway` plus
  `reesMap_isLocalizationAway`). `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.reesAlgebra I` goes
  through `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv`; `mem_reesAlgebra_grading_iff`
  describes the degree-`d` sections as `a t^d`, `a ∈ Γ(U, I)^d`.
- Hartshorne's relative-Proj hypotheses: `ReesAlgebraSheaf.degreeZeroIsStructureSheaf`,
  `degreeOneCoherent` (from finitely generated `J(U)`, using generators
  `g_i t`), `generatedInDegreeOne` (from Mathlib's
  `adjoin_monomial_eq_reesAlgebra`).
- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.blowupData [IsNoetherian X] I (hI : IsCoherent I.toModules)`
  is the `RelativeProjData`; coherence gives finitely generated `Γ(U, I)` via
  `forall_affine_finite_of_isCoherent` and `Module.Finite.iff_fg`. `blowup`
  and `blowupProjection` are its `relativeProj` and `projection`;
  `blowupRestrictIso_hom_projToOpen` is the affine comparison.
- Inverse-image ideal: `inverseImageIdeal f I := I.comap f`, with
  `inverseImageIdeal_le_iff`: it is the least ideal sheaf `K` on `Y` with
  `f^♯(I(V)) Γ(U) ⊆ K(U)` for all affine `V ⊆ X`, `U ⊆ f⁻¹V` (Galois connection
  `le_map_iff_comap_le` plus `le_map_iff`, which unfolds `Hom.ker` as
  `ofIdeals` and uses `subschemeι_app_eq_zero_iff`, a germ argument). The
  `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.inverseImage` / `inverseImage_le_iff` versions
  go through the equivalence. The module pullback `f^*I` is not used.

## Possible follow-ups

- The exact affine formula `(I.comap f).ideal U = (I.ideal V).map (f.appLE V U h)`
  is not proved (only `≥`, `map_ideal_le_inverseImageIdeal`); the route would
  reduce along open immersions (`ideal_comap_of_isOpenImmersion`, `comap_comp`)
  to `Spec A ⟶ Spec B` and compute the kernel of `Spec (A/IA) ⟶ Spec A`.
