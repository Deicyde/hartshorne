# Sheaf cohomology as Ext from the constant sheaf

## Scope

Project bridge (not a Hartshorne result) between Hartshorne's
`Hⁿ(X,-) = RⁿΓ(X,-)` (article `sheaf-cohomology`,
af_a3a60a624675778f44a80899) and Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H F n`
(`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Basic.lean`), which is
`Ext (constantSheaf J AddCommGrpCat).obj (AddCommGrpCat.of (ULift ℤ)) F n`.
Its source analogue is Hartshorne III.6.3(c) for the ringed space `(X, ℤ_X)`,
but Mathlib's Ext is derived-category Ext, not Hartshorne's derived functors
of `Hom`, so the content is the injective-resolution computation plus
compatibility with connecting maps. Split out of the old "Sheaf cohomology and
the Ext model" article by the roadmap revision.

Consumers that genuinely need it: `open-object-cohomology` (Mathlib's `Sheaf.H'`), plus anyone wanting
Mathlib's `Sheaf.H` API, e.g. the Mayer–Vietoris sequence in
`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/MayerVietoris.lean`.

## Universe

For `X : TopCat.{u}`, `Opens X` is a small category in `u`, so
`Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) AddCommGrpCat.{u}` is
`IsGrothendieckAbelian.{u}` (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/GrothendieckAxioms/Sheaf.lean`,
also `Mathlib/Topology/Sheaves/Abelian.lean`), hence
`IsGrothendieckAbelian.hasExt` gives `HasExt.{u}` by instance and
`Sheaf.H F n : Type u`. No `HasExt` hypothesis and no universe bump. The
universe parameter `w'` of `Sheaf.functorH` unifies to `u` from the target
type. `TopCat.Sheaf C X` is a plain `def` for
`Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) C`; if stating the isomorphism between
functors out of `TopCat.Sheaf` and `Sheaf (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) _`
does not elaborate directly, state it on the latter (same category instance).

## Candidate declarations

- `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH (X : TopCat.{u}) (n : ℕ) :
  sheafCohomology X n ≅ Sheaf.functorH (Opens.grothendieckTopology X) n`
  (the main result, a `def`).
- `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH_zero`: for `x`, 
  `Sheaf.H.equiv₀ F Limits.isTerminalTop ((sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH X 0).hom.app F x)
   = (sheafCohomologyZeroIso X).hom.app F x`.
  Check that `H.equiv₀` (built from `Ext.addEquiv₀`, `constantSheafAdj` and
  `uliftZMultiplesAddEquiv`) is "evaluate at the section 1"; the statement
  describes the degree-zero map that way.
- `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH_δ`: for `hS : S.ShortExact`,
  `rightDerivedδ (sheafSectionsOver ⊤) hS n ≫ (sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH X (n+1)).hom.app S.X₁
   = (sheafCohomologyIsoFunctorH X n).hom.app S.X₃ ≫
     AddCommGrpCat.ofHom (hS.extClass.postcomp _ rfl)`
  (`ShortComplex.ShortExact.extClass`, `Ext.postcomp`; this is the connecting
  map of `Ext.covariantSequence`,
  `Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/Ext/ExactSequences.lean`).

## Construction route (direct, no delta-functor framework)

1. `Hom(ℤ_X, -) ≅ Γ(X,-)` as functors to `AddCommGrpCat.{u}`: compose
   `constantSheafAdj J AddCommGrpCat Limits.isTerminalTop` (homEquiv) with
   `Hom(ULift ℤ, A) ≃+ A` (`uliftZMultiplesAddEquiv`), as in `Sheaf.H.equiv₀`.
2. For the chosen resolution `I := injectiveResolution F`:
   `(sheafCohomology X n).obj F ≅ H^n(Γ(X, I^•))` by
   `InjectiveResolution.isoRightDerivedObj`/`rightDerived_app_eq`; termwise
   step 1 identifies `Γ(X,I^•)` with `Hom(ℤ_X, I^•)`.
3. `H^n(Hom(ℤ_X, I^•)) ≃+ Ext ℤ_X F n` via `InjectiveResolution.extMk`
   (well defined and injective by `extMk_eq_zero_iff`, surjective by
   `extMk_surjective`, additive by `add_extMk`), or via
   `InjectiveResolution.extAddEquivCohomologyClass`
   (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/Injective/Ext.lean`). The project's
   `Hartshorne.extMk_aug` (`GlobalToSheafExt.lean`) already
   handles the degree-zero `extMk`/`Ext.mk₀` comparison.
4. Naturality in `F`: `InjectiveResolution.extMk_comp_mk₀` with a morphism of
   resolutions, against `H.map f n = x.comp (Ext.mk₀ f)` (`Sheaf.H.map_apply`).
5. Connecting maps: the hard step. `Hartshorne.rightDerivedδ` is built on a
   horseshoe resolution with differential twisted by a cochain map
   `I₃ ⟶ I₁[1]` (`RightDerivedLongExactSequence.lean`). Show that
   `hS.extClass` composed with `extMk` of a cocycle `ℤ_X ⟶ I₃ⁿ` is `extMk` of
   that cocycle followed by the twisting map, then compare with the homology
   connecting map. Mathlib has no lemma describing `extClass` through an
   injective resolution; that lemma (general abelian category) is the
   expected main work and could be its own declaration.

Sign: if the square in step 5 commutes only up to a sign `s_n` depending only
on `n`, rescale the degree-`n` isomorphism by `t_n` with `t_0 = 1`,
`t_{n+1} = s_n t_n`; the article's statement (iso of delta functors extending
the degree-zero one) is unaffected, and the rescaled comparison is the unique
such iso by universality (not part of this article).

Alternative route: universality of effaceable delta functors
(`effaceable-delta-functor-universal`, currently not ready because the
project has no Lean representation of delta functors). The direct route avoids
it.
