# Chevalley's affine descent theorem

## Lean

Module `Hartshorne.Cohomology.ChevalleyAffineDescent`; recorded statement
`Hartshorne.isAffine_of_isFinite_of_surjective`:
`[IsFinite f] [Surjective f] [IsNoetherian Y] [Y.IsSeparated] [IsAffine X] ⊢ IsAffine Y`.

The article's hypotheses that `X` is Noetherian and separated are not assumed: `X` is affine,
hence separated, and finite over the Noetherian `Y`, hence Noetherian
(`ChevalleyDescent.isNoetherian_of_isFinite`). Separatedness of `Y` is used by the affine
pushforward comparison `Hartshorne.sheafCohomologyIsoPushforwardOfIsAffineHom` (III, Ex. 4.1).

## Route (the book's)

- `ChevalleyDescent.isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme`: well-founded induction on `Closeds Y`
  (Noetherian space). For closed `W`, base change `f` along the reduced induced subscheme
  `W_red → Y` (`pullback.snd`; source affine since `pullback.fst` is a closed immersion into the
  affine `X`). A proper closed subset `A` of `W_red` is the preimage of its image `A' < W`, and
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.isAffine_reducedInducedSubscheme_preimageCloseds` identifies the
  reduced structures.
- `ChevalleyDescent.isAffine_of_isReduced`: III, Ex. 3.2
  (`Hartshorne.isAffine_iff_forall_irreducibleComponents_isAffine`); a component equal to `Y`
  makes `Y` integral.
- `ChevalleyDescent.isAffine_of_isIntegral`: Serre's criterion
  (`Hartshorne.isAffine_tfae_sheafCohomology`, (iii) ⇒ (i)), the frame
  `Hartshorne.exists_genericFiniteModuleFrame` and approximation
  `Hartshorne.exists_genericCoherentApproximation`, the short exact sequences
  `kernel (factorThruImage β) → f_* G → image β` and `image β → ⨁ J → cokernel β`, and a retract
  argument for `H^1(J) ⊆ H^1(J^r)`. Supports of kernel and cokernel avoid the generic point
  (`not_mem_support_of_shortExact`, `not_mem_support_of_epi`).
- `ChevalleyDescent.isZero_H_of_support_le`: `I_W^m K = 0`
  (`exists_isZero_powSmul`, using II.5.3 via `exists_pow_smul_eq_zero_of_isAffineOpen` and
  `exists_pow_smul_extension_of_isAffineOpen`, and `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.sub_powSmul_eq`), then
  the filtration by `ArtinReesKernelSheaf.powSmul I k ⊤`, whose quotients descend to `W_red` by
  `ReducedComponentsAffine.ClosedDescent.exists_isQuasicoherent_iso_pushforward` and have vanishing
  higher cohomology by `ReducedComponentsAffine.isZero_H_pushforward`.
- Final step: `W = ⊤`, `vanishingIdeal ⊤ = nilradical`, and III, Ex. 3.1
  (`Hartshorne.ReductionAffine.isAffine_iff_isAffine_reduced`).

The biproduct `⨁` uses the local instance `HasFiniteBiproducts.of_hasFiniteProducts`, as in the
approximation module, so that the approximation's target matches.
