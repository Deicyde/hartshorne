### Built (sorry-free, general scheme `X`, quasi-coherent ideal subsheaf `I`, `F : X.Modules`)

Write `i : |Y| ⟶ X` for the support inclusion, the underlying map of
`I.infinitesimalNeighborhoodι 1`.

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.restrictedStructureSheaf`: `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X` on `|Y|`, as
  Mathlib's `Functor.sheafPullback` of `X.sheaf` along `Opens.map i` (CommRingCat sheaves).
- `restrictedStructureSheafUnit`: the unit `𝒪_X ⟶ i_* i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`.
- `structureSheafToFormalCompletion`: `𝒪_X ⟶ i_* 𝒪_X̂`, sections `formalCompletionSectionsMap`.
- `restrictedStructureSheafToFormalCompletion`: its adjoint `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X ⟶ 𝒪_X̂`;
  `restrictedStructureSheafUnit_comp_toFormalCompletion` is the triangle identity.
- RingCat versions `restrictedStructureSheafUnitRingCat`, `structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat`,
  `restrictedStructureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat`, with `IsRightAdjoint` instances for the
  module pushforwards (via `PresheafOfModules.pullbackObjIsDefined_eq_top` and
  `SheafOfModules.PullbackConstruction.adjunction`; `inferInstance` does not find them).
- `restrictedModule F`: `F|_{|Y|}` as the module pullback along `restrictedStructureSheafUnitRingCat`
  (left adjoint of `i_*`), a module over `ringCatSheafOf restrictedStructureSheaf`.
- `formalCompletionRestrictedModule`: `𝒪_X̂` restricted to `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X` (`SheafOfModules.restrictScalars`).
- `restrictedModuleTensorFormalCompletion F`: `moduleSheafTensor (restrictedModule F)
  formalCompletionRestrictedModule`, the article's right-hand side as an `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`-module.
- `moduleCompletionRestrictedModule F`: `moduleCompletion I F` restricted to `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`.

Private helpers (proved) toward the comparison map `F ⟶ i_* F̂` (pushforward along
`structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat`):

- components `toModuleCompletionObjPushforward F n`: cokernel projection, unit of
  `Scheme.Modules.pullbackPushforwardAdjunction (ι (n+1))`, then `neighborhoodRestrict`
  (restriction of sections along `functor_obj_preimage_infinitesimalNeighborhoodι`);
- `unit_comp_pushforward_transition` (generic in schemes `s : Z ⟶ Y'`, `a : Y' ⟶ W`,
  `s ≫ a = b`): the unit along `a` followed by `a_*` of
  `a^* q ≫ unit_s ≫ s_*(pullbackComp.hom ≫ pullbackCongr.hom)` equals
  `q ≫ unit_b ≫ pushforwardCongr.inv ≫ pushforwardComp.inv`; this is the adjunction content of
  compatibility with `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap`;
- `neighborhoodRestrict_comp_transition` and `pushforwardCongr_comp_succRestrict`: the
  section-level identities saying that `neighborhoodRestrict (n+1)` followed by
  `moduleCompletionMap`'s shape equals `(ι (n+2))_* g` followed by `succRestrict n`, and that
  `pushforwardCongr.inv ≫ pushforwardComp.inv ≫ succRestrict n = neighborhoodRestrict n`.
  Both are instances of abstract lemmas over arbitrary sites
  (`pushforwardRestrictToComp_comp_transition`, `comp_pushforwardPushforwardRestrict`).

## Remaining goal

1. Compatibility of the components with the transition maps:
   `toModuleCompletionObjPushforward F (n+1) ≫ pushforward.map (moduleCompletionMap F n) =
   toModuleCompletionObjPushforward F n`. With the helpers above it only needs
   `unit_comp_pushforward_transition` instantiated at `g := moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap F n`,
   then `π_moduleCompletionQuotientMap`. Obstacle: identifying
   `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap F n` with its defining composite times out in the
   elaborator (`rfl`, `rw [moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap]`, `unfold` then `exact`), and
   `unfold` then `congr` passes the elaborator but times out in the kernel. The definition
   mixes `n + 1 + 1` (its source `moduleCompletionNeighborhood F (n + 1)`) and `n + 2` (inside the
   body), and the instantiated lemma lives in `(Y (n+2)).Modules`. The variant
   `unit_comp_pushforward_transition'` (arbitrary source `N'`, `p : N' ⟶ a^* Q`, and `v` with
   `v ≫ a_* p = q ≫ unit_a`) is proved. Its instance at `N' := moduleCompletionNeighborhood F
   (n + 1)`, `a := ι (n + 2)` and `v :=` the unit along `ι (n + 1 + 1)` elaborates, but `exact`
   against the stated goal still times out. The unifier ends up comparing the functors
   `pullback ⋙ pushforward` and `pushforward` inside `Functor.obj`, which unfolds the pullback
   functor. Next route: add an unfolding lemma `moduleCompletionNeighborhoodMap_eq` next to the
   definition in `FormalCompletionModule.lean`, stated exactly in the body's forms. That edits an
   existing module and needs lead approval. Alternatively, restate the components so that the
   object between the unit and the restriction is written as `(pushforward a).obj N'`, never as
   `(pullback ⋙ pushforward).obj Q'`.
2. Cone and lift: `pushforward` preserves the limit (it is a right adjoint, instance above), so
   the compatible components give `F ⟶ i_* F̂`. Turn this into `restrictedModule F ⟶
   moduleCompletionRestrictedModule F` through the pullback adjunction for
   `restrictedStructureSheafUnitRingCat`. This needs a sections-level identification of
   `pushforward (unit) ∘ restrictScalars` with `pushforward (structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat)`
   (equal sections; the scalar actions agree by
   `restrictedStructureSheafUnit_comp_toFormalCompletion`).
3. Bilinear map `(m, a) ↦ a • α(m)` into `moduleCompletionRestrictedModule F`, using the
   `𝒪_X̂`-action on `F̂`; then `moduleSheafTensorHomEquiv` gives the canonical map
   `restrictedModuleTensorFormalCompletion F ⟶ moduleCompletionRestrictedModule F`.
4. Isomorphism for `X = Spec A`, `I = specIdealSubsheaf A I`, `F = M~`, `A` Noetherian, `M`
   finite. Check on basic opens `D(f) ∩ V(I)` or on stalks: the sections of `F̂` over
   `D(f) ∩ V(I)` are `lim (M / I^{n+1} M)_f`, using `Hartshorne.tildePullbackIso` for the pullback
   along `Spec (A / I^{n+1}) ⟶ Spec A` and `isLimitModuleCompletionSections`. Compare with
   `AdicCompletion.ofTensorProductEquivOfFiniteNoetherian` for the finite `A_f`-module `M_f`.
   The tensor side is a sheafification, so compare stalks with `moduleSheafTensorStalkIso`, or
   compare the presheaf map on a basis.
5. Statement scope: the iso above is of `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`-modules. The article's iso is of
   `𝒪_X̂`-modules, which also needs the `𝒪_X̂`-module structure on the tensor sheaf (through the
   right factor). That structure is not built; it is not in the project or Mathlib.

## Friction

- `inferInstance` does not find `(SheafOfModules.pushforward φ).IsRightAdjoint` for maps along
  `Opens.map` of a scheme morphism when `φ` is built by hand; construct it explicitly.
- Element-level `change` on composites of pushforwards along concrete scheme morphisms times out.
  State such identities over abstract sites and functors, then instantiate.
