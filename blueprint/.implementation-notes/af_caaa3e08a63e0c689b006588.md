## How the statement is modelled

`f : (|Y|, 𝒪_X̂) ⟶ (X, 𝒪_X)` is the morphism of ringed spaces given by the support inclusion
`i` and `structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat : 𝒪_X ⟶ i_* 𝒪_X̂`. The article's
`M~|_{V(I)} ⊗_{𝒪|_{V(I)}} 𝒪_X̂` is the module pullback `f^* (M~)`, Mathlib's
`SheafOfModules.pullback` along that ring map. The project records Mathlib's pullback as
`f^{-1} G ⊗_{f^{-1} 𝒪_Y} 𝒪_X` on the article `module-push-pull-adjunction`, which is Hartshorne's
definition (II.5). The isomorphism is one of `𝒪_X̂`-modules.

The file also keeps the earlier explicit form `restrictedModuleTensorFormalCompletion F`
(`moduleSheafTensor` of `i⁻¹ F` and `𝒪_X̂` over `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`, an `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X`-module). It is not
used. Identifying it with `restrictScalars (f^* F)` is the general base-change fact
`f^* = h^* ∘ g^*` with `h^* N = N ⊗_R S` (for `g : (|Y|, i⁻¹ 𝒪_X) ⟶ X` and `h` the identity of
`|Y|` with `i⁻¹ 𝒪_X ⟶ 𝒪_X̂`). Neither the project nor Mathlib has it.

## Proof route (differs from the article's sketch)

The article's sketch compares sections on an affine basis, using `M_g ⊗ Â_g = (M_g)^`
(finite-module-completion-tensor). Sections of `f^* (M~)` over `D(g) ∩ V(I)` are sections of a
sheafified tensor product over a sheafified inverse image, so they cannot be computed directly.
The Lean proof instead runs the proof of Atiyah–Macdonald 10.13 at the sheaf level:

1. `toModuleCompletionPushforward F : F ⟶ i_* F̂` is the limit of `moduleCompletionLevelMap`
   (from `FormalCompletionModuleAffineSections`, with `χ := structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat`
   and compatibility `structureSheafToFormalCompletionRingCat_compat`). `pullbackToModuleCompletion`
   is its adjoint. Both are natural in `F`.
2. Rank one (`isIso_pullbackToModuleCompletion_unit`, for any `X` and `I`): `f^* 𝒪_X ≅ 𝒪_X̂`
   because `Opens.map i` is final (Mathlib `pullbackObjUnitToUnit`). The induced map
   `𝒪_X̂ ⟶ (𝒪_X)^` is bijective on sections over `U ∩ |Y|` for every affine `U`. This uses
   `formalCompletionAffineSectionsIso` (sections of `𝒪_X̂` are the `I(U)`-adic completion),
   surjectivity and kernel of `moduleCompletionSectionsMap`, and the basis exactness criterion
   `sheafOfModules_exact_of_isBasis_pushforward_sections` from 9.4(c).
3. Algebra (`isIso_app_of_finite_of_isIso_app_self`): let `c : Φ₁ ⟶ Φ₂` be a natural
   transformation of functors from `A`-modules to an abelian category, `A` Noetherian. Suppose
   `Φ₁` is right exact, `Φ₂` sends short exact sequences of finite modules to short exact
   sequences, and `c` is an isomorphism at `A`. Then `c` is an isomorphism at every finite
   module. The proof: split sequences `A ^ n ⟶ A ^ (n+1) ⟶ A`, then epi from a presentation, then
   mono by a four lemma (`mono_τ₃_of_exact_of_epi`, private).
4. Apply step 3 with `Φ₁ = tilde ⋙ f^*` (a left adjoint), `Φ₂ = tilde ⋙ completion` (exact by
   9.4(c), `moduleCompletion_tilde_shortExact`), and `tilde A = 𝒪_{Spec A}` (Mathlib `tildeSelf`
   is `rfl`).

## Friction

- `inferInstance` does not find `IsIso (pullbackObjUnitToUnit χ)` from the `Final` instance, or
  `(SheafOfModules.pullback χ).IsLeftAdjoint`, when `χ` is built by hand. Supply them
  explicitly: the instance term with `@`, or `Adjunction.isLeftAdjoint` of
  `tilde.adjunction.comp (pullbackPushforwardAdjunction χ)`.
- Elements of the pushforward of a module and of the unit module have types that are only
  definitionally equal to the ring or the transported module. Pass such elements through `show`.
  Make the module argument of `moduleCompletionLevelMap` explicit, because leaving it as `_`
  hits `whnf` timeouts. Use generic lemmas (`pushforward_smul_eq`, `pushforward_smul_congr`)
  to move scalars through ring maps.
- `rw` on goals about modules over `X.ringCatSheaf` often fails (the goal is not type-correct
  at instance transparency). Write the proofs as terms with `congrArg` and `.trans`.
