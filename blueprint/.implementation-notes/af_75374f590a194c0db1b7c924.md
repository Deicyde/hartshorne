# Dualizing sheaves

## Lean

- `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf f n hdim` (in `Hartshorne.Cohomology.DualizingSheaf`): for
  `f : X ⟶ Spec (CommRingCat.of k)` with `[IsProper f]` and `hdim : topologicalKrullDim X = n`,
  a structure with fields `sheaf : X.Modules`, `isCoherent : IsCoherent sheaf`,
  `trace : (cohomologyOverField f n).obj sheaf →ₗ[k] k`, and `bijective_dualityPairingMap`:
  for every coherent `F`, `dualityPairingMap f n trace F` is bijective. The dimension of `X` is
  the topological Krull dimension, as elsewhere in the project. `IsProper f` and `hdim` are
  parameters of the structure (Hartshorne's standing hypotheses); no field uses them.
- `Hartshorne.dualityPairingMap f n t F : homOverField f F ω →ₗ[k] Module.Dual k (Hⁿ(X, F))`,
  `φ ↦ t ∘ₗ Hⁿ(φ)`, the map induced by the pairing; its `k`-linearity is proved, and
  `Hartshorne.dualityPairingMap_naturality` gives naturality in `F`. Since the map is defined
  explicitly, requiring bijectivity encodes "natural linear equivalence";
  `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf.dualityEquiv` is the resulting `LinearEquiv` and
  `Hartshorne.DualizingSheaf.dualityEquiv_naturality` its naturality.

## The `k`-linear structures (built here)

- `Hartshorne.baseFieldToGlobalSections f : k →+* Γ(X, 𝒪_X)` is
  `(Scheme.ΓSpecIso (.of k)).inv ≫ f.appTop`.
- `Hartshorne.cohomologyOverField f n : X.Modules ⥤ ModuleCat k` is
  `moduleSheafCohomologyModule X.ringCatSheaf n ⋙ ModuleCat.restrictScalars (baseFieldToGlobalSections f)`
  (the `Γ(X, 𝒪_X)`-module cohomology of module-cohomology-comparison, restricted to `k`).
- `Hartshorne.homOverField f F G : ModuleCat k` is `F ⟶ G` with the `Γ(X, 𝒪_X)`-action of
  module-sheaf-hom (`Ringed.moduleSheafHomGlobalHomModule`, postcomposition with
  `Ringed.moduleSheafHomGlobalScalarRingHom`), restricted to `k`. `Hartshorne.baseFieldScalar`
  is the scalar endomorphism; `Hartshorne.homOverField_smul` unfolds the action.
- Linearity of the pairing map rests on
  `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafCohomologyModule_map_globalScalar` (any ringed space with a
  sheaf of commutative rings): `Hⁿ` sends multiplication by a global section `r` to
  multiplication by `r`. Proof: the multiplications by `r` on an injective resolution form a
  cochain endomorphism (`Ringed.globalScalarCochainMap`, using `Ringed.globalScalar_naturality`)
  lifting the one on `M`; global sections send it to multiplication by `r`
  (`Ringed.evaluation_map_globalScalar`); `Hartshorne.rightDerived_map_apply_of_lift` and
  `Hartshorne.homologyMap_apply_of_forall_f_apply` pass this through
  `InjectiveResolution.isoRightDerivedObj_hom_naturality` and homology, elementwise (no
  commutativity of `Γ(X, 𝒪_X)` is needed at the type-class level).

## Friction

- `ModuleCohomology.evaluation_additive` is not found by instance search for
  `SheafOfModules.evaluation (ringCatSheafOf R) (op ⊤)` or for `X.ringCatSheaf`; it is supplied
  with `haveI`.
- Elements of `homOverField f F G` are morphisms `F ⟶ G` only up to unfolding; rewriting with
  functor lemmas needs a statement over `F ⟶ G` first.
