# Tensor products of abelian sheaves on a site

## Status

Added by a roadmap revision that splits the etale-tate-twist leaf. Reviewer r75 asked that the
article be stated for any site and that it commit to the monoidal structure (associativity,
symmetry, unit), because ell-adic-cup-product needs `F(a) ⊗ F(b) ≅ F(a+b)`. The object and its
functoriality exist in Lean; the monoidal structure on it does not yet. Nothing is recorded in
the frontmatter.

## Candidate declarations (existing)

Module `Hartshorne.Weil.EtaleTateTwist`, for `J : GrothendieckTopology C` with
`[HasSheafify J Ab]` (any site, any universes):

- `Hartshorne.presheafTensor F G` (`U ↦ F(U) ⊗_ℤ G(U)`), `Hartshorne.presheafTensorMap`,
  `presheafTensorMap_id`, `presheafTensorMap_comp`.
- `Hartshorne.sheafTensor J F G` (sheafification of `presheafTensor`), `Hartshorne.sheafTensorMap`,
  `sheafTensorMap_id`, `sheafTensorMap_comp`.
- `Hartshorne.sheafTensorPowSucc J F k` (`F^{⊗(k+1)}`, bracketed to the left, which matches the
  article's `F^{⊗(k+1)} = F^{⊗k} ⊗ F` with the index shifted by one),
  `Hartshorne.sheafTensorPowSuccMap` (`_id`, `_comp`), `Hartshorne.sheafTensorPowSuccFunctor J k`.
- Also in the module: `presheafTensorMap_nsmul_left`, `sheafTensorMap_nsmul_left`,
  `sheafTensor_nsmul_id_eq_zero`, `sheafTensorPowSucc_nsmul_id_eq_zero` (used by the leaf for the
  `ℓ^r`-torsion of the twists).

## Still to build: the symmetric monoidal structure

Mathlib has no monoidal structure on `AddCommGrpCat` other than the cartesian one
(`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/Grp/CartesianMonoidal.lean`, products, not tensor products). Two
routes:

1. Coefficients in `ModuleCat ℤ`, which is symmetric monoidal closed
   (`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Monoidal/Closed.lean`). Mathlib's
   `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.monoidalCategory J A` and `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.symmetricCategory J A`
   (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Monoidal.lean`, not instances, `@[instance_reducible]` defs)
   need `[(J.W (A := A)).IsMonoidal]` and `[HasWeakSheafify J A]`; `J.W.IsMonoidal` is the
   instance `GrothendieckTopology.W.monoidal` under `HasFunctorEnrichedHom` /
   `HasEnrichedHom` for all presheaves, which holds for small sites. They also give
   `(presheafToSheaf J A).Monoidal` and `.Braided` (the article's "sheafification is a symmetric
   monoidal functor"). Transfer to `Ab` along `forget₂ (ModuleCat ℤ) AddCommGrpCat` (full,
   faithful, essentially surjective: `Mathlib/Algebra/Category/Grp/ZModuleEquivalence.lean`),
   and compare the resulting tensor with `Hartshorne.sheafTensor` by an isomorphism that is the
   identity on `f ⊗ g` (as `Hartshorne.Ringed.moduleSheafTensorIsoTensor` does for module
   sheaves).
2. Prove the associativity, unit and symmetry isomorphisms and the coherence axioms for
   `Hartshorne.sheafTensor` directly, by the universal property of sheafification. Longer.

The small étale site `X.Etale` is a large category (`Type (u+1)` objects), so the
`HasFunctorEnrichedHom` hypotheses are not available there. Use
`GrothendieckTopology.W.transport_isMonoidal` along a cover-dense, full, continuous functor from
a small site whose pushforward on sheaves is essentially surjective: the small model of
`X.AffineEtale` (`equivSmallModel`, `AffineEtale.Spec X`, which is `IsCoverDense` for
`X.smallEtaleTopology`, and `AffineEtale.sheafEquiv` in
`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/AffineEtale.lean`). This is the article's last paragraph.

The precedent in this project is the module-sheaf monoidal structure on ringed spaces
(`Hartshorne.RingedSpace.ModuleSheafMonoidal`, `Hartshorne.Ringed.monoidalCategory`), which uses
the same monoidal-localization route with a stalk argument in place of the internal-Hom one.

Recording suggestion once built: `lean: Hartshorne.sheafTensor` followed by the monoidal and
symmetric structure declarations and the comparison isomorphism, `Hartshorne.sheafTensorPowSucc`,
and the étale-site instance. `declaration: def` matches `sheafTensor`; if Formalize makes the
monoidal structure the main target, change it to `instance` or keep `def` to match the Mathlib
style.
