# Tensor products of abelian sheaves on a site

## Revision (review item 31)

The unitor clause now reads "a ⊗ f and f ⊗ a to af for an integer a": over a disconnected `U` a
section of the constant sheaf is only locally constant, and
`Hartshorne.AbSheaf.leftUnitor_hom_tmulSection` / `Hartshorne.AbSheaf.rightUnitor_hom_tmulSection`
prove the formula for the integer sections `Hartshorne.AbSheaf.intSection U n`. No Lean change is
needed: the recorded `lean:` list states the revised article. To re-record, restore
`statement: formalized` and `proof: formalized` and add `Hartshorne.sheafTensorMap_id`,
`Hartshorne.sheafTensorMap_comp` (functoriality in both variables) and
`Hartshorne.sheafTensorMap_comp_isoTensor` (the identification with the monoidal tensor product).
The existence of associated sheaves on the sites in question comes from Mathlib:
`CategoryTheory.hasSheafifyEssentiallySmallSite` for an essentially small site, and the
`HasSheafify` instance on `S.smallEtaleTopology` in `Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Sites.AffineEtale`.
