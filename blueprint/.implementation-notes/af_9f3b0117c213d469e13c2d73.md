# Torsion divisor linear systems are periodic

## Status

Module: `Hartshorne/Scheme/TorsionLinearSystem.lean` (imported by the library root). Complete,
sorry-free. Declarations to record (namespace `Hartshorne.LinearSystem`):

- `dim_complete_of_orderOf_eq`: invertible-sheaf form. `X` integral, projective over an
  algebraically closed `k`; `L` invertible with `orderOf [L] = r > 0` in `Pic X.sheaf`; `M`
  invertible with `[M] = [L]^n`; then `(complete hq hM).dim = if r ∣ n then 0 else -1`.
- `dim_complete_nsmul_of_addOrderOf_eq`: Cartier form, `|nD| = complete` of `(n • D).sheaf`, order
  taken as `addOrderOf (X.cartierDivisorToPicard D)`.
- `periodic_dim_complete_nsmul`: `Function.Periodic (n ↦ dim |nD|) r`, Cartier form.
- `dim_complete_weil_nsmul_of_addOrderOf_eq`: the book's form. `X` regular integral projective over
  `k`, `D` a Weil divisor, the class of `D` in `Pic X` through `weilClassEquivPicard`
  (Corollary 6.16), `|nD|` the complete linear system of `𝒪_X(nD)` via `weilCartierEquiv`
  (the same convention as `mem_divisors_complete_iff_weil`). Noetherian and separated instances
  are supplied by `isNoetherian_of_projective` / `isSeparated_of_projective` in the statement.
- `periodic_dim_complete_weil_nsmul`: periodicity, Weil form.

`n` ranges over all of `ℕ` (the book takes `n > 0`; `n = 0` is included and harmless).

## Route

The article's proof sketch: a nonzero section of `L ≅ 𝒪_X(D₀)` has an effective divisor of zeros
`E ~ D₀` (`Scheme.CartierDivisor.exists_isDivisorOfZeros`, Prop. 7.7(a)). With `m D₀ ~ 0`:

1. `m E` is effective (private `isEffective_nsmul'`, via the characterization "`E` effective iff
   `1` is a section of `𝒪_X(E)`": `isSection_of_isEffective_add_principal` one way, local
   equations the other, closed under sums by `isSection_mul`).
2. An effective `div h` is `0` (private `eq_zero_of_isEffective_of_linearEquivalent_zero`):
   effectivity makes `h` a section of `𝒪_X(0)`, so a global regular function
   (`exists_toK_eq_of_isSection_zero`), a nonzero constant by `Scheme.globalScalar_bijective`
   (`Γ(X, 𝒪_X) = k` for integral `X` universally closed over `k = k̄`), hence a unit; units of
   `𝒪_X` have zero divisor (`toCartierDivisorSheaf_apply_eq_iff_ratio`).
3. `m E = 0` gives `-E = (m-1) E` effective; `E` and `-E` effective forces `E = 0` (private
   `eq_zero_of_isEffective_of_isEffective_neg`: local equations regular with regular inverses).
4. So `D₀ ~ 0`. For `D₀ = nD` this gives `Γ = 0` unless `r ∣ n`; when `nD ~ 0` every nonzero
   section has divisor `0`, so `LinearSystem.divisor_eq_iff` (Prop. 7.7(c)) makes `Γ` a line.

`m D ~ 0 ↔ m • [D] = 0` in `Pic` uses `linearEquivalent_iff_nonempty_iso` and `Pic.mk_eq_mk_iff`.
Projective gives universally closed through `Projective.isProper`.

## Findings

- The scheme-level `Γ(X, 𝒪_X) = k` used here is `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.globalScalar_bijective`
  (in `InvertibleSectionDivisor.lean`), which no article records; the article's dependency on the
  classical-variety Theorem I.3.4(a) article is used mathematically through it, not through that
  article's recorded declarations.
- The Weil form's statement uses `weilClassEquivPicard`, recorded on the locally factorial
  Cl–Pic article; a Depends on link to it was added.
- No `IsEffective` closure lemmas (zero, sums, multiples) existed for Cartier divisors; they are
  private here (`isEffective_zero'`, `isEffective_add'`, `isEffective_nsmul'`) and are candidates
  for promotion to `EffectiveCartierDivisor.lean`.
