# Module ideal sheaves and ideal data

## Prior implementation guidance

For a scheme `X`, quasi-coherent subobjects `I ↪ O_X` in `X.Modules`
correspond to `X.IdealSheafData`. On every affine open `U`, the correspondence
sends `I` to the ideal `Γ(U,I) ⊆ Γ(U,O_X)`, and reconstructs `I|_U` as the
tilde sheaf of that ideal.

This is a representation bridge. `IdealSheafData` must not be treated as
definitionally equal to a module subsheaf: Mathlib explicitly stores
compatible ideals on affine opens rather than an object of `X.Modules`.

## Status (42981-w29, frontier/w29i)

A full draft of the bridge is in `Hartshorne/Scheme/ModuleIdealSheafData.lean`. It is not yet
compiled because load was above 150, and the library root does not import it.

- `Hartshorne.QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf X` is a structure. Its carrier is a submodule presheaf of
  `𝒪_X`, together with `IsLocal` and quasi-coherence of `toModules`. It represents subsheaves as
  local submodule families, not as `Subobject`.
- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.toIdealSheafData` sends `I` to its ideals on affine opens.
  `map_ideal_basicOpen` holds by quasi-coherence, via `exists_pow_smul_extension_of_isAffineOpen`
  and the unit `f|_{D(f)}`.
- `IdealSheafData.toQuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf J := powSmul J 1 ⊤`. It is quasi-coherent by
  `isQuasicoherent_powSmul`, and its affine sections are `J(V)` by `sub_powSmul_eq`.
- `quasicoherentIdealSubsheafEquiv` gives both round trips, plus the `_ideal` and `_symm_ideal`
  lemmas.
- `QuasicoherentIdealSubsheaf.restrictTildeIso` gives `I|_U ≅ (Γ(U, I))~`.
