# Local complete intersections are Cohen–Macaulay

## Implementation route (w30, frontier/w30h)

Draft module: `Hartshorne.Scheme.Differentials.LocalCompleteIntersectionCohenMacaulay`.
Target statement: `Hartshorne.isCohenMacaulay_of_isLocalCompleteIntersectionIn`. Given
`X.IsRegular`, a closed immersion `i : Y ⟶ X` and `IsLocalCompleteIntersectionIn i`, it
concludes `Y.IsCohenMacaulay`. The proof never uses that `X` is a variety; it needs only that
the local rings of `X` are Noetherian and regular.

For `y ∈ Y`, set `A = 𝒪_{X,i y}`; `A` is regular, hence Cohen–Macaulay
(`IsRegularLocalRing.isCohenMacaulayLocalRing`).

- The kernel of the surjective stalk map `A → 𝒪_{Y,y}` is generated by the germs of the
  `codim (Y, X)` local generating sections (`exists_ker_stalkMap_eq_span`).
  - First, the generating sections of `I_Y|_U` generate every stalk of `I_Y`. This uses
    `isGeneratedBySections_of_epi` and `FreeLocusAux.restrictStalkIso`.
  - These stalks then map into `A` by `idealStalkMap`.
  - An element of the kernel is the germ of a section that vanishes on `Y` near `y`. Hence it
    lifts to a section of `I_Y` (`modules_sections_exact`).
- The dimension bound `dim 𝒪_{Y,y} + codim (Y, X) ≤ dim A` is order-theoretic
  (`ringKrullDim_stalk_add_codim_le`). It uses `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight`. A chain of
  generizations of `y` in `Y` ends at a point `w` with `codim (Y, X) ≤ coheight (i w)`, and the
  chain can be extended by a chain above `i w`.
- Krull's bound gives the reverse inequality. Theorem 8.21A(c)
  (`isRegular_iff_ringKrullDim_quotient_ofList_add_length`, `quotient_ofList`) then makes
  `A/I` Cohen–Macaulay, and this is transported along `A/I ≅ 𝒪_{Y,y}`.

The ring-theoretic and order-theoretic parts compile. The stalk part had not compiled when the
host load blocked Lean runs.
