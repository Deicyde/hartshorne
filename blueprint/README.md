# Hartshorne, Algebraic Geometry

A Lean 4 formalization of material from Robin Hartshorne, *Algebraic Geometry*
(Springer, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 52, 1977), built on Mathlib. Which
chapters and results are in scope has not been decided yet; see the coverage
contract.

- [Roadmap](roadmap/README.md) — the book: chapters, statements, and their
  dependencies.
- [Coverage](coverage/README.md) — what counts as done, and what is out of
  scope.
