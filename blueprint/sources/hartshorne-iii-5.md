# Hartshorne III.5, cohomology of projective space

The primary source is the repository PDF, *Algebraic Geometry* (1977),
Chapter III, §5. The section begins on printed p.225 (zero-based PDF index
239) and ends on printed p.233 (index 247), immediately before §6. The often
quoted range pp.225–232 misses the end of Exercise 5.9 and all of Exercise
5.10. Page references below are printed pages.

## Cohomology of twists, pp.225–228

For a Noetherian ring `A`, `S=A[x₀,…,xᵣ]`, `r≥1`, and `X=Proj S`, Theorem
5.1 computes the cohomology of every `O_X(n)`. The standard cover
`D₊(xᵢ)` turns the direct sum of all twists into the graded localization
Čech complex. Degree zero is the section-ring isomorphism already stated in
II.5.13 and represented by
[`projective-space-section-ring.md`](../roadmap/schemes/projective-sheaves/projective-space-section-ring.md).
Top cohomology is freely generated by Laurent monomials in which
every exponent is negative; in particular `Hʳ(O(-r-1))≅A`, and multiplication
gives the stated perfect pairing. Intermediate cohomology vanishes by induction
on `r`, using the hyperplane exact sequence and affine-localized acyclicity.

Stacks Project, Lemma 30.8.1, tag
[01XT](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XT), gives the complete formula.
Equation 30.8.1.1, tag
[01XU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XU), is the equivalent
degree-zero/intermediate-zero/top-degree-dual formula; it is not a separate
reference for the Čech complex.

## Coherent finiteness and Serre vanishing, p.228

Theorem 5.2 says that for a projective scheme over a Noetherian ring and a
chosen very ample `O_X(1)`, every coherent cohomology module is finite over the
base, and one integer `n₀` works simultaneously for all `i>0` in
`Hⁱ(X,F(n))=0` when `n≥n₀`. The proof reduces through a closed immersion to
projective space and descends on cohomological degree using a finite twisted
free cover. Remark 5.2.1 recovers finite generation of global sections.

Stacks Project, Lemma 30.14.1, tag
[01YS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01YS), gives the projective-space
package. Section 30.16, tag
[0B5S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5S), gives the proper/ample
version and the closed-immersion reduction.

## Cohomological ampleness criterion, pp.229–230

Proposition 5.3 characterizes an ample invertible sheaf `L` on a proper scheme
over a Noetherian affine base by eventual vanishing of
`Hⁱ(X,F⊗L^n)` for every coherent `F` and every `i>0`. The reverse direction
uses `H¹(X,J_pF⊗L^n)=0`, Nakayama at a closed point, openness of generation,
and a finite cover. Stacks Project, Section 30.17,
[Ample invertible sheaves and cohomology](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/section/30.17),
especially Lemma 30.17.1, is the adopted detailed source.

## Adopted exercises, pp.230–233

- Exercise 5.1: additivity of `χ(F)=Σ(-1)^i dim Hⁱ(X,F)`. It is used in
  IV.1.3 (printed p.296), V.1.1–1.6 (pp.357–362), and Appendix A.4 (p.424).
- Exercise 5.2(a,b): the all-integer coherent-sheaf Hilbert polynomial and its
  equality with the Hilbert polynomial of `Γ_*(F)`. It is used in III.9.9
  (printed pp.261–263), IV.1.3.1 (p.296), and V, Exercise 1.2 (p.366).
- Exercise 5.3, definition and (a,b):
  `p_a(X)=(-1)^r(χ(O_X)-1)`, its cohomological formula for an integral
  projective scheme, and compatibility with the embedded definition. It is
  used in III.7.12.2–7.12.3 (pp.246–247), III.9.10 (p.263), IV.1.1
  (p.294), and V.1.6 (p.362).
- Exercise 5.5(c) only: intermediate twist cohomology vanishes on a positive-
  dimensional projective complete intersection. It is used in III, Exercise
  11.5 (printed p.281).
- Exercise 5.6(a–c): line-bundle cohomology on a nonsingular quadric surface,
  connectedness, existence and projective normality of curves of type `(a,b)`,
  and `p_a=(a-1)(b-1)`. It is used in IV.1.1.1 (p.294), IV.6.4–6.6
  (pp.352–355), and V.1.5.2 (p.362).
- Exercise 5.7(a–d): restriction, reduction, irreducible-component, and finite-
  surjective invariance of ampleness. Hartshorne points to *Ample Subvarieties
  of Algebraic Varieties*, Chapter I, §4; Stacks Section 30.17 and Lemma
  30.17.5, tag [09MS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09MS), are adopted
  supplementary sources. The exercise is used in III, Exercise 11.3 (p.281)
  and V.1.10 (pp.365–366).
- Exercise 5.8(a–d): every one-dimensional proper scheme over an algebraically
  closed field is projective. Stacks Project, Proposition 33.38.12, tag
  [09NZ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09NZ), is the adopted complete
  theorem. Its proof uses the reduced theorem (tag `09NX`), Picard lifting
  (tag `09NY`), and nilpotent invariance of ampleness (tag `09MS`). This is an
  approved scope exception, retained to preserve the III.3–4 reduction,
  Picard, and square-zero chain even though no later numbered result cites it.
  The roadmap keeps Hartshorne's normalization/component/nilpotent filtration.
- Exercise 5.9: the stated square-zero extension of `P²` by its canonical
  sheaf has trivial Picard group and is proper but nonprojective. The cocycle
  boundary calculation remains a source obligation. This is an approved scope
  exception preserving the deformation-classification and square-zero Picard
  chain from III, Exercises 4.10 and 4.6.
- Exercise 5.10, p.233: a finite exact sequence of coherent sheaves becomes
  exact on global sections after one uniform sufficiently positive twist. It
  is used in III.6.9 (printed p.236) and III.9.9 (p.262).

Exercise 5.9 uses Exercise 4.10's classification of infinitesimal extensions
and Exercise 4.6's square-zero Picard sequence. Exercise 5.10 is later used in
III.6.9 and III.9.9.
