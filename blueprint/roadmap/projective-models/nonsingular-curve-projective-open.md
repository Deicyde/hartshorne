# Completion of a nonsingular curve

Every nonsingular quasi-projective curve is isomorphic to an open subcurve of
a nonsingular projective curve.

This is the second assertion of Corollary 6.10.  [Proposition I.6.7](../curve-normalization/curve-to-valuation-space-iso.md) identifies
the original curve with an open of its valuation curve, and [Theorem I.6.9](function-field-projective-model.md)
identifies that valuation curve with its nonsingular projective model.
