# Cohomology on an open object

For an abelian sheaf $F$ on $X$, an open subset $U$, and a
nonnegative integer $i$, the objectwise site cohomology of $F$
at $U$ is naturally isomorphic to $H^i(U,F|_U)$, the cohomology
of the restricted sheaf on the topological space $U$.

This gives the comparison with Hartshorne's presheaf of
cohomology on inverse-image opens.

## Proof depends on

- [Sheaf cohomology as Ext from the constant sheaf](../ext-groups/global-ext/sheaf-cohomology-ext-comparison.md)
- Equivalence between sheaves on an open subspace and sheaves on the
  corresponding over-site, compatible with the constant integral sheaf.
- The direct abelian-sheaf restriction theorem: restriction to an open
  subspace sends injectives to injectives. This is also the specialization of
  the module-sheaf restriction theorem to modules over the constant sheaf
  $\mathbb{Z}$.
- Under the same specialization, the free abelian sheaf generated by the
  representable open object corresponds to extension by zero of the constant
  sheaf $\mathbb{Z}$ on that open.
- Naturality of $\operatorname{Ext}$ under that equivalence.
