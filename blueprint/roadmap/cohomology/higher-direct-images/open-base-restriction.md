# Restriction of higher direct images to an open base

Let $j : V \to Y$ be an open immersion, let $f' : f^{-1}(V) \to V$ be the
restricted map, and let $F$ be an abelian sheaf on $X$. For every $i$, prove
the natural isomorphism

$(R^i f_* F)|_V \simeq R^i f'_* (F|_{f^{-1}(V)})$.

This is Corollary 8.2. The isomorphism is natural in $F$ and compatible with
nested open subsets.
