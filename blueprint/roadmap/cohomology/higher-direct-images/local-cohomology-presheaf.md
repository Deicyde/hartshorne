# The local cohomology presheaf formula

For a continuous map `f : X -> Y`, an abelian sheaf `F` on `X`, and
`i : N`, construct a natural isomorphism from `R^i f_* F` to the sheaf
associated to

`V |-> H^i(f^{-1}(V), F|_{f^{-1}(V)})`.

This is Proposition 8.1. The unique main result is the natural isomorphism of
sheaves, including compatibility with restriction maps in `V` and
functoriality in `F`.
