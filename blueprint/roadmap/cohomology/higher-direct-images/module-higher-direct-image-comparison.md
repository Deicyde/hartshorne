# Module and abelian higher direct images agree

For a morphism of ringed spaces
$f:(X,\mathcal{O}_X)\to(Y,\mathcal{O}_Y)$, define module-valued
higher direct images as the degreewise right derived functors of
module-sheaf direct image. After forgetting module structure,
these are naturally isomorphic to the higher direct images
$R^i f_*$ of the underlying abelian sheaves.

The comparison includes degree zero and the connecting morphisms.
It equips abelian higher direct images of module sheaves with
their canonical module structure and applies to ringed spaces, as
in Hartshorne's Proposition III.8.4.
