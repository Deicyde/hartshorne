# Projective dimension of the residue field

Let $(A,m,k)$ be a Noetherian regular local ring. Then

$pd_A(k)=\dim A$.

This equality is the sharpness clause in the regular-local global-dimension
theorem.

Let $d=\dim A$, so that $m$ is generated by $d$ elements. First, $m$ is
generated by a regular sequence of length $d$; this is proved by induction on
$d$. If $d=0$ then $m=0$ and the empty sequence works. If $d>0$, choose
$x\in m\setminus m^{2}$. Then $A/(x)$ is a regular local ring whose maximal
ideal $m/(x)$ is generated by $d-1$ elements, so by induction $m/(x)$ is
generated by a regular sequence $\bar y_2,\ldots,\bar y_d$ in $A/(x)$. Since
$A$ is an integral domain and $x\neq0$, $x$ is a non-zero-divisor on $A$, so
for any lifts $y_i\in A$ of the $\bar y_i$ the sequence $x,y_2,\ldots,y_d$ is
regular, and it generates $m$. Finally, the quotient of a local ring by a
regular sequence of length $d$ in its maximal ideal has projective dimension
exactly $d$, and here that quotient is $A/m=k$.
