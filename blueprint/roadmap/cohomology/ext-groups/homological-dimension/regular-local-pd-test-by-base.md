# Projective dimension detected by Ext against the base ring

Let `A` be a Noetherian regular local ring and `M` a finitely generated
`A`-module. For every `n`,

`pd_A(M)≤n ↔ ∀ i>n, Ext^i_A(M,A)=0`.
