# The contravariant long exact sequence of sheaf Ext

For a short exact sequence `0 ⟶ F' ⟶ F ⟶ F'' ⟶ 0` and an `O_X`-module
`G`, the sheaves `𝓔xt^i_X(-,G)` form the natural contravariant long exact
sequence beginning

`0 ⟶ 𝓗om(F'',G) ⟶ 𝓗om(F,G) ⟶ 𝓗om(F',G) ⟶ 𝓔xt^1(F'',G) ⟶ ⋯`.
