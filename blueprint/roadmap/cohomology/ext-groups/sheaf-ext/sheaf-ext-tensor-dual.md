# Tensor-dual compatibility for sheaf Ext

For a finite-rank locally free `O_X`-module `L` and arbitrary module sheaves
`F,G`, construct natural isomorphisms

`𝓔xt^i_X(F⊗L,G) ≅ 𝓔xt^i_X(F,L∨⊗G)`

for every `i ≥ 0`.
