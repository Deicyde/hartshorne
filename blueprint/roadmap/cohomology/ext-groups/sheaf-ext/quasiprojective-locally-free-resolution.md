# Locally free resolutions on quasi-projective schemes

Let `X` be quasi-projective over a Noetherian affine scheme. Every coherent
`O_X`-module admits a resolution, possibly unbounded to the left, by
finite-rank locally free sheaves.

The unique main result is existence of the resolution; the successive kernel
construction is proof data.

## Depends on

- [Coherent extension](../../../schemes/coherent-sheaves/coherent-extension.md)

## Proof depends on

- Realize a quasi-projective scheme as an open subscheme of a projective
  scheme, obtain a finite-rank locally free surjection at each stage, and use
  Noetherian coherence to preserve finite presentation of successive kernels.
