# Tensor-dual compatibility for global Ext

For a finite-rank locally free `O_X`-module `L`, its dual `L∨`, and arbitrary
module sheaves `F,G`, construct natural isomorphisms

`Ext^i_X(F⊗L,G) ≅ Ext^i_X(F,L∨⊗G)`

for every `i ≥ 0`.
