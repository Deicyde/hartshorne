# Ext groups and sheaves

Hartshorne III.6 develops global Ext, sheaf Ext, their local and projective
comparisons, and the homological-dimension results needed by Serre duality.
The roadmap deliberately keeps global Ext groups, sheaf Ext objects, and
derived Hom models distinct.
