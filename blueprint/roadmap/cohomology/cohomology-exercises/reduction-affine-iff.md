# Affineness is invariant under reduction

For a Noetherian scheme `X`, `X` is affine if and only if its reduction
`X_red` is affine.

## Depends on

- [Reduced schemes](../../schemes/first-properties/reduced-scheme.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Serre's affineness criterion](../affine-cohomology/serre-affineness-criterion.md)
- The nilradical sheaf is nilpotent on a Noetherian scheme.
- Filter a coherent sheaf by successive powers of the nilradical and use the
  long exact cohomology sequence.
