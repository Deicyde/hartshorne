# Quasi-coherent sheaves are filtered unions of coherent subsheaves

Let `X` be a Noetherian scheme and `F` a quasi-coherent `O_X`-module. The
poset of coherent quasi-coherent subsheaves of `F` is filtered, and its
canonical colimit maps isomorphically to `F`.

## Depends on

- [Noetherian schemes and finite affine covers](../../schemes/first-properties/noetherian-scheme.md)

- Choose finitely many affine charts and finitely generated submodules
  containing any prescribed finite family of local sections, then extend and
  sum the resulting coherent subsheaves.
