# Cohomology under an affine morphism

Let `f : X ⟶ Y` be an affine morphism of Noetherian separated schemes and
let `F` be quasi-coherent on `X`. For every `i`, there are natural
isomorphisms

`H^i(X,F) ≅ H^i(Y,f_*F)`.
