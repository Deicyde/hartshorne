# The Picard group as first unit-sheaf cohomology

For every ringed space $(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$, transition functions give a natural group
isomorphism

$\operatorname{Pic} X \cong H^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X^*)$.

## Depends on

- [The group of invertible sheaves](../../schemes/divisors/cartier-picard/invertible-sheaf-group.md)
- [Sheaf cohomology](../sheaf-cohomology/sheaf-cohomology.md)

## Proof depends on

- [First cohomology as the refinement colimit](cech-refinement-colimit-h1.md)
- Local trivializations give unit-valued Čech cocycles.
- Coboundaries exactly change trivializations, and cocycles glue invertible
  sheaves.
