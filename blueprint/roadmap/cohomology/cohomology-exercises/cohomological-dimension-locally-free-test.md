# Testing cohomological dimension on vector bundles

If `X` is quasi-projective over a field, its cohomological dimension can be
tested using only finite locally free modules.

## Depends on

- [Cohomological dimension](cohomological-dimension.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Testing cohomological dimension on coherent sheaves](cohomological-dimension-coherent-test.md)
- [Finite twisted-free covers](../../schemes/projective-sheaves/finite-twisted-free-cover.md)
- Resolve coherent modules successively by finite locally free modules and
  dimension-shift along the kernels.
