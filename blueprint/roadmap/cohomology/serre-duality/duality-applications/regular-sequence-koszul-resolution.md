# A regular sequence gives a Koszul resolution

If `f₁,…,fᵣ` is a regular sequence on an `A`-module `M`, then

`H_p(K(f;M))=0` for `p>0`

and the augmentation induces
`H₀(K(f;M))≃M/(f₁,…,fᵣ)M`.
