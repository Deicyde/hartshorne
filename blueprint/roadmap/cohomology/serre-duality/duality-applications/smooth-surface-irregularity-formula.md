# The irregularity formula for a nonsingular surface

For a nonsingular projective surface `X` over an algebraically closed field
`k`, define
`q(X)=dim_k H¹(X,O_X)`. Then

`p_g(X)-p_a(X)=q(X)`,

so in particular `p_g(X)≥p_a(X)`.
