# Serre duality and dualizing sheaves

This chapter constructs projective dualizing sheaves, proves Serre duality
under the Cohen–Macaulay and equidimensional hypotheses, and develops its
connectedness, canonical-sheaf, Hodge-symmetry, and residue consequences.
It concludes by recording Kodaira vanishing as a deferred proof boundary.
