# Artin–Rees induced topology

Let `A` be Noetherian, `a` an ideal, and `M ⊆ N` finite `A`-modules. For
every `n` there is `n' ≥ n` such that

`a^n M ⊇ M ∩ a^n' N`.

Thus the `a`-adic topology on `M` is induced by that on `N`.
