# Serre's affineness criterion

For a Noetherian scheme `X`, the following are equivalent:

1. `X` is affine;
2. `H^i(X,F)=0` for every quasi-coherent `F` and every `i>0`;
3. `H^1(X,J)=0` for every coherent ideal sheaf `J`.
