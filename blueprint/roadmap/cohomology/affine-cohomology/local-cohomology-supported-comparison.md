# Algebraic and supported sheaf local cohomology

Let `A` be Noetherian, `a` an ideal, `X = Spec A`, `Y = V(a)`, and `M` an
`A`-module. There are natural isomorphisms

`H_a^i(M) ≅ H_Y^i(X,Mtilde)`

for all `i`.
