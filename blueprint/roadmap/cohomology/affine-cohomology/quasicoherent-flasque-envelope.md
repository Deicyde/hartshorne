# Quasi-coherent flasque envelopes

If `X` is Noetherian and `F` is a quasi-coherent `O_X`-module, there is a
monomorphism `F ⟶ G` with `G` both flasque and quasi-coherent.

- [Noetherian schemes and finite affine covers](../../schemes/first-properties/noetherian-scheme.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The sheaf of an injective module is flasque](tilde-injective-flasque.md)

- [Direct image of a sheaf](../../schemes/sheaf-functors/direct-image.md)
- Flasque sheaves remain flasque under direct image and finite products.
- The finite family of affine-chart embeddings is jointly monic on stalks.
