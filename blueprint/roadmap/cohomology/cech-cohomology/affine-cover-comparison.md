# Affine covers compute quasi-coherent cohomology

Let `X` be a Noetherian separated scheme, `U` an affine open cover, and `F`
a quasi-coherent `O_X`-module. For every `p`, the comparison map is an
isomorphism

`CechH^p(U,F) ≅ H^p(X,F)`.
