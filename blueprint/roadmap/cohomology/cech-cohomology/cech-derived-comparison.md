# Comparison from Čech to sheaf cohomology

For every open cover `U`, abelian sheaf `F`, and degree `p`, construct a
natural map

`CechH^p(U,F) ⟶ H^p(X,F)`

which in degree zero is the canonical global-sections isomorphism.

## Depends on

- [The ordered Čech complex](open-cover-cech-complex.md)
- [Sheaf cohomology](../sheaf-cohomology/sheaf-cohomology.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The sheafified Čech resolution](sheafified-cech-resolution.md)
- [Zeroth Čech cohomology is global sections](cech-zero-global-sections.md)
- [Comparison with an injective resolution](../derived-functors/resolution-to-injective-comparison.md)
- The comparison morphism between the Čech and an injective resolution is
  unique up to homotopy.
