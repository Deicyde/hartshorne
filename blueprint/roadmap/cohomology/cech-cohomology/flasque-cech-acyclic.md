# Flasque sheaves are Čech-acyclic

If `F` is flasque, then `CechH^p(U,F)=0` for every open cover `U` and every
`p>0`.

## Depends on

- [The ordered Čech complex](open-cover-cech-complex.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The sheafified Čech resolution](sheafified-cech-resolution.md)
- [Flasque closure in short exact sequences](../sheaf-cohomology/flasque-short-exact-closure.md)
- [Flasque sheaves are acyclic](../sheaf-cohomology/flasque-sheaf-acyclic.md)
- [Comparison by acyclic resolutions](../derived-functors/acyclic-resolution-comparison.md)
- [Limits of sheaves are computed pointwise](../../schemes/sheaves/limits-of-sheaves.md)
- Restrictions, direct images, and products of flasque sheaves are flasque.
- The sheafified Čech resolution therefore computes ordinary cohomology.
