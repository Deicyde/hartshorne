# Projective space is smooth

Let `k` be a field and let `Y` be a scheme of finite type over `Spec k`.
For every `n`, the projection

`P^n_k ×_(Spec k) Y → Y`,

canonically identified with `P^n_Y → Y`, is smooth of relative dimension `n`.
