# Smooth relative-dimension calculus in Hartshorne's setting

Fix a field `k` and work with finite-type schemes over `Spec k`. Open
immersions are smooth of relative dimension zero. Smoothness of relative
dimension `n` is preserved by base change, and relative dimensions add under
composition and products over a common base.

The finite type hypotheses record Hartshorne's standing convention; the
arbitrary base calculus proves these conclusions independently of those hypotheses.
