# The differential criterion for smoothness over a field

Let $k$ be a field, let $T$ be a finitely generated $k$-algebra, let
$\mathfrak q$ be a prime ideal of $T$, and let $n$ be a natural number with

$$\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak q)} \bigl(\kappa(\mathfrak q) \otimes_T \Omega_{T/k}\bigr)
\leq n \leq \dim T_{\mathfrak q} + \dim T/\mathfrak q.$$

Then $T$ is smooth over $k$ at $\mathfrak q$: the local ring
$T_{\mathfrak q}$ is formally smooth over $k$. Consequently, if these
inequalities hold at every prime of $T$, then $T$ is smooth over $k$.

Only this direction, from a bound on the fibre rank of the differentials to
smoothness, is asserted here.

Present $T = R/I$ with $R = k[x_1, \dots, x_N]$ and let $Q$ be the preimage of
$\mathfrak q$.

1. By the conormal exact sequence, $\kappa(\mathfrak q) \otimes_T \Omega_{T/k}$
   is the quotient of the $N$-dimensional space
   $\kappa(\mathfrak q) \otimes_R \Omega_{R/k}$ by the span of the differentials
   of elements of $I$. So there are $f_1, \dots, f_c \in I$ with linearly
   independent differentials and $N \leq c + n$.
2. Since $R_Q$ is regular,
   [the regular quotient theorem](independent-differentials-regular-quotient.md)
   makes $R_Q/(f)$ a regular local ring, hence a domain, of dimension
   $\operatorname{ht} Q - c$. As $\operatorname{ht} Q + \dim R/Q = N$ and
   $R/Q \cong T/\mathfrak q$, this dimension is at most $\dim T_{\mathfrak q}$.
3. $T_{\mathfrak q} \cong R_Q/IR_Q$ is a quotient of the domain $R_Q/(f)$ of at
   least the same dimension, so $IR_Q = (f)$.
4. The kernel of $R_Q \to T_{\mathfrak q}$ is generated by the $f_i$, whose
   differentials are linearly independent in $\kappa(\mathfrak q) \otimes \Omega_{R_Q/k}$,
   so the Jacobian criterion makes $T_{\mathfrak q}$ formally smooth over $k$.
