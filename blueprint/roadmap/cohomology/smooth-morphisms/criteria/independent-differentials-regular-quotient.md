# Elements with independent differentials cut out a regular local ring

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, let $P$ be a $k$-algebra, and let
$Q \subseteq P$ be a prime ideal such that $P_Q$ is a regular local ring. Let
$f_1, \dots, f_c \in Q$ be elements whose differentials
$1 \otimes df_1, \dots, 1 \otimes df_c$ are linearly independent over
$\kappa(Q)$ in $\kappa(Q) \otimes_P \Omega_{P/k}$. Then
$P_Q/(f_1, \dots, f_c)$ is a regular local ring, and

$$\dim P_Q/(f_1, \dots, f_c) = \operatorname{ht} Q - c.$$

The heart is a statement about a single regular local ring $(R, \mathfrak m)$:
if $f_1, \dots, f_c \in \mathfrak m$ have linearly independent classes in
$\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$, then $R/(f_1, \dots, f_c)$ is regular of dimension
$\dim R - c$. For one element this is the direction "$f \notin \mathfrak m^2$
implies $A/(f)$ regular" of
[Regular hypersurface quotients of a regular local ring](../../../schemes/differentials/canonical-bertini/regular-hypersurface-local-criterion.md).

To prove the local statement, let $d = \dim R = \dim_\kappa \mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$.
The maximal ideal of $R/(f)$ is generated by $d - c$ elements, so its
cotangent space has dimension at most $d - c$, which bounds
$\dim R/(f)$ above; Krull's height theorem gives
$\dim R/(f) \geq d - c$. Hence both numbers equal $d - c$ and $R/(f)$ is
regular. For the differential form, apply this to $R = P_Q$, whose dimension is
$\operatorname{ht} Q$: the conormal map
$\mathfrak m_Q/\mathfrak m_Q^2 \to \kappa(Q) \otimes_{P_Q} \Omega_{P_Q/k}
\cong \kappa(Q) \otimes_P \Omega_{P/k}$ sends the class of $f_i$ to
$1 \otimes df_i$, so independence of the differentials forces independence of
the classes.
