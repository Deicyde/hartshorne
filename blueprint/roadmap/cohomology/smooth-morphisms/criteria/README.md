# Smoothness and étale criteria

These leaves compare Hartshorne's fibre-dimension and differential definition
of smoothness with the modern scheme-theoretic predicate, establish the
relative-dimension calculus, and identify smooth morphisms through geometric
regularity of their fibres. The final leaf records the later-used étale
criterion from Exercise 10.3.
