# Smoothness and étale criteria

These leaves compare Hartshorne's fibre-dimension and differential definition
of smoothness with the modern scheme-theoretic predicate, establish the
relative-dimension calculus, and identify smooth morphisms through geometric
regularity of their fibres. The final leaf records the later-used étale
criterion from Exercise 10.3.

- [Constant fibre dimension of relative differentials](relative-differentials-rank-of-smooth.md)
- [Hartshorne's smoothness criterion](hartshorne-smoothness-criterion.md)

The comparison rests on standard inputs from commutative algebra and
scheme theory, recorded separately: elements with independent differentials
cut out regular quotients, which yields the dimension of a scheme smooth over a
field; the differential criterion over a field, which uses the Jacobian
criterion for local algebras and has a scheme form; and smoothness of a flat
morphism with smooth fibres.
