# Complexes, homotopies, and cohomology

For cochain complexes in an abelian category, a cochain homotopy between two
morphisms implies that the induced morphisms on every cohomology object are
equal. Supporting declarations introduce the cochain-complex, cohomology, and
homotopy conventions used by the rest of this chapter.

The main result is homotopy invariance, not the collection of supporting
definitions.
