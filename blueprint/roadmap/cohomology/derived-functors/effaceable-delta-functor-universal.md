# Effaceable delta functors are universal

A covariant delta functor `Tⁿ : A ⥤ B` whose positive-degree functors are
effaceable is universal: every morphism from `T⁰` to the degree-zero part of
another delta functor extends uniquely to a morphism of delta functors.

Grothendieck II.2.2.1 is adopted as the mathematical source. This node is not
ready because the project has not chosen a Lean representation of delta
functors, connecting morphism naturality, and their morphisms.
