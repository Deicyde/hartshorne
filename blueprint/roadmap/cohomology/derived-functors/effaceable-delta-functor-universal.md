# Effaceable delta functors are universal

A covariant delta functor $T^{n} : A \longrightarrow B$ whose positive-degree functors are
effaceable is universal: every morphism from $T^{0}$ to the degree-zero part of
another delta functor extends uniquely to a morphism of delta functors.

Grothendieck's Theorem II.2.2.1 is the adopted mathematical source.
