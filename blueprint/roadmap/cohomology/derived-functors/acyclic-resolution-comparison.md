# Acyclic resolutions compute derived functors

Let $F : A \longrightarrow B$ be left exact and let
$0 \longrightarrow A \longrightarrow J^{0} \longrightarrow J^{1} \longrightarrow \cdots$ be an exact resolution whose terms are
$F$-acyclic. There are natural isomorphisms
$R^{n}F(A) \cong H^{n}(F(J\cdot ))$ for every $n$.

The unique main result is this natural comparison; the definition of
$F$-acyclic is supporting data.
