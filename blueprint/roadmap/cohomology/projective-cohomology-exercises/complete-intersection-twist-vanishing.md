# Intermediate cohomology of a complete intersection

Let `Y⊆Pʳ_k` be a projective complete-intersection closed subscheme of
dimension `q≥1`. For every `n:Z` and every `0<i<q`,

`Hⁱ(Y,O_Y(n))=0`.

No restriction-surjectivity, connectedness, or genus clause from Exercise 5.5
is included in this node.
