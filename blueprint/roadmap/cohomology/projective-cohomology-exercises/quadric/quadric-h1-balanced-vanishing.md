# Balanced quadric H¹ vanishing

Let `Q⊆P³_k` be the split nonsingular quadric `xy=zw`, with line bundles
`O_Q(a,b)` normalized by the two rulings. If `|a-b|≤1`, then
`H¹(Q,O_Q(a,b))=0`.
