# One-dimensional proper schemes are projective

Every one-dimensional proper scheme over an algebraically closed field is
projective.

## Depends on

- [Projective morphisms](../../schemes/proper-and-projective/projective-morphism.md)

- Lift an ample invertible sheaf from `X_red`, use nilpotent invariance of
  ampleness, then take a very ample power.
