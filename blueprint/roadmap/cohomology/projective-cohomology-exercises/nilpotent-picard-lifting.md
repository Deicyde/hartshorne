# Picard lifting through a nilpotent thickening

Let `X` be a Noetherian scheme of dimension at most one and let
`i:X₀→X` be a closed immersion defined by a nilpotent quasi-coherent ideal.
Then restriction `Pic(X)→Pic(X₀)` is surjective.

## Depends on

- [The group of invertible sheaves](../../schemes/divisors/cartier-picard/invertible-sheaf-group.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The Picard sequence of a square-zero thickening](../cohomology-exercises/square-zero-picard-exact-sequence.md)
- [Grothendieck vanishing](../sheaf-cohomology/grothendieck-vanishing/grothendieck-vanishing.md)
- Filter by successive powers of the nilpotent ideal. Each adjacent
  thickening is square-zero, and its obstruction lies in `H²` of a coherent
  additive ideal sheaf, which vanishes in dimension at most one.
