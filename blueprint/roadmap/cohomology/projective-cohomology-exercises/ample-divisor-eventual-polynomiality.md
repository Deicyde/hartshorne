# Eventual polynomiality for ample divisors

If `D` is ample on a nonsingular projective variety, then `dim |nD|` is a
polynomial function of `n` for all sufficiently large `n`.

For large `n`, Serre vanishing identifies `h^0(O_X(nD))` with its Euler
characteristic, and the coherent-sheaf Hilbert polynomial makes that Euler
characteristic polynomial in `n`. The unresolved proof-source obligation is
therefore inherited from those dependencies rather than duplicated here.
