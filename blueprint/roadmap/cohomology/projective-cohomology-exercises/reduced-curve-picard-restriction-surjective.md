# Picard restriction to the components of a reduced curve

Let `X` be a reduced proper one-dimensional scheme over an algebraically
closed field, with reduced irreducible components `X₁,…,Xₘ`. The restriction
homomorphism

`Pic(X) ⟶ ⊕_i Pic(Xᵢ)`

is surjective.

## Depends on

- [The Picard group as first unit-sheaf cohomology](../cohomology-exercises/picard-h1-units.md)
- [The group of invertible sheaves](../../schemes/divisors/cartier-picard/invertible-sheaf-group.md)

## Proof depends on

- The unit-sheaf gluing sequence from Hartshorne III, Exercise 4.5 and the
  fact that pairwise component intersections are finite closed sets.
