# Flat modules in short exact sequences

For a short exact sequence `0 -> M' -> M -> M'' -> 0`, the middle module is
flat when the two outside modules are flat, and the kernel `M'` is flat when
`M` and `M''` are flat.

## Depends on

- Flat modules and short exact sequences.

## Proof depends on

- `Module.Flat.iff_lTensor_exact` and the long exact kernel-cokernel argument
  for tensor products.
- [The finitely generated ideal criterion for flatness](flat-module-fg-ideal-criterion.md).
