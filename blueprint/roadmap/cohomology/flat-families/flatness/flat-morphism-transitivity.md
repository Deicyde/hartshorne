# Flat morphisms are stable under composition

The composite of two flat scheme morphisms is flat.

## Depends on

- [The stalk criterion for flat morphisms](flat-morphism-stalk-criterion.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Transitivity for module sheaves flat over a base](module-sheaf-flat-transitivity.md),
  specialized to the structure sheaf.
