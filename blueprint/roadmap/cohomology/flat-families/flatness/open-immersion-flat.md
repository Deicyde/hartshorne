# Open immersions are flat

Every open immersion of schemes is flat.

## Depends on

- [Open immersions](../../../schemes/subschemes-and-dimension/open-immersions.md)
- [The stalk criterion for flat morphisms](flat-morphism-stalk-criterion.md)

## Proof depends on

- The open-immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.Flat` instance in pinned Mathlib.

The implementation is exact prior art, but its generated instance name is not
used as stable `mathlib_declaration` metadata.
