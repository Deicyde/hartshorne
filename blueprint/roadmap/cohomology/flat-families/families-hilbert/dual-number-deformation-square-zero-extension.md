# Dual-number deformations and square-zero extensions

For a finite-type `k`-scheme `X`, infinitesimal deformations over
`D=k[t]/(t^2)`, including their chosen closed-fibre markings, are equivalent
to square-zero extensions of `X` by `O_X` equipped with the compatible
`D`-algebra structure and the induced identification of the quotient with
`O_X`.

Under the equivalence, multiplication by `t` identifies the kernel in

`0 -> O_X -> O_X' -> O_X -> 0`

with `O_X`; conversely, the exact sequence and its `D`-action imply flatness
over the dual numbers.

## Depends on

- [Infinitesimal deformations over the dual numbers](infinitesimal-deformation-dual-numbers.md)

## Proof depends on

- Specify the category of marked square-zero extensions, its morphisms, and
  the compatible `D`-algebra structure sheaf.
- [The finitely generated ideal criterion for flatness](../flatness/flat-module-fg-ideal-criterion.md).
- Prove the stalkwise flatness criterion for modules over the dual numbers
  and show that the two constructions preserve the closed-fibre marking.

Hartshorne sketches the correspondence but does not specify this extension
category, so this representation root is not ready.
