# The local dimension formula for a flat fibre

Let `f : X -> Y` be a flat morphism of schemes of finite type over a field.
For `x : X`, with `y=f(x)`, the scheme-theoretic fibre satisfies

`dim O_{X_y,x} = dim O_{X,x} - dim O_{Y,y}`.

- The finite-type domain dimension formula and Krull's principal ideal
  theorem from Chapter I.
- Flatness preserves regular scalar multiplication.
- Induction after reduction and quotient by a non-zero-divisor in the base.
