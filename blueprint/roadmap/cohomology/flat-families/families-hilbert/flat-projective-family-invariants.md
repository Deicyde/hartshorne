# Numerical invariants in a flat projective family

Let `T` be connected and Noetherian and `X` a closed subscheme of `P^n_T`
flat over `T`. The dimension, degree, and arithmetic genus of every
scheme-theoretic fibre `X_t` are independent of `t`.

## Depends on

- [Scheme-theoretic invariants from the fibre Hilbert polynomial](scheme-fiber-hilbert-invariants.md)
- [The fibre of a morphism](../../../schemes/fiber-products/scheme-fiber.md)

## Proof depends on

- Restrict to reduced irreducible components of the connected base and use
  overlap chains to propagate one polynomial.
- [Flat closed projective families and Hilbert polynomials](closed-projective-family-flat-iff-hilbert-polynomial.md).
- Read fibre dimension and degree from the degree and leading coefficient of
  that common polynomial; the factorial uses the common fibre dimension.
