# Flat morphisms and families

Hartshorne III.9 develops flatness from algebraic modules through projective
families, Hilbert polynomials, normal fibres, and first-order deformations.
This milestone covers the running text on printed pp.253–266 and the one
later-used exercise on p.267.

- [Flatness foundations](flatness/README.md)
- [Families and Hilbert polynomials](families-hilbert/README.md)

The roadmap distinguishes module-sheaf flatness over a base from flatness of
a scheme morphism, and scheme-theoretic fibres from the reduced varieties in
Hartshorne's definition of an algebraic family.
