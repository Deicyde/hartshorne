# Coherent projective cohomology is finite

Let `X` be projective over a Noetherian ring `A`, with a chosen very ample
`O_X(1)`, and let `F` be coherent. For every `i≥0`, `Hⁱ(X,F)` is a finitely
generated `A`-module.
