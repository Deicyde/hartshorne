# The cohomological ampleness criterion

Let `A` be Noetherian, let `X` be proper over `Spec A`, and let `L` be
invertible. Then `L` is ample if and only if, for every coherent `F`, there is
one `n₀` such that

`Hⁱ(X,F⊗L^n)=0`

for every `i>0` and every `n≥n₀`.
