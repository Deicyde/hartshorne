# The Godement flasque embedding

For every abelian sheaf `F` on a topological space, the sheaf
`D(F)` of arbitrary, not necessarily continuous, choices of stalk elements is
flasque, and the germ map gives a natural monomorphism `F ⟶ D(F)`.

The unique main result is the functorial monomorphism into a flasque sheaf.
The construction of `D` and its restriction maps is supporting data.
