# Reduction to subsheaves of `Z_U`

Fix `n`. Assume that for every open `U`, both `Z_U` and every subsheaf of
`Z_U` have vanishing cohomology in all degrees greater than `n`. Then every
abelian sheaf has vanishing cohomology in all degrees greater than `n`.

The unique main result is this parameterized reduction. Write an arbitrary
sheaf as the filtered union of subsheaves generated by finitely many local
sections, induct on the number of generators, and present the one-generator
case as a quotient of some `Z_U`.
