# Constant-sheaf cohomology of the circle

For the circle `S¹` with its usual topology and the constant integral sheaf
`Z`, construct an isomorphism `H¹(S¹,Z) ≅ Z` using the derived-functor
definition of sheaf cohomology.

The unique main result is the displayed group isomorphism. Any concrete
resolution introduced for the computation is supporting data and must be
related to the `RΓ`/Ext comparison. This node is not ready until a concrete
resolution and the required topological sheaf model are adopted.
