# Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch

Let `f:X->Y` be a smooth projective morphism of smooth quasi-projective
varieties, and let `T_f` be its relative tangent bundle. For `x in K0(X)`,

`ch(f_!(x))=f_*(ch(x)*td(T_f))`

in `A(Y) tensor Q`.
