# Vector-bundle and coherent-sheaf K0 on a smooth variety

For a smooth quasi-projective variety, the Grothendieck group of coherent
sheaves is computed by finite locally free resolutions. Consequently every
coherent-sheaf class is an alternating sum of vector-bundle classes, and the
two K0 presentations are canonically isomorphic.
