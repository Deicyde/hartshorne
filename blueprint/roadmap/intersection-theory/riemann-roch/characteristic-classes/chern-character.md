# The Chern character

For a finite locally free sheaf `E` with formal Chern roots `a_i`, define

`ch(E)=sum_i exp(a_i)`

as an element of the rational Chow ring `A(X) tensor Q`. The formal-root
calculus makes this independent of the splitting presentation.
