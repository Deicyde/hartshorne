# The higher-dimensional Hodge index theorem

Let `X` be a smooth projective complex variety of even dimension `2k`, `H`
an ample divisor, and `Y` a codimension-`k` cycle. If the cohomology class of
`Y.H` vanishes but the class of `Y` does not, then

`(-1)^k * deg(Y.Y)>0`.
