# The projected self-intersection formula

Under the hypotheses of the regular-embedding self-intersection theorem,

`[Y].[Y]=i_*c_r(N_(Y/X)) in A^(2r)(X)`.

Here `[Y]=i_*(1_Y)`.  The identity is refined Chow self-intersection, not an
intersection of underlying sets.

## Depends on

- [The regular-embedding self-intersection formula](regular-embedding-self-intersection.md)
- [The Chow projection formula](../chow/product/projection-formula.md)

## Proof depends on

- Apply the projection formula to
  `i_*(1_Y).i_*(1_Y)=i_*(i^*i_*(1_Y))` and substitute C7.
