# The Chern polynomial of a dual bundle

If `E` has formal Chern roots `a_1,...,a_r`, then

`c_t(E^dual)=product_i(1-a_i*t)`.

Equivalently,

`c_i(E^dual)=(-1)^i c_i(E)`.

## Proof depends on

- On a splitting space, dualization replaces every line quotient by its
  dual and therefore negates each first Chern class; injectivity descends the
  identity.
