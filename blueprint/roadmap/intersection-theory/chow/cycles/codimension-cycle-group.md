# Codimension cycles on a variety

For a variety $X$ and $r\ge 0$, define $\mathbb{Z}^r(X)$ to be the free abelian group on
the irreducible closed subvarieties of codimension $r$. Thus a cycle is a
finite sum $\sum _i n_i[Y_i]$ with $n_i$ integral.

This is the codimension grading used throughout Appendix A. The same
finite-sum construction also defines codimension cycles on a scheme.
