# Rational equivalence via divisors on normalizations

Fix a codimension `r`. For every integral subvariety `V` of `X` of
codimension `r-1`, let `nu:V_norm->V->X` be its normalization map. Define the
rational-equivalence subgroup of the codimension-`r` cycle group to be
generated by

`nu_*D-nu_*D'`

for all linearly equivalent Weil divisors `D,D'` on `V_norm`. Two cycles are
rationally equivalent when their difference belongs to this subgroup.
