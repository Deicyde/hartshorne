# Localization sequence for rational equivalence via normalizations

Let $X$ be a variety, let $Y\subsetneq X$ be an integral closed subvariety of codimension
$c$, and let $U=X-Y$, with rational equivalence on $X$, $Y$ and $U$ defined
through divisors on normalizations. For every $r$:

- the closed-immersion pushforward $i_*:\mathbb{Z}^{r-c}(Y)\to\mathbb{Z}^r(X)$,
  $[W]\mapsto [W]$, sends $\operatorname{Rat}^{r-c}(Y)$ into
  $\operatorname{Rat}^r(X)$;
- the restriction $j^*:\mathbb{Z}^r(X)\to\mathbb{Z}^r(U)$, $[W]\mapsto [W\cap U]$
  (zero when $W\subseteq Y$), sends $\operatorname{Rat}^r(X)$ into
  $\operatorname{Rat}^r(U)$;
- the induced sequence

  $A^{r-c}(Y) \xrightarrow{\ i_*\ } A^r(X) \xrightarrow{\ j^*\ } A^r(U) \to 0$

  is exact.

No injectivity of $i_*$ is asserted.

A generator $\nu_*\operatorname{div}(g)$ attached to a subvariety $V\subseteq Y$ is
also a generator for $V\subseteq X$, with the same normalization, so $i_*$
descends. For $V\subseteq X$ meeting $U$, the normalization of $V\cap U$ is the
preimage of $U$ in that of $V$, so $j^*\nu_*\operatorname{div}(g)$ is the generator
attached to $V\cap U$ and $g$; it is zero when $V\subseteq Y$. Surjectivity of
$j^*$ takes closures, and $j^*i_*=0$. If $j^*\alpha=\sum_i\nu_{i*}\operatorname{div}(g_i)$
for subvarieties $V_i\subseteq U$, the closures $\overline{V_i}$ in $X$ have the same
function fields, and subtracting the corresponding generators on $X$ leaves a
cycle supported on $Y$, that is, a cycle in the image of $i_*$.
