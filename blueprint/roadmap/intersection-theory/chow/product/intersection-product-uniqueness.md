# Uniqueness of the Chow intersection product

There is at most one intersection theory on nonsingular quasi-projective
varieties satisfying A1–A7.

## Depends on

- [Chow's moving lemma](chow-moving-lemma.md)
- [Reduction to the diagonal](diagonal-reduction.md)
- [Intersection with a Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-normalization.md)
- [Local complete intersections in a nonsingular ambient variety](../../../schemes/differentials/local-complete-intersections/local-complete-intersection-in-nonsingular-ambient.md)

## Proof depends on

- Move one cycle to proper position, reduce its product with the other to
  intersection with the diagonal, work locally where the diagonal is a
  complete intersection of Cartier divisors, and apply A7 repeatedly.
