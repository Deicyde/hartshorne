# Proper pushforward is functorial on Chow groups

For a proper morphism `f:X->X'`, cycle pushforward induces

`f_* : A^r(X) -> A^(r+dim X'-dim X)(X')`.

For
composable proper morphisms,

`g_* after f_*=(g after f)_*`.
