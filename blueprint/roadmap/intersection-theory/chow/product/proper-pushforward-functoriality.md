# Proper pushforward is functorial on Chow groups

For a proper morphism $f:X\to X'$, cycle pushforward induces

$f_* : A^r(X) \to A^{r+\dim X'-\dim X}(X')$.

For
composable proper morphisms,

$g_*\circ f_*=(g\circ f)_*$.
