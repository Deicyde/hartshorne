# Serre's Tor formula gives the local intersection multiplicity

For properly intersecting `Y,Z` in a nonsingular variety and an irreducible
component `W` of their intersection, let `A=O_(X,W)` and let `a,b` be the
defining ideals. The finite sum

`sum_i (-1)^i length_A Tor_i^A(A/a,A/b)`

is a nonnegative integer and supplies the local intersection multiplicity
with the symmetry, locality, Cartier normalization, and functorial properties
required by A1–A7.
