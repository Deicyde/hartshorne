# Projective cycles are rationally equivalent to degree times a linear space

Let $Y \subset \mathbb{P}^n$ be an integral subvariety of degree $d$ and dimension
$m$. Then its Chow class is

$[Y]=d\cdot [\mathbb{P}^m]$.

Equivalently, $Y$ is rationally equivalent to $d$ times a linear subspace of
the same dimension.

- The exercise's projection argument (p.437, "similar to (III, 9.8.3)"):
  project from a general complementary linear centre and compare the generic
  finite degree with the family joining $Y$ to its projected linear limit.
- [Chow groups of projective space are generated by linear subspaces](../chow/cycles/projective-space-linear-subspace-generation.md),
  for Example 2.0.1's inductive route (p.429), which the recorded proof takes:
  $[Y] = a[L_m]$ for a linear subspace $L_m$ of dimension $m$.
- [The degree of cycles on projective space is invariant under rational equivalence](../chow/cycles/projective-cycle-degree-rational-invariance.md),
  the invariance of degree on that route: $a = \deg Y = d$, because
  $\deg L_m = 1$.
