# The local ring of a projective variety

For a projective variety $Y$ and a point $P\in Y$, Hartshorne's
Theorem I.3.4(b) gives

$$\mathcal O_{P,Y}\cong S(Y)_{(\mathfrak m_P)},$$

where $\mathfrak m_P$ is generated by homogeneous elements vanishing
at $P$.

Choose a standard affine chart $U_i$ containing $P$. Restriction to an
open neighbourhood and the chart isomorphism identify $\mathcal O_{P,Y}$
with the local ring of the affine chart image at its corresponding point.
The affine local-ring theorem identifies that ring with the localization
of $A(Y_i)$ at the point's maximal ideal.

The chart isomorphism $A(Y_i)\cong S(Y)_{(x_i)}$ carries this maximal
ideal to the contraction of the maximal ideal of
$S(Y)_{(\mathfrak m_P)}$. The graded-localization comparison therefore
identifies the two localizations. Indeed, $g/x_i^n$ belongs to the
contracted ideal exactly when $g$ vanishes at $P$, which is exactly when
its dehomogenization vanishes at the affine chart point.

The local dimension is the height of the affine point's maximal ideal;
the dimension formula identifies it with $\dim Y$.
