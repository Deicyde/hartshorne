# The local ring of a projective variety

**Theorem 3.4(b)**: for `Y` projective and `P ∈ Y`,

`𝒪_P ≅ S(Y)_(𝔪_P)`,

where `𝔪_P` is the homogeneous ideal generated by the homogeneous `f ∈ S(Y)`
vanishing at `P`.

## Status

The reduction to the affine case is proved (`Hartshorne.projLocalRingEquiv`):
for `P` in the chart `Uᵢ`,

`𝒪_{P,Y} ≅ A(Yᵢ)_𝔪`,

with `𝔪` the maximal ideal at `φᵢ(P)`. That is what Hartshorne means by "the
result follows from the affine case", and it is four isomorphisms:

- `𝒪_{P,Y} ≅ 𝒪_{P,Y∩Uᵢ}`, since the local ring does not see beyond an open
  neighbourhood;
- `𝒪_{P,Y∩Uᵢ} ≅ 𝒪_{φᵢ(P),Yᵢ}`, since `φᵢ` is an isomorphism of varieties onto
  its image and an isomorphism induces one on local rings;
- the abstract germ ring on `Yᵢ` is the affine germ ring, the two having been
  built separately;
- `A(Yᵢ)_𝔪 ≅ 𝒪_{φᵢ(P),Yᵢ}`, Theorem 3.2(c).

The first three are [the functoriality node](../local-ring-functorial.md) and
[the local ring node](../local-ring.md); supplying the second is why the chart
isomorphism was generalised from `Uᵢ` to `Yᵢ`.

One consequence comes free: `dim 𝒪_P = height 𝔪`
(`Hartshorne.ringKrullDim_projLocalRing`), since Mathlib computes the dimension
of a localisation at a prime as that prime's height. Turning the right side into
`dim Y` needs the same missing clause of Theorem 1.8A as the affine case does.

## What is left

The graded step, in the general form it needs, is proved on
[the graded localisation node](graded-localization.md): `S_(𝔭)` is the
localisation of `S_(f)` at the prime lying under its maximal ideal, for `f`
homogeneous of degree one outside `𝔭`. [Part (c)](projective-function-field.md)
already runs on the `𝔭 = (0)` case of it.

What is missing is the geometry that feeds it. `𝔪_P`, the homogeneous prime of
`S(Y)` at a point, does not exist yet, and the maximal ideal `𝔪 ⊆ A(Yᵢ)` has to
be identified with its image under `A(Yᵢ) ≅ S(Y)_(xᵢ)`. Unlike `(0)`, `𝔪_P` has
to be constructed and shown prime and homogeneous before the general lemma
applies.
