# Affine components as minimal primes of a cut ideal

Let `X` be an affine variety and let `T` be a finite family of polynomials in
its ambient coordinates.  If `W` is an irreducible component of
`X ∩ zeroLocus k T`, then the relative ideal of `W` in `A(X)` is a minimal
prime over the ideal generated by the images of `T`.  The sole main declaration
is planned as `Hartshorne.componentIdeal_mem_minimalPrimes`.

Define the relative ideal by mapping `I(W)` through the quotient by `I(X)`;
the component hypothesis supplies `I(X) ≤ I(W)`.  The affine
algebraic-set/radical-ideal anti-equivalence turns maximality of `W` among
irreducible closed subsets of the cut into minimality of its prime ideal over
the cut ideal.  Keep this bridge explicit so the later dimension node can use
Mathlib's Krull height theorem without encoding components a second time.
