# Affine components as minimal primes of a cut ideal

Let $X$ be an affine variety, let $T$ be a finite family of ambient
polynomials, and let $W$ be an irreducible component of $X\cap Z(T)$.
The relative ideal of $W$ in $A(X)$ is minimal among the prime ideals
containing the ideal generated by the images of $T$.

The relative ideal is the image of $I(W)$ in the quotient by $I(X)$.
The inclusion-reversing correspondence between algebraic sets and radical
ideals turns maximality of $W$ among irreducible closed subsets of the cut
into minimality of its prime ideal over the cut ideal.
