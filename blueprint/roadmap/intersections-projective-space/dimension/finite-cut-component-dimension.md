# A finite set of equations lowers component dimension by at most its size

Let $X$ be an affine variety, let $T$ be a finite set of ambient
polynomials, and let $W$ be an irreducible component of $X\cap Z(T)$.
Then

$$\dim X\leq\dim W+|T|.$$

The ideal of $W$ in $A(X)$ is minimal over the ideal generated by the
images of $T$. Krull's height theorem bounds its height by $|T|$.
The dimension formula for finitely generated domains and the quotient
identification with $A(W)$ turn that height bound into the stated inequality.
