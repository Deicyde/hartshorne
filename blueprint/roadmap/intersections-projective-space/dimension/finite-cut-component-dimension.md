# A finite set of equations lowers component dimension by at most its size

Let `X` be an affine variety, `T` a finite set of ambient polynomials, and `W`
an irreducible component of `X ∩ zeroLocus k T`.  Then, in the project's
bottom-valued dimension type,

`dim X ≤ dim W + T.ncard`.

The sole main declaration is planned as
`Hartshorne.dim_component_inter_zeroLocus_add_ncard`.

The component ideal in `A(X)` is minimal over the ideal generated by the
images of `T`.  Pinned Mathlib's exact Krull height theorem
`Ideal.height_le_card_of_mem_minimalPrimes_span` bounds its height by
`T.ncard`.  The already-formalized dimension formula for finite-type domains
and the quotient identification with `A(W)` convert that height bound into the
displayed geometric inequality.  The Mathlib theorem is an ingredient, not an
exact upstream replacement for this node.
