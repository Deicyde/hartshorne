# The coordinate ring of an affine product

For affine varieties `X ⊆ 𝔸^σ` and `Y ⊆ 𝔸^τ`, there is a canonical
`k`-algebra equivalence

`A(X × Y) ≃ₐ[k] A(X) ⊗[k] A(Y)`.

The sole main declaration is planned as
`Hartshorne.coordinateRing_affineProductEquiv`.  This is Exercise I.3.15(b),
adopted as the algebraic input to the dimension assertion in part (d).

Under `MvPolynomial.tensorEquivSum`, the ideal generated by the equations of
the two factors is the sum of their extended ideals.  Its zero locus is the
affine product.  Since the preceding node proves that product irreducible, the
ideal is prime and hence radical, so the affine Nullstellensatz identifies it
with the product's vanishing ideal.  Mathlib's
`Algebra.TensorProduct.quotientTensorEquiv` then performs the two quotient
steps.
