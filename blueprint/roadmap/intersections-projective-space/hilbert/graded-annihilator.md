# Homogeneous annihilators and cyclic modules

For an integer-graded `S`-module `M`, its annihilator

`Ann M = {s ∈ S | s · M = 0}`

is a homogeneous ideal. More precisely, if `m` is a nonzero homogeneous
element of degree `l`, then `Ann(m)` is a homogeneous proper ideal and the
cyclic submodule generated by `m` is graded-equivalent to
`(S/Ann(m))(-l)`. The sole main result is planned as
`Hartshorne.gradedCyclicModuleEquiv`, with homogeneity of `Ann M` recorded as
the general supporting theorem needed to state projective support.

To prove homogeneity, decompose a scalar and test its homogeneous components
against homogeneous components of arbitrary module elements. Distinct total
degrees cannot cancel. The ordinary first isomorphism theorem then identifies
the cyclic module with the quotient, while the twist records the degree of its
chosen generator.
