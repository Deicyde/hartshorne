# Homogeneous annihilators and cyclic modules

For an integer-graded $S$-module $M$, the annihilator

$$\operatorname{Ann}M=\{s\in S: sM=0\}$$

is a homogeneous ideal. If $m$ is a nonzero homogeneous element of degree
$l$, then $\operatorname{Ann}(m)$ is a proper homogeneous ideal and the
cyclic submodule generated by $m$ is isomorphic as a graded module to
$(S/\operatorname{Ann}(m))(-l)$.

Decompose a scalar and test its homogeneous components against homogeneous
module elements. Distinct degrees cannot cancel, proving homogeneity.
The first isomorphism theorem gives the cyclic-module description, while
the twist records the degree of its generator.
