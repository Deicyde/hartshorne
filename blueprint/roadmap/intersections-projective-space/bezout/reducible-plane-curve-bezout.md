# Bézout for reducible plane curves without a common component

Let `Y,Z ⊆ ℙ²` be pure one-dimensional projective algebraic sets with no
irreducible component in common. Then the sum of their pointwise intersection
multiplicities is

`projectiveDegree Y · projectiveDegree Z`.

This formalizes Remark 7.8.2. Decompose both algebraic sets into irreducible
curves, apply the irreducible plane-curve theorem pairwise, and use additivity
of degree and localized length.
