# Projective space has degree one

For projective `n`-space, the Hilbert function in nonnegative degree `l` is
`choose (l+n) n`, its Hilbert polynomial is the corresponding numerical
binomial polynomial, and `projectiveDegree (ℙⁿ) = 1`.

This is Proposition 7.6(c), with Hartshorne's displayed computation retained
as part of the statement. The proof identifies a homogeneous piece of the
polynomial ring with the monomials of total degree `l` and reads the leading
coefficient `1/n!` from the binomial polynomial.
