# Integral cohomology of a compact Riemann surface

For a compact genus-`g` Riemann surface `C^h`,

`H^0(C^h,Z)=Z`, `H^1(C^h,Z)=Z^(2g)`, and `H^2(C^h,Z)=Z`,

with no other cohomology.
