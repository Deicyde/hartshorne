# Analytic Pic0 of a curve

For a smooth projective complex genus-`g` curve,

`Pic^0(C) ~= C^g/Z^(2g)`

as a complex torus, with the lattice understood through the exponential
sequence rather than as an unspecified abstract subgroup.
