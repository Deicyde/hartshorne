# Every compact complex curve is a Hodge manifold

Every connected compact complex one-manifold admits a Kähler form whose
cohomology class is integral, and hence is a Hodge manifold.

In complex dimension one a Hermitian fundamental form is automatically
closed.  Scaling its positive total area gives the integral generator of
`H^2(M,Z)` under the fixed cohomology comparison.
