# Algebraicity implications for compact complex manifolds

For connected compact complex manifolds:

- Hodge is equivalent to projective algebraic;
- projective algebraic implies abstract algebraic, Kähler, and Moishezon;
- abstract algebraic implies Moishezon;
- Kähler together with Moishezon implies projective algebraic.

No implication from Kähler alone or Moishezon alone to algebraicity is
asserted.  General complex tori and Hironaka's contextual examples witness
the missing arrows, but their detailed constructions are not bundled into
this theorem leaf.
