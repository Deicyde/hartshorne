# Analytic cohomology of projective-space twists agrees with algebraic cohomology

For every $n\ge 0$, integer $q$, and cohomological degree $i$, analytification
induces an isomorphism

$H^i(\mathbb{P}_\mathbb{C}^n,\mathcal{O}(q)) \simeq H^i((\mathbb{P}_\mathbb{C}^n)^h,\mathcal{O}(q)^h)$.

The analytic groups have the same vanishing and explicit descriptions as the
algebraic projective-space calculation.
