# Projective GAGA identifies coherent cohomology

Let $X$ be projective over $\mathbb{C}$ and $F$ coherent. For every $i\ge 0$, the
natural map

$\alpha _i:H^i(X,F)\to H^i(X^h,F^h)$

is an isomorphism, functorially in $F$.
