# Projective GAGA identifies coherent cohomology

Let `X` be projective over `C` and `F` coherent. For every `i>=0`, the
natural map

`alpha_i:H^i(X,F)->H^i(X^h,F^h)`

is an isomorphism, functorially in `F`.
