# Proper GAGA

For a scheme `X` proper over `C`, analytification induces an equivalence on
coherent sheaves and isomorphisms on coherent cohomology, extending the
projective GAGA theorem without assuming projectivity.

Moreover analytification is fully faithful on proper finite-type
`C`-schemes: analytic morphisms between proper analytifications algebraize
uniquely, and analytic isomorphisms reflect unique algebraic isomorphisms.
