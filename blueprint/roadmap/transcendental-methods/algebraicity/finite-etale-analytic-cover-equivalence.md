# Finite étale covers correspond to finite topological covers

Let `X` be a connected normal finite-type `C`-scheme.  Analytification gives
an equivalence between finite étale algebraic covers of `X` and finite
unramified topological covering spaces of `X^h`, equipped with their natural
normal analytic-space structures.

## Depends on

- [Generalized Riemann existence](generalized-riemann-existence.md)
- [The analytification functor](../analytification/analytification-functor.md)

## Proof depends on

- A finite topological cover is locally analytically isomorphic to the base,
  hence normal and analytically unramified; generalized Riemann existence
  algebraizes it.  Analytification carries finite étale maps to topological
  covering maps and respects morphisms of covers.
