# Generalized Riemann existence for finite normal covers

Let `X` be a normal finite-type scheme over `C`.  If `M` is a normal complex
analytic space with a finite analytic morphism

`f:M->X^h`,

then there is a unique normal finite-type scheme `Y`, finite over `X`, and an
analytic isomorphism `Y^h~=M` over `X^h`.  Conversely, analytification sends
finite morphisms of normal finite-type schemes to finite analytic morphisms.

This node is not ready.  Hartshorne cites Grauert–Remmert and SGA 1 XII and
supplies no proof.  The theorem requires normal noncompact analytic spaces,
finite analytic morphisms, and analytic/algebraic normalization comparison.
