# Finite morphisms of complex analytic spaces

A morphism of complex analytic spaces is finite when it is proper in the
analytic-topological sense and has finite fibres.

This is the analytic notion used before generalized Riemann existence; it is
not definitionally identified with a finite morphism of schemes.
