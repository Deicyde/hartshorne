# Complex analytic spaces

Define a complex analytic space to be a locally ringed space admitting an
open cover by spaces isomorphic to closed analytic subspaces of polydiscs.
Morphisms are morphisms of locally ringed spaces locally induced by
holomorphic maps and compatible maps of quotient structure sheaves.

Include a gluing theorem: analytic locally ringed spaces with compatible open
overlap isomorphisms satisfying the cocycle condition glue uniquely to a
complex analytic space, and analytic morphisms glue across open covers.

This node is not ready until the local models, analytic gluing, and morphism
representation are available. It includes nonreduced spaces and is not a
synonym for complex manifold.
