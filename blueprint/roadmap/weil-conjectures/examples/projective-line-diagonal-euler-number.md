# The diagonal Euler number of the projective line is two

For `P^1/F_q`,

`E(P^1)=deg c_1(T_(P^1))=2`.

## Depends on

- [The diagonal Euler number](../weil/diagonal-euler-number.md)

- [The degree of a canonical divisor](../../curves/riemann-roch/canonical-divisor-degree.md)

## Proof depends on

- The tangent line bundle is the dual of the canonical bundle and therefore
  has degree two.
