# Point counts as ℓ-adic Frobenius traces

Let `X/F_q` be smooth projective of dimension `n`, and let `ell` differ from
the characteristic. For every `r>=1`,

`N_r = sum_(i=0)^(2n) (-1)^i Tr((F^r)^* | H^i(X_bar,Q_ell))`.
