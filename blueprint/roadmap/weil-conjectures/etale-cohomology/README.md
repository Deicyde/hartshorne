# Étale and ℓ-adic cohomology

This chapter expands Hartshorne Appendix C §3 into the étale-site,
coefficient, duality, trace, base-change, comparison, and cycle-class
interfaces needed by the Weil-conjecture roadmap.  The prime `ell` is always
different from the characteristic of the algebraically closed ground field.

- [Foundations](foundations/README.md)
- [Formal properties](properties/README.md)
- [Comparison with classical topology](comparison/README.md)
- [Cycle classes](cycle-classes/README.md)

Hartshorne's formulas are modernized by retaining Tate twists, geometric
fibres, trace normalization, and the noncanonical choice behind complex
coefficients.  Ordinary and compactly supported cohomology are not conflated.
