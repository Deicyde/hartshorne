# Diagonal self-intersection and cohomological Euler characteristic

For a smooth projective variety, the self-intersection number of the diagonal
equals its ℓ-adic Euler characteristic:

`Delta^2=sum_i (-1)^i dim H^i(X_bar,Q_ell)`.

## Proof depends on

- Under Künneth and Poincaré duality, the diagonal class is the graded
  coevaluation element. Its self-pairing is the supertrace of the identity,
  namely the alternating sum of cohomology dimensions.
