# Identification of the Weil polynomials

Deligne's integrality, ℓ-independence, and purity identify the cohomological
determinant polynomials with the integer polynomials appearing in the Weil
conjectures. Consequently their degrees give the conjectural Betti numbers,
and the cohomological and topological definitions agree after comparison.
