# The weights uniquely determine the Weil polynomials

Given the rational zeta function and a factorization whose reciprocal roots
have the prescribed distinct weights `0,...,2n`, the polynomials `P_i` are
unique.

## Depends on

- [The Weil polynomial factorization](weil-polynomial-factorization.md)
- [Deligne's weight theorem](deligne-weight-theorem.md)

## Proof depends on

- Zeros and poles of different weights have different complex absolute
  values, so no cross-weight cancellation is possible; assign each reciprocal
  root to its unique weight.
