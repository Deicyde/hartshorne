# The Zariski topology on affine space

The union of two algebraic sets is algebraic, the intersection of an
arbitrary family of algebraic sets is algebraic, and both the empty set
and affine space are algebraic. Thus algebraic sets form the closed sets
of a topology on $\mathbb A^n$, called the *Zariski topology*.

For the union property,

$$Z(T_1)\cup Z(T_2)=Z(T_1T_2),$$

where $T_1T_2$ denotes the set of pairwise products. The reverse inclusion
uses that the base field is a domain. This is Hartshorne's Proposition I.1.1.
