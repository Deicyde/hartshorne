# Homological and numerical equivalence for surface divisors

For divisors on a smooth projective complex surface, homological equivalence
to zero is equivalent to numerical equivalence to zero. Under this comparison,
the higher Hodge-index theorem specializes to the surface Hodge index theorem.

Hartshorne says only that this comparison “can be shown”; an exact proof-level
source is still required.
