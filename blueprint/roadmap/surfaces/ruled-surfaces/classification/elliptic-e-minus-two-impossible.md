# Invariant minus two is impossible over an elliptic curve

There is no normalized indecomposable rank-two bundle of invariant `e=-2`
on an elliptic curve.

- [Points of a genus-one curve and its degree-zero Picard group](../../../curves/riemann-roch/elliptic-points-picard-zero.md)

## Proof depends on

- An extension by `O(P+Q)` gives connecting maps for all decompositions
  `R+S~P+Q`. Varying these divisors fills the two-dimensional section space,
  forcing one connecting map to vanish and contradicting normalizedness.
