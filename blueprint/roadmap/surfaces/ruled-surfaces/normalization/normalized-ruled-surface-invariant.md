# The normalized degree is an invariant of a ruled surface

If `E` is a normalized rank-two bundle presenting a ruled surface
`X ~= P_C(E)`, then

`e(X) = -deg(E) = -deg(det(E))`

is independent of the normalized presentation. The normalized bundle itself
need not be unique.

## Proof depends on

- If two normalized presentations differ by a twist, minimality of the
  degree supporting a section forces the twist to have degree zero.
