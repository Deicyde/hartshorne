# A normalized bundle has a distinguished tautological section

Let `E` be normalized. A nonzero global section induces an exact sequence

`0 -> O_C -> E -> L -> 0`

with `L` invertible. The corresponding section has image `C0` satisfying

`O_X(C0) ~= O_P(E)(1)`.

## Proof depends on

- On the nonsingular curve, the rank-one quotient of a minimal nonzero
  section is torsion-free and hence invertible; otherwise its torsion lift
  would contradict normalization after a negative-degree twist.
