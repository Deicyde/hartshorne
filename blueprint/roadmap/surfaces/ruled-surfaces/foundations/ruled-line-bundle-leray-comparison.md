# Leray comparison for ruled line bundles

Let `D` be a divisor on a ruled surface `pi : X -> C` with `D.f >= 0`.
For every `i`, the natural comparison is an isomorphism

`H^i(X,O_X(D)) ~= H^i(C,pi_*O_X(D))`.
