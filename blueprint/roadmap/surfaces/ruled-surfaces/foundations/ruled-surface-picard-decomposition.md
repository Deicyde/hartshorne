# The Picard group of a ruled surface

Let $\pi : X \to C$ be a ruled surface and fix a section $C_0$. Pullback and
the class of $C_0$ induce an isomorphism

$\mathbb Z\oplus\operatorname{Pic}(C)\simeq\operatorname{Pic}(X)$.

Explicitly, a divisor $D$ is uniquely $n\cdot C_0 + \pi ^*b$, where $n=D\cdot f$ and
$b$ is a divisor class on $C$.
