# The Picard group of a ruled surface

Let `pi : X -> C` be a ruled surface and fix a section `C0`. Pullback and
the class of `C0` induce an isomorphism

`Z direct-sum Pic(C) ~= Pic(X)`.

Explicitly, a divisor `D` is uniquely `n*C0 + pi^*b`, where `n=D.f` and
`b` is a divisor class on `C`.
