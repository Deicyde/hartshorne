# Higher direct images in nonnegative fibre degree vanish

Let `D` be a divisor on a ruled surface `pi : X -> C` with `D.f >= 0`.
Then

`R^i pi_* O_X(D) = 0`

for every `i>0`.

- On every fibre the restriction is `O_P1(D.f)`, whose positive-degree
  cohomology vanishes.
