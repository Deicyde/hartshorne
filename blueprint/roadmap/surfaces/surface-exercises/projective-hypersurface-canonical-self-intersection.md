# Canonical self-intersection of a smooth hypersurface

If `X subset P^3` is a smooth surface of degree `d`, then

`K_X^2 = d*(d-4)^2`.

## Proof depends on

- Adjunction gives `K_X~(d-4)H`, while `H^2=d`.
