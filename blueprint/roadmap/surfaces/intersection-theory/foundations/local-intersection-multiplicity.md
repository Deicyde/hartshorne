# Local intersection multiplicity

Let $C ,D$ be effective curves on a Chapter V surface with no common
irreducible component, and let $P \in C \cap D$. For local equations
$f,g \in \mathcal{O}_{X,P}$, define

$(C\cdot D)_P=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{X,P}}\bigl(\mathcal O_{X,P}/(f,g)\bigr)$.

This length is finite and independent of the local equations. Over the
algebraically closed base this length also equals the $k$-dimension of the
Artinian quotient.

## Proof depends on

- No common component makes $(f,g)$ primary to the maximal ideal in the
  two-dimensional regular local ring; changing equations multiplies them by
  units.
