# Intersection numbers on a smooth projective surface

This subchapter gives Hartshorne's elementary moving construction of the
intersection pairing in V.1. Surfaces are integral regular projective
two-dimensional schemes over an algebraically closed field. The proof uses
only divisor theory, simultaneous Bertini, degrees on smooth curves, and
Euler characteristics; it does not depend on the Chow-ring construction of
Appendix A.
