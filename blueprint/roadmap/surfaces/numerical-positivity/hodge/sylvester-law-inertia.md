# Sylvester's law of inertia

Every nondegenerate quadratic form on a finite-dimensional real vector space
is equivalent to a weighted sum of squares whose weights are all `+1` or
`-1`.

This is the exact pinned declaration
`QuadraticForm.equivalent_one_neg_one_weighted_sum_squared`. The companion
signature declarations show that the numbers of positive and negative
weights are invariant under equivalence.
