# Positive square gives eventual effectivity

Let `H` be ample and let `D` be a divisor on a Chapter V surface with

`D*H>0` and `D^2>0`.

For every sufficiently large positive integer `n`, the divisor `nD` is
linearly equivalent to an effective divisor; in fact `l(nD)` tends to
infinity.

## Depends on

- [The surface Riemann–Roch theorem](surface-riemann-roch.md)
- [A numerical bound for second-cohomology vanishing](surface-h2-vanishing-bound.md)

## Proof depends on

- For large `n`, second cohomology vanishes and Riemann–Roch gives a
  quadratic lower bound with positive leading coefficient `D^2/2`.
