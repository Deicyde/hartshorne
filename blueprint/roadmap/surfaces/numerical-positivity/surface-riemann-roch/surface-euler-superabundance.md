# Sections, superabundance, and Euler characteristic

For a divisor $D$ on a Chapter V surface $X$, define

- $l(D)=\dim _k H^0(X,\mathcal{O}_X(D))$;
- the superabundance $s(D)=\dim _k H^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X(D))$;
- $p_a(X)=\chi (\mathcal{O}_X)-1$.

Serre duality identifies $\dim H^2(X,\mathcal{O}_X(D))$ with $l(K-D)$, so

$\chi (\mathcal{O}_X(D)) = l(D)-s(D)+l(K-D)$.

All Euler characteristics and later Riemann–Roch equalities are integer-valued.
