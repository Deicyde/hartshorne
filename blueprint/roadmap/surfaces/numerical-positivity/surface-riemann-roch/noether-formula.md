# Noether's Chern-number formula

For a Chapter V surface with canonical class `K` and second tangent-sheaf
Chern number `c_2`,

`12*(1+p_a(X)) = K^2+c_2`.

Hartshorne records this as a generalized Riemann–Roch consequence rather
than proving it in V.1.
