# Cohomology stabilization and global generation

Let `D` be effective on a Chapter V surface, put `L=O_X(D)`, and assume
`L|_D` is ample. Then `L^n` is generated by global sections for every
sufficiently large `n`.

## Proof depends on

- Tensor `0->L^(-1)->O_X->O_D->0` by `L^n`. Vanishing of
  `H^1(D,L^n|_D)` makes the dimensions of `H^1(X,L^n)` eventually
  nonincreasing and hence constant, so restriction of global sections to `D`
  is eventually surjective.
- Lift generators from `D`; the canonical section of `L` generates away from
  `D`, and Nakayama gives generation along `D`.
