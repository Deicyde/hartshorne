# Necessity of the numerical conditions

If `D` is ample on a Chapter V surface, then

`D^2>0`

and `D*C>0` for every irreducible curve `C`.

## Depends on

- [Intersection with an ample divisor is positive degree](../surface-riemann-roch/ample-intersection-degree-positive.md)
- [An ample sheaf has a very ample power](../../../schemes/projective-geometry/linear-systems-ampleness/ample-has-very-ample-power.md)

## Proof depends on

- For a very ample multiple `mD`, the numbers `(mD)^2` and `(mD)*C` are
  respectively the projective degrees of the surface and the curve.
