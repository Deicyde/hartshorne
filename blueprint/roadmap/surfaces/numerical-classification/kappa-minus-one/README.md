# Kodaira dimension minus one and rationality

This chapter records Hartshorne's classification of Kodaira-dimension-minus-one
surfaces, Castelnuovo's rationality criterion, and the separable surface
analogue of Lüroth's theorem.  All statements use an algebraically closed
ground field.  The surface-Lüroth theorem is explicitly separable; its
inseparable failure is a separate claim.

- [The twelfth plurigenus detects Kodaira dimension minus one](kappa-minus-one-p12-criterion.md)
- [Kodaira-dimension-minus-one surfaces are rational or ruled](kappa-minus-one-rational-ruled-classification.md)
- [Castelnuovo's rationality criterion](castelnuovo-rationality-criterion.md)
- [Separable generically finite maps descend surface invariants](separable-generically-finite-invariant-descent.md)
- [The separable surface Lüroth theorem](separable-surface-luroth.md)
- [Surface Lüroth fails without separability](inseparable-surface-luroth-failure.md)

The one-variable theorem `FieldTheory.RatFunc.Luroth` is not evidence for the
dimension-two results in this chapter.
