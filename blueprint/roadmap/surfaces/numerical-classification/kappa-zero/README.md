# Kodaira dimension zero

For relatively minimal surfaces in characteristic different from two and
three, Hartshorne identifies Kodaira dimension zero with torsion canonical
class and quotes the exhaustive four-type classification.  This chapter
keeps the four surface structures distinct and separates elementary numerical
consequences from the external classification roots.

- [The four surface-type interfaces](surface-type-definition-api.md)
- [Kodaira dimension zero and the twelfth canonical class](kappa-zero-twelve-canonical-torsion.md)
- [Numerical invariants of a K3 surface](k3-surface-numerical-invariants.md)
- [Numerical data of an Enriques surface](enriques-surface-numerical-data.md)
- [Abelian and hyperelliptic surface data](abelian-hyperelliptic-surface-data.md)
- [The exhaustive Kodaira-dimension-zero classification](kappa-zero-surface-classification.md)

The characteristic restriction excludes the exceptional and quasi-
hyperelliptic behavior of characteristics two and three.
