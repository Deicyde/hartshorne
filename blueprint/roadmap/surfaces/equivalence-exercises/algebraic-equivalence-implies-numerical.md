# Algebraic equivalence implies numerical equivalence

Algebraically equivalent divisors on a Chapter-V surface have the same
intersection number with every divisor. In particular, algebraic equivalence
implies numerical equivalence.

## Proof depends on

- For a very ample divisor `H`, flatness makes the Hilbert polynomial—and
  hence the intersection with `H`—constant in a family.
- Move an arbitrary test divisor to a difference of very ample divisors.
