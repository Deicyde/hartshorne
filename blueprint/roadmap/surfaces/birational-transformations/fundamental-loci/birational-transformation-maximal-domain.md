# The maximal domain of a birational transformation

For a birational transformation `T:X dashedrightarrow Y` of projective
varieties, compatible morphism representatives glue to a unique largest open
subscheme `Dom(T)` on which `T` is a morphism. Define the fundamental locus
to be the closed-set complement `X minus Dom(T)` and its closed points to be
the fundamental points of `T`.
