# Factoring through one point blowup lowers the contracted-curve count by one

In the factorization `f=pi after f_1` supplied by a fundamental point of
`f^(-1)`,

`n(f_1)=n(f)-1`.

Exactly one irreducible curve of `X'` dominates the exceptional curve of
`pi`; every other curve contracted by `f` remains contracted by `f_1`.

## Depends on

- [A fundamental point forces factorization through its blowup](fundamental-point-factor-through-blowup.md)
- [Only finitely many curves are contracted](contracted-curves-finite.md)

## Proof depends on

- The inverse birational transformation `f_1^(-1)` is defined away from
  finitely many points of the exceptional curve, so its generic point has a
  unique irreducible closure upstairs.
