# A contracted curve has negative self-intersection

Let `f:X -> X_0` be a morphism from a nonsingular projective surface to a
projective surface of dimension two. If an irreducible curve `Y` is exactly
the fibre over a point, then `Y^2<0`.

## Depends on

- [The divisor intersection pairing](../../intersection-theory/foundations/divisor-intersection-pairing.md)

## Proof depends on

- Pull back one very ample divisor through the contraction point and another
  avoiding it. Their difference is principal downstairs; comparison of the
  strict transforms forces the exceptional self-intersection to be negative.
