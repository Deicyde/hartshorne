# The original Castelnuovo contraction image is already normal

The finite normalization map $X_0\to X_1$ in Castelnuovo's construction is an
isomorphism.  Thus the image of the morphism defined by
$\mathcal{O}_X(H+kY)$ is already the nonsingular contraction target.

Sections of $A \otimes I_Y$ lift a basis of
$H^0(Y,A \otimes I_Y/I_Y^2) \simeq H^0(\mathbb{P}^1,\mathcal{O}(1))$.  They are hyperplanes through
the image point and generate the maximal ideal after normalization.  The
finite local extension is therefore an equality by Nakayama's lemma.
