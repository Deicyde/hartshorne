# The original Castelnuovo contraction image is already normal

The finite normalization map `X_0->X_1` in Castelnuovo's construction is an
isomorphism.  Thus the image of the morphism defined by
`O_X(H+kY)` is already the nonsingular contraction target.

Sections of `A tensor I_Y` lift a basis of
`H^0(Y,A tensor I_Y/I_Y^2) ~= H^0(P^1,O(1))`.  They are hyperplanes through
the image point and generate the maximal ideal after normalization.  The
finite local extension is therefore an equality by Nakayama's lemma.
