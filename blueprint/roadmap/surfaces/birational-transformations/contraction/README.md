# Contraction of curves on surfaces

This chapter proves Castelnuovo's criterion for contracting a smooth rational
curve of self-intersection minus one, constructs elementary transformations of
ruled surfaces, and records the boundary between algebraic and analytic
contraction.  The algebraic results are over an algebraically closed field of
arbitrary characteristic.  Grauert's theorem is confined to the complex
analytic leaf.
