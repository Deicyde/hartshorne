# Existence of relatively minimal models

Every nonsingular projective surface admits a birational morphism to a
relatively minimal surface.

## Depends on

- [Relative minimality and exceptional curves](relative-minimal-surface-criterion.md)
- [Orthogonality bounds the length of a contraction sequence](contraction-orthogonality-cohomology-bound.md)

## Proof depends on

- Contract an exceptional curve whenever one exists. The cohomological bound
  prevents an infinite sequence, and the terminal surface has no exceptional
  curve of the first kind.
