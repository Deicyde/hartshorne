# Positive-genus ruled surfaces are relatively minimal

Let `C` have genus `g>0`. Every geometrically ruled surface over `C` is
relatively minimal.

## Depends on

- [Relative minimality and exceptional curves](relative-minimal-surface-criterion.md)
- [Geometrically ruled surfaces](../../ruled-surfaces/foundations/geometrically-ruled-surface.md)
- [Genus is monotone under finite curve maps](../../../curves/ramification/finite-curve-map-genus-monotonicity.md)

## Proof depends on

- A rational curve on the ruled surface maps constantly to the positive-genus
  base, hence is a fibre and has square zero; therefore no `(-1)`-curve exists.
