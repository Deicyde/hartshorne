# Line positivity, ampleness, and very ampleness

For a divisor `D` on the marked cubic surface, the following are equivalent:

1. `D` is very ample;
2. `D` is ample;
3. `D^2>0` and `D.L>0` for every one of the twenty-seven lines `L`;
4. `D.L>0` for every one of the twenty-seven lines `L`.

The easy implications use the necessary direction of the Nakai--Moishezon
criterion.  For the last implication, put `D` into the greedy blowup marking
and apply the numerical very-ampleness lemma.
