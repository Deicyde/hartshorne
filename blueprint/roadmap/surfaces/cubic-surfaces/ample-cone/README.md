# The ample cone of a marked cubic surface

The symmetry of the twenty-seven lines reduces very ampleness to explicit
intersection inequalities.  All statements concern the fixed cubic surface
obtained from six plane points in cubic general position and are valid over an
algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic.

- [The standard basepoint-free basis](cubic-standard-basepointfree-basis.md)
- [The numerical very-ampleness lemma](cubic-numerical-very-ampleness-lemma.md)
- [The greedy skew-sextuple marking](cubic-greedy-skew-sextuple.md)
- [Line positivity, ampleness, and very ampleness](cubic-line-positivity-ampleness-equivalence.md)
- [Coordinate inequalities and smooth members](cubic-ample-cone-smooth-members.md)
- [A smooth curve of degree seven and genus five](cubic-degree-seven-genus-five.md)

The smooth-member result uses the all-characteristic hyperplane Bertini
theorem and connectedness of an ample divisor.  It does not depend on the
characteristic-zero generic-smoothness roadmap.
