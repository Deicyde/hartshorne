# Classification of embedded Del Pezzo surfaces

Over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic, every
embedded Del Pezzo surface is isomorphic either to one of the anticanonically
embedded plane blowups of degrees three through nine or to the two-uple
embedding of a smooth quadric. In particular every nonsingular cubic surface
in `P^3` is obtained by blowing up six plane points with no three collinear
and not all six on a conic.
