# The intersection lattice of a marked cubic surface

In the Picard basis `l,e_1,...,e_6`,

`l^2=1`, `e_i^2=-1`, `l.e_i=0`, and `e_i.e_j=0` for `i!=j`.

Thus the intersection form is the diagonal integral form
`diag(1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1)`.
