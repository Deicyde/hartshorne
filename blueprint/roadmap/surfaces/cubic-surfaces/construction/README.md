# Del Pezzo and cubic-surface construction

This subchapter applies the very ample cubic system to blowups of at most six
ordinary plane points. It constructs the anticanonical embeddings and records
the marked blowup coordinates used for divisors on the resulting cubic
surface. All project-proved statements are valid in arbitrary characteristic.

1. [The anticanonical embedding of a plane point blowup](anticanonical-point-blowup-embedding.md)
2. [Embedded Del Pezzo surfaces from plane blowups](embedded-del-pezzo-definition-examples.md)
3. [Classification of embedded Del Pezzo surfaces](embedded-del-pezzo-classification.md)
4. [Generic dominance of the six-point cubic construction](generic-cubic-construction-dominance.md)
5. [The marked cubic-surface blowup model](cubic-marked-blowup-convention.md)
6. [The Picard group of the marked cubic surface](cubic-surface-picard-group.md)
7. [The intersection lattice of the marked cubic surface](cubic-surface-intersection-lattice.md)
8. [Hyperplane and canonical classes](cubic-surface-hyperplane-canonical-classes.md)
9. [Degree, self-intersection, and arithmetic genus](cubic-divisor-degree-square-genus.md)
10. [Curves as strict transforms of plane curves](cubic-plane-strict-transform-dictionary.md)

The Del Pezzo classification and the generic-dominance conclusion are
source-blocked; the dimension count alone does not prove dominance.
