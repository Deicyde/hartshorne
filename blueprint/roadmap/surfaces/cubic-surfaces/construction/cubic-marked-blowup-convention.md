# The marked cubic-surface blowup model

Fix six ordinary points `P_1,...,P_6` of `P^2`, no three collinear and not
all six on a conic. Package the cubic surface `X` given by the transformed
cubic system together with its blowdown `pi:X->P^2`, exceptional curves
`E_i`, their classes `e_i`, and the pullback class `l` of a plane line.

The marking is data: later configuration symmetries may replace it by another
six-point blowdown of the same abstract cubic surface.
