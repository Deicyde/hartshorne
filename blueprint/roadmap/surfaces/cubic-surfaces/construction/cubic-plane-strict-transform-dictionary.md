# Curves on a marked cubic surface as strict transforms of plane curves

Let $C$ be an irreducible curve on the marked cubic surface other than an
exceptional curve. Its image is an irreducible plane curve $C _0$ of degree
$a$; if $C _0$ has multiplicity $b_i\ge 0$ at $P_i$, then

$C$ is the strict transform of $C_0$, and $[C ]=a\cdot l-\sum _i b_i\cdot e_i$.

Conversely, an irreducible curve in such a class is interpreted as the strict
transform of its plane image with the indicated multiplicities.
