# The transformed cubic system through at most six general points is very ample

Let `P_1,...,P_r`, `r<=6`, be ordinary points of `P^2`, with no three
collinear and no six on a conic. On their iterated blowup, the complete
system

`|3*l-E_1-...-E_r|`

is very ample.

## Depends on

- [Very ampleness via unassigned base points](very-ample-unassigned-basepoint-criterion.md)
- [Cubics through at most seven general assigned points have no extra base points](cubic-system-no-unassigned-basepoints.md)

## Proof depends on

- The system has no unassigned base points. Adding any ordinary or infinitely
  near test point still gives at most seven points satisfying the weaker
  no-four/no-seven hypotheses, so the once-assigned system has none either.
