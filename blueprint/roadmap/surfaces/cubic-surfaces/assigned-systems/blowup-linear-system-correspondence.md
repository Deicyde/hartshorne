# Assigned-point systems correspond to complete systems on blowups

Let `pi:X'->X` be the iterated blowup of assigned points with multiplicities
`m_i`. Write `E_i^*` for the total (virtual) transform on the final surface
of the exceptional divisor created at the `i`th stage. The map

`C |-> pi^*C-sum_i m_i*E_i^*`

induces a bijection between the assigned subsystem downstairs and the complete system

`|pi^*D-sum_i m_i*E_i^*|`

upstairs. If `C` has actual multiplicity `n_i>m_i`, its image retains the
surplus exceptional component `(n_i-m_i)E_i^*`; it is not merely the strict
transform. A divisor upstairs is effective exactly when the downstairs
divisor satisfies all assigned ordinary and infinitely-near conditions. The
proximity inequality compares an assigned point only with the points directly
proximate to it.
