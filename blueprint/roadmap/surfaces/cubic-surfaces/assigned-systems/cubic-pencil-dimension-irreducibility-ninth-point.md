# Eight assigned points determine a cubic pencil and a ninth base point

Under the no-four-collinear and no-seven-on-a-conic hypotheses, cubics
through `r<=8` ordinary assigned points have projective dimension `9-r`. For eight
points, all but finitely many members of the pencil are irreducible, and
there is a unique ninth base point, possibly infinitely near, through which
every member passes.

The ninth-point conclusion includes Hartshorne's stated configurations with
`P_2` infinitely near `P_1` and `P_8` infinitely near one of the first seven.
It does not assert the result for an arbitrary longer proximity cluster.

## Proof depends on

- Independent assigned conditions give the dimension. Only finitely many
  unions of three lines or of a line and irreducible conic pass through all
  eight points. Two irreducible pencil members have a residual ninth
  intersection point, shared by every linear combination.
