# Conics through three points resolve the quadratic transformation

Let `P_1,P_2,P_3` be the three coordinate points of `P^2`, and blow them up.
The transformed conic system is basepoint-free of projective dimension two
and defines a morphism to a second `P^2`. Away from the assigned points it is
the quadratic transformation

`[x_0:x_1:x_2] |-> [x_1*x_2:x_0*x_2:x_0*x_1]`.
