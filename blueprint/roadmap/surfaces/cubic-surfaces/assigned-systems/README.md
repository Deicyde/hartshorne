# Assigned-point systems on the plane

This subchapter packages assigned and unassigned base points on iterated
surface blowups, then applies that language to conics, the quadratic
transformation, and cubics through points. Infinitely near points are genuine
points of successive blowups, and all results are valid over an algebraically
closed field of arbitrary characteristic.

1. [Assigned and unassigned base points](assigned-unassigned-basepoint-api.md)
2. [Linear systems on point blowups](blowup-linear-system-correspondence.md)
3. [Very ampleness via unassigned base points](very-ample-unassigned-basepoint-criterion.md)
4. [Conics through assigned points](conic-assigned-points-system.md)
5. [The quadratic transformation from conics](quadratic-transformation-conic-morphism.md)
6. [The common blowup resolving the quadratic transformation](quadratic-transformation-common-blowup.md)
7. [Cubics have no unassigned base points](cubic-system-no-unassigned-basepoints.md)
8. [The eight-point cubic pencil and its ninth point](cubic-pencil-dimension-irreducibility-ninth-point.md)
9. [Very ampleness of the transformed cubic system](general-cubic-system-very-ample.md)

The one-point conic embedding is already represented by
[The cubic ruled surface from conics through a point](../../../schemes/projective-geometry/blowups/blowup-plane-conic-system-embedding.md)
