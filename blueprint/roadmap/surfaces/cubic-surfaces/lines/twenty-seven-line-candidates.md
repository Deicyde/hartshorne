# The twenty-seven candidate lines

Let `X` be the marked cubic surface obtained by blowing up
`P_1,...,P_6` in `P^2`, with no three points collinear and not all six on a
conic.  The following twenty-seven curves have hyperplane degree one and hence
are lines in the anticanonical embedding:

- the six exceptional curves `E_i`, of class `e_i`;
- the fifteen strict transforms `F_ij` of the lines through `P_i,P_j`, of
  class `l-e_i-e_j`;
- the six strict transforms `G_i` of the conic through the five points other
  than `P_i`, of class `2l-sum_(j != i)e_j`.

The hypotheses on the six points ensure that the indicated lines and conics
exist as the required irreducible plane curves.

  fixed embedding is a projective line.
