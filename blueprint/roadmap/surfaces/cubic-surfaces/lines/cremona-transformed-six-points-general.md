# The transformed six points remain in cubic general position

Apply the standard quadratic transformation to three of the six marked plane
points.  The three opposite base points together with the images of the other
three points are distinct proper points, no three are collinear, and not all
six lie on a conic.

The conclusion uses exactly the original assumptions that no three marked
points are collinear and that the six do not all lie on a conic.  It is not a
claim about arbitrary or infinitely near centers.

## Proof depends on

- Pull a hypothetical line or conic back through the conic linear system
  defining the quadratic transformation.  Each forbidden transformed
  incidence yields either an infinitely near image, three original collinear
  points, or a conic through all six original points.
