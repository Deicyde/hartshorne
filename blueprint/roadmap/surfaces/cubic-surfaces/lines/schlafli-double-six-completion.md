# Completion and realization of a Schläfli double-six

Five sufficiently general lines $G_2,\ldots ,G_6$ meeting a line $E_1$, and
otherwise in the classical general position, uniquely determine the remaining
lines $E_2,\ldots ,E_6,G_1$ of a double-six.  Every such double-six lies on a
unique nonsingular cubic surface.
