# Completion and realization of a Schläfli double-six

Five sufficiently general lines `G_2,...,G_6` meeting a line `E_1`, and
otherwise in the classical general position, uniquely determine the remaining
lines `E_2,...,E_6,G_1` of a double-six.  Every such double-six lies on a
unique nonsingular cubic surface.

This node is not ready.  Hartshorne quotes these claims without defining the
needed general-position locus or proving either reconstruction or uniqueness.
A precise scheme-theoretic statement and proof must be adopted from the cited
classical source before formalization, including its validity in small
characteristic.
