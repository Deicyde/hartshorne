# The incidence configuration of the twenty-seven lines

Intersection in the cubic-surface lattice gives all pairwise incidence
relations:

- distinct `E_i,E_j` and distinct `G_i,G_j` are skew;
- `E_i` meets `F_jk` exactly when `i` is `j` or `k`;
- `E_i` meets `G_j` exactly when `i != j`;
- `F_ij` meets `F_kl` exactly when `i,j,k,l` are all distinct;
- `F_ij` meets `G_k` exactly when `k` is `i` or `j`.

Distinct listed lines meet precisely when their intersection number is one.
