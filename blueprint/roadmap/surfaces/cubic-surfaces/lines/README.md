# Lines on a marked cubic surface

Fix the cubic surface obtained from six points of the plane with no three
collinear and not all six on a conic.  This chapter identifies its twenty-seven
lines, changes the marking by quadratic transformations, and records the
resulting finite incidence configuration.  The core results are over an
algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic.  The two classical
external leaves retain explicit source obligations, including verification of
their intended characteristic scope.
