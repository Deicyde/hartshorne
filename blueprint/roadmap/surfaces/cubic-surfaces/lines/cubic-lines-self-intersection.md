# Every line on the cubic surface has self-intersection minus one

If `L` is a line on the marked cubic surface `X` in `P^3`, then

`L^2 = -1`.

Indeed `deg L=1` and `p_a(L)=0`, so the cubic-surface genus formula gives the
claim.
