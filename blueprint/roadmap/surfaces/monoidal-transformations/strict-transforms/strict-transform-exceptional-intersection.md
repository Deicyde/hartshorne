# Intersection with the exceptional curve

Under the total-transform hypotheses,

`D_tilde*E = mu_P(D)`.

Equivalently, the strict transform meets the exceptional projective line in
the tangent-cone divisor of total degree equal to the multiplicity.

## Depends on

- [The total-transform formula](divisor-total-transform-formula.md)
- [Exceptional orthogonality and projection formula](../foundations/exceptional-orthogonality-projection-formula.md)
- [The exceptional curve has self-intersection minus one](../foundations/exceptional-curve-self-intersection.md)

## Proof depends on

- Intersect `pi^*D=D_tilde+rE` with `E`, using `pi^*D*E=0` and `E^2=-1`.
