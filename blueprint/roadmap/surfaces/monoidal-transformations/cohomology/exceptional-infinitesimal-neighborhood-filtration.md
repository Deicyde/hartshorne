# The exceptional infinitesimal-neighborhood filtration

Let $I=\mathcal{O}_\widetilde{X}(-E)$ be the exceptional ideal and let $E_n$ be the closed
subscheme defined by $I^n$, with $E_1=E$. For every $n\ge 1$ there is an exact
sequence

$0 \to I^n/I^{n+1} \to \mathcal{O}_{(E_{(n+1)})} \to \mathcal{O}_{(E_n)} \to 0$,

and, under $E\simeq \mathbb{P}^1$,

$I^n/I^{n+1} \simeq \operatorname{Sym} ^n(I/I^2) \simeq \mathcal{O}_E(n)$.

This indexing is Hartshorne's V.3 convention and is shifted by one from the
[infinitesimal-fibre convention](../../../cohomology/formal-functions/core/infinitesimal-fibers.md)
in Hartshorne III.11.
