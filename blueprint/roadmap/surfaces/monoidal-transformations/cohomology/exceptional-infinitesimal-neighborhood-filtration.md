# The exceptional infinitesimal-neighborhood filtration

Let `I=O_X_tilde(-E)` be the exceptional ideal and let `E_n` be the closed
subscheme defined by `I^n`, with `E_1=E`. For every `n>=1` there is an exact
sequence

`0 -> I^n/I^(n+1) -> O_(E_(n+1)) -> O_(E_n) -> 0`,

and, under `E~=P^1`,

`I^n/I^(n+1) ~= Sym^n(I/I^2) ~= O_E(n)`.

This indexing is Hartshorne's V.3 convention and is shifted by one from the
III.11 infinitesimal-fibre API.
