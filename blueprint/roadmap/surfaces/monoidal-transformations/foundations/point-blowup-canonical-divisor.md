# The canonical divisor of a point blowup

If `K_X` and `K_X_tilde` are canonical divisors, then

`K_X_tilde ~ pi^*K_X + E`.

## Proof depends on

- The canonical sheaves agree off `E`, so their difference is `nE`.
  Adjunction on `E~=P^1`, together with `E^2=-1`, gives `n=1`.
