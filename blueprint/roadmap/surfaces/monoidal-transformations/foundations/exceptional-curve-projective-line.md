# The exceptional curve of a point blowup is a projective line

For the monoidal transformation of a surface at `P`, the exceptional divisor
is canonically

`E ~= P(m_P/m_P^2) ~= P^1_k`.

The first projectivization uses Hartshorne's quotient convention.
