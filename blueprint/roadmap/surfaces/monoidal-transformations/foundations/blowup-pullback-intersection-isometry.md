# Pullback by a point blowup preserves intersection numbers

For divisor classes `C,D` on `X`,

`(pi^*C).(pi^*D) = C.D`.

## Depends on

- [Monoidal transformation at a point](point-monoidal-transformation.md)
- [The divisor intersection pairing](../../intersection-theory/foundations/divisor-intersection-pairing.md)
- [Simultaneous Bertini moving](../../intersection-theory/foundations/simultaneous-bertini-moving.md)

## Proof depends on

- Move `C,D` to differences of smooth transverse curves avoiding `P`; their
  intersections are unchanged under the isomorphism away from the center.
