# The normal bundle of the exceptional curve

Under `E ~= P^1`, the normal bundle of the exceptional curve in the blown-up
surface is

`N_(E/X_tilde) ~= O_E(-1)`.

Equivalently, the conormal line bundle `I_E/I_E^2` is `O_E(1)`.
