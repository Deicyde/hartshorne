# The double-point trichotomy after blowup

Assume all irreducible components of a reduced total curve are nonsingular,
and blow up a singular point of total multiplicity two. Exactly one of these
occurs:

1. the strict transform meets the exceptional curve transversally in two
   distinct points, giving a node and requiring no further blowup;
2. it is nonsingular and tangent to the exceptional curve at one point;
   blowing up that point creates a triple point, so one additional blowup
   lowers arithmetic genus;
3. it retains a double point on the exceptional curve, and the exceptional
   curve passes through it in a direction distinct from every tangent
   direction of the strict transform; together the total multiplicity is
   three, so the next blowup lowers arithmetic genus.
