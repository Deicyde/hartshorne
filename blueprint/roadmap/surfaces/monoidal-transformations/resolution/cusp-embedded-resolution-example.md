# Embedded resolution of a cusp

For the plane cusp `y^2=x^3`, one point blowup makes the strict transform
nonsingular, but three successive point blowups are required before the
reduced total transform has normal crossings.

## Depends on

- [Embedded resolution of curves on surfaces](embedded-resolution-curves-surfaces.md)
- [Local charts of a point blowup](../strict-transforms/point-blowup-local-charts.md)

## Proof depends on

- Track the strict and exceptional equations on the standard blowup charts;
  the first transform is smooth but remains tangent to the exceptional curve,
  and the next transform has a triple intersection.
