# Embedded resolution of curves on surfaces

Let `Y` be any effective curve on a Chapter V surface. There is a finite
succession of point blowups `f : X' -> X` such that the reduced total inverse
image `f^(-1)(Y)` is a normal-crossings divisor.

The theorem is valid over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary
characteristic.

## Proof depends on

- Work componentwise on each connected component and discard initial
  multiplicities. After component resolution, every non-node singularity
  causes, within at most two blowups, a strict decrease of the nonnegative
  arithmetic genus. Hence the process terminates with only transverse nodes.
