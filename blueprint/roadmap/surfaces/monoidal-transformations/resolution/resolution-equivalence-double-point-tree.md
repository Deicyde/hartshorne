# Resolution equivalence and double-point trees

Define two reduced curve singularities on smooth surfaces to be equivalent
when embedded-resolution towers admit compatible bijections of reduced total
transform components and singular points, preserving multiplicities,
incidence, and all transition maps. This is an equivalence relation and is
independent of harmless refinements by additional blowups.

For a double point, its equivalence class is determined recursively by the
number of blowups before the strict transform becomes smooth and whether the
final configuration is transverse nodal or tangent cuspidal.
