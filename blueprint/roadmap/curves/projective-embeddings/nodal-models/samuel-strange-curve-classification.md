# Samuel's classification of strange curves

Over an algebraically closed field, the only strange projective curves are a
line, in arbitrary characteristic, and a smooth plane conic in characteristic
two.

## Proof depends on

- Projection down to `P^3` preserves the relevant strange-point incidence.
- The two numerical possibilities from Samuel's calculation give exactly the
  basic examples.
