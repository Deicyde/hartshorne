# One projection lowers the ambient dimension

Let `X -> P^n` be an embedded Chapter IV curve with `n>=4`. There exists a
point `O` outside `X` such that projection from `O` is a closed immersion

`X -> P^(n-1)`.

The statement is valid over every algebraically closed field, with no
separability or characteristic-zero hypothesis.

## Depends on

- [Projection is an embedding away from secants and tangents](projection-closed-immersion-criterion.md)
- [The secant incidence has dimension at most three](secant-incidence-dimension.md)
- [The tangent variety is closed of dimension at most two](tangent-variety-closed-dimension.md)
- [Proper closed subsets of projective varieties have smaller dimension](../../../intersections-projective-space/dimension/projective-proper-closed-dimension-drop.md)

## Proof depends on

- The union of the secant and tangent loci has dimension at most three and
  therefore cannot fill `P^n` when `n>=4`.
