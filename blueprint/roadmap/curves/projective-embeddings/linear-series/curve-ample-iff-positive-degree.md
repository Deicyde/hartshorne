# A curve divisor is ample exactly when its degree is positive

For a divisor `D` on a Chapter IV curve,

`D is ample <-> deg D > 0`.

## Depends on

- [High-degree divisors are generated and very ample](high-degree-basepoint-free-very-ample.md)
- [An ample sheaf has a very ample power](../../../schemes/projective-geometry/linear-systems-ampleness/ample-has-very-ample-power.md)
- [Ampleness and tensor powers](../../../schemes/projective-geometry/linear-systems-ampleness/ample-power-equivalence.md)
- [Embedded degree equals divisor degree](embedded-curve-degree-compatibility.md)

## Proof depends on

- A positive tensor power of an ample divisor is very ample and hence has
  positive hyperplane-section degree.
- If `deg D>0`, a sufficiently large multiple has degree at least `2g+1` and
  is very ample.
