# Curves of degree two and three

Every curve of projective degree two is a conic in a projective plane. Every
curve of degree three is either a plane cubic or a twisted cubic in `P^3`,
after replacing the ambient projective space by the linear span of the curve.

## Depends on

- [Minimal-degree curves are rational normal](rational-normal-curve-minimal-degree.md)
- [The Chapter IV curve convention](../../riemann-roch/curve-convention.md)

## Proof depends on

- Apply the minimal-degree theorem when the span has dimension at least the
  degree; the only remaining degree-three span is the plane.
