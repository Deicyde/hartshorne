# Later-used projective-embedding exercises

Exercises IV.3.4(b)–(d), IV.3.6(a)–(b), and IV.3.9 are retained. They supply
the rational-normal and degree-four classifications and the general-plane
section used in later running text; the other IV.3 exercises remain outside
the adopted scope.

1. [Minimal-degree curves are rational normal](rational-normal-curve-minimal-degree.md)
2. [Curves of degree two and three](conic-cubic-low-degree-corollaries.md)
3. [The degree-four curve trichotomy](degree-four-curve-trichotomy.md)
4. [Elliptic quartics are intersections of two quadrics](elliptic-quartic-two-quadrics.md)
Exercise 3.9 is indexed separately under the downstream
[Castelnuovo prerequisites](../castelnuovo/README.md).
