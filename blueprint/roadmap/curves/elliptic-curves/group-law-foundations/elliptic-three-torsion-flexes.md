# Three-torsion and flexes

In the plane cubic embedding by `|3P_0|`, a point has order dividing three
exactly when it is an inflection point. If the characteristic is different
from two and three, there are exactly nine such points, forming the point
group `(Z/3Z)^2`; a line through two flexes meets the cubic in a third flex.

## Proof depends on

- A tangent has triple contact exactly when its intersection divisor is
  `3P~3P_0`.
- On a Weierstrass model, the pinned three-division polynomial has degree
  four when `3` is nonzero; separability of `[3]` gives four distinct
  `x`-coordinates, hence eight nonzero points together with the identity.
