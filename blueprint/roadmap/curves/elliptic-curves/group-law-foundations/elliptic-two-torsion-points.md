# Two-torsion and the degree-two pencil

A point `P` on a pointed elliptic curve has order dividing two exactly when
`2P~2P_0`, equivalently when it is a ramification point of the degree-two map
defined by `|2P_0|`. There are finitely many such points; in characteristic
different from two there are exactly four and their point group is
`(Z/2Z)^2`.

- The degree-two deck involution fixes exactly its simple ramification points;
