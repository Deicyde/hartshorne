# Multiplication by three

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two and
three, `[3] : X -> X` is finite of degree nine and its group of closed kernel
points is isomorphic to `(Z/3Z)^2`.

## Depends on

- [Integer multiplication is a morphism](../group-law-foundations/elliptic-integer-multiplication-morphism.md)
- [Three-torsion and flexes](../group-law-foundations/elliptic-three-torsion-flexes.md)

## Proof depends on

- The nine flexes are the kernel points; translation identifies fibres and
  separability gives degree nine.
