# The j=1728 congruence

For the elliptic curve `y^2=x^3-x`, reduction at every odd prime is
nonsingular, and its Hasse invariant is zero exactly for primes
`p congruent 3 mod 4`; it is one for primes `p congruent 1 mod 4`.

## Depends on

- [The plane-cubic coefficient criterion](plane-cubic-hasse-coefficient.md)

## Proof depends on

- The relevant coefficient of `(x^2-1)^((p-1)/2)` vanishes exactly when the
  half-exponent is odd.
