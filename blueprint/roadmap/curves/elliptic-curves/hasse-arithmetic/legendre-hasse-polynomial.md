# The Legendre Hasse polynomial

Assume $p \ne 2$, put $m=(p-1)/2$, and let

$X_\lambda : y^2 z = x(x-z)(x-\lambda \cdot z)$

with $\lambda \ne 0,1$. Define

$h_p(\lambda)=\sum_{i=0}^{m}\binom{m}{i}^{2}\lambda^i$.

Then $X_\lambda$ has Hasse invariant zero exactly when
$h_p(\lambda )=0$.

## Proof depends on

- Extract the coefficient of $(xyz)^{p-1}$ from the two factors
  $(y^2z)^m$ and $(x(x-z)(x-\lambda \cdot z))^m$.
