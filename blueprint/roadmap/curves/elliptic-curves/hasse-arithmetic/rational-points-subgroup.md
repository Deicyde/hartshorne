# Rational points form a subgroup

Let a pointed plane elliptic curve `(X,P_0)` and its cubic equation be defined
over a subfield `k_0` of the algebraically closed ground field. Then the set
`X(k_0)` is closed under the geometric group law and forms a subgroup of
`X(k)`.

## Depends on

- [A pointed elliptic curve is a group variety](../group-law-foundations/elliptic-group-variety.md)

## Proof depends on

- The chord-and-tangent group law and the chosen origin are defined over
  `k_0`.
