# A non-CM Hasse example

For `X : y^2=x(x-1)(x+2)`, the model is nonsingular away from two and three,
its `j`-invariant is not an integer and hence it has no complex
multiplication, and among primes at most `73` the only supersingular prime is
`23`.

## Depends on

- [The Legendre Hasse polynomial](legendre-hasse-polynomial.md)
- [Class-field facts for complex multiplication](../complex-multiplication/complex-multiplication-class-field-facts.md)

## Proof depends on

- Direct evaluation of the Hasse polynomial modulo the finitely many primes
  in the stated range.
