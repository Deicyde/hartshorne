# CM reduction and Deuring's criterion

Let a complex elliptic curve with complex multiplication be defined over a
number field. At a prime of good reduction, the reduction is supersingular
exactly when the rational prime ramifies or remains prime in the associated
imaginary quadratic CM field. The set of supersingular rational primes has
density one half.
