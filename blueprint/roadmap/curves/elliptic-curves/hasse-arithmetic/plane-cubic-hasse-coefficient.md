# The plane-cubic coefficient criterion

Let $X \subset \mathbb{P}^2_k$ be an elliptic plane cubic with homogeneous equation
$f=0$ over a field of characteristic $p>2$. Its Hasse invariant is zero if
and only if the coefficient of $(x\cdot y\cdot z)^{p-1}$ in $f^{p-1}$ is zero.

## Proof depends on

- Identify $H^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$ with $H^2(\mathbb{P}^2,\mathcal{O}(-3))$, generated by
  $(xyz)^{-1}$, and compute Frobenius through the diagram for
  $\mathcal{O}(-3p) \to O \to \mathcal{O}_{(X^p)}$.
