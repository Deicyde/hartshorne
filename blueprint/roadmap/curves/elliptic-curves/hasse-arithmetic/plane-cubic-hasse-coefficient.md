# The plane-cubic coefficient criterion

Let `X subset P^2_k` be an elliptic plane cubic with homogeneous equation
`f=0` over a field of characteristic `p>2`. Its Hasse invariant is zero if
and only if the coefficient of `(x*y*z)^(p-1)` in `f^(p-1)` is zero.

## Proof depends on

- Identify `H^1(X,O_X)` with `H^2(P^2,O(-3))`, generated by
  `(xyz)^(-1)`, and compute Frobenius through the diagram for
  `O(-3p) -> O -> O_(X^p)`.
