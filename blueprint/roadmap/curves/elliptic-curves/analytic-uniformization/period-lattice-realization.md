# Every complex elliptic curve comes from a period lattice

Let $c_2,c_3 \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfy $\Delta =c_2^3-27\cdot c_3^2 \ne 0$. There exist a nonreal
period ratio $\tau$ and $a \in \mathbb{C}$, $a \ne 0$, such that the lattice generated by
$1,\tau$ has

$g_2=a^4\cdot c_2$ and $g_3=a^6\cdot c_3$.

Consequently every elliptic curve over $\mathbb{C}$ is isomorphic to a Weierstrass
cubic obtained from a complex lattice.
