# Every complex elliptic curve comes from a period lattice

Let `c2,c3 in C` satisfy `Delta=c2^3-27*c3^2 != 0`. There exist a nonreal
period ratio `tau` and `a in C`, `a != 0`, such that the lattice generated by
`1,tau` has

`g2=a^4*c2` and `g3=a^6*c3`.

Consequently every elliptic curve over `C` is isomorphic to a Weierstrass
cubic obtained from a complex lattice.
