# Complex lattices and elliptic functions

For $\tau\in\mathbb{C}\setminus\mathbb{R}$, let
$\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}+\mathbb{Z}\tau$. The complex torus
$\mathbb{C}/\Lambda$ carries the field of meromorphic functions invariant
under translation by $\Lambda$.

The Weierstrass function $\wp$ and its derivative $\wp'$ are elliptic
functions for this lattice. Their poles and periodicity, the lattice
invariants $g_2,g_3$, and divisors of elliptic functions describe the geometry
of $\mathbb{C}/\Lambda$.
