# Countability of complex-multiplication invariants

Only countably many complex values of the `j`-invariant correspond to
elliptic curves with complex multiplication.

## Depends on

- [Classification of complex multiplication orders](complex-multiplication-order-classification.md)
- [The modular invariant and fundamental region](../analytic-uniformization/modular-j-fundamental-domain.md)

## Proof depends on

- There are only countably many imaginary quadratic numbers and hence only
  countably many relevant period-ratio classes.
