# The square, hexagonal, and nonmaximal examples

For the square lattice `tau=i`, the endomorphism ring is `Z[i]`, the
`j`-invariant is `1728`, and a model has the form `y^2=x^3-A*x`.

For a primitive cube root `omega`, the endomorphism ring is `Z[omega]`, the
`j`-invariant is zero, and a model has the form `y^2=x^3-B`.

For `tau=2*i`, the endomorphism ring is the proper order `Z[2*i]` of
conductor two in `Q(i)`.

  their extra units forces respectively `g3=0` and `g2=0`.
