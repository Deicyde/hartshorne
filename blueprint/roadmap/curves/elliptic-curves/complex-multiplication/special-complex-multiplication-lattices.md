# The square, hexagonal, and nonmaximal examples

For the square lattice $\tau =i$, the endomorphism ring is $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, the
$j$-invariant is $1728$, and a model has the form $y^2=x^3-A\cdot x$.

For a primitive cube root $\omega$, the endomorphism ring is $\mathbb{Z}[\omega ]$, the
$j$-invariant is zero, and a model has the form $y^2=x^3-B$.

For $\tau =2\cdot i$, the endomorphism ring is the proper order $\mathbb{Z}[2\cdot i]$ of
conductor two in $Q(i)$.
