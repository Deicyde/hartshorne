# The abstract group and torsion of a complex elliptic curve

For an elliptic curve `X` over `C`, analytic uniformization gives an abstract
group isomorphism

`X(C) ~= R/Z times R/Z`.

For every positive integer `n`, the subgroup killed by `n` is

`X[n] ~= Z/nZ times Z/nZ`.

## Depends on

- [Compatibility with the elliptic-curve group law](../analytic-uniformization/complex-uniformization-group-law.md)

## Proof depends on

- Express a complex number uniquely in the real basis formed by the two
  periods and pass to the quotient by the integer lattice.
