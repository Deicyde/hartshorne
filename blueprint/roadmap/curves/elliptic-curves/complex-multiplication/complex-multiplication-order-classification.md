# Classification of complex multiplication orders

Let $X(\mathbb C)=\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\tau)$. If $\operatorname{End}(X,P_0)$ is larger than $\mathbb Z$, then $\tau$ belongs
to an imaginary quadratic field and the endomorphism ring is an order in the
ring of integers of that field.

Conversely, write $\tau=r+s\sqrt{-d}$ with $r,s\in\mathbb Q$, $d>0$. Then $X$ has
complex multiplication and

$$\operatorname{End}(X,P_0)=
\left\{a+b\tau\;\middle|\;
a,b\in\mathbb Z,\quad 2br\in\mathbb Z,\quad
b(r^2+ds^2)\in\mathbb Z\right\}.$$

## Proof depends on

- Writing $\alpha =a+b\cdot \tau$ and $\alpha \cdot \tau =c+e\cdot \tau$ gives quadratic equations
  for both $\tau$ and $\alpha$; the latter shows every endomorphism is integral
  over $\mathbb Z$.
