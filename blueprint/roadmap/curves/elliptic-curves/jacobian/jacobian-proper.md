# The Jacobian is proper

A Jacobian representing the degree-zero Picard functor of a Chapter IV curve
is proper over the base field.

## Depends on

- [Existence and the universal property of the Jacobian](jacobian-existence-universal-property.md)
- [DVR-valuative criterion for properness](../../../schemes/proper-and-projective/dvr-valuative-criterion-proper.md)
- [Pullback on Picard groups](../../../schemes/divisors/divisor-exercises/picard-pullback.md)

## Proof depends on

- Extend a degree-zero line-bundle class over the generic point of a DVR to
  the regular surface obtained from the curve by base change, then use the
  representing property; separatedness gives uniqueness.
