# The Jacobian is a commutative group scheme

For a Jacobian `J` representing `Pic^0(X/-)`, tensor product, dual line
bundles, and the trivial class induce multiplication, inverse, and identity
morphisms making `J` a commutative group scheme. Under
`J(k) ~= Pic^0(X)`, these operations are the usual Picard-group operations.
