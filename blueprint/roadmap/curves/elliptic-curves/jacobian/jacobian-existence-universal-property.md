# Existence and the universal property of the Jacobian

For every Chapter IV curve $X$, there is a finite-type scheme $J$ and a
universal relative degree-zero line-bundle class on $X \times J$ such that

$\operatorname{Hom} (T,J) \simeq \operatorname{Pic} ^0(X/T)$

naturally for every finite-type parameter scheme $T$. The representing pair
is unique up to unique isomorphism, and $J(k)$ identifies with $\operatorname{Pic} ^0(X)$.
