# Existence and the universal property of the Jacobian

For every Chapter IV curve `X`, there is a finite-type scheme `J` and a
universal relative degree-zero line-bundle class on `X times J` such that

`Hom(T,J) ~= Pic^0(X/T)`

naturally for every finite-type parameter scheme `T`. The representing pair
is unique up to unique isomorphism, and `J(k)` identifies with `Pic^0(X)`.

This source-obligation node is not ready. Hartshorne defines the representing
object and states its general existence without proving it in this section;
a proof-level Picard-scheme/Jacobian construction must be adopted. The later
elliptic-curve theorem gives an independent explicit representation in genus
one and does not depend on this general existence node.
