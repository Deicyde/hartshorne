# Curves on a quadric cone

Let `X` be a Chapter-IV curve on a quadric cone in `P^3`.

- If `deg X = 2*a`, then `X` is the complete intersection of the cone with a
  surface of degree `a`, and `g(X)=a^2-2*a+1`.
- If `deg X = 2*a+1`, then `g(X)=a^2-a`.

This is the forward input quoted in Remark IV.6.4.1(d). Its source unit is
deliberately omitted until V Exercise 2.9 is inventoried.
