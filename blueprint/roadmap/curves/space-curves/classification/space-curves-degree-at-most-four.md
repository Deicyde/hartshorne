# Curves of degree at most four

The nonsingular curves of degree at most four in projective three-space are:

- degree one: a line;
- degree two: a plane conic;
- degree three: a plane cubic or a twisted cubic;
- degree four: a plane quartic, a rational quartic, or an elliptic quartic,
  with the elliptic quartic a complete intersection of two irreducible
  quadrics.
