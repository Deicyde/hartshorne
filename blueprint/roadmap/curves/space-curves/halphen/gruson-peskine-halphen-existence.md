# The Gruson–Peskine proof of Halphen's existence classification

Adopt a precise modern statement and proof of Halphen's general
classification of the pairs $(d,g)$ for which a nonsingular degree-$d$,
genus-$g$ curve exists in $\mathbb{P}^3$.

Hartshorne reports that Gruson–Peskine supplied a correct proof of
Halphen's classification; the full numerical criterion is not stated here.
