# The degree bound for a special hyperplane section

Let `X subset P^3` be a nonplanar curve and let `D=O_X(1)` be special. Then

`d=deg D >= 6`.

## Depends on

- [Clifford's inequality](../../canonical-curves/clifford/clifford-inequality.md)
- [Embedded degree equals divisor degree](../../projective-embeddings/linear-series/embedded-curve-degree-compatibility.md)

## Proof depends on

- Clifford gives `dim|D|<=d/2`; nonplanarity and very ampleness give
  `dim|D|>=3`.
