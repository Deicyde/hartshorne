# Inventory of known space-curve constructions

The following families have the stated degrees and genera:

1. smooth plane degree-$d$ curves, genus $(d-1)\cdot (d-2)/2$;
2. smooth complete intersections of degrees $(a,b)$ in $\mathbb{P}^3$, degree $a\cdot b$
   and genus $a\cdot b\cdot (a+b-4)/2+1$;
3. smooth type-$(a,b)$ curves on a nonsingular quadric, degree $a+b$ and
   genus $a\cdot b-a-b+1$;
4. the corresponding even- and odd-degree curves on a quadric cone described
   in V Exercise 2.9.
