# Inventory of known space-curve constructions

Record the following existing families and their degree/genus formulas:

1. smooth plane degree-`d` curves, genus `(d-1)*(d-2)/2`;
2. smooth complete intersections of degrees `(a,b)` in `P^3`, degree `a*b`
   and genus `a*b*(a+b-4)/2+1`;
3. smooth type-`(a,b)` curves on a nonsingular quadric, degree `a+b` and
   genus `a*b-a-b+1`;
4. the corresponding even- and odd-degree curves on a quadric cone described
   in V Exercise 2.9.
