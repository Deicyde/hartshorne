# Adopted classical prerequisites for curves

Later-used exercises in Hartshorne IV.2 activate three exercises that were
outside the earlier fine scope: local plane-curve intersection multiplicity,
the tangent morphism to the dual plane, and normality of square-root covers.
The first states its bounds with the multiplicity of a point on a plane curve,
so that definition is adopted too.

1. [Multiplicity of a point on a plane curve](plane-curve-point-multiplicity.md)
2. [Local intersection multiplicity of plane curves](local-plane-curve-intersection-multiplicity.md)
3. [The tangent morphism and dual plane curve](plane-curve-dual-tangent-morphism.md)
4. [Normality of square-root covers](quadratic-cover-normality.md)
