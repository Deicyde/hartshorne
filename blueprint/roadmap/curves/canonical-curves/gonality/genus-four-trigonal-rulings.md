# Trigonal systems in genus four

Let `X` be a nonhyperelliptic genus-four canonical curve on its unique
irreducible quadric `Q`.

- If `Q` is smooth, `X` has type `(3,3)` and the two rulings cut out exactly
  two `g^1_3` systems.
- If `Q` is a quadric cone, its unique ruling cuts out the unique `g^1_3`.

Thus every genus-four curve is trigonal, with the hyperelliptic case obtained
by adding a point to its `g^1_2`.

## Proof depends on

- Intersect the canonical curve with ruling lines and use uniqueness of the
  containing quadric to rule out any additional trigonal pencils.
- For a rank-three quadratic form, lines on its projective cone are exactly
  the generators through the vertex; their one-parameter family is the unique
  ruling. This elementary cone calculation is independent of the later V.2.9
  degree/genus formulas.
