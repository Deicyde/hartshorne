# Fine moduli and the isotrivial-family obstruction

A fine moduli variety for genus-`g` curves would carry a universal flat family
such that every other family is its pullback along a unique classifying
morphism. Such a fine variety cannot exist with this property: a nontrivial
family all of whose fibres are mutually isomorphic would have constant
classifying map and hence be a product family.

This node is not ready: the existing deformation example below is nonproduct
but does not prove that all fibres are mutually isomorphic. A genuinely
isotrivial nonproduct family or a precise external construction is still
required.
