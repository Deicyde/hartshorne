# Dimensions are superadditive under addition of linear systems

For effective divisors `D,E` on a Chapter IV curve, addition of divisors
defines a finite-to-one morphism

`|D| times |E| -> |D+E|`,  `(D',E') |-> D'+E'`.

Consequently

`dim|D| + dim|E| <= dim|D+E|`.

## Proof depends on

- Multiplication of global sections induces the projective morphism.
- An effective divisor has only finitely many coefficientwise decompositions
  as a sum of two effective divisors, so the morphism is quasi-finite onto its
  image and preserves dimension.
