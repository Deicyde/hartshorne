# Hyperelliptic curves and degree-two linear systems

For a Chapter IV curve of genus at least two, the following are equivalent:

1. there is a finite degree-two morphism `X -> P^1`;
2. `X` carries a basepoint-free linear system of degree two and dimension
   one, classically a `g^1_2`.

Every genus-two curve is hyperelliptic, and its canonical system is such a
`g^1_2`.

  associated to a basepoint-free pencil has degree equal to its divisor degree.
