# The hyperelliptic pencil is unique

A hyperelliptic Chapter IV curve has exactly one `g^1_2`.

## Depends on

- [The hyperelliptic canonical map factors through a rational normal curve](hyperelliptic-canonical-factorization.md)

## Proof depends on

- Every chosen degree-two pencil is recovered as the fibres of the canonical
  map before its rational-normal embedding, so two choices coincide.
