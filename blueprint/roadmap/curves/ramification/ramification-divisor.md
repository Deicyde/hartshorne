# The ramification divisor

For a finite separable morphism of curves, define the effective divisor

`R_f = sum_(P in X) length((Omega_(X/Y))_P) P`.

Its support is the ramification locus. The associated effective Cartier
subscheme is the vanishing scheme of the differential map, equivalently the
divisor cut out by the different/Fitting ideal of `Omega_(X/Y)`.
