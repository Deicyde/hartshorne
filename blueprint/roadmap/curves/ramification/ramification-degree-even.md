# The ramification divisor has even degree

For every finite separable morphism of curves, the degree of its ramification
divisor is even.

## Depends on

- [Riemann–Hurwitz](riemann-hurwitz.md)

## Proof depends on

- Rearranging Riemann–Hurwitz expresses `deg R_f` as a difference of even
  integers.
