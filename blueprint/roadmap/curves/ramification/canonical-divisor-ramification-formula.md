# The canonical-divisor ramification formula

Let `f : X -> Y` be a finite separable morphism of curves and let `R_f` be
its ramification divisor. For canonical divisors `K_X` and `K_Y`,

`K_X ~ f^*K_Y + R_f`.

The conclusion is linear equivalence; no preferred canonical-divisor
representatives are asserted.

## Proof depends on

- Tensor the differential sequence by `Omega_X^(-1)` and identify the ideal
  sheaf of `R_f` with `O_X(-R_f)`.
