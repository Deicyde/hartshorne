# Degree and flatness of a finite curve morphism

Let `f : X -> Y` be a finite morphism of curves over an algebraically closed
field. Define

`deg f = [K(X) : K(Y)]`.

Then `f` is finite flat, hence finite locally free of constant rank `deg f`.

## Proof depends on

- A finite map between integral curves is dominant.
- A finite flat module over a local ring is free, and its rank is its generic
  dimension over the function field.
