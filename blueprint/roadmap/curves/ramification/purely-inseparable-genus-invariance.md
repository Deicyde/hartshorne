# Genus invariance under purely inseparable curve maps

Under the hypotheses of the purely inseparable Frobenius factorization,

`g(X) = g(Y)`.

This is a theorem under Hartshorne's algebraically closed base-field
convention. No imperfect-field generalization is asserted: over an imperfect
field, purely inseparable maps of nonsingular projective curves can change
the genus.

## Depends on

- [Purely inseparable curve maps are iterated Frobenius](purely-inseparable-frobenius-factorization.md)

## Proof depends on

- Hartshorne's Frobenius twists have the same underlying abstract scheme, and
  genus is invariant under abstract-scheme isomorphism.
