# Length of the relative-differential stalk

Under the hypotheses and notation of the local differential ratio,

`(Omega_(X/Y))_P ~= O_(X,P)/(dt/du)`.

It is a finite-length cyclic module and

`length_(O_(X,P))((Omega_(X/Y))_P) = v_P(dt/du)`.

## Depends on

- [The local differential ratio](local-differential-ratio.md)
- [Ramification has finite support](finite-ramification-support.md)

## Proof depends on

- The cokernel description in the differential exact sequence.
- The length of a principal quotient of a DVR equals the valuation of its
  generator.
