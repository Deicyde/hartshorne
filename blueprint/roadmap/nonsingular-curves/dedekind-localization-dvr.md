# Localizations of a Dedekind domain are DVRs

Let $A$ be a Noetherian, integrally closed domain of Krull dimension one,
and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a nonzero prime ideal. Then the localization
$A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a discrete valuation ring.

The hypotheses are Hartshorne's definition of a Dedekind domain. The
conclusion is the observation immediately following that definition.
