# Effectivity of compatible adic module systems

Let `A` be Noetherian and `I` an ideal. Suppose `M n` is a finite
`A/I^(n+1)`-module, the transition maps are surjective, and for `n' >= n` the
kernel of `M n' ⟶ M n` is `I^(n+1) M n'`. Then

`M = lim M n`

is a finite `Ahat`-module and `M / I^(n+1)M ≅ M n` for every `n`.
