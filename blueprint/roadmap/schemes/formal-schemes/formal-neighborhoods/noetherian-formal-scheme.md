# Noetherian formal schemes

Define a Noetherian formal scheme to be a locally ringed space with a finite
open cover whose members are isomorphic to completions of Noetherian schemes
along closed subschemes. This is the source definition; it does not require
the chosen ambient schemes to be affine. Morphisms are exactly morphisms of
locally ringed spaces.

The unique main artifact is the bundled subtype/category carrying this finite
formal-affine cover. The structure sheaf remains ring-valued, not a sheaf of
topological rings.
