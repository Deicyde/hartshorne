# Ideals of definition are cofinal

If $J_{1}$ and $J_{2}$ are ideals of definition on a Noetherian formal scheme, then
there are positive integers $a,b$ such that

$J_{1} \supseteq J_{2}^a$ and $J_{2} \supseteq J_{1}^b$.
