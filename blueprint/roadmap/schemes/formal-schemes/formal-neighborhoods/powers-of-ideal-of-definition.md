# Powers of an ideal of definition

If `J` is an ideal of definition on a Noetherian formal scheme, then
`J^(n+1)` is an ideal of definition for every `n : Nat`.

## Depends on

- [Ideals of definition](ideal-of-definition.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The largest ideal of definition](largest-ideal-of-definition.md)
- [Ideals of definition are cofinal](ideals-of-definition-cofinal.md)
- Coherence of powers of a coherent ideal on each Noetherian scheme
  thickening.
- The quotient by a power has the same underlying support.
