# Scheme completions are formal schemes

The completion of any Noetherian scheme along a closed subscheme is a
Noetherian formal scheme. Such a formal scheme is called algebraizable.

## Depends on

- [Noetherian formal schemes](noetherian-formal-scheme.md)
- [Formal completion as a locally ringed space](formal-completion.md)

## Proof depends on

- A finite affine open cover of the Noetherian ambient scheme restricts to a
  finite formal-affine cover of its completion.
- [Affine sections of a formal completion](formal-completion-affine-sections.md)
