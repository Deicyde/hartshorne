# Abelian closure of coherent formal modules

The kernel, cokernel, and image of any morphism of coherent modules on a
Noetherian formal scheme are coherent. Equivalently, coherent formal modules
form an abelian subcategory of all structure-sheaf modules.

## Depends on

- [Coherent modules on a Noetherian formal scheme](coherent-formal-module.md)

## Proof depends on

- [The affine formal module equivalence](affine-formal-coherent-equivalence.md)
- Kernels, cokernels, and images of maps of finite modules over a Noetherian
  ring are finite.
- Locality on a finite formal-affine cover.
