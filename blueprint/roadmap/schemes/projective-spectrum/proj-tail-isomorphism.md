# Proj is invariant under an eventual graded isomorphism

Let `φ : S → T` be a degree-preserving homomorphism.  If there is `d₀` such
that `φ_d : S_d → T_d` is an isomorphism for every `d ≥ d₀`, then the natural
domain is all of `Proj T` and the induced morphism

`Proj T → Proj S`

is an isomorphism.

Use high powers to detect membership in homogeneous primes and to replace all
fractions on a standard open by numerators and denominators in the stable
range.  This identifies the underlying points and every standard affine chart;
the target-local isomorphism criterion finishes.
