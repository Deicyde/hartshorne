# Connected schemes

A scheme is connected when its underlying topological space is connected.

Reuse the standard topological predicate on the carrier of a scheme; no
scheme-specific replacement is introduced.

## Depends on

- [Schemes and affine schemes](../spectrum-and-schemes/scheme.md)

## Proof depends on

- Nothing beyond the topology carried by a scheme.
