# Sheaves of abelian groups

On a topological space $X$, a presheaf of abelian groups is a contravariant
functor from the category of open subsets of $X$ to the category of abelian groups. A sheaf is
such a functor in which compatible sections on an open cover glue uniquely.
Morphisms are natural transformations, and sections restrict functorially.

Hartshorne additionally requires a presheaf to have zero sections on
the empty open. For a sheaf, this follows from unique gluing for
the empty cover.
