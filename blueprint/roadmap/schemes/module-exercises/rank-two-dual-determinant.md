# A rank-two locally free sheaf is its dual twisted by its determinant

For a locally free sheaf $F$ of rank $2$,

$F\cong F^\vee\otimes\bigwedge^2 F$.

This is the case $n = 2$, $r = 1$ of the perfect exterior-power pairing
$\bigwedge^r F\cong(\bigwedge^{n-r}F)^\vee\otimes\bigwedge^n F$, together with the
identification $\bigwedge^1 F\cong F$ of the degree-one exterior power.
