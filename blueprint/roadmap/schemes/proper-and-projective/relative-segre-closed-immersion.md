# The relative Segre embedding

For every scheme `Y` and natural numbers `r,s`, there is a closed immersion

`ℙʳ_Y ×[Y] ℙˢ_Y ⟶ ℙʳˢ⁺ʳ⁺ˢ_Y`

whose homogeneous coordinates are `zᵢⱼ = xᵢyⱼ`. It is compatible with
arbitrary base change in `Y`.

Construct the map over `Spec ℤ` from the corresponding graded/bigraded
coordinate-ring map. On every standard affine chart, identify it with the
closed immersion cut out by the `2 × 2` minors and verify compatibility on
overlaps. Closed-immersion target locality assembles the charts. Define the
relative map by base change and transport along the projective-space pullback
isomorphisms.

The existing Chapter-I `Hartshorne.segre_embedding` treats classical points
over a field and is motivation, not a proof of this scheme-level statement.
