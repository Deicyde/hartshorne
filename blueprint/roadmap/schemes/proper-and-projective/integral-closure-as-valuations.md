# Integral closure as an intersection of valuation rings

For a subset `s` of a field `K`, the integral closure in `K` of the subring
generated by `s` is the intersection of all valuation subrings of `K`
containing `s`. Taking `s` to be the carrier of a subring `A ⊆ K` gives
Theorem 4.11A: the integral closure of `A` in `K` is the intersection of all
valuation rings of `K` containing `A`.

The proof separates an element outside the integral closure by a valuation
subring: adjoin its inverse, extend the resulting proper ideal to a valuation
subring, and show that the element itself is excluded. The reverse containment
uses integral closedness of every valuation ring.

## Proof depends on

- Maximal local subrings of a field are valuation subrings.
