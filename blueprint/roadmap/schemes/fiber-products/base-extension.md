# Base extension

For a scheme $X \to S$ and a morphism $S' \to S$, the base extension of $X$ to $S'$ is $X \times _S S'$, equipped with its second projection to $S'$.

## Depends on

- [Fiber products of schemes exist](schemes-have-fiber-products.md)

## Proof depends on

- The pullback universal property.
