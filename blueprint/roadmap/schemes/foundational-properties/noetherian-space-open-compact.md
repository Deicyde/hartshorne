# Noetherian spaces and quasi-compact opens

A topological space is Noetherian if and only if every open subset is
quasi-compact.

For the forward direction, use stabilization of the increasing sequence of
finite unions from an open cover.  Conversely, an increasing chain of opens
has union covered by the chain and therefore a finite, hence largest,
subcover.  This is Exercise 2.13(a).
