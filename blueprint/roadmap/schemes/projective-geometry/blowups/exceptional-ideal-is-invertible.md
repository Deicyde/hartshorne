# The exceptional ideal is invertible

For `pi : Bl_I(X) -> X`, the inverse-image ideal
`I_E = pi^{-1}I · O_{Bl_I(X)}` is invertible and canonically isomorphic to
`O(1)`. If `E` denotes its associated effective Cartier divisor, then

`I_E ≅ O(1) ≅ O(-E)`.

This statement fixes Hartshorne's Rees grading and sign convention.
