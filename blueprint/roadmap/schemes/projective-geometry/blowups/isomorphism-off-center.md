# A blowup is an isomorphism off its center

If `Y` is the closed subscheme defined by `I`, the blowup projection restricts
to an isomorphism `Bl_I(X) \ pi^{-1}(Y) ≅ X \ Y`.

## Proof depends on

- The local identity `Proj O_U[T] ≅ U` where `I|_U = O_U`.
