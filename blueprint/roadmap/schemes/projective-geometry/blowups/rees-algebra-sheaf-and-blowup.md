# Rees algebras and blowups

For a coherent ideal sheaf $I$ on a Noetherian scheme $X$, construct the
quasi-coherent graded Rees algebra $\mathcal{R}(I)=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}I^d$ and define
the blowup $Bl_I(X) = \operatorname{Proj} _X R(I)$ with its projection to $X$.

The inverse-image ideal is the image of $f^*I\to\mathcal{O}$;
it is distinct from the module pullback $f^*I$ itself.
