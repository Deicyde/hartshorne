# The cubic ruled surface from conics through a point

Let `P` be a point of `P^2`. The subsystem of conics through `P` embeds
`P^2 - {P}` into `P^4`; after blowing up `P`, it extends to a closed immersion
of the blowup as a degree-three ruled surface. The strict transforms of the
lines through `P` are pairwise disjoint lines covering that surface.

The extension, closed-immersion criterion, degree computation, and description
of the ruling are supporting clauses of this single geometric realization.
