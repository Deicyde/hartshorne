# A cotangent criterion for surjectivity of a local map

Let $A \to B$ be a local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings. If it induces
an isomorphism on residue fields, a surjection $m_A \to m_B / m_B^2$, and $B$
is finite as an $A$-module, then $A \to B$ is surjective.

## Proof depends on

- Nakayama's lemma, first for $m_B$ and then for the finite $A$-module $B$.
