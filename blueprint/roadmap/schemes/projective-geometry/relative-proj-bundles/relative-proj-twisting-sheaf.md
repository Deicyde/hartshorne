# The relative twisting sheaf

Let $X$ and $S$ satisfy condition (†): $X$ is a Noetherian scheme and $S$ is a
quasi-coherent sheaf of graded $\mathcal{O}_X$-algebras with $S_0 = \mathcal{O}_X$,
$S_1$ coherent, and $S$ locally generated by $S_1$ as an $\mathcal{O}_X$-algebra.
For the affine opens $U \subseteq X$, the twisting sheaves $\mathcal{O}(1)$ on the
schemes $\operatorname{Proj} S(U)$ are compatible with the identifications of these
schemes over smaller affine opens (Proposition II.5.12(c)), and so they glue to a
sheaf $\mathcal{O}(1)$ on the relative $\operatorname{Proj} S$. This sheaf is
invertible, and for every affine open $U \subseteq X$ its restriction to the
preimage $\pi^{-1}(U) \cong \operatorname{Proj} S(U)$ of $U$ under the projection
$\pi : \operatorname{Proj} S \to X$ is the twisting sheaf $\mathcal{O}(1)$ of
$\operatorname{Proj} S(U)$.

The same gluing gives a sheaf $\mathcal{O}(n)$ on $\operatorname{Proj} S$ for every
integer $n$, with the same compatibility over affine opens of the base. The
hypotheses (†) are kept as in the source; of them, the proof uses only that $S$ is
locally generated by $S_1$, and only for invertibility.
