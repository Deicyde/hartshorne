# Noetherian induction

Let `P` be a property of closed subsets of a Noetherian topological space. If
`P(Y)` follows whenever `P` holds for every proper closed subset of `Y`, then
`P` holds for every closed subset, in particular for the whole space.

The strict-inclusion relation on closed subsets of a Noetherian space is well
founded; apply well-founded induction.
