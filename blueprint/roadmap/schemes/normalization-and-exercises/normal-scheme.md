# Normal schemes

A scheme is normal when every local ring is an integrally closed domain.

Introduce a scheme-level predicate quantified over stalks. Record separately the domain and integral-closedness fields so it composes cleanly with localization and affine criteria.

- [Schemes and affine schemes](../spectrum-and-schemes/scheme.md)

## Proof depends on

- Integrally closed domains and local-ring localization.
