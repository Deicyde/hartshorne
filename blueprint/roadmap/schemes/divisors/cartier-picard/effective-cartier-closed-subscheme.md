# Effective Cartier divisors and locally principal subschemes

Define a Cartier divisor to be effective when it has local equations in
`O_X`. Sending those equations to the locally generated ideal sheaf gives a
bijection between effective Cartier divisors and closed subschemes locally cut
out by one non-zero-divisor.

The non-zero-divisor condition is retained explicitly: it follows from the
local equation being a unit in the total quotient sheaf and prevents an
arbitrary principal zero-divisor subscheme from being mislabeled Cartier.

## Proof depends on

- Compatible principal ideal sheaves glue to a closed subscheme.
- A local generator can be recovered from the closed subscheme up to a
  regular unit.
