# Weil classes and the Picard group on a locally factorial scheme

For an integral separated Noetherian locally factorial scheme `X`, the
Weil–Cartier comparison and the Cartier–Picard comparison compose to a natural
group isomorphism

`Cl X ≅ Pic X`.

This is Corollary 6.16. Naturality here means compatibility with the two
canonical quotient maps and the assignment `D ↦ O_X(D)`; it does not assert a
general pullback operation for Weil divisors.
