# The invertible sheaf associated to a Cartier divisor

For a Cartier divisor `D` represented by local equations `(U_i,f_i)`, define
`O_X(D)` as the `O_X`-submodule of `K_X` locally generated by `f_i^{-1}`.
Prove that it is invertible and that this construction is a bijection between
Cartier divisors and invertible `O_X`-submodules of `K_X`, where the embedding
in `K_X` is part of the data.

The unique main result is this embedded-sheaf correspondence; abstract
isomorphism classes are handled in the following nodes.
