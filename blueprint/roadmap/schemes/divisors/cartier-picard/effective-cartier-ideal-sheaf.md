# The ideal sheaf of an effective Cartier divisor

Let `D` be an effective Cartier divisor and `Y` its associated locally
principal closed subscheme. Prove that the ideal sheaf of `Y` is naturally
isomorphic, as an embedded `O_X`-submodule of `O_X`, to `O_X(-D)`.

Locally, both sheaves are generated by the same regular non-zero-divisor. This
is Proposition 6.18.
