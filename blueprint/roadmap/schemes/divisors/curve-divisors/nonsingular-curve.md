# Nonsingular curves over a field

Let $k$ be a field. A *curve over $k$* is an integral scheme $X$ that is
separated and of finite type over $k$ and has dimension one. A curve $X$ over
$k$ is *nonsingular* when every local ring $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$ is a regular
local ring.

Prove that a nonsingular curve $X$ over $k$ satisfies condition $(*)$: it is
Noetherian, integral, separated, and regular in codimension one. Prove that
its prime divisors are exactly its closed points: a point $x \in X$ is closed
if and only if it has codimension one. Hence a Weil divisor on $X$ is a finite
sum $\sum_i n_i P_i$ of closed points $P_i$.

Hartshorne states his results on curves in this section over an algebraically
closed field. The definition and the two facts above do not need that
hypothesis.
