# Inverse image is left adjoint to direct image

For a continuous map $f : X \to Y$, sheaves $G$ on $Y$, and $F$ on $X$,
there is a natural bijection

$\operatorname{Hom} _X(f^{-1}G, F) \simeq \operatorname{Hom} _Y(G, f_*F)$.
