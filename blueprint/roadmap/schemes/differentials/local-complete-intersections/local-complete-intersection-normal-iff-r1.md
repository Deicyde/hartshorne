# Normality of a local complete intersection

Let $Y$ be a local complete intersection subscheme of a nonsingular variety.
Then $Y$ is normal if and only if it is regular in codimension one, that is,
its local rings of dimension at most one are regular (condition $R_1$ of
Theorem 8.22A).

## Depends on

- [Serre's criterion for normality](serre-normality-criterion.md)
- [Local complete intersections are Cohen–Macaulay](local-complete-intersection-cohen-macaulay.md)

## Proof depends on

- A Cohen–Macaulay local ring satisfies $(S2)$.
- A normal Noetherian ring satisfies $(R1)$.
