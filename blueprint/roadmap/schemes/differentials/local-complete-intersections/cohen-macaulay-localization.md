# Localization of Cohen–Macaulay rings

If `A` is a Noetherian Cohen–Macaulay local ring and `p` is a prime ideal of
`A`, then `A_p` is Cohen–Macaulay.

## Depends on

- [Cohen–Macaulay local rings and schemes](cohen-macaulay-local-rings-and-schemes.md)

## Proof depends on

- Localization of regular sequences and the dimension-depth formula for
  Cohen–Macaulay local rings.
