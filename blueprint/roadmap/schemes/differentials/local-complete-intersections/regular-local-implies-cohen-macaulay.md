# Regular local rings are Cohen–Macaulay

Every Noetherian regular local ring is Cohen–Macaulay.

## Depends on

- [Cohen–Macaulay local rings and schemes](cohen-macaulay-local-rings-and-schemes.md)
- [Regular local rings](../../../nonsingular-varieties/regular-local-rings.md)

## Proof depends on

- A minimal generating set of the maximal ideal of a regular local ring is a
  regular sequence.
