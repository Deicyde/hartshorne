# Cohen–Macaulay local rings and schemes

For a module over a Noetherian local ring, define depth as the supremum of the
lengths of regular sequences in the maximal ideal. Define a Noetherian local
ring to be Cohen–Macaulay when its depth equals its Krull dimension, and define
a locally Noetherian scheme to be Cohen–Macaulay when all its local rings are
Cohen–Macaulay.
