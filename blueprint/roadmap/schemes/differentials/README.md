# Differentials, regularity, and canonical sheaves

Hartshorne II.8 develops Kähler differentials from their algebraic universal
property through the scheme differential sheaf, then uses them to study
nonsingularity, Bertini, canonical sheaves, adjunction, Cohen–Macaulay and
local-complete-intersection schemes, and blowups along regular centers.

The running text occupies printed pp. 172–187. The adopted exercise scope
extends through the top of p. 190 because later included chapters use Exercises
8.7 and 8.8. Generic smoothness and basepoint-free Bertini remain in Chapter
III.10 rather than being folded into the embedded hyperplane theorem here.
