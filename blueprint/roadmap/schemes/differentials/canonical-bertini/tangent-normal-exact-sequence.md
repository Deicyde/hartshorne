# The tangent-normal exact sequence

For a nonsingular closed subvariety $Y$ of a nonsingular variety $X$,
dualizing the conormal sequence gives a short exact sequence

$0 \to T_Y \to T_X|_Y \to N_{(Y/X)} \to 0$.
