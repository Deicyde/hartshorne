# Regular hypersurface quotients of a regular local ring

Let `(A, m)` be a regular local domain and let `f` be a nonzero element of
`m`. Then `A/(f)` is regular if and only if the class of `f` in `m/m^2` is
nonzero, equivalently `f` does not belong to `m^2`. The domain hypothesis is
exactly what the Bertini application supplies from the integral ambient
variety.

## Depends on

- [Regular local rings](../../../nonsingular-varieties/regular-local-rings.md)

## Proof depends on

- The embedding-dimension characterization of a regular local ring.
- A nonzero element of a domain is a non-zero-divisor, and its principal
  quotient has dimension one less.
