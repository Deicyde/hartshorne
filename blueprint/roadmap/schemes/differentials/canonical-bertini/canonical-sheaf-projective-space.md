# The canonical sheaf of projective space

For `X = P^n_k`, dualizing the Euler sequence gives

`0 -> O_X -> O_X(1)^(n+1) -> T_X -> 0`,

and taking determinants gives `omega_X ~= O_X(-n-1)`. Consequently
`p_g(P^n_k) = 0` for `n >= 1`.
