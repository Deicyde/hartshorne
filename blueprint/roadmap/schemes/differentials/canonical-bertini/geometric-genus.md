# Geometric genus

For a nonsingular projective variety `X` over `k`, define its geometric genus
by

`p_g(X) = dim_k Gamma(X, omega_X)`.

## Depends on

- [The canonical sheaf](canonical-sheaf.md)

## Proof depends on

- Finite-dimensionality of global sections of a coherent sheaf on a
  projective variety.
