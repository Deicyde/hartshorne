# The Euler sequence on projective space

For `X = ℙⁿ_A` over `Y = Spec A`, construct the exact sequence

`0 → Ω[X/Y] → 𝒪_X(-1)^(n+1) → 𝒪_X → 0`,

where the final map is given by the homogeneous coordinates.  On each
standard affine chart, identify the kernel with the free module generated by
the differentials of the coordinate ratios, and prove that these chartwise
isomorphisms glue.
