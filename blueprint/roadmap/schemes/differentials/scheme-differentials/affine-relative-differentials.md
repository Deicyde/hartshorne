# Differentials on affine schemes

For a ring map $A \to B$, identify the relative differential sheaf on the
induced affine morphism with the sheaf associated to the Kähler module:

$\Omega_{\operatorname{Spec}B/\operatorname{Spec}A}\cong\widetilde{\Omega_{B/A}}$.

Under this isomorphism the sheaf derivation is induced by the universal ring
derivation.  As a corollary, for every scheme $Y$, if
$X=\mathbf{A}^{n}_{Y}$, then $\Omega_{X/Y}$ is a free $\mathcal{O}_{X}$-module of
rank $n$, generated by the global sections $dx_{1},\ldots ,dx_{n}$.

## Proof depends on

- Compatibility of the construction with restriction to principal affine
  opens, using localization of Kähler differentials.
- [Localization of Kähler differentials](../kaehler-differentials/kahler-differential-localization.md)
- [The universal property of the relative differential sheaf](relative-differentials-universal.md)
- Locality of the relative differential sheaf: if $U\subseteq X$ and
  $V\subseteq Y$ are open with $f(U)\subseteq V$, then
  $\Omega_{X/Y}|_{U}\cong\Omega_{U/V}$.  Over an affine open
  $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Y$, the open subscheme of $\mathbf{A}^{n}_{Y}$
  lying over it is $\operatorname{Spec}A[x_{1},\ldots ,x_{n}]$, where the global
  sections $x_{i}$ restrict to the coordinates; these opens cover
  $\mathbf{A}^{n}_{Y}$, so the corollary reduces to the affine case.
