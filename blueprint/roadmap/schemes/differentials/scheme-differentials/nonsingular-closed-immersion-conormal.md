# The conormal bundle of a nonsingular closed subvariety

Let $Y \hookrightarrow X$ be an irreducible closed subscheme of a nonsingular variety over
an algebraically closed field, and assume that $Y$ is nonsingular.  Then the
conormal sequence is short exact:

$0 \to J/J^{2} \to \Omega_{X/k} \otimes \mathcal{O}_Y \to \Omega_{Y/k} \to 0$.

Moreover, if $r = \operatorname{codim} (Y,X)$, then $J/J^{2}$ is locally free of rank $r$ and
$J$ is locally generated by $r$ elements.
