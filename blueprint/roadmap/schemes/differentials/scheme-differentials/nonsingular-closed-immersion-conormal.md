# The conormal bundle of a nonsingular closed subvariety

Let `Y ↪ X` be an irreducible closed subscheme of a nonsingular variety over
an algebraically closed field, and assume that `Y` is nonsingular.  Then the
conormal sequence is short exact:

`0 → J/J² → Ω[X/k] ⊗ 𝒪_Y → Ω[Y/k] → 0`.

Moreover, if `r = codim(Y,X)`, then `J/J²` is locally free of rank `r` and
`J` is locally generated by `r` elements.

- Nakayama's lemma and the dimension bound for quotients by `r` generators.
- Local freeness of kernels in short exact sequences with locally free
  quotient.
