# Normal complete intersections are projectively normal

Let `Y` be a positive-dimensional normal global complete intersection in
`P^n_k`. Then `Y` is projectively normal.

## Depends on

- [Global complete intersections and hypersurface intersections](global-complete-intersection-iff-hypersurface-intersection.md)
- [Normality of a local complete intersection](../local-complete-intersections/local-complete-intersection-normal-iff-r1.md)
- [Projective normality criterion](../../projective-sheaves/projective-normality-criterion.md)

## Proof depends on

- The affine cone of a global complete intersection is a local complete
  intersection.
- Normality away from the cone vertex follows from normality of `Y`; the
  vertex has codimension at least two.
