# Differentials of a product

For schemes $X$ and $Y$ over $S$, with projections from $X \times _S Y$, there
is a natural isomorphism

$\Omega_{(X\times_S Y)/S}\simeq p_X^*\Omega_{X/S}\oplus p_Y^*\Omega_{Y/S}$.
