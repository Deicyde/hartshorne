# Birational invariance of regular q-forms

For a nonsingular projective variety `X` and `0 <= q <= dim X`, define

`h^(q,0)(X) = dim_k Gamma(X, exterior^q Omega_(X/k))`.

If `X` and `X'` are birationally equivalent nonsingular projective varieties,
then `h^(q,0)(X) = h^(q,0)(X')` for every `q`.

## Proof depends on

- Pullback of relative differentials on the common birational open subset.
- Sections of a finite locally free sheaf extend across a codimension-at-least-
  two complement on a normal variety after local trivialization.
- Choose common dense opens on which the birational maps are mutually inverse,
  then apply the construction symmetrically.
