# Global complete intersections and hypersurface intersections

Let `Y` be a codimension-`r` closed subscheme of `P^n_k`. Its homogeneous
ideal is generated by `r` elements exactly when there are hypersurfaces
`H_1, ..., H_r` such that

`Y = H_1 intersect ... intersect H_r`

as schemes.
