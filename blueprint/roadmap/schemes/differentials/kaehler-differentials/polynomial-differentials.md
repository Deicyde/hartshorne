# Differentials of a polynomial algebra

For a polynomial algebra `A[xᵢ | i ∈ ι]`, the differentials `dxᵢ` form a
basis of the module of relative differentials.  For finite `ι` this makes
`Ω[A[xᵢ]⁄A]` free of rank `|ι|`.
