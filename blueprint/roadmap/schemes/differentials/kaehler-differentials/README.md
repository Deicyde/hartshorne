# Kähler differentials and local regularity

This subchapter covers the algebraic foundation of Hartshorne II.8, printed
pp. 172–174 and the localization theorem on p. 177.  It separates the module
of Kähler differentials from the cotangent space of a local ring and from the
scheme-level differential sheaf constructed in the next subchapter.
