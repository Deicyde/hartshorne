# General-field finiteness of integral closure

Let `k` be an arbitrary field, let `A` be a finitely generated `k`-algebra
domain with fraction field `K`, and let `L/K` be finite. The integral closure
of `A` in `L` is a finite `A`-module.

This is the general-field normalization input actually used in Theorem 5.19.
The previously formalized Chapter I node assumes the book's algebraically
closed standing field and therefore is not used as a dependency here. The
stronger replacement of `A` by an arbitrary Nagata ring, mentioned in Remark
5.19.2, remains deferred.

## Depends on

No project-local statement prerequisites.

## Proof depends on

- Finite-type algebras over a field are Nagata, hence Japanese.
