# Twists and graded shifts

Let `S` be generated by `S₁` as an `S₀`-algebra. For a graded `S`-module `M`
and `n ∈ Z`, there is a natural isomorphism

`M~ tensor O_X(n) ≅ M(n)~`.

In particular, `O_X(n) tensor O_X(m) ≅ O_X(n+m)`. These isomorphisms are
associative and compatible with the chosen chart trivializations.
