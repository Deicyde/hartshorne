# Reconstruction from graded global sections

Let $S$ be finitely generated by $S_{1}$ over $S_{0}$, $X = \operatorname{Proj} S$, and $F$
quasi-coherent. The evaluation morphism

$\widetilde{\Gamma_*(F)} \to F$

is an isomorphism.
